## 3. Why grounding and labels did not stop it

- **A quote grounds a fragment, not the claim.** `paris-delay`'s quote is "where it lay unanswered a year wanting eight days…". It proves the delay, not who delayed. The grounder drops an item only when its quote is absent (`sourceQuoteGrounding.ts:17-24`).
- **80-89% of the sidecar carries no quote.** That covers focus, coreClaim, centralConcept, keyClaims, hardEdge, voiceCues, paraphraseNotes, and every fact's because/commonError/whyWrong (about 4.5k characters per chapter). Yet the object is labelled `source-text` provenance.
- **"NOT CITABLE" did not keep keyClaims out of the prose.** "lacking proper titles" appears only in keyClaims[5] and paraphraseNotes. The writer never saw paraphraseNotes (`sourcePacket.ts:151`), so the rr21 sentence came through the "NOT CITABLE" chapterContext block (INFERRED from the field locations).
- **Every fact must carry a "because"** (`CAUSAL_RE`, `sourceIntegrity.ts:69`), and the source often gives none. Invented cause or motive is the largest rr21 error class (INFERRED).
- **Stale prompt contract.** The researcher prompt still calls paraphraseNotes "primary source material" and "the field downstream prose writers read most carefully" (`researcher-chapter.system.md:54,155`), while the packet deliberately drops it.

## 4. Why nothing downstream fixed it

- **The editor rewords only.** It must keep the same key index and keyed words, the same set of digit numbers, and every name; it may not add a digit or a name (`chapterEditorContract.ts:99-108`). "1768" to "1758" is refused unless 1758 is already on the page, and a wrong key cannot be re-keyed. This explains the handoff's "the editor passed all 17 compile-era error phrases".
- **The panel has no source lane, and its attribution WARNs cannot gate** (09-23 report).
- **The fidelity judge never ran on rr21** (0 SF codes in 68 review files; 09-23 report). Where it does run, it is Sonnet 5 at high effort (section 7 shows that model missed invented causes and inverted sequences), and its matcher demotes findings (section 5).
- **The judge skips keys on "Imagine/Suppose/You are" items** that are not about Franklin's own events (`sourceFidelityJudge.ts:1007`), and 108/171 rr21 stems start "Suppose"/"Imagine" (HANDOFF). A key that encodes false history inside a transfer frame is judged only through its prompt, choices and explanation claims (INFERRED risk).
- **Scar fact pins were misrouted.** They carry 4-part labels: 11 pins labelled "(ch03)" cover the fire company, paving, hospital, library, Junto, lottery, academy, Whitefield and the almanac, and would render into the 19-chapter ch03 "Arrival in Philadelphia" (`config/book-scars/the-autobiography-of-benjamin-franklin.json`; scoper `sectionTasks.ts:293-298`; the 09-23 verify report confirms ch05-ch19 got no chapter pins).

## 5. The footnote-marker defect, reasoned RED/GREEN and then run with 0 calls

Code path: `classifySourceFidelityFindings` -> `sourceCitationProblems` (`sourceFidelityJudge.ts:725-741`) -> `contains(spanText, sourceQuote)` (`:621-625`) -> `normalizedQuote` (`sourceText.ts:174-206`). That normalization collapses whitespace and folds curly quotes, dashes and ellipses only (`:82-87`). Case, bracketed markers, elisions and ellipsis gaps survive. Ingestion (`normalizeIngestedText`, `:156-158`) strips only the BOM, CR and paired underscores. Footnote markers such as `[7]` and `[50]`, and the indented footnote *bodies* ("[116] A celebrated prehistoric ruin…"), stay inside every chapter span.

- **RED:** the source reads "He died in 1702, January 6, old style,[7] just four years to a day before I was born." A judge that correctly contradicts "Thomas Franklin died in 1703" but quotes the sentence without `[7]` gets `WARN SF1.contradicted | NOT ENFORCED - the source quote does not occur…`, gate GREEN (`footnote-red-green.mts`).
- **Control:** the same finding with `[7]` copied gives BLOCKER, RED.
- **GREEN:** stripping `(?<=\S)\[\d{1,3}\]` from the span before matching gives BLOCKER, RED.
- **Other demoters** (`matcher-variants.mts`, proprietaries slice): "sign'd" -> "signed", an ellipsis join, a capital changed at the start, and a quote that spans a footnote body all give WARN. The exact copy gives BLOCKER. The Opus probe checker used an ellipsis in 1 of its 6 source quotes, so the v25 matcher would have demoted that correct finding too.
- **Density:** 8.8% (ch I slice) and 4.1% (proprietaries slice) of source sentences carry a marker. Footnote bodies are 8-11% of slice characters, and the 09-23 report counts about 3.7k of the 66k source words as footnotes and captions. Footnote bodies are editorial and can be wrong. Footnote [118] identifies Franklin's "Lord Granville" as George Grenville, the 1763-65 premier; that identification is from outside knowledge, so it is INFERRED. A "the source is the only authority" checker would accept that as supported.

## 6. Can the judges run standalone on any v21 chapter plus a span?

- **Fidelity judge:** yes, as a library. `judgeChapterSourceFidelity({chapter, source:{provenance:"source-text", spanText}, ask})`, then `classifySourceFidelityFindings(report)`. Build `ask` from the exported `sourceFidelitySystemPrompt` and `buildSourceFidelityUserPrompt`, and have it call `claude -p --output-format json --model … --effort … --disallowedTools '*'` with the prompt on stdin and parse `result`. Verified with a stub `ask` on `ch01.opus5.chapter.json`: 63 surfaces (7 prose, 12 example, 6+6+6+6 quiz, 10 card, 7 plan, 3 lines), 1 call, 48.8k-char prompt (`standalone-judges.mts`). The chapter needs only `chapterId` and `number` added. There is **no CLI verb**; its only caller is `CandidateQcEvaluator` at fresh-qc.
- **Answer-key judge:** runs as a library with a fake runner object (`{run: async req => ({attemptId, outcome:"SUCCEEDED", output})}`). It **fails on 4-choice items** and is not blind. `JUDGE_SYSTEM` is not exported, so a script must go through `makeLiveAskModel({execution:{runner, context}})`. The CLI `quiz-judge <bookId>` needs `--v25-root --attempt-root --candidate-id --manifest-digest --source-git-sha`, reads `PIPE/state/chapters/`, and grounds on the sidecar JSON cut at 8,000 chars under a header that calls it verbatim book text.
- **What a small script needs:** about 50 lines of tsx, root `node_modules/.bin/tsx`, and a `claude` binary. For v26 I would not reuse either judge as-is. Their value is the fidelity prompt's checklist (who, order, cause, credit, membership, finality). The chunk-merge and "contested absence" machinery (about 500 lines) exists only for spans over 45k characters and for a probabilistic gate that v26 does not have.

## 8. Things a plan should decide or watch

- **Choice count:** 3 or 4. If 4, the app is fine but `finalGate` A5 (`critics/finalGate.ts:1215`) and the old key judge assume 3. The new path should not call them.
- **Footnotes:** drop them, or keep them as labelled editor notes. The Pine notes carry dates and identifications a reader may want, but they are not Franklin and may be wrong.
- **Blind-solver input:** the app shows one tier per reader (HANDOFF: `ChapterReaderClient.tsx:144`). If the quiz tests details that appear only in fullRead, a fast-path reader cannot answer them. Give the solver the tier the app defaults to.
- **Chunking:** Franklin spans are about 20k characters on average (378k / 19, HANDOFF figure) and fit one call. Only a book with 45k+ spans needs splitting. Split on paragraph boundaries and check each half against the whole span; do not rebuild the v25 merge logic.
