# Standalone chapter audit protocol

This run uses the ChapterFlow Evidence, Learning, and Reader Experience Rubric v2.0 for three supplied standalone chapter artifacts.

## Scope boundary

- Each source filename explicitly identifies one chapter from a larger book.
- No result from this run is a full-book Content Design Score, certification, or claim about unsupplied chapters.
- Domain 9 (Whole-Book Coherence, Consistency, and Completion Value) is unassessable and excluded.
- Domains 1-8 are rated for observable chapter-local support only. Criteria whose normative anchors require book-wide evidence are interpreted narrowly as their within-chapter manifestation and identified as scope-limited.
- The chapter diagnostic score is the sum of Domains 1-8 weighted points (maximum 95), divided by 95 and multiplied by 100. It is comparable only across the three supplied chapter artifacts.

## Isolation and evidence

- Two mutually blind raters independently read each assigned source in full.
- Raters may use only the assigned source, repository instructions, rubric, scoring protocol, and this protocol.
- External facts, author reputation, reviews, remembered source-book content, prior scores, and counterpart results are excluded.
- External accuracy is `not_assessed`.
- Each rater uses integer 0-4 subcriterion ratings, source-section locators, and exactly three improvements.
- A fresh adjudicator receives one source and its sealed pair of rater records, resolves differences against the source, and may use half-points.

## Interpretation

Rubric bands are used only as descriptive chapter-diagnostic bands. Because the source inventory is incomplete for every actual book, book certification and full-book confidence are `unevaluable`.
