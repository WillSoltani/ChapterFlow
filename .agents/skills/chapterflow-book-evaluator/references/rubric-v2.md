# ChapterFlow Evidence, Learning, and Reader Experience Rubric v2.0

This is the normative scoring reference. Apply it only to the reader-facing content and metadata in the assigned local package. Do not use external reputation, reviews, prior scores, or remembered source-book content. Scores assess observable content design and support for learning; they do not establish actual retention, behavior change, completion, or satisfaction.

## Evaluation construct

Use this default construct unless the package explicitly and credibly declares a narrower audience:

- Reader: ordinary, genuinely interested, non-expert adult.
- Product context: using ChapterFlow for the first time.
- Goal: gain trustworthy understanding, retain useful mental models, recognize relevant situations, and apply ideas appropriately.
- Reading posture: thoughtful and motivated, but not an academic critic, subject-matter specialist, professional editor, or fan of the author.
- Tolerance: willing to think, but should not have to decode avoidable jargon, artificial complexity, bloated framework loads, or repetitive filler.
- Evidence scope: package content and metadata only.

Record the declared or inferred intended reader, assumed prior knowledge, nonfiction type, declared or inferred purpose, intended outcomes, relevant contexts and exclusions, and whether this default construct fits before scoring.

## Rubric philosophy

The learning pathway is:

> attention → manageable processing → coherent understanding → active construction → retrieval → transfer → motivated and calibrated action

Apply these commitments:

- Truth and justification are not interchangeable with entertainment.
- Understanding requires connected explanatory relationships, not isolated claims.
- Practical usefulness includes judgment, context, tradeoffs, and knowing when not to use a technique.
- Reader autonomy matters. Shame, coercion, hype, and false certainty are not legitimate substitutes for learning.
- A weighted average cannot erase serious epistemic, ethical, or safety defects.
- Design inspection can assess support for learning outcomes, but cannot prove actual reader outcomes.
- Do not score examples, novelty, emotionality, recommendation likelihood, or satisfaction separately when that would double-count the same strength. Credit them only inside the domain function they actually serve.

## Base rating scale

Rate every subcriterion from 0 to 4.

| Rating | General meaning |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Invalid, absent, seriously misleading, internally broken, or harmful. |
| 1 | Weak. Major recurring deficiencies substantially obstruct trust, understanding, memory, or use. |
| 2 | Adequate or mixed. The basic function exists, but important weaknesses, inconsistency, or superficiality remain. |
| 3 | Strong. The feature works reliably across most chapters, with limited and repairable weaknesses. |
| 4 | Exceptional. The feature is exemplary across nearly the entire book, handles difficult cases and boundaries, and is hard to improve materially. |

A 4 is rare; “very good” is insufficient. It requires book-wide evidence and resistance to obvious material improvement. Primary and verification raters use integers only. An adjudicator may use a half-point only after reviewing evidence and finding adjacent anchors equally supported. Never use tenths for a subcriterion.

## Detailed anchors

### Domain 1: Epistemic Integrity and Intellectual Honesty — 15%

#### 1.1 `claim_support_fit` — Claim-support fit

- **0:** Material claims are fabricated, contradicted by the package itself, or asserted without any intelligible basis; the teaching is unsafe or fundamentally misleading.
- **1:** Broad, confident, or causal claims routinely exceed the support presented; anecdotes are treated as proof; assertions replace reasoning.
- **2:** Most claims are plausible and usable, but qualification is uneven, some generalizations outrun the material, or support is thin in important places.
- **3:** Claims are normally proportionate to the reasoning and examples presented; fact, interpretation, inference, and recommendation are usually distinguishable; only limited gaps remain.
- **4:** Claim strength is consistently calibrated; competing explanations and evidence limits are made visible; the package models excellent evidential reasoning throughout.

#### 1.2 `uncertainty_limitations` — Uncertainty and limitations

- **0:** False certainty is central; the package denies or conceals obvious uncertainty, exceptions, or unresolved questions.
- **1:** Limitations are rarely acknowledged and principles are presented as universal despite clear contextual dependence.
- **2:** Some caveats and exceptions appear, but they are local, late, generic, or not integrated with the main teaching.
- **3:** Important uncertainty, boundary conditions, and alternatives are stated at the point of use in most chapters.
- **4:** Intellectual humility is built into the explanatory system; uncertainty changes recommendations, and difficult countercases are treated seriously rather than appended defensively.

#### 1.3 `internal_consistency_qa` — Internal consistency and instructional QA

- **0:** Contradictory teaching, broken mappings, materially wrong answer keys, or severe chapter inconsistencies remain unresolved.
- **1:** Recurring contradictions, mislabeled concepts, mismatched quiz explanations, or structural defects undermine trust.
- **2:** The package is generally coherent but contains minor errors, occasional mismatches, or ambiguous terminology that can misteach some readers.
- **3:** Reader-facing content, terminology, answer keys, and chapter relationships are clean across almost the entire package; small errors are easy to repair.
- **4:** Internal QA is exemplary: terminology, exercises, answers, examples, and cumulative claims remain aligned throughout, including difficult edge cases.

#### 1.4 `misuse_safeguards` — Misuse safeguards

- **0:** Advice encourages harmful, coercive, reckless, or predictably misleading use and omits necessary safety boundaries.
- **1:** Techniques are presented mechanically with little attention to power, risk, consent, context, or situations where they should not be used.
- **2:** Some warnings or exceptions exist, but coverage is uneven or generic.
- **3:** Material misuse risks and important “do not use when” boundaries are clear and tied to relevant chapters.
- **4:** Safeguards are integrated into the mental model; the reader learns to detect risk, adapt responsibly, seek help, document, escalate, or exit when appropriate.

### Domain 2: Audience Fit, Comprehensibility, and Cognitive Economy — 12%

#### 2.1 `language_clarity` — Language clarity

- **0:** The intended reader cannot reliably decode the prose, terminology, syntax, or explanations.
- **1:** Jargon, abstraction, compression, or awkward prose frequently obstructs meaning.
- **2:** Broadly understandable, but recurring dense passages, vague wording, or unnecessary terminology demand avoidable effort.
- **3:** Clear and precise across most chapters; technical terms are explained when needed and transitions usually expose the logic.
- **4:** Complex material is made exceptionally accessible without distortion; wording is precise, economical, and almost never creates avoidable ambiguity.

#### 2.2 `beginner_onboarding` — Beginner onboarding

- **0:** Essential prerequisites are absent and the intended non-expert cannot enter the subject.
- **1:** The package assumes substantial undeclared knowledge, roles, or context.
- **2:** Basic onboarding exists, but some concepts, acronyms, or contexts arrive before the reader is prepared.
- **3:** Prerequisites, terms, stakes, and starting models are supplied in a sensible order for most readers.
- **4:** The package anticipates novice misconceptions, activates useful prior knowledge, and creates a smooth path into complexity without patronizing the reader.

#### 2.3 `signal_noise_framework_load` — Signal-to-noise and framework load

- **0:** Noise, repetition, competing labels, or irrelevant detail overwhelms the central ideas.
- **1:** The reader must manage excessive acronyms, examples, provenance detail, decorative specificity, or repeated restatement.
- **2:** The key ideas remain recoverable, but compression or consolidation would materially improve the experience.
- **3:** Most detail earns its cognitive cost; frameworks are limited, distinct, and appropriately reinforced.
- **4:** The package achieves exceptional cognitive economy: every major framework has a clear function, redundancy is purposeful, and attention stays on the central learning problem.

#### 2.4 `audience_context_accessibility` — Audience and context accessibility

- **0:** Examples, assumptions, role requirements, or cultural context make the content inaccessible to the declared audience.
- **1:** The package is narrowly tied to specialist, executive, managerial, or culturally specific contexts without translation.
- **2:** Some broader relevance exists, but important reader groups must perform substantial adaptation themselves.
- **3:** Examples and explanations cover the intended audience well and make context-specific assumptions visible.
- **4:** The package is highly accessible across relevant contexts while preserving necessary specificity; it clearly signals who may need adaptation or additional support.

### Domain 3: Mental-Model Coherence and Explanatory Depth — 15%

#### 3.1 `central_model` — Central model

- **0:** The book is a collection of disconnected claims, slogans, or tips with no stable structure.
- **1:** A theme exists, but the reader cannot reconstruct a coherent model from it.
- **2:** A useful central framework exists but is incomplete, inconsistently applied, or insufficiently discriminating.
- **3:** The central model is clear, memorable, and useful across most chapters.
- **4:** The model is exceptionally coherent, compact, generative, and capable of organizing both ordinary and difficult cases across the entire book.

#### 3.2 `mechanism_causal_explanation` — Mechanism and causal explanation

- **0:** The package gives conclusions or instructions without explaining why they work.
- **1:** Mechanisms are mostly slogans, post hoc stories, or unsupported causal claims.
- **2:** Some mechanisms are explained, but important advice remains surface-level or causally ambiguous.
- **3:** Most major recommendations are connected to credible mechanisms or explicit reasoning.
- **4:** Mechanisms are deep, appropriately qualified, and predictive; they help readers diagnose failures and choose among interventions.

#### 3.3 `cross_concept_integration` — Cross-concept integration

- **0:** Chapters and concepts remain isolated or contradict one another.
- **1:** Connections are implied but rarely made explicit; framework accumulation creates confusion.
- **2:** Some integration occurs, especially near summaries, but readers must build many links themselves.
- **3:** Later chapters regularly build on, distinguish, and combine earlier ideas.
- **4:** The whole book functions as one integrated explanatory system; concepts constrain and clarify one another rather than merely accumulating.

#### 3.4 `nuance_diagnostic_power` — Nuance and diagnostic power

- **0:** Advice is simplistic, universalized, or unable to distinguish materially different situations.
- **1:** The package relies on binary categories or generic advice that breaks in common cases.
- **2:** Important distinctions and exceptions appear, but diagnostic guidance is incomplete.
- **3:** The reader learns to distinguish causes, contexts, severity, and appropriate responses in most relevant cases.
- **4:** The model supports subtle diagnosis, competing explanations, edge cases, tradeoffs, and confident recognition of when more information is needed.

### Domain 4: Learning Architecture and Productive Processing — 12%

#### 4.1 `sequencing_scaffolding` — Sequencing and scaffolding

- **0:** Chapter order is arbitrary, prerequisites are violated, or information is dumped without a learning path.
- **1:** Major ideas arrive too early, too late, or without the preparation needed to use them.
- **2:** The order is serviceable, but some chapters are displaced, repetitive, or insufficiently cumulative.
- **3:** Chapters generally progress from problem to model to practice to complexity in a defensible sequence.
- **4:** The sequence is exemplary: each chapter prepares the next, complexity rises deliberately, and cumulative integration is continuous.

#### 4.2 `worked_examples_contrasts` — Worked examples and contrasts

- **0:** Examples are absent, misleading, or unrelated to the principle.
- **1:** Examples decorate claims, provide only obvious success cases, or fail to reveal reasoning.
- **2:** Examples clarify local ideas but offer limited contrast, variation, or diagnosis.
- **3:** Worked examples, counterexamples, and contrasts expose how the principle operates in most chapters.
- **4:** Examples are deliberately sequenced from straightforward to ambiguous; the reader sees reasoning, failure modes, competing choices, and transfer across contexts.

#### 4.3 `active_processing` — Active processing

- **0:** The reader only consumes information; tasks are absent or purely cosmetic.
- **1:** Activities mainly ask for agreement, recognition, or generic reflection without requiring construction of knowledge.
- **2:** Some prediction, explanation, retrieval, comparison, or application occurs, but it is inconsistent or chapter-local.
- **3:** Most chapters require meaningful retrieval, self-explanation, comparison, decision, or application.
- **4:** Productive processing is systematic and cumulative; readers repeatedly generate, discriminate, diagnose, apply, and revise understanding.

#### 4.4 `feedback_metacognitive_calibration` — Feedback and metacognitive calibration

- **0:** Incorrect understanding is reinforced, answer explanations are broken, or readers receive no way to detect misunderstanding.
- **1:** Feedback is limited to correctness labels or overly obvious answers that create false confidence.
- **2:** Explanations usually identify the intended answer, but confidence calibration, error diagnosis, or revision guidance is uneven.
- **3:** Feedback explains why alternatives fail, helps readers diagnose errors, and supports realistic self-monitoring in most chapters.
- **4:** The package produces excellent calibration: readers predict, commit, receive discriminating feedback, compare confidence with performance, and know what to revisit.

### Domain 5: Retention and Retrieval Support — 10%

#### 5.1 `meaningful_retrieval_cues` — Meaningful retrieval cues

- **0:** Few usable cues exist or labels are so generic that they do not support recall.
- **1:** Memorable phrases or acronyms exist but are arbitrary, excessive, or detached from mechanisms.
- **2:** Several useful cues exist, though some compete or depend heavily on recognition.
- **3:** Most major ideas have compact, meaningful cues linked to their function and context.
- **4:** Retrieval cues are exceptionally diagnostic, semantically meaningful, and integrated with the reader's mental model.

#### 5.2 `cumulative_reinforcement` — Cumulative reinforcement

- **0:** Earlier ideas disappear and later chapters do not require them.
- **1:** Repetition is mostly verbatim or isolated within chapters.
- **2:** Some ideas recur, but spacing and cumulative integration are inconsistent.
- **3:** Important ideas reappear across chapters in new applications and useful intervals.
- **4:** Reinforcement is deliberately spaced, cumulative, varied, and required for increasingly complex reasoning.

#### 5.3 `quiz_retrieval_depth` — Quiz and retrieval depth

- **0:** Assessments are wrong, answerable without reading, or test irrelevant details.
- **1:** Questions rely heavily on obvious distractors, wording cues, or recognition of terminology.
- **2:** Assessments cover core content but remain mostly chapter-local or recognition-based.
- **3:** Questions often require recall, discrimination, explanation, diagnosis, or application.
- **4:** Retrieval is effortful, cumulative, well-calibrated, and resistant to test-taking shortcuts; feedback strengthens the model.

#### 5.4 `interference_control_consolidation` — Interference control and consolidation

- **0:** Competing frameworks, acronyms, or near-duplicate ideas create severe confusion.
- **1:** Labels accumulate without comparison or consolidation.
- **2:** Summaries help, but some interference and framework competition remain.
- **3:** Related ideas are contrasted, consolidated, and located within a common map.
- **4:** The package actively prevents interference through integration, comparison, pruning, and cumulative synthesis.

### Domain 6: Purpose-Appropriate Transfer, Action, and Practical Judgment — 15%

#### 6.1 `concrete_actions` — Concrete actions

- **0:** Advice is abstract, unsafe, or impossible to operationalize.
- **1:** Recommendations remain slogans such as “communicate better” or “be disciplined.”
- **2:** Some usable steps, scripts, or exercises exist, but they are incomplete or inconsistently connected to diagnosis.
- **3:** The reader usually knows what to do, when to begin, and what a competent attempt looks like.
- **4:** Actions are precise, feasible, purpose-aligned, and connected to diagnosis, rehearsal, evidence, and meaningful outcomes.

#### 6.2 `cross_context_transfer` — Cross-context transfer

- **0:** Ideas only work in copied examples or one narrow context.
- **1:** The package assumes transfer without teaching readers how to recognize analogous situations.
- **2:** Multiple contexts are shown, but underlying transferable features are not always explicit.
- **3:** Readers are taught to recognize the principle across several relevant settings.
- **4:** The package explicitly teaches deep structure, near and far transfer, adaptation across roles, and diagnosis of superficially similar but different cases.

#### 6.3 `implementation_feedback_support` — Implementation and feedback support

- **0:** No implementation path, practice loop, or feedback mechanism exists.
- **1:** The package relies on motivation or one-time intention without anticipating barriers.
- **2:** Plans, prompts, or exercises exist, but follow-up, environmental support, measurement, or revision is uneven.
- **3:** Readers receive implementation cues, practice, feedback, and adjustment guidance in most important areas.
- **4:** The package supports a complete loop: cue, action, barrier handling, observation, feedback, reflection, and adaptation over time.

#### 6.4 `boundaries_adaptation_tradeoffs` — Boundaries, adaptation, and tradeoffs

- **0:** Techniques are taught mechanically and predictably fail or cause harm in common contexts.
- **1:** Exceptions, power differences, costs, and competing goals are largely ignored.
- **2:** Some boundaries and tradeoffs appear, but the reader still has to infer major adaptation rules.
- **3:** The package explains when to adapt, stop, escalate, choose another tool, or accept a tradeoff.
- **4:** Practical wisdom is central: readers learn to balance competing values, detect misuse, select among tools, and know when no simple technique is sufficient.

### Domain 7: Motivation, Autonomy, and Calibrated Agency — 8%

#### 7.1 `personal_relevance` — Personal relevance

- **0:** The material feels disconnected from the intended reader's goals or lived situations.
- **1:** Relevance is asserted generically rather than demonstrated.
- **2:** Several relatable situations exist, but applicability is narrow or uneven.
- **3:** The reader can regularly connect ideas to meaningful goals, choices, and problems.
- **4:** Relevance is exceptionally well aligned while preserving reader choice; the package helps readers identify their own reasons for learning.

#### 7.2 `achievable_progress` — Achievable progress

- **0:** The package produces helplessness, impossible demands, or no credible path to improvement.
- **1:** Change is framed as a large personality transformation or a long list of obligations.
- **2:** Some manageable actions exist, but sequencing, progress markers, or recovery from setbacks is limited.
- **3:** The package offers realistic starting points, small wins, and visible progress.
- **4:** Challenge is carefully calibrated; readers can build competence through staged practice, recover from failure, and see evidence of improvement.

#### 7.3 `autonomy_non_shaming_tone` — Autonomy and non-shaming tone

- **0:** Shame, coercion, fear, dehumanization, or manipulative urgency is central.
- **1:** The tone is moralizing, prescriptive, or dismissive of legitimate reader constraints.
- **2:** Generally respectful, but some pressure, simplistic responsibility framing, or narrow prescriptions remain.
- **3:** The package respects choice, context, dignity, and compassionate correction.
- **4:** Autonomy support is exemplary; readers are invited to choose, experiment, revise, and set boundaries without being blamed for structural or contextual limits.

#### 7.4 `calibrated_confidence` — Calibrated confidence

- **0:** The package creates dangerous certainty or confidence unrelated to demonstrated understanding.
- **1:** Encouragement substantially exceeds practice, feedback, or evidence of competence.
- **2:** Confidence is supported in broad terms, but readers receive limited help recognizing gaps.
- **3:** Confidence grows alongside practice, feedback, and accurate awareness of limitations.
- **4:** Agency is exceptionally well calibrated: readers understand what they can do, what they cannot yet do, when to seek help, and what evidence should change their confidence.

### Domain 8: Instructionally Aligned Engagement and Reading Momentum — 8%

#### 8.1 `curiosity_momentum` — Curiosity and momentum

- **0:** The material is inert, confusing, or gives little reason to continue.
- **1:** Interest appears sporadically, but pacing stalls or chapters feel obligatory.
- **2:** The package is generally readable, though hooks, transitions, or chapter endings are uneven.
- **3:** Curiosity, stakes, progression, and chapter transitions sustain attention across most of the book.
- **4:** Momentum is exceptional and cumulative; the desire to continue comes from increasingly meaningful questions and understanding, not artificial cliffhangers.

#### 8.2 `narrative_example_vividness` — Narrative and example vividness

- **0:** Examples are absent, incomprehensible, or misleading.
- **1:** Stories are generic, repetitive, implausibly staged, or emotionally manipulative.
- **2:** Many examples are clear, though formulaic construction, decorative details, or narrow contexts reduce authenticity.
- **3:** Examples are vivid, varied, credible, and tightly linked to the learning problem.
- **4:** Narrative evidence is exceptional: cases are memorable because they expose mechanisms, decisions, ambiguity, and consequence.

#### 8.3 `emotional_relevance` — Emotional relevance

- **0:** Emotion is absent where human stakes matter or is used in a harmful or manipulative way.
- **1:** Emotional stakes are generic, melodramatic, or disconnected from the principle.
- **2:** Relatable human stakes appear, but emotional range or authenticity is uneven.
- **3:** Emotion supports attention, perspective, and memory in most relevant chapters.
- **4:** Emotional relevance is nuanced, humane, and inseparable from deeper understanding without displacing reasoning.

#### 8.4 `instructional_alignment` — Instructional alignment and absence of decoration

- **0:** Spectacle, anecdotes, quotes, or sensory detail displace or contradict the lesson.
- **1:** A significant amount of attention is consumed by decorative specificity, repeated hooks, or content added to satisfy a template.
- **2:** Most engaging material helps, but some examples, timestamps, props, or stories do not earn their cognitive cost.
- **3:** Engagement devices usually clarify, motivate, or support transfer.
- **4:** Nearly every narrative and emotional device advances the mental model, retrieval, judgment, or transfer; decorative load is minimal.

### Domain 9: Whole-Book Coherence, Consistency, and Completion Value — 5%

#### 9.1 `chapter_necessity_order` — Chapter necessity and order

- **0:** Major chapters are misplaced, missing, or unrelated to the promised journey.
- **1:** Several chapters feel redundant, displaced, or insufficiently connected.
- **2:** Most chapters contribute, but the opening, middle, or order has material inefficiencies.
- **3:** Nearly every chapter has a clear role and appears in a defensible sequence.
- **4:** Every chapter earns its place and the order produces a cumulative experience that is hard to improve materially.

#### 9.2 `quality_consistency_pacing` — Quality consistency and pacing

- **0:** Quality is unstable or major sections fail.
- **1:** Strong chapters alternate with weak, repetitive, or inaccessible ones.
- **2:** The package is mostly serviceable, but noticeable pacing dips or quality variance remain.
- **3:** Quality and pacing remain strong across almost the entire book.
- **4:** The experience is consistently excellent while still varying rhythm and difficulty appropriately.

#### 9.3 `redundancy_cumulative_load` — Redundancy and cumulative load

- **0:** Repetition, framework accumulation, or component volume overwhelms the learning value.
- **1:** Repeated explanations and fixed template quantities create substantial bloat.
- **2:** Some repetition is useful, but compression or consolidation would materially improve the whole.
- **3:** Repetition is mostly strategic and cumulative load remains manageable.
- **4:** The package achieves exceptional compression and reinforcement; repetition changes context or deepens reasoning rather than merely restating.

#### 9.4 `synthesis_completion_value` — Synthesis and completion value

- **0:** The ending fails to integrate the book or leaves the promised journey unresolved.
- **1:** The conclusion mostly summarizes or ends abruptly without helping the reader consolidate and act.
- **2:** A useful synthesis exists, but major concepts or next steps remain disconnected.
- **3:** The ending integrates major ideas, clarifies next use, and creates a satisfying sense of completion.
- **4:** The conclusion produces an exceptional final model, retrieval opportunity, transfer plan, and realistic understanding of what remains to be learned.

## Hard gates

Assess gates before interpreting the weighted score. Always report a diagnostic weighted score when reliable enough to calculate, even if a gate fails. Never let the weighted average erase a gate consequence.

Use gate statuses `pass`, `conditional`, `fail`, `not_assessed`, and `unevaluable`.

### Gate 1: Technical completeness — `technical_completeness`

Pass when all required chapters and reader-facing components are accessible and no material duplication or corruption prevents evaluation.

- One minor inaccessible supplemental component that is not a chapter: `conditional`; score the otherwise complete book and lower confidence.
- Any source chapter inaccessible: `unevaluable`; do not fabricate or publish a weighted score. A structured coverage defect may be returned outside the canonical evaluation record.
- Document missing, duplicate, malformed, and inaccessible content separately from content quality.

### Gate 2: Epistemic and instructional safety — `epistemic_instructional_safety`

Pass when no serious internal contradiction, fabricated support, materially misleading teaching, or answer-key defect that could teach the wrong lesson remains unresolved.

- Minor local defects: `conditional`; score normally but require correction.
- Material repeated defects or a defect in a central principle: `fail` until corrected.
- A high weighted average cannot convert this gate to pass.

### Gate 3: Ethics and reader autonomy — `ethics_reader_autonomy`

Pass when the package does not promote harmful, coercive, dehumanizing, unsafe, or shame-based conduct and states necessary safety boundaries.

- A material failure is `fail` until corrected.
- Do not soften or average away ethical defects.

### Gate 4: Purpose and audience declaration — `purpose_audience_declaration`

Pass when the package can be judged against an identifiable reader, purpose, and prerequisite burden.

- If absent but inferable: `conditional`; record the inference and lower confidence.
- If impossible to infer: `unevaluable` for audience-dependent domains.

### Gate 5: External accuracy — `external_accuracy`

For the default isolated run, set `status` to `not_assessed` and state that external factual verification was intentionally out of scope. Do not convert internal plausibility into a claim of external truth.

### Certification status

- `pass`: gates 1–4 pass; gate 5 may be `not_assessed` when isolation is declared.
- `conditional`: no gate fails, but at least one of gates 1–4 is conditional.
- `fail`: gate 2 or 3 fails, or technical corruption makes reliable evaluation impossible.
- `unevaluable`: readable content or a defensible evaluation construct is insufficient.

A score from 90 through 100 may be labeled `reference-standard design` only when certification is `pass` and no core domain among Domains 1–6 is below 3.0/4.

## Score calculation

For a scoreable book:

```text
Subcriterion rating = 0, 1, 2, 3, or 4
Domain score = arithmetic mean of its four subcriterion ratings
Weighted domain points = (domain score / 4) × domain weight
Content Design Score = sum of all nine weighted domain points
```

Store full precision internally. Display adjudicated subcriteria to the nearest 0.5, domain scores and weighted points to two decimals, and the overall score to one decimal.

| Content Design Score | Classification |
|---:|---|
| 90–100 | Reference-standard design, subject to gate and core-domain rules |
| 80–89.9 | Strong design with identifiable improvements |
| 70–79.9 | Valuable but materially uneven; targeted redesign needed |
| 60–69.9 | Substantial redesign needed |
| Below 60 | Not ready as a ChapterFlow learning product |

Do not force a bell curve or predetermined winner. Do not inflate scores to avoid criticism. Treat books within 1.0 overall point as effectively tied unless qualitative evidence supports a meaningful distinction.

## Evidence requirements

For every domain, record at least two chapter-level strengths, at least one chapter-level limitation, at least one whole-book pattern, a concise rationale tied to the anchor, and precise chapter/section/item locators where available.

For every chapter, record its index/id/title and read status; central ideas; mental-model contribution; engagement and pacing; comprehension/learning support; retention/retrieval support; transfer/action support; trust, QA, or safety issues; and concise paraphrased evidence with local locators.

Apply these standards:

- Paraphrase rather than quote at length.
- Explain the observable basis for praise or criticism; avoid unsupported labels such as “well written.”
- Explain both high and low ratings.
- Distinguish personal preference from observable design.
- Inspect actual component quality. A field named `quiz`, `exercise`, or `implementation plan` earns nothing by existing.
- Component quantity alone earns no score.
- Do not credit the same vivid example in multiple domains without separate evidence that it performs each distinct function.
- In inspection and failure logs, record whether a chapter was `full`, `partial`, or `inaccessible`. A canonical evaluation record may contain only exact source-inventory chapters marked `full`; any other status blocks final scoring.
- Treat a structurally valid but semantically wrong answer key or explanation as a reader-facing epistemic defect.
