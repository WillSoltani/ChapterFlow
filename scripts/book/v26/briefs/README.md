# Book briefs

A book brief is the short, book-specific section that `prompts/write.md` splices in as `@@BOOK_SECTION@@`. The generic lesson-first rules (one lesson, opener → problem → story as evidence → lesson, plain words, modern examples and quiz) live in `prompts/write.md`. Keep a book section to 4-6 lines. Do not add a rule to fix a single chapter: fix that chapter through the pipeline's fix call.

What changes by book type:

| Type | Where the lesson comes from | What the "story" is | Quoting |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Memoir** (e.g. Franklin) | What the chapter's strongest episodes show; the author's own stated reflection wins | The author's episodes, told plainly and in order, with no invented motives | Only short lines that carry the lesson; explain old words |
| **How-to** (e.g. Bennett) | The author's own point for the chapter | The author's argument and worked cases | Sparing; the author's wording is often the hardest part |
| **Argument** | The author's claim for the chapter, with its strongest reason | The author's evidence and cases | Sparing; never quote a claim out of its context |

Examples and quiz questions are modern and invented for every type (owner decision R1-b = A). Only the book's own story and the lesson are fact-checked against the source.
