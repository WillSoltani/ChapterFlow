
# Grade Bands

Easy:
- target grade 8 to 9
- short sentences
- concrete nouns and actions
- one idea at a time
- no stacked abstractions
- no dense framework phrasing unless grounded immediately in an example

Medium:
- target grade 10 to 11
- may use mechanism language if explained plainly
- can hold 2 linked ideas in one paragraph
- should still prefer concrete examples over abstract summary

Hard:
- target grade 12
- may hold multiple ideas in tension
- must add a real boundary condition, contradiction, or synthesis move
- density is allowed only if every sentence earns new substance

Cross-band rules:
- harder does not mean wordier by default
- a later band must unlock a new layer, not just intensify the same line
- if easy sounds like compressed medium, rewrite easy
