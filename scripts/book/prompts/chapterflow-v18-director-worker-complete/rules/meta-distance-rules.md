
# Meta-Distance Rules

Teach the idea, not the chapter.

Zero-tolerance zones:
- keyTakeaways
- moreDetails
- prompts
- review cards
- quiz explanations
- scenarios
- implementation plans

Do not write:
- "the chapter says"
- "this chapter teaches"
- "Chapter N argues"
- "the chapter closes by"
- "the author wants to show"

Breakdowns may use the author name sparingly for source attribution, but only when it helps orientation. Prefer the idea in direct form.
