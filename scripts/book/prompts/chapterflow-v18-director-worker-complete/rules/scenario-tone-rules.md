
# Scenario Tone Rules

Scenario, whatToDo, and whyItMatters are all required to be tone objects.

Scenario tones must differ in:
- framing
- sentence rhythm
- stakes language
- emotional temperature

Minimum scenario bar:
- 3 concrete details
- 1 sensory or emotional cue
- one live human tension
- no generic "principle delivery vehicle" scenes
- at least one messy or imperfect outcome somewhere in the chapter
