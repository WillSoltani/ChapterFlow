# Hard-depth rules

Hard depth must earn its difficulty.

## Required
- at least one boundary condition, failure mode, unresolved tension, or threshold question
- at least one takeaway that adds a real limit or reversal
- at least one recap or prompt that forces synthesis or evaluation

## Forbidden
- medium with extra words
- harder vocabulary without a harder idea
- abstract prestige language used to simulate depth

## Preservation rule
If the hardest edge appears in the hard breakdown, it must still appear in hard takeaways or prompts.
