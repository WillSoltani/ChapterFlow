# Meta-distance rules

Teach the idea, not the chapter.

## Hard rule
Do not write:
- the chapter says
- Chapter 1 teaches
- this chapter argues
- the chapter closes by
- the chapter's lesson is

## Allowed attribution
- in breakdowns only, sparse author attribution is allowed where it clarifies source ownership
- use the author name sparingly, not as a crutch

## Reader-facing surfaces with zero tolerance for chapter-talk
- key takeaways
- moreDetails
- activation prompts
- self-check prompts
- prediction prompts
- one-minute recap
- examples
- quiz explanations
- implementation plan
- review cards
- key takeaway card
