# Readability and flow rules

## Core standard
Readable does not mean simplistic. Dense does not mean smart.

## Checks
- no sentence should exist only to sound polished
- prune repeated thesis restatement
- prefer one sharp sentence over two cloudy ones
- previews should prompt a prediction, not summarize the next chapter
- readingTimeMinutes must be computed from final word count, not guessed

## Easy-depth protection
- easy should orient and clarify
- easy should not carry the same abstraction load as medium

## Hard-depth protection
- hard may get denser only when the reader is paid back with a stronger distinction, limit, or threshold
