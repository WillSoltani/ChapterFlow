# Grade-band calibration

The reader should be able to choose a depth that matches comfortable reading difficulty.

## Easy
- target comfort band: grade 8 to 9
- short sentences dominate
- concrete nouns and active verbs
- minimal abstraction
- no stacked clauses unless the meaning would become inaccurate without them
- define or replace technical terms immediately
- use one main idea at a time

## Medium
- target comfort band: grade 10 to 11
- can introduce mechanism language, but explain it in plain words
- may hold two related ideas in tension
- use analytical vocabulary only when it adds precision
- examples should remain vivid and grounded

## Hard
- target comfort band: grade 12
- may hold multiple conditions, tradeoffs, or boundary questions together
- can use more abstract nouns, but each paragraph still needs concrete traction
- harder does not mean denser for its own sake
- if a sentence gets harder, it must also get more valuable

## Shared rules
- easy is not allowed to sound like compressed medium
- medium is not allowed to sound like watered-down hard
- hard must add at least one real tension, limit, failure mode, or threshold question
