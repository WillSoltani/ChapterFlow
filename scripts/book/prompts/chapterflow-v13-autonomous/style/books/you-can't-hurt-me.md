Can't Hurt Me book contract

This file is mandatory when `bookId: you-can't-hurt-me`.

Core requirement:
- keep the teaching paid for by events, body cost, humiliation, service burden, correction, repair, or reconciliation
- do not let toughness turn into poster language
- do not let memoir pressure dissolve into generic mindset prose

Chapter-family controls:
- Chapters 1 to 3:
  - abuse, racism, grief, poverty, obesity, academic failure, and self-deception stay concrete
  - identity rebuilding must feel paid for, not inspirational by default
  - accountability must face real damage, not erase it
- Chapters 4 to 6:
  - suffering, service, and the calloused-mind material must preserve bodily cost and anti-glamour pressure
  - private ferocity must remain tied to standards, team burden, or duty
- Chapters 7 to 8:
  - the 40% rule and strategy chapters must stay interpretive, not pseudo-clinical
  - no brute-force mythology
  - capacity gains must stay tied to planning, pacing, logistics, or judgment
- Chapters 9 to 10:
  - elite standards must preserve ethical burden and anti-legend rhetoric
  - discipline should stabilize the group or the standard, not decorate the self
  - failure must become redesign, not mythology
- Chapter 11:
  - recovery, disease, stillness, stretching, retirement, and reconciliation must stay visible
  - the ending must keep edge without conquest pose, transcendence pose, or sentimental closure

Identity-language budget:
- terms like identity, standards, ego, pain, edge, range, legend, machine, greatness, and destiny may appear only when tied to chapter-specific event pressure
- if those terms could be removed without losing factual content, remove them

Competitive tone rules for this book:
- keep it short, severe, and credible
- no hard-truth slogan leads
- no macho filler
- no legend talk
- no machine talk unless the chapter is explicitly correcting that frame
- if the line sounds like it belongs on a motivational poster, cut it

Reinforcement-layer rules for this book:
- key takeaway card: one earned memory line, not a slogan
- review cards: retrieval hooks tied to the chapter's real pressure, not floating toughness language
- one-minute recap: compresses and tests; does not replay the chapter's emotional beat
- implementation plan: practical and chapter-specific, never generic self-discipline coaching
- reflection prompts: must feel earned by the chapter's cost structure

Quick rejection tests:
- if the sentence could fit another toughness memoir with only a noun swap, reject it
- if the line sounds more impressed with Goggins than with the actual pressure, reject it
- if the line teaches the concept without the cost that produced it, rewrite it
