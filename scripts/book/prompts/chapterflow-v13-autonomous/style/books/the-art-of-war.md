The Art of War book contract

This file is mandatory when `bookId: the-art-of-war`.

Core requirement:
- preserve Sun Tzu's strategic logic, classification systems, and consequence reasoning
- keep the prose disciplined, readable, and grounded in observable action
- do not let analytical strength drift into dissertation-like density
- do not let hard variants become commentary about the framework instead of teaching through it
- the book should read like a finished classical adaptation, not brilliant analytical notes

Voice target:
- strategic
- disciplined
- classical without being archaic
- sober and consequence-aware
- readable on first pass at every depth
- precise without being ponderous
- grounded in observation, conflict, leverage, and decision

Do not drift into:
- dissertation prose
- framework-about-framework meta-analysis
- theory-heavy reinforcement layers
- abstract clause stacking
- overbuilt system-analysis expansions
- generic self-help, motivational, or corporate tone
- casual or chatty register
- modern-corporate leadership language

Density control (CRITICAL for this book):
- maximum paragraph length: 120 words in breakdowns
- no paragraph may carry more than two major concepts
- no paragraph may contain more than one significant caveat or qualification
- no stacked abstract clauses beyond three in a single sentence
- ground abstract claims in concrete consequence before moving to the next abstraction
- prefer one strong insight per paragraph over layered overcompression
- if a paragraph exceeds 120 words, split it at the natural thought boundary
- wall-of-text single paragraphs are an auto-fail for any breakdown

Variant control:
- easy: one clean model from the chapter, one usable implication, lean and concrete
- medium: mechanism plus application, still readable on first pass, no paraphrase padding
- hard: deeper chapter insight with limits, boundaries, and synthesis — but still teaching
- hard must deepen the chapter, not orbit around it with meta-commentary
- hard may include framework limits only when directly central to the chapter's argument
- hard must NOT become "what this framework implies about frameworks"
- medium and hard must feel separately authored, not expanded from the same seed

Hard-variant guardrail (CRITICAL for this book):
- block expansion into meta-framework analysis unless the chapter itself raises the issue
- block speculative adversarial extensions unless directly required by the chapter logic
- block "structural gap" analysis unless it is truly essential and directly sourced
- block "the competence the chapter assumes without providing" style meta-commentary
- block "confidence trap" style analysis-of-analysis
- block "the argument has a live tension it does not resolve" unless Sun Tzu explicitly raises the tension
- prioritize deeper teaching of what the chapter says over system-level analysis of what it implies
- hard should feel like a sharper, more layered reading of the same chapter — not a different genre
- the reader should finish hard feeling they understood the chapter more deeply, not that they read a dissertation about it

Reinforcement-layer rules:
- keyTakeawayCard: maximum 80 words per tone; one distilled thesis, not a compressed essay
- review cards: crisp retrieval hooks; backs should be 20-50 words, not mini-paragraphs
- oneMinuteRecap: genuinely brief; retrieve asks one focused recall question, not compound lists
- retrieve must NOT say "reconstruct the five takeaways" or stack multiple recall demands
- connect ties one mechanism to one consequence; one sentence or two at most
- preview is one sentence pointing forward, not explaining the next chapter
- implementationPlan: concrete, human-scaled, actionable; not a doctrinal checklist
- coreSkill: one sentence naming the practical skill
- 24-hour challenge: specific and bounded, not a strategic planning exercise
- moreDetails: deepen selectively; add mechanism, limit, or failure mode — do not double the density
- prompts: invite reflection, not simulate exams; keep them short and thought-provoking
- activationPrompt: one clear reflective question or exercise, not a compound instruction set
- selfCheckPrompt: one focused self-assessment, not a multi-step audit

Competitive tone rules for this book:
- severity is allowed; escalation is not
- keep competitive lines sharp, short, and grounded
- no stacked heavy abstractions
- no analytical combat prose
- no substrate/signal/framework/operational language pileups
- competitive should sound like a strategist who speaks plainly about consequences
- if a competitive line sounds more impressed with its own analytical weight than with the chapter's point, cut it
- competitive must not escalate every paragraph into maximum analytical force

Gentle tone rules for this book:
- accessible without sounding soft or patronizing
- maintain the book's strategic register even at the easiest level
- use narrative framing ("a general walking into a temple") over abstract setup
- keep sentences shorter and concepts singular
- do not compress the chapter's logic into miniaturized theory-speak

Direct tone rules for this book:
- clear, exact, no-filler
- prefer concrete nouns over abstract ones where possible
- keep sentence rhythm varied: short-long-short, not uniform density
- avoid monotone analytical cadence

Abstraction control:
- every important abstract claim must be grounded in a concrete consequence, example, or observable difference within the same paragraph or the one immediately following
- do not stack three abstractions without a concrete anchor
- reinforcement layers must keep a visible practical anchor
- if a sentence replaces a concrete military/strategic observation with pure framework language, rewrite it

Ending control:
- every paragraph lands once
- every support surface lands once
- no extra sentence after the real ending
- no second-landing restatement dressed as synthesis
- do not close a paragraph with a sloganized abstraction of what the paragraph already said

Prose quality for this book:
- prefer varied sentence lengths over uniform long sentences
- break long analytical sentences into two shorter ones when clarity improves
- trim transitional phrases that add structure but not meaning
- remove "the result is" / "the implication is" / "the consequence is" openers unless needed
- do not over-explain what a classification means after defining it
- trust the reader to carry one idea across two paragraphs without being reminded

Meta-distance rules for this book:
- support surfaces should rarely say "the chapter" or "Sun Tzu argues"
- teach the strategic idea directly
- if a recap or card can function without referencing the text as an artifact, write it that way
- attribution to Sun Tzu in breakdowns is allowed sparingly but should not dominate

Quick rejection tests:
- if the paragraph could appear in a strategy textbook's literature review, it is too academic — rewrite
- if the hard variant reads like analysis of the chapter rather than teaching from the chapter, cut back
- if a key takeaway card exceeds 80 words, compress it
- if a review card back exceeds 50 words, tighten it
- if a single paragraph exceeds 120 words, split it
- if a breakdown has only 1 paragraph, it is a wall-of-text — break it into 4-6 paragraphs
- if a sentence has more than three nested clauses, simplify it
- if the recap retrieve asks the reader to "reconstruct" or "name all five," simplify to one focused question
