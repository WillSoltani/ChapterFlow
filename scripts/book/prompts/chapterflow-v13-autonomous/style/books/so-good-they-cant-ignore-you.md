So Good They Can't Ignore You book contract

This file is mandatory when `bookId: so-good-they-cant-ignore-you`.

Core requirement:
- preserve Newport's skeptical, mechanism-first career analysis
- keep the prose calm, sharp, and unsentimental
- keep the chapter logic rooted in leverage, sequencing, evidence, and misreading
- do not let the book drift into generic self-help encouragement

Voice target:
- analytical
- concrete
- evidence-aware
- diagnostic
- restrained
- useful without sounding motivational

Keep visible:
- the anti-passion-myth diagnosis
- career capital as exchange logic
- control as leverage plus timing plus support
- mission as later-stage, built, and evidence-bound
- the distinction between desire, leverage, and consequence
- the question of how people misread work and judge it badly

Do not drift into:
- motivational uplift
- startup jargon
- therapy language
- vague productivity sermonizing
- slogan stacks
- chest-thumping competitive copy
- “follow your passion” paraphrase even as a straw man

Variant control:
- easy gives one clean, concrete model with one usable implication
- medium adds mechanism, consequence, and practical interpretation
- hard adds boundary, sequencing error, cost, misreading, or hidden structure
- hard must deepen the diagnosis, not broaden the wording
- medium and hard must feel separately authored
- competitive must compress the diagnosis and sharpen the consequence; it must not merely sound louder

Support-layer rules:
- implementation plans must sound like chapter-specific judgment, not generic coaching
- if/then plans must be clean single-condition actions with no doubled conditional scaffolding
- review cards must teach mechanism, misreading, limit, or consequence with chapter-earned nouns
- review card fronts must not default to “state the mechanism” or “which mistaken belief”
- review card backs must not sound like reusable summary templates
- one-minute recap must compress, retrieve, or bridge; it must not replay the breakdown
- key takeaway card must be the final distilled line, not a body paragraph cut-down
- support surfaces should be at least 90 percent as polished as the breakdown

Duplication control:
- no repeated paragraph job inside a breakdown
- no paragraph cluster copied across tones with only light synonym changes
- no medium anchor paragraph may reappear in hard without a meaningfully deeper rewrite
- support surfaces must not lift body sentences directly unless the line is transformed and shortened
- if the same sentence family appears in breakdown, recap, card, and implementation plan, rewrite the support surfaces

Competitive rules for this book:
- shorter is usually better
- sharper means cleaner diagnosis, not more aggression
- use consequence, limit, and exposure language instead of flex language
- no macho cadence
- no “winner,” “dominate,” “crush,” or startup-pressure posturing unless directly quoted from a source, which is unlikely here
- if competitive sounds impressed with itself, cut it

Hard-depth rules for this book:
- add diagnostic precision rather than commentary about depth
- surface hidden assumptions, threshold conditions, tradeoffs, or failure modes
- preserve readability
- do not restate medium's thesis and then add filler
- if hard cannot name the mistake a reader is likely to make, it is not hard enough

Meta-distance rules:
- support surfaces should rarely say `the chapter`, `chapter 7`, or `the book`
- teach the mechanism directly
- do not narrate Newport's move from outside the argument when you can state the argument itself

Quick rejection tests:
- if the line could fit in a generic career book, reject it
- if the line sounds like advice without diagnosis, reject it
- if the competitive line gets louder without getting narrower, reject it
- if the hard line gets longer without getting more exact, reject it
- if the support layer reads like a weaker paraphrase of the body, reject it
