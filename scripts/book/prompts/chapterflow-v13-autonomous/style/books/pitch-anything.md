Pitch Anything book supplement

Apply these rules whenever the brief or manifest identifies `bookId: pitch-anything`.

Voice target:
- tactically sharp
- strategically literate
- concise
- controlled
- high-status calm
- direct without macho theater

Keep visible:
- sequence pressure inside the pitch
- status management
- frame control
- what changes in the room
- why the move works and where it fails

Do not drift into:
- generic self-help
- motivational uplift
- startup-summary sludge
- fake-alpha theater
- chest-thumping cadence
- reusable room-command slogans

Pitch Anything conversion rules:
- easy gives the clean usable move, not an abstract summary
- medium explains mechanism, pacing, and room dynamics
- hard explains architecture, hidden tension, failure modes, and constraints
- medium and hard must feel separately authored, not expanded from the same stems
- every reinforcement layer must compress or deepen; none may paraphrase another layer

Forbidden boilerplate:
- use it as a practical rule
- make it hold in the room
- that is the pressure-tested edge
- that is the practical rule
- state the mechanism clearly
- use this as the practical mechanism behind the takeaway, not as a slogan

Reinforcement rules:
- key takeaways must sound distilled, not padded
- moreDetails must add one of: mechanism, limit, failure mode, operational implication
- review card fronts must vary by function: definition, contrast, diagnosis, sequence, failure trigger
- review card backs must answer crisply and stop
- recap retrieve must ask for recall
- recap connect must link one live mechanism to one practical consequence
- recap preview must predict what changes in the next live pitch, not narrate the next chapter

Read-aloud test:
- if a sentence sounds like a reusable sales-content template, cut it
- if the same landing could sit in takeaways, cards, and recap unchanged, cut it
- if hard mainly sounds like medium with extra language, cut it
