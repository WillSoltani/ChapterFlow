The One Thing book contract

This file is mandatory when `bookId: the-one-thing`.

Core requirement:
- preserve the book's leverage logic, consequence logic, and operational practicality
- keep the prose sharp, grounded, and unsentimental
- do not let a clean idea drift into generic productivity sermonizing
- do not turn pressure into macho slogan language

Voice target:
- clear
- consequential
- practical
- high-signal
- disciplined
- readable without feeling simplified

Keep visible:
- leverage over volume
- sequence over busyness
- tradeoff over fairness theater
- downstream consequence over motivational uplift
- what changes next in work, school, or personal execution

Do not drift into:
- generic productivity advice
- vague focus talk
- abstract insight language
- reusable coaching copy
- slogan stacks
- self-conscious hardness

Variant control:
- easy gives the shortest clear usable model
- medium explains mechanism, implication, and failure pressure
- hard explains hidden structure, limits, tradeoffs, and synthesis
- hard must deepen the architecture, not broaden the wording
- medium and hard must feel separately authored

Reinforcement-layer rules:
- key takeaway card: one distilled earned line, not an amplified paraphrase
- review cards: retrieval hooks with chapter-earned nouns, not floating abstractions
- one-minute recap: compresses, tests recall, and points to consequence; it does not re-explain
- moreDetails: adds mechanism, limit, failure mode, or operational implication
- implementation plan: concrete and chapter-shaped, never generic consistency coaching
- prompts: specific enough that they could not be swapped into another chapter unchanged

Competitive tone rules for this book:
- force is allowed; verbal flex is not
- keep competitive lines short, clean, and disciplined
- no stacked punch lines
- no chest-thumping cadence
- no repeated combat or arena metaphor clusters unless the chapter itself clearly earns them
- if a line sounds impressed with its own toughness, cut it

Abstraction control:
- if a sentence could replace a concrete lever with words like aim, field, move, winner, path, or shadow and lose nothing, rewrite it
- reinforcement layers must keep a visible practical anchor such as task choice, calendar control, sequencing, question quality, time protection, or tradeoff
- every important claim should imply a downstream consequence, not just a general insight

Ending control:
- every paragraph lands once
- every support surface lands once
- no extra sentence after the real ending
- do not restate the point with a second sloganized tail

Meta-distance rules for this book:
- support surfaces should almost never say `the chapter`, `chapter 14`, `the book`, or narrate Keller's move
- teach the mechanism directly
- if a recap or card can ask for recall without naming the chapter artifact, rewrite it

Quick rejection tests:
- if the sentence sounds like generic productivity content, reject it
- if the sentence sounds like a generated learning object instead of authored help, reject it
- if the same line could sit in the takeaway, recap, review card, and prompt unchanged, reject it
- if the line is clean but not memorable, compress it again
