Antifragile book contract

This file is mandatory when `bookId: antifragile`.

Core requirement:
- preserve Taleb's payoff-aware, suspicious, asymmetry-first logic
- keep the prose severe, concrete, and structurally disciplined
- preserve chapter fidelity without turning the book into generic self-help
- treat metadata, support layers, and package hygiene as production-critical rather than decorative

Voice target:
- skeptical
- payoff-sensitive
- consequence-aware
- narrow rather than preachy
- sharp without becoming cartoonishly aggressive
- concrete about exposure, downside, survival, optionality, and intervention risk

Keep visible:
- downside before upside
- survival before comfort
- exposure before prestige
- optionality before prediction
- convexity / asymmetry / tail logic where the chapter actually calls for it
- intervention risk, opacity, nonlinearity, and hidden structural fragility where relevant
- chapter-to-chapter handoffs that reflect the actual conceptual sequence of the book

Do not drift into:
- generic self-help
- motivational uplift
- startup cliché language
- therapy language
- pop-stoic sloganizing
- vague resilience sermons
- "take more risk" simplifications
- anti-math theater
- anti-expert caricature
- soft educational summary voice

Metadata and package hygiene:
- title must normalize to `Antifragile: Things That Gain from Disorder`
- author must normalize to `Nassim Nicholas Taleb`
- no curly quotes or decorative punctuation in metadata
- edition, provenance, and chapterRange are not optional when the frozen source bundle supports them
- chapter review wrappers must carry the same clean book object as the release package, adjusted only for package scope
- strong chapter body plus dirty wrapper is a failed package, not a pass

Variant control:
- easy stays readable, but it still has to sound like Taleb rather than a school summary
- medium adds mechanism, consequence, and a practical reading of the asymmetry
- hard adds boundary, threshold, hidden structure, or payoff-shape precision
- hard must deepen the chapter, not inflate it with abstract commentary
- competitive should become narrower, cleaner, and more severe, not merely louder

Boundary control:
- each chapter must preserve its proper limit somewhere in the body or support layer
- critiques of averages must not become anti-arithmetic
- critiques of theory must not become anti-theory
- critiques of institutions or professional codes must not become anti-institution caricature
- critiques of intervention must not become total passivity
- asymmetry must not become cowardice
- exposure must not become anti-expertise theater

Bridge control:
- endings must hand off to the actual next concept in Antifragile
- no generic `next chapter` filler
- recap.preview and any bridge review card should prepare the next move in the book's logic, not narrate the table of contents
- the handoff should feel structural: exposure to asymmetry, asymmetry to barbell, nonlinearity to intervention, ethics back into final synthesis

Support-layer rules:
- review cards must sound authored, not templated
- implementation plans must name the real mechanism, not generic improvement advice
- keyTakeawayCard must sound final and earned, not like a trimmed paragraph
- recap surfaces must compress, retrieve, connect, or hand off; they must not replay the breakdown
- support layers should stay within Taleb's tonal register, not soften into generic educational framing
- package completeness is mandatory: examples, quiz, reviewCards, implementationPlan, keyTakeawayCard, and all required contentVariants must be fully present

Quick rejection tests:
- if the line could fit in a generic resilience book, reject it
- if the line sounds like life advice detached from payoff shape, reject it
- if the line criticizes theory, arithmetic, or institutions in an absolute way, reject it
- if the bridge could fit almost any book, reject it
- if the chapter body sounds strong but the wrapper metadata looks dirty, reject the package
- if the support layer sounds safer or smoother than the body, rewrite it until the bite comes back
