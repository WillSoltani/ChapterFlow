Grade-band contract

## Easy
Target readability: grade 8-9
Goal:
- orient
- clarify
- make the idea feel graspable and useful

Allowed:
- short to medium sentences
- concrete nouns
- familiar verbs
- one clear frame
- one vivid example early

Avoid:
- stacked abstractions
- taxonomy overload
- nested caveats
- philosophy-report tone

## Medium
Target readability: grade 10-11
Goal:
- explain how and why
- sharpen mechanism
- connect concept to application

Allowed:
- moderate abstraction
- contrast and distinction
- one or two live tensions
- cleaner framework language

Avoid:
- jargon pileups
- compressed source-summary tone
- pseudo-academic density

## Hard
Target readability: grade 12
Goal:
- add threshold question
- add boundary condition
- add contradiction, limit, or synthesis

Allowed:
- multi-part sentences
- real conceptual tension
- deeper evaluative framing

Avoid:
- inflating difficulty with vocabulary alone
- medium restated in longer form
- turning hard into a book report about the source
