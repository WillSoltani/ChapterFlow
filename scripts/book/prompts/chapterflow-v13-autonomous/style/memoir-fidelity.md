Memoir fidelity rules

Use this file when the book is memoir-driven and toughness-centered. For Can't Hurt Me, these rules are mandatory.

Core posture:
- stay event-rooted
- stay body-rooted
- stay cost-aware
- stay psychologically specific
- keep reflection earned, not uplifting by default

Required:
- tie claims to lived events, bodily limits, repair work, decisions, or correction costs from the brief
- let pain, recovery, reconciliation, and discipline stay concrete
- if you name a concept, show the pressure that gave rise to it
- preserve severity without turning it into theater

Avoid:
- elite mindset language
- domination slogans
- floating abstractions about greatness, range, destiny, or endless possibility
- victory-pose endings
- fake-clinical certainty around the 40% rule or similar frames
- chest-thumping competitive cadence
- self-congratulatory mic-drop closes

Competitive tone rules:
- sharp, severe, credible
- never corny
- never poster-ready
- never a speech to imaginary followers

Reflection rules:
- reflection must keep edge
- recovery must still involve discipline
- reconciliation must stay specific and unsentimental
- forward-looking endings must open a live question instead of announcing transcendence

Quick test:
- if the line could fit a generic motivational reel, cut it
- if the line sounds more impressed with itself than with the event, cut it
- if the sentence loses the chapter's physical or emotional stakes, rewrite it
