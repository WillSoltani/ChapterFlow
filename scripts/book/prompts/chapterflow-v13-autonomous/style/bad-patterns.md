# Anti-Benchmark: Reject These Patterns

Reject writing that:
- could fit almost any chapter in the book
- repeats the same idea at easy, medium, and hard with only extra words
- repeats the same sentence ending or paragraph landing more than once
- uses fake-deep mechanism language to sound smarter than the brief supports
- swaps adjectives to simulate tone variation
- uses abstract workplace or relationship scenarios that could belong anywhere
- turns implementation advice into generic life coaching
- relies on sentence skeletons like:
  - "X is not Y. It is Z."
  - "The real issue is..."
  - "What matters is..."
  - "This changes everything."
  - "The difference is..."
- manufactures authority with neuroscience, hormones, faux-evolution, or timing trivia

Reject final content that:
- contains brief or outline instructions
- contains validator or calibration notes
- contains internal field labels turned into prose
- copies raw source prose into the breakdown without explicit quote support
- collapses all three tones into the same sentence
- creates pseudo-drafts headed by "## Easy / ## Medium / ## Hard"

Auto-Reject Signals
- opening paragraph could be pasted into another chapter unchanged
- two paragraphs do the same job
- hard depth is just longer medium depth
- examples feel polished but interchangeable
- implementation plan could apply to any chapter in the book
- quiz distractors are too weak to be tempting
- the chapter sounds like a clever content machine instead of a good writer
- later chapters suddenly become more templated than approved early chapters
- memoir chapters drift into floating uplift once concrete anchors thin out
- recap, cards, prompts, and implementation surfaces all sound like cleaned-up versions of the same sentence
- competitive lines lead with `The hard truth is` or similar ready-made severity
- identity, standards, pain, edge, or ego language carries no event pressure

Quick Test
If you can imagine swapping the chapter title and keeping 70 percent of the prose, reject it.
If a sentence sounds like a note to the writer or a seed field, reject it.
If hard could be made by expanding medium, reject it.
If the line sounds like it belongs on a motivational poster, reject it.
