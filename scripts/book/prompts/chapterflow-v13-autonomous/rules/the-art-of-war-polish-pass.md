The Art of War polish pass

Run this pass whenever `bookId: the-art-of-war`.

Purpose:
- remove density overload without flattening the book's strategic spine
- preserve chapter fidelity, classification precision, and consequence reasoning
- force reinforcement layers to sound authored and crisp, not like compressed analytical notes
- make hard variants teach more deeply, not more abstractly

Paragraph control:
- split any paragraph exceeding 120 words at the natural thought boundary
- split any single-paragraph breakdown into 4-6 paragraphs
- ensure each paragraph carries at most two major concepts
- ensure each paragraph contains at most one significant caveat

Hard-variant cleanup:
- identify sentences that analyze the framework rather than teach the chapter
- if the sentence says "the competence the chapter assumes without providing" or similar meta-commentary, rewrite to teach the skill directly
- if the sentence says "the sequential structure has a confidence trap" or similar analysis-of-analysis, convert to a concrete observation about what goes wrong
- if a paragraph speculates about what happens when both sides apply the framework, keep only if Sun Tzu explicitly raises the adversarial mirror — otherwise cut
- replace "framework gap" language with direct teaching of what the reader needs to know
- replace "the argument has an unresolved tension" with a direct statement of the tension itself

Reinforcement-layer cleanup:
- compress keyTakeawayCard to 80 words or fewer per tone
- compress review card backs to 50 words or fewer
- simplify recap retrieve to one focused recall question (not compound lists)
- simplify recap connect to one mechanism-to-consequence bridge
- shorten preview to one sentence
- make activationPrompt a single reflective question or bounded exercise
- make implementation plan coreSkill a single sentence
- make 24-hour challenge specific and completable, not a strategic planning exercise

Prose cleanup:
- shorten sentences with more than three nested clauses
- break monotone analytical cadence with varied sentence lengths
- trim transitional phrases that add structure but not meaning ("the result is," "the implication is")
- remove duplicate landings: if the paragraph's point has landed, do not restate it
- remove over-explanation of classifications after they have been defined
- replace generated-sounding phrasing with cleaner prose

Competitive tone cleanup:
- cut stacked heavy abstractions
- cut analytical combat prose
- cut substrate/signal/framework/operational language pileups
- keep competitive lines sharp and grounded, not escalating

Gentle tone cleanup:
- ensure accessibility without patronizing
- maintain strategic register
- prefer narrative setup over abstract setup

Final read-aloud test:
- if the line sounds like a PhD thesis on strategic theory, simplify it
- if the line sounds like a literature review of Sun Tzu, rewrite it as teaching
- if the line could be one sentence shorter without losing meaning, cut it there
- if the paragraph feels like it requires rereading, it needs a split or a simplification
- if a reinforcement surface feels heavy, compress until it feels crisp
