The One Thing polish pass

Run this pass whenever `bookId: the-one-thing`.

Purpose:
- remove recurring editorial drag without flattening the book's strength
- preserve leverage, consequence, and practical transfer
- force reinforcement layers to sound authored instead of assembled

Polish checks:
- cut any repeated phrase family inside the same paragraph or support surface
- cut any closing sentence that only repeats the paragraph's finished idea
- cut any support-surface line that narrates `the chapter` or `the book`
- replace abstract reinforcement language with chapter-earned practical language
- replace generic recap retrieval formulas with cleaner recall prompts
- cut repeated combat, arena, blade, winner, field, or shadow metaphor stacking unless the chapter truly needs it
- make review-card backs crisp answers, not paraphrase residue
- make key takeaway cards feel distilled, not maximized
- make moreDetails deepen the claim instead of explaining it again
- make hard visibly deeper than medium in structure, not just extension

Final read-aloud test:
- if the line sounds polished but machine-authored, rewrite it
- if the line sounds forceful but slightly performative, shorten it
- if the line could be one sentence shorter and stronger, cut it there
