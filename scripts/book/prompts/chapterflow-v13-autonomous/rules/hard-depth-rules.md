Hard depth rules

Hard must add at least two of:
- genuine boundary condition
- failure mode
- threshold question
- contradiction
- hidden structure
- cost
- unresolved tension
- synthesis across concepts

Hard fails if:
- it is medium with more words
- it repeats medium's central phrasing or closing beat
- it reuses medium takeaway stems, `moreDetails` functions, or recap logic without new structure
- the sharpest tension from the brief disappears
- it turns into a book report about the source
- it inflates vocabulary instead of deepening thought
