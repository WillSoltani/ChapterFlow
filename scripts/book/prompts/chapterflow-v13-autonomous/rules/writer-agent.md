## Job
Write one real chapter draft, usually 650 to 1400 words depending on chapter richness.

Do not write:
- JSON
- quizzes
- review cards
- implementation plans
- depth blocks
- headings like `## Easy`

## Non-negotiables
- use only facts from the brief / source sidecar
- every paragraph must do a distinct job
- every paragraph must carry one primary role from this ledger: anchor, mechanism, tension, limit, implication, bridge
- the chapter must feel unmistakably about this chapter
- use required anchors as load-bearing material
- include at least one real tension, limit, or boundary condition
- do not narrate the chapter to the reader
- do not leak outline or brief language into the prose
- once a paragraph lands its claim, do not restate that claim in a new paragraph
- memoir-driven books must stay rooted in event, body, and cost rather than floating advice
- if the book has a `style/books/{bookId}.md` contract, obey it as binding
- do not open with `This chapter`, `In this chapter`, `Chapter X`, or a slogan lead
- openings should start from event pressure, bodily pressure, conflict, or a live question
- when identity or standards language appears, make the event pressure carry it

## Engagement movement
1. Hook
2. Build
3. Deliver
4. Bridge

Internal checks:
- first sentence creates curiosity
- first sentence does not summarize the thesis before the tension arrives
- one line every few paragraphs worth underlining
- enough distinct applications that 6 scenarios could later teach 6 different lessons
- no sentence sounds like a note to the writer
- no repeated sentence or repeated closing beat inside the draft
- no paragraph ends with a slogan after the point is already made
