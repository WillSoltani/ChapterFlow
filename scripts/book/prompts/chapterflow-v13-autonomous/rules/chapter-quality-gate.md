#chapter-quality-gate.md
Use this rubric to judge whether a chapter is good enough to convert and good enough to scale.

## Auto-Fail Conditions

If any are true, reject the chapter immediately:

- invented facts, quotes, studies, or mechanisms
- generic enough to fit another chapter
- paragraph-job repetition
- repeated sentence or near-duplicate sentence inside a breakdown
- repeated concluding beat inside the same section
- hard depth that is just medium made longer
- hard depth that reuses medium's central phrasing without new function
- key takeaways, recap, cards, or prompts fall into repeated template tails or interchangeable scaffolds
- moreDetails restate instead of deepen
- pseudo-science, filler neuroscience, fake precision
- memoir chapter drifting into generic self-help uplift
- moral complexity framed as endorsement rather than strategic awareness when the brief requires distance
- first sentence is thesis-first instead of curiosity-first
- references concepts not introduced in prior chapters per the concept graph (prerequisite violation)

## Required Craft Checks

Before scoring, verify:

- the first sentence creates curiosity
- the chapter uses at least 2 strong anchors from the brief where available
- the chapter contains a real tension, limit, or failure mode
- the prose is readable, concrete, and not inflated
- the ending opens a next question rather than flattening into summary
- medium and hard clearly do different cognitive work
- reinforcement layers compress or deepen instead of replaying the same logic
- there is at least one genuinely memorable line every 200 to 300 words

## 10-Point Rubric

Score each category 0, 1, or 2.

### 1. Chapter Specificity

0 = could fit multiple chapters
1 = partly specific but still drifts generic
2 = unmistakably this chapter

### 2. Anchor Use

0 = anchor stories are absent or decorative
1 = anchors appear but are underused
2 = anchors carry the chapter's thinking

### 3. Analytical Value

0 = mostly summary or restatement
1 = some insight but not enough real distinction
2 = gives the reader a real mechanism, distinction, or tension

### 4. Paragraph Motion

0 = paragraphs repeat jobs or meander
1 = mostly distinct but with overlap
2 = every paragraph earns its place and advances the chapter

### 5. Prose Quality

0 = templated, padded, or flat
1 = competent but uneven
2 = sharp, readable, vivid, and authored

### 6. Hook And Bridge

0 = weak opening and dead ending
1 = one works, the other is weak
2 = the opening pulls and the ending opens the next question

## Passing Thresholds

- Chapter 1 must score at least 10/12
- later chapters must score at least 10/12
- any auto-fail overrides numeric score

## What To Report

When scoring a chapter, write:

- the score out of 10
- one sentence per category explaining the score
- the weakest paragraph or section
- the most reusable generic sentence, if one exists
- the strongest sentence in the draft
- whether the chapter is approved, revise-and-repeat, or reject

Additional v12 note:
- contamination leakage from brief / outline / seed language is an auto-fail
- identical tone collapse in required learning surfaces is an auto-fail
- prose audit failures for repetition, ending echoes, or hard/medium collapse are an auto-fail
- repeated template tails in takeaways, cards, recap, or prompts are an auto-fail
