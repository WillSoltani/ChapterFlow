
# Grade-Band Calibration

These bands are mandatory.

## Easy
- Reader comfort target: grade 8 to 9
- Use short sentences and concrete nouns.
- Open with a scene, person, or concrete tension in the first 2 sentences.
- Avoid stacked abstractions, dense nominalizations, and compressed theoretical phrasing.
- Easy should orient and clarify, not compress medium depth.

## Medium
- Reader comfort target: grade 10 to 11
- Explain the mechanism cleanly.
- Add one clear layer of analysis beyond the scene.
- Medium should explain and apply.

## Hard
- Reader comfort target: grade 12
- Hard may hold contradiction, boundary conditions, and strategic tension.
- Hard must deepen, not just lengthen.
- Every abstract move must stay anchored to a concrete example or source-supported distinction.

## Cross-depth rules
- Easy: orient and clarify.
- Medium: explain and apply.
- Hard: test the idea against limits, tensions, and failure modes.

If a sentence would fit better one level up, move it.
