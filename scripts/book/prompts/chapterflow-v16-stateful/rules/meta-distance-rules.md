
# Meta-Distance Rules

Teach the idea, not the chapter.

In learning surfaces, avoid:
- "the chapter says"
- "this chapter teaches"
- "Chapter N"
- excessive author-name repetition

Allowed:
- sparse author attribution in breakdowns where needed for source orientation

Target:
- near-zero meta-distance in takeaways, moreDetails, review cards, prompts, examples, and quiz explanations
