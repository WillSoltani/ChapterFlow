
# Scenario Tone Rules

For every example:
- `scenario` must be a tone object
- `whatToDo` must be a tone object
- `whyItMatters` must be a tone object

The three tones must differ in function, not just intensity.
If any two variants are identical, the example fails.

Scenario quality:
- 80 to 150 words
- 3 or more concrete details
- at least 1 sensory or emotional cue
- title must be vivid and specific
- each scenario teaches a distinct lesson from the chapter
