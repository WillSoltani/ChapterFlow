# Gold Benchmark: Patterns

This benchmark chapter succeeds because it gets the underlying patterns right.

Use this file to teach agents what to imitate at the level of craft.
Do not copy wording or sentence skeletons.

## What The Benchmark Does Well

### 1. It anchors the chapter in specific stories

- The chapter opens on Fouquet, Louis XIV, and Galileo.
- These are not decorative anecdotes. They carry the argument.
- The chapter's core claim becomes impossible to confuse with another chapter because the stories are chapter-specific and repeatedly functional.

### 2. Easy, medium, and hard do different jobs

- Easy simplifies without flattening.
- Medium explains the mechanism through contrast and evidence.
- Hard adds boundary conditions, failure modes, and deeper strategic distinctions.
- Hard is not medium with extra adjectives or extra length.

### 3. Gentle, direct, and competitive differ in function

- Gentle reassures, contextualizes, and lowers resistance.
- Direct names the mechanism cleanly and efficiently.
- Competitive sharpens stakes, leverage, and strategic consequence.
- The tones do not merely intensify the same sentence.

### 4. The examples are socially realistic

- They happen in recognizable modern environments.
- Each scenario has concrete details: names, rooms, times, objects, stakes.
- Each example tests the chapter's principle inside a plausible human situation.
- The examples feel different across work, school, and personal life.

### 5. whatToDo is specific

- It does not say "be strategic" or "show humility."
- It gives a precise reframing move or conversational move.
- The reader can picture the intervention immediately.

### 6. whyItMatters pays off the scenario

- It does not just repeat whatToDo.
- It explains the downstream consequence of the move.
- It connects the situation back to the chapter's governing principle.

### 7. The quiz tests judgment inside situations

- Strong questions are not glorified flashcards.
- The distractors are plausible.
- The correct answer wins because it better models the chapter's mechanism.
- The explanations teach, not just mark right or wrong.

### 8. The chapter carries a real analytical spine

- It introduces a distinction: complement vs. substitute.
- It introduces a boundary condition: secure vs. paranoid superior.
- It introduces an advanced concept: credit as lock-in.
- Those additions make hard depth feel deeper, not just longer.

## What Agents Should Learn

- Start with a vivid anchor, not a thesis announcement.
- Keep one governing principle visible across the whole chapter.
- Use named stories and concrete modern scenarios to carry abstraction.
- Make tone differences functional.
- Make depth differences structural.
- End paragraphs with consequence, not mush.

## What Agents Must Not Copy

- exact opening shapes
- exact sentence rhythms
- exact scenario templates
- the same contrast structure in every chapter
- the same rhetorical cadence for competitive tone

## Benchmark Checklist

A new chapter is approaching this benchmark if:

- you could not swap it into another chapter without breaking it
- the reader can name the chapter's distinct mechanism afterward
- medium and hard feel materially different
- examples feel vivid and chapter-specific
- quiz explanations teach the real principle
- implementation advice is specific to the chapter, not generic life advice

If those conditions are not met, the chapter is not benchmark quality yet.
