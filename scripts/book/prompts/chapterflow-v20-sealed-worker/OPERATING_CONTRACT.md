# Operating Contract

## Absolute rules

1. The Director may orchestrate but may not author reader-facing chapter content.
2. No script may author chapter prose, examples, quiz content, review cards, implementation text, or release chapter content from metadata, seeds, or outlines.
3. Every committed chapter must have a verified lineage chain.
4. Release may be assembled from committed validated chapters only.
5. For copyrighted books, if lawful web-first source coverage is insufficient for full-fidelity chapter writing, stop with a true blocker.
6. No human approval gates mid-pipeline.
7. No cover generation.

## Shortcut ban
The following are forbidden and are immediate blockers:
- manufacture the run artifact tree from chapter metadata
- authoring chapters by expanding fresh metadata alone
- shortcut the remaining chapters
- any Python/Node generator that writes chapter prose or final chapter JSON content
- writing validation reports that claim stages happened when receipts do not prove it
