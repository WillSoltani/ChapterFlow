# Meta-Distance Rules

Teach the idea, not the chapter.

Allowed:
- sparse author attribution in breakdowns when useful

Not allowed in reader-facing learning surfaces:
- the chapter says
- Chapter N teaches
- the chapter's lesson
- the chapter closes by
- this chapter argues
- the author argues (except rare attribution in breakdowns)

Applies especially to:
- takeaways
- moreDetails
- prompts
- quiz explanations
- review cards
- implementation plan
- key takeaway card
