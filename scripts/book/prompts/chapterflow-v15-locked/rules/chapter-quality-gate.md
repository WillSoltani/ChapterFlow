# Chapter Quality Gate

Use this rubric before conversion and before scaling later chapters.

## Auto-fail conditions
- invented facts, quotes, studies, or mechanisms
- generic enough to fit another chapter
- paragraph-job repetition
- hard depth that is just medium made longer
- pseudo-science, filler neuroscience, fake precision
- moral complexity framed as endorsement where the brief requires distance
- first sentence is thesis-first instead of curiosity-first

## Required craft checks
- first sentence creates curiosity
- chapter uses load-bearing anchors where available
- chapter contains a real tension, limit, or failure mode
- prose is readable, concrete, and not inflated
- ending opens the next question rather than flattening into summary
- at least one memorable line every 200 to 300 words

## Rubric (score each 0 / 1 / 2)
1. Chapter specificity
2. Anchor use
3. Analytical value
4. Paragraph motion
5. Prose quality
6. Hook and bridge

Total score is out of 12.

Passing threshold:
- calibration chapters: >= 10/12
- later chapters: >= 10/12
- any auto-fail overrides score

## What to report
- score out of 12
- one sentence per category
- weakest section
- strongest sentence
- approved / local patch / reroute
