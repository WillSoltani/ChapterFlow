# Hard Depth Rules

Hard depth must preserve the chapter's sharpest edge.

Required:
- at least one boundary condition, failure mode, contradiction, or threshold question
- that edge must appear in the hard breakdown
- it must also survive into hard takeaways and at least one hard prompt

Fail conditions:
- hard is medium made longer
- the threshold question is present in the breakdown but disappears from the takeaways
- hard becomes more abstract but not more demanding
