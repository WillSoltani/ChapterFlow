# Readability Rules

## Easy
- aim for grade 8-9
- start concrete
- keep sentence structure simpler
- avoid stacking multiple abstract nouns in one sentence
- do not let easy sound like compressed medium

## Medium
- aim for grade 10-11
- can use one clear mechanism or distinction per paragraph
- still readable on first pass

## Hard
- aim for grade 12
- may hold tension, limit, or synthesis
- must remain readable
- do not drift into prestige prose

## All depths
- use concrete nouns where possible
- vary paragraph jobs
- avoid repeated skeletons
- do not end paragraphs with generic mush
- if a line sounds like a podcast summary instead of real writing, cut it
