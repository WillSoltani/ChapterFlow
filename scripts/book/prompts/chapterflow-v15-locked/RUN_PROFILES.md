# Run Profiles

## serial_safe (default)
Use for:
- long books
- fragile books
- books that previously drifted after early chapters

Behavior:
- chapters 1 and 2 solo
- premium chapters solo where risk is high
- no aggressive parallelism if source coverage is thin
- strongest drift protection

## balanced_flagship
Use for:
- most nonfiction books once the pack is behaving well

Behavior:
- chapters 1 and 2 solo
- later waves of 2 when safe
- premium chapters still get stricter critic review

## prestige_flagship
Use for:
- highest-quality runs
- rhetorically dense or morally sharp books

Behavior:
- more chapters routed through the strictest critic path
- more local patching and less “good enough” acceptance
- slowest, highest-cost profile

## fast_backfill
Not recommended for first runs.
Only use if you already trust the book family and the pack behavior.
