# Grade-Band Calibration

These targets are mandatory for structured surfaces and should influence prose decisions upstream.

## Easy
Reader target: grade 8-9

Goals:
- orient
- clarify
- reduce abstraction
- use concrete nouns and active verbs
- make the idea feel usable quickly

Rules:
- open with a concrete scene, image, or practical tension inside the first 2 sentences
- prefer short to medium sentences
- avoid stacked clauses
- avoid jargon unless the brief requires a source term
- avoid thesis-first openings
- if a line cannot be pictured, questioned, or used, simplify it

## Medium
Reader target: grade 10-11

Goals:
- explain
- contrast
- connect mechanism to application

Rules:
- can name distinctions and mechanisms
- must still stay readable on first pass
- can use a source term if it is explained cleanly
- should not sound like compressed hard mode

## Hard
Reader target: grade 12

Goals:
- deepen
- add boundary conditions
- add unresolved tension, limit, contradiction, or synthesis

Rules:
- may ask the reader to hold 2-3 ideas in tension
- must still remain concrete enough to read without re-parsing every sentence
- cannot become abstract prestige prose
- must preserve the chapter's sharpest threshold question or hard edge
- hard depth is not medium with extra words

## Cross-depth rules
- easy, medium, and hard must do different jobs
- easy must not simply compress medium
- hard must not simply stretch medium
- each depth should change the reader's cognitive task
