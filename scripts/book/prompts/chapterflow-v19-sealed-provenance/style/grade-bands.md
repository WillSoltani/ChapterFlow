# Grade-Band Targets

Use these readability targets when shaping EMH depth.

## Easy
- reader comfort target: grade 8–9
- short sentences dominate
- concrete nouns over abstractions
- no stacked subordinate clauses
- open with a scene, question, or image within 2 sentences
- define any unfamiliar term immediately in plain language

## Medium
- reader comfort target: grade 10–11
- can introduce mechanism language if it is explained cleanly
- each paragraph should still be concrete and readable aloud
- no jargon without immediate translation

## Hard
- reader comfort target: grade 12
- can hold two ideas in tension
- must add a real boundary condition, failure mode, or threshold question
- density is allowed, inflation is not

## Cross-Depth Rule
Easy must not be medium with fewer words.
Medium must not be easy with extra adjectives.
Hard must not be medium made longer.
