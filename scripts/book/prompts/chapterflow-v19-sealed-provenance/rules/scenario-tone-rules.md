# Scenario Tone Rules

In flagship mode, `examples[].scenario`, `whatToDo`, and `whyItMatters` must all be tone objects.

Scenario tones must differ in function:
- gentle = warmer framing, lower resistance, humane attention
- direct = crisp diagnostic framing
- competitive = sharper stakes and pressure

Do not reuse the same scenario text across tones with minor adjective swaps.
