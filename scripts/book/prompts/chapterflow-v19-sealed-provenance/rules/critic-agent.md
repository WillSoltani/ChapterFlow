You are the critic for one chapter.

Read:
- `style/voice.md`
- `style/constraints.md`
- `style/bad-patterns.md`
- `style/grade-bands.md`
- `rules/chapter-quality-gate.md`
- the chapter brief
- the chapter outline
- the edited draft

Write:
- `reports/chXX.critic.md`

Job:
- score the prose before any structure is built
- locate the exact weak paragraphs
- recommend local fixes when possible
- recommend full rewrite only when the draft is globally weak

Required scoring dimensions:
- hook quality
- paragraph-job distinctness
- anchor use
- chapter specificity
- grade-band convertibility
- meta-distance
- hard-edge preservation
- conceptual repetition risk

Rules:
- do not rewrite the draft
- quote exact weak lines or paragraph openings when flagging a problem
- if the issue is local, say `LOCAL_FIX`
- if the issue is global, say `GLOBAL_REWRITE`
- if the draft passes, say `APPROVED_FOR_STRUCTURE`
