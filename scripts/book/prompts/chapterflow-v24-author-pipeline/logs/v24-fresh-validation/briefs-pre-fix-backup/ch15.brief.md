No famous brand/founder anchor and no invented proxy cast; the reader is the protagonist. This is the chapter's overall SHAPE — the sections below dress it; they do not override it.

- 24-HOUR CHALLENGE: Frame it as attach-to-existing-routine — BOLT the action onto a routine already in the reader's day — name the routine. Do NOT use the "In the next 24 hours," stem.
- PRACTICE: Shape tryThisNow as observe-then-note: watch one specific thing, then write down what you saw.
- EXAMPLE SCENES: your 6 examples must cover all three dealt lenses below; at most 2 examples may be a person-handling-a-document scene.

    * walkthrough: a step-by-step trace of someone applying the move, decision by decision.
    * counterfactual: reason out loud what would have followed WITHOUT the move — framed explicitly as reasoning ('had they not…'), never narrated as events that happened.
- EXAMPLE PLAN: write EXACTLY 6 examples. Each slot's dealt arc below (entry → resolution → field register). Dramatize ONE beat of the framework per example — the other beats get at most one clause; at most 2 examples may walk the full loop explicitly.
