## VARIETY (dealt — do NOT default to the house pattern)
- CHAPTER ARCHITECTURE (two-way-contrast): Structure the chapter as exactly TWO cases held in tension — the contrast itself is the lesson. No third edge case, no proxy chorus; let the two do all the work. This is the chapter's overall SHAPE — the sections below dress it; they do not override it.
- OPENER: Open the hook with a QUESTION the reader can't answer yet. Carry the SAME mode into the fastRead opening sentence.
- 24-HOUR CHALLENGE: Frame it as audit-one-artifact — have the reader AUDIT one existing artifact for the chapter's pattern. Do NOT use the "In the next 24 hours," stem.
- PRACTICE: Shape tryThisNow as ONE direct command — no 'Pick one…' menu, no a/b/or-c options.
- EXAMPLE SCENES: your 6 examples must cover all three dealt lenses below; at most 2 examples may be a person-handling-a-document scene.
    * before-after-ledger: show the same numbers or facts BEFORE and AFTER the move — the delta is the story.
    * postmortem: a past-tense autopsy: the decision already played out; trace why it went the way it went.
    * walkthrough: a step-by-step trace of someone applying the move, decision by decision.
- EXAMPLE PLAN: write EXACTLY 6 examples. Each slot's dealt arc below (entry → resolution → field register). Dramatize ONE beat of the framework per example — the other beats get at most one clause; at most 2 examples may walk the full loop explicitly. An example that scenes one of YOUR owned cases must enter it at a DIFFERENT point than the breakdown's telling. Slots marked +anchor get exactly ONE concrete physical/sensory detail; the others get none (props on every scene read as scaffold).
    * ex01: open when someone outside the room first feels the effect → the move is skipped or botched and the miss lands → lead with WHY it works, then the move
    * ex02: open on the early signal nobody has flagged yet → ends unresolved; the return point is set but not yet met → open with the question the reader would ask, answer it
    * ex03: open at the moment the demand/standard is stated → the move works — but earn it, show the cost paid → make both fields the tersest in the chapter — no preamble
