## 0. Executive summary

The v24 author pipeline is **not failing because of a "writer ceiling."** That was the previous
session's terminal classification, and this audit finds it **premature**. Three verified pipeline
defects fully explain why the anti-sameness campaigns kept stalling, and none of them is the writer:

1. **The pipeline re-injects the sameness mold into every writer prompt.** The manual brief is a
   derived artifact regenerated by `derive-artifacts` (auto-run by `book-gate` and the QC entry) from
   the frozen research TOC, whose `authorVoice.signatureMoves` literally mandate the mold devices
   ("opens with recognizable business… cases", "turns a case into a simple three-part distinction
   such as WHY, HOW, and WHAT"). Those moves become `do:` lines in the HOUSE STYLE voice card of
   every writer prompt — in the same card where the content-device deal bans those exact devices.
   The campaign's hand-fix of the brief (commit `dae308a01`) was **reverted by the pipeline itself
   on 2026-07-07T23:11Z** (live, verified in the working tree). → Finding F-01.
2. **Device removal is requested, never verified.** The repair drivers accept a re-authored chapter
   on composite/keys/validity alone; `detectChapterDevices` is never run on the new bytes, and the
   planner's `bannedDevices` list is dropped before it reaches the driver. "Writer reproduces banned
   devices" is unenforced, not unenforceable. → Finding F-02.
3. **The acceptance loop regresses its own passing chapters.** Acceptance-driven regen has no
   PASS-lock, no restore-on-regress, and no durable reopen note — the exact churn class campaign 1
   fixed for the budget lane was left open in the acceptance lane, which is where the loop actually
   spends its regen budget. → Finding F-03.

On top of these, `start-with-why` is in a **live, materialized deadlock** (F-04): 14/14 chapters
hold durable PASSes, book acceptance FAILs (median 74.6, gate FAIL sticky for these bytes), 6/14
chapters are regen-exhausted, and the only unspent repair lane (`content-repair-book`,
`contentRepairConsumed` empty) is manual-only — the conductor cannot reach it.

Secondary but important: the acceptance gate's semantics have drifted from what everyone (including
the previous report) believes they are — **churn is telemetry, not a veto**; the real gate is floor
74 + hardcoded +5-over-shipped; a fresh book with no shipped control gates at 74 alone (F-05). The
entire deterministic anti-sameness stack is advisory-only and includes a dead
`severe ? "major" : "major"` ternary (F-06). The device catalog misses the single most saturated
device on the current book — the weekly practice shell, present in ~13/14 chapters (F-07).

**What did the margin in:** `74.6 < 72.7 + 5` (hardcoded `BEAT_SHIPPED_MARGIN`) plus a sticky
1P/2F gate FAIL. The "margin-policy" half of the previous report's classification stands. The
"writer ceiling" half cannot be assessed until F-01/F-02/F-03 are fixed, because the writer was
being simultaneously mandated and banned from the same devices, and compliance was never checked.

**Totals: 15 findings — 4×S1, 7×S2, 3×S3, 1×S4. No S0** (nothing found that causes a false
publish today; the closest, key-judge fail-open, is S2 because the author path has an independent
reader-side key check).

## 4. Findings (full detail)

### F-01 — Derived manual brief re-mandates the sameness mold in every writer prompt (S1, High)

**Affected stage:** brief derivation → voice card → chapter writing (and every repair/regen write).

**What the system currently does.** The full chain, each link verified:

1. The frozen research TOC for start-with-why carries mold-mandating voice moves —
   `.chapterflow/runs/start-with-why/20260704T125509997Z-*/source-freeze/toc.json` `authorVoice.signatureMoves`:
   *"opens with recognizable business, aviation, civil-rights, or consumer-technology cases"*,
   *"turns a case into a simple three-part distinction such as WHY, HOW, and WHAT"*,
   *"returns to Apple, the Wright brothers, and Martin Luther King Jr. as recurring reference points"*.
   (Emitted by the bibliography agent per `prompts/researcher-bibliography.system.md:40`, which asks
   for "3-5 specific moves the author uses".)
2. `runDeriveArtifacts` (`src/cli.ts:948-1003`) copies `toc.authorVoice` verbatim into
   `state/briefs/<book>.manual-brief.json` `voiceCharter` (`cli.ts:991-997` via `tocAuthorVoice`,
   `cli.ts:454`) and **unconditionally overwrites** the file (`cli.ts:1003`).
3. `derive-artifacts` is **auto-run** by `book-gate` ("Auto-runs derive-artifacts first", `cli.ts:206`)
   and force-run at the QC entry (`cli.ts:3465`, comment: "book-gate force-derives for exactly this
   reason; mirror it at the QC entry").
4. `formatVoiceBible` (`src/lib/voiceBible.ts:53-55`) renders `signatureMoves.slice(0,3)` as a
   `do:` line and `avoidMoves` as `never:`; `voiceCard` (`lib/voiceCard.ts:120-146`) passes them
   through (drops only the "sounds like" specimen); `buildAuthorCard` injects the card as HOUSE
   STYLE into **every** writer prompt (`authorRun.ts:562`, rendered :465-469).
5. The same card carries the content-device deal (`authorRun.ts:460-463`) banning 2-3 of those
   exact devices per chapter. The writer receives **"do: open with a recognizable case; turn it
   into a WHY/HOW/WHAT distinction"** and **"Do NOT lean on: named-anchor lead / three-part
   split…"** simultaneously.

**Proof of live impact.** The campaign hand-edited the brief to de-mandate the moves and committed
it in `dae308a01`. The working tree now shows that file ` M`-modified with
`derivedAt: 2026-07-07T23:11:34.612Z` — a book-gate/QC run that evening re-derived it, restoring
the mold-mandating `signatureMoves` and deleting the anti-uniform-machinery `avoidMove`. (The
review-clears ledger and a fresh acceptance read were written minutes later, 23:22Z/23:26Z, same
run family.) The 14 `state/plans/*.manual-plan.json` were re-derived in the same batch.

**Why this is a problem.** Every future write — including every repair the fix prompts will
trigger — carries the contradiction. The voice card's own contract says it must teach *"HOW to
sound, never WHAT to say"* (`voiceCard.ts:14-16`); "opens with recognizable business cases" is
WHAT, violating the card's stated design. This alone plausibly explains the "writer reproduces
banned devices" and "balloon effect" observations that led the previous session to conclude
writer-ceiling.

**Previous report:** noted the derived-artifact trap ("durable lever is the code, not the brief")
but did not identify that the reverted brief actively re-mandates the mold in-prompt, and did not
build the durable code-side lever. **Status: confirmed, and more severe than reported.**

**Root cause.** Voice data is treated as pure style, but the bibliography agent emits content
devices as "signature moves," and no layer between TOC and writer prompt filters WHAT from HOW.

**Tests:** none — `runDeriveArtifacts` has zero test coverage (grep of `tests/` confirms; F-13).

**Recommended fix direction.** Sanitize device-mandating moves at the choke point every book passes
through — `formatVoiceBible`/`voiceCard` (style-only enforcement) — plus make `derive-artifacts`
merge-aware or at minimum loudly refuse to clobber a hand-edited brief; add derivation tests.
See Prompt 1.

**Red-team notes.**
- *Symptom or root cause?* Root cause for the prompt contradiction; upstream root is the
  bibliography prompt, but fixing only the prompt leaves ~119 existing frozen TOCs poisoned —
  the filter must live downstream of the TOC.
- *Could it already be fixed?* No — the live revert happened after the last commit on the branch.
- *Could the fix overfit start-with-why?* The filter must key on device *shapes* (named-case
  openers, N-part framework reflexes, recurring-anchor mandates), not Sinek vocabulary.
- *Could it weaken a true protection?* `avoidMoves` like "does not build dense academic literature
  reviews" are legitimate; the filter must only strip device *mandates*, never avoid-rules.

### F-02 — Banned-device removal is requested, never verified (S1, High)

**Affected stage:** `diversify-book` / `content-repair-book` repair drivers; by extension every
"de-saturation" claim the pipeline makes.

**What the system currently does.** `diversifyOne` (`src/orchestrator/bookSamenessRun.ts:185-252`)
re-authors a chapter with the ban directive injected as a complaint, then keeps the draft iff
(`:243`):

```ts
const keep = review.valid && keysClean && noReservedHarm && review.composite >= bar - band;
```

No call to `detectChapterDevices` on the fresh bytes. `detectChapterDevices` has exactly two
callers — the planner (`contentDeviceRepair.ts:113`, before re-author) and the advisory critic
(`contentMachinery.ts:69`). The book-level "after" report at `bookSamenessRun.ts:342` is logged as
`residualOverCap` only — it never gates or rolls back. Worse, the planner computes the exact
`bannedDevices` per target (`contentDeviceRepair.ts:149`) but the driver call site
(`bookSamenessRun.ts:328`) passes only `{ chapterNumber, directive, label }` — **the banned list is
dropped** and `diversifyOne` could not verify removal even if it wanted to.

**Proof.** Live campaign-4 evidence in the previous report matches exactly: ch06 kept the
hard-detail device verbatim and ch01 kept its proxies while "passing" repair at 87.3; proxy-cast
moved only 93%→79%; second-setting *rose* (balloon effect). All predicted by an unenforced ban.

**Previous report:** identified the symptom ("passing review ≠ devices removed") in its traps
section but did not locate the missing check or the dropped `bannedDevices`. **Confirmed +
root-caused.**

**Tests:** no test exercises the revert branch of `diversifyOne`, and no test anywhere asserts a
re-authored chapter sheds its banned devices (F-13).

**Recommended fix direction.** Thread `bannedDevices` through the target type; after the
self-check, run `detectChapterDevices(fresh)` and treat a still-present banned device as a
revert-with-status (`status: "devices-persisted"`), so failure is honest instead of fake success.
Bound as today (grant already consumed). See Prompt 2.

**Red-team notes.**
- *Could enforcement cause non-convergence?* Yes — if the writer genuinely cannot comply, every
  attempt reverts. That is the *correct* honest outcome (it finally distinguishes "writer ceiling"
  from "never asked properly"), but the fix must surface a distinct status so the operator sees
  compliance-failure, not silence.
- *Detector false positives?* The 6 detectors are regexes with thin fixture coverage (only
  proxy-cast has FP tests). A FP would wrongly revert a good draft. The fix must add FP/FN fixtures
  per detector before enforcement (bundled into Prompt 2).
- *Could it fight F-01?* Until F-01 is fixed the writer is still being *told* to use the devices —
  enforcement would burn grants on guaranteed reverts. **Order matters: Prompt 1 before Prompt 2.**

### F-03 — Acceptance-driven regen reopens passing chapters unguarded (S1, High)

**Affected stage:** book-acceptance rejection routing (`authorReview.ts:1483-1605`).

**What the system currently does.** On acceptance rejection, up to `AUTHOR_BOOK_REGEN_CHAPTER_CAP=3`
chapters (`:139`) are fully re-authored (`:1560-1583`). Verified properties of this block:

- `holdsDurablePass` / `appendReopenNote` are **not imported** into `authorReview.ts` — no
  PASS-lock protection, no durable reopen note (grep-verified).
- The only softening is the churn-branch `strongPass ≥ 87` filter (`:1516-1520`) which exempts
  *saturation-fill* contributors — reader-named chapters and all non-churn targets are routable at
  any passing score.
- The re-authored draft is kept if it merely re-passes review (`:1581-1582`) — a 74-scoring draft
  silently replaces an 85-scoring one (the documented ch04 85.6→73.4 regression class).
- **If the regen review FAILs, the block halts with the regressed bytes left on disk** — there is
  no restore of the prior passing bytes anywhere in the block (`:1565-1585`). A previously-PASSING
  chapter can be left FAILING by an acceptance round.

Contrast: the budget lane got exactly these protections in campaign 1 (`partitionBudgetBlockers`,
A4 hash guard, `protected-downgrade`/`reopened-anomaly` reopen notes — `authorRun.ts:863-879`,
`:1048-1071`). The asymmetry is the finding: the loop that actually spends the book's regen budget
is the unprotected one.

**Evidence from state.** `reopen-notes.json` does not exist for start-with-why — no protection has
ever fired — while the regen ledger shows 6 chapters exhausted, largely by acceptance rounds.
`tiebreak-notes.json` has 27 entries; the acceptance reopens have no durable attribution at all.

**Previous report:** campaign 1 claimed carry-churn "fixed"; true only for the budget lane.
**Partially contradicted.**

**Recommended fix direction.** In the acceptance-regen block: snapshot prior bytes; restore on
regen-write failure and on review FAIL; keep a passing draft only if its composite is not worse

### F-05 — Acceptance-gate semantics drifted from shared understanding (S2, High)

**Affected stage:** book acceptance (`authorReview.ts`, `evalBookProxy.ts`).

**Verified facts:**
1. **Churn never vetoes.** `accepted = quorumMet ∧ gatePooled==="PASS" ∧ compPooled>=74 ∧
   (shipped===null ∨ compPooled>=shipped+5)` (`authorReview.ts:1087-1090`). Churn appears only in
   telemetry and post-rejection routing (`:1082-1084`, `:1505`). A book unanimous-churn-HIGH on all
   reads is accepted if the numbers clear. This was a deliberate 2026-07-04 calibration — but the
   previous report, the memory files, and the campaign framing all still describe churn as the
   rejector.
2. **Fresh books gate at floor 74 alone.** `BEAT_SHIPPED_MARGIN` applies only when a shipped
   control exists (`shippedControl.ts:176-191`); a first-time book needs only median 74 — below the
   demonstrated good-book noise floor (a 9×85-89 board read 75.0-78.7, comment at `:409`).
3. **`AUTHOR_BOOK_ACCEPT_BAR = 80` is dead** — logged as "premium telemetry target" only
   (`:870`, `:1091`); anyone reading the constant name assumes it gates.
4. **Ties favor PASS** in `composeBookVerdict` (`evalBookProxy.ts:487`): 1P/1F on a degraded
   2-valid-reader panel passes. Acceptance quorum (≥3 valid) mostly shields this, but the same
   function scores the **shipped control** (`shippedControl.ts:222`) where no quorum guard applies —
   a 2-reader control read can set the baseline the +5 margin is measured against.
5. **Churn is judged on a 4-chapter sample** (`evalBookProxy.ts:73`), salted round-to-round with
   regen targets force-included (`authorReview.ts:1591-1594`) — it was never a whole-book
   measurement, so "7/14 repaired but churn still HIGH" partially reflects sampling, and a ≤3-chapter
   repair *can* flip the sampled label without fixing the book.
6. **No test touches the predicate** — grep confirms no test references `AUTHOR_BOOK_ACCEPT_FLOOR`,
   `BEAT_SHIPPED_MARGIN`, or drives `runBookAcceptance`'s `accepted` (F-13).

**Why this matters.** Three sessions of campaign work were aimed at a gate whose actual semantics
nobody had written down. Decisions like "(b) lower the +5 premium margin" were framed to the owner
against a wrong model of the gate (churn-vetoes). And the under-strict fresh-book floor means the
*next* new book can ship at 74-median with unanimous churn-HIGH — the exact quality the campaigns
were fighting — without tripping anything.

**Recommended fix direction.** Tests pinning the real predicate; rename/derive the dead constant;
quorum guard for shipped-control reads; an explicit owner-decision memo (in-repo doc) for churn's
gate status and the fresh-book floor — **surfaced, not silently changed** (calibration was an owner
decision). See Prompt 6.

**Red-team notes.**
- *Is demote-churn wrong?* Not necessarily — churn is noisy and holistic; making it a hard veto
  invites accept-time flapping. The defect is that the demotion is invisible: no doc, no test, and
  the reporting still says "rejected as churn HIGH."
- *Would re-promoting churn to a veto be safe?* Not without owner sign-off and a flap analysis;
  the fix prompt deliberately does NOT re-promote it.
