Title: Lexicon Naming Wall Scenario: On the second morning in Lexicon Branding's naming studio, the naming lead first tapes one favorite product name at the center of the wall and hears the room argue taste. Later the product naming wall holds three routes from multiple teams: one descriptive, one suggestive, one short and invented. The brand-name development talk now marks which route signals use, which carries feeling, and which could stand apart in a crowded category. What to do: The naming lead keeps the three routes visible and asks the team to mark what each route teaches before voting on any name.
