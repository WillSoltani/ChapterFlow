Title: Snapple At The Table Scenario: In a Quaker strategy room, the Quaker executive taps the Snapple packet beside the deal binder. "Snapple is the growth answer," he says. The strategist keeps his pencil on the $1.8 billion line. "Or it is one answer," he says. "If growth is the job, what else buys growth with this much money?" What to do: The Quaker executive changes the meeting question from "Should Quaker buy Snapple?" to "What are the growth paths this money could fund?" The deal becomes one path.
