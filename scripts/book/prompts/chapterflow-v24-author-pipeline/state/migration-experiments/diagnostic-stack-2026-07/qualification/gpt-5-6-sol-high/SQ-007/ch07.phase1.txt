## Full read
Still, there is a situation that calls for the opposite move. When someone is about to be hurt, do not pause to draw the whole clock map. Stop the harm first. Pull the person back, lock the door, call for help, or say the blunt sentence. The layered account belongs after safety is held. Analysis should make action smarter, not slower when danger is still unfolding.
