## Full read
The cold-water study gives the caution. The 90-second trial did not become shorter because it ended less painfully. People still endured more total discomfort than in the 60-second trial. The finding warns you not to judge an experience by duration alone. It also warns you not to use a better ending as cover for needless pain. A gentle final minute is not permission to make the first part careless. This idea fails when the basic promise is broken. A red phone cannot make an unsafe pool feel fine. A ceremony cannot replace real college support. A soft ending cannot make honest harm harmless. Moments work best when the base is sound enough, and the design gives memory something true to keep.
