The four moves name the repair job: Widen your options, Reality-test your assumptions, Attain distance before deciding, Prepare to be wrong. Each move meets a different danger. Narrow framing needs more live paths. Confirmation bias needs better evidence. Short-term emotion needs distance. Overconfidence needs a wider range of futures.
