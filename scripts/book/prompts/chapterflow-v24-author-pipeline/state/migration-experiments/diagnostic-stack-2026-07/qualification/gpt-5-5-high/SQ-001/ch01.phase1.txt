This is why contribution language has to stay sharp. It is not a polite way to say everyone is equally at fault. In many conflicts, one person may hold most of the harm, power, or responsibility. Naming your part does not forgive theirs. It simply gives you one lever you can move today. Your ten percent does not erase their ninety. It gives you a place to stop feeding the pattern while still naming the ninety clearly.
