- QUIZ STEMS: draw your 9 stems from these dealt shapes — failure-postmortem (it already failed; ask which earlier step was the cause); cold-diagnosis (present symptoms; ask what is actually wrong); choose-next-move (a live scenario; ask for the single best next action); predict-consequence (a choice was just made; ask what happens downstream). - DISTRACTOR MODES: derive every wrong answer FROM your key via one of — wrong-target (the right move aimed at the wrong person/thing); wrong-timing (the right move too early or too late); wrong-proof (accepts the wrong evidence as settling it); wrong-scope (applies the move too broadly or too narrowly). - MEMORABLE LINES: your 3 lines use these dealt shapes, one each — pointed-question (a question sharp enough to reread); imperative (a command short enough to say from memory); reversal (a line that flips the expected direction).
- LIMITS PLACEMENT: close the full read with the limits paragraph (the classic slot — fine here, other chapters own other slots)
- FIRST-MENTION GROUNDING: ground anchors with a parenthetical era/role — '(GE's 1990s operating review)'.
