# Meta-Distance Rules

Teach the idea, not the chapter.

In takeaways, moreDetails, quiz explanations, prompts, review cards, and implementation plans:
- zero references to "this chapter", "Chapter N", or "the chapter says"
- author-name references only when attribution is truly needed
- prefer direct teaching to source narration

In breakdowns:
- author references are allowed sparingly
- "the chapter" constructions should be near-zero
