# Readability Rules

Easy:
- grade 8–9
- short sentences
- concrete nouns
- no stacked abstraction
- feels like orientation, not compressed analysis

Medium:
- grade 10–11
- mechanism is clear
- still readable
- no needless jargon

Hard:
- grade 12
- may carry tension, failure mode, and synthesis
- harder in thinking, not just in vocabulary

Across all depths:
- no pre-manufactured podcast-summary voice
- no repeated sentence skeletons
- no thesis-first opening
