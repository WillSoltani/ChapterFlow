# Book Skeleton

## Metadata
- Book: Blue Ocean Strategy
- Authors: W. Chan Kim and Renée Mauborgne
- Locked edition: expanded English edition family
- Wave size: 2 by default
- Residual final wave note: chapter 11 remains after paired waves because the chapter count is odd

## Rotation Plan For Examples
- Use the canonical six-format rotation once per chapter: decision_point, postmortem, dialogue, predict_reveal, dilemma, before_after.
- Keep the 2 work / 2 school / 2 personal split in each chapter package.
- Rotate ending types across broader_principle, self_directed_question, surprising_implication, cross_domain, common_trap, perspective_reframe.

## School-Setting Plan
- Use campus venture labs, MBA case rooms, engineering capstones, student newspaper teams, debate societies, and project-demo rehearsals.
- Avoid turning school examples into generic study-harder advice.

## Vocabulary Watchlist
- Keep red ocean, blue ocean, value innovation, strategic move, strategy canvas, noncustomers, strategic sequence, tipping point leadership, fair process, alignment, renewal, and red-ocean traps precise.
- Avoid disruption cliches, startup-bro swagger, and empty innovation talk.

## Motif / Callback Opportunities
- Chapter 1 should set strategic move and value innovation as the governing lens for everything that follows.
- Chapter 2 should turn the big contrast into visible tools rather than abstract inspiration.
- Chapters 3 through 6 should read like a disciplined formulation sequence, not a grab bag of frameworks.
- Chapters 7 through 9 should keep execution, buy-in, and proposition alignment tightly coupled.
- Chapters 10 and 11 should feel like maintenance of strategic judgment rather than a loose epilogue.

## Chapter Plan
### ch01 Creating Blue Oceans
- Intent: Establish the core break from head-to-head competition and frame value innovation as the path to uncontested market space.
- Source richness: high
- Concept density: medium-high
- Moral complexity: medium
- Thin-chapter risk notes: Must not collapse into generic innovation rhetoric or unsupported case detail.
- Wave routing: baseline wave

### ch02 Analytical Tools and Frameworks
- Intent: Show how the strategy canvas, four actions, and ERRC make blue-ocean thinking operational.
- Source richness: high
- Concept density: high
- Moral complexity: low
- Thin-chapter risk notes: Avoid turning the chapter into tool-list recitation without strategic movement.
- Wave routing: baseline wave
