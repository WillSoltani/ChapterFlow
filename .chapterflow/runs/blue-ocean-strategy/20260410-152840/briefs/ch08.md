Core Claim:
This chapter argues that execution holds better when it is built into the strategy process itself rather than bolted on after the move is chosen. The frozen bundle supports fair process and its three public elements: engagement, explanation, and expectation clarity. The chapter's burden is to keep execution tied to procedural legitimacy, not to generic participation slogans.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the book's fair-process chapter. Chapter 7 broke the key organizational hurdles. Chapter 8 asks how leaders make follow-through durable by shaping the process people experience around the strategy.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter argues that people resist not only outcomes but processes they experience as opaque or one-sided.
Then it frames fair process as the bridge between strategy and committed execution.
Then it anchors engagement, explanation, and expectation clarity.
Then it argues that perceived procedural fairness strengthens voluntary cooperation even when every preference is not granted.
