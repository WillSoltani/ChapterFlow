Core Claim:
This chapter argues that blue ocean strategy becomes sustainable only when the value proposition, profit proposition, and people proposition reinforce one another. The frozen official support is narrow but clear: the three propositions must align, and execution weakens when a strong value/profit pairing is not matched by an equally strong people proposition.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the book's strategic-alignment chapter. Chapter 8 focused on fair process inside execution. Chapter 9 widens the lens and asks whether the full strategic system is internally aligned across buyers, economics, and people.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter says a compelling buyer offer is not enough by itself.
Then it adds the profit proposition as the economic condition that makes the move viable.
Then it adds the people proposition as the motivational and organizational condition that makes the move executable.
Then it argues that durable success depends on the three propositions reinforcing one another rather than pulling in different directions.
