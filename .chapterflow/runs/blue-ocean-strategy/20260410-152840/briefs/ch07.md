Core Claim:
This chapter argues that execution failure is often blamed on general resistance when the real issue is a small set of organizational hurdles that can be attacked with concentrated leverage. The frozen bundle supports the public tipping-point leadership vocabulary and its four hurdle types. The chapter's burden is to make execution feel like focused strategic intervention rather than broad change-management ritual.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the book's organizational-hurdles chapter. Chapter 6 proved whether a move works commercially. Chapter 7 asks how leaders can move the organization through the few barriers that still block execution.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter argues that viable strategy still dies if the organization cannot move.
Then it frames tipping point leadership as concentrated action instead of diffuse reform.
Then it anchors the four hurdle types: cognitive, resource, motivational, and political.
Then it argues that leaders should focus energy where small leverage changes can unlock wider movement.
