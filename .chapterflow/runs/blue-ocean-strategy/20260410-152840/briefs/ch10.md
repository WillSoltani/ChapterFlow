Core Claim:
This chapter argues that blue oceans have to be renewed over time rather than treated as permanent victories. The frozen official support is the pioneer-migrator-settler map, which frames renewal as a portfolio judgment about future growth and the balance among settlers, migrators, and pioneers.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the book's renewal chapter. Chapter 9 focused on alignment within one move. Chapter 10 widens the frame to the portfolio level and asks how leaders judge whether their current mix supports future growth.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter warns against treating a successful move as permanently safe.
Then it introduces settlers, migrators, and pioneers as a portfolio map.
Then it argues that the map helps leaders see how much future growth is likely to come from each type of business or initiative.
Then it uses renewal to push the book from one winning move toward an ongoing discipline of portfolio judgment.
