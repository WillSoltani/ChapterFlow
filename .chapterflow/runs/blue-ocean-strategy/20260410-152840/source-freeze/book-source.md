## Authorized Bundle Snapshot

- Official book page: frames the book as a move from bloody red-ocean competition toward uncontested market space and says the argument is built on 150 strategic moves across more than 100 years and 30 industries.
- Official FAQ: clarifies that blue ocean strategy is about value innovation rather than technology novelty, and explains that the expanded edition adds material on alignment, renewal, and red-ocean traps.
- Official tool pages: define the public tool vocabulary that structures the middle and later chapters, including strategy canvas, four actions, ERRC, six paths, noncustomers, tipping point leadership, fair process, strategic alignment, and portfolio renewal.
- Official six-paths article and example pages: support Chapter 3's boundary-reconstruction logic and provide narrow authorized examples for buyer-group and functional-emotional shifts.
- Official red-ocean articles: support Chapter 11's warning that benchmarking rivals and fighting over existing demand pull strategy back toward imitation and ordinary competition.
- Authorized O'Reilly contents: confirms the expanded-edition chapter map from `Creating Blue Oceans` through `Avoid Red Ocean Traps`.
