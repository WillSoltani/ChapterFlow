Chapter 2 frozen source notes: Analytical Tools and Frameworks

- Official tools hub lists the public framework family that anchors this chapter: strategy canvas, four actions framework, and ERRC grid.
- Official strategy-canvas page frames the canvas as a one-page visual analytic that lets a team see the current state of play and a possible future value curve.
- Official four-actions support says the framework challenges the factors an industry takes for granted by asking what to eliminate, reduce, raise, and create.
- Official ERRC page says the grid turns those questions into action and helps produce a new value curve instead of scattered improvements.
- Safe chapter emphasis: visible value logic, strategic sameness, challenge to inherited factors, and coherent curve redesign.
- Unsafe zone: invented workshop stories, detailed company case retellings, or claims that filling in a grid guarantees market creation.
