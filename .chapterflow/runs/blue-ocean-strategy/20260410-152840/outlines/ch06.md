Chapter Promise:
Readers will understand why a promising blue-ocean opportunity still has to survive an ordered sequence of buyer value, price, cost, and adoption.

Opening Move:
Open on a team excited by visible demand until someone asks whether the move can actually survive price, cost, and adoption pressure after the idea sounds compelling.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 for why opportunity alone is not enough
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 for the ordered sequence itself
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 for alignment across the sequence and the bridge to execution barriers

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: show the trap of falling in love with demand before viability
- P2: explain why sequence matters as commercial discipline
- P3: establish buyer utility first
- P4: move through price, cost, and adoption as ordered pressures
- P5: show that misalignment across the sequence breaks the move
- P6: bridge to organizational hurdles in Chapter 7

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter must distinguish between a compelling opportunity and a commercially viable one by preserving the order of buyer utility, price, cost, and adoption.

Hard-Depth Tension:
