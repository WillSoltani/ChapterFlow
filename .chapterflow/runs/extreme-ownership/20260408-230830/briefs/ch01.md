The opening combat dilemma in Ramadi is not presented as a clean case study with perfect information. The chapter's job is to show why the rest of the book is organized around leadership principles rather than around equipment, motivation, or slogans. It also sets up the idea that each later principle is a practical answer to the opening question of what good leadership looks like when conditions are unstable.
