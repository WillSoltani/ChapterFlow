Core Claim:
This chapter argues that plans and communication fail when they become too complicated to survive real pressure. The chapter's force comes from the idea that complexity looks impressive in calm moments but collapses when people are stressed, moving fast, or trying to act at the front. Simplicity is treated as operational strength: the clearer the plan and explanation, the more likely execution will survive chaos.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It shifts from interdependence to clarity under pressure. Its mechanism is simplification, not support or ego correction alone.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter shows overcomplicated plans and explanations failing under stress.
Then it argues that leaders must simplify the plan until people at the front can understand and use it.
Then it shows that mission success rises when clarity survives pressure better than complexity does.

Required Anchors:
- overcomplicated plans collapsing under pressure

- mission success depending on simplicity that survives chaos
