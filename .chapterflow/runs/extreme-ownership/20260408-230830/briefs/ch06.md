Core Claim:
This chapter argues that teams fail when units operate as silos and succeed when they support one another as mutually dependent elements. The chapter's force comes from the idea that no team wins alone. Every subgroup's output depends on the support, timing, and awareness of the others. The chapter treats cover and move as a principle of interdependence: you protect the mission by helping adjacent teams succeed, not by maximizing your own lane in isolation.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It shifts from inner humility to outer mutual support. Its mechanism is interdependence and cross-team support, not buy-in or ego alone.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter shows siloed behavior failing because units act as if their lane is self-contained.
Then it shows teams needing to cover one another as mutually dependent elements.
Then it argues that mission success improves when leaders think in terms of shared support instead of local status or isolated performance.
