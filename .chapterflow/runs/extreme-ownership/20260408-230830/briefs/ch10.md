Core Claim:
This chapter argues that careful planning, commander's intent, rehearsal, and contingency thinking make teams more adaptable under pressure rather than more rigid. The chapter's force comes from preparation as flexibility: planning does not eliminate uncertainty, but it gives teams shared structure so they can adjust without collapsing.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It shifts from distributed authority to the preparation that makes distributed execution coherent. Its mechanism is planning for adaptability, not spontaneous improvisation alone.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter shows why chaos punishes teams that begin moving without enough preparation.
Then it argues that careful planning, clear intent, rehearsal, and contingencies raise mission success.
Then it shows that teams adapt better under pressure when they already share the shape of the plan.
