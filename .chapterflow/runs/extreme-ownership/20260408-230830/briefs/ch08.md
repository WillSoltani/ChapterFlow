Core Claim:
This chapter argues that leaders regain control in chaos by detaching enough to identify the highest-priority problem and act on it before trying to solve everything else. The chapter's force comes from sequencing under pressure: disorder becomes survivable when leaders stop reacting to every problem at once and restore order one decisive priority at a time.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It shifts from simplified clarity to triage and sequencing. Its mechanism is detachment plus priority selection under pressure, not simplicity alone.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter shows chaos multiplying faster than a leader can react emotionally.
Then it argues that leaders must detach, identify the most urgent problem, and act.
Then it shows that once the highest priority is handled, teams can move to the next problem with restored order.
