Chapter Promise:
This chapter will make the reader see leadership as the factor that matters most when the environment is confused, dangerous, and still unresolved.

Opening Move:
Open on Leif Babin inside a dark, fast-moving Ramadi combat situation where the next judgment call could hit either the enemy or his own people.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: drop into the Ramadi dilemma before the reader has a clean answer
- P2: show why the situation matters as a leadership test, not just a combat anecdote
- P3: widen from the scene to the claim that leadership quality determines team outcomes
- P4: explain what that claim does and does not mean
- P5: frame the rest of the book as practical answers to the opening dilemma
- P6: bridge to Chapter 2 by asking what the first concrete leadership move looks like after failure

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Leadership is not decoration around conditions. It is the leverage point that changes how a team reads confusion, responds to pressure, and recovers from danger.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must preserve the fact that leaders often have to act before certainty arrives. Hard depth should not pretend that strong leadership removes ambiguity.

Threshold Question:
What does real leadership look like when the situation is still confused and the cost of a bad call is already live?

Takeaway Count Lock:
- Easy: 3
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 5

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- Takeaway 1: leadership as leverage point
- Takeaway 2: chaos exposes leadership quality
- Takeaway 3: uncertainty does not excuse paralysis
- Takeaway 4: the book's principles are answers, not slogans
- Takeaway 5: ownership is the first narrowing move

Scenario Lesson Map:
