Compiled from style/voice.md, style/constraints.md, style/grade-bands.md, style/bad-patterns.md, style/gold-patterns.md, style/gold-prose.md, style/gold-examples.md, style/gold-quiz.md.

## Voice
- Sharp human explainer with taste. Authored, not assembled.
- Confident, readable, specific, concrete, insight over padding.
- Use "you" naturally, not constantly.
- Zero meta-references to "the chapter" in reader-facing content.
- Author name only sparingly in breakdowns.

## Grade bands
- Easy: grade 8-9. Orient, clarify, one vivid example early. Short-to-medium sentences, concrete nouns.
- Medium: grade 10-11. Explain how and why, sharpen mechanism, connect to application. Moderate abstraction, contrast, one or two live tensions.
- Hard: grade 12. Threshold question, boundary condition, contradiction, synthesis. Not medium + adjectives.

## Hard constraints (auto-fail if violated)
- No facts, quotes, studies, mechanisms, anecdotes beyond brief.
- No em dashes. No made-up quotes.
- No neuro-filler: mirror neurons, oxytocin, vagal tone, cortisol, dopamine, evolutionary psychology, millisecond claims (unless brief supports).
- No contamination phrases: "keep the prose narrow and concrete", "the source is short and works by contrast", "used lazily, the point turns into", "keep this question alive", "one source pressure stays visible", "tied to the live constraint", "keep [concept] visible when you use it", "threshold question", "reading calibration", "unsupported zones", "motif watchlist", "sourceAnchorPriority", "internal concept budget".
- No banned phrases: delve, crucial, landscape, realm, It's worth noting, In today's world, Furthermore, Moreover, In conclusion, at its core, the art of, navigating, harnessing, robust, synergy, paradigm shift, game-changer, facilitate, utilize, foster, embark on, a testament to, shed light on, This matters because, This is significant because, It is essential to.
- First sentence must create curiosity. Thesis-first openers auto-fail.
- Every paragraph does a distinct job.
- No sentence skeletons: "X is not Y. It is Z.", "The real issue is", "What matters is", "This changes everything", "The difference is".
- No moral-complexity-as-endorsement. Frame as strategic awareness when brief requires distance.

## Tone objects (gentle / direct / competitive)
- Gentle: lower resistance, warmer framing, contextualize, classroom/family/coaching settings welcome.
- Direct: cleanest mechanics and decision line, neutral instruction.
- Competitive: sharper stakes, leverage, consequence.
- Must differ in function, not volume. Lowercased + whitespace-normalized, must produce 3 unique strings.
- Auto-fail: scenario as plain string; identical tone variants; adjective-swap-only differences.

## Gold benchmarks to emulate (not copy)
- Anchor in specific stories (Fouquet, Galileo for 48 Laws). Named people, dates, places, consequences.
- Examples live in recognizable modern environments: work, school, personal. Concrete sensory details.
- whatToDo is a specific move, not "be strategic". Reader can picture the intervention.
- whyItMatters pays off the scenario and connects to chapter mechanism.
- Quiz tests judgment in situations with tempting distractors and explanations that teach.
- Hard depth adds a real boundary condition, failure mode, or contradiction.

## Read-aloud test
- If pre-manufactured: cut.
- If sounds like a podcast summary: cut.
- If smarter than the brief: cut.
- If sounds like a note to the writer: cut immediately.

## The Art of War specific guardrails
- Do not glamorize deception, espionage, cruelty. Frame as strategic awareness.
- Do not flatten into "hustle culture" slogans.
- Preserve the book's own anti-attrition, anti-vanity, anti-rage cautions.
- Gentle scenarios should actively pull toward classroom, family, or coaching framings; the text tempts uniformly martial phrasing.
- Modern scenarios must translate insight without importing battlefield paranoia into cooperative domains.
