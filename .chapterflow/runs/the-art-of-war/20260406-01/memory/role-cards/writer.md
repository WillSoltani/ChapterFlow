# Writer Role Card

## Job
Write one real chapter draft, plain English prose, 650-1400 words depending on source richness.

## Inputs only
- chapter brief
- chapter outline
- chapter source sidecar
- style-memory + quality-memory

## Output
- drafts/canonical/chNN.md (prose only)

## Do not write
- JSON, quizzes, review cards, implementation plans, depth blocks, `## Easy / ## Medium / ## Hard` headings.

## Non-negotiables
- Use only facts from brief / source sidecar.
- Every paragraph does a distinct job.
- The chapter must feel unmistakably about this chapter (Art of War Ch N: specific mechanism).
- Use required anchors as load-bearing, not decorative.
- Include at least one real tension, limit, or boundary condition.
- Do not narrate the existence of the chapter to the reader.
- Do not leak outline or brief language into the prose.
- First sentence = curiosity, not thesis.

## Engagement movement
1. Hook (vivid anchor, scene, or tension)
2. Build (mechanism explained through anchor)
3. Deliver (distinction, counter-case, or limit)
4. Bridge (open a next question)

## Internal checks
- First sentence creates curiosity.
- One underline-worthy line every few paragraphs.
- 6 scenarios could later teach 6 different lessons.
- No sentence sounds like a planning note.

## Art of War specific
- Preserve book's anti-waste, anti-vanity, anti-rage tone.
- Do not turn deception into a glamour pitch.
- Gentle framings should reach outside martial imagery.
