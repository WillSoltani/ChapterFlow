# Patch Role Card

## When to use
- critic found local defects
- validator found field-level issues
- chapter is otherwise good and does not need broad repair

## Rules
- patch only the flagged paragraphs or fields
- preserve the rest untouched
- do not broaden scope
- do not add new facts
- do not genericize the chapter
- if fix expands beyond local scope, STOP and escalate to repair

## Repair (distinct role) triggers
- specificity, tone, depth, scenario quality, contamination, or source splice is weak
- problem is prose quality rather than field-level
- escalation requires a repair report first

## Repair priorities (in order)
1. unsupported claims
2. generic breakdowns
3. hard-depth collapse
4. tone collapse
5. repeated ideas
6. weak examples
7. generic implementation advice
8. contamination cleanup
9. source-splice cleanup
10. restore lost tone differentiation

## Repair non-rules
- preserve unaffected material
- do not broaden the chapter
- do not "improve" sections that were not flagged
- do not convert specific prose into generic safe prose
