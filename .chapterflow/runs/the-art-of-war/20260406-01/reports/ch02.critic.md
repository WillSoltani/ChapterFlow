No two paragraphs share a job.

## Easy-mode convertibility
Good. The four core moves (tempo as accumulating cost, the compounding chain, the forage rule, the governance frame) translate cleanly into grade 8-9 language without losing their shape.

## Meta-distance score
Strong. No "the chapter" or "the book" or "this chapter" references in the edited draft. Author name "Sun Tzu" appears five times, all in breakdown-support positions consistent with the sparing-use allowance for a chapter specifically about one author's argument.

## Hard-edge preservation
The tension between "finish quickly" and "stupid haste is not cleverness" is preserved in P5 and extended to the ethical weight question at the bridge. The threshold question the outline specified (when does strategic patience become moral failure?) survives into the edited draft at the governance reframing.

## Conceptual repetition risk
Low. The thesis is stated once in P1-P2, the mechanism is developed once in P3, the operational answer once in P4, the distinction once in P5, the governance reading once in P6.

## Weakest paragraph
Paragraph 3. The staccato sequence of short sentences about dulled weapons, damped morale, and dropping strength is the one spot where the voice risks sounding pre-manufactured. It works rhetorically for a compounding-chain structure, but if the converter needs sharper takeaway language for the compounding-cost concept, this paragraph is the right place to mine for it.

## Strongest sentence
"The speed looks identical from outside. The outcomes are not."

This pair captures the entire distinction between stupid haste and decisive finishing in thirteen words. The converter should consider building hard-depth language around this observation.

## Contamination or source-splice suspicion
None. The three approved quotes (no prolonged warfare benefit, bring war material from home but forage on the enemy, victory not lengthy campaigns) are referenced in paraphrase or near-quote form only.

The edited draft clears the prose gate. Proceed to Phase 5 conversion. The slightly flat cadence in P3 does not require a local patch; it is within the acceptable range for the 1/2 Prose Quality score.
