Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader understands that self-justification is the everyday mechanism that lets harmful or foolish conduct stay emotionally livable from the inside.

Opening Move:
Start with the familiar public sentence "mistakes were made" and the queasy feeling of hearing damage named without anybody stepping forward to own it.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 non-apology opening
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P2-P4 puzzle of hypocrites and villains living with themselves
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P4-P6 self-justification versus lying, then bridge to dissonance

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: anchor, public non-apology and missing owner

- P4: distinction, self-justification versus deliberate lying
- P5: implication, why this makes error sticky and correction hard

- P7 if needed: limit, this mechanism explains ordinary minds but does not excuse harm

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The key distinction is between deceiving other people and rearranging your own story so you can still feel like a decent person.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must ask what happens when identity protection becomes more important than accuracy, and whether that distortion recruits memory and morality rather than just words.

Threshold Question:
When the self feels threatened, where does honest explanation end and self-protective fiction begin?
