Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader understands how self-justification turns ordinary marital conflict into recurring blame, revised memory, and failing repair.

Opening Move:
Open with a small domestic argument where both partners feel obviously provoked and neither one feels like the aggressor.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 self-serving attribution split
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 grievance accumulation and argument escalation
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 apology, letting go, and repair conditions

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: anchor, small conflict with two innocent stories
- P2: mechanism, situational excuse for self and character blame for partner
- P3: anchor, repeated fights turning into stored grievance
- P4: implication, arguments shifting from problem-solving to prosecution
- P5: anchor, apology and pain as possible repair openings
- P6: bridge, why unresolved grievance scales into larger rifts and group conflict
- P7 if needed: limit, the chapter critiques defended blame rather than asking injured people to deny pain

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter's distinct mechanism is that relationship conflict hardens when each partner gives themselves situational mercy and the other partner dispositional indictment.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must ask why justified anger can still become a destructive self-story, and why giving up accusation can feel like giving up dignity.

Threshold Question:
When an argument has become part of who each partner believes they are in the relationship, what could still make either side loosen the story?
