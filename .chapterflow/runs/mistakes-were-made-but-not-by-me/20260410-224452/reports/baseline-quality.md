# Baseline Quality Report

Baseline chapters:
- Chapter 1: `Knaves, Fools, Villains, and Hypocrites: How Do They Live with Themselves?`
- Chapter 2: `Cognitive Dissonance: The Engine of Self-justification`

Shared quality floor:
- both chapters open from live tension instead of abstract thesis
- both chapters keep the mechanism human and morally serious
- both chapters preserve a real threshold question for hard depth
- both chapters keep examples socially realistic and non-generic
- both chapters passed critic gate at 11/12 and cleared chapter validation

Chapter 1 strengths:
- strong emotional doorway into the book's problem
- clean distinction between lying and self-justification
- good restraint under thinner source support

Chapter 2 strengths:
- stronger conceptual density with load-bearing anchors
- sharper explanation of post-decision defense and confirmation bias
- clearer bridge into later blind-spot material

Quality floor for later waves:
- chapter-specific anchor pressure must stay visible in every draft
- hard depth must add a real boundary or contradiction, not longer medium
- support surfaces must avoid meta phrasing and template tails
- confidence or certainty claims must stay analytically qualified rather than sloganized

Risk watch for later waves:
- denser mechanism chapters may tempt generic anti-bias prose
- conflict chapters may overreach beyond frozen support if examples are not kept narrow
