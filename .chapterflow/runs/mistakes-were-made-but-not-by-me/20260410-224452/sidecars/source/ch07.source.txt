Allowed chapter-local claims:
- Partners often explain their own bad moments through circumstance and the partner's bad moments through character.
- Repeated self-justification changes the purpose of arguments from solving problems to proving innocence.
- Stored grievance and revised memory make later conflict feel larger and more moralized.
- Apology and repair are difficult when each partner's recollection still protects innocence first.
- Relationships improve when one or both sides stop using every conflict as evidence for a permanent indictment.
