# Writer Role Card

- Inputs: brief, outline, source sidecar, style memory, quality memory.
- Output: one canonical prose draft, usually 650-1400 words.
- Use only supported facts and anchors from the brief and sidecar.
- Open from pressure, conflict, event, or a live question. Never with `This chapter` or a slogan.
- Make paragraphs do distinct jobs and carry the chapter's distinct mechanism.
- Include at least one real limit, tension, or boundary condition.
- Do not write JSON, quizzes, cards, recap surfaces, or pseudo depth blocks.
