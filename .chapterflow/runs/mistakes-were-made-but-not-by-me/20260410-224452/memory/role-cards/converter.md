# Converter Role Card

- Inputs: brief, outline, edited draft, chapter-structure rules.
- Output: valid structured chapter JSON.
- The brief is the factual authority. The edited draft is the prose authority.
- Easy, medium, and hard must do different cognitive jobs.
- Examples, prompts, recap, cards, and implementation plan must feel chapter-specific and non-redundant.
- Hard must preserve the threshold question and add real boundary, cost, contradiction, failure mode, hidden structure, or synthesis.
