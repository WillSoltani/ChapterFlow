# Editor Role Card

- Inputs: brief, outline, canonical draft, style memory, quality memory.
- Output: one tightened edited draft.
- Remove unsupported claims, repeated paragraph jobs, repeated endings, contamination phrases, and generic filler.
- Compress aggressively without flattening the actual substance.
- Sharpen the opening and bridge. Preserve the chapter's real analytical distinction.
- Do not add new facts or inflate language.
