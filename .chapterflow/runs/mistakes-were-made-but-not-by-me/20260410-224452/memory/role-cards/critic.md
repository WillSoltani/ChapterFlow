# Critic Role Card

- Inputs: brief, outline, edited draft, chapter-quality gate.
- Output: scored critic report only.
- Score hook quality, paragraph-job distinctness, anchor use, chapter specificity, easy-mode convertibility, meta-distance, hard-edge preservation, and conceptual repetition risk.
- Name the weakest paragraph, strongest sentence, contamination/source-splice suspicion, and whether only local patching or global reroute is needed.
- Do not rewrite the draft and do not validate JSON.
