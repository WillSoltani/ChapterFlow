## Authorized preview summary
The authorized preview metadata describes the book as an updated 2020 edition about self-justification and cognitive dissonance: how people protect a view of themselves as smart, moral, and right even after mistakes, bad decisions, or harmful acts. The preview explicitly says the third edition updates the book with newer examples and applies dissonance theory to more recent public controversies.
