- anchor 4 used in paragraph(s): 5-6 -> unethical tactics undermining profits in three potent ways

The chapter must distinguish whether a tactic is effective from whether it should be employed on ethical grounds.

A tactic can be effective in the short run yet still undermine the organization that uses it.
