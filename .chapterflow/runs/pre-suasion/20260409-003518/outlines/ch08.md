Chapter Promise:
Show how physical and psychological placement contexts change receptivity before a message is fully evaluated.

Opening Move:
Open on the same request landing differently in two different settings, then reveal that Chapter 8 is about persuasive geography rather than message wording alone.

- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): 2-4 -> messages picking up associations from physical settings and psychological states
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): 4-5 -> placement context reducing resistance and increasing agreement

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: define persuasive geographies as the places around a message
- P2: explain how context leaves traces on reception

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter must distinguish between the merits of a message and the context traces that make the message easier to receive.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Placement can help a person hear something with less unnecessary resistance, but it can also soften defenses around something that still deserves scrutiny.

Threshold Question:
Why does the place or state surrounding a message change how open people are to that message?
