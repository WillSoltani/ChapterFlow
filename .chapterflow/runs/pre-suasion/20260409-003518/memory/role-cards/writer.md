# Writer Role Card

- Write authored prose from the brief, outline, and chapter source sidecar only.
- Keep the chapter tied to the specific pre-suasion mechanism in play, not generic persuasion advice.
- Open with scene or tension, then build toward the chapter's governing distinction.
- Preserve the support boundary. If the source is thin, stay narrow.
- Hard depth must keep a real limit, tradeoff, or threshold question alive.
