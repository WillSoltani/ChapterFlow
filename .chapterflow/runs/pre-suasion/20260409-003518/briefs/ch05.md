Core Claim:
Chapter 5 argues that certain cues seize attention with unusual force. The chapter's job is to identify attractors that pull the spotlight toward themselves before judgment even begins.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Chapter 4 explained how focality can become causal judgment. Chapter 5 moves one step earlier by asking what naturally captures focality in the first place.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author claims that attention is not random because some cues are built to attract it.
Then he argues that survival-linked, goal-fitting, and standout cues pull the mind fast.
Then he shows why this matters for persuasion before a message is evaluated.
Then he preserves a limit: attention capture is not the same as truth or merit.
