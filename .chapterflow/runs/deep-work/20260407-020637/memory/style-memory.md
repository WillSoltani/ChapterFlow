# Style Memory — Deep Work

Operating cheat sheet for writer, editor, and converter. Distilled from the v13 pack style files plus this run's source limits.

## Voice
Concrete, calm, and high-agency. Explain the mechanism without sounding like hustle culture, digital panic, or monk cosplay. Use modern rooms, devices, calendars, and stakes.

## Book-specific posture
- This book argues that focused, cognitively demanding work is valuable, rare, trainable, and meaningful.
- Keep the contrast between deep work and shallow work visible.
- Favor operational distinctions over motivational slogans.
- The book is about designing time, attention, and obligations, not performing ascetic purity.

## Non-negotiables
- Use only claims supported by the brief and source sidecar.
- No em dashes.
- No fake neuroscience, productivity folklore, or page-specific claims not visible in the frozen bundle.
- No raw source splice into reader-facing prose.
- No contamination phrases, validator language, or prompt leakage.

## Banned phrasing
- Avoid thesis-first openings such as "In this chapter" or "The author argues."
- Avoid generic self-help filler like "unlock your potential," "be the best version of yourself," and "crush your goals."
- Avoid treating depth as moral superiority over other people.

## Depth contract
- Easy: one clean frame, one live contrast, one practical implication.
- Medium: mechanism plus application, with one boundary or tradeoff.
- Hard: preserve the unresolved tension, failure mode, or strategic limit.

## Tone differentiation
- Gentle: lowers resistance and clarifies the path.
- Direct: names the mechanism cleanly.
- Competitive: raises cost and consequence without becoming theatrical.

## Book-specific craft checks
- Introduction should define deep work and shallow work without sounding dictionary-flat.
- Idea chapters must keep one distinct claim each: valuable, rare, meaningful.
- Rule chapters must stay actionable and not drift into generic focus advice.
- Conclusion must synthesize the deep life pitch without pretending a new framework appears.
