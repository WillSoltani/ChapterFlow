# Deep Work Is Valuable

## Core Sections
1. The chapter ties depth to two high-value abilities: learning hard things and producing strong work.
2. Deep work speeds learning because uninterrupted attention holds difficult structures together longer.
3. Output quality improves when a person can stay with a demanding problem past the first pass.
4. Faster learning improves what you can make, and stronger work raises the value of what you have learned.
5. Interruption is more than a time cost. It also degrades two of the most valuable capacities in knowledge work.

## Activation
Audit one domain where you need difficult learning or high-quality output. What would deeper work change there?
