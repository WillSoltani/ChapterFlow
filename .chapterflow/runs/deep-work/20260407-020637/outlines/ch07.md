# Quit Social Media

## Core Sections
1. The chapter replaces default adoption with a craftsman test tied to what matters most.
2. The chapter asks for meaningful contribution, not marginal usefulness.
3. The chapter insists on counting tool costs honestly instead of pretending only benefits matter.
4. Strategic subtraction is intelligent selectivity, not panic.
5. The chapter treats digital minimalism as a consequence of priorities rather than as an aesthetic.

## Activation
Identify one real place where digital tools that keep their place by default rather than by contribution is shaping your schedule or attention.
