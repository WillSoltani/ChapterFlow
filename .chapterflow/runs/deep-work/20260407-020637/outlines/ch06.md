# Embrace Boredom

## Core Sections
1. Boredom tolerance is part of the skill of focus.
2. Frequent quick stimulation makes patience with demanding work weaker.
3. Pre-decided limits beat live negotiation with impulses.
4. Re-centering is training, not proof of failure.
5. The chapter wants agency over attention rather than purity theater.

## Activation
Identify one real place where the reflex to escape boredom with quick stimulation is shaping your schedule or attention.
