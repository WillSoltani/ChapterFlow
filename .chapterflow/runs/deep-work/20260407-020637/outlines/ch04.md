# Deep Work Is Meaningful

## Core Sections
1. Meaning rises when effort is fully engaged against something demanding.
2. Where attention goes, the felt texture of life follows.
3. A craftsman standard makes work more satisfying from within.
4. Shallow attention interrupts the state where meaningful work is most likely to emerge.
5. The chapter turns meaning into a practice of sustained attention.

## Activation
Identify one real place where the difference between fragmented effort and fully engaged work is shaping your schedule or attention.
