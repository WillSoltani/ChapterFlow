# Deep Work Is Rare

## Core Sections
1. Organizations measure responsiveness more easily than depth.
2. Interruption-heavy norms keep slicing work into shallow fragments.
3. Many workplaces praise visible activity instead of harder contribution.
4. Unnamed and unprotected depth loses calendar territory fast.
5. Scarcity turns depth into leverage for the worker who defends it.

## Activation
Identify one real place where workplace norms that reward visibility faster than depth is shaping your schedule or attention.
