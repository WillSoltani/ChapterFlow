# Drain the Shallows

## Core Sections
1. The chapter distinguishes unavoidable shallow work from unbounded shallow sprawl.
2. Honest estimation is a control mechanism for shallow work.
3. Planning exposes how quickly shallow obligations can overfill the day.
4. Communication hygiene is part of defending depth, not a side issue.
5. The chapter asks the reader to protect depth by making sacrifices visible.

## Activation
Identify one real place where shallow obligations that keep spreading because they are left vague is shaping your schedule or attention.
