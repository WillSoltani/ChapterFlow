Core Claim:
Book Two answers Book One's diagnosis with a posture: turning pro. Its job is to define professionalism as disciplined return to the work without waiting for mood, permission, or perfect conditions. The section must show that turning pro is not glamour, confidence theater, or hustle branding. It is a daily acceptance of labor, repetition, and impersonal commitment to the calling.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the posture chapter. It should feel less diagnostic than Book One and less spiritual than Book Three. Its distinct pressure is the shift from naming Resistance to outworking it through routine, standards, and depersonalized commitment.

Author Logic Chain:
First the section moves from enemy recognition to the need for a way of working that does not bargain with Resistance.
Then it contrasts amateur waiting with professional return.
Then it sharpens the idea through routine, patience, and indifference to mood.
Finally it lands on a limit: turning pro is stronger than inspiration, but it can still harden into sterile grind if it loses contact with calling.
