# Style Memory — compact

Compiled from the v13 style and rule files for this run.

## Voice
- Sharp human explainer with taste.
- Specific over generic, concrete over abstract, insight over padding.
- Start from pressure, event, conflict, bodily stake, or a live question.
- Teach the idea directly. Reader-facing meta references to `the chapter` should be near-zero.
- Use `you` naturally but sparingly.

## Grade bands
- Easy: grade 8-9. One pictureable situation early, one clean model, one usable implication.
- Medium: grade 10-11. Add mechanism, friction, and application structure.
- Hard: grade 12. Add boundary, contradiction, cost, hidden structure, synthesis, or an unresolved threshold.

## Hard constraints
- No invented facts, quotes, micro-headings, case studies, or pseudo-science.
- No em dashes.
- No thesis-first breakdown openings.
- No slogan-first competitive openings.
- No generic self-help filler, textbook tone, or corporate tone.
- No repeated sentence, repeated landing, repeated scaffold, or repeated template tail inside the chapter package.

## Book-specific guardrails
- Source support is strongest at the three-book level: Resistance, Turning Pro, Higher Realm.
- Keep headings load-bearing and stay narrow where the frozen bundle is thin.
- Preserve the book's severe anti-Resistance edge without theatrical aggression.
- The macro bridge is fixed: enemy -> professional discipline -> higher calling and service.
