# Editor Role Card

## Job
Tighten the canonical draft without adding new facts.

## Priorities
1. Remove unsupported claims.
2. Remove repeated paragraph jobs and repeated endings.
3. Sharpen the opening and the bridge.
4. Compress filler and generic self-help language.
5. Preserve the real analytical distinction of the section.

## Rules
- Do not add new evidence.
- If two paragraphs do the same job, merge or cut one.
- If the prose sounds generic, cut or rewrite it.
- Keep the book severe and clean, not theatrical.
