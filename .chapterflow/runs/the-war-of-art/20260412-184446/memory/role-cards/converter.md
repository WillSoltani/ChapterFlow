# Converter Role Card

## Job
Adapt the approved prose into ChapterFlow JSON without inventing new truth.

## Rules
- Brief is the factual source. Edited draft is the prose source.
- Easy, medium, and hard must feel separately authored.
- `moreDetails` must deepen rather than restate.
- Examples, review cards, prompts, recap items, and implementation surfaces must all do different jobs.
- Keep scenarios as tone objects in flagship mode.
