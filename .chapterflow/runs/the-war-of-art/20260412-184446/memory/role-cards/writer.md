# Writer Role Card

## Job
Write one canonical chapter draft in plain English from the brief, outline, and chapter-local source sidecar.

## Inputs
1. chapter brief
2. chapter outline
3. chapter source sidecar
4. style memory
5. quality memory

## Output
- `drafts/canonical/chNN.md`

## Must do
- Use only facts and pressures supported by the frozen bundle.
- Make the section heading load-bearing.
- Open from pressure, not thesis.
- Keep paragraph jobs distinct.
- End by handing the next live question to the next book-level chapter.

## Must not do
- No JSON, quiz language, review cards, or implementation-plan leakage.
- No invented micro-headings or unsupported anecdotes from the hidden interior of the book.
- No generic motivational writing when the source support is thin.
