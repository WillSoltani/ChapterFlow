Chapter Promise:
The reader will understand that discipline is not the final answer; the work becomes steadier and cleaner when it is received as service to something larger than ego.

Opening Move:
Open on someone who has already learned routine but still feels trapped by self-display, approval hunger, or the need to make the work prove personal worth.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): higher realm named in P2 and clarified in P3
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): service versus ego in P3 and P4
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): faithful delivery / stewardship in P5 and P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: anchor. A concrete scene where disciplined labor is still being distorted by vanity or fear of approval.
- P2: mechanism. Define the move beyond Resistance into a higher or larger frame.
- P3: implication. Contrast ego-driven work with work treated as service.
- P4: tension. Show how this larger frame steadies the worker against praise, fear, or self-display.
- P5: limit. Preserve craft and discipline while refusing vague mysticism or chosenness theater.
- P6: bridge/close. Release the worker from self-worship and close the book through stewardship language.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Turning pro answers whether the work happens. The higher realm answers what the work is for and what authority it serves.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The hard variant must preserve the risk that higher-realm language can become vanity theater, passivity, or vague spiritual inflation if it loses contact with faithful craft.

Threshold Question:
How can a person treat work as received or entrusted without becoming mystical, self-important, or irresponsible about standards?
