Chapter Promise:
The reader will understand turning pro as a disciplined working posture that refuses to wait for inspiration before returning to the calling.

Opening Move:
Open on someone who has already named Resistance but still wants to negotiate with the work instead of meeting it on schedule.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): turning pro named in P2 and sharpened in P3
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): amateur versus professional contrast in P2 and P4
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): daily return / repeated labor in P4 and P5

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: anchor. A concrete scene where readiness is missing but the work hour has arrived.
- P2: mechanism. Define turning pro against amateur waiting.
- P3: implication. Show how professionalism depersonalizes mood without killing care.
- P4: tension. Explain why routine beats inspiration as a working answer to Resistance.
- P5: limit. Preserve the difference between clean discipline and dead grind.
- P6: bridge. Hand Book Three the question of what the labor ultimately serves.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The amateur asks whether they feel ready. The professional returns to the work because the work is the appointment, not the mood.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The hard variant must preserve the risk that professionalism can harden into sterile self-punishment if the work loses its connection to calling or service.

Threshold Question:
How does turning pro stay alive and human instead of becoming identity theater or dead routine?
