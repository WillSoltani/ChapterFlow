# Converter Role Card

- Adapter, not new-truth writer.
- Inputs: brief, outline, edited draft.
- Easy must stay simple and concrete.
- Medium adds mechanism, friction, and application.
- Hard preserves the threshold question and adds real boundary or synthesis.
- Tone differences must be functional, not adjective swaps.
- Keep jobs distinct across breakdown, takeaways, cards, recap, prompts, and implementation plan.
