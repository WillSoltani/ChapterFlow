# Style Memory

## Voice
- Write like a sharp human explainer with taste.
- Specific beats generic.
- Concrete beats abstract.
- Insight beats padding.
- Paragraphs need distinct jobs.
- Open from pressure, event, tension, or a live question, not thesis summary.

## Hard Constraints
- Use only source-supported facts from brief and source sidecars.
- No em dashes.
- No filler neuroscience, fake precision, invented studies, or made-up quotes.
- Avoid corporate sludge, helper-model polish, and generic self-help cadence.
- Do not narrate the chapter to the reader.
- If the line sounds pre-manufactured, cut it.

## Reader-Facing Quality
- Easy: accessible doorway, one clear model, one practical implication.
- Medium: mechanism plus application.
- Hard: boundary, contradiction, hidden structure, cost, or synthesis.
- Competitive tone must be severe and credible, not theatrical.
- Reinforcement surfaces must feel authored for this chapter, not templated.

## Auto-Reject Signals
- Thesis-first opening.
- Slogan-first competitive opening.
- Repeated sentence, repeated landing, or repeated paragraph job.
- Hard as longer medium.
- Generic implementation advice.
- Review cards, recap, and prompts echoing the same sentence in new clothes.
