Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter's mechanism is that weak people judgment usually comes less from missing data than from refusing to face what the role truly needs and what the person can truly do now.

The chapter has to preserve that honesty about fit need not become brutality. Hard depth should ask how leaders tell the truth about role mismatch while still taking development seriously where development is plausible.
