Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should be able to name the seven essential behaviors, explain why they function together as an execution system, and see why self-knowledge is part of execution rather than an optional leadership trait.

Opening Move:
Open on a leader who thinks the problem is motivation until a closer look shows the real breakdown is weak knowledge, vague priorities, softened truth, and missing follow-through.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 execution turns from concept into leader behavior
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P5 the outward behavior cluster of realism, priorities, follow-through, and rewarding doers
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P6-P7 coaching, self-knowledge, and emotional fortitude as the sustaining inner standard

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: frame, why a behavior chapter has to follow the diagnostic opening
- P2: overview, the seven behaviors as one linked standard rather than a loose list
- P3: knowing people and business plus insisting on realism
- P4: clear goals, priorities, and follow-through
- P5: rewarding doers and the visible signals this sends
- P6: coaching as capability expansion rather than comfort theater
- P7: know yourself, emotional fortitude, authenticity, self-awareness, self-mastery, humility
- P8 if needed: tension, realism without brittle control and the bridge into culture

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter's mechanism is that execution quality depends on linked behaviors, not isolated virtues. Knowledge without realism gets sentimental. Priorities without follow-through stay decorative. Coaching without accountability softens standards. Self-knowledge without outward discipline becomes private insight with no operating effect.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter has to preserve that strong execution leadership is demanding without collapsing into command-and-control caricature. Hard depth should ask how leaders stay candid, demanding, and emotionally steady without training the organization to hide truth from them.

Threshold Question:
What behaviors make people bring reality to a leader early instead of editing it for safety or convenience?
