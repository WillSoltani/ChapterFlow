Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should be able to explain how a disciplined people process links talent, succession, evaluation, and candid dialogue to business execution.

Opening Move:
Open on a team that thinks it has enough talent until one key role becomes shaky and reveals that the pipeline is thinner than the language around it.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 people linked to strategy and operations
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 pipeline, assessment, succession depth, retention risk
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 nonperformers, business linkage, candid dialogue

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: frame, why people process matters beyond isolated people calls
- P2: mechanism, linkage to strategy and operations
- P3: pipeline and assessment discipline
- P4: succession depth and retention risk
- P5: dealing with nonperformers and linking HR to business results
- P6: candid dialogue as live ammo and the bridge into strategy

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The people process matters only when it changes real deployment, development, and succession decisions. A process that stores opinions without changing the business becomes theater.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter has to preserve that strong process can still become bureaucracy if candor goes soft. Hard depth should ask how leaders keep the people process both systematic and alive enough to face uncomfortable talent truths.

Threshold Question:
What proves that a people review is shaping execution rather than merely documenting people?

Takeaway Count Lock:
- Easy: 3 (always)
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 5

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- Takeaway 1: people must be linked to strategy and operations
- Takeaway 2: pipeline depth reduces scramble and wishful succession
- Takeaway 3: succession depth and retention risk expose fragility
- Takeaway 4: nonperformance must be faced inside the process
- Takeaway 5: candid dialogue keeps the process alive instead of ceremonial

Scenario Lesson Map:
