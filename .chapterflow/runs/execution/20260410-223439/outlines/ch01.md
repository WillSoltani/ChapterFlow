Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand that execution is the missing discipline between ambition and result, and that leaders create the gap when they treat follow-through as someone else's problem.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Execution is not the tail end of strategy. It is the discipline that turns strategy into reality by tying realism, ownership, and follow-through together.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Execution is indispensable, but disciplined follow-through cannot save a strategy built on bad assumptions. The deeper question is whether organizations confuse commitment to execution with refusal to revisit flawed premises.

Threshold Question:
How do you stay fiercely committed to execution without becoming fiercely committed to a bad plan?
