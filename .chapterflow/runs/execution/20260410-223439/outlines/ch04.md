Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should be able to explain how culture becomes operational through rewards, dialogue, tolerated behavior, and repeated management signals.

Opening Move:
Open on a team that says it values candor, then reveal that the real culture is defined by what gets rewarded and what leaders let slide.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 culture as operational rather than atmospheric
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 rewards, social software, and robust dialogue
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 tolerated behavior and the bridge into people decisions

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: frame, culture is not slogans but repeated lessons
- P2: mechanism, operationalizing culture through leader signals
- P3: linking rewards to performance
- P4: social software and robust dialogue as execution mechanisms
- P5: leaders get the behavior they exhibit and tolerate
- P6: tension, dialogue without aligned rewards becomes theater
- P7 if needed: bridge toward Chapter 5

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Culture is the accumulated operating consequence of what leaders reward, inspect, permit, and model. Dialogue matters only when it carries truth into a system whose incentives and tolerated behavior can actually absorb it.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter has to preserve that robust dialogue can be real without becoming performative. Hard depth should ask how leaders keep candor linked to consequence rather than staging brave conversations that change nothing.

Threshold Question:
If a team's stated values and its repeated incentives conflict, which one is teaching the real culture?
