Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should be able to explain how a serious strategy review pressures a plan through competition, capability, focus, linkage, and follow-through rather than treating the meeting as a presentation.

Opening Move:
Open on a review session that looks organized until one real question exposes that the room has been admiring the plan more than testing it.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 strategy review as live management practice, not presentation theater
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 competition knowledge, capability, focus, and idea choice
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 linkage to people and operations plus follow-through

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: frame, why strategy review fails when it becomes a presentation ritual
- P2: mechanism, the kinds of questions leaders must raise
- P3: competition knowledge and organizational capability
- P4: focus versus scatter and choosing the right ideas
- P5: linkages with people and operations
- P6: follow-through as the proof that the review mattered

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The review is real only when leaders use it to test strategy and sharpen action rather than to admire the plan's polish.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter has to preserve that review can become performance theater even in disciplined organizations if the questions do not bite and nothing changes afterward.

Threshold Question:
What proves that a strategy review is testing the plan rather than protecting it?

Takeaway Count Lock:
- Easy: 3 (always)
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 5

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- Takeaway 1: review is a live pressure test, not a presentation
- Takeaway 2: competition knowledge and capability matter together
- Takeaway 3: focus and idea choice reveal strategic discipline
- Takeaway 4: people and operations linkages must be explicit
- Takeaway 5: follow-through is the proof that the review was real
