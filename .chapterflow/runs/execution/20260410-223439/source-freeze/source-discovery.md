# Source Discovery

## Objective
Freeze a lawful, chapter-usable source bundle for `Execution: The Discipline of Getting Things Done` before any chapter work begins.

## Discovery Result
- No public-domain full text was found.
- A strong authorized bundle exists through the Penguin Random House product page, Google Books preview, and a CIO-hosted excerpt tied directly to the book.
- Library and retailer metadata provide a stable chapter map and edition cross-checks.

## Edition Decision
- Locked edition: `Execution: The Discipline of Getting Things Done`, Crown Business, 1st ed., 2002.
- Why it won: it is the canonical hardcover edition with the primary ISBN and stable chapter sequence.
- Why no user question was needed: later 2009/2011 English editions preserve the chapter-level structure relevant to this run.

## Frozen Bundle Components
1. Publisher metadata and synopsis from Penguin Random House.
2. Authorized Google Books preview metadata and selected contents lines.
3. Full chapter map from WorldCat and Ingram-supplied retailer metadata.
4. Secondary subheading map from the contents digest at `rlaexp.com`, used narrowly for chapter briefs and outlines.
5. Chapter 6 excerpt support from CIO.
6. Secondary confirmation of chapter 3's seven behaviors from Porchlight.

## Sufficiency Assessment
- Strong enough for chapter order, chapter intent, and narrow paraphrase-first exposition.
- Strongest chapters by source richness: chapters 3, 6, 7, 8, 9.
- Thinner chapters: chapter 1, chapter 2, and chapter 10 rely more on overview-level support and should stay tighter.

## Guardrails
- Do not claim episode-level anecdotes unless supported by the frozen bundle.
- Do not use pirate mirrors or unauthorized reposts.
- Use exact quotes only when directly supported by frozen authorized preview or excerpt text.
- Prefer narrower claims over synthetic management folklore.
