Useable chapter pressure:
- review has to pressure the plan instead of protecting it
- competition knowledge and capability matter inside the review itself
- focus and idea choice reveal whether the strategy is disciplined or scattered
- follow-through is part of the review's proof, not a separate afterthought

Narrow support limits:
- Do not invent review-meeting dialogue, executive scenes, or corporate anecdotes.
- Avoid unsupported facilitation frameworks or meeting templates.
- Keep the chapter on question quality, linkage, and follow-through.
