Core Claim:
Execution needs a disciplined people process that links talent decisions to strategy and operations. This chapter shows that a serious people process evaluates individuals accurately and in depth, develops the leadership pipeline, supports succession, and forces candid dialogue about performers and nonperformers. Its pressure point is that talent management becomes real only when it is tied to business results and live decisions rather than treated as a ceremonial HR exercise.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Chapter 5 focused on hard individual judgments about fit. Chapter 6 turns those judgments into an institutional process. Its distinct job is to show how leadership talent gets identified, developed, reviewed, and linked to strategy and operations over time.

Author Logic Chain:

Then the author develops the leadership pipeline and assessment summaries.
Then the author addresses succession depth, retention risk, and talent review.
Then the author forces candid dialogue about nonperformers and business-linked HR.
