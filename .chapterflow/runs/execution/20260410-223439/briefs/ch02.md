Core Claim:
Chapter 2 shows what the execution gap looks like in practice by contrasting organizations that carry plans into reality with organizations that do not. The chapter uses named cases, including EDS on the positive side and Xerox and Lucent on the negative side, to argue that execution differences are visible in leader behavior, realism, and follow-through long before final results collapse. It also implies that the failure is not mysterious. Leaders often widen the gap by delegating responsibility they still own.
