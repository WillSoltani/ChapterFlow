# Book Skeleton — The 33 Strategies of War

## Metadata

- **Title:** The 33 Strategies of War
- **Author:** Robert Greene
- **Published:** 2006 (First Edition)
- **Book ID:** the-33-strategies-of-war
- **Variant family:** EMH (Easy / Medium / Hard)
- **Output profile:** flagship_v4_compatible
- **Chapter range:** 1–10 (consolidated from Greene's 33 strategies; see source `chapter-map.md`)
- **Source provenance:** Paraphrased summaries and structural outlines only. No verbatim text. Greene's signature historical case studies (Napoleon Austerlitz, Hannibal Cannae, Sun Tzu, Mao, Patton, Rommel, Lawrence of Arabia, the Spartans, Caesar, Khrushchev, etc.) are **excluded** from reuse. All examples are invented fresh as modern professional / school / personal scenarios.

## Premium-routing candidates

- **Ch1** — opener, sets register and contract; must be top-tier.
- **Ch9** — densest single chapter (5 strategies); risk of becoming a list.
- **Ch10** — closing, ethical ceiling, meta-frame; can collapse into either preachiness or false neutrality.

## Thin-chapter risk notes

- **Ch3** and **Ch4** are 2-strategy chapters and may feel narrow. Compensate with sharper anchor stories and more decisive boundary conditions.
- **Ch8** is also 2-strategy; same compensation.
- None of the chapters is structurally thin enough to require defer/skip.

## Moral complexity flags

- **Ch9 and Ch10** carry the bulk of the ethical load. Both must explicitly name the line between strategic framing of real facts and fabrication / coercion every single time the relevant move is taught.
- **Ch7** carries lower but real moral load (indirection cluster); name the legitimacy of opposed interests.
- **Ch2** must preserve Greene's warning that manufactured crusades are detected and backfire.
- **Ch1** must be careful **not** to drift into self-help register; presence of mind is strategic discipline, not mindfulness.

## Example rotation plan

Six canonical formats per chapter, each format used exactly once: `decision_point`, `dialogue`, `dilemma`, `cold_open`, `case_in_progress`, `aftermath`. Six ending types per chapter, each used exactly once. Category mix per chapter: 2 work / 2 school / 2 personal.

**Cross-book name ledger plan** (no name reuse across chapters by default; track in continuity-state):
- Chapter 1 names already reserved (see brief): Maya, Devon, Ruth, Aiden, Elena, Priya. (Note: "Maya" is also a gold-examples benchmark name from another book — banned for this run to avoid pattern fatigue. Real Ch1 names will be: Imani, Devon, Ruth, Aiden, Elena, Priya.)
- Avoid all gold-benchmark anchor names from any prior gold file: Fouquet, Louis XIV, Galileo, Medici, Daria, Raj, Marcus, Jordan, Gloria, Ethan, Harding.

## School-setting plan

Rotate K–12, undergrad seminar, grad school, and professional certification settings across chapters. Ch1 uses an undergrad / pre-professional setting and a graduate seminar. Vary department (history, engineering, medical school, law) across the book to keep school scenarios from feeling templated.

## Vocabulary watchlist

These appear naturally in this book and must not become tics:
- "strategic" — limit to once per breakdown depth max.
- "the operator" — Greene's term, fine, but avoid more than 3 uses per breakdown.
- "the move" — vary with "the play", "the choice", "the discipline".
- "leverage" — easily inflates; use only when literal.
- Military analogies should not pile up; rotate metaphor families across chapters.

**Banned in this book** (per global style-memory + book-specific):
- "the art of war" framing in titles or sentences (cliche).
- "Sun Tzu says" or any direct attribution paraphrase to Sun Tzu / Clausewitz unless source-anchored.
- Therapy register: "boundaries", "self-care", "showing up for yourself", "honor your feelings".

## Motif / callback opportunities

- **Self-directed → other-directed pivot:** Ch1's discipline of self-mastery enables Ch2's leadership through others. Make Ch2 reach back once.
- **OODA loop:** introduced in Ch1's third strategy area (Counterbalance / presence of mind), formalized in Ch5/6.
- **The "decline the fight" thread:** Ch3 (decline most), Ch4 (decline by deterring), Ch8 (decline by exiting), Ch9 (decline by being unfindable). One thread the reader can follow.
- **Center of gravity:** previewed in Ch5 (intelligence), delivered in Ch6, recalled in Ch7 (the flank IS often the center of gravity in disguise).

## Concept budget (per chapter)

Per the learning-loop contract, each chapter holds 3–5 ideas. Density column above is the planned count. Ch9 is the only 5-concept chapter; everything else 2–4.

## Forbidden drift (book-wide)

- Bulk generation of any kind.
- Synthesizing later chapters from seeds.
- Therapeutic / self-help register.
- Reuse of Greene's signature historical case studies.
- Mirror neurons, oxytocin, vagal tone, cortisol claims.
- "It's worth noting", "in today's world", em dashes, all banned-phrase list items.
- Pretty restatement of Greene's amoral framing without the explicit ethical reframe.
