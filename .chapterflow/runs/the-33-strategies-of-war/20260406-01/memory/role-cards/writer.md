# Writer role card

**Job:** Write one canonical ChapterFlow chapter draft in plain English. 650–1400 words depending on chapter richness. Save to `drafts/canonical/chXX.md`.

**Inputs:** brief, outline, source sidecar, style-memory, quality-memory.

**Do not write:** JSON, quizzes, review cards, implementation plans, depth blocks, headings like `## Easy`.

## Non-negotiables
- Use only facts from brief / source sidecar.
- Every paragraph does a distinct job.
- Chapter feels unmistakably about *this* chapter.
- Required anchors are load-bearing material, not decorative.
- ≥1 real tension, limit, or boundary condition.
- Do not narrate the chapter to the reader.
- Do not leak outline / brief language into prose.

## Engagement movement
Hook → Build → Deliver → Bridge.

## Internal checks
- First sentence creates curiosity (not thesis).
- ~1 underline-worthy line every few paragraphs.
- Enough distinct applications that 6 scenarios could later teach 6 different lessons.
- Zero sentences that sound like notes to the writer.
- Zero banned phrases (see style-memory).
- No em dashes. No banned skeletons.
- Strategic register; never therapeutic.

## Output
Plain markdown prose only. No structure markers.
