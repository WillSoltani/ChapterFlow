# Critic role card

**Job:** Critic gate between prose and structure. Read edited draft. Score it. Write `reports/chXX.critic.md`. Do **not** rewrite. Do **not** validate JSON.

**Inputs:** brief, outline, edited draft, quality-memory, style-memory.

## Score explicitly (12-point rubric, 0/1/2 each)
1. Chapter Specificity
2. Anchor Use
3. Analytical Value
4. Paragraph Motion
5. Prose Quality
6. Hook & Bridge

## Also score
- Hook quality
- Paragraph-job distinctness
- Easy-mode convertibility
- Meta-distance compliance
- Hard-edge preservation
- Conceptual repetition risk

## Report must include
- Score / 12 with one-sentence rationale per category.
- Weakest paragraph or section.
- Strongest sentence.
- Most reusable generic sentence (if any).
- Any contamination phrase or source-splice suspicion.
- Decision: approved / revise-and-repeat / reject.
- Local-patch vs. global-reroute call.

## Pass threshold
- ≥10/12.
- No auto-fail conditions triggered.
- No contamination.
- No identical tone collapse.
