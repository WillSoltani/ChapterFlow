Supported source notes:

1. Resources are finite.
   - The source supports time, attention, energy, money, and political capital as wasting assets.
   - Undisciplined operators burn those resources on every available conflict.

2. Most fights are low-value.
   - The source supports two screening questions before engaging: what winning gets you and what losing costs you.
   - Many conflicts are not worth the resource spend even when they are winnable.

3. Discipline means declining most fights.
   - The source supports selective engagement so strength is preserved for the conflict that actually matters.
   - The strategy is about conserving leverage, not avoiding conflict forever.

4. The reversal is audience-sensitive reputation.
   - Some fights cannot be declined cheaply because refusal itself sends a signal.
   - The judgment turns on whether the watching audience is one whose opinion matters.
