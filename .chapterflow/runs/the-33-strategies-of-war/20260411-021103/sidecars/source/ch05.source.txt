Supported source notes:

1. Groupthink is a structure problem.
   - The source frames bad group decisions as a consequence of hierarchy and incentives, not as evidence that the people involved are foolish.
   - Silence wins when the cost of speaking up is visible and the cost of silence is hidden.

2. Command should carry purpose more than procedure.
   - The source supports the mission-first model: tell people the objective, let them adapt the method.
   - The contrast case is micromanagement that fails as soon as conditions change.

3. Modern translation is adaptive leadership.
   - Teams that receive mission and context can respond to new information faster than teams that wait for instructions.
   - The core mechanism is initiative downward and dissent upward.

4. The reversal is non-optional.
   - Crises, novice teams, and unfamiliar terrain can require more explicit instruction.
   - The real judgment is whether the people on the ground have enough context to make sound decisions.
