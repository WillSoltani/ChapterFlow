Strategy 23 begins the dirty-war section by focusing on perception. The operator shifts the opponent's picture of reality by arranging true and false elements so smoothly that the false ones disappear inside the overall frame. The strategic point is not noise for its own sake. It is control over what the other side thinks is happening.

The fair modern reading is strategic framing. An operator emphasizes certain true facts, de-emphasizes others, and guides attention toward a conclusion without fabricating the core reality. The unfair reading is propaganda or deception that relies on the same mechanics but crosses into falsehood and manipulation. The package should be explicit about that difference.

The teaching emphasis should preserve the strategic content while naming the ethical line more clearly than Greene does. Readers need to leave knowing that perception management can be legitimate when it clarifies or sequences truth, and illegitimate when it hides core reality, induces false belief, or destroys informed consent.
