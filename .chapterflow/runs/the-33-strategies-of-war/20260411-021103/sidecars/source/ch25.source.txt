Strategy 25 is about third-party judgment. When an audience is watching, the side read as just gains structural advantage because attacks against it create sympathy and cost for the attacker.

The source makes the move partly substantive and partly performative. The operator has to stand on a position the audience can actually recognize as just, and then frame that position in language the audience already knows how to honor. Moral posture without recognizably moral conduct collapses fast.

The reversal is the real teaching pressure. Occupying the moral high ground is expensive to maintain because a single hypocrisy can destroy it. The package should treat this strategy as a consistency problem first and a messaging problem second.
