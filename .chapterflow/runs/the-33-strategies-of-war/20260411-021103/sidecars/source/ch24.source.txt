Strategy 24 focuses on expectation. Opponents form a forecast from your recent pattern, then start acting against that forecast instead of against your actual intention. The move is to vary the texture of your actions so their prediction fails.

The source makes the strategy narrower than mere surprise. Sometimes the correct break is the most ordinary move precisely because an extraordinary one is expected. Sometimes the right move is extraordinary because everyone has normalized your ordinary behavior. The pattern is the absence of an exploitable pattern.

The fair modern reading is disciplined unpredictability. The operator refuses the role the other side has assigned and does not let one visible habit become a handle for control. The teaching package should stress the difference between strategic variation and random thrashing. The objective stays stable even when the outward texture changes.
