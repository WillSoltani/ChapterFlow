Strategy 26 shifts from legitimacy to target denial. The weaker operator survives by becoming diffuse, indirect, and difficult to strike. If the opponent cannot locate a fixed center, public face, or stable position, its attacks dissipate.

The source makes the move structural rather than mystical. The operator does not vanish for drama. The operator removes or distributes the obvious target: no single headquarters to hit, no single celebrity face to disgrace, no central node whose loss collapses the whole effort.

The fair modern reading is resilience through diffusion. The package should stress the difference between strategic decentralization and evasive cowardice. The aim is to deny efficient attack while preserving enough coherence to keep operating.
