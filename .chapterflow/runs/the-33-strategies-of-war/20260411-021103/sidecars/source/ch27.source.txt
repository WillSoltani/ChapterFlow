Strategy 27 shifts from denied target to aligned incentives. The operator advances by finding a real overlap between their aims and the aims of a stronger party, then building the arrangement so the stronger party pushes the operator's position while pursuing its own advantage.

The source makes the honest version clear. The move is not flattery or dependency by default. It is the skill of finding where another actor already wants something that your success would help deliver. The darker version appears when the overlap is fake, staged, or temporary enough to collapse once scrutiny arrives.

The fair modern reading is structural sponsorship through genuine interest alignment. The package should separate authentic coalition design from manipulative pseudo-alignment. The point is to borrow momentum from stronger actors without confusing borrowed leverage for permanent loyalty.
