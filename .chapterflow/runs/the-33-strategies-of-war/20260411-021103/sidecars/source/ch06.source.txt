Supported source notes:

1. Monolithic organizations are brittle.
   - The source supports the claim that one big team, one chain of command, and one shared structure often fail slowly and all at once.
   - Centralization can make the whole organism vulnerable to one bottleneck or one bad read.

2. Smaller semi-autonomous units create resilience.
   - The source supports smaller units that can act on their own within a shared purpose.
   - These units move faster, take bounded risks, fail locally, and recombine as conditions change.

3. The principle scales.
   - The source supports translation from small startup teams to much larger formations.
   - The core idea is structural, not industry-specific.

4. The reversal is competence.
   - Segmentation works only when each unit can actually think and execute on its own.
   - Breaking weakness into smaller pieces does not create strength.
