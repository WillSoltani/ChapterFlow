# The Annihilation Strategy: Envelop the Enemy

1. Open on Sabina declining another narrow reprimand and choosing comprehensive closure against a contractor network that keeps regenerating.
2. Define annihilation as envelopment: denying retreat, support, and recomposition rather than merely hitting one visible edge.
3. Explain why partial attacks fail when the opponent can keep slipping into an open route or untouched constituency.
4. Return to Sabina and show the cost burden: procurement, legal, customer migration, and internal backup all have to align for the move to work.
5. Shift to Ilyas in a graduate disaster logistics seminar, where a proposal keeps surviving by retreating into whatever dimension is still unanswered.
6. Deliver the reversal: incomplete annihilation is catastrophic because the operator has committed heavily and kept little in reserve.
7. Bridge forward. Chapter 18 found the weak side. Chapter 19 closes every side. Chapter 20 will step back toward maneuver and ripening rather than total enclosure.
