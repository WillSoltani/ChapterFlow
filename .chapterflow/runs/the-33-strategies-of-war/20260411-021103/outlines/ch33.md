# The Chain-Reaction Strategy: Sow Uncertainty and Panic Through Acts of Terror

1. Open on Viren making one bounded, unexpected move that forces a total strategic recalculation without threatening anyone.
2. Define the strategy as disproportionate psychological effect from surprise, then narrow the legitimate reading to ethical pattern break.
3. Explain why an unexpected move can change the whole picture more than its material size would suggest.
4. Return to Viren and show the move working through recalibration rather than fear.
5. Shift to Calla in a graduate risk analysis seminar, where the chapter explicitly rejects intimidation and panic language.
6. Deliver the reversal: once dramatic moves become your pattern, they stop surprising anyone and can trap you in theater.
7. Close the book by tightening the ethical perimeter: surprise is usable, intimidation is not, and the operator must know when drama is serving ego instead of strategy.
