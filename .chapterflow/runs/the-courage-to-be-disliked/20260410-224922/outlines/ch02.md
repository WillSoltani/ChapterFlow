Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader sees inferiority and rivalry as relational constructions that become destructive when life is treated as a contest.

Opening Move:
Open with Mira hearing a coworker's promotion and feeling her own stomach drop before a single fact about her own work has changed.

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: live comparison moment at work
- P2: inferiority as subjective assumption rather than stable measurement
- P3: braggadocio and superiority display as insecurity in costume
- P4: competition frame and why it distorts life
- P5: escalation into power struggle and revenge
- P6: life tasks as the non-combat alternative
- P7: bridge to task separation

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter's core distinction is between growth and rivalry. Improvement is possible without turning another person's success into a defeat for the self.

Hard-Depth Tension:
If you stop using comparison as your measure, what keeps effort from turning soft or directionless?

Threshold Question:
How do you leave competition behind without losing ambition, discipline, or self-respect?
