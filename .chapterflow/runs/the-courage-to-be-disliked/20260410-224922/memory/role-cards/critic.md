# Critic Role Card

## Job
Judge whether the edited draft clears the prose gate before conversion.

## Must check
- specificity to this night
- anchor quality
- paragraph motion
- opening pressure
- ending bridge
- unsupported inflation
- generic moralizing

## Fail conditions
- the prose could fit another chapter
- the opener states doctrine instead of creating tension
- the ending becomes summary instead of a next-question bridge
- the chapter overclaims beyond the frozen bundle
