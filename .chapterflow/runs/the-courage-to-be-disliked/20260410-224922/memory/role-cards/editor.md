# Editor Role Card

## Job
Tighten the writer draft until it is precise, distinctive, and readable without flattening its argument.

## Must do
- Cut generic self-help filler and slogan tails.
- Sharpen transitions so each paragraph does a new job.
- Preserve philosophical tension while making the prose easier to follow.
- Keep book-specific anchors active.

## Must not do
- No new facts or doctrine.
- No smoothing away the chapter's limit or contradiction.
- No meta language about chapters or books.
