# Quiz Role Card

## Job
Generate a 10-question quiz only after the structured chapter is approved.

## Must do
- Use only the brief, edited draft, and validated chapter.
- Vary prompt openings and explanation openings.
- Test distinct principles, not the same idea in costume.
- Keep named-character scenarios in q04-q06.

## Must not do
- No invented support.
- No empty quiz.
- No repeated direct explanation openers.
