# Style Memory — compact

Compiled from the v13 style benchmark files and frozen for this run.

## Voice
- Calm, lucid explainer with philosophical pressure.
- Specific over generic. Concrete relational moments over abstract self-help haze.
- Use "you" sparingly and naturally.
- No reader-facing "this chapter says" or "the book argues" language in final prose.
- Preserve the dialogue-born tension: claims should feel tested, not merely announced.

## Grade bands
- Easy: grade 8-9. Clean doorway, concrete situation early, no jargon pileup.
- Medium: grade 10-11. Mechanism plus friction, one live tension, practical consequence.
- Hard: grade 12. Boundary, contradiction, cost, or synthesis. Not medium with bigger words.

## Hard constraints
- No invented facts, quotes, case studies, or pseudo-Adlerian doctrine.
- No em dashes. No mock therapy voice. No TED-talk cadence.
- First sentence must create pressure or curiosity, not summarize doctrine.
- Every paragraph must do a distinct job.
- Keep the prose human and direct, never mystical or bumper-sticker clean.

## Tone-object contract
- Gentle: lowers fear without losing the point.
- Direct: names the mechanism and decision line cleanly.
- Competitive: sharpens stakes and consequence without macho theater.
- Tone variants must differ in function, not just intensity.

## Gold-pattern reminders
- Examples should feel like recognizable conversations, classrooms, workrooms, or family moments.
- `whatToDo` gives a move the reader can picture doing.
- `whyItMatters` pays off the consequence instead of repeating the instruction.
- Hard-depth content should keep the tension between freedom and responsibility alive.

## Book-specific guardrails
- Stay close to Adlerian themes visible in the frozen bundle: trauma denial, inferiority, task separation, contribution, trust, present-focused living.
- Do not import unsupported clinical claims or biography about Adler, the authors, or the youth.
- Keep the philosopher-youth frame implicit in the pressure of the prose, not as meta commentary.
- Preserve the book's anti-fatalist edge without turning it into shallow motivation.
