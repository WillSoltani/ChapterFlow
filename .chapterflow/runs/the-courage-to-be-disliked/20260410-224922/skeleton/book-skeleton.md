## Chapter order
1. The First Night: Deny Trauma
   Intent: break fatalism by arguing that people are not ruled by past causes and can choose new life directions.
   Source richness: medium
   Concept density: high
   Moral complexity: high
   Thin-chapter risk: trauma-denial overclaiming if the prose gets too absolute
   Premium-routing candidate: yes
2. The Second Night: All Problems Are Interpersonal Relationship Problems
   Intent: shift suffering from isolated self-description toward relational comparison, inferiority, and power struggle.
   Source richness: medium_high
   Concept density: high
   Moral complexity: high
   Thin-chapter risk: can drift into oversimplified "people are the problem" phrasing
   Premium-routing candidate: yes
3. The Third Night: Discard Other People's Tasks
   Intent: build freedom through task separation, recognition detachment, and boundary-setting.
   Source richness: high
   Concept density: medium_high
   Moral complexity: high
   Thin-chapter risk: can sound selfish if contribution and responsibility vanish
   Premium-routing candidate: yes
4. The Fourth Night: Where the Center of the World Is
   Intent: pivot from self-focus toward community feeling, non-domination, contribution, and horizontal relationship ethics.
   Source richness: medium_high
   Concept density: medium_high
   Moral complexity: high
   Thin-chapter risk: vague belonging language if grounded examples are weak
   Premium-routing candidate: moderate
5. The Fifth Night: To Live in Earnest in the Here and Now
   Intent: synthesize contribution, trust, self-acceptance, work, and present-focused living into an anti-teleological close.
   Source richness: high
   Concept density: very high
   Moral complexity: high
   Thin-chapter risk: recap replay and spiritual vagueness
   Premium-routing candidate: yes
