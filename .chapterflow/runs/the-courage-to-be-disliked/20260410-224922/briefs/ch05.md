Core Claim:
The fifth night closes the book by arguing that a person uses the self properly by trusting without guarantees, working as contribution rather than as identity theater, and living meaningfully in present-tense moments rather than postponing life until a final answer arrives.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the synthesis chapter. It must gather anti-fatalism, anti-comparison, task separation, contribution, and non-centrality into a final ethic of trust, work, self-use, and present seriousness.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter warns that self-consciousness can stifle the self.
Then it distinguishes trust from confidence.
Then it defines work as contribution while rejecting workaholism.
Then it turns from life-plan obsession toward life as a series of moments.
Finally it argues that meaning is given through earnest present living.

Counterarguments or Limits:
The chapter supports present seriousness, not anti-responsibility. It supports trust, not naivete. It supports work as contribution, not work as total identity. It must preserve dignity, finitude, and commitment together.
