Chapter function in the book:
This final chapter has to synthesize the book's anti-fatalism, anti-comparison, task separation, and contribution logic into a closing argument about trust, work, self-use, and present-focused living. It must feel earned rather than mystical, and serious rather than inspirationally vague.

Safe paraphrase zone:
- self-consciousness can choke action by turning the self into an object under inspection
- trust is risk-bearing and cannot be reduced to confidence that betrayal will not happen
- work matters as contribution, not as self-glorification or total identity
- workaholism is a form of life-lie rather than proof of worth
- life is lived in moments rather than in a final completed narrative
- meaning is given through committed living in the present, not discovered as a guaranteed cosmic answer

Unsupported zones:
- no mystical timelessness language detached from concrete life
- no anti-planning overclaim that the future does not matter at all
- no claim that trust means gullibility or removal of judgment
- no claim that work is the only source of human value
- no nihilistic paraphrase that life has no meaning in any sense

Preview bridge:
This chapter must close the book by turning contribution and courage into present-tense practice rather than into a future self-image.
