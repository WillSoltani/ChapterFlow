Chapter-local interpretation limits:

- the chapter can frame self-hatred as bound up with chosen protective patterns
- the chapter must not overclaim that all trauma is unreal or morally trivial
- the chapter must stay narrow: the supported move is against identity fatalism, not against suffering itself

Operational note:
The safest formulation is that the chapter denies trauma as a total explanation or final sentence over a life, not that painful events never matter.
