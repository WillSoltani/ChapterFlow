Core Claim:
Gallo argues that the eighteen-minute rule forces clearer prioritization and protects audience cognition. The chapter treats brevity as a discipline that makes the message stronger when the speaker cuts drift instead of substance.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This chapter shifts from warmth to compression. After Chapter 6 opened the room socially, Chapter 7 asks how a speaker respects that openness by saying less, choosing more carefully, and protecting limited attention.
