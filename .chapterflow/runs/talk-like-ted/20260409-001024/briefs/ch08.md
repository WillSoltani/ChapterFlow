Core Claim:
Gallo argues that speakers make ideas easier to imagine and remember when they create vivid mental pictures through concrete, multisensory language. The chapter treats imagery as a communication tool that clarifies the point rather than decorating it.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This chapter shifts from compression to vividness. After Chapter 7 asks the speaker to say less, Chapter 8 asks how the tighter message becomes easier to picture, feel, and retain.
