# Source Discovery

## Goal

Freeze a lawful, chapter-usable source bundle before any chapter writing begins.

## Discovery Path

1. Confirm the intended book from the malformed launch input.
2. Find an authorized publisher page or preview tied to a stable edition.
3. Confirm the chapter map from a reliable catalog record.
4. Add secondary chapter summaries only where the authorized preview is thinner than the workflow needs.

## Chosen Bundle

- Authorized primary anchor: Macmillan / St. Martin's page for `Talk Like TED` with a readable excerpt covering Chapter 1.
- Authorized metadata support: Google Books preview landing page for the 2014 edition.
- Chapter map support: UNT library catalog record listing the introduction, parts, and all nine chapter titles.
- Secondary support: SuperSummary introduction and part summaries for chapter-level interpretation where the authorized preview is thin.

## Why This Bundle Is Trustworthy Enough

- The publisher page directly identifies the 2014 St. Martin's edition and exposes substantial Chapter 1 content.
- Google Books corroborates the same book identity, publisher family, year, and preview availability.
- The UNT catalog gives a clean chapter list that matches the book's three-part, nine-chapter structure.
- Secondary summaries are used narrowly for chapter intent and high-level sequencing, not for unsupported quote mining.

## Constraints Applied

- Paraphrase-first unless the authorized excerpt directly supports a short quote.
- No pirate mirrors.
- No inferred chapter content beyond what the frozen bundle supports.
- Where support is thinner, keep the chapter brief narrower instead of inventing detail.
