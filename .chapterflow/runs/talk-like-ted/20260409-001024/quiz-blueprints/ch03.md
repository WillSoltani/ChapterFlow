# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter: Have a Conversation
Core concepts to test:
- conversational delivery as the goal
- practice as the route to naturalness
- passion / practice / presence
- rate, volume, pitch, pauses
- body-language congruence and the trap of visible overcontrol

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: evaluate

Question plan:
- q01: basic recall of why the chapter wants a talk to feel conversational
- q02: comprehension of the practice -> presence mechanism
- q03: work application to a stiff sales or product presentation
- q04: named school scenario about memorized cadence versus spoken cadence
- q05: trap around confusing conversational with unprepared
- q06: limit or misreading around body language and belief
- q07: transfer to a personal toast or community appeal
- q08: scenario judgment about rate, pauses, or vocal control
- q09: deeper synthesis on why good content can still die in rigid delivery
- q10: bridge to Chapter 4 on why strong delivery still needs fresh content

Distractor notes:
- tempting wrong answer type 1: rehearse less so the talk sounds spontaneous
- tempting wrong answer type 2: add gestures or confidence cues without fixing the underlying delivery mismatch

Explanation diversity notes:
- avoid repeated openers
- each direct explanation must begin differently

CorrectIndex plan:
- keep distribution roughly balanced across 0 / 1 / 2
