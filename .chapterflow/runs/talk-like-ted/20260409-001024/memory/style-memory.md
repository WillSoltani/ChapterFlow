# Style Memory

- Teach the idea directly. Avoid narrating the chapter to the reader.
- Open on a pictureable scene or live tension, not a thesis announcement.
- Every paragraph must do a distinct job.
- Keep the prose specific, concrete, and authored. Cut generic coaching language.
- Use at least two strong anchors when the brief supports them.
- Easy simplifies, medium explains the mechanism, hard adds a boundary condition or unresolved tension.
- Tone differences must be functional, not adjective swaps.
- No em dashes, fake neuroscience, filler abstractions, or contamination phrases.
