Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader understands why a talk becomes more persuasive when it sounds rooted in genuine conviction and original voice rather than in imitation of somebody else's style.

Opening Move:
Open on a speaker who sounds polished but borrowed, then show why the room trusts real conviction more than copied mannerisms.

Paragraph Job Map:

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Authenticity is not spontaneity without preparation. It is craft delivered through a voice the audience can believe belongs to the speaker.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Speakers must learn from strong performers without disappearing into imitation.

Threshold Question:
