## Core thesis frozen from the lawful bundle
- Charisma is not an innate personality trait. It is learnable and can be raised or lowered through specific internal states and external behaviors.
- The book's recurring operating triad is presence, power, and warmth.
- The official framing repeatedly insists that internal state shows up in body language, so charismatic behavior is not just technique layered over anxiety.
- The later book turns from diagnosis toward application: first impressions, speaking/listening, body language, digital settings, difficult situations, presentations, and crisis moments.

## Current-edition interpretation rule
- The 2012 family and the current official family do not share the same late-book chapter map.
- This run uses the current official family because the author site, Google Books, and Penguin Random House align on it.
- Do not mix `The Charismatic Life: Rising to the Challenge` from the older family into this run's chapter sequence.

## Visible concept support
- Official site support:
  - charisma can be learned
  - presence, power, and warmth are the core components
  - internal state shapes outward behavior
  - online charisma belongs to the current package
- Secondary support:
  - later chapters apply charisma to presentations, listening, difficult interactions, and crisis settings
  - the style chapter distinguishes multiple workable charisma styles rather than one universal personality mold
- Authorized preview support:
  - first impressions form quickly and can shape long-term outcomes
  - appearance, demeanor, and tribe-matching cues matter early
  - body language and listening behavior carry more weight than many people assume

## Run-safe interpretation rules
- Stay closest to chapter titles, official overview, and explicit current-edition framing.
- Prefer practical behavioral distinctions over speculative neuroscience language.
- Do not invent stories, studies, or chapter-specific subframeworks that are not visible in the frozen bundle.
- Where support is thin, keep the chapter tighter instead of broadening it.
