# Source Discovery — The Charisma Myth

## Objective
Freeze a lawful, high-signal source bundle for `The Charisma Myth` before any chapter work begins.

## Candidate scan
- The current official author site (`charismamyth.com`) frames the book as a learnable-behavior system, explicitly names presence, power, and warmth, and notes revised material for online charisma and virtual-reality practice.
- The current Google Books page exposes the 320-page 2013 Portfolio family and a 13-chapter map that includes `Digital Charisma`.
- Penguin Random House confirms the March 26, 2013 Portfolio release, ISBN `9781591845942`, page count `320`, and the official overview.
- The ICPL library record exposes an older 2012 Portfolio/Penguin chapter map ending with `The Charismatic Life: Rising to the Challenge`.
- The authorized sample PDF on the official site exposes `Charismatic First Impressions` and later continuation pages, giving the run one lawful direct-preview surface beyond metadata.
- Readingraphics provides secondary paraphrase-first support for the triad model, obstacle/mental-state chapters, charisma styles, and later application chapters.

## Material ambiguity review
The book has a real chapter-level edition split:
- the older 2012 family ends with `The Charismatic Life: Rising to the Challenge`
- the current official family uses `Digital Charisma` in that late-book slot and aligns with the current author-site framing around online charisma

This is a material difference, so the edition could not be chosen casually.

## Decision
Lock the current official English family built around the 2013 Portfolio package.

Why this lock wins:
- it is the only family supported simultaneously by the current author site, Penguin Random House metadata, and the current Google Books preview page
- it matches the present official framing of the book readers are most likely to encounter
- it gives the run a stable 13-chapter package without mixing old and current chapter maps

No user question was required because the current official family is dominant across the strongest lawful sources.

## Working policy
- Use paraphrase-first throughout the run.
- Exact quotes are allowed only when a short line is visible in the frozen bundle and support is unambiguous.
- Treat the official author site, the current Google Books page, Penguin Random House metadata, and the sample PDF as the primary evidence surface.
- Treat Readingraphics as secondary concept support only.
- Stay narrower rather than speculative whenever support thins out.
