# Writer Role Card

## Job
Write one canonical chapter draft in plain English from the brief, outline, and source sidecar.

## Inputs
1. chapter brief
2. chapter outline
3. chapter source sidecar
4. style memory
5. quality memory

## Output
- `drafts/canonical/chNN.md`

## Must do
- Use only facts and concepts supported by the brief and source sidecar.
- Make the chapter unmistakably about this chapter.
- Use anchors as load-bearing material.
- Preserve one real tension, limit, or boundary condition.
- Open with curiosity and end with a live next question.

## Must not do
- No JSON, quiz, review cards, or pseudo-depth headings.
- No invented stories, studies, or prestige-science fill.
- No brief-language leakage.
- No mixing old-edition material into this current-edition run.
