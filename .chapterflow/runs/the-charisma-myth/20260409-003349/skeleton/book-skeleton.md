## Governing voice guardrails for the whole book
- Keep the book practical, behavioral, and situational rather than mystical or hypey.
- Do not flatten charisma into extroversion, surface charm, or dominance.
- Preserve the triad logic: presence, power, and warmth are distinct and often need balancing.
- Avoid unsupported neuroscience prestige language even when the source gestures at science.
- Treat authority-style charisma and difficult-situation tactics as strategic awareness, not endorsement of manipulation.
- The current run includes `Digital Charisma`; do not import the older 2012 ending chapter family.

## Motif / callback opportunities
- `presence, power, warmth` is the book's load-bearing triad and should recur as a diagnostic lens rather than a slogan.
- Internal state -> body language -> social read should surface in ch02, ch05, ch07, ch09, ch12, and ch13.
- Fast judgment appears early in first impressions and should echo later in presentations, digital rooms, and crises.
- Style adaptation in ch06 should feed later chapters instead of disappearing after classification.
- Difficult interactions and crisis handling should feel like stress tests of earlier skills, not unrelated bonus chapters.

## Vocabulary watchlist
- keep precise: presence, power, warmth, state, signal, status, trust, attention, composure, projection
- use carefully: authority, influence, magnetism, charisma, confidence, dominance
- avoid overusing: vibe, aura, hack, game, crush, command the room, natural leader

## Thin-chapter risk notes
- `ch01`: easy to become generic motivational myth-busting; keep concrete consequences and trainability visible.
- `ch03`: obstacle chapters drift vague if the specific blockers are not named and differentiated.
- `ch04`: repair chapters can collapse into wellness fluff; keep them operational.
- `ch06`: style chapters are vulnerable to personality-typing mush and interchangeable examples.
- `ch10`: digital chapter can become generic remote-work etiquette if the charisma problem stays blurry.
- `ch13`: crisis chapter can flatten into leadership slogans unless steadiness under pressure stays specific.

## Premium-routing candidates
- `ch02` because the triad definition sets the entire book's vocabulary
- `ch05` because internal-state chapters drift pseudo-scientific fast if not tightly bounded
- `ch07` because the source bundle includes direct preview support and the chapter has to land cleanly
- `ch11` because difficult-situation advice can become generic or manipulative if the tension is not sharp
- `ch13` because the closing crisis chapter carries the strongest authority/warmth tension

## Cross-chapter tensions to preserve
- Trainable behavior vs authentic feeling: the book insists charisma is learnable without collapsing into fakery.
- Power vs warmth: too much force chills trust, too much warmth can weaken authority.
- Presence vs performance: many later chapters are really tests of whether the reader can stay present under pressure.
- Adaptation vs personality drift: style flexibility should not become inauthentic mimicry.

## Quality decay watch
- If any later chapter falls more than `1.5` critic points below the Chapter 1-2 baseline, stop and reroute.
- Watch especially for generic self-help drift in ch04, ch06, ch10, and ch13.
