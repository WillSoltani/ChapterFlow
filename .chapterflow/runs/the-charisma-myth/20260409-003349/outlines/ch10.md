Chapter Promise:
This chapter will show the reader that charisma in digital settings depends on how clearly presence, power, and warmth survive through reduced or altered channels.

Opening Move:
Open on the mistake of assuming charisma disappears online or that digital success is only a matter of equipment, etiquette, or speed.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P2-P4
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P3-P5
- anchor 4 used in paragraph(s): P4-P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: establish that digital settings reduce some cues but do not remove charisma from the interaction
- P2: explain how reduced bandwidth makes presence harder to feel and easier to fake poorly
- P3: connect digital power to clarity, timing, and concise consequence rather than cold brevity
- P4: connect digital warmth to responsiveness, tone, and human acknowledgment without overcompensation
- P5: show how latency, camera habits, and written ambiguity can distort the triad online
- P6: bridge into Chapter 11 by asking what happens when already-reduced channels must also carry conflict and difficulty

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter's mechanism is channel adaptation. Presence, power, and warmth still matter digitally, but each has to survive with fewer cues and more opportunities for distortion.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must keep this question alive: how do you make digital contact feel human and consequential without overproducing warmth or flattening yourself into blunt efficiency?

Threshold Question:
When the channel removes context, what remains of your presence, power, and warmth for the other person to actually feel?

Takeaway Count Lock:
- Easy: 3

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- Takeaway 1: charisma does not vanish online, but the signal becomes thinner and easier to distort
- Takeaway 2: digital presence depends on visible attention, timing, and reduced fragmentation
- Takeaway 3: digital power depends on concise consequence rather than cold compression
- Takeaway 4: digital warmth depends on human acknowledgment without excess performance
- Takeaway 5: difficult situations get harder online because reduced cues must also carry tension
