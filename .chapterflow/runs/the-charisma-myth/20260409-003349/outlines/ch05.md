Chapter Promise:
This chapter will show the reader how to prepare mental states before a room begins so presence, power, and warmth have a better chance of landing cleanly.

Opening Move:
Open on the limit of Chapter 4: repair is useful, but relying on repair alone is expensive because the room has already started reading the leak.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P2-P5
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: explain why post-slip repair cannot be the whole system and why this chapter has to move earlier
- P2: show that internal state leaks outward into timing, body language, and social read
- P3: explain why different prepared states support presence, power, and warmth differently
- P4: make mental rehearsal practical by tying it to pre-room attention, conviction, and goodwill rather than to magic confidence
- P5: show that preparation is partial but still changes what the room is likely to feel
- P6: bridge from prepared state into the style-choice chapter by asking what kind of charisma should be expressed next

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter's mechanism is state preparation before performance. The reader changes what is likely to leak outward by preparing a better internal starting point.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must keep this question alive: how do you prepare a better state without turning inward so hard that you become performative or self-absorbed?

Threshold Question:
What state do you want to enter the room from before the room starts reading you?

- Takeaway 1: internal state changes outward read, so preparation matters before technique

- Takeaway 4: warmth starts from visible goodwill rather than private niceness
