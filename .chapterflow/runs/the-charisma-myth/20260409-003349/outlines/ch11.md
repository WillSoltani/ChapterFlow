This chapter will show the reader that charisma under pressure depends on keeping presence, power, and warmth usable when disagreement, tension, or relational strain would normally break the signal.

Opening Move:
Open on the fact that difficult situations do not create a new charisma system; they expose whether the existing signal can survive pressure.

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: establish that difficult situations amplify failures in presence, power, and warmth
- P2: explain why pressure makes split attention, defensiveness, and fake calm more visible
- P3: show how power under difficulty must keep consequence without turning hard or escalating blindly
- P4: show how warmth under difficulty must preserve dignity without collapsing into surrender

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter's mechanism is pressure-tested triad balance. Difficult exchanges magnify whichever signal is weakest or most distorted.
