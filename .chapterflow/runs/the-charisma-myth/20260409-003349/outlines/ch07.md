Chapter Promise:
This chapter will show the reader that charismatic first impressions form quickly and that early demeanor, visible fit, and triad balance shape what the room thinks it is meeting before conversation gets far.

Opening Move:
Open on the speed of early judgment in a new room and the mistake of assuming words arrive before the room has already formed a working read.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P2-P4
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P3-P5
- anchor 4 used in paragraph(s): P4-P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: establish that first impressions form quickly and matter because early reads shape later interpretation
- P2: explain that the room reads demeanor, body language, and visible fit before it fully trusts words
- P3: connect the opening read to presence, power, and warmth balance rather than to image alone
- P4: show how tribe-matching or visible-fit cues can help or hurt the opening read
- P5: explain how over-managing appearance or energy can look calculated and weaken credibility
- P6: bridge into Chapter 8 by asking how speaking and listening sustain the opening read once the interaction starts

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter's mechanism is early cue interpretation. The room infers trustworthiness, confidence, and relational fit from visible signals before it has much conversational evidence.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must keep this question alive: how do you respect the speed of first impressions without collapsing into fake image control or deterministic fatalism?

Threshold Question:
What is the room likely to read from your first seconds before your words have earned a second look?
