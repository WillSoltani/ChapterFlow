Chapter Promise:
This chapter will show the reader that presenting with charisma means carrying presence, power, and warmth deliberately while the room's attention is concentrated on them.

Opening Move:
Open on the fact that presentations intensify the signal because the room is now watching more continuously and more deliberately than in ordinary interaction.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P2-P4
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P3-P5
- anchor 4 used in paragraph(s): P4-P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: establish that presentations concentrate attention and therefore expose weak triad balance faster
- P2: explain why presence matters because overcontrol and memorized delivery can hollow the signal out
- P3: show how power in presentation depends on consequence and clarity rather than volume or inflation
- P4: show how warmth keeps the room reachable and respected without becoming performance sweetness
- P5: explain how overproduction creates distance and weakens trust even when the delivery is polished
- P6: bridge into Chapter 13 by asking what happens when the room's stakes become crisis-level rather than presentational

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter's mechanism is centralized-attention triad management. Presentation does not replace charisma; it concentrates and exposes it.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must keep this question alive: how do you hold a room deliberately without becoming more produced than present?

Threshold Question:
When the room's attention concentrates on you, which part of the signal becomes hardest to keep real: presence, power, warmth, or their balance?
