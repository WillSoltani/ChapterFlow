Chapter Promise:
This chapter will show the reader that crisis does not suspend charisma; it exposes whether presence, power, and warmth can still hold under urgency, instability, and fear.

Opening Move:
Open on the fact that crisis raises the cost of every signal failure because people are scanning for steadiness, consequence, and reassurance at the same time.

- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P2-P4

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: establish that crisis is the highest-pressure test of triad balance
- P2: explain why presence matters because false calm and split attention become expensive under alarm
- P3: show how power in crisis depends on consequence and direction rather than command theater
- P4: show how warmth keeps reassurance real without weakening action
- P5: explain how steadiness requires authority and reassurance to remain linked
- P6: close the book by framing crisis as the final stress test of the entire triad

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter's mechanism is high-stakes triad load. Crisis does not create a separate charisma model; it intensifies the cost of imbalance.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must keep this question alive: how do you remain reassuring and consequential in a crisis without pretending certainty, freezing the room, or performing calm?

Threshold Question:
When a room turns unstable, which part of the signal breaks first: presence, authority, warmth, or the balance between them?
