Chapter Promise:
This chapter will show the reader that charismatic conversation depends on how speaking and listening make another person feel attention, clarity, and relational balance in real time.

Opening Move:
Open on the mistake of treating charisma in conversation as one-sided performance instead of a two-way signal exchange that the other person is constantly reading.

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: explain why conversational charisma is more than sounding impressive
- P2: show that presence matters because people can feel whether they are being fully listened to
- P3: connect speaking style to pacing, timing, and verbal framing rather than volume alone
- P4: connect listening to warmth and credibility without making it passive
- P5: explain how interruption, over-talking, or weak framing can distort the triad in conversation

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter's mechanism is live interaction calibration. Charisma in conversation depends on whether speech and listening jointly communicate presence, consequence, and goodwill.
