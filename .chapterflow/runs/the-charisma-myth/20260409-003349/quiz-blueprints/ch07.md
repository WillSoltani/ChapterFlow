# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter: Charismatic First Impressions
Core concepts to test:
- why first impressions form quickly and influence later interpretation
- how demeanor and nonverbal cues shape the opening read
- why visible fit cues matter without becoming the whole chapter
- how presence, power, and warmth change a first impression
- how this chapter bridges into speaking and listening

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: evaluate

Question plan:
- q01: orienting distinction about fast first impressions versus slow earned reputation
- q02: anchor comprehension of early cue reading before words fully land
- q03: anchor comprehension of appearance and fit cues without shallow reduction
- q04: named school scenario on entering a new seminar or lab
- q05: named work scenario on a first meeting or new team read
- q06: named personal scenario on how warmth/power balance changes an introduction
- q07: transfer question about why over-managing the opening can weaken credibility
- q08: scenario judgment on what the room is likely to infer first
- q09: deeper synthesis on why first impressions matter but are not total destiny
- q10: bridge question connecting opening reads to later speaking and listening

Distractor notes:
- tempting wrong answer type 1: words are always the first thing people judge
- tempting wrong answer type 2: first impressions are only about attractiveness or polish

Explanation diversity notes:
- avoid repeated openers
- each direct explanation must begin differently

CorrectIndex plan:
- keep distribution roughly balanced across 0 / 1 / 2
