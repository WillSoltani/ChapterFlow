Core Claim:
This chapter argues that charisma in conversation depends on how presence, pacing, listening, and verbal framing make another person feel in real time. The chapter's job is to move from the opening read into sustained interaction without collapsing into generic communication tips.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Chapter 7 handled the first seconds of entry. Chapter 8 now asks how charisma survives once people start speaking, listening, responding, and deciding whether they feel met or managed.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter explains that speaking with charisma is not only about sounding confident; it is also about timing, pacing, and signal clarity.
Then it shows that listening is a charisma skill because felt attention changes whether other people feel received.
Then it connects both sides of conversation back to presence, power, and warmth.
