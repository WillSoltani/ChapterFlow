Core Claim:
This chapter argues that charisma does not come in one ideal personality mold. Different rooms reward different balances of presence, power, and warmth, so readers need workable style options rather than one borrowed performance. The chapter's job is to make style adaptation practical without turning it into personality-typing mush.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the style-selection chapter. Chapter 5 established that internal state needs preparation before the room begins. Chapter 6 now asks what kind of charisma the reader should actually express once their state is steadier.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter rejects the idea that charisma has one correct look.
Then it shows that different styles emphasize different mixes of presence, power, and warmth.
Then it explains how to choose and adapt a style without copying someone else's personality.
