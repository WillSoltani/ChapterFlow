Core Claim:
This chapter argues that charisma breakdowns can be interrupted. Once the obstacle is visible, the reader can use practical reset moves to restore enough presence, power, or warmth for the interaction to recover. The chapter's job is to make repair feel workable without pretending that repair is effortless.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the repair chapter that follows diagnosis. It should turn Chapter 3's obstacle language into usable resets while staying narrower than Chapter 5's deeper mental-state work.

Author Logic Chain:

Then it gives the reader workable reset logic for restoring the triad in time.

Counterarguments or Limits:
The chapter should not promise instant mastery. Repair is partial, situational, and sometimes incomplete. The point is that a bad internal loop can often be interrupted enough to change the room's read. Keep the chapter practical and do not drift into therapy, pseudo-neuroscience, or total-confidence fantasies.
