Core Claim:
This chapter argues that first impressions form quickly and that charisma starts affecting the room before trust has time to grow slowly. The chapter's job is to show how immediate demeanor, visible presence, and fit cues shape the opening read without turning the chapter into shallow appearance advice.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Chapter 6 asked what style of charisma the room should meet. Chapter 7 now asks how that style gets read in the first moments of contact, especially when people are deciding quickly what kind of person has arrived.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter shows that first impressions form quickly and carry lasting consequences.
Then it explains that early reads are shaped by visible demeanor, body language, and social-fit cues rather than words alone.
Then it asks the reader to make those first seconds more credible by aligning outer signals with the right balance of presence, power, and warmth.
