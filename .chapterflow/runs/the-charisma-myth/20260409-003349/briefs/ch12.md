Core Claim:
This chapter argues that presenting with charisma is not stage polish alone. Its job is to show how presence, power, and warmth must be carried deliberately while the reader is visibly holding the room, without drifting into generic public-speaking advice.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Chapter 11 handled charisma under difficult relational strain. Chapter 12 now asks what happens when the reader has to sustain the signal more intentionally in a presentation, briefing, pitch, or capstone talk.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter shows that presenting concentrates attention, so weaknesses in presence, power, and warmth become more visible.
Then it explains that charismatic presentation depends on carrying consequence without sounding overproduced or cold.
Then it shows how warmth and presence keep authority usable while the room's focus is centralized.
