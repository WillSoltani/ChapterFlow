Core Claim:
This chapter argues that charisma still matters in digital settings, but the channel changes which signals survive, which get flattened, and which require more intentional clarity. Its job is to keep online charisma inside the book's triad logic instead of letting the chapter collapse into generic remote-work etiquette.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Chapter 9 focused on the full body in shared physical space. Chapter 10 now asks what charisma looks like when contact is filtered through screens, delay, written messages, and reduced nonverbal bandwidth.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter shows that digital settings alter charisma because they strip or distort some cues rather than eliminating charisma altogether.
Then it explains that presence, power, and warmth still matter online, but they have to be carried through narrower channels.
Then it shows how timing, clarity, responsiveness, and selective nonverbal care matter more when context is reduced.
