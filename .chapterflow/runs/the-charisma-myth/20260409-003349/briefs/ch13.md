Core Claim:
This chapter argues that charisma matters most when stability breaks. Its job is to show how presence, power, and warmth must remain usable in a crisis, where reassurance, authority, and steadiness have to coexist without collapsing into leadership slogans or false calm.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Chapter 12 handled room-holding under concentrated attention. Chapter 13 now asks what happens when the room is unstable, urgency rises, and the reader has to carry signal under crisis conditions rather than rehearsed ones.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter shows that crisis raises the cost of every signal failure.
Then it explains that charisma in a crisis depends on steadiness that keeps contact real without pretending nothing is wrong.
Then it shows how power and warmth must remain visible together so authority reassures instead of freezing or confusing the room.
