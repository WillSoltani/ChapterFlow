Core Claim:
This chapter argues that charisma remains visible in the body even when words are absent or secondary. Its job is to explain body language as the outward carrier of internal state and triad balance, not as a bag of disconnected tricks.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Chapter 8 focused on live speaking and listening. Chapter 9 widens the lens and asks how posture, gaze, movement, stillness, and physical ease keep signaling presence, power, and warmth before, during, and after words.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter shows that body language matters because people read the body continuously, not only speech.
Then it explains that internal state shows up physically, so body language cannot be treated as pure surface technique.
Then it ties nonverbal signal quality back to presence, power, and warmth.

Counterarguments or Limits:
Keep the chapter practical and behavior-level. The frozen bundle supports body-language importance and state leakage, not an exhaustive catalog of gestures or universal decoding rules.
