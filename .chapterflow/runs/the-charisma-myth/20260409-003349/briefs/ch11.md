Core Claim:
This chapter argues that charisma is hardest to maintain when pressure, conflict, or relational strain enter the room. Its job is to show how presence, power, and warmth have to stay usable under difficulty without turning the chapter into generic conflict advice or manipulative tactics.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Chapter 10 handled reduced-channel charisma in digital settings. Chapter 11 now asks what happens when the interaction itself becomes difficult: disagreement, pressure, tension, status friction, or emotionally loaded exchanges.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter shows that difficult situations intensify every weakness in presence, power, and warmth.
Then it explains that charisma under pressure depends on staying present without collapsing, defensive hardness, or fake calm.
Then it shows how consequence and warmth must remain visible together when the exchange gets tense.

Counterarguments or Limits:
