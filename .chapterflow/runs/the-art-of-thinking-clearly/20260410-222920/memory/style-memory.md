# Style Memory

- Teach the idea directly, not the artifact.
- Specific beats generic. Concrete beats abstract. Compression beats restatement.
- No em dashes, fake precision, filler neuroscience, made-up quotes, or chapter-generic coaching.
- Easy opens with a pictureable situation and stays grade 8-9.
- Medium adds mechanism, friction, and application.
- Hard adds boundary, contradiction, cost, hidden structure, threshold, or synthesis. It cannot be medium made longer.
- Competitive tone must stay severe and credible, never theatrical.
- Every paragraph needs a distinct job and one landing only.
- First sentence should open from pressure, event, or live tension rather than thesis.
