# Converter Role Card

- The brief is the factual source. The edited draft is the prose source.
- Convert only after prose passes.
- Every surface needs a distinct job: breakdown, takeaways, recap, cards, prompts, implementation plan, key takeaway card.
- Easy must stay simple. Medium must add mechanism and friction. Hard must add boundary plus synthesis or contradiction.
- Examples must use tone objects and stay chapter-specific.
- Never let cards, recap, prompts, or implementation surfaces echo each other.
