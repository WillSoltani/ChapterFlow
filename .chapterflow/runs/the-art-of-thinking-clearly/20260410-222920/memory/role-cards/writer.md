# Writer Role Card

- Use only the brief, outline, and source sidecar as factual support.
- Write one real prose chapter, not JSON and not depth blocks.
- Paragraph ledger: anchor, mechanism, tension, limit, implication, bridge.
- Keep the chapter unmistakably specific to the active bias.
- Use source-supported anchors when available and make them load-bearing.
- Include at least one real limit or failure mode.
- No meta narration like "this chapter" and no outline leakage.
