# Quiz Blueprint: ch20

- chapterId: ch20
- title: Outcome Bias

## Concepts to test

1. outcome bias means judging decisions mostly by results instead of by process quality
2. luck and uncertainty can make weak decisions look good and strong decisions look bad
3. visible payoff can overshadow the information, tradeoffs, and reasoning available at the time
4. correction requires process review before verdict
5. same process with different outcomes should trigger evaluation discipline

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify outcome bias in ordinary decisions and evaluations
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where results distort judgment of the process
- q07-q08: distinguish decision quality from outcome quality under uncertainty
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on process-first evaluation

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat good results as proof of good judgment
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming outcomes do not matter at all
- one distractor can confuse process review with excuse-making after failure
