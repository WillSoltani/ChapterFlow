# Quiz Blueprint: ch46

- chapterId: ch46
- title: Hedonic Treadmill

## Concepts to test

1. positive gains often lose emotional intensity through adaptation
2. novelty fades and baseline feelings reset
3. people overestimate the lasting happiness from improvements
4. better judgment separates durable value from temporary excitement
5. correction requires asking what remains after the novelty wears off

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify hedonic treadmill patterns in ordinary settings
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where gains lose emotional force
- q07-q08: distinguish lasting utility from fading thrill
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on adaptation and future expectation

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should assume the newest gain will keep delivering the same emotional payoff
- another distractor should confuse temporary excitement with durable well-being
- one distractor can overcorrect by claiming gains never matter at all
