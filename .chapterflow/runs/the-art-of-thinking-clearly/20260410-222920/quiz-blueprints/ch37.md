# Quiz Blueprint: ch37

- chapterId: ch37
- title: False Causality

## Concepts to test

1. sequence is not sufficient proof of cause
2. correlation can mislead when alternative explanations are ignored
3. repeated patterns can still be accidental or driven by a hidden factor
4. stronger causal judgment asks what evidence would rule out coincidence
5. correction requires naming alternatives before accepting a story

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify false causal claims in ordinary settings
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where timing gets overread
- q07-q08: distinguish healthy hypothesis-making from causal overclaim
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on evidence needed for causation

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat sequence or repetition as automatic proof
- another distractor should deny that any causal inference is ever possible
- one distractor can confuse association with mechanism
