# Quiz Blueprint: ch18

- chapterId: ch18
- title: Incentive Super-Response Tendency

## Concepts to test

1. incentives can strongly reshape behavior, attention, and justification
2. people often undercount payoff structures and overcount personality-based explanations
3. systems can reward conduct that looks irrational or unethical from the outside
4. correction requires asking what the structure is paying for before judging what people do
5. reward mapping helps reveal why stated intentions and actual behavior diverge

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify incentives driving behavior in ordinary settings
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where rewards bend conduct or reasoning
- q07-q08: distinguish personal virtue stories from payoff-structure explanations
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on reward mapping and system-level judgment

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should explain behavior only with personality or morals while ignoring incentives
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming people have no agency at all
- one distractor can confuse visible intention with the real reward structure
