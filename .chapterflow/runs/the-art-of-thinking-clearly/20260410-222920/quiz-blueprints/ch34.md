# Quiz Blueprint: ch34

- chapterId: ch34
- title: Exponential Growth

## Concepts to test

1. compounding growth builds on prior growth
2. linear intuition often underestimates multiplicative change
3. early stages of compounding can look deceptively modest
4. repeated percentage change can outrun straight-line expectations
5. correction requires checking multiplicative behavior directly

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify exponential-growth misunderstanding in ordinary settings
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where linear intuition fails
- q07-q08: distinguish additive from multiplicative change
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on compounding over time and delayed acceleration

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat repeated percentage growth like simple addition
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming any increase is automatically exponential
- one distractor can confuse short calm periods with long-term safety
