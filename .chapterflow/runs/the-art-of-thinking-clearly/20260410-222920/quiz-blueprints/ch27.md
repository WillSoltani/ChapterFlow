# Quiz Blueprint: ch27

- chapterId: ch27
- title: Scarcity Error

## Concepts to test

1. scarcity error means rarity inflates desire beyond actual value
2. urgency and limited access can feel like proof of quality
3. hard to get is not the same as worth getting
4. correction requires imagining the item or opportunity without scarcity pressure
5. fairer judgment asks whether desire survives abundance

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify scarcity error in ordinary choices
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where rarity drives interest
- q07-q08: distinguish scarcity pressure from real value
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on removing urgency and testing desire under abundance

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat limited access as proof of higher quality
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming scarcity never matters at all
- one distractor can confuse urgency with importance
