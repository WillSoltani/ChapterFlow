# Quiz Blueprint: ch49

- chapterId: ch49
- title: Beginner's Luck

## Concepts to test

1. early success can mislead people about durable ability
2. luck, timing, and easy first conditions can create flattering results
3. one win is weaker evidence than repeated performance
4. confidence should track repeatability, not only early outcomes
5. correction requires testing whether performance survives repetition and tougher conditions

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify beginner's luck in ordinary cases
- q04-q06: work or school examples where one early win is overread
- q07-q08: distinguish promising evidence from reliable skill
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on repetition, pressure, and false confidence

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat one success as enough proof of stable talent
- another distractor should assume luck stops mattering after a single win
- one distractor can overcorrect by claiming early success never contains any signal
