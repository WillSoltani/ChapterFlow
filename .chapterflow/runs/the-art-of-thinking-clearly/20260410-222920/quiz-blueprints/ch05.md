# Quiz Blueprint: ch05

- chapterId: ch05
- title: Sunk Cost Fallacy

## Concepts to test

1. sunk costs are unrecoverable and should not decide future investment by themselves
2. people continue because stopping feels like admitting waste
3. future choices should be based on current and forward value, not on what has already been spent
4. the fallacy appears in work, school, and personal commitments
5. persistence can still be rational, but not because previous costs demand more loyalty

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify sunk-cost logic in ordinary examples
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases driven by past investment
- q07-q08: distinguish rational persistence from sunk-cost continuation
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on restart questions and forward-looking judgment

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat previous spending as a reason that must be defended
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming all persistence is irrational
- one distractor can confuse emotional discomfort with economic or practical evidence
