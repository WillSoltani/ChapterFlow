# Quiz Blueprint: ch36

- chapterId: ch36
- title: Fundamental Attribution Error

## Concepts to test

1. behavior often gets overexplained by personality
2. situational pressure can disappear from judgment
3. salience of the actor makes context easy to ignore
4. correction requires checking incentives, constraints, and conditions
5. better judgment delays trait conclusions until situation is examined

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify character-overreach in ordinary situations
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where context is ignored
- q07-q08: distinguish responsible context-checking from excuse-making
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on trait judgment versus situational explanation

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat observed behavior as direct proof of personality
- another distractor should overcorrect by denying that character ever matters
- one distractor can confuse understanding the situation with excusing the behavior
