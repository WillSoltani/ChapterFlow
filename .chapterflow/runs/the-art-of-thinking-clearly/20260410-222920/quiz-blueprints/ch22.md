# Quiz Blueprint: ch22

- chapterId: ch22
- title: Liking Bias

## Concepts to test

1. liking bias means affection or dislike distorts evaluation
2. positive feeling can hide flaws and negative feeling can exaggerate them
3. source-feeling often spills into judgment of work, ideas, or products
4. correction requires separating evaluation target from emotional spillover
5. fairer judgment asks whether the verdict would survive without the feeling

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify liking bias in ordinary judgments
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where affection softens or dislike hardens evaluation
- q07-q08: distinguish source-feeling from object quality
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on removing liking from the verdict

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat warm feeling as evidence of quality
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming relationships should never matter
- one distractor can confuse familiarity with fair judgment
