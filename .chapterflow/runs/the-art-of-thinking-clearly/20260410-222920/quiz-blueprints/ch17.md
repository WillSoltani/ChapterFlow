# Quiz Blueprint: ch17

- chapterId: ch17
- title: Illusion of Control

## Concepts to test

1. illusion of control means overestimating control over outcomes that still depend heavily on chance
2. rituals, customization, and extra activity can create false ownership of the result
3. the bias appears in work, school, and personal life when involvement gets mistaken for influence
4. controllable inputs should be separated from uncontrollable outcomes
5. correction requires naming what action can change and what remains random

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify illusion of control in ordinary choice, ritual, and chance settings
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where activity gets mistaken for control
- q07-q08: distinguish controllable inputs from random outcomes under uncertainty
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on separating agency-feeling from actual control

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat effort, monitoring, or personalization as proof of control
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming no inputs matter at all
- one distractor can confuse preparation with ownership of the final outcome
