# Quiz Blueprint: ch39

- chapterId: ch39
- title: Alternative Paths

## Concepts to test

1. the chosen path can feel obvious because alternatives faded from view
2. present reality becomes more vivid than abandoned options
3. reconstruction of realistic alternatives improves judgment
4. a good current outcome does not prove the other paths were poor
5. correction requires evaluating what options looked like at the time, not only now

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify missing-alternative thinking in ordinary settings
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where one path feels overly natural
- q07-q08: distinguish healthy evaluation from hindsight about invisible alternatives
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on reconstructing real options at decision time

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat the chosen path as obviously best because it is the one that happened
- another distractor should overcorrect by romanticizing every unchosen path
- one distractor can confuse realistic alternatives with fantasy alternatives that were never actually available
