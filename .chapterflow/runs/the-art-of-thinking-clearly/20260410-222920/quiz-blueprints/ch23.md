# Quiz Blueprint: ch23

- chapterId: ch23
- title: Endowment Effect

## Concepts to test

1. the endowment effect means ownership inflates value
2. possession can make parting with an item feel costlier than acquiring it felt
3. owners often demand more than neutral buyers would offer
4. correction requires judging the item from outside ownership
5. fairer valuation asks what you would pay if the item were not already yours

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify the endowment effect in ordinary pricing or keep/sell choices
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where ownership inflates judgment
- q07-q08: distinguish real market value from owner-inflated value
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on pricing from the perspective of a non-owner

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat ownership itself as proof of higher value
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming familiarity or use never matter at all
- one distractor can confuse personal attachment with objective market evidence
