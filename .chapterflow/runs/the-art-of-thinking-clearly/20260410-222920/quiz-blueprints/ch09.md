# Quiz Blueprint: ch09

- chapterId: ch09
- title: Authority Bias

## Concepts to test

1. authority bias means giving extra weight to a claim because of the speaker's status rather than the evidence
2. titles, uniforms, prestige, and rank can create borrowed credibility
3. the bias appears in work, school, and personal judgment when people defer too quickly
4. real expertise exists, so the correction is not to ignore authority but to test whether the evidence stands on its own
5. the core corrective question is whether the claim would still persuade if the prestige cue were removed

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify authority cues and mistaken deference in ordinary scenes
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where rank changes evaluation of a weak claim
- q07-q08: distinguish real expertise from authority-shaped overtrust
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on evidence-first judgment and status-removal tests

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat rank or credentials as sufficient proof by themselves
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming authority should never matter
- one distractor can confuse confidence, polish, or official appearance with actual evidential strength
