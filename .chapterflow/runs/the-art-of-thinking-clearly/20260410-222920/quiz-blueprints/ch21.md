# Quiz Blueprint: ch21

- chapterId: ch21
- title: Paradox of Choice

## Concepts to test

1. the paradox of choice means more options can make decisions harder and satisfaction lower
2. comparison cost and fear of missing out rise as the menu expands
3. more options can reduce commitment and increase post-decision doubt
4. correction requires narrowing options and setting criteria early
5. not every added option improves the real quality of the decision

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify paradox-of-choice patterns in ordinary menus and decisions
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where more options create overload
- q07-q08: distinguish helpful variety from comparison-heavy clutter
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on narrowing and criteria-first selection

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should assume bigger menus always improve decisions
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming choice is always harmful
- one distractor can confuse abundance with quality
