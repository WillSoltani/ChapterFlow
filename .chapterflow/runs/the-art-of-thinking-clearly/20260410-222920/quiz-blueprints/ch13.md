# Quiz Blueprint: ch13

- chapterId: ch13
- title: Story Bias

## Concepts to test

1. story bias means a neat narrative can outrank the actual evidence
2. coherent explanations feel safe and satisfying, which makes them persuasive
3. a clean story can hide missing facts, competing causes, and unresolved uncertainty
4. the bias appears in work, school, and personal life when narrative quality replaces evidence quality
5. correction requires asking what facts support the story and what uncertainty it is concealing

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify scenes where a satisfying explanation is being trusted too quickly
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where narrative neatness outruns the evidence
- q07-q08: distinguish a useful story from a supported conclusion
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on uncertainty, competing causes, and evidence checks

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat coherence as proof
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming stories are always useless
- one distractor can confuse emotional resonance with factual support
