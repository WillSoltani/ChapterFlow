# Quiz Blueprint: ch16

- chapterId: ch16
- title: Chauffeur Knowledge

## Concepts to test

1. chauffeur knowledge means performing expertise without deeper ownership of the knowledge
2. fluency can be mistaken for understanding
3. pressure, follow-up, and transfer questions expose shallow grasp
4. the bias appears in work, school, and personal life when polished talk gets overtrusted
5. correction requires testing depth rather than rewarding smooth delivery alone

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify chauffeur knowledge in ordinary explanations and performances
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where fluency outruns grasp
- q07-q08: distinguish smooth recitation from understanding under pressure
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on pressure-testing and real mastery

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat smooth explanation as proof of knowledge
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming teaching or presentation is useless
- one distractor can confuse memorized language with adaptive understanding
