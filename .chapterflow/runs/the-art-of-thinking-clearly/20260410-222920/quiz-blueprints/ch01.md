# Quiz Blueprint: ch01

- chapterId: ch01
- title: Survivorship Bias

## Concepts to test

1. visible winners are incomplete evidence
2. hidden failures change the real odds
3. denominator blindness distorts confidence
4. practical fix is to seek the missing graveyard, not just admire survivors
5. success stories can inspire and still mislead

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify survivorship bias in ordinary examples
- q04-q06: named-character work or school decisions distorted by visible winners
- q07-q08: distinguish inspiration from evidence
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on when success examples are useful and when they are dangerous

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should confuse vivid winners with representative evidence
- another distractor should confuse hard work with proof that the odds are favorable
- one distractor can treat failure as irrelevant noise
