# Quiz Blueprint: ch06

- chapterId: ch06
- title: Reciprocity

## Concepts to test

1. receiving something first can create pressure to give something back
2. gratitude and compliance are not the same thing
3. reciprocity can distort work, school, and personal decisions
4. a requested return should still be tested on its own merits
5. reciprocity can be socially useful without making every follow-on request legitimate

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify reciprocity pressure in ordinary examples
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases shaped by obligation after a favor
- q07-q08: distinguish healthy gratitude from manipulated compliance
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on the no-favor counterfactual and boundary-setting judgment

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat repayment as automatically owed
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming all favors are traps
- one distractor can confuse politeness discomfort with a real obligation
