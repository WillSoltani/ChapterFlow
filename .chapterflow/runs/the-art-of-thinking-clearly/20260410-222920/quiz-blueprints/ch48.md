# Quiz Blueprint: ch48

- chapterId: ch48
- title: Association Bias

## Concepts to test

1. judgments can borrow emotion from linked people, symbols, and outcomes
2. positive association can create undeserved overcredit
3. negative association can create undeserved overblame
4. linked feeling is not the same as earned merit
5. correction requires separating the object from the emotional transfer around it

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify association bias in ordinary cases
- q04-q06: work or school examples where admiration or disgust spreads unfairly
- q07-q08: distinguish relevant context from emotional contamination
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on borrowed prestige, borrowed stain, and judgment repair

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should assume a linked symbol proves quality directly
- another distractor should treat a disliked association as enough proof of flaw
- one distractor can overcorrect by claiming associations never matter at all
