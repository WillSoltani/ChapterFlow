# Quiz Blueprint: ch44

- chapterId: ch44
- title: Omission Bias

## Concepts to test

1. passive harm can be judged more gently than active harm
2. visibility of agency can distort moral judgment
3. omission can feel cleaner even when consequences remain serious
4. better judgment compares action and inaction by consequence and duty
5. correction requires putting omission and commission on the same evaluative scale

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify omission bias in ordinary settings
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where non-action gets a moral discount
- q07-q08: distinguish true outcome difference from agency-based softness
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on passive harm and responsibility

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should assume inaction is automatically less blameworthy
- another distractor should confuse lower visibility with lower responsibility
- one distractor can overcorrect by claiming action and inaction are always morally identical
