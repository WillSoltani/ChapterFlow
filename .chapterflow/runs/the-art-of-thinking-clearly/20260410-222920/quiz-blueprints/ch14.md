# Quiz Blueprint: ch14

- chapterId: ch14
- title: Hindsight Bias

## Concepts to test

1. hindsight bias means known outcomes feel more predictable after the fact
2. outcome knowledge can erase memory of earlier uncertainty
3. after-the-fact certainty can flatten real alternatives that were live beforehand
4. the bias appears in work, school, and personal life when people say the result was obvious all along
5. correction requires reconstructing what was actually known before the outcome and what alternatives were still open

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify hindsight bias in ordinary after-the-fact judgments
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where known results get treated as inevitable
- q07-q08: distinguish learning from an outcome from rewriting prior uncertainty
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on before-the-fact reconstruction and live alternatives

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat the known outcome as if it had always been obvious
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming outcomes teach nothing
- one distractor can confuse confidence after the fact with evidence before the fact
