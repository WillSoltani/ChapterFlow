# Quiz Blueprint: ch33

- chapterId: ch33
- title: Social Loafing

## Concepts to test

1. effort can drop when responsibility is shared broadly
2. hidden contribution weakens accountability
3. group settings can reduce effort without requiring open bad intent
4. visible ownership can counteract diluted responsibility
5. correction requires making contribution specific and attributable

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify social loafing in ordinary group settings
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases with blurred ownership
- q07-q08: distinguish collaboration from diluted accountability
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on visibility, attribution, and ownership of effort

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat lower group effort as always pure character failure
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming group work never lowers effort
- one distractor can confuse strong collaboration with hidden accountability
