# Quiz Blueprint: ch26

- chapterId: ch26
- title: Neglect of Probability

## Concepts to test

1. neglect of probability means reacting to outcomes without weighting their odds properly
2. vivid fear or hope can dominate attention even when likelihood is low
3. importance and probability are different parts of judgment
4. correction requires asking how likely the outcome really is
5. fairer decisions put the odds beside the story before reacting

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify neglect of probability in ordinary decisions
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where dramatic outcomes crowd out likelihood
- q07-q08: distinguish vivid possibility from actual chance
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on restoring odds to a fear or opportunity decision

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat emotional vividness as enough reason by itself
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming outcomes never matter if odds are low
- one distractor can confuse possibility with probability
