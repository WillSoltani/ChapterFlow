# Quiz Blueprint: ch24

- chapterId: ch24
- title: Coincidence

## Concepts to test

1. coincidence means random overlap gets over-read as meaningful connection
2. chance feels unsatisfying, so the mind prefers a story or pattern
3. surprise is not the same as evidence of hidden intent or destiny
4. correction requires asking how many opportunities there were for the overlap
5. fairer judgment widens the frame before declaring significance

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify coincidence overread in ordinary life
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where overlap gets mistaken for signal
- q07-q08: distinguish surprise from real support
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on opportunity count, pattern hunger, and meaning inflation

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat unusual timing or matching detail as proof by itself
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming no unusual overlap ever deserves attention
- one distractor can confuse emotional impact with evidentiary strength
