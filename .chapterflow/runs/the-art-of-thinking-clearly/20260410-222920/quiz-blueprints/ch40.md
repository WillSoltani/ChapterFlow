# Quiz Blueprint: ch40

- chapterId: ch40
- title: Forecast Illusion

## Concepts to test

1. detail can create false confidence in forecasts
2. coherent prediction stories can hide uncertainty
3. precise timing or numbers do not guarantee reliability
4. better forecasting judgment keeps ranges and unknowns visible
5. correction requires asking what the forecast leaves out

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify forecast illusion in ordinary settings
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where precision hides uncertainty
- q07-q08: distinguish useful planning from overconfident prediction
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on missing uncertainty and alternative outcomes

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat forecast detail as proof of accuracy
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming planning is pointless
- one distractor can confuse a precise estimate with a well-calibrated estimate
