# Quiz Blueprint: ch43

- chapterId: ch43
- title: Action Bias

## Concepts to test

1. pressure can make action feel safer than restraint
2. visible movement can be mistaken for effectiveness
3. discomfort with waiting can distort judgment toward intervention
4. better judgment tests whether action improves the result
5. correction requires separating outcome benefit from emotional relief

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify action bias in ordinary settings
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where pressure rewards movement
- q07-q08: distinguish useful action from performative intervention
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on discomfort, control, and restraint

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat action itself as proof of responsibility
- another distractor should confuse visible movement with outcome improvement
- one distractor can overcorrect by claiming waiting is always best
