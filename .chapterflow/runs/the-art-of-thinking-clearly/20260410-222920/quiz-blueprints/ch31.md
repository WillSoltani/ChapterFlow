# Quiz Blueprint: ch31

- chapterId: ch31
- title: Induction

## Concepts to test

1. induction means drawing broad rules from limited observations
2. a small pattern can feel stable before enough evidence exists
3. thin samples can support hypotheses but not necessarily strong conclusions
4. correction requires asking how much evidence is really present
5. fairer judgment delays general rules until the pattern has earned them

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify induction error in ordinary judgments
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where thin evidence becomes a broad rule
- q07-q08: distinguish a tentative pattern from a justified generalization
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on evidence thresholds and sample-size discipline

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat a few repeated observations as enough for a law
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming small observations are useless for any learning
- one distractor can confuse noticing a pattern with having proved it
