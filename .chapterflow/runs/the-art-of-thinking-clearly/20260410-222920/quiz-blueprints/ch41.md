# Quiz Blueprint: ch41

- chapterId: ch41
- title: Conjunction Fallacy

## Concepts to test

1. a detailed combined story can feel more believable than a simpler possibility
2. vivid fit is not the same as higher likelihood
3. the narrower case cannot exceed the broader parent case that contains it
4. better judgment checks the broader option before accepting the richer story
5. correction requires separating narrative satisfaction from probability sense

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify conjunction fallacy in ordinary settings
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where richer stories outrank simpler truths
- q07-q08: distinguish representativeness from actual likelihood
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on parent-case checking and combined claims

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should reward the most vivid and personality-matching story
- another distractor should confuse descriptive fit with statistical likelihood
- one distractor can overcorrect by claiming detail is always useless
