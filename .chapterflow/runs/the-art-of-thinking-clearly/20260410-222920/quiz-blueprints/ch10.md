# Quiz Blueprint: ch10

- chapterId: ch10
- title: Contrast Effect

## Concepts to test

1. contrast effect means judgments shift because of what was seen nearby
2. the same target can look better or worse when sequence changes
3. value, quality, and difficulty judgments can be distorted by immediate comparisons
4. the target itself may be unchanged while the comparison frame changes
5. correction requires isolating the target from the surrounding contrast cue

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify comparison-driven distortions in ordinary scenes
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where sequence changes evaluation
- q07-q08: distinguish real quality differences from contrast-driven impressions
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on isolation checks and sequence-aware judgment

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat the first impression after a contrast as objective truth
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming all comparison is invalid
- one distractor can confuse genuine quality change with frame-driven appearance change
