# Quiz Blueprint: ch38

- chapterId: ch38
- title: Halo Effect

## Concepts to test

1. one salient trait can spill over into unrelated evaluation
2. admiration and dislike both create halo effects
3. polished appearance or one flaw can distort competence judgments
4. correction requires separating observed evidence from extra inference
5. better judgment asks what the visible trait actually proves and what it does not

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify halo spillover in ordinary settings
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where one trait colors the whole judgment
- q07-q08: distinguish legitimate evidence from halo overreach
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on separating direct evidence from spillover inference

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat one positive or negative cue as enough for a broad verdict
- another distractor should overcorrect by denying that any first trait matters at all
- one distractor can confuse a real trait with the unrelated conclusions that trail behind it
