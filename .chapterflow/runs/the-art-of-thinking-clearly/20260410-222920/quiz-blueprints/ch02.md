# Quiz Blueprint: ch02

- chapterId: ch02
- title: Swimmer's Body Illusion

## Concepts to test

1. visible outcomes can be shaped by selection before they are shaped by training
2. elite examples are not clean evidence of what an activity produces for everyone
3. selection and causation can both be present, but they should not be collapsed
4. practical fix is to ask who gets drawn into, survives in, and becomes visible in a system
5. the illusion appears in work, school, and personal imitation, not only in sports

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify the illusion in everyday examples
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases that confuse selection with production
- q07-q08: distinguish training effects from filtering effects
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on how to copy a visible model without getting fooled by the sample

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat the visible winner as if it proves what the activity does for everyone
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming practice never matters
- one distractor can confuse admiration with evidence
