# Quiz Blueprint: ch04

- chapterId: ch04
- title: Social Proof

## Concepts to test

1. crowd behavior can be mistaken for truth, safety, or quality
2. imitation under uncertainty can create feedback loops
3. visible consensus is not the same as independent confirmation
4. practical fix is to ask what the group actually knows and whether members are adding independent evidence
5. social proof can distort work, school, and personal decisions

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify social proof in ordinary examples
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases distorted by visible consensus
- q07-q08: distinguish informative crowds from self-reinforcing crowds
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on when agreement deserves trust and when it should be discounted

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat popularity as automatic truth
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming groups are never informative
- one distractor can confuse comfort with evidence
