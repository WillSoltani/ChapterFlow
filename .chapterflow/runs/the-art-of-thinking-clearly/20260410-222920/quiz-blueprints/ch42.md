# Quiz Blueprint: ch42

- chapterId: ch42
- title: Framing

## Concepts to test

1. wording and presentation can change judgment without changing facts
2. gain and loss framing can redirect preference
3. labels and emphasis can shift attention to different aspects of the same choice
4. better judgment tests whether a preference survives alternate framing
5. correction requires neutral restatement before commitment

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify framing in ordinary settings
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where wording shifts judgment
- q07-q08: distinguish changed presentation from changed substance
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on reframing and neutral comparison

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat the most emotionally loaded wording as the truest version
- another distractor should confuse a frame change with a fact change
- one distractor can overcorrect by claiming wording never matters
