# Quiz Blueprint: ch15

- chapterId: ch15
- title: Overconfidence Effect

## Concepts to test

1. overconfidence means certainty exceeds what evidence supports
2. strong confidence can coexist with weak grounding
3. the bias appears in work, school, and personal life when people speak beyond what they know
4. action may require confidence, but judgment still needs calibration
5. correction requires asking what evidence actually earns the current level of certainty

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify overconfidence in ordinary judgments and forecasts
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where certainty outruns support
- q07-q08: distinguish confidence from evidence quality
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on calibration and earned conviction

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat strong confidence as proof
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming confidence is always bad
- one distractor can confuse decisiveness with evidentiary support
