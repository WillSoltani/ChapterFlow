# Quiz Blueprint: ch25

- chapterId: ch25
- title: Groupthink

## Concepts to test

1. groupthink means belonging pressure suppresses dissent
2. silence can be false consensus rather than true agreement
3. groups can become less accurate when disagreement feels unsafe
4. correction requires creating real space for objection before closure
5. fairer group judgment asks what remains unsaid because of social pressure

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify groupthink in ordinary group decisions
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where dissent gets suppressed
- q07-q08: distinguish real consensus from pressured silence
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on creating dissent safety and testing false agreement

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat silence as proof of strong agreement
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming groups should always stay in conflict
- one distractor can confuse speed and harmony with decision quality
