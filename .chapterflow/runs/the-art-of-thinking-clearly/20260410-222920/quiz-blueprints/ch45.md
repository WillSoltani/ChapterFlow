# Quiz Blueprint: ch45

- chapterId: ch45
- title: Self-Serving Bias

## Concepts to test

1. success is often attributed inward while failure is attributed outward
2. ego protection can distort causal explanation
3. identity concerns can reshape the story of outcomes
4. better judgment tests whether the same standard is used for wins and losses
5. correction requires symmetrical evidence across positive and negative results

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify self-serving bias in ordinary settings
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where outcome stories protect ego
- q07-q08: distinguish confidence from biased attribution
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on symmetric explanation and learning

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat favorable outcomes as obvious proof of personal virtue
- another distractor should excuse failure with unsupported external stories
- one distractor can overcorrect by denying any personal role in success
