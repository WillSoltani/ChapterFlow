# Quiz Blueprint: ch35

- chapterId: ch35
- title: Winner's Curse

## Concepts to test

1. winning can be evidence of overestimating value
2. competitive pressure can make winning feel more validating than it deserves
3. the highest bid or strongest offer may win for the wrong reason
4. a win should trigger scrutiny of assumptions and price
5. correction requires treating the win as diagnostic information

## Question spread

- q01-q03: identify winner's curse in ordinary competitive settings
- q04-q06: named-character work or school cases where winning masks a bad deal
- q07-q08: distinguish healthy competition from overaggressive bidding
- q09-q10: deeper transfer on informational meaning of winning

## Distractor policy

- tempting wrong answers should treat winning as automatic proof of good judgment
- another distractor should overcorrect by claiming every win is bad
- one distractor can confuse fair confidence with ignoring what the win implies
