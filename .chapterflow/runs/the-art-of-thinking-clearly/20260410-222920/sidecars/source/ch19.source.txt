Chapter: Regression to Mean

Frozen support for this chapter is narrow but sufficient:

- Google Books confirms the chapter heading "Regression to Mean."
- The safest paraphrase from the heading and skeleton is that extreme outcomes often move back toward typical levels and that rebound is often misread as intervention effect.
- The official Hachette source-notes PDF confirms the chapter heading and supports keeping the chapter in the family of common decision errors.

Safe paraphrase zone:

- unusually high or low results often soften naturally
- the next less-extreme outcome is not automatic proof that the last action caused it
- a corrective move is to compare the rebound against ordinary variation before telling a causal story

Unsupported zone:

- exact quotes
- blanket claims that feedback or interventions never work
- broad fatalism that treats every change as random noise
