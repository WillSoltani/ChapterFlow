Chapter: Endowment Effect

Frozen support for this chapter is narrow but sufficient:

- Google Books confirms the chapter heading "Endowment Effect."
- The safest paraphrase from the heading and skeleton is that ownership can inflate value because possession changes perception.
- The official Hachette source-notes PDF confirms the chapter heading and supports keeping the chapter in the family of common decision errors.

Safe paraphrase zone:

- owning something can make it seem more valuable
- possession can make giving something up feel more expensive
- a corrective move is to judge price or worth as if the object were not already yours

Unsupported zone:

- exact quotes
- blanket claims that all attachment is irrational
- broad claims that owners are always wrong about value
