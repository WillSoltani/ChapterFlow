Chapter: Liking Bias

Frozen support for this chapter is narrow but sufficient:

- Google Books confirms the chapter heading "Liking Bias."
- The safest paraphrase from the heading and skeleton is that liking or dislike can distort judgment by making it too generous or too harsh.
- The official Hachette source-notes PDF confirms the chapter heading and supports keeping the chapter in the family of common decision errors.

Safe paraphrase zone:

- affection can soften scrutiny
- dislike can harden judgment
- a corrective move is to separate the source you feel about from the thing you are evaluating

Unsupported zone:

- exact quotes
- blanket claims that emotion always ruins judgment
- broad claims that all positive regard is irrational
