Chapter: Availability Bias

Frozen support for this chapter is narrow but sufficient:

- Google Books confirms the chapter heading "Availability Bias."
- The safest paraphrase from the heading and skeleton is that vivid, recent, or emotional examples crowd out representative evidence because they are easier to recall.
- The official Hachette source-notes PDF confirms the chapter heading and supports keeping the chapter in the family of common decision errors.

Safe paraphrase zone:

- easy recall can be mistaken for real frequency or importance
- vivid and recent examples can distort judgment because memory offers them first
- a corrective move is to ask what evidence is representative, not only memorable

Unsupported zone:

- exact quotes
- broad media-system or cultural claims not frozen in this bundle
- claims that memorable examples are always irrelevant
