Chapter: Contrast Effect

Frozen support for this chapter is narrow but sufficient:

- Google Books confirms the chapter heading "Contrast Effect."
- The safest paraphrase from the heading and skeleton is that nearby comparisons distort value and make neutral things look better or worse than they would in isolation.
- The official Hachette source-notes PDF confirms the chapter heading and supports keeping the chapter in the family of common decision errors.

Safe paraphrase zone:

- sequence and comparison frames can distort evaluation
- the same target can feel different after a stronger or weaker comparison item
- a corrective move is to judge the target again in isolation

- exact quotes
