Chapter: Outcome Bias

Frozen support for this chapter is narrow but sufficient:

- Google Books confirms the chapter heading "Outcome Bias."
- The safest paraphrase from the heading and skeleton is that visible results can distort evaluation of the decision process that produced them.
- The official Hachette source-notes PDF confirms the chapter heading and supports keeping the chapter in the family of common decision errors.

Safe paraphrase zone:

- a good result can flatter a bad decision process
- a bad result can hide a sound process
- a corrective move is to inspect the reasoning before letting the outcome decide the verdict

Unsupported zone:

- exact quotes
- claims that outcomes never matter
- broad fatalism that treats decision quality as unknowable
