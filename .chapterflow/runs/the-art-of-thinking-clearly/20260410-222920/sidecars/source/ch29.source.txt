Chapter: Gambler's Fallacy

Frozen support for this chapter is narrow but sufficient:

- Google Books confirms the chapter heading "Gambler's Fallacy."
- The safest paraphrase from the heading and skeleton is that people can mistake random streaks for self-correcting systems.
- The official Hachette source-notes PDF confirms the chapter heading and supports keeping the chapter in the family of common decision errors.

Safe paraphrase zone:

- a streak does not make the opposite outcome due in an independent random process
- random events can be mistaken for systems with memory
- a corrective move is to ask whether the next event is actually influenced by the previous ones

Unsupported zone:

- exact quotes
- blanket claims that patterns never matter in any system
- broad probability instruction beyond the chapter's narrow independence-and-streak scope
