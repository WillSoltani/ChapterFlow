Chapter: Authority Bias

Frozen support for this chapter is narrow but sufficient:

- Google Books confirms the chapter heading "Authority Bias."
- The safest paraphrase from the heading and skeleton is that status cues can overpower evidence, especially when expertise is assumed rather than demonstrated.
- The official Hachette source-notes PDF confirms the chapter heading and supports keeping the chapter in the family of common decision errors.

Safe paraphrase zone:

- titles, rank, uniforms, prestige, and confidence can create borrowed credibility
- people can defer too quickly because a high-status source feels trustworthy
- a corrective move is to test whether the claim still works when the prestige cue is mentally removed

Unsupported zone:

- exact quotes
- broad anti-institution or anti-expert claims not frozen in this bundle
- claims that all authority is illegitimate
