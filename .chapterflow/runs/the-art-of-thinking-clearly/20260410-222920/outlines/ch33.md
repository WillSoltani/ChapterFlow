# Outline: ch33

## Governing principle

Shared responsibility can reduce effort when individual contribution fades into the crowd.

## Canonical prose movement

1. Hook: effort changes when no one can clearly see who carried what.
2. Mechanism: responsibility diffuses and personal accountability weakens.
3. Anchor: work, school, and personal group tasks all show hidden contribution lowering effort.
4. Application: group settings can invite less effort without anyone naming it directly.
5. Tension: teams matter, but team structure can either dilute or sharpen ownership.
6. Bridge: better judgment makes contribution visible and specific.

## Depth intentions

- Easy: people may work less when their effort disappears into the group.
- Medium: diluted accountability can lower effort even without bad intent.
- Hard: fairer judgment tests whether effort would hold if contribution were clearly attributable.

## Threshold question for hard

Would the same level of effort appear if each person's contribution were visible and owned?

## Example routing

- work: shared projects, meetings, and team tasks with blurred ownership
- school: group assignments and study projects with uneven contribution
- personal: volunteer tasks, household coordination, and informal group plans

## Reinforcement surfaces

- recap retrieve should ask why visibility changes effort
- implementation plan should force one move from shared vagueness to explicit ownership
