# Outline: ch41

## Governing principle

Narrative richness can make a narrower combined claim feel more probable than the broader possibility that contains it.

## Canonical prose movement

1. Hook: a detailed story often feels more convincing than a plain one.
2. Mechanism: specificity creates coherence, and coherence gets mistaken for likelihood.
3. Anchor: hiring impressions, student predictions, relationship judgments, and news interpretation all reward richer stories.
4. Application: people choose the version that sounds like it fits better, even when the simpler category must cover more cases.
5. Tension: the mind prefers a satisfying picture over a sparse but more probable truth.
6. Bridge: better judgment compares the specific story to the broader parent case before accepting the detailed version.

## Depth intentions

- Easy: a vivid detailed explanation can feel true without being more likely.
- Medium: the narrower story inherits all the limits of the broader one and cannot outrank it.
- Hard: judgment should test whether narrative fit is replacing a basic containment check.

## Threshold question for hard

If the detailed version must first be part of the broader possibility, why am I treating the narrower story as more likely?

## Example routing

- work: candidate impressions, market narratives, project risk stories
- school: student typecasting, club predictions, application judgments
- personal: dating interpretations, friend assumptions, health worries, everyday social reading

## Reinforcement surfaces

- recap retrieve should ask why richer stories feel more probable than they are
- implementation plan should force one step of checking the broader parent case before trusting the detailed claim
