# Outline: ch28

## Governing principle

Specific detail can overpower the broader statistical pattern that should frame the judgment.

## Canonical prose movement

1. Hook: one vivid case arrives and the larger pattern disappears from sight.
2. Mechanism: specific detail feels more diagnostic than abstract population information.
3. Anchor: work, school, and personal life all show people trusting the case over the base rate.
4. Application: decisions drift because the unusual story feels more real than what usually happens.
5. Tension: case detail matters, but it should not erase the background pattern.
6. Bridge: better judgment asks what normally happens before letting the case take over.

## Depth intentions

- Easy: one specific case can make people forget the wider pattern.
- Medium: vivid detail can crowd out population-level frequency.
- Hard: fairer judgment checks the base rate before trusting the case too much.

## Threshold question for hard

What does the broader population usually look like before I let this case dominate the conclusion?

## Example routing

- work: hiring, forecasting, and client judgments based on one story over general rates
- school: interpreting student performance, admissions, or outcomes from vivid cases
- personal: medical fears, social impressions, and everyday judgments where one example overpowers population reality

## Reinforcement surfaces

- recap retrieve should ask why one case is not the whole pattern
- implementation plan should force one step of naming the base rate before concluding
