# Outline: ch46

## Governing principle

Positive changes often become normal faster than expected, reducing their long-term emotional payoff.

## Canonical prose movement

1. Hook: a desired upgrade or achievement feels life-changing before it becomes ordinary.
2. Mechanism: adaptation resets the emotional baseline after novelty fades.
3. Anchor: purchases, promotions, grades, routines, and personal milestones all lose emotional intensity over time.
4. Application: people keep overestimating how long a gain will keep them happier.
5. Tension: gains can still matter, but their emotional effect is rarely as durable as imagined.
6. Bridge: better judgment distinguishes lasting value from temporary emotional lift.

## Depth intentions

- Easy: new gains often stop feeling new quickly.
- Medium: adaptation makes the improved state feel ordinary.
- Hard: judgment should test whether the pursuit is for durable value or for a short-lived emotional spike.

## Threshold question for hard

Am I chasing the lasting value of this gain, or the short-lived emotional surge of getting it?

## Example routing

- work: promotions, raises, recognition, new roles
- school: grades, admissions, wins, accomplishments
- personal: purchases, lifestyle upgrades, milestones, routines

## Reinforcement surfaces

- recap retrieve should ask why gains lose emotional force faster than expected
- implementation plan should force one distinction between lasting utility and fading excitement
