# Outline: ch02

## Governing principle

Visible excellence can make an activity look more causally powerful than it is because selection has already shaped who stays visible.

## Canonical prose movement

1. Hook: a pool deck or sports poster makes the swimmer's body look like a purchasable result.
2. Mechanism: people confuse traits selected into a field with traits produced entirely by the field.
3. Anchor: height, frame, persistence, and fit can influence who becomes a visible swimmer before training gets credited.
4. Application: careers, schools, companies, and hobbies all create the same illusion when visible members look like proof of what the system produces.
5. Tension: practice still matters, but not in the simple way the visible outcome suggests.
6. Bridge: better judgment asks what kind of person or condition gets selected into the visible sample before drawing a lesson.

## Depth intentions

- Easy: do not copy the surface result without asking who was likely to end up there.
- Medium: the mechanism is selection versus production.
- Hard: visible outcomes often advertise a system more confidently than the hidden selection process warrants.

## Threshold question for hard

How much of the admired outcome was built by the activity, and how much of it was filtered into view before the activity got the credit?

## Example routing

- work: elite firms that appear to create exceptional people but also select them heavily
- school: scholarship winners or top students whose visible polish gets mistaken for something the program fully produced
- personal: fitness or hobby choices copied because the visible practitioners look like proof

## Reinforcement surfaces

- recap retrieve should ask what hidden selection filter may be operating
- implementation plan should force one selection-versus-causation check before copying a visible winner
