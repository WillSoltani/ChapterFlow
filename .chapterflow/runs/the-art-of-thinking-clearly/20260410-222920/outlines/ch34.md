# Outline: ch34

## Governing principle

Compounding growth often outruns intuition because the early curve looks gentler than the later reality.

## Canonical prose movement

1. Hook: growth looks manageable at first and then accelerates past expectation.
2. Mechanism: intuition projects linearly while the process is compounding.
3. Anchor: work, school, and personal situations all suffer when multiplicative growth is treated like straight-line increase.
4. Application: early calm stages hide what later acceleration will do.
5. Tension: slow beginnings can feel safe even when the structure is not safe.
6. Bridge: better judgment tests repeated percentage change directly rather than trusting the eye.

## Depth intentions

- Easy: compounding growth does not move like simple addition.
- Medium: early stages can look small and still become large quickly later.
- Hard: fairer judgment checks whether a process is multiplicative before projecting it forward.

## Threshold question for hard

Am I assuming straight-line change in a situation where each increase builds on the last one?

## Example routing

- work: debt, subscriptions, audience growth, and demand curves
- school: study backlog, missed work accumulation, and repeated percentage improvement
- personal: savings, interest, habits, and spreading effects over time

## Reinforcement surfaces

- recap retrieve should ask why early compounding can look deceptively small
- implementation plan should force one move from linear guess to explicit compounding check
