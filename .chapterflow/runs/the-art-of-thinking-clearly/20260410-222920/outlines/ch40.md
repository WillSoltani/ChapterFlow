# Outline: ch40

## Governing principle

Forecast detail can create an illusion of knowledge that exceeds what the future will actually allow.

## Canonical prose movement

1. Hook: a precise forecast sounds reassuring and therefore more trustworthy than it should.
2. Mechanism: detail and coherent narrative disguise real uncertainty.
3. Anchor: plans, schedules, budgets, market calls, and personal timelines all invite precision beyond what is justified.
4. Application: people trust forecasts because they feel concrete, not because uncertainty has truly shrunk.
5. Tension: planning matters, but a plan is not proof that the future will honor it.
6. Bridge: better judgment asks what the forecast does not know and what range of outcomes still deserves respect.

## Depth intentions

- Easy: detailed forecasts can still be badly wrong.
- Medium: precision can hide large uncertainty instead of removing it.
- Hard: judgment should inspect what the forecast leaves out, not only what it confidently includes.

## Threshold question for hard

What uncertainty, alternative outcomes, or unknowns are being hidden by the forecast's precision?

## Example routing

- work: timelines, budgets, launches, revenue forecasts
- school: study plans, grade predictions, application outcomes
- personal: moving plans, savings timelines, project completion, life schedules

## Reinforcement surfaces

- recap retrieve should ask why detailed predictions feel safer than they are
- implementation plan should force one step of naming uncertainty range before trusting a forecast
