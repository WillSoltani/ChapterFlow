# Outline: ch01

## Governing principle

Visible success is biased evidence because the failures that define the real odds are harder to see.

## Canonical prose movement

1. Hook: a shelf full of bestseller stories or celebrated winners creates a false map.
2. Mechanism: the mind mistakes visibility for frequency.
3. Anchor: failed projects and vanished attempts form the missing denominator.
4. Application: career advice, investing, and entrepreneurship become distorted when only success stories are sampled.
5. Tension: success stories are still useful, but only after the hidden graveyard is restored to view.
6. Bridge: better judgment starts by asking what did not survive long enough to be admired.

## Depth intentions

- Easy: winner stories feel persuasive because losers are hard to see.
- Medium: the mechanism is denominator blindness plus selection effects.
- Hard: success stories are not useless, but they are dangerous when used without the hidden failure field.

## Threshold question for hard

At what point does inspiration from winners turn into statistical self-deception?

## Example routing

- work: startup or product launch selection
- school: students copying the visible top scorer's habits while missing the broader cohort
- personal: fitness or career imitation based on survivors only

## Reinforcement surfaces

- recap retrieve should ask what evidence is missing when a winner story feels convincing
- implementation plan should force one graveyard check before a major decision
