## Core idea

Judgment borrows emotion from nearby people, symbols, and outcomes, so a good or bad feeling can spread to something that did not actually earn it.

## Required anchors

1. the chapter centers on borrowed emotion distorting evaluation
2. people, brands, ideas, and choices can be judged by what they are linked with rather than by their own merits
3. practical correction: separate the thing itself from the glow or stain carried by its associations

## Allowed support

- The frozen title supports a narrow paraphrase about associative emotional transfer in judgment.
- The skeleton scope supports examples from brands, teams, celebrities, friends, workplaces, or ideas that gain borrowed appeal or borrowed disgust from nearby symbols or outcomes.
- The official source-notes PDF confirms the chapter heading and supports keeping the concept inside judgment errors rather than broad moral philosophy.

## Boundaries

- Stay narrow to borrowed emotional coloring through association.
- Do not drift into full advertising theory or deep psychoanalysis.
