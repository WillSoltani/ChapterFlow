## Core idea

A good result can make a poor decision process look wise, and a bad result can make a sound process look foolish.

## Required anchors

1. outcome bias means judging the quality of a decision mostly by how things turned out
2. luck, variance, and hidden conditions can separate decision quality from visible result quality
3. practical correction: inspect the reasoning and process before letting the outcome decide the verdict
