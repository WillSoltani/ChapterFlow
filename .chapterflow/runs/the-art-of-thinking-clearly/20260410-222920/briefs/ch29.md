## Core idea

Random sequences get mistaken for self-correcting systems.

## Required anchors

1. gambler's fallacy means people think past random outcomes change what independent random events are now due to do
2. streaks can create the false feeling that the opposite result is becoming more likely
3. practical correction: ask whether the next event is actually influenced by the previous ones or only feels that way
