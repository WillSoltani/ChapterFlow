## Core idea

Once people carry a label or social impression into a judgment, they start noticing behavior that confirms the label and treating conflicting behavior as less representative.

## Required anchors

1. confirmation bias can extend from facts and arguments into judgments about people and groups
2. stereotypes and social sorting feel stable because confirming behavior gets noticed first while conflicting behavior gets explained away
3. practical correction: force an individuating check and ask what concrete evidence would make the label weaker
