## Core idea

People adapt to gains faster than they expect, so improvements often lose emotional force sooner than the original hope promised.

## Required anchors

1. the chapter centers on adaptation reducing the lasting emotional payoff of gains
2. novelty fades and the new normal becomes ordinary quickly
3. practical correction: separate the excitement of change from the long-term emotional baseline it is likely to become
