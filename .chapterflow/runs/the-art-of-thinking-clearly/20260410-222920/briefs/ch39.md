## Core idea

The path that happened can feel inevitable or uniquely sensible because the abandoned alternatives are no longer visible in the same way.

## Required anchors

1. the chapter centers on forgetting or underseeing the roads not taken
2. the chosen path starts feeling natural because alternatives fade from view
3. practical correction: deliberately reconstruct realistic alternatives before judging the chosen path as obviously right
