## Core idea

Once people form an initial view, they begin protecting it by noticing, selecting, and welcoming information that fits it more easily than information that challenges it.

## Required anchors

1. prior conclusions change what evidence gets noticed or treated as relevant
2. confirming inputs feel easier, cleaner, and more persuasive than disconfirming inputs
3. practical correction: deliberately ask what would count against the current view before collecting more support for it
