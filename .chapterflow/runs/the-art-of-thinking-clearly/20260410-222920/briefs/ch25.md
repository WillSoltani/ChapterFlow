## Core idea

Belonging pressure suppresses dissent long before a bad decision becomes visible.

## Required anchors

1. groupthink means the desire for unity and safety inside a group starts crowding out honest disagreement
2. silence and conformity can look like consensus even when doubts remain active underneath
3. practical correction: create pressure for dissent before the group settles too fast
