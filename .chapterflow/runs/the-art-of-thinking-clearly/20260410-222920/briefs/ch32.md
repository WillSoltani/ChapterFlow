## Core idea

Avoiding losses often weighs more heavily than acquiring equivalent gains.

## Required anchors

1. people often react more strongly to the possibility of losing something than to the possibility of gaining the same amount
2. this can distort judgment by making protective choices feel more compelling than balanced evaluation would support
3. practical correction: ask whether the feared loss is being weighted more heavily than an equivalent gain simply because it is framed as a loss
