## Core idea

People often treat other people's behavior as evidence of what is true, safe, or appropriate, even when the group is merely copying itself under uncertainty or social pressure.

## Required anchors

1. crowd behavior as a shortcut to certainty
2. belonging pressure turning imitation into fake evidence
3. practical correction: ask whether the group knows something real or is only amplifying visible consensus
