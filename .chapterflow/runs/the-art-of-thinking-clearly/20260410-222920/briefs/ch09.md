## Core idea

People can mistake status signals, titles, uniforms, confidence, and position for evidence, allowing authority to overpower thinner reasoning than it deserves.

## Required anchors

1. authority bias is about borrowing confidence from status cues rather than from the strength of the evidence
2. people often comply, trust, or defer more quickly when an idea comes from a high-status source
3. practical correction: separate who is speaking from what support the claim actually has
