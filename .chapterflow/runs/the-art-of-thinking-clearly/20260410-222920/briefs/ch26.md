## Core idea

Dramatic outcomes hijack judgment when the actual odds stay invisible or ignored.

## Required anchors

1. neglect of probability means people react to a possible outcome without properly weighting how likely it is
2. vivid, frightening, or exciting scenarios can dominate attention even when their odds are small
3. practical correction: force the likelihood question into the decision before reacting to the outcome alone
