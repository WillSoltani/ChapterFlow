## Core idea

People tend to explain success in ways that protect self-image and explain failure in ways that soften personal blame.

## Required anchors

1. the chapter centers on ego-protective interpretation of outcomes
2. wins get linked to personal ability more easily than losses get linked to personal error
3. practical correction: evaluate success and failure with the same standard of evidence and contribution
