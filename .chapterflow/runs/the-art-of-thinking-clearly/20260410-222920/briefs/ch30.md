## Core idea

First numbers quietly set the range of later judgment.

## Required anchors

1. anchoring means an initial number or reference point exerts too much influence on later estimates or decisions
2. early figures can shape what feels high, low, fair, or plausible even when the figure is arbitrary
3. practical correction: generate an independent estimate before reacting to the anchor
