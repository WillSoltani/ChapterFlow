## Core idea

Early success can arrive before real skill exists, causing luck to be misread as evidence of lasting ability.

## Required anchors

1. the chapter centers on first wins creating false confidence about durable competence
2. early favorable outcomes can come from randomness, timing, or low initial difficulty rather than stable skill
3. practical correction: separate the result of the first round from the evidence needed to trust repeatable ability
