## Core idea

People see patterns in random groupings because the mind is uncomfortable with noise and would rather promote coincidence into a meaningful structure.

## Required anchors

1. random streaks or bunching that feel designed
2. the mind turning coincidence into story, cause, or warning
3. practical correction: ask what would look strange only because you expected randomness to appear smoother than it does
