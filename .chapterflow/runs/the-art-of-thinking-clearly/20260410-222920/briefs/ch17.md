## Core idea

People often act as if ritual, activity, or confidence gives them control over outcomes that are still mostly shaped by chance.

## Required anchors

1. the illusion of control means mistaking influence-feeling for real control over chance
2. extra action, ritual, or personalization can create false ownership of the outcome
3. practical correction: separate controllable inputs from uncontrollable results
