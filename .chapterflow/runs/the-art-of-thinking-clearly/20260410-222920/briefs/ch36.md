## Core idea

People often explain behavior by character or personality while failing to give proper weight to the surrounding situation.

## Required anchors

1. the chapter centers on explaining behavior too quickly through stable traits
2. context, pressure, incentives, and circumstance often disappear from judgment
3. practical correction: ask what the situation might be doing before concluding what the person is
