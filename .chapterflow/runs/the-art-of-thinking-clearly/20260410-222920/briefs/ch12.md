## Core idea

Temporary pain, confusion, or disruption is often overread as proof that improvement is underway, even when the pain may simply be waste, friction, or failure.

## Required anchors

1. the fallacy treats deterioration as evidence of progress without checking whether the pain is connected to improvement
2. people can romanticize discomfort, confusion, or setbacks because struggle feels meaningful
3. practical correction: ask what mechanism links the present pain to a better outcome
