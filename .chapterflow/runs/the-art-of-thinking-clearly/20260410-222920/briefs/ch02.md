## Core idea

People often mistake selection for causation. They see the bodies of elite swimmers and conclude swimming created that body type, when the more accurate story is often that people with certain traits are more likely to become visible swimmers in the first place.

## Required anchors

1. swimmer's body as a misleading advertisement for training
2. selection before performance: people with fitting traits are more likely to enter, persist, and become visible
3. practical correction: ask whether the outcome is selecting the people or being caused by the activity
