## Core idea

When participation is voluntary, the people who step forward are often different from the silent majority, so the sample can look more extreme, more enthusiastic, more angry, or more committed than reality.

## Required anchors

1. the chapter centers on voluntary participation distorting what a sample appears to prove
2. people who respond, join, complain, review, or opt in are not random representatives of everyone else
3. practical correction: ask who is missing before trusting the apparent pattern

## Allowed support
