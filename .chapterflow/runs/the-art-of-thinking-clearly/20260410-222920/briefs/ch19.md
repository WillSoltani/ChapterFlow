## Core idea

Extreme results often move back toward a more typical level on their own, and people misread that natural softening as proof that an intervention caused the change.

## Required anchors

1. regression to the mean means unusually high or low results often soften naturally over time
2. people often attach the next result to praise, punishment, or a recent change even when the swing would likely have eased anyway
3. practical correction: compare against natural variation before claiming the intervention worked
