## Core idea

Under pressure, movement and intervention can feel safer or smarter than restraint even when waiting or holding steady would lead to the better outcome.

## Required anchors

1. the chapter centers on acting because action feels relieving, responsible, or courageous
2. pressure, uncertainty, and visibility can make doing something feel better than doing the right thing
3. practical correction: ask whether the action improves the outcome or only reduces the discomfort of standing still
