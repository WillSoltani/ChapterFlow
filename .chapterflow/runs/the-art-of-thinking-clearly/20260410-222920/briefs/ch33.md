## Core idea

Individual effort often drops when responsibility is spread across a group.

## Required anchors

1. social loafing means people may contribute less when their effort is harder to isolate inside a group
2. shared responsibility can weaken personal effort because accountability feels diluted
3. practical correction: make contribution visible, specific, and owned rather than hidden inside the crowd
