## Core idea

Once an outcome is known, people start treating it as if it had been more obvious and more inevitable than it really was before the event happened.

## Required anchors

1. hindsight bias makes known outcomes feel predictable after the fact
2. outcome knowledge hides how much uncertainty existed beforehand
3. practical correction: reconstruct what was actually known before the event and what live alternatives existed
