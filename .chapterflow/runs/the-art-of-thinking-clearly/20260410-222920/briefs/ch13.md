## Core idea

People prefer neat, causal narratives to messier reality, so a coherent story can feel truer than incomplete or uncertain evidence.

## Required anchors

1. story bias means explanation quality can overpower evidence quality
2. neat narratives feel safer and more satisfying than ambiguity
3. practical correction: ask what facts support the story and what uncertainty the story is hiding
