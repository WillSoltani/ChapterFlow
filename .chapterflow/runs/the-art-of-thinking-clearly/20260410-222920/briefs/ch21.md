## Core idea

More options can feel liberating at first but often reduce clarity, commitment, and satisfaction when comparison and second-guessing start taking over.

## Required anchors

1. the paradox of choice means larger choice sets can make deciding and feeling satisfied harder rather than easier
2. extra options increase comparison costs, fear of missing a better choice, and post-decision doubt
3. practical correction: narrow the field and define criteria before entering a large option set
