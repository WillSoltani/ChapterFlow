## Core idea

People judge value, size, quality, and difficulty relative to what they just saw nearby, so neutral things can look better or worse because of comparison rather than because of their own merits.

## Required anchors

1. contrast effect means nearby comparisons distort evaluation
2. a thing can feel bigger, smaller, better, worse, easier, or harder because of what came right before it
3. practical correction: evaluate the object on its own terms after noticing the comparison frame
