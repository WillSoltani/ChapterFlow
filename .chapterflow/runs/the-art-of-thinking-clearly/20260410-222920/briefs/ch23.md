## Core idea

Once people possess something, they start valuing it more highly because ownership changes perception.

## Required anchors

1. the endowment effect means ownership inflates value beyond what the object would command from a more neutral viewpoint
2. possession makes giving something up feel costlier than acquiring it felt before ownership
3. practical correction: price, judge, or negotiate as if the item were not already yours
