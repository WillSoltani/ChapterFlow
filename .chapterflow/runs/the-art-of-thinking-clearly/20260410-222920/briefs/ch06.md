## Core idea

People can feel pressured to repay favors, gifts, attention, or concessions even when the return response is not actually wise or freely chosen.

## Required anchors

1. favors and gestures can create silent obligation pressure
2. repayment instinct can override independent judgment about what is fair, useful, or wanted
3. practical correction: separate gratitude from compliance and ask whether you would choose the same response without the favor

## Allowed support

- The frozen chapter title supports a narrow favor-obligation reading across sales, work, school, and personal life.

## Boundaries

- Stay narrow. Do not turn the chapter into a general ethics essay about generosity.
