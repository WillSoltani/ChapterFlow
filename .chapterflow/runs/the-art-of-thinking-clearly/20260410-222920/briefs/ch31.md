## Core idea

Small observed patterns get promoted into sweeping rules too early.

## Required anchors

1. induction means drawing broad conclusions from limited observations
2. a small pattern can feel stable even when the sample is too thin to support a strong general rule
3. practical correction: ask how much evidence is really behind the pattern before turning it into a law
