## Core idea

Incentives can reshape attention, belief, and behavior so strongly that people underestimate how much the reward structure is driving what they see and do.

## Required anchors

1. incentives do not just influence behavior at the edges; they can reorganize perception, motivation, and judgment
2. people often explain conduct in moral or personal terms while undercounting the payoff structure behind it
3. practical correction: inspect what the system rewards before trusting the visible behavior or stated reasoning
