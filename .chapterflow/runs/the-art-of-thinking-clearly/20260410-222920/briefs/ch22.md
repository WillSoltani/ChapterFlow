## Core idea

When people like someone or something, their judgment becomes too generous; when they dislike it, judgment becomes too harsh.

## Required anchors

1. liking bias means affection or aversion spills into evaluations that should be more separate and sober
2. positive feeling can make flaws look smaller and negative feeling can make them look larger
3. practical correction: separate the object of judgment from the feeling toward the person, brand, or source
