## Core idea

When actions, beliefs, or self-image clash, discomfort appears and people often resolve it by changing the story instead of changing the behavior.

## Required anchors

1. the chapter centers on internal inconsistency creating tension
2. people often reduce that tension by rationalizing, reframing, or selectively editing the narrative
3. practical correction: notice whether you are solving the conflict or only making the story feel less painful

## Allowed support

- The frozen title supports a narrow paraphrase about internal contradiction and story adjustment.
- The skeleton scope supports ordinary cases in spending, work, school, relationships, or habits where behavior and belief do not line up cleanly.
