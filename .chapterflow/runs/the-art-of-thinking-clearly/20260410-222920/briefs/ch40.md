## Core idea

Detailed predictions can feel credible and precise even when the future remains far less knowable than the model or narrative suggests.

## Required anchors

1. the chapter centers on overconfidence in detailed forecasts
2. specificity and narrative coherence can hide how much uncertainty still remains
3. practical correction: ask what the forecast does not know and what wider range of outcomes should still stay visible
