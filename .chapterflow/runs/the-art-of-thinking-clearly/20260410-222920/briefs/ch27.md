## Core idea

Rarity inflates desire even when value has not changed.

## Required anchors

1. scarcity error means limited supply or access starts making something seem more valuable than it really is
2. urgency and rarity can create desire without adding corresponding quality
3. practical correction: ask whether the item would still deserve the same interest if it were easy to get
