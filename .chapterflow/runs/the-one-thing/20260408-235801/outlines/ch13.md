Chapter Promise:
Show that strong answers need a larger direction, so the reader stops treating focus as a detached tactic and starts connecting it to purpose.

Opening Move:
Open on the weakness of a strong answer that still lacks a larger reason or direction.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): 1-2 -> Live With Purpose as title and core claim
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): 3-4 -> purpose as the larger frame for focus
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): 5-6 -> purpose as direction rather than vague motivation alone

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: introduce purpose as the next issue after better answers
- P2: explain why focus without purpose can stay narrow or detached
- P3: define purpose as an organizing frame
- P4: distinguish operational purpose from vague inspiration
- P5: apply purpose logic in work, school, and personal settings
- P6: preserve the limit and bridge to priority

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Purpose gives larger direction to focus, but it is not a substitute for later priority and execution.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Purpose can sharpen focus by giving it direction, but it can also become lofty language that never guides a real choice.

Threshold Question:
When does purpose sharpen focus, and when does it become vague aspiration that sounds meaningful but does not guide real choices?
