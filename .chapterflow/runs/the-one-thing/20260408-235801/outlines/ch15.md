Chapter Promise:
Show that productivity means carrying the chosen priority forward through concentrated execution, so the reader stops confusing ordered action with generic busyness.

Opening Move:
Open on the difference between having a first priority and actually living in a way that carries it out.

- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): 3-4 -> productivity as concentrated execution of priority
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): 5-6 -> productivity as different from generic busyness

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Productivity is concentrated execution of the chosen priority, not faster motion across many tasks.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Productivity can express clear priority, but it can also collapse back into respectable busyness if concentration disappears.

Threshold Question:
When does productivity express clear priority, and when does it collapse back into busyness that only looks productive?
