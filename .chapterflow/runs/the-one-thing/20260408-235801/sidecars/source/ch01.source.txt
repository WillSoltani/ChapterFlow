Chapter anchors supported by the frozen bundle:
- Gary Keller traces the book's governing idea back to watching `City Slickers`, where the secret of life is framed as finding one thing of importance and sticking to it.
- Keller places this realization at a point when he felt overworked, overextended, and stuck inside his business life.
- Secondary chapter support says that after narrowing his role and focus, his company entered a period of very strong year-over-year growth.
- Chapter 1 defines the book's main move: identify the most important task or concern and focus on it so other work becomes easier or unnecessary.
- Keller ties uneven results to uneven focus, framing narrow concentration as the difference-maker behind extraordinary outcomes.
