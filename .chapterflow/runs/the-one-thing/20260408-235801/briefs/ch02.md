Core Claim:
Chapter 2 argues that extraordinary results are not usually produced by one giant burst. They come from a sequence of right moves that build on each other like falling dominos. Keller uses the domino metaphor to show that a well-chosen first step matters because it creates momentum and changes what the next step can do.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This chapter is strongest when it replaces fantasy-leap thinking with sequential, cumulative progress. It turns Chapter 1's leverage idea into an argument about order.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author introduces the domino metaphor as a model for how progress actually spreads.
Then the author argues that large outcomes are built through linked, cumulative actions rather than through simultaneous effort everywhere.
Then the author makes the practical point: choose the next move that can start the chain instead of obsessing over the whole distance at once.

Counterarguments or Limits:
