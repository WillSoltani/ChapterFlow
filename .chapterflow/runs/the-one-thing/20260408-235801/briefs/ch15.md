Core Claim:
Chapter 15 should argue that once purpose and priority are clear, productivity means executing from that ordered focus instead of spreading effort across many competing demands. The chapter should treat productivity as concentrated execution, not generic busyness.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This chapter should feel like the execution follow-through to priority. It should not replay the argument for purpose or priority; it should show that productivity is what ordered focus looks like in motion.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author shifts from first claim to execution.
Then the author argues that productivity comes from concentrating action around the chosen priority.
Then the author preserves the limit: productivity is not frantic activity or doing more things at once, but concentrated execution shaped by priority.
