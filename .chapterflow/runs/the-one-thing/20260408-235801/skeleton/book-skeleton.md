# Book Skeleton

## Metadata
- Book: The One Thing
- Author: Gary Keller, Jay Papasan
- Locked edition: English Bard Press / John Murray trade family
- Numbered chapter range: 1-18
- Wave size: 2 by default
- Premium / solo routing candidates: ch10, ch13, ch16, ch17, ch18

## Rotation Plan For Examples
- Maintain the canonical six-format rotation once per chapter: decision_point, postmortem, dialogue, predict_reveal, dilemma, before_after.
- Maintain 2 work / 2 school / 2 personal examples in every chapter package.
- Rotate endings across broader_principle, self_directed_question, surprising_implication, cross_domain, common_trap, perspective_reframe.

## School-Setting Plan
- Vary school contexts across study planning, exam prep, club leadership, team projects, classroom participation, and college or career choices.
- Keep school examples specific to the chapter mechanism rather than generic student-busy-ness filler.

## Vocabulary Watchlist
- Keep one thing, domino, priority, focus, leverage, purpose, habit, productivity, commitment, and thief precise and chapter-bound.
- Avoid hustle slogans, vague optimization language, and unsupported cognitive-science claims.

## Motif / Callback Opportunities
- The domino effect from ch02 should echo in priority, productivity, and small-step execution chapters.
- The focusing question from ch10 should function as a callback lens for later chapters rather than appearing out of nowhere.
- The ladder of purpose -> priority -> productivity should culminate in Part 3 and return in ch18.
- The contrast between noise and leverage should recur without flattening the book into one repeated slogan.

## Chapter Plan
### ch01 The One Thing
- Section: Opening sequence
- Intent: Introduce the core thesis that extraordinary results come from narrowing attention to the most leverage-rich task instead of spreading effort across everything.
- Source richness: high
- Concept density: high
- Moral complexity: medium
- Thin-chapter risk notes: avoid turning the chapter into generic focus advice; keep leverage and sequence visible.
- Wave routing: wave 1

### ch02 The Domino Effect
- Section: Opening sequence
- Intent: Show that big outcomes are built through sequential, compounding actions rather than one heroic burst.
- Source richness: high
- Concept density: medium
- Moral complexity: low
