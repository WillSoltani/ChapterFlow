# Chapter 10 Quiz Blueprint

## What the quiz must test

- what it means for preferences to come from others rather than from direct need or judgment
- how wealth or status insulation can distort taste
- why luxury signaling can create vulnerability rather than freedom
- how the chapter fits Taleb's wider critique of asymmetry and detachment from reality

## Bloom spread

- 3 retrieve / identify
- 4 apply / analyze
- 3 evaluate / transfer

## Must-cover scenarios

- distinguishing direct preference from socially imported taste
- recognizing manipulation or prestige pressure in high-status choices
- testing whether a preference is lived, useful, and owned rather than merely borrowed
