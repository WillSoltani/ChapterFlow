# Chapter 12 Quiz Blueprint

## What the quiz must test

- how facts can be true while the news built from them is misleading
- why framing, salience, and packaging matter in Taleb’s critique
- how mediated narrative differs from direct contact with events
- how the chapter extends the book’s wider distrust of polished but distorted signals

## Bloom spread

- 3 retrieve / identify
- 4 apply / analyze
- 3 evaluate / transfer

## Must-cover scenarios

- separating raw fact from the story assembled around it
- recognizing narrative distortion through emphasis and omission
- testing whether a mediated account tracks reality or only salience
