# Chapter 17 Quiz Blueprint

## What the quiz must test

- why revealed preference matters more than stated doctrine in this chapter
- how Taleb distinguishes real belief from verbal or institutional posture
- why the chapter should not be reduced to a simple hypocrisy charge
- how the chapter extends the book's broader concern with deeds, exposure, and consequence

## Bloom spread

- 3 retrieve / identify
- 4 apply / analyze
- 3 evaluate / transfer

## Must-cover scenarios

- checking whether behavior under consequence contradicts stated belief
- distinguishing hypocrisy language from revealed-preference analysis
- using costly action to infer what an institution or believer actually treats as real
