# Chapter 11 Brief

- title: Facta non Verba (Deeds Before Words)

## Chapter job

Make action under risk the real test of conviction, competence, and loyalty. Keep the chapter anchored to revealed preference, sacrifice, and the difference between speech that costs little and deeds that expose the actor to consequence.

## Required anchors

- deeds versus words as evidence
- action under risk as the test of sincerity
- commitment revealed by sacrifice or exposure
- continuity with earlier themes of signaling, preference, and skin in the game

## Restraints

- no exact quotations
- no unsupported named anecdotes presented as chapter facts
- do not drift into generic motivational language about action
