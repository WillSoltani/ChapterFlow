# Chapter 4 Brief

- chapter: 4
- title: The Skin of Others in Your Game
- intent: show how people with moral or political agency are constrained when spouses, children, staff, reputations, and institutions absorb the consequences of their actions.
- source stance: anchored primarily in Taleb's authorized INCERTO excerpt plus edition-lock metadata.

## Core Throughline

Taleb widens skin in the game from "your own downside" to the downside placed on people around you. The moral problem is not only that an actor may be insulated. It is that institutions and adversaries exploit family vulnerability, mortgages, jobs, and reputational dependence to control behavior. Ethical freedom becomes rare because most people are entangled with others who can be punished in their place.
