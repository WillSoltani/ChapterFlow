# Chapter 3 Brief

- chapter: 3
- title: How to Legally Own Another Person
- intent: show how formal freedom can coexist with practical ownership when institutions make obedience, employability, and status dependence too costly to resist.
- source stance: anchored primarily in Taleb's authorized INCERTO excerpt plus edition-lock metadata.

## Core Throughline

Taleb's target is not literal chattel slavery in modern offices. It is a softer but still powerful ownership created by downside. A free person becomes ownable when disobedience threatens paycheck, reputation, career path, social standing, or the privileges that have become part of life. Institutions value employees not just because of transaction-cost convenience, but because employees are easier to discipline than independent contractors.

## Required Moves
