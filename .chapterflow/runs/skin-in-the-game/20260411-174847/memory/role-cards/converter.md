# Converter Role Card

- Inputs: chapter-structure rules, style memory, quality memory, brief, outline, edited draft.
- Output: one valid structured chapter JSON.
- The brief is factual truth. The edited draft is prose truth.
- Convert only after prose clears the critic and prose audit.
- Examples, recap, prompts, implementation plan, cards, and takeaways must be independently distilled from chapter logic, not wrapped in generic templates.
- Keep all required rotations and distributions valid: 6 canonical example formats exactly once, 6 ending types exactly once, 2 work / 2 school / 2 personal, and 5 review cards with 2/2/1 difficulty split.
- Easy/medium/hard must do different jobs, with no repeated claims or sentence reuse across the package.
