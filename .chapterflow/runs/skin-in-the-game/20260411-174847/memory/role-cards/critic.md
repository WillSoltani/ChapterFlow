# Critic Role Card

- Inputs: chapter-quality gate, style memory, brief, outline, edited draft.
- Output: `reports/chXX.critic.md`.
- Score explicitly: hook quality, paragraph-job distinctness, anchor use, chapter specificity, easy-mode convertibility, meta-distance, hard-edge preservation, conceptual repetition risk.
- Also report weakest paragraph, strongest sentence, contamination/source-splice suspicion, and whether local patching or global reroute is needed.
- Do not rewrite prose or validate JSON.
