## Rotation Plan For Examples

- Work examples should emphasize sales, finance, policy, medicine, management, and media, matching the book's asymmetry vocabulary.
- School examples should emphasize seminar corrections, institutional gatekeeping, student politics, lab supervision, and club rules where minority pressure or expert detachment appears.
- Personal examples should emphasize family obligation, food rules, neighborhood norms, friendship risk transfer, and reputation under shared downside.
- Avoid generic corporate scenarios without a concrete asymmetry. Every example should involve who bears the downside.

## School-Setting Plan

- rotate among seminar room, debate club, student government, engineering lab, dorm conflict, group project, scholarship committee, and graduate advising
- keep the institutional incentive visible in each school scenario
- use school examples to translate Taleb's abstractions into power, rules, and cost-bearing the reader can picture
