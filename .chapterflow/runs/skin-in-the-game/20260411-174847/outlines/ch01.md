Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The non-generic mechanism is the difference between ordinary uncertainty shared by both sides and exploitative asymmetry where one side knows, benefits, or stays protected while the other side bears the downside.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must keep the uncomfortable boundary alive: markets cannot eliminate all asymmetry, so when does acceptable persuasion or incomplete information become unacceptable risk transfer?

Threshold Question:
When does a recommendation, sale, or omission stop being ordinary market behavior and become a moral violation because the recommender is no longer exposed to the downside?
