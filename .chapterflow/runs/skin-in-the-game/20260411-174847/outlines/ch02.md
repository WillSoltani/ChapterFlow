Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand how a tiny stubborn minority can impose its preference on a much larger population when the majority can tolerate the minority-compatible default.

Opening Move:
Open on the kosher lemonade example and the tiny symbol most people never notice, because it shows how the minority rule can dominate invisibly.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The mechanism is not mere preference strength. It is asymmetric tolerance: one side cannot accept the loose option while the other side can accept the strict option.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must preserve both the scaling force and the constraint. Minority rule spreads when the strict option is neutral enough for the flexible side to absorb, but it can fail under taboo, resistance, or decentralized separation.

Threshold Question:
When does a stubborn minority become strong enough to set the default for everyone else, and when do cost, disgust, or decentralization stop the spread?
