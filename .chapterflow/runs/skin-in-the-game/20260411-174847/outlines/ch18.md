# Chapter 18 Outline

1. Open by contrasting abstract rationality with rationality that must survive contact with consequence.
2. Explain why action and revealed preference test rationality more reliably than verbal or formal consistency alone.
3. Show how bounded rationality and instrumental belief fit Taleb's survival-oriented frame.
4. Connect the chapter to earlier themes of asymmetry, ruin, and skin in the game.
5. Close by treating rationality as whatever preserves agents under exposure rather than whatever looks clean in theory.
