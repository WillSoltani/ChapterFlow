# Chapter 16 Outline

1. Open with the difference between professed belief and belief tested through cost.
2. Explain why ritual and sacrifice reveal commitment more reliably than statement alone.
3. Show how worship becomes legible through action under exposure.
4. Connect the chapter to earlier themes of deeds, revealed preference, and skin in the game.
5. Close by treating real belief as something proven by what the believer is willing to risk or give up.
