# Chapter 17 Outline

1. Open with the gap between declared belief and the behavior that reveals actual commitment.
2. Explain why institutional doctrine is less informative than what believers and authorities do under consequence.
3. Show how the chapter uses revealed preference to read religion without reducing it to cheap accusation.
4. Connect the argument to earlier themes of ritual, sacrifice, deeds, and skin in the game.
5. Close by treating real belief as legible through action, cost, and institutional exposure rather than proclamation alone.
