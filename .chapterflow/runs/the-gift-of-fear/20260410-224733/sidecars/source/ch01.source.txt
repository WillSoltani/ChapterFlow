Heading: In the presence of danger

Primary support:
- Open Library and IUCAT confirm the heading and chapter position.
- Frozen secondary notes identify Kelly as the opening case.
- Kelly is carrying groceries upstairs when a man insists on helping after she has already refused.
- The interaction escalates because social pressure and the wish to seem reasonable keep overriding her discomfort.
- A later action by the attacker makes Kelly realize she is in immediate danger of being killed, and that realization triggers decisive action.
- Official Gavin de Becker site material states that true fear is tied to a signal in the environment, while worry is tied to imagination or memory.
- Official site material also treats worry as counterproductive rather than protective.

Usable chapter truths:
- fear can be protective when it is tied to a real present cue
- insistence after refusal is meaningful data
- politeness can become a liability when danger is already present
- waiting for socially satisfying proof can lag behind survival-relevant perception

- unsupported dialogue
- unsupported forensic details
- unsupported timeline after the assault
