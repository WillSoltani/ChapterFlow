Chapter 7 source freeze notes

Heading: Promises to kill (understanding threats)

Primary support:
- Open Library and IUCAT confirm the heading and chapter position.
- Prior frozen doctrine supports reading stronger forecasts from sequence, escalation, and surrounding pattern rather than from isolated cues.
- Skeleton intent supports treating threats as data and distinguishing signaling from stronger attack concern.

Usable chapter truths:
- threats should be interpreted, not ignored
- words alone are weaker than words inside a pattern
- function, context, and sequence matter more than isolated phrasing
- dismissal and melodrama are both errors

Do not claim:
- unsupported threat statistics
- unsupported named threat taxonomies
- certainty from words alone
