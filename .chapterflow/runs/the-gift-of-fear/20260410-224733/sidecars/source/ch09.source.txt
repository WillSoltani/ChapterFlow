Chapter 9 source freeze notes

Heading: Occupational hazards (violence in the workplace)

Primary support:
- Open Library and IUCAT confirm the heading and chapter position.
- Prior frozen doctrine supports reading repetition, escalation, access conflict, and grievance as patterned pressure rather than isolated incidents.
- Skeleton intent supports applying prediction and boundary logic to work settings where institutions often miss warning signs.

Usable chapter truths:
- workplaces can normalize warning patterns through routine process
- grievance and access conflict can become meaningful pressure patterns
- repeated signals matter before one decisive incident appears
- process should support behavioral reading, not replace it

Do not claim:
- unsupported workplace-violence statistics
- unsupported named case specifics
- that every grievance pattern predicts the same outcome
