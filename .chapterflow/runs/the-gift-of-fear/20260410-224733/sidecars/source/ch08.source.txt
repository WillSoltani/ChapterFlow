Chapter 8 source freeze notes

Heading: Persistence, persistence (dealing with people who refuse to let go)

Primary support:
- Open Library and IUCAT confirm the heading and chapter position.
- Prior frozen doctrine supports reading repetition, refusal-testing, and access-seeking as patterned pressure rather than isolated incidents.
- Skeleton intent supports a focus on unwanted pursuit escalation and why clear refusal beats negotiated ambiguity.

Usable chapter truths:
- repeated pursuit after refusal is meaningful data
- persistence is not the same thing as sincerity
- ambiguity and ongoing negotiation can feed the pattern
- clear refusal and reduced access are stronger than managed ambiguity

Do not claim:
- unsupported stalking statistics
- unsupported case specifics
- that every repeated contact carries identical danger
