Chapter 14 source freeze notes

Heading: Extreme hazards

Primary support:
- Open Library and IUCAT confirm the heading and chapter position.
- Prior frozen doctrine supports the limit that where signal is thin, overconfidence becomes risky and preparation may matter more.
- Skeleton intent supports covering low-frequency severe risks where clean prediction is difficult and planning matters.

Usable chapter truths:
- some severe hazards offer thinner predictive signal
- preparation and margin can matter more when prediction is weak
- confidence can become a liability when certainty outruns evidence
- planning is not the same as panic

Do not claim:
- unsupported catastrophic statistics
- unsupported emergency doctrine
- named extreme-hazard case specifics
