Chapter 6 source freeze notes

Heading: High-stakes predictions

Primary support:
- Open Library and IUCAT confirm the heading and chapter position.
- Prior frozen doctrine already supports prediction as behavior-pattern reading rather than mystical foresight.
- Skeleton intent supports a move into consequential risk assessment where hesitation becomes costly.

Usable chapter truths:
- stronger forecasts need stronger pattern support
- sequence and escalation matter more than isolated impressions
- high stakes can justify earlier protective action before certainty
- action under uncertainty must stay narrower than prophecy

Do not claim:
- unsupported case specifics
- unsupported formal threat-assessment models
- that uncertainty disappears before action is justified
