Chapter 2 source freeze notes

Heading: The technology of intuition

Primary support:
- Open Library and IUCAT confirm the heading and chapter position.
- Frozen secondary notes frame the chapter as intuition understood as a practical process rather than mystery.
- Frozen secondary note supports the claim that judgment can get in the way of perception.
- Official Gavin de Becker site material supports the boundary between true fear tied to environment and worry tied to imagination or memory.

Usable chapter truths:
- intuition is fast recognition of meaningful cues
- perception often arrives before explanation
- social judgment and rationalization can override a real signal
- the disciplined move is to identify the cue rather than worship the feeling
- intuition fails when it detaches from concrete signals and becomes vague certainty

Do not claim:
- unsupported scientific mechanisms
- unsupported stories or case details
- that intuition is infallible
