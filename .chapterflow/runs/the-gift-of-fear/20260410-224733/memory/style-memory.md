# Style Memory

- Teach the idea directly, not the chapter artifact.
- Keep prose concrete, specific, and authored.
- No em dashes, fake profundity, filler neuroscience, or generic self-help slogans.
- Open from pressure, event, bodily stakes, or a live question rather than thesis summary.
- Each paragraph needs one distinct job.
- Easy must stay readable and concrete; medium must explain mechanism; hard must preserve a real limit or threshold question.
- Competitive tone must stay credible, concise, and unsentimental.
- Use paraphrase-first and stay narrower than unsupported source detail.
