# Source Discovery

## Objective
Freeze a lawful, high-signal source bundle for `The Power of Habit` before any chapter work begins.

## Candidate Scan
- Official author page located at `https://charlesduhigg.com/the-power-of-habit/` and used as the primary rights-safe overview of the book's scope, themes, and flagship case studies.
- Penguin Random House product page located at `https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/202855/the-power-of-habit-by-charles-duhigg/` and used to confirm the trade-family metadata plus the presence of a publisher sample and the paperback afterword note.
- Google Books metadata page located at `https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Power_of_Habit.html?id=syiPEAAAQBAJ` and used to confirm title, author, 2012/2014 trade-family metadata, and contents signals.
- SuperSummary chapter map located at `https://www.supersummary.com/the-power-of-habit/summary/` and used as the reputable secondary source for the numbered chapter map and chapter-level framing.

## Decision
The run auto-locks the dominant English Random House trade family for `The Power of Habit: Why We Do What We Do in Life and Business`.

Working edition policy:
- use the 2012 Random House launch family as the canonical core structure
- treat the 2014 paperback afterword as a later-format extra
- run the numbered pipeline on Chapters `1-9` only

No edition question was needed because:
- the numbered chapter count and order remain stable across the reviewed English trade-family variants
- the paperback afterword is supplementary rather than a renumbered chapter
- no translation or abridged-vs-full ambiguity was requested

## Working Policy
- Use paraphrase-first throughout the run.
- Permit exact quotes only where the frozen bundle explicitly supports them, most safely in the publisher sample surface for Chapter 1.
- Treat the TOC, official framing pages, and reputable chapter-map support as the chapter-level evidence surface.
