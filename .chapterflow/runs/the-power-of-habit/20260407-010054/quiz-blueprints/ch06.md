# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter: The Power of a Crisis
Core concepts to test:
- crisis as the moment routines become negotiable
- leaders using disruption to install new habits
- institutional accidents exposing the cost of bad automatic patterns
- Crisis creates leverage, but it also raises stakes. The chapter works only if the reader keeps both possibility and danger visible at once.

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: evaluate / transfer

Question plan:
- q01: What is the chapter's main claim about crisis?
- q02: Why does the chapter say institutions often change late?
- q03: What does a crisis change first in the chapter's model?
- q04: Why is crisis only an opportunity and not automatically a solution?
- q05: What ethical warning does the chapter include?
- q06: A student council event almost fails because ownership was vague. Which chapter move fits best?
- q07: What happens if leaders fail to install a new routine before the crisis window closes?
- q08: Why does the chapter say crisis can weaken resistance to change?
- q09: What is the difference between panic and reform in this chapter?
- q10: Which statement best fits Chapter 6?

- tempting wrong answer type 2: answer borrowed from a neighboring chapter rather than this one
