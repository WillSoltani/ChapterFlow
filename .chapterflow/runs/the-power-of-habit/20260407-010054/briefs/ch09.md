Core Claim:
Chapter 9 asks how free will and responsibility survive within a strong habit framework. Duhigg's answer is that habits explain conduct, but explanation does not automatically remove accountability. The chapter's cases suggest that responsibility turns on awareness, foreseeability, and whether someone had reason to manage a dangerous pattern.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Strongest at refusing the easy extremes: habits explain behavior powerfully, but explanation is not the same thing as absolution.
