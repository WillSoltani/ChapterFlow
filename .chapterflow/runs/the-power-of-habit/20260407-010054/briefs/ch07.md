Core Claim:
Chapter 7 applies habit science to consumer prediction and influence. Duhigg's Target example demonstrates the central claim: purchase routines generate enough structure that companies can infer major life changes from behavior patterns alone. Target's analysts did not need direct confession from customers. They needed enough data to see stable signals in what people were buying and when.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
