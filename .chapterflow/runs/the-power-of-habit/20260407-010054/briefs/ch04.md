Core Claim:
Chapter 4 introduces keystone habits, routines that matter disproportionately because they trigger cascades of additional change. Duhigg's main example is Paul O'Neill's decision to prioritize worker safety at Alcoa.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Strongest at showing how one disciplined operating routine can reorder a much larger system.
