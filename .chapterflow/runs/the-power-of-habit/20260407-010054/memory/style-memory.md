# Style Memory

- Write like a sharp human explainer with taste: specific, concrete, insightful, and readable.
- Start with a live scene, tension, or anchor rather than a thesis announcement.
- Keep paragraph jobs distinct and make each chapter feel unmistakably about that chapter.
- Easy opens the door, medium explains mechanism and application, hard adds a real limit or unresolved tension.
- Tone differences must be functional: gentle lowers resistance, direct clarifies mechanism, competitive sharpens stakes.
- Avoid generic self-help phrasing, corporate smoothness, fake profundity, and repeated sentence skeletons.
- If a sentence sounds pre-manufactured or smarter than the brief supports, cut it.
