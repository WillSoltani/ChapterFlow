# Quality Memory

- Never invent facts, quotes, studies, mechanisms, or anecdotes beyond the brief and frozen bundle.
- Use paraphrase-first; exact quotes require explicit support.
- Hard fails include thesis-first openings, contamination leakage, fake mechanism language, tone collapse, source splicing, and pseudo-drafts built from structured fields.
- Every paragraph must earn a distinct job and every chapter must keep a chapter-specific mechanism visible.
- Easy targets grade 8-9, medium grade 10-11, hard grade 12 with a real boundary condition.
- Reject anything that could fit another chapter with minimal title swapping.
- Review scenarios, quizzes, and implementation plans for concrete specificity rather than generic coaching.
