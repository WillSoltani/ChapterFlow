Core Claim:
Chapter 10 explains the conditions under which people shift from ordinary self-focus into more group-minded coordination, which Haidt frames as the hive switch.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the experiential mechanism chapter of Part III. The job is to explain how groupishness can become vividly active rather than staying a background trait.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author carries Chapter 9's selfish-versus-groupish tension into a more dynamic mechanism.
Then he explains the hive switch as a shift into more collective coordination.
Then he prepares the book's later argument about religion and team binding.
