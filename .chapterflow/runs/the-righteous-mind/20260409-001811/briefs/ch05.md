Core Claim:
Chapter 5 argues that modern WEIRD populations mistake a narrow slice of moral concern for morality as such. The chapter expands the field by showing that harm and fairness are not the only moral dimensions people use across cultures and traditions.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the first major widening move of Part II. The chapter is less about politics than about correcting a culturally narrow picture of moral life.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author identifies WEIRD moral assumptions as unusually narrow.
Then he shows that many people and cultures moralize domains beyond harm and fairness.
Then he uses that widening move to prepare for a fuller map of moral concerns.
