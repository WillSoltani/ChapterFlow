# Quality Memory

- Auto-fail if the prose could fit another chapter, repeats paragraph jobs, or turns hard depth into longer medium.
- No contamination phrases, source-splice leakage, pseudo-drafts, or tone collapse.
- The brief is factual truth. The edited draft is prose truth. Conversion adapts; it does not invent.
- Quiz quality matters: ten questions, three choices each, scenario judgment over weak flashcards.
- Review packages must wrap the full validated chapter JSON, never a partial proxy.
- Seal hashes only after chapter validation passes and artifacts are complete on disk.
