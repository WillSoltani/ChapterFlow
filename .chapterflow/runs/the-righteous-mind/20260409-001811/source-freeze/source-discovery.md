# Source Discovery

## Objective
Freeze a lawful, high-signal source bundle for *The Righteous Mind* before any chapter work begins.

## Discovery result
- No public-domain full text exists.
- An authorized source bundle does exist:
  - official Penguin Random House retail page with the full table of contents
  - official Righteous Mind site introduction
  - official Righteous Mind Chapter 9 PDF
  - official Righteous Mind endnotes PDF
- The official site also links a public summary/outline, which is acceptable as secondary support when kept narrow.

## Why the bundle is sufficient
- The official introduction provides the book's governing frame, three-part architecture, and core metaphors.
- The official table of contents provides the chapter order and titles.
- The official Chapter 9 PDF gives one complete chapter and validates the tone, density, and Part III framing.
- The official endnotes confirm chapter-level support surfaces for named thinkers, studies, and concepts used in the early chapters.
- Secondary sources are used only to narrow chapter intent when the authorized preview is too thin.

## Constraints carried forward
- Paraphrase first.
- Exact quotes only when directly supported by the frozen authorized preview.
- Chapter sidecars must be derived from this frozen bundle, not from memory.
- Unsupported claims stay out, even if they are broadly associated with Haidt's work.
