Chapter Promise:
You will leave Chapter 1 understanding why moral judgment cannot be reduced to calm reasoning, even when people feel certain that it can.

Opening Move:
Open on the unnerving moment when someone knows a judgment is right but cannot explain why without reaching for weak reasons.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P2-P3 for Piaget and Kohlberg
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P4-P5 for Turiel and cross-cultural boundary pressure
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P1 and P6 for moral dumbfounding as the opening tension and closing demonstration

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: hook with a dumbfounded moral judgment
- P2: explain the old rationalist expectation through Piaget and Kohlberg
- P3: show why that prestige model is too narrow
- P4: widen the frame with convention versus morality and cross-cultural variation
- P5: sharpen what this means for where morality comes from
- P6: land on moral dumbfounding as the chapter's decisive pressure point
- P7 if needed: bridge to Chapter 2, where reasoning becomes servant rather than source

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter must distinguish felt judgment from articulated justification.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The harder question is not whether people feel first. It is what moral seriousness looks like once you admit that feeling arrives before explanation.

Threshold Question:
If certainty can outrun reasons, what keeps moral judgment from becoming self-righteous noise?
