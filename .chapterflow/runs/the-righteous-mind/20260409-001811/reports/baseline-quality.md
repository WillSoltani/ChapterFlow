# Baseline Quality: Chapters 1 and 2

Chapters 1 and 2 now form the baseline quality floor for the rest of the run.

## Chapter 1 strengths
- Strong pressure on rationalist prestige without drifting into generic anti-reason prose.
- Clear anchor use around Piaget, Kohlberg, Turiel, cross-cultural variation, and moral dumbfounding.
- Good humility frame that avoids both relativism and self-righteousness.

## Chapter 2 strengths
- Stronger intuition-first sequence with clear Hume support.
- Better integration of persuasion, justification, reputation, and belonging as social jobs of reasoning.
- Cleaner bridge into Chapter 3's control problem.

## Floor to preserve in later waves
- Hooks must open on a live tension, not abstract summary.
- Chapter-specific mechanism must stay unmistakable.
- Examples must feel socially real and tied to distinct lessons.
- Hard depth must preserve a genuine live question instead of repeating medium.
- Reader-facing prose must stay free of contamination and pseudo-analytic filler.

## Risk notes
- The validator allows thesis-first warnings, but later chapters should aim to reduce them where possible without reopening already-approved chapters.
- Political chapters must keep the same specificity and moral distance as the baseline pair.
