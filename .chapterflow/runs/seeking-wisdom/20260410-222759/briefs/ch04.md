Core Claim:
The first visible effect of a decision is often not the most important one. Better judgment comes from seeing systems, secondary effects, reactions from others, and downstream tradeoffs before calling a move smart. This chapter should make short-term wins feel incomplete without second-order review.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is about consequence structure, not generic strategic thinking. Systems, delayed effects, reactions, and hidden costs must stay visible.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author shows that actions sit inside systems rather than isolated events.
Then the author shows how second-order and higher-order consequences change what a move really means.
Then the author pushes the reader to evaluate a decision by its downstream structure, not its first visible payoff.
