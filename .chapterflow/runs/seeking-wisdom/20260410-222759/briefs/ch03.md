Core Claim:
Strong feelings about an outcome are not the same thing as strong odds. Better judgment starts when you replace certainty language with probabilities, base rates, ranges, and margin for error. This chapter should make overconfidence feel expensive and probabilistic thinking feel practical.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is about uncertainty discipline, not generic caution. The chapter should keep base rates, chance, sample weakness, and margin of safety visible.
