# Book Skeleton

Metadata:
- Title: Seeking Wisdom: From Darwin to Munger
- Author: Peter Bevelin
- Working edition: 3rd Edition (PCA Publications, 2007)
- Variant family: EMH
- Package chapter count: 6

Chapter order:
1. Study Repeated Patterns of Misjudgment
   - intent: teach the reader to see recurring error patterns instead of treating every mistake as unique
   - source richness: high
   - concept density: medium-high
   - moral complexity: low
2. Read Human Nature Through Incentives
   - intent: show how self-interest, reciprocity, status, fairness, and social pressure shape judgment
   - source richness: high
   - concept density: high
   - moral complexity: medium
3. Use Probability to Resist Overconfidence
   - intent: move the reader from certainty-language to odds, ranges, base rates, and margin of safety
   - source richness: medium-high
   - concept density: high
   - moral complexity: low
4. Think Past the First Consequence
   - intent: treat systems, second-order effects, and hidden tradeoffs as mandatory thinking moves
   - source richness: medium
   - concept density: high
   - moral complexity: medium
5. Use Checklists to Protect Judgment
   - intent: turn misjudgment awareness into a repeatable pre-commitment process
   - source richness: high
   - concept density: medium
   - moral complexity: low
6. Protect Temperament While You Learn
   - intent: show why knowledge fails when fear, ego, memory drift, and emotional pressure stay unmanaged
   - source richness: medium
   - concept density: medium-high
   - moral complexity: medium
