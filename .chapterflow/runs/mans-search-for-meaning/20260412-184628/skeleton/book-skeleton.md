## Chapter Order

1. `ch01` - Preface to the 1992 Edition
   Intent: frame why Frankl wrote the book, why he stayed in Vienna, and why meaning must be proven under extremity rather than asserted abstractly.
   Source richness: medium
   Concept density: medium
   Moral complexity: low
2. `ch02` - Part One: Induction and Adjustments
   Intent: show the first psychological phase of camp life, the collapse of old expectations, and the early formation of survival posture.
   Source richness: high
   Concept density: medium
   Moral complexity: high
3. `ch03` - Part One: Facts, Fulfillment, and Freedom
   Intent: trace apathy, inner freedom, chance, hope, and liberation without turning suffering into uplift.
   Source richness: high
   Concept density: high
   Moral complexity: high
4. `ch04` - Part Two: Logotherapy in a Nutshell
   Intent: explain the will to meaning, existential vacuum, routes to meaning, and therapeutic tools without flattening them into slogans.
   Source richness: high
   Concept density: very high
   Moral complexity: medium
5. `ch05` - Postscript: The Case for a Tragic Optimism
   Intent: sharpen Frankl's answer to pain, guilt, and death while holding open the risk of sentimental misuse.
   Source richness: medium
   Concept density: high
   Moral complexity: high
