Chapter Promise:
Readers will understand that the preface is a pressure frame, not a courtesy note: Frankl is insisting that meaning must be tested where life looks stripped of every reason to affirm it.

Opening Move:
Open on the decision not to leave Vienna and the pressure contained inside that choice, then widen into why the memoir has to come before logotherapy.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter distinguishes meaning as a pressure-tested response from meaning as abstract comfort language, and it distinguishes testimony as evidence from theory as explanation.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Preserve the risk that meaning-talk can become cruel if it starts sounding like suffering itself justifies the lesson. Hard depth must hold the boundary between witness and romanticization.

Threshold Question:
How can suffering function as evidence for meaning without turning meaning into a demand that dishonors the sufferer?
