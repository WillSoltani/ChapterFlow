Chapter Promise:
Readers will understand that the first camp phase is ruled by shock, misreading, and desperate adjustment rather than by immediate moral clarity.

Opening Move:
Open on arrival pressure, selection, and the sudden nearness of death.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 for transport arrival and selection
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 for shock, delusion of reprieve, and suicidal thinking
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 for early adaptation and what that adaptation costs

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: arrival shock and collapse of ordinary expectations
- P2: selection and immediate awareness of death
- P3: define delusion of reprieve and why the mind reaches for it
- P4: show the first phase of shock, including suicidal thinking and disorientation
- P5: explain early adjustment as survival narrowing, not wisdom
- P6: mark the limit: adjustment protects survival while threatening older emotional life
- P7 if needed: bridge to later apathy and inner freedom

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter distinguishes shock from later apathy and distinguishes early survival narrowing from any stable philosophical stance.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Shock helps a person survive the first collision with horror, but it may already be deadening parts of the self. Hard depth must keep both sides visible.

Threshold Question:
When does the first adjustment to horror begin protecting survival at the cost of the person's earlier moral and emotional world?

Takeaway Count Lock:
- Easy: 3 (always)
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 5

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- arrival as collapse of normal scale
- selection and proximity to death
- delusion of reprieve
- shock as first-phase psychology
- adjustment as both shield and danger

Scenario Lesson Map:
- Scenario 1 (decision_point, school): misreading an emergency because the mind wants a safer story
- Scenario 2 (dialogue, work): team members bargaining against obvious bad news
- Scenario 3 (postmortem, personal): looking back on how shock narrowed perception
- Scenario 4 (predict_reveal, school): an authority figure's calm tone hides the real stakes
- Scenario 5 (dilemma, work): choosing between false reassurance and hard-eyed clarity
- Scenario 6 (before_after, personal): before and after a destabilizing event changes emotional scale

Scenario Vividness Targets:
Use train doors, wet shoes, a fluorescent corridor, a badge scanner, a fire alarm tone, and a kitchen chair after a bad phone call.

Preview Constraint:
The preview must ask what remains after shock hardens into camp routine. It must not summarize the next chapter's answer fully.
