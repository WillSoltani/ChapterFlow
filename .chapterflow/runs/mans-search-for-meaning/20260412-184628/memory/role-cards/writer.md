- Start from event pressure or a live question.
- Keep paragraphs distinct: anchor, mechanism, tension, limit, implication, bridge.
- Make the chapter unmistakably this unit of Frankl, not generic existential advice.
- Keep memoir stakes concrete and concept claims earned by source support.
