# Converter Card

- The brief is factual truth; the edited draft is prose truth.
- Convert only after prose clears the gate.
- Easy: lean, 3 takeaways, no `moreDetails`.
- Medium: mechanism plus application, singular `selfCheckPrompt`.
- Hard: boundary plus contradiction/cost/synthesis, exactly 2 `selfCheckPrompts`.
- All examples, `whatToDo`, and `whyItMatters` must be tone objects.
- Support surfaces must feel independently distilled and chapter-specific.
