# Baseline Quality

Baseline chapters: `ch01`, `ch02`

## Chapter 1 strength

- Establishes the book's proof-pressure frame without softening the moral stakes.
- Keeps meaning tied to witness before theory.
- Preserves the boundary against romanticizing suffering.

## Chapter 2 strength

- Delivers psychologically exact first-phase memoir pressure without imported later theory.
- Shows shock, reprieve, and early adjustment as human rather than theatrical.
- Preserves the adaptive-costly tension that later chapters must deepen.

## Baseline floor for later waves

- No generic uplift.
- No theory outrunning witness.
- Memoir pressure must stay concrete and unsentimental.
- Hard depth must preserve a real limit or threshold, not just intensify language.
- Support surfaces must clear lint and prose audit without repetitive scaffolds.
