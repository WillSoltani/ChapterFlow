   - source richness: high
   - concept density: medium
   - moral complexity: high
   - thin-chapter risk: could collapse into generic adversity inspiration if the concrete family violence and escape dynamics get blurred
   - premium-routing candidate: yes
2. Chapter 2: Truth Hurts

   - source richness: high
   - concept density: high

   - premium-routing candidate: yes
