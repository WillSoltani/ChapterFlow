# Ch05 Outline — Armored Mind

1. Open on the shock of bodily failure after surviving elite external pressure.
2. Show why injury is psychologically harder because it makes progress feel reversible.
3. Keep humiliation and insecurity visible so the chapter does not become clean legend.
4. Introduce the calloused-mind idea through repeated return to pain and setback.
5. Distinguish durable armor from fake numbness or denial.
6. Show how rebuilding after damage becomes the new proving ground.
7. Bridge to Chapter 6 by asking what happens when toughness gets tied to service, memory, and a different motive than proving yourself.

## Hard-depth threshold
- toughness here is persistence through honest weakness, not denial of weakness
- repeated return matters more than one clean victory
