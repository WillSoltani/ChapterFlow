# Ch08 Outline — Talent Not Required

1. Open on the temptation to think more effort alone will solve everything.
2. Show why larger endurance efforts expose planning failures more clearly.
3. Keep capacity and grit visible while making poor execution costlier.
4. Reframe failure as corrective information, not proof that the whole identity project was false.
5. Show how judgment and logistics become part of toughness.
6. Preserve the limit that effort remains necessary even though it is not sufficient.
7. Bridge to Chapter 9 by asking what happens when endurance and discipline move into even higher-status proving grounds and leadership settings.

## Hard-depth threshold
- effort without execution wastes range
- failure becomes useful when it is converted into strategy instead of ego damage
