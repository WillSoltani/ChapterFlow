# Ch01 Outline — Should Have Been a Statistic

1. Open inside the false appearance of safety: nice house, terrible home.
2. Show how Trunnis's abuse and greed make fear the governing household rule.
3. Make the child-labor detail load-bearing so exhaustion and school struggle feel earned, not decorative.
4. Shift to the escape plan and the move to Brazil, Indiana.
5. Preserve the bitter contrast: leaving abuse does not end hardship, it changes its form into poverty and instability.
6. Land the chapter on the dangerous convenience of becoming what the past predicts.
7. Bridge to Chapter 2 by setting up the need for a harsher kind of self-confrontation than survival alone.

## Hard-depth threshold
- pain can explain a person's position without being allowed to become their permanent identity
- the honest question is not whether the past was bad enough, but whether the past gets to become the final author
