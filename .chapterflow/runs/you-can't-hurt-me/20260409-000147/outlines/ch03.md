# Ch03 Outline — The Impossible Task

1. Open on the humiliating contrast between Goggins's current life and the standard he now cannot ignore.
2. Use exterminator work and obesity as concrete proof that the mirror's truth now needs a mission.
3. Introduce the Navy SEAL target as a shape clear enough to demand total commitment.
4. Make the deadline and weight-loss burden load-bearing so the task feels borderline irrational.
5. Show how chosen suffering becomes the tool for breaking negotiation with the old self.
6. Preserve the limit that the chapter is about total commitment under pressure, not generic extremity worship.
7. Bridge to Chapter 4 by asking what happens when this mission collides with elite training and direct opposition.

## Hard-depth threshold
- a mission can save a person from drift without becoming a universal rule
- the task matters because it destroys negotiation, not because pain is holy
