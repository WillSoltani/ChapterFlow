# Ch09 Outline — Uncommon Amongst Uncommon

1. Open on Ranger School as an elite proving ground where suffering now happens inside a leadership system, not only inside a private endurance test.
2. Show how deprivation and fatigue expose both competence and character.
3. Track the post-9/11 military setting as a context that raises seriousness, duty, and identity stakes.
4. Reframe uncommon performance as something that can steady other people rather than only humiliate rivals.
5. Mark the danger of moral superiority, ego, or using elite status as permission to look down on others.
6. Keep standards, discipline, and example central without turning the chapter into military mythmaking.
7. Bridge to Chapter 10 by asking what happens when elite ambition collides with failure and demands harder after-action review.

## Hard-depth threshold
- elite status increases responsibility, not entitlement
- leadership under deprivation matters more than private pain tolerance
