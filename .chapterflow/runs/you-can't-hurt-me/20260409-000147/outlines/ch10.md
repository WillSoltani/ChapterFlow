# Ch10 Outline — The Empowerment of Failure

1. Open on failure as unavoidable inside serious standards rather than as a side issue.
2. Use Delta Force failure and repeated record attempts as proof that elite ambition still gets humbled.
3. Show why ego-protection ruins the value of failure.
4. Make after-action review and tactical correction the chapter's load-bearing tools.
5. Preserve ambition while demanding cleaner learning from mistakes.
6. Show that respecting the standard means accepting what failure reveals.
7. Bridge to Chapter 11 by asking what larger life possibilities open once a person stops treating failure as final identity verdict.

## Hard-depth threshold
- failure empowers only when converted into correction
- respect for the standard matters more than protecting pride
