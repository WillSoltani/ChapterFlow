# Baseline Quality — Chapters 1 and 2

## Baseline floor

- Chapter 1 establishes the adversity-and-identity floor. It stays concrete, keeps family violence and forced labor load-bearing, and refuses both sentimentality and fatalism.
- Chapter 2 establishes the accountability-and-measurement floor. It keeps grief and unfairness visible while turning truth into disciplined work instead of slogans.

## What later waves must preserve

- chapter-specific anchors that could not be pasted into another chapter
- a real limit or tension in hard depth rather than inflated wording
- examples that teach different lessons instead of repeating one idea in six skins
- reader-facing prose that stays concrete and does not drift into generic toughness language
- accountability language that remains earned by context rather than detached from it

## What would count as decay

- later chapters becoming more templated than Chapters 1 and 2
- example surfaces collapsing into one repeated lesson
- hard depth becoming longer medium depth
- slogans about suffering or discipline replacing chapter-specific argument
- generic implementation advice that ignores the chapter's actual logic
