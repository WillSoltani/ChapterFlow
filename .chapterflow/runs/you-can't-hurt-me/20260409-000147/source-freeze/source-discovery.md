# Source Discovery

## Objective

Freeze a lawful, chapter-usable source bundle for *Can't Hurt Me: Master Your Mind and Defy the Odds* before any chapter work begins.

## Discovery path

1. Checked authorized Google Books records for edition metadata and visible contents support.
2. Cross-checked chapter order against a public library catalog record.
3. Verified ongoing publisher continuity through Lioncrest's public catalog.
4. Collected chapter-level secondary summaries for all 11 chapters from SuperSummary to support paraphrase-first chapter dossiers where the authorized preview is thin.

## Result

- Locked the standard English edition family.
- Confirmed a stable 11-chapter structure plus introduction.
- Froze a paraphrase-first bundle rather than a full-text bundle because no lawful complete text was available in the authorized sources inspected.
- Limited exact-quote support to short visible lines and chapter titles directly visible in the frozen authorized bundle.
