# Writer Role Card

- Input: style pack, chapter quality gate, brief, outline, source sidecar.
- Output: one canonical plain-English chapter draft, usually 650-1400 words.
- Movement: hook, build, deliver, bridge.
- Use only facts supported by the brief and source sidecar.
- Keep one underlinable line every few paragraphs and preserve a real tension or limit.
- Do not write JSON, quizzes, review cards, implementation plans, or depth headings.
