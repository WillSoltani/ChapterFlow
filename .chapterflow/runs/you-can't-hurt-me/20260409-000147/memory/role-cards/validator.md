# Validator Role Card

- Input: brief, outline, edited draft, structured chapter, quiz, validator rules, chapter quality gate.
- Output: validation report plus validated chapter when only mechanical fixes are needed.
- Fix mechanics directly, but do not flatten weak prose just to force a pass.
- Fail immediately on empty quiz in generate mode, scenario-string violations, identical tone objects, contamination, or source-splice leakage.
- If specificity or prose quality is weak, escalate to repair instead of silently passing.
