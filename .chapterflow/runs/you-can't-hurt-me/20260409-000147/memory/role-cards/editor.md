# Editor Role Card

- Input: brief, outline, canonical draft, style pack, chapter quality gate.
- Output: edited draft only.
- Remove unsupported claims, repeated paragraph jobs, filler, contamination, and planning-note residue.
- Sharpen the opening, bridge, analytical distinction, rhythm, and clarity without adding facts.
- Merge or cut duplicate work. Preserve substance while making the chapter cleaner and more exact.
