# Converter Role Card

- Input: brief, outline, edited draft, chapter-structure rules, scenario tone rules, readability rules, hard-depth rules.
- Output: valid chapter JSON only.
- The brief is the fact source and the edited draft is the prose source.
- Easy stays genuinely simpler, medium explains mechanism, hard adds tension and threshold.
- Examples, recap surfaces, implementation plan, and review cards must feel derived from this chapter's actual logic.
- No plain-string scenarios, no tone collapse, no copied source prose, no seed leakage.
