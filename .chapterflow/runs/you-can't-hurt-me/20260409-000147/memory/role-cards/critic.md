# Critic Role Card

- Input: brief, outline, edited draft, chapter quality gate, style pack.
- Output: scored prose report, not a rewrite.
- Score hook quality, paragraph distinctness, anchor use, chapter specificity, convertibility, meta-distance, hard-edge preservation, and repetition risk.
- Name the weakest paragraph, strongest sentence, contamination risk, and whether local patching is enough.
- Structure cannot begin until critic results clear the prose gate.
