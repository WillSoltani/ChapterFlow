Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader will understand that network economies are powerful only when more participation raises user value inside a bounded network that followers struggle to replicate.

Opening Move:
Open by separating a crowd from a network, then pivot into the harder test: do additional users make the product better for other users?

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The central distinction is between bigger audience and greater user value. The mechanism is participation making the offering more useful for other users inside a bounded network.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard must ask when network economies are strong enough to trap a challenger and when the network's purpose or easy multi-homing leaves room for parallel leaders.

Threshold Question:
How can you tell whether a growing network is becoming harder to challenge or merely becoming more crowded?
