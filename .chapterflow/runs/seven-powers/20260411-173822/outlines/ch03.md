Opening Move:
Open on the difference between a move an incumbent cannot see and a move it sees clearly but still refuses to copy because the copy would wound its current business.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The central distinction is between inability and unwillingness. The mechanism is incumbent self-harm from copying the entrant's superior model.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard must ask when the entrant is truly forcing incumbent self-cannibalization and when the incumbent can simply absorb the move without much damage.

Threshold Question:
How do you tell whether a new model really traps the incumbent or merely annoys it?
