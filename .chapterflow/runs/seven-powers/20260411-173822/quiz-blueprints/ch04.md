# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter: Switching Costs
Core concepts to test:
- value built up with the incumbent
- financial, procedural, and relational switching costs
- rival offer versus transition bill
- distinction between lock-in and brand affection
- limits where portability weakens the barrier

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: analyze and apply

Question plan:
- q01: basic definition of switching costs
- q02: identify continuity value
- q03: distinguish switching costs from branding
- q04: named work scenario on migration burden
- q05: school lab scenario on retraining and workflow disruption
- q06: identify relational switching cost
- q07: local service scenario with accumulated knowledge
- q08: analyze why a better rival can still lose
- q09: deeper synthesis on portability weakening the barrier
- q10: bridge question toward branding

Distractor notes:
- tempting wrong answer type 1: repeat purchase mistaken for switching costs
- tempting wrong answer type 2: warm feeling mistaken for lock-in

Explanation diversity notes:
- avoid repeated openers
- each direct explanation must begin differently
- explanations should name the customer value lost in the switch or its absence
