Frozen support for Chapter 5: Branding.

Supported:
- branding creates higher willingness to pay for an objectively similar offering
- historical signals shape expectation and feeling
- time and uncertainty are the barrier to copying equivalent trust and emotional meaning
- branding is not exclusive by default; competing brands can both be strong

Do not add:
- detailed advertising war stories from memory
- unsupported market-share claims
- blanket claims that awareness alone equals brand power

Working emphasis:
- keep the premium explicit
- keep the barrier tied to time and uncertainty
- preserve the possibility of several strong brands coexisting
