Frozen support for Chapter 6: Cornered Resource.

Supported:
- preferential access on attractive terms to a coveted resource that independently raises value
- examples can involve unique talent pools, patents, and control of scarce assets
- chapter must distinguish genuinely cornered inputs from ordinary good assets

Do not add:
- detailed litigation or industry stories from memory
- casual claims that any excellent team or supplier is cornered
- unsupported scarcity claims without substitution checks

Working emphasis:
- keep independent value explicit
- check whether rivals can secure comparable access or substitutes
- separate true cornering from ordinary quality
