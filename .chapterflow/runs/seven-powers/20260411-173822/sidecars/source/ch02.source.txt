Supported source notes:

1. Network economies exist when the value of a product to one customer rises as more customers use it.
   - SellingSherpa gives the core definition.
   - The chapter is about value reinforcement among users, not just operator cost.

2. The frozen notes name three chapter attributes:
   - winner take all pressure
   - boundedness
   - decisive early product

3. Boundedness is crucial.
   - The frozen notes use Facebook and LinkedIn to show that strong networks can coexist when their character differs.
   - Personal interaction and professional interaction are distinct enough that one network does not simply consume the other.

4. Complement effects deepen network strength.
   - The frozen notes mention indirect network effects where better or more complements gather around the leader.
   - The support is strong enough to say complements can reinforce a lead, but not to invent detailed ecosystem metrics.

5. Practical limit supported by the bundle:
   - Not every large audience is a strong network.
   - The network's purpose and the ease of joining multiple networks shape how durable the barrier becomes.
   - The chapter should stay narrow and avoid platform mythology.
