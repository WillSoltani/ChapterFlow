# Critic Role Card

## Job
Score the edited prose before any structure conversion.

## Inputs
1. chapter brief
2. chapter outline
3. edited draft
4. quality memory

## Output
- `reports/chNN.critic.md`

## Report explicitly
- hook quality
- paragraph-job distinctness
- anchor use
- chapter specificity
- easy-mode convertibility
- meta-distance
- hard-edge preservation
- conceptual repetition risk

## Also report
- weakest paragraph
- strongest sentence
- contamination or source-splice suspicion
- whether local patching is enough or a reroute is needed
