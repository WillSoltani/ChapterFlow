# Chapter Outline

Chapter Promise:
Show why real decisions live in a poker-like world of hidden information and luck, and why that makes calibrated confidence stronger than false certainty.

Opening Move:
Open from the tension that people crave certainty even when the environment does not support it.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1, P2
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P2, P3
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P3, P4

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: Expose the human hunger for certainty and clean narratives.
- P2: Show why hidden information breaks black-and-white judgment.
- P3: Use poker versus chess to make the structural contrast memorable.
- P4: Reframe uncertainty admission as disciplined accuracy.
- P5: Bridge into explicit betting language for the next chapter.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter must show that uncertainty is not occasional noise on top of a certain world. It is the ordinary structure of the decision world itself.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard mode must preserve the problem of acting decisively when certainty is unavailable and luck still moves outcomes after the choice.

Threshold Question:
How do you commit strongly without pretending you know more than you do?

Takeaway Count Lock:
- Easy: 3
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 5

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- Takeaway 1: hidden information is normal, not exceptional
- Takeaway 2: luck changes outcomes without erasing skill
- Takeaway 3: resulting is a structural judgment error
- Takeaway 4: calibrated confidence beats certainty theater
- Takeaway 5: uncertainty should sharpen action quality rather than paralyze it

Scenario Lesson Map:
- Scenario 1 (decision_point, work): choose a strategy under incomplete market data
- Scenario 2 (postmortem, school): review a club competition loss with luck and process separated
- Scenario 3 (dialogue, personal): push back on a friend who demands total certainty before acting
- Scenario 4 (predict_reveal, work): early metrics distort the true quality of a decision
- Scenario 5 (dilemma, school): present a probabilistic forecast to a certainty-hungry room
- Scenario 6 (before_after, personal): shift from right-wrong self-talk to odds-based self-review

Scenario Vividness Targets:
- Scenario 1: conference room screen with partial market data
- Scenario 2: folded score sheets and pizza boxes after a club event
- Scenario 4: dashboard split between one green line and one red line
- Scenario 5: seminar room with a professor pressing for a single number

Preview Constraint:
The preview must ask how explicit betting language changes belief revision and accountability.

Genericity Risks:
- repeating the introduction's football case too heavily
- turning poker-versus-chess into a slogan instead of a mechanism
- letting uncertainty sound like indecision training

Forbidden Drift:
- no gambling glamor
- no fake precision
- no unsupported scientific claims
- no brief-language leakage
