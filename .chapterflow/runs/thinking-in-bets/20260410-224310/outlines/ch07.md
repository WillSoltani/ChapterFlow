# Chapter Outline

Chapter Promise:
Show how widening perspective across time can improve present decisions without pretending the future is knowable with certainty.

Opening Move:
Open from the way present emotion, social pressure, or immediacy can trap judgment inside too small a frame.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1, P2
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P2, P3
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P3, P4

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: Expose the limits of present-bound judgment.
- P2: Introduce mental time travel as a way to widen perspective.
- P3: Show how future-oriented reflection can improve current decisions and reviews.
- P4: Clarify the limit between useful perspective and empty speculation.
- P5: Close the book-level arc by tying together uncertainty, feedback, dissent, and future perspective.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter must distinguish future-facing perspective from fantasy, overconfidence, or fake foresight.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard mode must keep the tension between widened perspective and real uncertainty: how do you imagine a future review point without pretending you can see the future clearly?

Threshold Question:
What changes when you judge today's decision from outside today's emotions?

Takeaway Count Lock:
- Easy: 3
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 5

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- Takeaway 1: present-bound judgment can be too narrow
- Takeaway 2: mental time travel can widen the decision frame
- Takeaway 3: future perspective is different from fantasy certainty
- Takeaway 4: later review points can discipline current choices
- Takeaway 5: the book's method closes by combining uncertainty with widened perspective

Scenario Lesson Map:
- Scenario 1 (dialogue, work): a teammate asks how the choice will look in six months
- Scenario 2 (postmortem, personal): someone reviews a decision from the vantage point they wish they had earlier
- Scenario 3 (decision_point, school): a student imagines how a future review panel would judge the current call
- Scenario 4 (before_after, work): a team shifts from immediate pressure to longer-horizon evaluation
- Scenario 5 (dilemma, personal): someone confuses hopeful fantasy with useful future perspective
- Scenario 6 (predict_reveal, school): a workshop improves after one person asks what later evidence would say

Scenario Vividness Targets:
- Scenario 1: conference room calendar open to a later milestone
- Scenario 2: journal entry split between then and now
- Scenario 4: planning board showing today's pressure beside later outcomes
- Scenario 6: workshop worksheet with a future review column

Preview Constraint:
The preview should function as a book-closing recap rather than pointing to another numbered chapter.

Genericity Risks:
- drifting into vague life-coaching language
- treating mental time travel like certainty instead of perspective
- losing the connection back to uncertainty and evidence

Forbidden Drift:
- no generic manifestation language
- no faux-neuroscience
- no source-free exercises presented as if they were exact chapter method
