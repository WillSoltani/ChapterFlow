Core Claim:
This chapter argues that bet framing is valuable not only for speaking more honestly, but for learning better as outcomes unfold over time. Duke uses the idea of the unfolding future to show that feedback arrives noisily, often in fragments, and only helps if people keep separating process from result. The chapter has to teach learning discipline under uncertainty, not just prediction discipline.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the chapter that turns bet language into a feedback system. It must show how to read unfolding evidence without snapping back into outcome worship. If it becomes a repeat of Chapters 1 or 2, the learning loop collapses.

Author Logic Chain:
First Duke shows that the future reveals itself gradually rather than all at once.
Then she explains why people misread that feedback when they crave immediate verdicts.
Then she reframes outcomes as data points that should update beliefs without overruling process.
Then she pushes the reader toward active learning from unfolding evidence.

Required Anchors:
- The unfolding future as a stream of evidence rather than a single reveal.
- Bet framing as a way to learn, update, and inspect process over time.
- The need to separate noisy short-run outcomes from the quality of the original judgment.

Frameworks or Terms Introduced:

- update discipline: changing confidence in proportion to evidence

Specific Applications:
- Read early project feedback without treating one data point as final proof.
- Review a forecast in stages as new evidence arrives.
- Use outcomes to refine confidence rather than to defend ego.

Common Misreadings:
- The chapter says results barely matter.
- Updating means abandoning conviction whenever new data appears.

Counterarguments or Limits:
The chapter must concede that some results do contain strong signal and that stubbornness can hide inside "process talk." Its nuance is not endless patience. Its nuance is disciplined updating while the evidence stream is still incomplete.
