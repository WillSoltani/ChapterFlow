The future rarely arrives as a clean verdict. People want one result to settle whether a choice was smart, whether a forecast was accurate, and whether they should feel proud or foolish. Duke pushes against that craving by reminding the reader that the future unfolds. Feedback comes in pieces. Some pieces carry real signal. Some are mostly noise. Most arrive before the whole picture is visible.

That is why this chapter follows the move into bet language. Once a belief has been priced honestly, the outcome can start teaching instead of merely hurting. But the teaching is rarely simple. A rollout can show one metric rising while another slips. A thesis can look weak early and stronger later. Duke's point is not that results do not matter. It is that early results often come mixed, and mixed evidence has to be handled with more discipline than a fast verdict allows.

Fielding the unfolding future means receiving outcomes without forcing them into a final meaning too quickly. A result is information, but not every piece of information deserves the same weight. Some outcomes are strong signals. Others feel decisive only because they are fresh. Learning improves when the reader stops demanding a complete answer from the first fragment that appears.

This is where update discipline matters. Updating is not wobbling. It does not mean reversing course every time a number moves or one painful result lands. It means changing confidence in proportion to the evidence. Strong evidence should move the estimate more. Noisy or partial evidence should move it less. That keeps the learner from clinging to a bad belief on one side or abandoning a sound process on the other.

Duke also keeps process review alive. Incomplete feedback does not erase responsibility, and one bad result does not settle the whole case. The reader has to ask what the new evidence is saying while also asking what the original decision deserved given what was known then. That makes learning slower than a hot verdict but far more honest. Once that posture is in place, outcomes become inputs for revision instead of just ammunition for ego. The next chapter can then bring other people into the learning loop without losing that discipline.
