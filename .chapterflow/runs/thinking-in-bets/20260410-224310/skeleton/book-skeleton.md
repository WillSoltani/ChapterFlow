## Chapter Order And Intent

1. `Introduction: Why this isn't a poker book`
- intent: Open the book by breaking the assumption that outcomes cleanly prove decision quality and by establishing uncertainty as the book's real subject.
- source richness: medium
- concept density: medium
- moral complexity: low
- premium-routing risk: low

2. `Life is poker, not chess`
- intent: Establish the book's master contrast by showing why hidden information and luck make life a probabilistic game rather than a perfect-information puzzle.
- source richness: high
- concept density: high
- moral complexity: medium
- premium-routing risk: high

3. `Wanna bet?`
- intent: Reframe decisions and beliefs as bets so confidence becomes explicit, revisable, and more honest.
- source richness: high
- concept density: high
- moral complexity: medium
- premium-routing risk: medium

4. `Bet to learn: fielding the unfolding future`
- intent: Show how people misread outcomes through self-serving bias and how better feedback habits separate luck from skill.
- source richness: high
- concept density: high
- moral complexity: medium
- premium-routing risk: high

5. `The buddy system`
- intent: Explain why truth-seeking works better inside a well-structured group than inside a solitary mind defending its own beliefs.
- source richness: high
- concept density: medium
- moral complexity: medium
- premium-routing risk: medium

6. `Dissent to win`
- intent: Turn group truth-seeking into a sharper operating system built on information sharing, standard-based evaluation, conflict awareness, and organized skepticism.
- source richness: high
- concept density: high
- moral complexity: medium
- premium-routing risk: medium

7. `Adventures in mental time travel`
- intent: Close the book by showing how regret, precommitment, scenario planning, backcasting, and premortems improve present decisions.
- source richness: high
- concept density: high
- moral complexity: medium
- premium-routing risk: high
