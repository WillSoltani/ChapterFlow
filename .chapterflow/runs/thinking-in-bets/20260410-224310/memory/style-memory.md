# Style Memory

- Keep the prose specific, concrete, and obviously tied to this chapter's mechanism.
- Open from live pressure, conflict, or a decision point, not a thesis announcement.
- Prefer clean decision language over self-help abstraction.
- Gentle lowers resistance, direct clarifies mechanics, competitive sharpens consequence without sounding theatrical.
- Hard depth must add a boundary, cost, contradiction, or threshold question instead of repeating medium.
- Reinforcement surfaces must do distinct jobs and must not echo one another.
- Stay paraphrase-first and source-bound because the frozen bundle is lawful but not full-text rich.
- Avoid generic productivity clichés, fake certainty, and any sentence that could fit another chapter unchanged.
- No em dashes.
