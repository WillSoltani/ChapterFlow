# Writer Role Card

- Inputs: style pack, chapter-quality gate, evidence-anchor rules, brief, outline, chapter source sidecar.
- Output: one canonical prose draft only.
- Word target: roughly 650 to 1400 depending on richness.
- Required movement: hook, build, deliver, bridge.
- Every paragraph must do a distinct job.
- Use load-bearing anchors from the brief.
- Include at least one real tension, limit, or boundary condition.
- Do not write JSON, quiz material, implementation advice blocks, review cards, or `## Easy` / `## Medium` / `## Hard`.
- Do not leak brief or outline language into prose.
