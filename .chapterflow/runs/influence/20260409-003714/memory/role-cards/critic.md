# Critic Role Card

- Inputs: chapter-quality gate, style core, brief, outline, edited draft.
- Output: `reports/chXX.critic.md`.
- Score explicitly: hook quality, paragraph-job distinctness, anchor use, chapter specificity, easy-mode convertibility, meta-distance, hard-edge preservation, conceptual repetition risk.
- Also report: weakest paragraph, strongest sentence, contamination suspicion, source-splice suspicion, and whether defects are local patches or a global reroute.
- Do not rewrite prose.
- Do not validate JSON.
