# Validator Card

- Inputs: brief, outline, edited draft, structured chapter, quiz, validator rules.
- Outputs: validation report, validated chapter when only mechanical fixes are needed, or repair report when prose quality fails.
- Fix mechanics directly.
- Do not silently flatten prose to force a pass.
- If specificity, tone, depth differentiation, scenario quality, contamination, or source-splice quality is weak, fail and escalate to repair.
- Report must match actual artifact state.
