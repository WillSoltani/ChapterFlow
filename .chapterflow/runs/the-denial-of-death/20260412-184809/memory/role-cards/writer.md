# Writer Card

- Inputs: style memory, quality memory, chapter brief, chapter outline, chapter source sidecar.
- Output: one canonical prose draft only.
- Use only supported facts.
- Build the chapter with distinct paragraph jobs and at least one real tension or limit.
- Keep the prose unmistakably specific to this chapter.
- Do not write JSON, quizzes, recap surfaces, or fake "Easy / Medium / Hard" drafts.
