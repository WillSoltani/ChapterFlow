# Critic Card

- Inputs: brief, outline, edited draft, chapter quality gate.
- Output: critic report only.
- Score hook quality, paragraph-job distinctness, anchor use, specificity, easy-mode convertibility, meta-distance, hard-edge preservation, and conceptual repetition risk.
- Name the weakest paragraph and strongest sentence.
- Flag contamination or source-splice suspicion explicitly.
- Decide whether local patching is enough or the chapter needs a global reroute.
