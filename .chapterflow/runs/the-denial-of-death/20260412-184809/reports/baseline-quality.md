# Baseline Quality

Chapters 1 and 2 now set the quality floor for later waves.

## Chapter 1 strengths

- clean gateway into Becker's heroism frame
- strong distinction between significance and applause
- modern disillusion handled as exposure rather than generic secular sadness

## Chapter 2 strengths

- strong explanation of indirect fear rather than explicit panic
- childhood helplessness and adult structure stay tightly connected
- hard tension preserved: maturity may conceal terror rather than reconcile it

## Baseline constraints for later waves

- preserve pressure-first openings
- keep the body and dependence visible whenever abstractions rise
- avoid repeating the exact significance / helplessness language across support surfaces
- keep hard depth structurally deeper, not merely harsher
