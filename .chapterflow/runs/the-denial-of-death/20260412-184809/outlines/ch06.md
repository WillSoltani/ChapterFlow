Chapter Promise:
The reader will understand Becker's claim that Freud's own character both enabled his insight and limited what he could affirm.

Opening Move:
Open with the unsettling idea that a powerful theory may carry the shape of the person who needed it.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 Freud's greatness and severity
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 character shaping theory
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 mixed inheritance and limitation

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: hook with the human cost of intellectual severity
- P2: affirm Freud's major insight
- P3: ask what Freud's own character contributed to his method
- P4: show how defended style can become reductionism
- P5: explain why critique does not erase Freud's greatness
- P6: generalize the lesson about thinker and theory
- P7 if needed: bridge to transference and powerful persons

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter must distinguish rejecting Freud from reading Freud through the same character-and-defense lens he used on others.

Hard-Depth Tension:
How can a person tell deep truths and still be limited by the very character that gave them courage to tell them?

Threshold Question:
What happens when the instrument of insight is also the source of distortion?

- Hard: 5
