Chapter Promise:
The reader will understand Becker's general view that mental illness remains an intelligible but damaged attempt to secure meaning, protection, and transcendence.

Opening Move:
Open by rejecting the idea that severe disorder is simply alien nonsense.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 general view and continuity principle
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 distorted hero-system effort
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 intelligibility without romanticizing breakdown

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: hook with the temptation to call pathology meaningless
- P2: introduce Becker's general view
- P3: show continuity from ordinary defense to distorted hero-system effort
- P4: explain why pathology can become more reality-breaking
- P5: preserve intelligibility without sentimentalizing illness
- P6: show why this matters for the whole book's argument
- P7 if needed: bridge to the heroic individual and religion question

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter must distinguish intelligibility from approval, and continuity from sameness.

Hard-Depth Tension:
How can severe disorder remain part of the same human struggle for meaning without erasing the real extremity of breakdown?

Threshold Question:
What changes when pathology is read as damaged hero-system effort instead of pure incomprehensible chaos?
