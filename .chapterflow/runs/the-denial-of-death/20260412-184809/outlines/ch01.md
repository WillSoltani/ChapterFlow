Chapter Promise:
The reader will understand why Becker treats heroism, the need to matter against vulnerability, as the opening key to the human condition.

Opening Move:
Start from a pressure-filled modern scene: people want ideas that explain their predicament, not decorative theory, and Becker offers heroism as the first serious answer.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 opening pressure for vital concepts
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P2-P4 heroism as the drive to matter
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 modern disillusion and cultural breakdown

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: hook through the hunger for ideas equal to the human dilemma
- P2: define heroism in Becker's sense and show why it exceeds ordinary ambition
- P3: connect heroism to narcissism and the refusal to feel merely small
- P4: clarify that culture gives ordinary people a stage for symbolic significance
- P5: show what changes in modernity when inherited heroic systems weaken
- P6: tension paragraph on what happens when the need remains but the old stories fail
- P7 if needed: bridge into Chapter 2 by naming death-terror as the underlying pressure

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Heroism is not vanity plus applause. It is the symbolic answer people build against insignificance and vulnerability.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Can a culture strip away illusion and still give people a livable form of heroism, or does demystification leave them exposed?

Threshold Question:
What kind of life still feels worth inhabiting once inherited heroic scripts stop working but the need for significance remains?

Takeaway Count Lock:
- Easy: 3 (always)
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 5

If the chapter makes one argument from multiple angles, use the lower end.
If it builds a multi-part framework, use the higher end.
Do not leave this decision to the converter.

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- Takeaway 1: heroism as a human motive
- Takeaway 2: narcissism as fuel for significance
- Takeaway 3: culture as a stage for symbolic value
- Takeaway 4: modern disillusion as a crisis of belonging
- Takeaway 5: the unanswered problem of post-illusion courage

Preview Constraint:
The preview must ask what fear or pressure makes heroism so necessary. It must not merely announce "next comes death."

- making heroism sound like self-esteem advice
- skipping modern disillusion and becoming a broad intro to mortality
