# Baseline Quality — Chapters 1 and 2

## Purpose
Chapters 1 and 2 now define the baseline quality floor for later waves.

## Chapter 1 strengths
- strong causal reframing from visible distraction to internal trigger
- clean substitution logic that will carry later tactical chapters
- honest hard-depth limit: diagnosis helps without pretending to cure

## Chapter 2 strengths
- strong extension from internal triggers into calendar behavior
- respectable substitute tasks are concrete and load-bearing
- preserves the structural-delay boundary so the chapter does not become a lazy accusation

## Shared quality floor
- chapter-specific mechanism stays visible the whole way through
- examples do different jobs across work, school, and personal life
- implementation plans stay tied to the governing concept instead of generic advice
- hard depth adds a real boundary condition rather than inflated vocabulary

## What later waves must preserve
- no drift into generic productivity prose
- no treating technology as the only cause
- no collapsing planning into cosmetic list management
- no loss of the humane but unsentimental tone established here
