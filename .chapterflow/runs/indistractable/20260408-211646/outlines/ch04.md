# Ch04 Outline — Reimagine the Internal Trigger

1. Open with the old interpretation: discomfort means escape now
2. Replace that interpretation with a calmer, more accurate one
3. Show how naming and observing the trigger weakens false urgency
4. Keep the chapter practical rather than spiritual or clinical
5. Preserve the limit that discomfort may remain unpleasant
6. Bridge to Chapter 5 by asking how the task itself can be reshaped once the trigger loses authority

## Hard-depth threshold
- the chapter must change meaning, not just repeat "notice the urge"
- discomfort stays real even after reinterpretation
