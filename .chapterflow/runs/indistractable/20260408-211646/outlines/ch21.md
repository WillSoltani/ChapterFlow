# Ch21 Outline — Prevent Distraction with Price Pacts

2. Define a price pact as a prechosen cost attached to breaking prior intent
3. Keep the sequencing rule explicit: this is a late-stage support, not the first answer
4. Show concrete ways explicit cost can protect a real plan across work, school, and personal settings
5. Preserve the limit that price pacts should support values rather than become punishment theater or gimmicks
6. Bridge to Chapter 22 by showing that cost is one kind of pact, while identity can become another
