# Ch19 Outline — Hack Back Feeds

1. Open on the way feeds differ from articles because they keep supplying the next item with almost no stopping cue
2. Reframe a feed as a novelty sequence rather than a neutral information list
3. Show concrete controls: deliberate entry, bounded session rules, fewer default feed surfaces, and scheduled retrieval instead of endless refresh
4. Keep the chapter distinct from smartphone hardware and from browser-article drift
5. Preserve the limit that some feeds may contain useful material even though the sequence design is destabilizing
6. Bridge to Chapter 20 by showing why future-facing constraints may be needed when feed loops keep beating present intention
