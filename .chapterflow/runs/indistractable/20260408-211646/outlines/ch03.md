# Ch03 Outline — Deal with Distraction from Within

1. Open before the visible distraction and locate the inner urge
2. Show that the decisive move often happens inside the person first
3. Separate urge from action so the reader gets an intervention point
4. Explain why tool-only defenses often arrive too late
5. Keep the limit that noticing is not the same as making discomfort pleasant
6. Bridge to Chapter 4 by asking how the internal trigger itself should be understood

## Hard-depth threshold
- the chapter must feel like a skill of inner interception, not vague mindfulness wallpaper
- discomfort stays real even when automatic obedience weakens
