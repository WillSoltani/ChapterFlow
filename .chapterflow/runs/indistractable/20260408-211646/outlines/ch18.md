# Ch18 Outline — Hack Back Online Articles

1. Open on the way one online article quietly becomes many paths through links, sidebars, and recommendations
2. Reframe online reading as a bounded act that can spill into drift if left open-ended
3. Show concrete controls: read with purpose, contain tab multiplication, save later instead of opening now, and limit expansion paths
4. Keep the chapter distinct from general desktop design and from feed scrolling
5. Preserve the limit that reading itself remains valuable
6. Bridge to Chapter 19 by showing how feeds remove even more friction from browser wandering

## Hard-depth threshold
- the chapter must stay about article-to-wandering drift
- it must not become anti-reading or duplicate the feed chapter
