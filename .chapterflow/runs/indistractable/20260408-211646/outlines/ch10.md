# Ch10 Outline — Sync with Stakeholders at Work

1. Open on the fact that many work interruptions come from people filling ambiguity with pings
2. Reframe stakeholder syncing as expectation-setting that protects traction and coordination
3. Show what must be aligned: response windows, urgency rules, escalation paths, meeting norms, and focus blocks
4. Include remote-work and videoconferencing pressure without drifting into generic etiquette advice
5. Preserve the limit that syncing is not permission to disappear or dump costs onto coworkers
6. Bridge to Chapter 11 by showing how aligned expectations make reactive checking easier to question

## Hard-depth threshold
- the chapter must stay about coordination failure and expectation alignment
- it must not collapse into politeness tips or anti-coworker rhetoric
