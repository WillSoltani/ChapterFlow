# Ch23 Outline — Fixing Distraction Is a Test of Company Culture

1. Open on the idea that chronic distraction at work is often produced by norms rather than by isolated weak people
2. Reframe workplace distraction as a cultural and coordination test
3. Show how interruption norms, responsiveness pressure, meeting sprawl, and fear of speaking up shape attention
4. Preserve that culture is visible in remote and video contexts too, not only in physical offices
5. Keep the chapter systemic and anti-blame without turning it into a rant
6. Bridge to Chapter 24 by moving from diagnosis of culture to the habits and norms of an indistractable workplace

## Hard-depth threshold
- the chapter must stay systemic and coordination-centered
- it must not become generic anti-office complaint or consume Chapter 24's operational habits
