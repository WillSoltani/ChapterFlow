# Ch28 Outline — Teach Them to Make Their Own Pacts

1. Open by moving from environmental awareness to commitments children can increasingly own
2. Reframe pacts for children as guided, self-authored precommitments rather than purely imposed rules
3. Keep adult scaffolding and developmental limits visible throughout
4. Show concrete child-owned pact examples across school, home, and peer settings
5. Resist making autonomy sound sudden, total, or frictionless
6. Bridge to Chapter 29 by showing that social settings will test these commitments among peers

## Hard-depth threshold
- the chapter must keep gradual ownership and scaffolding load-bearing
- it must not make child autonomy sound magically complete or overly neat
