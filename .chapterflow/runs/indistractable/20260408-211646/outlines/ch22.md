# Ch22 Outline — Prevent Distraction with Identity Pacts

1. Open on the way behavior gets steadier when it feels tied to who you are trying to be
2. Define an identity pact as a commitment that links chosen identity to concrete repeated action
3. Keep the chapter grounded in behavior-level proof rather than slogans or affirmations
4. Show concrete identity-linked follow-through across work, school, and personal settings
5. Preserve the limit that identity supports earlier work and is strengthened by action
6. Bridge to Chapter 23 by widening from individual identity to workplace culture and shared norms

## Hard-depth threshold
- the chapter must stay concrete and behavior-level
- it must not become empty self-talk or drift into workplace culture too early
