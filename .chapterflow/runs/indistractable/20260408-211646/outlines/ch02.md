# Ch02 Outline — Time Management Is Pain Management

1. Open with a respectable-looking delay that is doing emotional work
2. Reframe time trouble as pain avoidance around uncomfortable tasks
3. Show how busy substitute tasks camouflage the avoidance
4. Keep planning in scope but refuse to let planning become the alibi
5. Preserve the boundary that some delays are structural, not avoidant
6. Bridge to Chapter 3 by asking how a person meets discomfort directly

## Hard-depth threshold
- a busy day can still be strategically false
- the chapter must not become a blanket accusation against every missed task
