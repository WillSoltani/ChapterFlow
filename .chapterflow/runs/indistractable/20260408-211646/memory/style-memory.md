# Style Memory — *Indistractable* v13 Run

## Voice
- sharp human explainer, not helper-model polish
- concrete over abstract
- specific over generic
- readable, confident, and authored
- "you" is allowed but should feel natural
- teach the idea directly instead of narrating chapter existence

## Depth contract
- Easy: grade 8-9, one clean frame, vivid early concrete hook, no stacked abstractions
- Medium: grade 10-11, mechanism plus application, real contrast, sharper distinctions
- Hard: grade 12, add a live limit, contradiction, or boundary condition without turning into a report

## Tone contract
- gentle lowers resistance and gives a humane entry
- direct names the mechanism plainly
- competitive sharpens stakes, leverage, and consequence
- tones must differ functionally, not by adjective swap

## Hard constraints
- no em dashes
- no made-up quotes, studies, or anecdotes
- no filler neuroscience or pseudo-precision
- no contamination phrases from briefs, outlines, or validators
- no thesis-first openings in reader-facing prose
- no pseudo-drafts headed with Easy / Medium / Hard

## Pattern checks
- each paragraph must do a different job
- every chapter must feel unmistakably like that chapter, not like a productivity template
- examples need concrete names, objects, and settings
- implementation advice must be chapter-specific, not generic life coaching
