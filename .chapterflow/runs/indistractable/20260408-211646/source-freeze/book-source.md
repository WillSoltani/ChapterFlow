## Public framework support frozen from author articles
- traction means actions aligned with values and prior intent
- distraction means actions that pull a person away from what they said they would do
- internal triggers come from uncomfortable internal states and urges
- external triggers come from cues in the environment or from other people
- pacts are precommitments that shape future behavior
