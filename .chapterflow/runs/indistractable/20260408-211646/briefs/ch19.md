## Governing Question
How can a person break the relationship with feeds that keeps novelty, refresh, and the next item in charge of attention?

## Core claim
The chapter should argue that feeds are not just collections of posts. They are next-item engines with weak stopping cues, easy refresh, and constant novelty. The answer is not generic panic about social media. It is to stop treating the feed as an always-open habitat and to reintroduce entry rules, exit rules, and narrower purpose so novelty stops governing the session.

## Required anchors
- feeds are distinct from one chosen article because the next item keeps arriving without a stable boundary
- feed logic depends on weak stopping cues, refresh behavior, novelty, and endless succession
- the correction is to break automatic feed wandering and turn feed use into bounded retrieval or scheduled use
- this chapter must stay distinct from smartphone hardware and from article drift
- the chapter should bridge naturally into precommitments when intention alone is too weak

## Reader value
- see why feeds feel bottomless even when each item looks small
- identify one feed rule that restores a stopping point
- understand why the feed problem is about sequence design, not just content quality

## Hard-depth boundary
Do not turn the chapter into a generic anti-phone or anti-internet sermon. Preserve that some feeds can surface useful information. The problem is the feed relationship that keeps novelty and next-item motion in charge.
