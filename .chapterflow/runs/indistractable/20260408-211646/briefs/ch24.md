## Governing Question
What habits and shared norms define a workplace that protects focus instead of quietly draining it?

## Core claim
The chapter should describe the operational habits of an indistractable workplace: clearer expectations, healthier meeting norms, better interruption boundaries, permission to protect focus, and coordination habits that reduce ambient urgency. It should feel like a practical design chapter rather than vague future-of-work talk. The chapter should stay downstream of the culture diagnosis in Chapter 23.

## Required anchors
- an indistractable workplace is defined by shared habits and norms, not by one heroic employee
- focus protection requires explicit operational practices around meetings, interruptions, response expectations, and planning
- the chapter should stay concrete about coordination and permission
- the workplace should be framed as a designed environment, not a motivational slogan
- this chapter should bridge into the parenting section without overreaching
