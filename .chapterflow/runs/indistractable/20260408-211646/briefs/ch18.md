## Governing Question
How can reading online stay connected to a chosen purpose instead of dissolving into endless browser wandering?

## Core claim
The chapter should argue that online articles become distracting when reading stops being a bounded act and becomes an open pathway into links, sidebars, recommended stories, and new tabs. The answer is not to stop reading. It is to contain the reading session so one article does not quietly turn into a tour of adjacent temptations.

## Required anchors
- online articles are a specific browser-based trigger path where one reading task expands into many
- sidebars, recommendations, links, and tab drift make reading unbounded
- bounded reading, saved-later handling, and deliberate article choice are stronger than open-ended wandering
- the chapter should preserve reading as valuable while resisting browser sprawl
- this chapter should bridge naturally into feeds

## Reader value
