### 1. Concept Coverage — 2/2

All three core concepts are present and developed:

- **The internal voice**: Named and mechanized — three specific tasks identified (counter-arguments, judging the speaker, managing identity stakes) and distinguished from each other. The concept is not presented as generic distraction; it is precise. A person occupied with all three tasks is "staging a performance of hearing" — the language captures the chapter's central claim.
- **The inside-out move**: Present in full — the two-step structure (notice, name; then set aside deliberately) is explicit. The suppression distinction is present: "Not suppression — suppressing the voice makes it louder. Deliberate acknowledgment, then intentional suspension." The move is demonstrated through Seren: she names it to herself, sets it down, asks the honest question. The consequence — the conversation changes register — follows immediately.
- **The three external moves**: All three present with sufficient development. Genuine-curiosity questions are distinguished from rhetorical and leading ones. Paraphrasing is defined as proof of reception, not agreement, with the "Is that right?" closing specified as a correction invitation. Acknowledging feelings is separated from agreement: "naming the feeling signals it is legitimate material — that is a different claim from 'you are right about what caused it.'"
- **Hard depth (paraphrasing backfire)**: Present at full weight in a dedicated paragraph. Three specific misfire conditions named: scripted phrasing, wrong timing, register mismatch. The fix is stated in concrete terms — paraphrase in language that sounds like you, not a protocol — and demonstrated with a specific example ("Wait — I think what you're actually saying is that nobody asked you."). The hard-depth content is substantive, not a footnote.
- **Sequencing constraint**: Present and stated explicitly: the external moves "can be performed before the inside-out move is made, but they will be performed on top of inattention." The chapter closes on this: "without the inside-out move, the most careful question, the most accurate paraphrase, and the most well-timed acknowledgment are still performances." The sequencing is structural, not incidental.
