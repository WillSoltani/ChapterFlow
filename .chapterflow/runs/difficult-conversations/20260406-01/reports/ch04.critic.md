## Quality Gate Rubric

### 1. Chapter Specificity — 2/2
The chapter is unmistakably about the blame-to-contribution shift. The Dara/Felix anchor (a specific three-month cycle of withdrawal and oversight, a specific three-draft HR email) and the Marta/Sione anchor (a decade of holiday fights, a specific role structure) are chapter-specific and load-bearing. The four contribution types in P6 are introduced as the chapter's own analytical inventory, not as generic interpersonal advice. The contribution message Dara sends Felix at the end is specific in form — "I've been less communicative than usual, and I think that's made things harder for both of us" — and cannot be moved to another chapter.

### 2. Anchor Use — 2/2
Dara/Felix opens (P1), returns in P5 to surface the contribution system, and resolves in P9. Marta/Sione (P6) extends the mechanism to a decade-long personal pattern and shows the role-assumption contribution type. Yemi/Kwame (mentioned briefly in P7) illustrates style-intersection contribution as distinct from the others. All three anchors are load-bearing; none is decorative.

### 3. Analytical Value — 2/2
The chapter delivers two real structural insights: (1) blame is retrospective and asks who deserves punishment, while contribution is prospective and asks what each person can change — these are not just framing differences but point toward different actions; (2) the asymmetry note — contributions are rarely equal, and naming your piece does not require claiming the proportions are equal. The contribution system framing (P5 and P8) — that the system is the target, not the villain — is the chapter's sharpest analytical edge.

### 4. Paragraph Motion — 2/2
Ten paragraphs, ten distinct jobs:
- P1: Hook — three drafts of the HR email, certainty without clarity
- P2: The thing underneath the certainty — Dara stopped communicating
- P3: Why blame fails structurally — retrospective, asks the wrong question
- P4: Introduce contribution — not equal blame, but a different question and a different target

No paragraph repeats another's job. The chapter moves forward.

### 5. Prose Quality — 2/2
Strong throughout. "Blame is retrospective in a situation that is entirely about what happens next" is the chapter's sharpest sentence. "Her non-role is itself a role" for Sione's contribution is precise and vivid. "Her competence at logistics has invisibly guaranteed that Sione never had to develop any logistics capacity" captures the role-assumption contribution type without jargon. The contribution types in P8 are named clearly and each is illustrated by its own anchor example. Grade level approximately 10-11.

### 6. Hook and Bridge — 2/2
Hook: Dara's three draft emails, each with a different subject line. The reader knows this feeling — the certainty about the other person combined with the uncertainty about what to do. Bridge: "the recognition of one's own contribution to harm can produce guilt, grief, or something closer to relief. Those feelings need somewhere to go." Holds Ch05 as a live question without summarizing it.

## Observations

**Weakest paragraph:** P8 (four contribution types) — the most inventory-heavy paragraph in the draft. The list format risks reading as a taxonomy rather than a discovery. It works because each type is anchored concretely, but it is the paragraph most likely to feel like an explanation rather than a revelation.

**Strongest sentence:** "Blame is retrospective in a situation that is entirely about what happens next."

**Contamination check:** CLEAN. "Contribution" and "blame" are permitted framework vocabulary. No contamination phrases from the banned list.

**Hard-depth note:** The asymmetric case (90/10 contributions) is seeded in P4 and P9. The hard-depth extension will need to address the power-gap complication: when contribution-mapping is used by a more powerful party to deflect accountability from a less-powerful party. That complication is not present in the draft (appropriately — it is hard-depth territory).

**Moral complexity note:** The chapter handles the asymmetric case correctly — naming your 10% does not erase the other person's 90%. The Dara message at the end is careful: "I've been less communicative than usual, and I think that's made things harder for both of us" is not a concession that Felix's oversight is appropriate. The moral complexity is held correctly.
