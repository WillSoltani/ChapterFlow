Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Composition versus performance. The earlier moves have to appear as tools selected for the moment, not as a rigid sequence recited to prove competence.

---

Hard-Depth Tension:
What success means when the framework does not "work" in the obvious sense. The conversation can still be skillful and still end without engagement, agreement, or safety for full candor.

---

Threshold Question:
Are you using these moves to learn and be changed, or to sound reasonable while forcing the other person toward your conclusion?
