Chapter: 4 — Abandon Blame

Core concepts to test:
- Blame vs. contribution distinction: blame asks who deserves punishment; contribution asks what each person can change
- Contributions are rarely equal — contribution-mapping is not assigning 50/50 responsibility
- The four contribution types: avoiding-until-now, unapproachability, style intersections, problematic role assumptions
- The contribution system — both people are playing roles in a pattern that produces the same outcome; the system is the target, not a villain
