Chapter: 10 — Expression: Speak for Yourself with Clarity and Power

Core concepts to test:
- Expression as speaking from your own experience rather than making a case against the other person
- The three-part form: observation, internal response, and the story you were telling yourself
- The observation must be clean: camera-describable, not a disguised conclusion about motive or character
- The internal response must be owned as yours, not turned into accusation
- The story component is offered as a story to check, not a verdict to impose
- Honesty calibration: say the hardest thing first, and avoid both overstatement and understatement
- Durability: do not erase the honest point the moment the other person reacts
- (Hard) Moral complexity: holding the point can become stubbornness when the speaker stops treating their story as revisable

Bloom ceiling:
- Highest justified level: analyze / evaluate
- Moral complexity flag is present on this chapter. The hardest move is not simple recall of the three-part form; it is judging when durable honesty is preserving truth and when it has shifted into disguised accusation or rigid insistence. q07-q08 should diagnose execution failures. q09-q10 can reach evaluate because readers must weigh competing goods: courage versus openness, steadiness versus stubbornness.
