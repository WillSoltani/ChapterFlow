--- Slice A: Part II, Good Games (source lines 496-530) ---
Berne acknowledged not all games are destructive. 'Good game': social contribution outweighs the complexity of its motives. Still ulterior, still imperfect, net positive.

BUSMAN'S HOLIDAY: take work expertise on vacation (cook volunteers at clinic, programmer at hackathon). Motives mixed (identity, escape from boredom, usefulness). Effect benign.
CAVALIER: express admiration, attention, romantic compliments in way explicitly free of sexual pursuit. Pleasure is in giving the stroke.
HAPPY TO HELP: offers help sincerely without the trap in I'm Only Trying to Help You. Helper does not need recipient to fail.
HOMELY SAGE: usually past middle age, shares hard-won wisdom with younger people without insisting on being heeded. Knowledge offered as gift not test.
THEY'LL BE GLAD THEY KNEW ME: commits to becoming notable in honest way. Partly vanity, work is real.
