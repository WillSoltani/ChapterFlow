--- Slice A: Part II, Marital Games (source lines 248-309) ---
Corner: partner engineers situations where any choice proves the accusation. No winning move. Payoff: confirmation partner is hopeless; avoidance of intimacy.
Courtroom: couple brings disputes to a third party for judgment. Payoff: moral vindication; continuation of dispute under 'seeking help.'
If It Weren't For You: one partner blames restrictions on the other. Structural insight: the restricting partner was often chosen BECAUSE they provided the restriction. Payoff: protection from the fear of trying and failing.
Look How Hard I've Tried: player performs maximum visible effort at an impossible task. Payoff: moral vindication; freedom from responsibility.
Sweetheart: player tells affectionate-seeming story where the punchline is a veiled insult. Partner cannot object. Payoff: aggression with deniability; stored grievance released safely.
