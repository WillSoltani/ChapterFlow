--- Slice A: Part II, Consulting Room Games (source lines 430-491) ---
I'M ONLY TRYING TO HELP YOU (ITHY): helper offers help the recipient predictably refuses or fails to use. When the help fails, the helper has proof the recipient is hopeless and the helper is blameless. Helper's preferred position: I'm OK, you're not OK, and I tried.
WOODEN LEG: player uses a real or claimed disability as permanent exemption from normal accountability. Disability real; game is the deployment.
GREENHOUSE: patient becomes amateur therapist, uses psychological vocabulary to observe and label feelings while avoiding actual change. Theory talk substitutes for experience.
STUPID: player presents as slower/dumber, tasks fail as expected and are forgiven, expectations lowered permanently.
PSYCHIATRY: patient who has been in many therapies uses specialized vocabulary to tour their problems intellectually.

--- Slice B: scope ---
Ch8 covers games inside helping-profession relationships. Helper is structurally part of the game by profession. The chapter must not moralize clients or absolve professionals. Berne's Indigence game is NOT covered (1964 welfare-reform framing is unusable).

--- Slice C: moral complexity ---
Wooden Leg especially: real disabilities exist. The game is in the deployment, not the existence. The chapter must say this clearly.
