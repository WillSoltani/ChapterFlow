# Book skeleton — Games People Play

## Metadata
- Title: Games People Play
- Author: Eric Berne
- Edition: First edition (Grove Press, 1964)
- bookId: games-people-play
- Chapter count: 10 (ChapterFlow mapping; source book has 18)
- Source provenance: paraphrased reference, no verbatim reproduction
- 1960s framings flagged globally: gendered case studies, dated psychiatric language, and mid-century welfare politics must be re-cast in neutral modern terms.

## Chapter order and one-line intents

1. **The Three Voices Inside You** — Parent/Adult/Child as three observable behavioral states every adult can shift between.
2. **What a Transaction Is and Why They Go Wrong** — complementary vs crossed transactions; how conversations "go sideways."
3. **Ulterior Transactions and the Anatomy of a Game** — two-level messages and the definition of a game (thesis/antithesis/payoff).
4. **Why Games Feel So Hard to Stop** — strokes, six payoff advantages, life positions; why shaming doesn't work.
5. **Life Games** — Alcoholic, Kick Me, NIGYSOB, See What You Made Me Do, Debtor; patterns that run for years.
6. **Marital Games** — Corner, Courtroom, If It Weren't For You, Harried, Sweetheart; intimacy avoidance under cover of closeness.
7. **Party and Social Games** — Ain't It Awful, Blemish, Schlemiel, Why Don't You Yes But; short-duration bonding through grievance or deflection.
8. **Professional and Therapy-Room Games** — I'm Only Trying to Help You, Stupid, Wooden Leg, Psychiatry, Greenhouse; games dressed as help.
9. **Games That Aren't Worth Fighting** — Busman's Holiday, Cavalier, Happy to Help, Homely Sage; "good games" whose net effect is positive.
10. **What Comes After Games** — Awareness, Spontaneity, Intimacy as the three qualities of autonomy.
