# Chapter 10 Outline — What Comes After Games

## Seven-paragraph job map

### P1 — Hook (scene, no vocabulary yet)
**Job:** Open in a specific moment where something that has always been a pattern is not, this time, a pattern. The reader should feel the absence of the game before the vocabulary arrives. Do not name awareness, spontaneity, or intimacy yet.
**Anchor:** Solenne in the meeting, noticing the pre-formed sentence.
**Move:** The scene ends on the noticing, not on what she decides. The question is left open.
**Forbidden drift:** No vocabulary from Ch1–Ch9 catalog. No naming of awareness yet. Do not let the paragraph open with a thesis.

### P2 — Name the question and introduce the triad
**Job:** Name the question the book has been building toward. Nine chapters have been about patterns. This chapter is about what remains when patterns go. Introduce Berne's claim: three qualities constitute autonomy. Name the three briefly but do not unpack them yet.
**Move:** One paragraph, brisk. Awareness, spontaneity, intimacy each get one clause. "None of them is a technique" arrives here.
**Forbidden drift:** Do not explain any of the three yet. Do not drift into self-help.

### P3 — Awareness
**Job:** Unpack awareness using Solenne. Define it as Berne meant it: direct perception rather than filtered through Parent programming or Child reaction. Show what it looks like when it happens. Name the failure mode.
**Move:** Return to Solenne, extend the scene. Then name the failure mode: awareness can collapse back into observation-as-practice, which is itself a structure.
**Forbidden drift:** Not mindfulness-as-commonly-used. Do not imply it is a practice. Do not make the failure mode sound minor.

### P4 — Spontaneity
**Job:** Unpack spontaneity using Casimir and Valeria. Define it as freedom to respond from whichever ego state the situation calls for. Distinguish it from impulsivity and "just being yourself." Name the failure mode.
**Move:** Introduce Casimir and his automatic reach. Show the spontaneous moment. Name the failure mode: spontaneity can collapse into compulsive reaction, which is also a pattern.
**Forbidden drift:** Do not moralize Casimir's usual behavior. Do not make spontaneity sound like a skill to be practiced.

### P5 — Intimacy
**Job:** Define Berne's intimacy (game-free directness) using Takeshi and Fenna. Name it as the rarest of the three. Keep the anchor concrete and small. Do not dramatize it.
**Move:** Introduce the monthly coffee. Describe the March conversation. Name what is different: both present with what the other is actually saying rather than with what they have decided to say back. Name it as intimacy without sentimentalizing it.
**Forbidden drift:** Not warmth, not closeness in the generic sense. Not a romantic framing. Do not promise it will be frequent.

### P6 — The honest limit
**Job:** The triad is available but rare. Name the labor required to reach each quality. Refuse the technique framing. Name explicitly that most of the previous nine chapters' work was quietly about making the triad possible, not about it directly.
**Move:** Name the prerequisites for each quality and the difficulty of meeting them. Close on the honest answer to why naming the triad is worth doing even if it is rare.
**Forbidden drift:** No promise. No life-coach register. Do not start this paragraph with "In conclusion."

### P7 — Close (book-ending paragraph)
**Job:** This is the only chapter where the ending is not a bridge. Close the ten-chapter arc. Name what the book has tried to give the reader and what it has refused to promise. End with a sentence that could stand as the last sentence of the book.
**Move:** Name the vocabulary the book offered (patterns, payoffs, positions, net effects). Name the honest limit of what it has offered. End on what becomes possible, not on a question.
**Forbidden drift:** No new question. No preview. No moralizing. Do not use "In conclusion" or any banned opener. The ending must close, not open.

## Threshold question
If the triad is rare even for people who have done significant work on their patterns, is naming it still worth doing? Yes, because naming a direction is not the same as promising arrival. The catalog has been useful to readers who will never fully leave the games in it. The triad is useful to readers who will never fully reach autonomy. Both the catalog and the triad are maps. Maps are valuable even when the territory is distant.
