Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader knows that not every game requires an antithesis. Some games should be seen and then left alone, because their net effect is good and interrupting them would be a loss.

Opening Move:

P2: name the category. Good games. Still games in the technical sense, still ulterior, still producing strokes and confirming positions. Different in one way: the world is better for them. The chapter is a correction to the instinct the previous chapters have been building.

P6: the limit. The instruction is not 'never interrupt.' The instruction is 'distinguish the games whose interruption would be a loss from the games whose interruption would be a gain.' The distinction is in the net effect, which includes effects on the player, effects on the recipients, and effects on the world. When the net is positive, the reader's job is to see the game and leave it alone.
P7: bridge to Ch10. The chapters so far have been about patterns. The last chapter is about what sits underneath patterns and what remains when they go.
