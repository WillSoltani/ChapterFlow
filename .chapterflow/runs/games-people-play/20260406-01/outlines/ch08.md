Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader can recognize a game that is hiding inside a professional helping relationship, from either side of the table, and can see that the helper is part of the structure rather than standing outside it.

Opening Move:
Open inside Ife's Thursday supervision meeting with Rémy. Third year. Same structure.

P1: Scene. Rémy presents a case. Ife offers perspective. Rémy thanks her. Both know this is the third year. Neither has said so.
P2: Name the category. Professional games run in helping relationships. The distinctive feature: the helper's role is the cover. The game is invisible from either seat because both seats are being played well.
P3: ITHY anatomy (Rémy and Ife). Thesis: present a problem, receive help, demonstrate the help did not land, return next week. Antithesis: Ife declines the generator role and asks Rémy what has actually changed in his practice in three years. Dynamics: Ife needs to be helping; Rémy needs the weekly hour. Payoff for both: reliable strokes in a structured frame, plus confirmation that the work is ongoing.
P4: Wooden Leg (Bo and Caleb). The limitation is real. The game is in how it is deployed. The helper accepts the limitation because challenging it feels like denying the reality. The antithesis is the one that does not deny the reality AND does not accept the limitation as a permanent exemption: 'The accommodation is available. The accommodation is for working with the disability, not around having to work at all. Which accommodation do you want to try?'
P5: Greenhouse (Tamsin and Zara). The theory talk is sophisticated. The self-observations are accurate. Nothing is changing. The antithesis requires Tamsin to notice that she has been rewarded by the sophistication of the conversations and has forgotten that the sessions are supposed to produce different experiences, not better descriptions of old ones.
P6: The limit. The helper is part of the game. The professional role is the cover. This means the games cannot be diagnosed by looking only at the client; the helper has to be willing to look at what they are getting from the arrangement. That is the hardest part of this chapter, and it is the only chapter where the catalog applies to both sides of the table equally.
P7: Bridge. Not all games are destructive. The next chapter covers the ones Berne called good games, whose net effect on the world is positive even when the structure is still ulterior.

Takeaway count: Easy 3, Medium 5, Hard 6.
