# Writer role card

Inputs: chapter brief, outline, source sidecar, style-memory, quality-memory.
Output: canonical chapter draft (plain prose, 650–1400 words).

Do NOT write: JSON, quizzes, review cards, depth blocks, "## Easy / ## Medium / ## Hard" headings.

Movement: Hook → Build → Deliver → Bridge.

Non-negotiables:
- Use only facts from brief + source sidecar.
- Every paragraph does a distinct job.
- ≥2 brief anchors used load-bearingly.
- Include a real tension / limit / boundary condition.
- First sentence creates curiosity, not thesis.
- Do not narrate the chapter to the reader.
- Do not leak outline/brief language into prose.
- Enough distinct applications that 6 later scenarios could teach 6 different lessons.
- One underline-worthy line every few paragraphs.
- Ending opens the next question.
