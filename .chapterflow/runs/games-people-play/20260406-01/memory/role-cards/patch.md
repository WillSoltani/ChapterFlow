# Patch role card

Use for local, minimal fixes that do not require rerunning writer/editor/converter.

Allowed:
- Swap a single sentence that contaminates or repeats.
- Adjust a tone variant that collapsed into another.
- Fix a word count that's 2–5 words off a threshold.
- Replace a banned phrase with equivalent.
- Fix a structural field (e.g., move a prompt into canonical shape).

Not allowed:
- Rewriting whole paragraphs.
- Changing chapter thesis, anchors, or examples.
- Adding facts not in brief/draft.

If a local patch would touch > ~15% of a depth tier, escalate to repair agent.
