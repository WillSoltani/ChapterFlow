# Style memory — condensed

## Voice
Sharp human explainer with taste. Specific > generic. Concrete > abstract. Insight > padding. Authored, not assembled. "You" naturally, not sprayed. Teach the idea, not the chapter.

- Easy = accessible doorway (not compressed theory).
- Medium = mechanism + application.
- Hard = live tension / limit / threshold question. Not medium-with-adjectives.

## Hard constraints
- No em dashes.
- No invented facts, quotes, studies, mechanisms.
- No filler neuroscience (mirror neurons, oxytocin, cortisol, dopamine, vagal tone, evolutionary psychology, millisecond timing) unless brief explicitly supports.
- No copying raw source text into breakdowns.
- No brief/outline/seed/validator language in reader prose.
- Moral complexity → strategic awareness, never endorsement.
- Meta-references to "the chapter" near-zero in reader-facing content.

## Banned phrases (partial)
delve, crucial, landscape, realm, at its core, the art of, navigating, harnessing, robust, synergy, paradigm shift, game-changer, facilitate, utilize, foster, embark on, a testament to, shed light on, "It's worth noting", "In today's world", Furthermore, Moreover, In conclusion, "This matters because", "This is significant because", "It is essential to".

## Banned contamination phrases
"keep the prose narrow and concrete", "used lazily, the point turns into", "keep this question alive", "threshold question", "reading calibration", "motif watchlist", "sourceAnchorPriority", "internal concept budget".

## Banned sentence skeletons
"X is not Y. It is Z." — "The real issue is..." — "What matters is..." — "This changes everything." — "The difference is..."

## Auto-fail moves
- Opening with thesis instead of live scene/tension.
- Hiding weak thinking behind smooth phrasing.
- Abstraction as avoidance of concrete claim.
- Producing canonical drafts from structured depth blocks.
- Opening paragraph pasteable into another chapter.
- Hard depth = longer medium.
- Tone variants that collapse (adjective swap).
- Identical tone lines across three tones.

## Gold patterns to imitate (at craft level, not wording)
- Anchor chapter in specific named stories that carry the argument.
- Easy / medium / hard do different jobs (not length variations).
- Tones differ in function: gentle = lower resistance, direct = clean mechanism, competitive = sharper stakes.
- Socially realistic examples with names, rooms, times, objects, stakes.
- whatToDo = precise move, not "be strategic".
- whyItMatters = downstream consequence, not restatement of whatToDo.
- Quiz tests judgment in situations; distractors plausible; explanations teach.
- End paragraphs on consequence, not mush.

## Swap test
If you could change the chapter title and keep 70% of the prose, reject it.
