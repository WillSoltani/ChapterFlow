Core Claim:
Chapter 6 should explain interest as an early internal source of sustained return. The safest source-bound claim is that long-horizon persistence is easier to sustain when a person has real curiosity or attraction to the work, and harder to sustain when motivation is only borrowed from pressure or novelty. The chapter must stay conservative because the source bundle is thin on chapter-local detail.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This chapter shifts from whether grit can grow to what helps it start and keep renewing itself from the inside.

Author Logic Chain:
