A weak stretch can feel permanent while you are living inside it. A manager may decide someone simply lacks perseverance after watching one season of inconsistency. A student may interpret a rough semester as proof that persistence is a fixed trait they were never given. Chapter 5 pushes against that conclusion. Once Chapter 4 has made grit something observable, the next question is whether the pattern stays frozen or can change.

The title, "Grit Grows," makes a strong move, but the chapter has to use it carefully. Growth is not the same as instant transformation. The claim is narrower and more useful than that. If grit is a pattern of return, follow-through, and recovery, then it is at least possible to ask whether that pattern can strengthen over time. The chapter is not promising miracles. It is loosening fatalism.

That matters because fixed-trait thinking can become its own trap. If a person treats current inconsistency as destiny, they may stop experimenting with slower improvement. If a teacher, coach, or manager treats today's pattern as final, they may judge in ways that make change harder rather than easier. Chapter 5 interrupts that logic. It argues that persistence can be trained, deepened, and shaped, even if the process is slower and messier than slogans suggest.

The chapter stays more honest when it keeps difficulty in the frame. Growth is not linear. A person may improve in one domain while still falling short in another. They may show stronger recovery after setbacks without becoming perfectly steady. They may change through repetition so gradual that the difference is easier to see in retrospect than in the middle of the work. "Grit Grows" keeps the door open to development without pretending the door is effortless to walk through.

That caution is part of the chapter's value, not a weakness in it. Overpromised growth quickly turns dishonest. If the language of change ignores slowness, unevenness, context, or relapse, it becomes motivational theater. Chapter 5 is trying to preserve a more credible form of hope. The point is not that anyone can become anything at speed. The point is that persistence is not best understood as permanently sealed.

This also changes how judgment should sound. A person can be described as not yet steady rather than permanently incapable. A rough pattern can be treated as current evidence rather than final identity. That is a more demanding way to judge because it preserves accountability without collapsing into despair. It also leaves room for structure, practice, and recovery to matter.

Once the book has made that move, another question follows naturally. If grit can grow, what helps it start growing in the first place? What gives someone a reason to keep returning before discipline is fully mature? Chapter 5 does not answer that fully. It clears the ground for Chapter 6, where interest begins to look like one of the first ingredients that keeps persistence alive long enough to strengthen.
