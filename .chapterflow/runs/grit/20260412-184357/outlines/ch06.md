Show interest as a returning internal pull that helps persistence endure beyond novelty.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Durable interest is different from a first spike of novelty and can coexist with boredom while still drawing a person back.

Hard-Depth Tension:
People often misread interest because they expect constant excitement instead of a slower returning pull.

Threshold Question:
What keeps a person coming back before discipline is fully mature and after novelty starts fading?
