Show how grit can be examined as repeated evidence rather than admired as a vague personality halo.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Grit should be inferred from repeated return across time, not from a single impressive or embarrassing moment.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Measurement can clarify behavior, but it can also tempt people to oversimplify character into a quick score.
