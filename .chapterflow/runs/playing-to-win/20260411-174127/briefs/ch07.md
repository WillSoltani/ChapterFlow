Core Claim:
Strategy thinking is iterative. The chapter argues that teams should generate strategic possibilities and analyze them in a logic flow rather than waiting for perfect certainty before choosing. Its pressure point is that analysis detached from live possibilities becomes sterile, while possibility generation without analysis becomes wishful.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is not a generic brainstorming chapter. Its distinct mechanism is the strategy logic flow: possibilities and analysis informing one another through iterative strategic thinking. The chapter is specifically about how to think through choices rather than merely reinforce or administer them.

Author Logic Chain:
First the authors show that strategy work cannot wait for full certainty.
Then they explain why possibilities and analysis must interact rather than happen in isolation.
Then they frame the strategy logic flow as iterative rather than linear.
Then they warn against detached analysis and undisciplined ideation alike.

Counterarguments or Limits:
The chapter can be misread as if iteration means endless drift. Hard depth must preserve that iteration is valuable because it sharpens choices rather than postponing them indefinitely. The limit is that analysis without live possibilities and possibilities without disciplined testing are both weak.
