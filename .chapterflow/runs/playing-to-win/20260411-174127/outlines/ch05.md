By the end, the reader should understand that capabilities make the chosen edge real only when they fit the strategy and reinforce one another as a system.

Opening Move:

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Capabilities are not a generic list of strengths. They are the reinforcing things the organization must do well for its chosen field and edge.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter values strengths, but hard depth must preserve that existing strengths do not deserve loyalty when they do not support the chosen strategy.

Threshold Question:
When is a strength strategically valuable, and when is it only impressive but misaligned?
