Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand that strategy thinking works through an iterative logic flow between possibilities and analysis rather than certainty-first linearity.

Opening Move:
Open on a team demanding better analysis before it has generated real options, until the strategy process stalls.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 certainty trap and option generation
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 strategy logic flow and iterative process
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 analysis-without-options, ideation-without-tests, and the limit on drift

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: expose stalled strategy caused by certainty-first thinking
- P2: define possibilities and analysis as paired work
- P3: strategy logic flow as the main mechanism
- P4: iterative strategic thinking
- P5: two-sided warning against sterile analysis and loose ideation
- P6: limit, iteration must still narrow toward consequence
- P7 if needed: bridge into shortening odds

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Strategy thinking is iterative because possibilities and analysis shape one another. Neither works well in isolation.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter values iteration, but hard depth must preserve that iteration should sharpen choices rather than create endless exploration.

Threshold Question:
When is a strategy process genuinely thinking through possibilities, and when is it only circling?
