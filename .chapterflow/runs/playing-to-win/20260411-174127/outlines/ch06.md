Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand that management systems keep strategy alive only when reviews, measures, and routines reinforce the chosen game instead of performing discipline theatrically.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Management systems are not neutral administration. They are the recurring reviews, measures, and routines that either reinforce or erode the strategy.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter values systems, but hard depth must preserve that more review and more metrics can still become bureaucratic noise if they do not reinforce the chosen field, edge, and capabilities.
