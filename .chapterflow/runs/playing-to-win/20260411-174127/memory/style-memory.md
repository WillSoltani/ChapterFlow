# Style Memory

- Teach the idea directly, not the existence of the chapter.
- Open from pressure, conflict, or a live strategic decision, not a thesis sentence.
- Keep prose authored, concrete, and compressed. Every paragraph needs a distinct job.
- Easy should feel graspable and useful. Medium explains mechanism and application. Hard adds a real limit, contradiction, or threshold question.
- Competitive tone must stay credible and sharp, not sloganized.
- Avoid corporate filler, generic self-help language, planning-note leakage, and recycled sentence skeletons.
- If the source is thin, get narrower rather than sounding smarter than the evidence.
- Support surfaces must do distinct jobs: takeaways distill, moreDetails deepen, recap compresses/tests memory, review cards retrieve, implementation plans apply.
- Do not reuse the same landing beat across paragraphs, tones, cards, recap items, or prompts.
- Reader-facing content must be paraphrase-first unless the quote ledger explicitly clears a short quote.
