# Source Discovery

## Objective
Freeze a lawful, chapter-usable source bundle for `Playing to Win: How Strategy Really Works` before any chapter work begins.

## Discovery Result
- No public-domain full text exists.
- A strong authorized bundle exists through Harvard Business Review pages, Google Books metadata/contents, and O'Reilly preview pages for the introduction plus Chapters 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, and 8.
- Chapter-order stability is cross-checked by Google Books, O'Reilly contents, MIT Bookstore metadata, and the Case Centre chapter listings.
- Secondary chapter summaries are available for the thinly exposed parts of Chapters 3 and 5 and for cross-checking later chapter emphasis.

## Edition Decision
- Locked edition: `Playing to Win: How Strategy Really Works`, Harvard Business Review Press, English-original trade family, February 5 2013.
- Why it won: every high-confidence source points to the same title, authors, publisher family, 272-page length, and eight numbered chapters.
- Why no user question was needed: no competing edition or translation surfaced that would materially change chapter count, chapter order, or chapter-level meaning.

## Frozen Bundle Components
1. Official HBR book page and free-chapter landing page.
2. Google Books authorized metadata and visible contents lines.
3. O'Reilly authorized contents page and chapter preview pages.
4. MIT Bookstore metadata for ISBN and publication-date confirmation.
5. The Case Centre chapter catalog for chapter-level cross-checking.
6. Narrow secondary support from PDMA and HowToes where authorized preview coverage is thin.
7. Adjacent primary support from `Bringing Science to the Art of Strategy` for the Chapter 7-8 process posture.

## Sufficiency Assessment
- Strong enough for chapter order, chapter intent, named case anchors, and paraphrase-first exposition across the whole book.
- Strongest chapters by source richness: Chapters 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, and 8.
- Thinner chapters: Chapters 3 and 5 rely more heavily on chapter-title evidence plus secondary cross-checks and should stay tighter and more mechanism-focused.

## Guardrails
- Do not use pirate mirrors or unauthorized reposts.
- Exact quotes require direct support in the frozen authorized preview bundle.
- Where preview support is thin, prefer narrower claims and chapter-specific mechanism over decorative detail.
- Use secondary summaries only to cross-check chapter emphasis, not to widen the fact set beyond the frozen bundle.
