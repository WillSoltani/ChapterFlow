## Stable Book-Level Claims Supported By The Bundle
- The book frames strategy as an integrated set of choices rather than a vision statement or planning ritual.
- The core cascade is built around five questions:
  - winning aspiration
  - where to play
  - how to win
  - capabilities
  - management systems
- The book repeatedly uses P&G brand cases to show how those choices work in practice.
- The book moves from defining strategy, to the five choices, to how to think through strategic choice under uncertainty.
