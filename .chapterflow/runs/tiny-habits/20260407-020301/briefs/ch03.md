Core Claim:
When a behavior does not happen, the missing ingredient is often not desire but ability. Making the action tiny and simple increases the odds that it will actually occur.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It dignifies ease instead of treating ease like weakness.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author shows that hard behaviors fail for structural reasons.
Then the author reframes ease as smart design.
Then the author concludes that tiny starts beat heroic intentions.
