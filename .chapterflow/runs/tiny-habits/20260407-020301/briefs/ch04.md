Core Claim:
Even a well-matched, easy behavior will not happen reliably without the right prompt. Anchor prompts work because they tie the new behavior to something that already happens.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It makes sequence and timing the chapter's real mechanism rather than generic reminder talk.
