Core Claim:
Motivation matters, but it fluctuates too much to carry a habit system by itself. The practical move is matching: choose a behavior that fits the aspiration and the person you are right now.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It turns motivation from a heroic inner resource into an unreliable variable that should influence design choices.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author lowers trust in motivation.
Then the author reframes good design as matching behavior to aspiration and present reality.
Then the author concludes that fit beats force.

Required Anchors:
- unstable motivation
- matching aspiration to behavior
- compassionate redesign instead of self-blame

Allowed Quotes or Near-Quotes:

Specific Applications:
- choosing a smaller workout goal
- choosing a study habit that actually fits the day
- redesigning a morning routine instead of shaming yourself

Common Misreadings:

Counterarguments or Limits:
Matching can drift into over-accommodation if readers never raise the challenge when readiness grows.
