Chapter Title: Emotions Create Habits

Core Claim:
Positive emotion immediately after a behavior helps wire the habit faster than grim repetition alone. Celebration is therefore a design tool, not fluff.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It gives emotion a central causal role instead of treating reward as a vague afterthought.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author argues that feeling drives habit wiring.
Then the author reframes celebration as a deliberate skill.
Then the author concludes that authentic positive emotion accelerates habit formation.
