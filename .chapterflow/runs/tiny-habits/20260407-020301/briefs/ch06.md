Core Claim:
Tiny habits are seeds. Once they are rooted, they can grow naturally and multiply into wider change without abandoning the tiny-habit logic that made them stable.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It explains scaling without returning to force and overreach.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author shows why tiny should not be mistaken for trivial.
Then the author shows how rooted habits grow and multiply.
Then the author concludes that transformative change can emerge from small stable starts.
