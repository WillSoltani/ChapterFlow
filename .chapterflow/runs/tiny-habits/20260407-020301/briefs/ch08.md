Core Claim:
Behavior change is easier, safer, and more durable when it is supported by other people and by environments designed for shared success.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It expands Tiny Habits from solo self-change to family, team, and community design while preserving the same mechanism logic.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author shows that change is not purely private.
Then the author shows how people and environments reinforce behavior.
Then the author concludes that shared design can make change kinder and stickier.
