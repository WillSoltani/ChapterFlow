Chapter Promise:
Readers will understand B=MAP well enough to diagnose a behavior without turning first to blame or vague willpower talk.

Opening Move:
Open on a small failed habit attempt and show how the model changes the diagnosis.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 for B=MAP
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 for design-not-blame framing
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 for practical diagnosis

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: a behavior fails and the reader reaches for blame
- P2: introduce B=MAP cleanly
- P3: show how motivation can be present but insufficient
- P4: show how ability can be the bottleneck
- P5: show how prompts matter
- P6: conclude with design-first diagnosis and bridge to motivation

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter distinguishes explanation by interacting parts from explanation by character.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The model can sound simple but still be used lazily if the reader names the wrong bottleneck.

Threshold Question:
