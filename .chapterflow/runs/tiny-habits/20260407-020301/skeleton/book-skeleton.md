# Book Skeleton

## Metadata
- Book: Tiny Habits
- Author: BJ Fogg
- Locked edition: 2019 English trade family
- Wave size: 2 by default
- Premium / solo routing candidates: ch05, ch07, ch08

## Rotation Plan For Examples
- Use the canonical six-format rotation once per chapter: decision_point, postmortem, dialogue, predict_reveal, dilemma, before_after.
- Keep the 2 work / 2 school / 2 personal split in each chapter package.
- Rotate ending types across broader_principle, self_directed_question, surprising_implication, cross_domain, common_trap, perspective_reframe.

## School-Setting Plan
- Use classrooms, study desks, group projects, clubs, practice sessions, and dorm routines.
- Avoid turning every school example into generic procrastination talk.

## Vocabulary Watchlist
- Keep B=MAP, anchor, prompt, Shine, celebration, ability factors, and matching precise.
- Avoid generic willpower sermons, fake neuroscience, or borrowed Atomic Habits phrasing.

## Motif / Callback Opportunities
- ch01 B=MAP becomes the analytical lens for the next three chapters.
- ch03 simplicity and ability feed directly into ch04 anchors and ch05 celebration.
- ch06 growth logic should echo earlier seed language without sounding mystical.
- ch07 untangling should preserve the book's compassionate, design-first stance.
- ch08 should widen from self-change to households, teams, and social spread without losing specificity.

## Chapter Plan
### ch01 The Elements of Behavior
- Intent: Establish B=MAP so readers stop blaming character and start diagnosing behavior.
- Source richness: high
- Concept density: medium
- Moral complexity: low
- Thin-chapter risk notes: Avoid turning the model into a sterile formula.
- Wave routing: wave 1

### ch02 Motivation-Focus on Matching
- Intent: Reframe motivation as unreliable and teach readers to match aspirations to behaviors they both want and can do.
- Source richness: medium-high
- Concept density: medium
- Moral complexity: low
- Thin-chapter risk notes: Keep matching concrete instead of vague anti-willpower rhetoric.
- Wave routing: wave 1

### ch03 Ability-Easy Does It
- Intent: Show that simplicity and tiny starts outperform heroic effort because ability is often the real bottleneck.
- Source richness: high
- Concept density: medium-high
- Moral complexity: low
- Thin-chapter risk notes: Avoid reducing the chapter to one generic "start small" slogan.
- Wave routing: wave 2

### ch04 Prompts-The Power of After
- Intent: Make prompts and anchor moments the reliable ignition system for new habits.
- Source richness: medium-high
- Concept density: medium
- Moral complexity: low
- Thin-chapter risk notes: Keep the chapter about timing and fit, not reminders in general.
- Wave routing: wave 2

### ch05 Emotions Create Habits
- Intent: Explain why celebration and positive emotion wire habits faster than repetition plus self-criticism.
- Source richness: high
- Concept density: medium
- Moral complexity: medium
- Thin-chapter risk notes: Needs distance from cheesy overstatement while preserving the method claim.
- Wave routing: solo/premium

### ch06 Growing Your Habits from Tiny to Transformative
- Intent: Show how tiny habits grow, multiply, and create ripple effects once they are rooted.
- Source richness: medium
- Concept density: medium
- Moral complexity: low
- Thin-chapter risk notes: Avoid garden-metaphor drift and keep the scaling mechanics visible.
- Wave routing: wave 3

### ch07 Untangling Bad Habits: A Systematic Solution
- Intent: Reframe bad-habit change as systematic untangling, not one dramatic act of force.
- Source richness: medium
- Concept density: medium
- Moral complexity: medium
- Thin-chapter risk notes: Needs nuance about grief, stress, and the limit between downhill and freefall habits.
- Wave routing: solo/premium

### ch08 How We Change Together
- Intent: Expand behavior design from the individual to households, teams, and supportive social leadership.
- Source richness: medium
- Concept density: high
- Moral complexity: medium
- Thin-chapter risk notes: Avoid generic teamwork advice; keep the Tiny Habits mechanisms visible.
- Wave routing: solo/premium
