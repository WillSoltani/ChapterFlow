# Tiny Habits Frozen Book Source

This file is a rights-safe source bundle summary for the run. It is not a full-text reproduction.

## Core Official Support

### Tiny Habits Challenge
- BJ Fogg presents Tiny Habits as a practical method for making change easy and sustainable.
- The official framing emphasizes starting small, positive emotion, and method over willpower.

### Fogg Behavior Model
- Behavior happens when motivation, ability, and a prompt converge at the same moment.
- This is the core mechanism behind the chapter sequence for Chapters 1 through 4.

### Extra Credit Learning
- Habit design depends on making the new behavior simple and attaching it to a reliable anchor moment.
- Pre-loading simplicity and reducing friction are part of the method, not an optional convenience trick.

### Emotions Create Habits
- Positive emotion immediately after the behavior helps the brain tag the behavior as worth repeating.
- Celebration is presented as a deliberate skill, not as cheesy decoration.

## Secondary Chapter Support

- Chapter 2 support: motivation is unreliable; choose behaviors that match both aspiration and reality.
- Chapter 3 support: ability factors include time, money, physical effort, mental effort, and routine fit; make the behavior tiny.
- Chapter 4 support: no behavior happens without a prompt; anchors are superior to vague reminders when they fit naturally.
- Chapter 5 support: emotion is the wiring signal; celebration should feel authentic.
- Chapter 6 support: habits can grow or multiply; treat them like seeds and gardens.
- Chapter 7 support: untangle unwanted habits systematically; separate downhill habits from severe freefall problems.
- Chapter 8 support: change spreads through households, teams, coaches, and social design, not just isolated individuals.
