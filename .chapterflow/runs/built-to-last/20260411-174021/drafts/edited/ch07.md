Waiting for certainty can make an organization look careful while quietly making it slow and blind. Teams keep asking for more proof, more forecasting, and one clean answer before they commit. Chapter 7 pushes in another direction. Durable institutions often move forward by running many trials and then keeping the approaches that actually work.

The title sounds loose, but the logic is not loose at all. Trying a lot of stuff is not praise for chaos. The chapter is describing disciplined experimentation. A company creates multiple paths, watches what happens, and keeps the methods that strengthen the institution. That matters because prediction has limits. The right move is often discovered through contact with reality rather than through advance confidence alone.

The bridge from Chapter 6 is important. Strong culture gives an institution coherence. Chapter 7 asks how that coherence can survive active exploration. The answer is that the core remains stable while the methods remain testable. The company does not experiment with its purpose every week. It experiments with ways of advancing that purpose under uncertain conditions.

That distinction keeps the chapter from collapsing into generic innovation talk. The value of many trials is not novelty by itself. The value is learning. A serious institution can compare results across experiments, notice which paths create real progress, and retain those paths with more confidence over time.

The hazard sits close by. Leaders can turn experimentation into a cover for disorder, endless piloting, or motion without memory. When that happens, the organization is no longer learning from variation. It is scattering effort and calling the scatter adaptive. The chapter holds the line by keeping trial and retention tied together.

So Chapter 7 is about disciplined institutional learning under uncertainty. Durable companies do not need perfect prediction before they act, but they do need enough judgment to notice what deserves to stay. A stable core plus remembered experimentation lets progress accumulate without drifting into chaos. The next chapter asks who inside the institution can carry that continuity and judgment over time.
