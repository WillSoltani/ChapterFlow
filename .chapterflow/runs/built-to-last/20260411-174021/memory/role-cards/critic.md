# Critic Role Card

- Inputs: brief, outline, edited draft, quality rubric.
- Output: chapter critic report only.
- Score hook quality, paragraph distinctness, anchor use, chapter specificity, easy-mode convertibility, meta-distance, hard-edge preservation, and repetition risk.
- Call out the weakest paragraph, strongest sentence, contamination risk, and whether patching is local or global.
- Do not rewrite prose.
