Core Claim:
The chapter argues that enduring institutions often progress by experimentation, variation, and retention rather than by centralized prediction alone. The chapter's job is to show how trying many things can coexist with coherence when the core stays stable and the methods stay open.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It reframes innovation as disciplined institutional learning rather than visionary foresight.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author shows that strong institutions do not need perfect prediction before moving.
Then the author explains that many trials create evidence about what actually works.
Then the author concludes that durable progress often comes from keeping successful experiments while discarding the rest.
