Core Claim:
The chapter argues that visionary companies are not held together by profit alone. They operate from a core ideology made of values and purpose that remains meaningful beyond any one leader's preferences. Profit still matters, but it is treated as a condition for continuity rather than the deepest reason the institution exists. The chapter's job is to separate enduring purpose from empty purpose language.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It explains what gives a clock-building company a stable direction worth preserving. Without this chapter, institutional durability risks becoming hollow process.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author rejects the idea that profit alone can provide an enduring center.
Then the author shows that core values and purpose hold the organization together beyond individual leaders.
Then the author concludes that ideology gives continuity its direction.
