Core Claim:
The chapter argues that durable institutions develop leaders from within so that authority carries the organization's core values, memory, and operating logic over time. The chapter's job is to show why internal leadership development protects continuity better than dependence on outside saviors.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It turns succession and leadership development into institutional design rather than a talent-market question.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author shows that enduring companies are built to outlast individual stars.
Then the author explains why internal leadership pipelines preserve continuity and institutional knowledge.
Then the author concludes that home-grown management helps the company renew itself without forgetting what it is.
