Core Claim:
The chapter argues that bold institutional goals channel progress by giving the company a target large enough to organize energy, risk, and coordination. A BHAG is not random hype. It is ambitious enough to stretch the institution while still serving the preserved core. The chapter's job is to distinguish directed audacity from empty bravado.
