Core Claim:
The chapter argues that enduring institutions embed relentless standards into the organization so that success does not decay into adequacy. The chapter's job is to show that high standards are institutional discipline, not private perfectionism or punishment theater.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It turns quality pressure into a system property rather than a personality trait.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author shows that continuity and success can drift into complacency.
Then the author explains why durable companies maintain standards that resist settling.
Then the author concludes that institutional excellence requires pressure against adequacy, not merely moments of inspiration.
