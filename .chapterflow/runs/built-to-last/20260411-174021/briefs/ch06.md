Core Claim:
The chapter argues that strong institutions turn core values into lived membership standards rather than vague sentiments. A powerful culture attracts people who fit the core and pushes out people who do not. The chapter's job is to explain why strong belonging can create coherence without drifting into empty culture-speak or theatrical harshness.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It shows how core ideology becomes social reality inside the institution. Without it, values remain abstract.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author shows that culture becomes strong when values shape belonging.
Then the author explains that real alignment includes selection and exclusion pressure.
Then the author concludes that durable institutions create cultures that are intense for the right people and uncomfortable for the wrong ones.
