Core Claim:
The chapter argues that enduring companies do not choose between stability and change. They preserve a stable core of values and purpose while still creating movement, experimentation, and progress around that core. The power of the chapter lies in refusing the false choice between rigidity and drift. It turns continuity into a platform for change rather than an excuse to avoid change.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It resolves the tension that could otherwise break the whole framework: how a company can stay itself without becoming stale.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author frames core and progress as a false either-or.
Then the author shows that enduring companies hold values stable while changing methods aggressively.
Then the author concludes that real durability requires both preservation and movement.
