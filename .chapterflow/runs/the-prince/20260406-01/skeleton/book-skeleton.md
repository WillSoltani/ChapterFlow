## Rotation Plan For Examples

- Default per chapter: 6 examples, one each of `decision_point`, `postmortem`, `dialogue`, `predict_reveal`, `dilemma`, and `before_after`.
- Default category split per chapter: 2 work, 2 school, 2 personal.
- Vary which category carries the chapter's hardest lesson so school does not become the default "soft" lane and work does not become the default "strategic" lane.
- Reserve dialogue examples for moments where status, correction, persuasion, or misreading can be dramatized in live speech.
- Use before/after only when the chapter's mechanism is best understood through reclassification or changed framing.
- Avoid within-chapter name reuse unless a deliberate callback is logged.

## School-Setting Plan

- Rotate across seminar discussion room, student government office, campus newspaper, lab bench, debate prep room, robotics workshop, group-project study room, office hours hallway, athletic film room, dorm common area, and career fair line.
- Keep each school setting tied to the chapter's specific pressure, not just age variety.
- Reuse settings across the book only after enough spacing that they feel deliberate rather than default.
