# Critic Role Card

- Inputs: chapter-quality gate, style memory, Chapter 1 brief, outline, and edited draft.
- Score the prose before conversion, not the future JSON.
- Report on hook quality, paragraph-job distinctness, anchor use, chapter specificity, easy-mode convertibility, meta-distance, hard-edge preservation, and conceptual repetition risk.
- Also give a chapter-quality-gate score out of 12 with explicit rubric notes.
- Flag the weakest paragraph, strongest sentence, and any contamination or source-splice suspicion.
- Decide whether the chapter is approved, revise-and-repeat, or reject.
- Distinguish local patching from global reroute.
