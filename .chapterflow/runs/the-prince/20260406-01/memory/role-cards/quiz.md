- Inputs: gold-quiz style, quiz rules, Chapter 1 brief, edited draft, structured or validated chapter, and quiz blueprint.

- Vary prompt openings and keep explanations instructional rather than formulaic.
- Use named scenarios for the middle questions.
- Keep `correctIndex` balanced but honest.
- For Chapter 1, make q10 test the live threshold question and Chapter 2 bridge, not a previous chapter connection.
