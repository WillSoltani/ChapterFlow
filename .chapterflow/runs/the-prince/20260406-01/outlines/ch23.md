Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand why Chapter 23 rejects both total openness and total secrecy, and how a wise prince creates a disciplined truth channel that still leaves judgment in his own hands.

Opening Move:
Open on the trap itself: the prince wants truth to escape flatterers, but truth from everyone weakens respect.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1 for flatterers, courts full of them, and truth from everyone making respect abate
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P2 for the third course and selected truth-tellers
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P3 for questioning on everything, listening, concluding, rewarding freer speech, and listening to no one outside the gate
- anchor 4 used in paragraph(s): P4 for contempt through varying opinions
- anchor 5 used in paragraph(s): P5 for Fra Luca and Maximilian's secretive-pliant failure
- anchor 6 used in paragraph(s): P6 for counsel only when the prince wishes, constant inquiry, patient listening, anger at withheld truth, and the final reversal about wisdom and counsel

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: define the flattery trap and why easy defenses fail
- P2: explain the third course of selected truth-speaking
- P3: show how wide inquiry and narrow speaking liberty work together
- P4: explain why unmanaged opinions produce contempt
- P5: use Maximilian to show the opposite failure of secrecy and pliancy
- P6: preserve the final axiom that good counsels are born of the prince's wisdom

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The key distinction is between invited truth and uncontrolled speech. The prince should ask widely and listen carefully, but only inside a channel he governs.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must preserve that counsel can sharpen wisdom only when the prince already has enough discrimination to choose, question, and control counsellors. Otherwise advice becomes either noise, flattery, or surrender to one stronger person.

Threshold Question:
If truth is necessary but truth from everyone destroys respect, what exact speaking regime does Machiavelli prescribe?

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- Takeaway 1: flatterers are unavoidable without disciplined discrimination
- Takeaway 2: the third course is selected truth on invited questions
- Takeaway 3: openness to every opinion and secrecy from every opinion both end in contempt
- Takeaway 4: Maximilian shows how hidden plans and pliancy undermine resolutions
