- P2: explain the river metaphor and why barriers matter
- P3: shift from fortune in general to the prince whose stable character stops fitting the times
- P4: explain why adaptation is rare even when the ruler can see the change
- P5: use Julius II as a case of method fitting moment

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The key distinction is between two forms of resistance: structures built before the flood and strategic adjustment after the times have shifted. The chapter needs both.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must preserve that fortune is not denied and free will is not romanticized. The limit lies partly outside the ruler and partly inside him, because nature and habit make course changes rare.

Threshold Question:
When fortune changes, is the ruler ruined more by the flood itself or by his inability to alter the method that once made him prosper?
