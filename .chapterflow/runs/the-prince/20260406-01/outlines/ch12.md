Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The load-bearing mechanism is not simply that hired soldiers are cowardly. It is that both the rank-and-file and the captain are aligned elsewhere. The soldiers are tied by pay, and the captain is tied to his own advancement.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must keep the chapter from becoming a single complaint. The reader has to see why mercenary dependence corrupts incentives, command, military practice, and eventually the freedom of the state itself.

Threshold Question:
If mercenaries appear competent and successful, what is the real danger the prince should read first: enemy pressure, captain ambition, or the absence of shared stake?
