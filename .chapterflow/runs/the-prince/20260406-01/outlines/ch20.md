Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The key mechanism is conversion. A good expedient turns risky men, arms, or institutions into something belonging to the prince. A bad expedient advertises distrust, dependence, or false security.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must keep the chapter from flattening into either anti-device purity or pro-device cleverness. Devices sometimes help, but they help only when fitted to the state's condition and kept below the question of popular hatred.

Threshold Question:
Why does the chapter end by subordinating the strongest visible defense, the fortress, to the least visible condition, not being hated by the people?
