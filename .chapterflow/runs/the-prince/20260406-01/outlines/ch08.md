Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The load-bearing distinction is not simply cruelty versus kindness. It is secure control versus glory on one axis, and concentrated necessary harms versus multiplying repeated harms on the other. If those two lines blur, the chapter collapses into bad edginess or bad sentimentality.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must keep both halves of the argument together: some wicked rulers do hold, and understanding how they hold does not redeem them. The chapter is strongest when it refuses both naivete and admiration.

Threshold Question:
When a regime begins in a shocking seizure, what makes it politically less unstable afterward: stopping the harms at once and then stabilizing subjects, or continuing to injure people in smaller waves?
