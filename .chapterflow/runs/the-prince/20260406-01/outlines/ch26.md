Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The key distinction is between emotional readiness and durable founding capacity. Willingness, anger, and courage are not enough without head, order, own forces, and new institutions.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must preserve that the chapter's hot rhetoric does not cancel its mechanism. The exhortation is grounded in leadership, military order, and founding, not in feeling alone.

Threshold Question:
When people are ready to follow a banner but no one has yet supplied a head, what must the founder build first so the moment becomes durable rather than theatrical?
