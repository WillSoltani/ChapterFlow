Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand why Machiavelli treats war study as the ruler's proper art, why peace is the main time for preparation, and how action, terrain knowledge, rehearsal, and history fit together into readiness.

Opening Move:
Open with the abrupt severity of the claim: after condemning borrowed force, Machiavelli now says the prince's proper study is war itself, and neglect of it is one of the first causes of loss.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1 for sole-art claim, ease versus arms, and Sforza
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P2 for armed versus unarmed, contempt, and soldier respect
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P3 for drill, hunting, terrain, and practical uses of locality
- anchor 4 used in paragraph(s): P4 for Philopoemen and constant scenario rehearsal
- anchor 5 used in paragraph(s): P5 for histories, imitation, and exemplary figures
- anchor 6 used in paragraph(s): P6 for peace-time industry, resources, fortune, and the bridge into Chapter 15

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: state the sole-art claim and show the cost of neglect through Sforza and his sons
- P2: explain why unarmed rule breeds contempt and makes trust with armed servants unstable
- P3: detail the action side of preparation through drill, hardship, hunting, and terrain study
- P4: use Philopoemen to show mental rehearsal as a peace-time habit
- P5: explain the study side through histories, victories, defeats, and imitation
- P6: close on prepared resistance to fortune and bridge from military study to conduct under reality in Chapter 15

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The key distinction is between treating war as an occasional emergency and treating it as the ruler's permanent discipline. Peace does not suspend the duty. It concentrates it.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must keep both halves of the chapter alive: the bodily and local side of readiness, and the intellectual and historical side. It must also preserve the claim that competence in war shapes respect, not just success in battle.

Threshold Question:
If no enemy is currently present, why is peace the right time for harder military study rather than for comfort?
