Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The key distinction is not honesty versus dishonesty in itself. It is between rigid observance that destroys rule and conditional fidelity under changing grounds, plus the political difference between truly having virtues and successfully appearing to have them.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must preserve the relation among three things at once: law and force, fox and lion, and appearing virtuous without always being bound to every virtue in action.

Threshold Question:
Why can praiseworthy faith become politically unusable without making craft the same thing as mere fraud?
