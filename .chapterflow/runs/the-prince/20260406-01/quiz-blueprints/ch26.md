# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter:
- ch26 - An Exhortation to Liberate Italy from the Barbarians

Core concepts to test:
- the chapter as exhortation and final synthesis
- the present time as especially fit for a new prince
- Moses, Cyrus, and Theseus as founder analogies
- Italy's extremity and headlessness
- the rejected spark and the waiting country
- the people's willingness to follow a banner
- the justice-and-necessity claim about arms
- free will remaining even under providential rhetoric
- valour in the limbs and failure in the head
- own forces and a new order as the real foundation of liberation

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: evaluate what a founder must add when suffering, willingness, and courage already exist but durable redemption still does not happen

Question plan:
- q01: recall of what kind of chapter this is at the end of the book
- q02: recall of why the present time is described as especially fit
- q03: comprehension of the Moses, Cyrus, Theseus analogies
- q04: comprehension of Italy's extremity list and what it is meant to reveal
- q05: scenario on public willingness without organizational head
- q06: recall/comprehension of the justice-and-necessity claim about arms
- q07: comprehension of why providence does not cancel free will
- q08: analysis of "valour in the limbs whilst it fails in the head"
- q09: analysis of own forces versus borrowed strength
- q10: threshold question on what must be supplied first when willingness and raw courage exist without effective leadership

Distractor notes:
- tempting wrong answer type 1: turning the chapter into pure patriotic feeling
- tempting wrong answer type 2: turning providence into an excuse for inaction
- tempting wrong answer type 3: treating courage as sufficient without command
- tempting wrong answer type 4: treating foreign or borrowed strength as interchangeable with own forces

Explanation diversity notes:
- vary language around opportunity, extremity, founder, head, order, own forces, willingness, and new laws
- keep explanations tied to Chapter 26's merger of exhortation and mechanism
- let hard distractors fail because they erase either free will, leadership, or the own-forces requirement

CorrectIndex plan:
- keep distribution at 4 answers for `0`, 3 answers for `1`, and 3 answers for `2`
