# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter:
- ch14 - That Which Concerns a Prince on the Subject of the Art of War

Core concepts to test:
- war as the ruler's proper study
- neglect of arms as a cause of losing states
- Francesco Sforza and his sons
- armed versus unarmed respect
- peace-time drill and organization
- hunting and bodily hardening
- terrain knowledge and its transferability
- Philopoemen's continual scenario reasoning
- histories, victories, defeats, and imitation
- preparation for fortune in peace

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: evaluate why peace-time readiness matters more than comfort for a ruler who wants to hold power

- q04: work decision-point scenario about using a quiet quarter for readiness rather than ease
- q05: school postmortem scenario about not learning the ground before pressure

- q07: analysis question on why terrain knowledge matters beyond one place
- q08: analysis question on Philopoemen's habit
- q09: analysis question on histories and imitation
- q10: hard-threshold question on why peace is the main time for war study

Distractor notes:
- tempting wrong answer type 1: treating the chapter as a praise of fighting for its own sake
- tempting wrong answer type 2: confusing activity with disciplined preparation
- tempting wrong answer type 3: reducing historical study to culture rather than usable judgment

Explanation diversity notes:
- vary language around readiness, respect, terrain, rehearsal, and fortune
- keep examples tied to preparation before adversity instead of generic planning
- let hard distractors fail because they mistake comfort for security

CorrectIndex plan:
