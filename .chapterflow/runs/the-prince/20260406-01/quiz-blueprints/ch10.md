# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter:
- ch10 - Concerning the Way in Which the Strength of All Principalities Ought to Be Measured

Core concepts to test:
- the self-support versus assistance distinction
- field-capable strength versus sheltering behind walls
- provision and fortify the town, not the whole country
- the anti-odium condition of not being hated by the people
- the German cities as the concrete model of fortified preparedness
- the role of stores, work, exercises, and ordinances
- the objection about burnt outside property
- hope, fear of the enemy, and handling the bold
- the paradox that people are bound by benefits they confer as much as by those they receive
- the final requirement that the prince support and defend his citizens

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: evaluate what a prince should secure first when he cannot meet the enemy in the field

Question plan:
- q01: recall of the core distinction between self-support and need of others
- q02: recall of the difference between field battle and sheltering behind walls
- q03: comprehension of why fortifying the town and not defending the whole country is prudent in the second case
- q04: work decision-point scenario about concentrating defence on a defensible core
- q05: school postmortem scenario about supplies, work, and visible difficulty in a defended space
- q06: personal dialogue scenario about not trying to defend every exposed outlying concern
- q07: analysis question on why not being hated by the people matters to fortification
- q08: analysis question on the German cities example
- q09: analysis question on the objection about burnt outside property and the prince's response
- q10: hard-threshold question on what secures a wall-backed prince first

Distractor notes:
- tempting wrong answer type 1: treating walls as sufficient without the people
- tempting wrong answer type 2: assuming defending more territory always signals more strength
- tempting wrong answer type 3: reducing the chapter to morale alone and ignoring stores and visible difficulty

Explanation diversity notes:
- keep explanations tied to town, country, stores, odium, and steadfastness
- vary the way the final paradox is explained so it does not sound like generic gratitude
- let hard distractors fail because they misread the order of priorities in a siege situation

CorrectIndex plan:
- keep distribution roughly balanced across 0 / 1 / 2
