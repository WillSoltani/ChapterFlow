## Goal

Freeze a lawful, high-signal source bundle for *The 48 Laws of Power* before any chapter drafting.

## Discovery Notes

- The book is not public domain, so the frozen bundle uses official Penguin metadata, authorized edition-preview metadata, a stable public chapter map, and reputable grouped secondary summaries instead of any scraped or pirated full-text copy.
- The English-original 48-law sequence is stable across the original 1998 publication, the dominant Penguin paperback family, and the accessible metadata trail.
- The cleanest workable chapter map came from the stable law-title list, while SuperSummary provides grouped law summaries that can be narrowed into chapter briefs without pretending to be the full text.
