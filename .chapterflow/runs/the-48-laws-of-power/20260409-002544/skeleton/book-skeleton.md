## Rotation Plan For Examples

- Rotate all six canonical formats exactly once per chapter.
- Keep the 2 work / 2 school / 2 personal distribution locked in every chapter.
- Avoid repeating the same lesson across all six scenarios; each chapter should teach six distinct edges of the law.
- Use names with no within-book reuse by default, and reserve intentional callbacks only for late-book synthesis if continuity state stays clean.

## School-Setting Plan

- Cycle through seminar room, lab team, student government, debate club, athletics, dorm life, performing arts, internship-linked coursework, campus media, and scholarship or recommendation dynamics.
- Use school examples to test authority, peer competition, reputation, timing, and conformity without turning every scenario into classroom correction scenes.
- Keep at least one school scenario per wave tied to institutional gatekeepers and one tied to peer dynamics.

## Vocabulary Watchlist

- Avoid repetitive overuse of power, leverage, status, control, manipulation, dominance, advantage, and strategy.
- Prefer chapter-specific verbs such as eclipse, disarm, bait, scatter, mirror, pace, withdraw, frame, and compress only when the chapter truly warrants them.
- Watch for contamination phrases and AI-softeners banned by the style constraints.
