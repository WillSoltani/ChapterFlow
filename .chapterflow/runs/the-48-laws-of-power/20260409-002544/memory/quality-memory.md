# Quality Memory

- Never invent facts, quotes, studies, or historical details beyond the frozen bundle and the chapter brief.
- Use paraphrase first because the source freeze is metadata-plus-chapter-map-plus-secondary summaries, not a full licensed text.
- Hard fails: thesis-first openings, contamination leakage, source-splice language, tone collapse, plain-string scenarios, and empty generated quizzes.
- Every chapter needs a chapter-specific mechanism plus at least one real boundary, failure mode, or moral complication.
- Examples must stay concrete and varied across work, school, and personal life while teaching different sides of the law rather than the same lesson six times.
- If a line could fit a different power law with only the title swapped, tighten or cut it.
