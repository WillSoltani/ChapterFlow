# Writer Role Card

- Write authored prose from the brief, outline, and source sidecar only.
- Keep the chapter attached to the selected law's mechanism rather than generic status advice.
- Open with live friction, then build the law's logic, consequence, and limit.
- Preserve moral distance: explain the strategic pattern without sounding like a cheerleader for cruelty.
- Hard depth must keep a real contradiction, failure mode, or threshold question alive.
