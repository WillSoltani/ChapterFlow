# Style Memory

- Write like a sharp explainer of status, power, and strategic behavior, not like a hype-man for manipulation or a generic self-help summarizer.
- Open on a pictureable tension, named figure, or concrete social risk rather than a thesis announcement.
- Keep each law unmistakably itself: the prose should lock to the law's exact mechanism, not drift into general ambition advice.
- Easy should clarify the law cleanly, medium should explain its mechanism and applications, and hard should preserve a real limit, contradiction, or threshold question.
- Tone differences must change function and stakes, not merely intensify the same sentence.
- Avoid corporate mush, fake psychology, invented historical texture, and any authority signal the frozen bundle cannot support.
