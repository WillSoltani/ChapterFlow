# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter: Play a Sucker to Catch a Sucker - Seem Dumber Than Your Mark
Core concepts to test:
- visible cleverness cost
- strategic underestimation
- lowered guard
- overconfidence and disclosure
- camouflage versus incompetence
- Chapter 21 to Chapter 22 bridge

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: evaluate

Question plan:
- q01: define why obvious cleverness can provoke defense here
- q02: identify what underestimation preserves or creates
- q03: identify why the chapter is not advice for actual incompetence
- q04: named work scenario about letting a rival reveal more by seeming less threatening
- q05: school scenario on visible brilliance making a team easier to guard against
- q06: personal scenario on pacing competence without surrendering credibility
- q07: explain how lowered guard changes what other people reveal
- q08: judge when camouflage starts becoming self-sabotage
- q09: synthesize how Chapter 20 noncommitment leads into Chapter 21 perceptual camouflage
- q10: connect underestimation to Chapter 22's transformation of weakness into power

Distractor notes:
- confuse camouflage with incompetence
- confuse underestimation with humiliation
- confuse visible intelligence with guaranteed advantage

Explanation diversity notes:
- avoid repeated openers
- each explanation should teach one piece of lowered-guard, disclosure, or credibility-limit logic

CorrectIndex plan:
- keep distribution roughly balanced across 0 / 1 / 2
