# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter: Learn to Keep People Dependent on You
Core concepts to test:
- necessity versus goodwill
- substitution risk
- irreplaceable function
- dependence and leverage
- backlash / workaround limit
- Chapter 11 to Chapter 12 bridge

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: evaluate

Question plan:
- q01: define why necessity can create stronger leverage than appreciation
- q02: identify what substitution risk means in this chapter
- q03: identify how dependence is built without reducing the chapter to sabotage
- q04: named work scenario about becoming necessary through real function
- q05: school scenario on why one student becomes hard to replace in a shared system
- q06: personal scenario on usefulness versus unhealthy dependency
- q07: explain why visible gatekeeping can fail
- q08: judge when dependence turns brittle and motivates replacement
- q09: synthesize how Chapter 10 field protection gives way to Chapter 11 necessity
- q10: connect dependence to the next chapter's selective honesty and generosity problem

Distractor notes:
- confuse being liked with being necessary
- confuse necessity with sabotage in every case
- confuse leverage with absolute control

Explanation diversity notes:
- avoid repeated openers
- each explanation should teach one piece of necessity, substitution, leverage, or backlash logic

CorrectIndex plan:
- keep distribution roughly balanced across 0 / 1 / 2
