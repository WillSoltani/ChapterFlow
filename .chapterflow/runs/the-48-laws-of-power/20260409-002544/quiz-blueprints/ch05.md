# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter: So Much Depends on Reputation - Guard It with Your Life
Core concepts to test:
- reputation as preloaded influence
- appearances, actions, and hearsay as the basis of social judgment
- one-trait anchoring
- repair by credible association
- reputational attack and blowback
- Chapter 5 to Chapter 6 bridge

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: evaluate

Question plan:
- q01: define why reputation matters before direct contact
- q02: identify why people rely on appearances and circulating stories
- q03: identify the advantage of one outstanding quality
- q04: named work scenario about stabilizing credibility after a visible mistake
- q05: school scenario about preventing one correction from becoming a total image collapse
- q06: personal scenario on repairing reputation through consistent conduct rather than frantic denial
- q07: explain why association with trusted people can help image repair
- q08: judge when attacking another person's reputation is likely to backfire
- q09: synthesize how Chapter 4's speech control feeds Chapter 5's public image logic
- q10: connect reputation to the next chapter's problem of visibility and attention

Distractor notes:
- confuse reputation with raw popularity
- confuse one clear trait with pretending to be perfect
- confuse disciplined defense with reckless rumor

Explanation diversity notes:
- avoid repeated openers
- each explanation should teach one piece of anticipation, shielding, repair, or blowback logic

CorrectIndex plan:
- keep distribution roughly balanced across 0 / 1 / 2
