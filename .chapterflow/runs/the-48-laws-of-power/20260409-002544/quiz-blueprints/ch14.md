# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter: Pose as a Friend, Work as a Spy
Core concepts to test:
- friendly access versus confrontation
- lowered vigilance
- conversational reveal
- information advantage
- visible-extraction / exposure limit
- Chapter 14 to Chapter 15 bridge

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: evaluate

Question plan:
- q01: define why friendly access can reveal more than open hostility
- q02: identify what lowered vigilance means in this chapter
- q03: identify why the law is not generic charm advice
- q04: named work scenario about learning more through ordinary rapport than direct pressure
- q05: school scenario on why club-fair or mentoring chatter reveals more than formal probing
- q06: personal scenario on curiosity versus extractive observation
- q07: explain why obvious snooping can fail
- q08: judge when friendliness becomes so overplayed that the tactic is exposed
- q09: synthesize how Chapter 13's cooperation leads into Chapter 14's information advantage
- q10: connect gathered information to Chapter 15's total closure against opposition

Distractor notes:
- confuse friendship with proof of sincerity
- confuse observation with interrogation
- confuse information gathering with constant lying

Explanation diversity notes:
- avoid repeated openers
- each explanation should teach one piece of access, vigilance, observation, or exposure logic

CorrectIndex plan:
- keep distribution roughly balanced across 0 / 1 / 2
