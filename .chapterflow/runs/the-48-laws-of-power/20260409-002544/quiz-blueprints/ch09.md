# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter: Win Through Your Actions, Never Through Argument
Core concepts to test:
- argument as resistance trigger
- verbal victory versus durable change
- action as embodied proof
- relevant and legible demonstration
- performative-action and ambiguity limit
- Chapter 9 to Chapter 10 bridge

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: evaluate

Question plan:
- q01: define why argument can fail even when the logic is sound
- q02: identify what verbal victory often produces in the other person
- q03: identify what makes action persuasive in this chapter
- q04: named work scenario about replacing debate with visible process proof
- q05: school scenario on letting a prepared example settle a seminar claim
- q06: personal scenario on proving reliability through follow-through instead of repeated defense
- q07: explain why relevant proof can outlast verbal sparring
- q08: judge when action becomes theatrical or too ambiguous to settle the issue
- q09: synthesize how Chapter 8 field control gives way to Chapter 9 proof through action
- q10: connect demonstrated proof to the next chapter's contagion and influence problem

Distractor notes:
- confuse argument with explanation in every case
- confuse action with silence or swagger
- confuse visible motion with relevant proof

Explanation diversity notes:
- avoid repeated openers
- each explanation should teach one piece of resistance, proof, ego, or ambiguity logic

CorrectIndex plan:
- keep distribution roughly balanced across 0 / 1 / 2
