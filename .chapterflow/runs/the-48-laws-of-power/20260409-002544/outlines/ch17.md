Chapter Promise:
Readers will understand how predictability makes people easier to manage, how controlled unpredictability creates caution, and when instability stops helping and starts damaging credibility.

Opening Move:
Open on the comfort others feel when they can forecast your pattern, then show how broken rhythm introduces caution and hesitation.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 for predictability, readable pattern, and the comfort of stable expectations
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 for uncertainty, hesitation, and strategic unreadability
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 for excess volatility, credibility limits, and the cost of chaos

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: define why stable pattern makes a person easier to read
- P2: explain how unpredictability creates caution and hesitation
- P3: distinguish controlled unreadability from randomness
- P4: connect uncertainty to timing and strategic discomfort
- P5: apply the pattern to work, school, and personal settings
- P6: preserve the limit where instability damages trust and control
- P7 if needed: bridge to Chapter 18 on isolation danger
