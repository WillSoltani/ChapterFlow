Chapter Promise:
Readers will understand why reputation shapes outcomes before direct contact, how one clear trait can organize anticipation, how damaged standing can be repaired, and where reputational attack becomes costly or self-defeating.

Opening Move:
Open on the unsettling fact that people are often judged before they enter the room because others already carry a story about them.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 for reputation as preloaded power and anticipatory judgment
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 for one-trait anchoring and reputation as shield
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 for repair by association and offensive-limit boundary

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: define reputation as force that arrives before the person does
- P2: explain why people rely on appearances, actions, and hearsay when true character is inaccessible
- P3: show why one outstanding quality is more usable than a blurry cluster of traits
- P4: explain how reputation protects vulnerability by narrowing what others think they know
- P5: apply the law to work, school, and personal settings through repair and reinforcement
- P6: preserve the limit that reputational attack can escalate and that image without evidence becomes brittle
- P7 if needed: bridge to Chapter 6 on attention and visibility

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter distinguishes durable reputation built from repeated visible cues from empty image claims that collapse when behavior contradicts them.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must preserve the line between reputation as a strategic shield and reputation as a fragile performance that can detach from truth or invite destructive conflict.

Threshold Question:
When does guarding reputation create useful anticipatory trust or caution, and when does it turn into brittle image maintenance or reckless reputational warfare?

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- reputation works before direct contact
- people judge through visible signals and hearsay because character is opaque
- one standout trait organizes anticipation
- association can repair damaged image when it is credible
- attacking another person's reputation can shift power but also trigger blowback
