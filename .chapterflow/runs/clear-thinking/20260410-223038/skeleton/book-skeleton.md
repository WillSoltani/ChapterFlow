## Rotation Plan For Examples

- Keep work, school, and personal domains balanced in every chapter, but bias early chapters toward work and school because defaults show up fast in status and group settings.
- Use family, parenting, and close-relationship scenes more heavily in late-book chapters where the stakes shift from performance to what matters.
- Avoid recycled office-politics templates. Rotate executive, classroom, team-sport, volunteer, medical, family, and friend-group pressures.

## School-Setting Plan

- Ch01-Ch06: classroom tension, student leadership, team projects, debate teams, and coach-player dynamics to make defaults visible.
- Ch07-Ch16: study habits, advisor relationships, standards, practice routines, and self-management.
- Ch17-Ch22: project definition, option evaluation, and execution inside group assignments, clubs, labs, and capstone choices.
- Ch23-Ch26: college major, friendship time, family expectations, and graduation-pressure scenes where wanting the right things matters.
