Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader will understand how to stop treating clear thinking as a last-second act of willpower and start building it into their defaults, position, and environment.

Opening Move:
Open on the shift from merely resisting bad defaults to deliberately designing better ones, then show why clearer responses need setup before pressure arrives.

- anchor 4 used in paragraph(s): P5 practical space-before-response as a designed condition
- anchor 5 used in paragraph(s): P6 bridge into the strengths chapters

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: close the diagnosis arc and introduce the possibility of default rewiring
- P2: explain why environment and position matter as much as resolve
- P3: show how deliberate defaults widen options and reduce last-second improvisation
- P4: make the contrast between willpower and designed response practical
- P5: preserve the tension that clarity must be built before the moment, not begged from it during the moment
- P6: bridge into self-accountability and the strengths sequence

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Clarity is not a mood. The mechanism is that better positions, better environments, and deliberate patterns make clear action easier before pressure arrives.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must not imply that design eliminates struggle. Hard should ask how someone can lower dependence on willpower without pretending pressure disappears.

Threshold Test:
When is a good decision the result of strong character in the moment, and when is it actually the result of better design before the moment?
