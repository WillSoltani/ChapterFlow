Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader will understand why clearer thinking requires restraint strong enough to keep fear, desire, and urgency from overruling what they already know.

Opening Move:
Open on the difference between knowing the right move and still failing to hold it when pressure rises.

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: position self-control as the strength that keeps self-knowledge operational
- P2: show how pressure can overrule a known better answer
- P3: define self-control as restraint and emotional discipline, not emotional deadness
- P4: show practical value: protecting standards, slowing reaction, preserving judgment
- P5: preserve the tension that steadiness is not rigidity or suppression
- P6: bridge into self-confidence, where steadiness supports independent judgment

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Self-control is not generic willpower. The mechanism is pressure resistance: keeping action aligned with a truer map when fear, desire, anger, or urgency tries to override it.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must not imply that good judgment means feeling nothing. Hard should ask how a person can stay under truth instead of under impulse without becoming brittle or detached.

Threshold Test:
