Paragraph Job Map:

- P2: show why delay can quietly undo good judgment

- P4: show why follow-through matters after the choice is made
- P5: preserve the tension against stubbornness or motivational hype

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Doing it is not deciding again. The mechanism is converting judged choice into a real next step and sustained follow-through without reopening the same question on weak grounds.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must not imply that commitment means rigidity or that speed proves strength. Hard should ask how execution can stay decisive while remaining open to genuinely new evidence.

Threshold Test:
Have you taken the real next step on the judged option, or are you still hiding in reconsideration without new information?
