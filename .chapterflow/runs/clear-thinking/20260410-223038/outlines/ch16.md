Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader will understand why mistakes should trigger repair, reflection, and recovery, so the miss changes later action instead of becoming denial, rumination, or excuse.

Opening Move:
Open on the difference between making a mistake and handling a mistake well after protection has already failed.

- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 move from failed safeguard to disciplined response
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P2-P3 repair before self-story
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 reflection and learning loop
- anchor 4 used in paragraph(s): P4-P5 recovery as return to sound action
- anchor 5 used in paragraph(s): P5 boundary against rumination or easy absolution
- anchor 6 used in paragraph(s): P6 bridge into clearer problem definition

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: position mistake-handling as the next need after safeguards
- P2: show why immediate repair matters
- P3: define reflection as lesson extraction rather than self-attack
- P4: show recovery as returning to better action with a changed understanding
- P5: preserve the tension against excuse-making, denial, or rumination
- P6: bridge into defining the real problem more clearly

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Handling mistakes is not emotional display. The mechanism is disciplined recovery: repair the damage, study the sequence, keep the lesson, and re-enter action with something changed.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must not imply that every mistake becomes good because it teaches, or that seriousness requires self-punishment. Hard should ask how a person learns fast without trivializing the miss.

Threshold Test:
When a mistake gets through, how do you repair it, study it, and return to sound action without either denial or collapse?
