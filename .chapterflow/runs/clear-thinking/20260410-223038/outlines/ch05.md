Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Inertia is not the same as rest. The mechanism is that the known path keeps its place because interruption feels more expensive than continuing a weak pattern.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must not imply that every stable routine is bad. Hard should ask how someone can stay steady while still remaining available for needed change.
