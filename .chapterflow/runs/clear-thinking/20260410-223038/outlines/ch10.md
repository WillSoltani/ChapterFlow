Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader will understand why clearer thinking requires grounded self-confidence strong enough to preserve independent judgment under pressure.

Opening Move:
Open on the difference between noisy confidence and earned steadiness, then show why people can know the better answer and still abandon it when the room leans the other way.

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: position self-confidence as the strength that lets a person stand on a tested judgment
- P2: show how approval pressure and inner wobble can still distort choices
- P3: define self-confidence as earned steadiness rather than swagger
- P4: show practical value: clearer speech, firmer choices, less dependence on room temperature
- P5: preserve the tension that confidence is not arrogance or stubbornness
- P6: bridge into Strength in Action, where internal strengths become visible habits

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Self-confidence is not ego. The mechanism is grounded self-trust: judgment can stay independent because it no longer needs constant social permission.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must not imply that strong confidence means closed-mindedness. Hard should ask how a person can remain firm enough to think independently while still being revisable by truth.
