Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader will understand why better decisions need a broader option search before narrowing into evaluation and choice.

Opening Move:
Open on the difference between grabbing the first workable answer and keeping the search open long enough to find stronger alternatives.

Paragraph Job Map:

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Exploring possible solutions is not choosing yet. The mechanism is widening the set of serious candidates before judgment starts ranking and narrowing them.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must not imply that more options are always better or that endless exploration is wise. Hard should ask how broad search can stay disciplined enough to move toward choice.

Threshold Test:
Have you opened the option set enough to avoid trap answers, or are you still mistaking the first workable idea for the best one?
