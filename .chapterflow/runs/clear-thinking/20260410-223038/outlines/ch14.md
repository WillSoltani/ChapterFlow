Open on the difference between having a practiced standard and knowing where that standard still collapses because your exposure remains hidden.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Weakness knowledge is not self-attack. The mechanism is diagnostic clarity: repeated failures reveal exposure points that can later be protected.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must not imply that self-knowledge becomes shame or that diagnosis alone solves the problem. Hard should ask how a person can face exposure honestly without turning it into identity.
