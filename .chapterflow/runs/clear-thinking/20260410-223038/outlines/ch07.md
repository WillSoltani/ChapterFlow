Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader will understand why ownership is the first strength after the defaults and how accountability restores agency faster than excuse or blame.

Opening Move:
Open on the difference between explaining a bad outcome and owning your part in it, then show how autopilot survives when responsibility gets delayed.

- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P4 ownership restores agency and widens options
- anchor 4 used in paragraph(s): P5 distinction between responsibility and self-punishment
- anchor 5 used in paragraph(s): P6 bridge into self-knowledge

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: position accountability as the first strength because better defaults need an owner
- P2: explain how excuse, blame, and externalization keep autopilot alive
- P3: define accountability as ownership of contribution rather than total fault
- P4: show how ownership restores options by returning attention to controllable moves
- P5: preserve the tension that accountability is not self-attack or shame theater
- P6: bridge into self-knowledge as precision about where ownership should focus

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Accountability is not accepting blame for everything. The mechanism is that ownership interrupts autopilot by moving attention from excuse toward controllable contribution and next action.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must not imply that a person controls every outcome. Hard should ask how someone can own their real contribution without inflating their control or collapsing into shame.

Threshold Test:
When is a person's explanation of failure actually clarifying the situation, and when is it only protecting them from the first mirror?
