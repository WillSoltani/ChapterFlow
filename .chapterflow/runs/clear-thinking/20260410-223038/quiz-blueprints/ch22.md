Core checks:

- distinguish experience from actual learning

- reject rumination and self-attack

- 4 medium apply / analyze questions
- 3 hard analyze / evaluate questions

Question targets:
1. definition of feedback
2. why experience alone is not enough
3. what a postmortem does
4. work scenario about process versus result
5. dialogue about feedback versus criticism
6. school scenario about reviewing a finished project
7. luck and noise
8. why rumination is not reflection
9. how learning improves future judgment
10. threshold test: when a real lesson has been extracted

Choice-shape rules:
- 3 choices per question
- only one answer should preserve the chapter's actual mechanism
- wrong answers should fail by endorsing outcome-only thinking, self-attack, or overconfident lessons

Explanation requirements:
- easy explanations should restate feedback, postmortem, reflection, or improvement plainly
- medium explanations should tie a scenario back to process legibility
- hard explanations should preserve the learning-versus-noise tension
