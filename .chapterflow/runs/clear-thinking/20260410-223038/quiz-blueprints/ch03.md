Quiz Blueprint — Chapter 3

Core checks:
- define the ego default as image defense rather than confidence
- distinguish status threat from ordinary disagreement
- identify how ego blocks learning and revision
- connect the chapter to hierarchy / territory / self-preservation support
- preserve the bridge into social pressure rather than collapsing the chapters together

Difficulty split:
- 3 easy recall / understanding questions
- 4 medium apply / analyze questions
- 3 hard analyze / evaluate questions

Question targets:
1. definition of ego default
2. why correction can feel like attack
3. cost of protecting image before truth
4. work meeting correction scenario
5. founder or team-lead revision scenario
6. school critique scenario
7. difference between dignity and defensiveness
8. why space before response matters
9. bridge distinction between ego pressure and social pressure
10. threshold test: protecting a standard versus protecting identity

Choice-shape rules:
- 3 choices per question
- only one answer should fit the chapter's actual mechanism
- wrong answers should fail by confusing ego with emotion, confidence, or social approval

Explanation requirements:
- easy explanations should restate the core mechanism plainly
- medium explanations should tie a scenario back to status threat or lost learning
- hard explanations should preserve the dignity-versus-defensiveness tension without flattening it
