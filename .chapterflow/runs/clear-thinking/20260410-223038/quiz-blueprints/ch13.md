Quiz Blueprint — Chapter 13

Core checks:
- define exemplars as useful models rather than abstract heroes
- define practice as rehearsal that makes a standard easier to use
- distinguish calibration and measurement from ego scoring
- preserve the boundary against blind copying or performance vanity
- keep the bridge into weakness mapping visible

Difficulty split:
- 3 easy recall / understanding questions
- 4 medium apply / analyze questions
- 3 hard analyze / evaluate questions

Question targets:
1. definition of exemplar
2. why practice matters after standards
3. why measurement is not vanity by default
4. work scenario about choosing a model or rehearsal
5. dialogue about admiration versus imitation
6. school scenario about rehearsal and feedback
7. calibration
8. boundary against blind copying or ego theater
9. why practiced standards can still fail
10. threshold test: model, rehearsal, or feedback

Choice-shape rules:
- 3 choices per question
- only one answer should preserve the chapter's actual mechanism
- wrong answers should fail by turning exemplars into worship, practice into slogans, or measurement into vanity

Explanation requirements:
- easy explanations should restate exemplars, practice, or measurement plainly
- medium explanations should tie a scenario back to calibration or rehearsal
- hard explanations should preserve the independence-versus-imitation tension
