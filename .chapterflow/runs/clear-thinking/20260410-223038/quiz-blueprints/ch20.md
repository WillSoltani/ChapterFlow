Quiz Blueprint — Chapter 20

Core checks:
- define execution, commitment, action, and follow-through operationally
- distinguish settled action from repeated reconsideration
- preserve why readiness feelings are weaker than real next steps
- reject rigidity and motivational fluff
- keep the bridge into margin of safety

Difficulty split:
- 3 easy recall / understanding questions
- 4 medium apply / analyze questions
- 3 hard analyze / evaluate questions

Question targets:
1. definition of execution
2. why commitment matters
3. what follow-through means
4. work scenario about stalling after choosing
5. dialogue about reopening without new evidence
6. school scenario about launch versus delay
7. readiness versus action
8. when reevaluation is justified
9. how decisive action can avoid rigidity
10. threshold test: what shows a decision has actually become action

Choice-shape rules:
- 3 choices per question
- only one answer should preserve the chapter's actual mechanism
- wrong answers should fail by endorsing drift, recklessness, or motivational theater

Explanation requirements:
- easy explanations should restate execution, commitment, action, or follow-through plainly
- medium explanations should tie a scenario back to actual movement
- hard explanations should preserve the decisiveness-versus-rigidity tension
