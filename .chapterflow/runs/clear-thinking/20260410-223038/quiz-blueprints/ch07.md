Quiz Blueprint — Chapter 7

Core checks:
- define self-accountability as ownership of contribution and next move rather than self-blame
- distinguish agency recovery from excuse and blame
- identify the mirror / ownership turn as the chapter's mechanism
- preserve the bridge into self-knowledge
- keep the chapter candid and practical rather than corporate or moralizing

Difficulty split:
- 3 easy recall / understanding questions
- 4 medium apply / analyze questions
- 3 hard analyze / evaluate questions

Question targets:
1. definition of self-accountability
2. why accountability interrupts autopilot
3. difference between ownership and shame
4. missed-prep work scenario
5. blame-to-contribution dialogue scenario
6. school preparation scenario
7. agency versus blame distinction
8. why accountability is narrower than total fault
9. bridge distinction between self-accountability and self-knowledge
10. threshold test: explanation or self-protection

Choice-shape rules:
- 3 choices per question
- only one answer should preserve the chapter's actual mechanism
- wrong answers should fail by turning accountability into self-punishment, total fault, or empty slogan language

Explanation requirements:
- easy explanations should restate the ownership-and-agency mechanism plainly
- medium explanations should tie a scenario back to contribution, responsibility, or next action
- hard explanations should preserve the accountability-versus-shame tension
