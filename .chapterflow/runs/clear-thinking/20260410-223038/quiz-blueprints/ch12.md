Quiz Blueprint — Chapter 12

Core checks:
- define standards as pre-decided rules or thresholds that protect judgment
- distinguish standards from rigidity, slogans, or one-time intentions
- identify decision protection and anti-improvisation as the chapter's mechanism
- preserve the bridge into exemplars and practice
- keep the chapter practical rather than preachy or managerial

Difficulty split:
- 3 easy recall / understanding questions
- 4 medium apply / analyze questions
- 3 hard analyze / evaluate questions

Question targets:
1. definition of standards
2. why improvisation is risky here
3. difference between standards and rigidity
4. work convenience-versus-standard scenario
5. dialogue about rules protecting judgment
6. school pressure-versus-threshold scenario
7. decision protection
8. standards-versus-blind-obedience distinction
9. bridge distinction between standards and exemplars/practice
10. threshold test: what keeps the standard from being renegotiated away

Choice-shape rules:
- 3 choices per question
- only one answer should preserve the chapter's actual mechanism
- wrong answers should fail by turning standards into slogans, bureaucracy, or rigid obedience

Explanation requirements:
- easy explanations should restate decision protection plainly
- medium explanations should tie a scenario back to rules, thresholds, or improvisation
- hard explanations should preserve the standards-versus-rigidity tension
