Quiz Blueprint — Chapter 14

Core checks:
- define weakness as a recurring point of exposure rather than a moral label
- define blind spot and failure pattern operationally
- distinguish diagnosis from shame or identity
- preserve the bridge into safeguards
- keep the chapter practical rather than therapeutic or abstract

Difficulty split:
- 3 easy recall / understanding questions
- 4 medium apply / analyze questions
- 3 hard analyze / evaluate questions

Question targets:
1. definition of weakness
2. why blind spots matter
3. why repeated failure patterns matter
4. work scenario about noticing exposure before failure repeats
5. dialogue about diagnosis versus self-attack
6. school scenario about tracing a mistake to a pattern
7. exposure
8. why diagnosis should not become identity
9. why safeguards follow
10. threshold test: where does judgment repeatedly weaken

Choice-shape rules:
- 3 choices per question
- only one answer should preserve the chapter's actual mechanism
- wrong answers should fail by turning weakness into shame, personality labeling, or vague self-awareness talk

Explanation requirements:
- easy explanations should restate weakness, exposure, or pattern plainly
- medium explanations should tie a scenario back to diagnosis and recurring failure
- hard explanations should preserve the diagnosis-versus-identity tension
