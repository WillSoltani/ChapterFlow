Quiz Blueprint — Chapter 18

Core checks:
- define options, possibilities, open search, and early lock-in operationally
- distinguish generation from evaluation
- preserve why multiple serious candidates matter
- reject novelty worship and endless drift
- keep the bridge into evaluating the options

Difficulty split:
- 3 easy recall / understanding questions
- 4 medium apply / analyze questions
- 3 hard analyze / evaluate questions

Question targets:
1. definition of open search
2. why early lock-in is risky
3. what options do for later judgment
4. work scenario about first answer crowding out alternatives
5. dialogue about separating search from judgment
6. school scenario about widening candidates before committing
7. possibility space
8. why novelty is not the same as quality
9. how exploration helps evaluation
10. threshold test: when the search is wide enough to start narrowing

Choice-shape rules:
- 3 choices per question
- only one answer should preserve the chapter's actual mechanism
- wrong answers should fail by endorsing premature lock-in, endless ideation, or novelty bias

Explanation requirements:
- easy explanations should restate options, search, or lock-in plainly
- medium explanations should tie a scenario back to broader candidate generation
- hard explanations should preserve the openness-versus-drift tension
