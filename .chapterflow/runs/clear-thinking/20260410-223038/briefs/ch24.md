Core Claim:
The Happiness Experts matters because regret and elder hindsight reveal that many commonly pursued goals do not line up cleanly with a life well-lived. The frozen support here is medium and the heading-index lane is clear: regret, elder wisdom, life satisfaction, and priorities. The chapter should therefore stay practical and humane. It is not about sentimentality. It is about using hindsight to test what people wish they had valued, protected, or spent more of their life on.
