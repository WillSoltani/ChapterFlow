Core Claim:
Knowing your weaknesses matters because standards and practice still break down in the places where your vulnerabilities, blind spots, and failure patterns remain unexamined. The frozen support here is medium and the heading-index lane is clear: vulnerabilities, blind spots, exposure, and failure patterns. The chapter should therefore stay practical. It is not about self-criticism for its own sake. It is about identifying where judgment is predictably weaker so that clearer protection becomes possible.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Chapter 13 was about reinforcement through exemplars and practice. Chapter 14 asks where reinforcement still fails because the person does not yet understand their real exposure. Its texture should feel diagnostic, candid, and preventive rather than shaming or confessional.

Frameworks or Terms Introduced:
Weakness: a recurring point where pressure, habit, or exposure makes judgment less reliable.
Blind spot: a weakness that feels ordinary or invisible from the inside.
Exposure: the condition or setup that makes a person more likely to fail.
Failure pattern: a repeated form of error that reveals where protection is still missing.

Specific Applications:
- identifying the times, settings, or emotions that make a good standard collapse
- reviewing repeated errors for pattern rather than treating each as isolated
- separating shame from useful diagnosis
- spotting exposure before the next avoidable failure
