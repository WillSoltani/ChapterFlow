Core Claim:
Exploring possible solutions matters because a well-framed problem can still be mishandled if the search for options closes too early. The frozen support here is medium and the heading-index lane is clear: options, possibilities, open search, and avoiding early lock-in. The chapter should therefore stay practical. It is not about endless brainstorming. It is about widening the option set enough that the reader is not trapped by the first workable answer.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Chapter 17 clarified what problem is actually being solved. Chapter 18 asks how to search broadly enough to avoid premature narrowing. Its texture should feel exploratory, disciplined, and possibility-aware rather than scattered or indulgent.

Frameworks or Terms Introduced:
Options: distinct possible ways of addressing the defined problem.
Possibilities: the wider search space before the best answer is chosen.
Open search: deliberately resisting premature narrowing while useful alternatives are still available.
Early lock-in: committing too soon to the first workable answer before the option set is explored well enough.

Specific Applications:
- generating more than one serious path before committing
- widening a search that has collapsed around habit or convenience
- separating option generation from option judgment
- noticing when the first viable answer is crowding out better candidates
