Frameworks or Terms Introduced:
Margin of safety: room built into a plan so normal uncertainty does not break it.
Slack: extra time, space, or capacity that keeps a system from running at the edge.
Buffer: a practical reserve that absorbs error, delay, or pressure.
Resilience: the ability to continue functioning when reality departs from the ideal case.

Specific Applications:
- leaving time, money, or operational room instead of planning to the edge
- resisting overcommitment that depends on everything going right
- seeing buffers as protection rather than inefficiency
- designing action that can survive ordinary variance

Common Misreadings:
