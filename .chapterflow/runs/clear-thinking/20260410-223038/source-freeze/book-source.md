# Frozen Source Bundle — Clear Thinking

## Official anchor

### Farnam Street book page
Source: `https://fs.blog/clear/`

- Frames the book as practical guidance for gaining an advantage through clearer decisions.
- Establishes the core positioning idea: good positions let people think more clearly instead of getting forced by circumstances.
- Confirms the book's emphasis on defaults, deliberate rules, and turning desired behaviors into default behaviors.

### Penguin Random House Higher Education excerpt
Source: `https://penguinrandomhousehighereducation.com/book/?isbn=9780593086117`

- Supplies the strongest lawful excerpt for Chapter 1 and the opening of Chapter 2.
- Confirms Chapter 1's opening logic:
  - rationality is useless if a person does not realize a moment requires thought
  - the critical move is noticing the moment between stimulus and response
  - default reactions often make situations worse
  - biology, not deliberate reasoning, often runs the show during reactive moments
- Confirms the four defaults named in Chapter 1:
  - emotion
  - ego
  - social
  - inertia
- Preserves a concrete anchor: the "angry CEO" example used to show how circumstances can think for someone before they recognize what is happening.

### Farnam Street podcast highlight page
Source: `https://fs.blog/knowledge-project-podcast/shane-parrish/`

- Confirms five core ideas surfaced by the author himself:
  - positioning matters because it widens options
  - environment shapes choices more than willpower alone
  - social pressure rewards conformity before truth
  - self-accountability interrupts autopilot
  - the practical task is to create space between stimulus and response
