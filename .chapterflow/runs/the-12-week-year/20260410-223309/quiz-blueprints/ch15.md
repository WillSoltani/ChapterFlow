- weekly plans as the execution layer
- accountability and not going it alone

- highest justified level for this chapter: analyze

- q06: misreading routine as bureaucracy or optional support
- q07: transfer to team or personal execution

- q10: bridge toward scorekeeping

- each direct explanation must begin differently

CorrectIndex plan:
