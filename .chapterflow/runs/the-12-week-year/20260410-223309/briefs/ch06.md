Core Claim:
Chapter 6 argues that execution stays weak when the reader relies on intention, feeling, or activity instead of measurable truth. The chapter introduces result measurement and the weekly scorecard as feedback mechanisms that reveal whether the plan is actually being executed. Its job is to make honesty and measurement part of the operating system.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the truth-and-scorekeeping chapter, not the weekly-planning chapter and not yet the broader intentionality chapter. It has to show why visible measurement matters without drifting into later expanded scorekeeping systems or generic performance-dashboard talk.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter says planning and effort still need a truth-telling mechanism.
Then it introduces measuring results as the antidote to vague self-assessment.
Then it frames the weekly scorecard as practical feedback on execution.
Then it prepares the reader for intentional protection of time and attention in the next chapter.
