Core Claim:
Chapter 7 argues that good plans and honest scorekeeping still need deliberate protection if execution is going to hold. The chapter says intentionality shows up in what the reader says yes to, what the reader says no to, and how time is blocked for the work that matters. Its job is to make execution protection deliberate instead of hopeful.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the protection-and-boundary chapter, not the scorekeeping chapter and not yet the accountability chapter. It has to show how execution gets guarded against distraction and drift without collapsing into generic productivity rhetoric or later performance-time mechanics.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter says visible truth alone does not protect execution.
Then it introduces the importance of yes and no as a filtering discipline.
Then it links blocked time to protected execution.
Then it prepares the reader for ownership-based accountability in the next chapter.
