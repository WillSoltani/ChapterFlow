Core Claim:
Chapter 20 prepares the reader for the lived resistance of the first 12 weeks. It argues that early execution friction, gratification pull, old habits, victim thinking, and staged phase pressures are normal and have to be handled deliberately.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This chapter is the first-cycle friction chapter. It has to stay concrete about the resistance pattern across the first, second, and last four weeks without drifting into invented program detail beyond the frozen subheads.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter names the resistance pattern and immediate-gratification pull.
Then it explains why big change and multiple goals collide with old habits and victim thinking.
Then it walks the reader through the first four weeks, second four weeks, last four weeks, and the 13th week.
Then it closes with success tips, team application, and after-action review.
