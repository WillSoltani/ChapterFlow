Core Claim:
Chapter 10 argues that greatness is not a distant identity claim but a present-tense performance choice. The chapter says execution happens in the moment directly in front of the reader, where commitment either becomes action or stays abstract. Its job is to move the book from durable resolve into actual performance in the moment.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the moment-of-action chapter, not the commitment chapter and not yet the later daily-time-design chapter. It has to show why execution becomes real in present-tense performance without drifting into broad sports metaphors, generic mindset hype, or later performance-time mechanics.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter rejects greatness as distant self-image.
Then it reframes greatness as performance in the current moment.
Then it shows why present-tense execution is where commitment becomes real.
Then it prepares the reader for intentional imbalance around priorities in the next chapter.
