Core Claim:
Chapter 19 argues that execution strengthens when commitments become explicit and binding rather than remaining soft preferences. The chapter uses personal commitments and commitments to others to show that seriousness has to be formalized.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This chapter is the commitment-hardening chapter. It has to push beyond vague intention and into binding commitment without drifting into empty motivational language or inventing unsupported commitment systems beyond the frozen subheads.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter argues for the force of commitments.
Then it narrows into personal commitments and commitments to others.
Then it frames the thinking shift that treats commitment as binding rather than optional.
Then it extends the lesson to teams and recurring commitment failures.
