Chapter Promise:
The reader will understand why annualized thinking weakens execution and why a 12 week horizon creates sharper urgency and focus.

Opening Move:
Open on the frustration of having plenty of good ideas but watching a long calendar quietly drain urgency from them.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P2-P3
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P4-P5
- anchor 4 used in paragraph(s): P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: anchor with plenty of ideas but weak application
- P2: mechanism through annual goals and annual plans creating distance
- P3: define annualized thinking
- P4: introduce the year-end effect and why deadlines change behavior
- P5: explain the 12 week reset and periodization logic
- P6: bridge to the need for emotional commitment and the next chapter

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter must distinguish between planning across a year and executing inside a year. The problem is not planning itself but the long horizon that dilutes urgency and focus.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must preserve the risk that compressing the horizon can create frantic activity if priorities and emotional commitment are still weak.

Threshold Question:
What changes when time feels short enough to matter, and what still fails if urgency shows up before commitment and clear priorities do?
