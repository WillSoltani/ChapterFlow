Chapter Promise:
The reader will understand why explicit commitments create more force than vague intention, and why commitments to self and others make execution harder to soften.

Opening Move:
Open on the gap between saying something matters and taking on the kind of commitment that makes backing away more costly.

- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: expose preference without commitment as weak
- P2: explain the power of commitments
- P3: distinguish personal commitments and commitments to others
- P4: show the thinking shift toward binding seriousness
- P5: widen into team application and pitfalls
- P6: bridge to first-12-weeks resistance

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter must distinguish commitment from enthusiasm by showing that commitment changes the cost of backing away.
