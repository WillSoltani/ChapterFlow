The reader will understand why execution should be planned in 12 week windows instead of annual ones and how goal selection changes under that frame.

Opening Move:
Open on the clutter of an annual plan that still looks responsible while asking too much of the execution cycle.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter must distinguish between having a plan and having a plan built for execution. A 12 week plan is shorter not just in length but in consequence, selectivity, and practicality.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must preserve the risk that people will rebuild the annual plan in miniature and then call it a 12 week plan without gaining focus.

Threshold Question:
