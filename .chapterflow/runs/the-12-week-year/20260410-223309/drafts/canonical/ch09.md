Owning the work still does not guarantee the work will keep moving. That is the pressure Chapter 9 takes up. Moran and Lennington move from ownership into commitment because a person can still want the result, speak seriously about the goal, and then start bargaining the moment the work feels costly, dull, or inconvenient.

The chapter title matters because it separates interest from commitment. Interest likes the outcome while conditions feel favorable. Commitment is different. Commitment keeps acting when comfort weakens, the mood changes, and the work stops feeling attractive. The chapter says execution depends on that difference.

That distinction becomes visible when the reader starts negotiating with discomfort. Interest sounds strong while the work is exciting or emotionally rewarding. It becomes weaker when the calendar tightens, the task gets repetitive, or the payoff feels far away. The chapter treats that bargaining moment as a test. A person learns quickly whether the goal is backed by commitment or only by preference.

Commitment matters because it keeps action alive after enthusiasm fades. The chapter does not make commitment a feeling of dramatic intensity. It makes commitment a steadier refusal to let changing emotion or temporary inconvenience decide whether the work gets done. The action continues because the decision stays intact.

This does not mean pretending the work feels easy. The chapter is not interested in emotional theater. Commitment is not louder desire or harsher self-talk. It is the willingness to keep acting when the work becomes less appealing than the outcome it serves.

That is why Chapter 9 follows ownership so closely. Chapter 8 asked who owns the work. Chapter 9 asks what kind of resolve that owner actually has when comfort begins renegotiating the deal. It moves execution from ownership into durable commitment.

The chapter leaves one more question waiting. If commitment is what survives the bargaining moment, how does that commitment show itself in the moment of action itself? That is where Chapter 10 goes next.
