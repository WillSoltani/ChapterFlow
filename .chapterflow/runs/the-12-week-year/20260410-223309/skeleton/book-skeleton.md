# Book Skeleton

- Chapter count: 21
- Variant family: EMH

## Chapter Order And Intent
1. `ch01` The Challenge
   Intent: establish the gap between people's capacity and their actual execution, then frame execution as the real bottleneck.
2. `ch02` Redefining the Year
   Intent: attack annualized thinking and replace the yearly horizon with a 12 week execution window.
3. `ch03` The Emotional Connection
   Intent: explain why a compelling vision has to beat short-term comfort.
4. `ch04` Throw Out the Annual Plan
   Intent: move from annual planning to 12 week planning and goal selection.
5. `ch05` One Week at a Time
   Intent: make weekly planning the practical bridge between strategy and execution.
6. `ch06` Confronting the Truth
   Intent: bring scorekeeping and measurable truth into the system.
7. `ch07` Intentionality
   Intent: show how saying yes and no and blocking time turn intention into protected execution.
8. `ch08` Accountability as Ownership
   Intent: redefine accountability as self-owned responsibility rather than external punishment.
9. `ch09` Interest versus Commitment
   Intent: separate casual preference from durable commitment.
10. `ch10` Greatness in the Moment
   Intent: shift performance from grand ambition to present-tense execution.
11. `ch11` Intentional Imbalance
   Intent: argue for deliberate imbalance around priorities instead of equalized attention everywhere.
12. `ch12` The Execution System
   Intent: consolidate the system into principles, disciplines, and a closed loop.
13. `ch13` Establish Your Vision
   Intent: define the long-range vision that gives the 12 week cycle emotional force.
14. `ch14` Develop Your 12 Week Plan
   Intent: translate vision into a concrete 12 week plan.
15. `ch15` Installing Process Control
   Intent: build weekly routines and control mechanisms that keep plans from decaying.
16. `ch16` Keeping Score
   Intent: reinforce feedback through visible scorekeeping.
17. `ch17` Take Back Control of Your Day
   Intent: protect daily execution through time design and performance windows.
18. `ch18` Taking Ownership
   Intent: deepen accountability into owned action.
19. `ch19` 12 Week Commitments
   Intent: sharpen commitments to self and others as execution leverage.
20. `ch20` Your First 12 Weeks
   Intent: prepare the reader for resistance, old habits, and the cadence of the first cycle.
21. `ch21` Final Thoughts and the 13th Week
   Intent: close the run with reflection and the reset period after each cycle.

## Moral Complexity Flags
- ch01: performance ambition can drift into self-optimization rhetoric if the execution gap stays abstract.
- ch02: urgency can mutate into false busyness if the cycle gets detached from real priorities.
- ch07: intentional yes/no language can drift into rigid productivity posturing if context disappears.
- ch11: intentional imbalance can be misread as permission to neglect obligations permanently.
- ch16: scorekeeping can turn performative or punitive if it loses its truth-telling function.
