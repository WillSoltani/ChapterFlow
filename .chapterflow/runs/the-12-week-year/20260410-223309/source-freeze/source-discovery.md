# Source Discovery

## Goal
Freeze a lawful, high-signal source bundle for *The 12 Week Year* before any chapter work begins.

## Frozen Result
- Locked edition family: 2013 Wiley unabridged English trade edition.
- Primary frozen source: authorized O'Reilly preview for chapter map, subheads, and early chapter preview text.
- Secondary authorized support: Google Books bibliographic and overview metadata.
- Secondary corroboration: Open Library contents metadata and the official 12 Week Year system page.

## Why This Bundle Is Trustworthy Enough
- O'Reilly preserves the full numbered chapter order from Chapter 1 through Chapter 21 and exposes preview text for the opening chapters.
- Google Books confirms the same title, author pair, publisher family, and 2013 edition metadata.
- Open Library corroborates the early contents sequence and edition family.
- The official 12 Week Year site confirms the system's durable public framing around vision, 12 week goals, and weekly execution.

## Constraint
The book is not public domain and full text is not available in the frozen bundle. Reader-facing prose must therefore remain paraphrase-first and stay narrower than the book whenever support is thin.
