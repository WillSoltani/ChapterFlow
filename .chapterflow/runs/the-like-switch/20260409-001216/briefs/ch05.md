Core Claim:
Rapport becomes durable when it shows up in conversation as active listening, empathic responses, and talk that keeps the other person feeling heard rather than managed. This chapter should translate earlier attraction logic into concrete conversational behavior.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Chapter 5 narrows the lens from broad attraction forces into moment-by-moment dialogue. Its job is not generic communication advice. Its job is to show how listening, empathy statements, and responsive follow-up turn goodwill into lived interaction.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter moves from attraction structure into actual conversational behavior.
Then it shows that people feel closer when they are listened to and emotionally understood.
Then it frames empathy statements and responsive talk as practical rapport tools.
