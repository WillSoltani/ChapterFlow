# Source Freeze Report

## Frozen bundle
- `source-freeze/book-source.md`
- `source-freeze/toc.json`
- `manifests/source-ledger.json`
- `manifests/edition-lock.json`
- `sidecars/source/source-heading-index.json`

## Authorized basis
- Official Simon & Schuster title page for the book.
- Google Books metadata page with chapter contents for the 2015 edition.

## Secondary support
- Jack Schafer Psychology Today articles used only for bounded concept support where they overlap clearly with chapter titles and recurring book terminology.

## Exclusions
- No pirate mirrors.
- No OCR dumps.
- No user-supplied text files.
- No unsupported quote harvesting from partial previews.

## Writing consequence
- Reader-facing content must be paraphrase-first.
- Unsupported examples, studies, mechanisms, and quotes are out of bounds.
- Later chapters may need narrower framing than a fully accessible text would permit.
