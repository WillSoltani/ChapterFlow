#chapter-outline-template.md
Chapter Promise:
Show that attraction usually deepens through repeated contact, reciprocity, calibrated self-disclosure, and supportive responsiveness.

Opening Move:
Open by challenging the idea that attraction is only chemistry and show that repeated, rewarding, reciprocal contact matters more than people admit.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1, P2
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P2, P3
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P3, P4

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: Move from mystery talk to repeated-contact logic.
- P2: Explain reciprocity as the switch from acquaintance to deeper bond.
- P3: Show how calibrated self-disclosure deepens attraction.
- P4: Explain supportive responsiveness and identity support.
- P5: Draw the limit: oversharing or one-sided support weakens attraction.
- P6: Bridge into Chapter 5's conversation habits.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The key distinction is between exposure alone and exposure that is reciprocal, disclosive, and supportive.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard mode must preserve that attraction can weaken when disclosure is too intimate too early, when reciprocity is missing, or when the relationship stops supporting the self the person now values.

Threshold Question:
How much openness and support deepens attraction, and when does the same behavior start to feel premature or one-sided?

Takeaway Count Lock:
- Easy: 3 (always)
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 5

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- Takeaway 1: repeated contact raises the odds of attraction
- Takeaway 2: reciprocity is the tipping factor
- Takeaway 3: calibrated self-disclosure deepens rather than overwhelms
- Takeaway 4: support and identity fit help attraction last
- Takeaway 5: attraction fades when reciprocity or calibration breaks

Scenario Lesson Map:
- Scenario 1 (decision_point, work): choose whether to overshare or pace disclosure with a promising colleague
- Scenario 2 (postmortem, school): analyze why repeated club meetings never became friendship
- Scenario 3 (dialogue, personal): compare one-sided support with reciprocal support
- Scenario 4 (predict_reveal, work): predict whether attraction grows when only one side discloses
- Scenario 5 (dilemma, school): decide how much vulnerability is appropriate early
- Scenario 6 (before_after, personal): compare an attraction that deepens through reciprocity with one that stalls
