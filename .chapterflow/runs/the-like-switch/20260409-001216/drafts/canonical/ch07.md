Chapter 7 changes the book's question again. Earlier chapters showed how attention, attraction, conversation, and closeness help a bond form. This chapter asks what happens after that. Schafer's answer, at least at the level supported by the frozen chapter map, is that a strong relationship still needs ongoing care. Closeness is not the same thing as durability.

That means reciprocity still matters. A relationship may feel established, but if effort, attention, and support start moving mostly in one direction, the bond can thin even while both people still think of it as important. Early reciprocity helps attraction begin; later reciprocity helps the relationship stay livable. It keeps the bond from quietly turning into obligation, resentment, or drift.

The chapter also points toward repair. Long-term relationships are not maintained because nothing goes wrong. They last because strain, misunderstanding, and neglect do not get left alone forever. Repair does not need to mean a dramatic confrontation. More often it means noticing distance, naming it early enough, and choosing to re-enter the relationship before the gap hardens into habit.

This is also why maintenance matters as a repeated practice rather than as a rescue move. A relationship usually weakens through omission before it breaks through one spectacular event. Small failures to reciprocate, follow up, respond, or repair can accumulate. A bond that once felt easy can start running on memory instead of current investment.

The chapter should stay careful here. The frozen support does not justify a detailed system for conflict resolution, nor does it support sweeping claims about relationship longevity. But it does justify a narrow, useful point: long-term relationships need maintenance, reciprocity, and repair because past closeness does not protect a bond from present neglect.

That makes Chapter 7 an important turn before the book closes. Once a relationship has to be maintained, the reader is ready for a harder question: what are the risks inside human connection itself? Chapter 8 can then widen the frame from maintenance into the danger and value of trust, especially where deception and truth bias complicate the relationship field.
