# Book Skeleton

## Metadata
- Title: *The Like Switch*
- Authors: Jack Schafer, Marvin Karlins
- Edition: 2015 Touchstone / Simon & Schuster
- Chapter count: 8
- Variant family: EMH

## Chapter Order And Intent
1. `ch01` The Friendship Formula
   Intent: establish the book's core claim that likeability can be increased by readable, repeatable social signals and predictable rapport dynamics.
2. `ch02` Getting Noticed Before a Word Is Spoken
   Intent: show how first impressions and nonverbal cues create initial approachability before conversation begins.
3. `ch03` The Golden Rule of Friendship
   Intent: argue that people like those who make them feel good about themselves.
4. `ch04` The Laws of Attraction
   Intent: explain the conditions that increase attraction and willingness to engage.
5. `ch05` Speaking the Language of Friendship
   Intent: turn rapport into conversational practice through listening, empathy, and responsive talk.
6. `ch06` Building Closeness
   Intent: show how disclosure, common ground, and trust deepen a relationship.
7. `ch07` Nurturing and Sustaining Long-Term Relationships
   Intent: cover maintenance, reciprocity, and repair after the first bond forms.
8. `ch08` The Perils and Promise of Relationships
   Intent: address risk, deception, and the upside or cost of connection in modern settings.
