# Baseline Quality

Chapters 1 and 2 now define the baseline quality floor for later waves.

## Chapter 1 strengths
- Strong mechanical clarity around the friendship formula.
- Clear hard-edge limit: the model explains attachment but does not morally validate it.
- High convertibility into examples and quiz judgment.

## Chapter 2 strengths
- Strong gateway logic connecting first impressions to later relationship runway.
- Clear distinction between access, safety, and deeper trust.
- Strong cross-chapter bridge into Chapter 3 without collapsing into recap.

## Baseline floor to preserve
- Curiosity-first opening, not thesis-first summary.
- One governing mechanism per chapter with explicit limits.
- No generic self-help drift.
- Hard depth must keep a real moral or strategic tension alive.
- Examples must teach distinct lessons rather than cosmetic variants of one idea.
