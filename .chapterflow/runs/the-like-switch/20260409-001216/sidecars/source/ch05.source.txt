# Source Sidecar

Chapter: ch05 — Speaking the Language of Friendship
Richness: low-medium
Source IDs: src-02, src-06, src-07
Canonical edition: Touchstone / Simon & Schuster, 2015, ISBN 9781476754482
Quote posture: paraphrase_first

Core claim:
- Rapport becomes conversational when listening, empathy statements, and responsive follow-up keep the other person feeling heard.

Distinct mechanism:
- Good conversation deepens rapport by validating the person rather than redirecting the exchange back toward the self.

Required anchors:
- active listening as outward attention
- empathic statements as emotional validation
- responsive follow-up questions as rapport maintenance

Specific applications:
- improve one-on-one check-ins
- avoid interrupting with advice too early
- spot canned empathy

Common misreadings:
- active listening is just word repetition
- empathy means agreement
- more talking builds more closeness

Hard-edge requirement:
- conversational empathy weakens when it feels scripted, intrusive, or self-serving.

Threshold question:
- How do you show understanding without sounding like technique?
