# Source Sidecar

Chapter: ch07 — Nurturing and Sustaining Long-Term Relationships
Richness: low
Source IDs: src-01, src-02
Canonical edition: Touchstone / Simon & Schuster, 2015, ISBN 9781476754482
Quote posture: paraphrase_first

Core claim:
- Long-term relationships remain strong only when reciprocity, maintenance, and repair continue after closeness has formed.

Distinct mechanism:
- A bond does not stay durable by default; it weakens when care and repair are assumed away.

Required anchors:
- long-term relationships need maintenance
- reciprocity remains necessary
- repair matters when distance or strain appears

Specific applications:
- sustain a friendship or team bond through repeated attention
- notice one-sided maintenance
- address drift before it hardens into distance

Common misreadings:
- close relationships run on autopilot
- reciprocity matters only early
- repair requires a dramatic intervention

Hard-edge requirement:
- neglect and one-sided effort can weaken even a relationship that once felt solid.

Threshold question:
- What keeps a close bond durable instead of merely familiar?
