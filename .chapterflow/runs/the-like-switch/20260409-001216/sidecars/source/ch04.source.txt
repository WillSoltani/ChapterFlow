Chapter: ch04 — The Laws of Attraction
Richness: low-medium

Canonical edition: Touchstone / Simon & Schuster, 2015, ISBN 9781476754482
Quote posture: paraphrase_first

Core claim:
- Attraction grows through repeated contact, reciprocity, calibrated self-disclosure, and supportive responsiveness.

Distinct mechanism:
- Exposure starts the process, but reciprocity and calibrated disclosure turn exposure into deeper attraction.

Required anchors:
- repeated contact with classmates, coworkers, or neighbors
- reciprocity as the transition marker
- self-disclosure and support as deepening forces

Specific applications:
- understand stalled attraction
- avoid oversharing too early
- read one-sided support as a warning sign

Common misreadings:
- attraction is only physical
- more frequency solves a missing reciprocity problem
- intimacy means disclosing everything quickly

Hard-edge requirement:
- attraction weakens when disclosure is premature, reciprocity is missing, or support becomes one-sided.

Threshold question:
- How much openness deepens attraction, and when does the same openness start to feel insecure or miscalibrated?
