# Source Sidecar

Chapter: ch06 — Building Closeness
Richness: low-medium
Source IDs: src-02, src-08, src-09, src-10
Canonical edition: Touchstone / Simon & Schuster, 2015, ISBN 9781476754482
Quote posture: paraphrase_first

Core claim:
- Closeness deepens through common ground, reciprocal self-disclosure, and trust that is paced to the relationship.

Distinct mechanism:
- Similarity and disclosure help only when they are real, mutual, and not pushed past the trust already earned.

Required anchors:
- common ground accelerates rapport
- self-disclosure can increase attraction
- reciprocity and pacing determine whether trust strengthens

Specific applications:
- build early friendship without oversharing
- find real similarity without inventing it
- diagnose one-sided closeness

Common misreadings:
- deeper disclosure is always better
- common ground has to be dramatic
- trust follows openness automatically

Hard-edge requirement:
- closeness weakens when disclosure outruns reciprocity or when common ground is forced rather than real.

Threshold question:
- When does openness deepen trust, and when does it reveal imbalance?
