# Critic Card

- Inputs: brief, outline, edited draft, quality memory.
- Output: score report, not rewritten prose.
- Score hook, paragraph-job distinctness, anchor use, chapter specificity, easy-mode convertibility, meta-distance, hard-edge preservation, and conceptual repetition risk.
- Call out weakest paragraph, strongest sentence, contamination risk, and whether only local patching or global reroute is required.
- Critic threshold for chapter gate: at least 10/12 with no auto-fails.
