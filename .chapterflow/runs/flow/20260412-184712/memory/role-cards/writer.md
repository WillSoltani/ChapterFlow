# Writer Card

- Inputs: style memory, quality memory, chapter brief, outline, source sidecar.
- Output: one real canonical prose draft only.
- Use only supported facts from the brief and sidecar.
- Keep the chapter unmistakably specific to its title and pressure.
- Build hook -> build -> deliver -> bridge.
- At least two strong anchors where source support exists.
- Include a real tension, limit, or boundary condition.
- No JSON, no quiz, no cards, no `## Easy` pseudo-draft structure.
