Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand that attention is limited psychic energy, that inner disorder comes from fractured attention, and that growth requires ordered consciousness.

Opening Move:
Open on the fact that consciousness cannot hold everything at once, so every life is shaped by what attention keeps admitting and what it leaves out.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): self-mastery traditions in P1 as framing contrast
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): attention as psychic energy in P2-P3
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): entropy versus flow and differentiation/integration in P4-P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: anchor the value of self-mastery across traditions
- P2: explain the limit structure of consciousness
- P3: define attention as psychic energy and connect it to the self
- P4: explain psychic entropy as scattered attention
- P5: explain flow as ordered consciousness
- P6: explain growth through differentiation and integration
- P7 if needed: bridge into enjoyment as the lived quality of this ordered state

The core distinction is not happy versus unhappy but entropy versus order in consciousness.
