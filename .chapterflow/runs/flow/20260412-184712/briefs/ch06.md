Core Claim:
Chapter 6 argues that thought can become a domain of flow just as the body can. Csikszentmihalyi shows that language, history, science, philosophy, and other mental practices become deeply rewarding when the mind has rules, symbols, and challenges strong enough to organize attention.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the cognition chapter. Chapter 5 showed the body as a domain of flow; Chapter 6 shows how the mind itself becomes a site of play, discipline, and lifelong challenge.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author frames thought as a structured game rather than a passive mental stream.
Then the author moves through language, history, science, and philosophy as examples of mental flow.
Then the author keeps intellectual flow open to amateurs and lifelong learners rather than professionals alone.
