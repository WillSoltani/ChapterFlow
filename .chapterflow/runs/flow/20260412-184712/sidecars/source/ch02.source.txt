Chapter 2: The Anatomy of Consciousness

Frozen support:
- WorldCat headings: The limits of consciousness; Attention as psychic energy; Enter the self; Disorder in consciousness: psychic entropy; Order in consciousness: flow; Complexity and the growth of the self

Safe chapter-level claims:
- Consciousness is limited, so where attention goes matters.
- The self is shaped by what consciousness repeatedly orders around.
- Inner disorder is not just bad mood; it is scattered attention that cannot hold a meaningful goal.
- Flow is presented as ordered consciousness rather than mere pleasant feeling.
- Real growth requires both becoming more differentiated and more integrated.

- Keep biological claims high level.
