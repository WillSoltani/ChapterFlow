Chapter 4: The Conditions of Flow

Frozen support:
- WorldCat headings: Flow activities; Flow and culture; The autotelic personality; The people of flow
- Secondary freeze: some activities are structurally flow-friendly; culture and family conditions can either support or obstruct flow; autotelic personality matters because some people are better at turning conditions into engagement; flow is not restricted to one elite domain or one type of person

Safe chapter-level claims:
- Some activities make flow more likely because of their structure.
- Flow is shaped not only by individual effort but by cultural and social conditions.
- The autotelic personality describes a tendency to find or create engagement more readily.
- Flow-prone people are not defined only by talent or status.
- The chapter broadens flow from isolated episodes to repeatable conditions.

Support limits:
- Do not pretend every activity can be made equally flow-friendly.
- Do not turn autotelic personality into a fixed born-trait story.
- Do not overclaim cross-cultural detail beyond the frozen note that culture can support or obstruct flow.
