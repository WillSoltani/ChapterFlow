## Secondary chapter interpretation freeze

Secondary summaries are frozen for chapter-local narrowing only. They support these chapter-level signals:

- Ch. 1 argues that material progress does not secure happiness and that control over consciousness matters more than possessions.
- Ch. 2 frames attention as limited psychic energy, introduces psychic entropy versus flow, and treats self-growth as differentiation plus integration.
- Ch. 3 distinguishes pleasure from enjoyment and explains flow as challenge-skill balance plus clear goals, feedback, concentration, control, self-loss, and altered time.
- Ch. 4 explains why some activities are structurally flow-friendly and why autotelic personality and family conditions matter.
- Ch. 5 argues that bodily disciplines, movement, sexuality, yoga, martial arts, and trained senses can all become sources of flow.
- Ch. 6 argues that memory, symbolic systems, conversation, writing, history, science, and philosophy can structure the mind against chaos.
- Ch. 7 argues that work can become a primary source of flow when challenge, skill, and self-direction align.
- Ch. 8 argues that solitude and relationships both require skill, and that loneliness and social chaos are not solved by mere contact.
- Ch. 9 argues that adversity can be transformed by disciplined attention rather than treated as pure defeat.
- Ch. 10 argues that isolated flow episodes are not enough; a coherent life theme is needed to unify purpose and meaning.
