## Chapter Order

   Concept density: medium

## Rotation Plan For Examples

- Therapy or counseling pressure anchors early chapters 2 through 7 without overusing clinician-client rooms.
- Education-heavy chapters: 13, 14, 15.
- Family and personal relationship pressure: 8, 9, 16, 17, 18.

- Maintain 2 work / 2 school / 2 personal categories inside each chapter package even when the chapter source domain is therapeutic.

## Premium Routing Candidates

- Chapter 1 because it sets voice and the whole book's human stance
- Chapter 2 because it defines the growth-facilitation logic the rest of the book depends on
- Chapter 9 because the good-life framing can become generic very fast
- Chapter 17 because intergroup communication can drift into vague civility language
- Chapter 21 because the ending must preserve the person against systems without becoming anti-science caricature
