Chapter Promise:
Readers will understand that becoming a person means an ongoing process of living less defensively and more openly from experience, not reaching a final polished identity.

Opening Move:
Open on the tension between wanting a finished self and discovering that personhood is something more lived than possessed.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 for the move from therapeutic direction to the meaning of becoming
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 for becoming as less defensive and more open contact with experience
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 for the limit against making personhood a fixed ideal or loose self-display

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: open on the temptation to treat selfhood as a final achievement
- P2: define becoming as process rather than possession
- P3: explain movement away from defensive falseness
- P4: explain movement toward more owned and open living
- P5: mark the limit against undisciplined expression or identity slogans
- P6: show why conflict and uncertainty remain part of becoming
- P7 if needed: bridge to a process conception of psychotherapy

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter distinguishes process selfhood from fixed identity attainment and from careless self-expression.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Becoming a person cannot be a finished possession without turning into another defensive ideal. Hard depth must ask how selfhood stays alive as process without dissolving into looseness.

Threshold Question:
How do you become more fully yourself without turning that very aim into another rigid identity project?
