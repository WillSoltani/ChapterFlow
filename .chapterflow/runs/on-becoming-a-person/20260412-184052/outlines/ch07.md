Chapter Promise:
Readers will understand that Rogers conceives psychotherapy as a disciplined unfolding process rather than a fixed external technique.

Opening Move:
Open on the mismatch between living persons in process and any attempt to reduce therapy to a mechanical method.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 for the chapter title and transition from becoming to psychotherapy
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 for therapy as process rather than fixed technique
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 for the limit against looseness and against mechanization

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: move from personhood as process to therapy as process
- P2: reject fixed-technique framing as too crude
- P3: describe psychotherapy as unfolding experiencing and awareness
- P4: explain less distortion and more direct contact in process terms
- P5: mark the limit against vague anti-structure readings
- P6: explain why formula can damage the very process it tries to manage
- P7 if needed: bridge to personal goals in Chapter 8

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter distinguishes disciplined therapeutic process from both mechanical procedure and loose unstructured encounter.

Hard-Depth Tension:
If the person is process, therapy cannot be honest as a fixed technique. But if therapy is process, it still needs form. Hard depth must preserve that middle tension.

Threshold Question:
How can psychotherapy stay disciplined enough to help without freezing a living person into a technique-managed object?
