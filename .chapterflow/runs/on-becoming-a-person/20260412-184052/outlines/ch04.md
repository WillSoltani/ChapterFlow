Chapter Promise:
Readers will understand that psychotherapy must be known both objectively and subjectively, because neither angle alone captures the whole process.

Opening Move:
Open on the problem of describing psychotherapy from outside while something crucial is happening from within.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 for the two ways of knowing psychotherapy
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 for what objective description can and cannot catch
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 for the value and limits of subjective experience

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: open on the problem of incomplete description
- P2: define objective and subjective angles without caricature
- P3: explain the value of objective knowledge
- P4: mark what external observation can miss
- P5: explain why subjective process matters
- P6: mark the limits of subjectivity too
- P7 if needed: bridge to the directions evident in therapy

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter distinguishes outside description from lived inward process and argues that psychotherapy requires both modes of knowing.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Each knowledge mode becomes distorted when it tries to rule alone. Hard depth must ask what gets lost under single-mode dominance.

Threshold Question:
What disappears when psychotherapy is described only from outside or only from inside?
