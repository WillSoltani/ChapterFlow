Chapter Promise:
Readers will understand that Rogers treats the relation between persons and science as a real philosophical tension, not as a simple victory for one side.

Opening Move:
Open on the power of scientific inquiry, then surface the question of what may be missed when persons are treated only as objects of study.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 for the chapter title and move into Part V
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 for the legitimacy of science and the risk of reduction
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 for why the issue is philosophical rather than anti-scientific

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: introduce the tension between persons and science
- P2: affirm scientific power and legitimacy
- P3: mark the incompleteness of reducing persons to scientific objects alone
- P4: explain why the issue is philosophical and not mere resistance to evidence
- P5: mark the limit against anti-science caricature
- P6: show why a powerful method can still be incomplete regarding persons
- P7 if needed: bridge to personality change in psychotherapy

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter distinguishes respect for scientific inquiry from reduction of persons to only what scientific method can manage.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Science can be powerful without being the whole truth. Hard depth must preserve that double claim.

Threshold Question:
How can persons be studied scientifically without being reduced to only what scientific method can capture?
