Supported framing

- The book moves from Rogers's personal stance into helping conditions, psychotherapy process, philosophical anthropology, research, and wider implications for education, family life, communication, creativity, and social science.
- The human person is treated as capable of growth under relational conditions marked by acceptance, understanding, and reduced defensiveness.
- Later chapters extend the therapeutic stance into broader domains rather than abandoning the same core commitments.
