# Source Discovery

## Decision

The run locks to the original 1961 Houghton Mifflin text of **On Becoming a Person: A Therapist's View of Psychotherapy** by **Carl Rogers**.

## Why this edition

- Open Library exposes a later authorized preview with the full 21-chapter table of contents, page starts, and part structure.
- That preview explicitly ties back to the 1961 work and does not introduce any new numbered chapters.
- Google Books confirms the book remains in print in later editions with a Peter D. Kramer introduction, but the added introduction sits outside the core numbered chapter set.
- No translation ambiguity is present, and no later revision appears to change chapter order or scope in a way that would require user intervention.

## Source ladder outcome

1. No public-domain full text was found.
2. Authorized preview metadata exists through Open Library and Google Books.
3. Official or near-official bibliographic corroboration exists through Open Library work records and standard catalog mirrors.
4. Reputable secondary summaries exist for broad theme support.
5. Chapter-local sidecars must remain narrower than the temptation to fill gaps from general Rogers knowledge.

## Working constraints

- Paraphrase first throughout.
- Exact quote use is disabled by default for this run.
- Only short wording already visible in the frozen preview may be treated as verified quote support.
- Chapter briefs and prose must stay anchored to the frozen chapter map, preview excerpt support, and named themes from the locked bundle.
