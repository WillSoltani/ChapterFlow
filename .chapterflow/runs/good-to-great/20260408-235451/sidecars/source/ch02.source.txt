Chapter 2 source focus: Level 5 leadership as the mix of personal humility and fierce professional will.

- Public support is strongest for the paradox itself: the leaders behind the leap were institution-first, modest in style, and relentless in standards.
- The frozen bundle supports the contrast with celebrity leadership, the window-and-mirror pattern around credit and blame, and the idea that durable greatness requires something stronger than charisma.
- Keep claims narrow: do not turn humility into passivity, and do not invent clinical explanations for ego or temperament.
