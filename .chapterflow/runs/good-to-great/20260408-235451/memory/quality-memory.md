# Quality Memory

- Never invent facts, studies, quotes, company anecdotes, or causal claims beyond the frozen bundle and chapter brief.
- Use paraphrase first because the source bundle is official-concept-plus-metadata, not a full licensed text.
- Hard fails: thesis-first openings, contamination leakage, unsupported quote language, tone collapse, plain-string scenarios, empty quizzes, and pseudo-drafts posing as prose.
- Every chapter needs a chapter-specific mechanism plus at least one real limit, tradeoff, or failure mode.
- Keep scenarios concrete and varied across work, school, and personal life while preserving the leadership and organizational logic of the chapter.
- If a line could fit another leadership book with only a title swap, tighten it until it cannot.
