# Style Memory

- Write like a sharp operator explaining institutional performance, not like a motivational business-book summarizer.
- Open with a vivid company, leader, or decision tension rather than a thesis announcement.
- Keep Good to Great tied to its actual study spine: disciplined people, disciplined thought, disciplined action, and cumulative momentum.
- Easy should clarify the principle cleanly, medium should explain how the mechanism works, and hard should preserve a real boundary, failure mode, or sequencing tension.
- Tone differences should change function and stakes, not just swap intensity words.
- Avoid generic management platitudes, hero-founder worship, fake precision, and any company detail the frozen bundle cannot support.
