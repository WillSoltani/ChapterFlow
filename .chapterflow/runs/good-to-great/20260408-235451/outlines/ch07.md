Chapter Promise:
Readers will understand why technology strengthens a disciplined concept but cannot replace one.

Opening Move:
Open on the temptation to treat a new tool as the breakthrough itself, then flip to the idea that tools only amplify existing clarity and momentum.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 for technology as accelerator, not cause
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 for hedgehog-fit adoption
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 for anti-frenzy discipline and the bridge to momentum

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: show the common habit of hoping for rescue through tools
- P2: define technology as amplifier rather than root cause
- P3: connect adoption to the hedgehog
- P4: contrast disciplined use with prestige or fear-driven adoption
- P5: show why frenzy reveals weak clarity
- P6: bridge to the flywheel as the real source of breakthrough

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter distinguishes technology-led excitement from concept-led technology use.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must preserve the idea that chasing technology can mimic progress while exposing fear, imitation, or conceptual weakness.

Threshold Question:
How can leaders tell whether a technology choice is accelerating their concept or distracting them from the fact that the concept is still weak?
