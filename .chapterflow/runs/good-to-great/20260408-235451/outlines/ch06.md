Chapter Promise:
Readers will understand that a culture of discipline is freedom within a coherent framework, not bureaucracy with better branding.

Opening Move:
Open on the false choice between chaotic freedom and controlling bureaucracy, then show the third path Collins wants.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 for freedom within framework
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 for disciplined people versus bureaucracy
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 for stop-doing discipline and the bridge to technology

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: show the false freedom-versus-control choice
- P2: define discipline inside a framework
- P3: explain why disciplined people reduce bureaucracy
- P4: tie action back to the hedgehog

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter distinguishes aligned self-discipline from externally imposed control.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must preserve the idea that control-heavy systems can look disciplined while actually exposing weak people fit or weak conceptual clarity.

Threshold Question:
How can leaders tell whether their discipline system is building aligned freedom or just masking fragility with control?
