Chapter Promise:
Readers will understand why Collins treats humility-plus-will leadership as the human starting point of a durable leap.

Opening Move:
Open on the kind of boardroom instinct that wants a dazzling savior, then flip the expectation toward the quieter Level 5 profile.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 for humility plus will
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 for the anti-celebrity contrast
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 for window-mirror and succession logic

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: describe the instinct to look for a larger-than-life hero
- P2: define Level 5 as the paradoxical mix of humility and professional will
- P3: contrast institution-first leadership with ego-first leadership
- P4: explain why charisma can impress without building durability
- P5: show the window-and-mirror pattern with credit and blame
- P6: connect Level 5 leadership to succession and bridge to "First Who... Then What"

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter distinguishes institution-serving resolve from ego-serving visibility.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The profile must stay paradoxical. Humility without will is too soft; will without humility slides toward celebrity power.

Threshold Question:
Can a leader with strong ego drives genuinely grow into Level 5 behavior, or will the need for personal credit eventually corrupt the sequence?
