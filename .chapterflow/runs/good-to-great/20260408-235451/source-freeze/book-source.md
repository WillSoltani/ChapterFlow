## Chapter Map And Working Focus

1. Good Is the Enemy of Great
   - Study setup, why "good" blocks greatness, and the disciplined research design.
2. Level 5 Leadership
   - Humility plus will, leadership that serves the institution rather than ego.
3. First Who... Then What
   - Right people before direction, rigor over charisma, bus metaphor.
4. Confront the Brutal Facts
   - Truth-telling, the Stockdale paradox, and climates where reality can be heard.
5. The Hedgehog Concept
   - The three circles and the discipline of clarity.
6. A Culture of Discipline
   - Freedom within constraints, self-disciplined people, and adherence to the hedgehog.
7. Technology Accelerators
   - Technology as momentum amplifier rather than root cause.
8. The Flywheel and the Doom Loop
   - Breakthrough as cumulative momentum, not one dramatic move.
9. From Good to Great to Built to Last
   - How the earlier findings connect to enduring institutional longevity.
