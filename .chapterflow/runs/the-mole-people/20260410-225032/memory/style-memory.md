# Style Memory — The Mole People

Operating cheat sheet for writer, editor, and converter. Distilled from the v13 pack style files plus this run's frozen-source limits.

## Voice
Concrete, unsentimental, and human. The prose should feel observational and pressure-aware, not theatrical, preachy, or sociology-textbook flat.

## Book-specific posture
- This is reportage about people surviving below ground in New York, not a thriller and not poverty voyeurism.
- Keep danger, deprivation, fear, improvisation, and dignity visible at the same time.
- When tunnel life is described, make the social and physical cost visible.
- When institutions appear, keep the friction between help, control, neglect, and survival visible.

## Non-negotiables
- Use only claims supported by the brief and chapter source sidecar.
- No em dashes.
- No invented tunnel lore, body counts, geography, or underground mythology.
- No raw source splice into reader-facing prose unless a short exact quote is explicitly verified.
- No contamination phrases, validator language, or abstract social-justice padding.

## Banned drift
- Do not turn the book into urban-legends content.
- Do not romanticize the tunnels as rebellion or purity.
- Do not flatten the people into symbols of resilience or symbols of decay.
- Do not pretend chapter-level certainty where the frozen source bundle is thin.

## Depth contract
- Easy: one grounded picture, one clean frame, one practical implication.
- Medium: mechanism, friction, and chapter-specific application.
- Hard: preserve ambiguity, cost, boundary conditions, or an unresolved ethical tension.

## Tone differentiation
- Gentle: lowers distance without softening the stakes.
- Direct: names the operating pressure plainly.
- Competitive: should usually stay severe and consequential, never swaggering.

## Book-specific craft checks
- The opening chapters should keep fear, scarcity, and entry pressure concrete.
- Character chapters should stay person-rooted, not become generic homelessness commentary.
- Systems chapters should still feel tied to lived tunnel conditions.
- Any use of contested population claims or folklore must be minimized or carefully qualified.
