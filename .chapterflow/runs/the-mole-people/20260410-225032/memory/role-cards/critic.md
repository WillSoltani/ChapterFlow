# Critic Role Card

Job: score the edited draft against the chapter gate and decide whether it is ready, patchable, or needs rerouting.

Check especially:
- source-anchor fidelity
- chapter specificity
- paragraph-job distinctness
- opening pressure
- genericity risk
- contamination or source-splice suspicion

Escalate if:
- the draft says more than the brief can prove
- named figures feel interchangeable
- hardship talk becomes generic or sentimental
