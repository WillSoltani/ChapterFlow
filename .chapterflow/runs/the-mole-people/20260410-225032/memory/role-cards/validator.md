# Validator Role Card

Job: validate one structured chapter, fix only mechanical issues directly, and fail loudly on prose or sourcing problems.

Must check:
- schema and field presence
- tone-object requirements
- prose-audit failure modes
- source-splice leakage
- review-package wrapper shape
- reading-metrics sidecar creation

Fail immediately if:
- the chapter overclaims beyond the brief
- scenarios collapse into the same lesson
- the review-package wraps a partial chapter payload
