Core Claim:
This chapter turns Seville from a pressure witness into a person whose humor, history, and guarded self-presentation reveal what long exposure to violence, homelessness, and tunnel life do to identity. The chapter's center is not pity. It is the unstable way Seville keeps dignity, distance, and liveliness alive while describing a life shaped by family violence, street adaptation, drugs, and underground survival. His story arrives in fragments on purpose. That fragmented delivery is part of the meaning: some lives cannot be told as tidy origin stories because self-protection survives inside how the story is spoken.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the first narrow portrait chapter in the package. It follows Chapter 2's shelter logic by showing the person inside that logic rather than widening back out to structure alone.

Author Logic Chain:
