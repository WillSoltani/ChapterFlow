Core Claim:
This chapter presents Mac as a person whose life has narrowed into an organized war against scarcity, exposure, and indignity. The lawful bundle does not support a full biography, but it does support a disciplined tunnel portrait: an older man who hunts rats for food, names them with hard practicality, cooks what he catches, and recites Thoreau without letting that detail soften the conditions around him. The center is not quirky contrast. It is the way severe pressure can turn survival into a code.
