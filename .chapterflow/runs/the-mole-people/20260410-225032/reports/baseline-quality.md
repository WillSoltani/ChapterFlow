# Baseline Quality — Chapters 1 and 2

## Quality floor
- Chapter 1 sets the frame standard: hidden reality without spectacle, strong witness-versus-myth tension, and a clean accusation aimed at visible civic failure.
- Chapter 2 sets the decision-pressure standard: comparative risk, damaged shelter logic, and a disciplined explanation that never turns into romance.

## Chapter 1 strengths
- Strong opening control over rumor versus dark fascination.
- Clean coexistence of danger and community without flattening either side.
- Ethical limit on voyeurism and disputed lore stays visible through hard depth.

## Chapter 2 strengths
- Stronger causal mechanism than Chapter 1 because relative safety is concrete and teachable.
- Seville anchor stays narrow and lawful while still giving the chapter historical compression.
- The damaged meaning of `home` holds across all depths and examples.

## Shared floor for later waves
- No chapter may rely on title energy alone; it needs one defendable mechanism as clear as Chapter 2's comparative-risk move.
- No chapter may drift into tunnel mythology, pity shorthand, or generic homelessness commentary.
- Hard depth must preserve a real boundary or contradiction, not merely add more emotionally intense wording.
- Example scenarios must stay chapter-specific and teach the mechanism instead of paraphrasing the recap.

## Watchpoints for later waves
- Thin-source chapters must stay narrower than these first two chapters unless fresh lawful support is frozen first.
- Character chapters must avoid inflating review-level names into full scenes or dialogue without source support.
- Any later chapter weaker than this baseline on specificity, mechanism, or anti-spectacle discipline should be rerouted before the wave continues.
