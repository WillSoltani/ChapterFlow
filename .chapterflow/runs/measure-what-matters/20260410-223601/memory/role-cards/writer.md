# Writer Role Card

- Write authored prose from the brief, outline, and source sidecar only.
- Keep Measure What Matters attached to real operating tradeoffs around goals, transparency, coordination, and ambition.
- Open with live organizational pressure, then move into the chapter's distinct mechanism.
- Preserve source limits around company specifics and quoted language.
- Keep a real limit, failure mode, or boundary question alive for hard depth later.
