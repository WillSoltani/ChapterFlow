# Chapter Outline

Chapter: Superpower #2: Align and Connect for Teamwork

1. Open by shifting from one committed team to multiple teams that must move in related directions.
2. Explain why isolated local optimization breaks ambitious work even when each team is sincere.
3. Define alignment as visible, connected priorities rather than sameness.
4. Show how dependencies become manageable when teams can see how their work links.
5. Preserve the warning that bureaucracy and forced sameness are false substitutes for real alignment.
6. Bridge into the MyFitnessPal company case as a concrete alignment story.
