Chapter Promise:
Readers will understand why focus and commitment operate together inside OKRs and why a crowded goal system weakens execution even when every goal sounds worthwhile.

Opening Move:
Open on the strain of too many simultaneous priorities, then show how focus and commitment turn selection into real force.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 for focus as choosing what matters most
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 for commitment as real buy-in and follow-through
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 for overload as the enemy of execution and the bridge into company stories

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: show the cost of priority overload
- P2: define focus as sacrifice and selection
- P3: distinguish commitment from passive awareness
- P4: show why buy-in matters for execution
- P5: explain how overload weakens all goals at once
- P6: bridge into lived company examples of focus

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter distinguishes having many worthy goals from having a small enough set of commitments that execution can actually organize around them.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The reader must feel the difference between disciplined focus and fake simplicity that hides unresolved tradeoffs or weak buy-in.

Threshold Question:
How do you tell whether a team has truly focused or only renamed its overload while keeping every real obligation alive?

Takeaway Count Lock:
- Easy: 3 (always)
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 5

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- focus as sacrifice
- commitment as buy-in
- overload as execution noise
- worthiness versus priority
- symbolic alignment versus real force

Scenario Lesson Map:
- Scenario 1 (decision_point, work): leader cuts a crowded roadmap to a few true priorities
- Scenario 2 (postmortem, school): student group fails because every workstream stayed active
- Scenario 3 (dialogue, personal): family argues over three priorities that cannot all be first
- Scenario 4 (predict_reveal, work): team claims commitment but keeps hedging effort
- Scenario 5 (dilemma, school): club officers must choose between ambitious breadth and executable focus
- Scenario 6 (before_after, personal): side project becomes viable once commitments narrow
