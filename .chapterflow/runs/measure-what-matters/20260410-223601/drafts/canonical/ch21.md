# The Goals to Come

The comparative proof is over. After company cases, coaching stories, culture arguments, and transfer tests across settings, the book no longer needs another demonstration that OKRs can work. The closing move is different. It asks what the reader is supposed to do with a method that has already been shown, pressured, corrected, and carried into practice. A forward-looking ending matters because the method stays alive only if it keeps being aimed at goals that have not been reached yet.

That turn forward cannot become vague uplift. A book about goals, measurement, support, and culture does not earn its ending by telling the reader to dream bigger and leave the mechanisms behind. The stronger ending preserves the operating logic. Ambition still needs a structure. A worthy goal still needs visibility, commitment, tracking, and enough honesty to reveal whether progress is real or imagined. The future stays demanding precisely because inspiration by itself does not run a system.

What changes at the end is the reader's position. Earlier chapters asked whether bold goals can clarify effort, whether scoring can sharpen learning, whether transparency can strengthen alignment, and whether culture can make serious work repeatable. Now the burden moves outward. The reader is no longer evaluating stories at a distance. The reader has to decide what future goal deserves that whole operating discipline. The unresolved question is not whether the method can produce movement, but whether the next goal will be important enough to justify using it well.

That creates a second pressure. Future goals can be noble in language and still weak in design. They can sound urgent while remaining too blurry, too private, or too unsupported to survive contact with real work. The book's closing horizon therefore depends on responsibility. Choosing the next goal means choosing the system that will carry it, the measures that will test it, the conversations that will keep it honest, and the culture that will make repetition possible when enthusiasm fades.

So the ending does not close with comfort. It closes with transfer of duty. The point is not to admire the stories behind the reader. The point is to carry forward the same discipline into the goals still ahead. Future work, future teams, and future commitments will still separate rhetoric from reality in the same old way. A worthy closing chapter turns that fact into a charge: keep aiming forward, but only with goals that can survive measurement, support, and repeated practice.
