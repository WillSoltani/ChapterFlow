# The Goals to Come

The proof sequence has already done its work. After the cases, the counterexamples, and the culture transfer tests, the book does not need one more demonstration that OKRs can matter. The closing move asks a harder question: what should happen next if the method has already been shown to work? A forward-looking ending matters because the discipline behind the method stays alive only when it is carried into goals that are still unfinished.

That final turn cannot drift into mood alone. Goals, metrics, support, and culture have been the operating logic of the book. The ending holds only if that logic stays intact. A meaningful future goal still needs a visible target, a way to test progress, and enough honesty to show whether the work is moving or only sounding ambitious. Inspiration can start motion, but it cannot replace a system.

Earlier chapters tested whether bold goals can sharpen focus, whether measurement can expose learning, and whether culture can keep better behavior repeating. The closing chapter changes the reader's role. The stories no longer sit in front of the reader as examples to judge. The burden shifts to selection and responsibility. Which future goal deserves the full discipline of the method? Which goal is important enough to justify visible commitments, honest scoring, and the friction of real follow-through?

That question rules out a sentimental ending. Future goals can sound admirable while remaining blurry, unsupported, or structurally weak. The book's closing horizon is therefore practical. Choosing the next goal also means choosing the system that will carry it, the measures that will challenge it, the people who will keep it honest, and the surrounding norms that will make the work repeat when novelty wears off.

The final note is demanding, not decorative. The reader is not being asked to admire the method from a safe distance. The reader is being asked to carry it forward. The goals to come will still expose the difference between rhetoric and reality. The right response is not louder intention. It is disciplined aim, visible measurement, and repeated support strong enough to keep the next important goal from dissolving into talk.
