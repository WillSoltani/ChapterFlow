Core Claim:
This chapter argues that OKRs create teamwork when teams align visible priorities and connect their work across functions instead of optimizing in parallel silos. Alignment should feel like linked direction, not bureaucratic uniformity.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the conceptual bridge from commitment inside one team to coordinated execution across teams and should explain why ambitious work breaks down when priorities stay private or disconnected.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author moves from one committed team into the problem of multiple teams working on related priorities.
Then the author shows that alignment comes from visible, connected objectives rather than isolated local optimization.
Then the author concludes that teams collaborate better when dependencies and shared direction are explicit.
