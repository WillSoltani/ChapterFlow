Core Claim:
This chapter argues that culture is shaped by repeated operating signals rather than by slogans alone. After the first Part 2 company stories, the chapter should widen toward culture as the environment created by ongoing priorities, conversation, feedback, recognition, and accountability.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is a conceptual widening chapter after Adobe and Zume Pizza, so it should not behave like another single-company story. The title is broad, which means the chapter must stay narrow and operational rather than drifting into generic culture language.
