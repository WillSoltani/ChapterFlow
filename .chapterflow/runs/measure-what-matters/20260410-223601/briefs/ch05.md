Core Claim:
This chapter argues that focus becomes real inside a company when leaders cut strategy down to a smaller number of decisive moves and keep those moves from being crowded out by every worthy idea. The Remind story should show focus as practical narrowing, not as generic simplicity advice. The chapter's job is to make selection visible inside a company case after Chapter 4 explained the principle in the abstract.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the first company story dedicated to the focus superpower and should translate Chapter 4's sacrifice logic into a concrete operating case without overclaiming unsupported Remind detail.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author moves from the general focus principle into the Remind case.
Then the author shows that focus worked because the company reduced the number of moves that could truly lead.
Then the author concludes that strategic force comes from protecting a smaller set of priorities from overload.
