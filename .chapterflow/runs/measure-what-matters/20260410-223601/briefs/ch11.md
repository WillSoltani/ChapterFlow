Core Claim:
This chapter argues that tracking becomes real inside an organization when progress stays visible enough for people to review movement, surface slippage, and adjust before the work hardens into retrospective explanation. The Gates Foundation story should make accountability feel like evidence-guided ownership, not reporting theater.
