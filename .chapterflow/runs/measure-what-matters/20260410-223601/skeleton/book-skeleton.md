# Book Skeleton

## Metadata
- Book: Measure What Matters
- Author: John Doerr
- Locked edition: 2018 English Portfolio / Penguin trade family
- Wave size: 2 by default
- Residual final wave note: chapter 21 remains after the paired waves because the chapter count is odd

## Rotation Plan For Examples
- Use the canonical six-format rotation once per chapter: decision_point, postmortem, dialogue, predict_reveal, dilemma, before_after.
- Keep the 2 work / 2 school / 2 personal split in each chapter package.
- Rotate ending types across broader_principle, self_directed_question, surprising_implication, cross_domain, common_trap, perspective_reframe.

## School-Setting Plan
- Use student startup teams, class project groups, campus publications, engineering labs, debate teams, and club leadership transitions.
- Avoid turning every school example into generic exam prep or GPA advice.

## Vocabulary Watchlist
- Keep OKRs, objectives, key results, CFRs, focus, alignment, commitment, tracking, stretch, transparency, and culture precise.
- Avoid startup-bro hype, generic productivity slogans, and fake-operational jargon.

## Motif / Callback Opportunities
- Chapter 1 should establish OKRs as a way to force priority under pressure, not as neutral management vocabulary.
- Chapters 2 and 3 should connect Grove's discipline to Intel's wartime execution logic.
- Chapters 4 through 14 should feel like superpowers applied through concrete organizational stories, not disconnected framework tiles.
- Chapter 15 should pivot from scorecards alone to the human-management layer of CFRs.
- Chapters 18 through 21 should show that OKRs affect culture only when operating behavior changes with them.
