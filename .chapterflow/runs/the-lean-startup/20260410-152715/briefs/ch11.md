Core Claim:
This chapter argues that adaptive organizations use feedback loops to improve process and quality without freezing learning speed. Ries uses the Five Whys as a root-cause and organizational learning tool, especially for recurring technical problems that often trace back to human systems. The chapter therefore reframes quality and process adaptation as supports for learning rather than as enemies of speed.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Chapter 10 explains how growth mechanisms work. Chapter 11 asks what kind of organization can keep learning while scaling. Its distinctive move is tying process and quality improvement to feedback-rich adaptation instead of to bureaucratic slowdown.

Author Logic Chain:

Then the author introduces the Five Whys as a root-cause learning tool.
Then the author ties recurring technical problems back to human systems and organizational adaptation.
