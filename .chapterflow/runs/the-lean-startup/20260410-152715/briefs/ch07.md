Core Claim:
This chapter argues that startups need innovation accounting rather than vanity metrics. Ries emphasizes actionable, accessible, auditable measurement, along with baselines, cohort analysis, and tuning the growth engine so the team can decide whether to pivot or persevere on real evidence. The chapter therefore reframes measurement as a steering tool, not a reporting performance.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Chapter 6 explains how to run MVP tests. Chapter 7 explains how to measure what those tests actually proved. Its distinctive move is replacing impressive numbers with evidence strong enough to support startup decisions.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author rejects vanity metrics as weak guidance.
Then the author introduces innovation accounting and the qualities of useful measurement.
Then the author ties baselines and tuned metrics to future pivot-or-persevere decisions.
