Core Claim:
This chapter argues that startups should decide whether to pivot or persevere based on validated learning. Ries defines a pivot as a structured course correction to test a new core hypothesis, not as random change. The chapter also reframes runway around how many meaningful pivots remain, so the central issue is disciplined decision making under uncertainty.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Chapter 7 explains how to measure startup learning honestly. Chapter 8 uses that learning to decide whether the current strategy deserves continuation or course correction. Its distinctive move is turning change into evidence-based strategy rather than emotional reaction.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author says validated learning should govern whether the startup keeps its current path.
Then the author defines a pivot as a structured course correction.
Then the author reframes runway around how many serious changes the startup can still test.
