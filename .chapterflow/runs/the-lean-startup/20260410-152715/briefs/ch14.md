Core Claim:
This final chapter widens the lean startup method from one company's toolkit into a broader practice the reader is invited to carry forward. Ries closes by treating the method as something to apply, spread, and continue in many organizational settings. The chapter therefore works as an invitation to practice rather than as a new conceptual chapter.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Chapter 13 compressed the book into an anti-waste epilogue. Chapter 14 turns from synthesis to invitation. Its distinctive move is widening the method into broader innovation practice without inventing new doctrine.
