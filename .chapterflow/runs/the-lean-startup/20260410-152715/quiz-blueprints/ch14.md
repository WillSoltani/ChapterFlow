# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter:
Join the Movement

Core concepts to test:
- practice over admiration
- broader application beyond one startup
- movement as continued disciplined learning
- adoption in schools, nonprofits, labs, and firms
- the closing invitation as application rather than doctrine

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: evaluate

Question plan:
- q01: recall what the invitation is asking readers to do
- q02: identify why the method should spread beyond one context
- q03: apply practice-over-admiration logic to a case
- q04: named scenario on a school or campus network
- q05: distinction between movement rhetoric and disciplined application
- q06: identify why agreement without use changes little
- q07: transfer to another organizational setting
- q08: judge a team that admired the method but never changed practice
- q09: deeper synthesis on why methods stall at admiration
- q10: final close on broader practice

Distractor notes:
- tempting wrong answer type 1: treat movement as vague enthusiasm
- tempting wrong answer type 2: assume the method belongs only to startups

Explanation diversity notes:
- avoid repeated openers
- each direct explanation must begin differently

CorrectIndex plan:
- keep distribution roughly balanced across 0 / 1 / 2
