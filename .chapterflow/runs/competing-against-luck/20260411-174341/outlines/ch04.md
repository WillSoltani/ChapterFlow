Show how a team starts looking for jobs deliberately by going toward live struggling situations and real substitutes instead of waiting for accidental insight.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Job hunting is not passive feedback collection. It is disciplined search for circumstance-bound struggle and the alternatives surrounding it.

Hard-Depth Tension:
If teams hunt through categories or wish lists instead of live situations, the search degrades before it begins.

Threshold Question:
Where does reliable evidence of a job actually show up before a team has named it cleanly?
