Chapter Promise:
Show how a team interprets what customers reveal indirectly by listening for forces, hesitation, habits, and tradeoffs inside a live situation rather than taking literal statements at face value.

Opening Move:
Open on the failure mode where a team reaches the field but still hears only polished feature language.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 move from literal statements to deeper hearing
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 explain circumstances and forces as the listening frame
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 bridge toward resumes of repeated hiring

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Hearing what customers do not say is not mind-reading. It is disciplined interpretation of push, pull, habit, and anxiety in a concrete circumstance.

Hard-Depth Tension:
If teams listen only to literal requests, they miss the forces that explain why buyers switch, stall, or stay put.

Threshold Question:
What evidence reveals the real job when the customer never states it cleanly?

- Hard: 5

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- Takeaway 1: literal customer language is incomplete evidence
- Takeaway 2: circumstances and forces explain what behavior means
- Takeaway 3: push, pull, habit, and anxiety shape what is heard
- Takeaway 4: interpretation must stay grounded in observable compromise and switching pressure
- Takeaway 5: better listening prepares the reader for resumes of repeated hires
