Chapter Promise:
Show how repeated hires create a usable resume for what a solution is trusted to do, while keeping the metaphor tied to evidence rather than branding.

Opening Move:
Open on the difference between one successful hire and a record of repeated hires that buyers have come to trust.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
A resume is the accumulated hiring record buyers build through repeated trust in a solution's ability to create certain progress, not a marketing claim or a literal HR document.

Hard-Depth Tension:
If teams confuse repeated hiring evidence with self-described positioning, they will overstate what the offering is really trusted to do.

Threshold Question:
What does a solution's repeated hiring history reveal that one interpreted hire cannot?

Takeaway Count Lock:
- Easy: 3
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 5

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- Takeaway 1: resumes are built through repeated hires
- Takeaway 2: repeated patterns reveal trusted progress, not just claimed identity
- Takeaway 3: a resume is narrower and more earned than positioning language
- Takeaway 4: one-off stories are weak evidence for a durable resume
- Takeaway 5: clear resumes prepare the transition to organizational integration
