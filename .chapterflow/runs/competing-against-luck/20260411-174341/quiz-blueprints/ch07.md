# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter: Integrating Around a Job
Core concepts to test:
- a clear job record creates an organizational integration requirement
- siloed optimization can weaken delivery of the progress buyers actually hire
- integrating around a job means coordinating decisions around consistent progress, not adopting a slogan
- internal convenience can conflict with the hired job
- the chapter sets up keeping focus on the job over time

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: evaluate

Question plan:
- q01: orienting distinction between knowing a job and integrating around it
- q02: what siloed optimization breaks
- q03: apply the chapter to a team whose departments optimize separately
- q04: named work scenario about a company aligning priorities around the earned job record
- q05: trap of confusing integration with messaging
- q06: limit case where a clear job still fails because no one owns coordinated delivery
- q07: school scenario about reducing fragmented handoffs
- q08: personal-choice transfer about coherent delivery versus polished fragments
- q09: deeper synthesis on why job insight must become organizational coordination
- q10: bridge toward Chapter 8 maintaining focus on the job

Distractor notes:
- tempting wrong answer type 1: assume naming the job alone solves delivery
- tempting wrong answer type 2: confuse integration with generic teamwork or culture language

Explanation diversity notes:
- avoid repeated openers
- each direct explanation must begin differently

CorrectIndex plan:
