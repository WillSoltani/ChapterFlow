# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter: Building Your Resume
Core concepts to test:
- a solution resume is built through repeated hires, not one isolated success
- repeated hiring patterns reveal what progress buyers trust the solution to deliver
- resume logic is narrower and more earned than positioning language
- one-off stories are weak evidence for a durable resume
- the chapter sets up organizational integration around jobs next

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: evaluate

Question plan:
- q01: orienting distinction between one hire and a resume
- q02: what repeated hiring reveals
- q03: apply resume logic to a product with mixed feature claims
- q04: named work scenario about a team discovering the resume is narrower than its messaging
- q05: trap of treating positioning as proof
- q06: limit case where repeated use is still too thin to define a stable resume
- q07: school scenario about a service learning what students reliably trust it for
- q08: personal-choice transfer from one good experience to repeated rehiring
- q09: deeper synthesis on why resumes are earned through repeated evidence
- q10: bridge toward Chapter 7 organizational integration

Distractor notes:
- tempting wrong answer type 1: assume one vivid story defines the resume
- tempting wrong answer type 2: confuse the product's message with its earned hiring history

Explanation diversity notes:
- avoid repeated openers
- each direct explanation must begin differently

CorrectIndex plan:
- keep distribution roughly balanced across 0 / 1 / 2
