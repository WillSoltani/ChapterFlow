- Edition family: first-edition English trade family

## Official Positioning Notes

Google Books preview metadata describes the book as an attempt to move innovation from hit-or-miss toward a process grounded in understanding the job customers hire a product or service to do. The same metadata states that the authors reject the idea that understanding customer profiles alone is enough and instead center customer jobs.

Harvard Business School's 2017 innovation-books note describes the book as taking the assumption that innovation is often hit-or-miss and answering it with Jobs Theory.

The HBR reprint metadata for "Know Your Customers' Jobs to Be Done" confirms the same four-author team and the same causal-demand framing in September 2016, immediately adjacent to the book's release window.

## Framework Support

Christensen Institute's "Forces of Progress" graphic defines the push of the situation and the pull of the new solution as forces compelling change, while habit of the present and anxiety of the new solution work against change. This framework is safe support for chapters dealing with customer switching and adoption friction.
