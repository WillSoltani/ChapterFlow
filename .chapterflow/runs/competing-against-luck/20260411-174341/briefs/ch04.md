Core Claim:
The fourth chapter shifts from recognizing jobs to actively finding them. The frozen bundle safely supports a narrower claim that once jobs can be seen in the wild, teams need a disciplined way to go search for circumstance-bound demand rather than waiting for lucky anecdotes.
