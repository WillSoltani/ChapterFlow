Core Claim:
The sixth chapter has to turn clearer listening into an account of repeated hiring. The frozen bundle safely supports a narrower claim that once teams can hear jobs more clearly, they can see how a solution builds a kind of resume through the jobs it gets hired to do repeatedly. The chapter therefore has to frame a resume as accumulated evidence about reliable progress, not as a literal employment metaphor or a marketing slogan.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Chapter 5 teaches how to hear the forces and indirect evidence inside one hiring situation. Chapter 6 asks what happens when those situations repeat often enough that a solution starts to carry a recognizable record of what progress buyers trust it to deliver.

Author Logic Chain:
First the authors move from one interpreted hire to a sequence of repeated hires.
Then they show that a solution's \"resume\" is built from the jobs it has reliably been hired to do, not just from feature claims.
Then they prepare the book to move into organizational implications, where firms must align around serving jobs consistently.

Required Anchors:
- The chapter title supports a metaphor of accumulated hiring history; the friction is teams may over-literalize it into branding or credential language, the consequence is the chapter must keep the metaphor tied to repeated job evidence.
- Chapter 5 makes demand logic clearer at the single-hire level; the friction is isolated interviews still look unique, the consequence is Chapter 6 has to show what becomes visible across many hires.
- The section position supports a move from interpretation toward durable patterns that can guide later organizational design; the friction is teams may still chase one-off stories, the consequence is the chapter has to privilege repeated hiring evidence.
