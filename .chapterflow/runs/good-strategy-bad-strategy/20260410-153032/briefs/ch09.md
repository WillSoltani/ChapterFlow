Frameworks or Terms Introduced:
Chain-link system: a system whose overall performance is capped by its weakest indispensable link.
Limiting link: the necessary part whose weakness constrains the whole system.
Compensation error: the mistaken belief that strength elsewhere can offset failure at a critical link.

Specific Applications:
- identifying bottlenecks in systems with indispensable steps
- distinguishing weakest-link systems from broad average-performance systems
- testing whether a next objective is blocked by one failing link

Common Misreadings:
