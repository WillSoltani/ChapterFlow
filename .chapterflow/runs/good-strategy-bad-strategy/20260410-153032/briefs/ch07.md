Core Claim:
Once a strategist has identified a source of power, the next task is to use leverage by concentrating force at the point where it will change the outcome most. On the support available in this run, Chapter 7 should present leverage as focused application rather than broad effort. The chapter's practical lesson is that force spread across too many fronts loses value, while force aimed at the right pivot point can outperform much larger but diffused effort.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This chapter is distinct because it turns discovered power into application. Chapter 6 located where force sits. Chapter 7 asks how a strategist should concentrate action to exploit that force.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author shifts from finding power to applying it.
Then he frames leverage as concentrated force at a point of high effect.
Then he contrasts leverage with broad or head-on effort that wastes strength.
Then he prepares the later chapters on proximate objectives and design by showing that force must be aimed, not merely accumulated.
