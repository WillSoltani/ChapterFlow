On the support available in this run, Chapter 6 should present sources of power as recurring conditions or asymmetries that make some moves stronger than others. Frameworks or Terms Introduced:
Sources of power: recurring conditions that make a strategy stronger or more effective.
Durable power: an advantage or position that keeps mattering beyond a one-off surprise.
Leverage bridge: the move from finding power to applying concentrated force.

Specific Applications:
- testing whether an apparent advantage is durable or just a temporary trick
- separating force-generating conditions from raw effort or aspiration
- preparing a later leverage discussion by locating where power actually sits

Common Misreadings:
- treating any success factor as a true source of power
- assuming more effort automatically creates strategic power
- treating clever surprise as durable advantage without checking whether it persists
