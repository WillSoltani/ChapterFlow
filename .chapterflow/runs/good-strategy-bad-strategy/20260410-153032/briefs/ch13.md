Frameworks or Terms Introduced:
Dynamics: the strategic logic of movement, sequence, and reaction over time.
Opening: an opportunity created because the situation is changing rather than standing still.
Trap path: a sequence of moves that looks favorable early but becomes costly as the system reacts.

Specific Applications:
- judging how a move changes later options rather than only current position
- using timing or sequence to create an opening before rivals or constraints catch up
- spotting when momentum or expansion is creating a future trap instead of a future advantage

Common Misreadings:
- treating dynamics as generic speed rather than strategic movement over time
- assuming the current position tells the whole story without considering reactions and sequence
- confusing motion with progress when the path is actually worsening the position
