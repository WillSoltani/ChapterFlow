# Style Memory

- Write like a sharp human explainer with taste: specific, concrete, compressed, and authored.
- Teach the idea directly. Avoid narrating the artifact, chapter, or workflow.
- Open from pressure, conflict, event, or a live question, not thesis-first summary.
- Every paragraph needs a distinct job from the ledger: anchor, mechanism, tension, limit, implication, bridge.
- Stay narrow when support is thin. No invented facts, quotes, studies, mechanisms, or anecdotes.
- No em dashes. No generic self-help cadence. No corporate or textbook voice.
- Cut contamination, brief leakage, validator language, and reusable template tails.
- Easy: one pictureable opening, one clean model, one usable implication, grade 8-9, exactly 3 takeaways.
- Medium: mechanism plus application, grade 10-11, 5-6 takeaways, singular `selfCheckPrompt`.
- Hard: boundary/contradiction/cost/synthesis, grade 12, 5-7 takeaways, exactly 2 `selfCheckPrompts`, preserve threshold question.
- Tone differences must be functional:
  - gentle lowers resistance
  - direct clarifies mechanism
  - competitive sharpens stakes without sounding theatrical
- Examples must be socially realistic, vivid, chapter-specific, and load-bearing.
- Quiz questions should test judgment in situations with plausible distractors and distinct explanations.
