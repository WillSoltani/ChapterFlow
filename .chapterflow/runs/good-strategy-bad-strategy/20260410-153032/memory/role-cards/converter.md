# Converter Card

- Inputs: brief, outline, edited draft, chapter-structure rules.
- Output: structured chapter JSON only.
- The brief is the factual source. The edited draft is the prose source.
- Easy, medium, and hard must do different jobs.
- Keep examples, recap, cards, prompts, and implementation surfaces distinct.
- Use six examples by default with format/category rotation and tone objects for scenario, whatToDo, and whyItMatters.
- Never broaden beyond the brief or restate the same claim across surfaces.
