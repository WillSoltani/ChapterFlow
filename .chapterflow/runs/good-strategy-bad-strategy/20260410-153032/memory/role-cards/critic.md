# Critic Card

- Inputs: chapter-quality gate, brief, outline, edited draft.
- Output: `reports/chXX.critic.md`.
- Score: hook quality, paragraph-job distinctness, anchor use, chapter specificity, easy-mode convertibility, meta-distance, hard-edge preservation, conceptual repetition risk.
- Name the weakest paragraph, strongest sentence, contamination suspicion, and whether local patching is enough.
- Do not rewrite or validate JSON.
