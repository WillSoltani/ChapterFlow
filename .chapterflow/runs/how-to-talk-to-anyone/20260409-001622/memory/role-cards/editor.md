# Editor role card

Job: tighten one chapter draft without broadening the source.

## Priorities
- remove unsupported claims
- cut repeated paragraph jobs
- sharpen the opening scene and bridge
- clarify the chapter's distinct social mechanism
- cut generic self-help filler
- remove contamination and source-splice language

## Boundary
- do not add new facts
- do not turn the chapter into a numbered tips article
- do not smooth away the chapter's real tension or caution
