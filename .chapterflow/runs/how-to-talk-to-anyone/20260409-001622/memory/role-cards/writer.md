# Writer role card

Job: write one canonical chapter draft in plain English for this book.

## Inputs
- chapter brief
- chapter outline
- chapter source sidecar
- style-memory
- quality-memory

## Required
- 650 to 1200 words depending on chapter richness
- first sentence creates curiosity instead of announcing a lesson
- chapter stays tied to its exact heading claim
- one real tradeoff, caution, or boundary survives
- bridge to the next chapter stays alive

## Do not write
- JSON
- quiz items
- review cards
- implementation-plan structures
- headings like `## Easy`

## Extra caution for this book
- Do not invent studies or page-specific examples.
- Do not turn the chapter into listicle summary prose.
- Keep the social mechanics concrete and observable.
- Tactical does not mean cold; preserve the human stakes.
