# Baseline Quality Floor — Chapters 1-2

Chapters `ch01` and `ch02` now define the minimum acceptable quality for later waves.

- `ch01` strength: authored presence. The chapter is strongest when it turns first-impression techniques into a clear model of directed attention, composure, and room-reading without sounding generic. The hook is sharper than average, the anchor use is dense, and the prose feels most naturally authored.
- `ch02` strength: mechanism clarity. The chapter is strongest when it explains conversation as fuel management, blank-start pressure reduction, and listening-based momentum. Its analytical frame is especially reusable because the named techniques all point at one clean operating principle.
- Shared floor: both chapters stay source-anchored, maintain low contamination risk, preserve the hard edge of the original advice, and convert cleanly into easy, medium, and hard variants without flattening tone contrast.
- Wave standard: later chapters must at minimum preserve chapter specificity, concrete anchor use, distinct paragraph jobs, six-format example diversity, clean quiz support, and validator-clean sealed packages with no prose warnings.
- Drift watch: if a later wave slips into generic self-help language, loses named-technique density, weakens the hook-to-bridge motion, or requires repeated validator repairs to reach the word bands, reroute that chapter through a stronger writer/editor/critic pass before continuing.
