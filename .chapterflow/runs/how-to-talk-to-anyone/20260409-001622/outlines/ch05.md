Chapter Promise:
This chapter will make the reader see that rapport grows when people feel their style and inner language are being recognized rather than merely heard.

Opening Move:
Open on the strange speed with which some conversations start feeling like "us" while others remain politely separate.

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: name the real problem: friendliness is not the same as likeness
- P2: use Copyclass and Echoing to show how reflected style creates rapport
- P3: use Potent Imaging and the Empathizers to show how vivid empathy deepens recognition
- P4: use Anatomically Correct Empathizers to keep empathy embodied and specific
- P5: use Premature We and Instant History to show how shared identity accelerates bonding
- P6: preserve the limit that likeness can become imitation or manipulation, then bridge to praise

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter's mechanism is reflected recognition. People feel closer when their rhythms, imagery, and identity cues come back to them in a natural rather than copied form.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must preserve the line between affirming resonance and strategic imitation. Hard depth should ask when rapport moves stop feeling human and start feeling stolen.

Threshold Question:
How do you sound like someone is with another person without sounding like you borrowed their skin?
