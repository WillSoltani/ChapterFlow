Chapter Promise:
This chapter will make the reader see why credible praise can elevate someone while flattery often exposes the praiser instead.

Opening Move:
Open on the difference between a compliment that lands and changes the room, and one that makes everyone slightly brace.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P2-P4
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P4-P5

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: name the real problem: positive words can either elevate or cheapen depending on credibility
- P2: use the indirect-recognition cluster to show why praise often gains force through social travel and implication
- P3: use killer compliments, little strokes, and knee-jerk wow to show how direct praise works when it feels specific and naturally timed
- P4: use boomeranging to show how praise reflects valued traits back to the person
- P5: use the tombstone frame to show the deepest form of identity-level recognition
- P6: preserve the limit that praise becomes flattery when motive gets louder than observation, then bridge to the phone chapter

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter's mechanism is credible recognition. Praise works when it feels observed, specific, and socially believable rather than oily or self-serving.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must preserve the line between elevating recognition and visible handling. Hard depth should ask when praise starts feeling like pressure instead of appreciation.

Threshold Question:
How do you let admiration feel like truth instead of leverage?
