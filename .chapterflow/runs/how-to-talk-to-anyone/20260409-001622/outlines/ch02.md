Chapter Promise:
This chapter will make the reader see that good conversation is carried by social fuel, not by constant originality.

Opening Move:
Open on the relief of getting through hello and then realizing the interaction can still die if no one gives it usable material.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P2-P4
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P4-P5
- anchor 4 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: name the real problem after hello: conversational dead air and pressure to invent
- P2: use The Mood Match to show that conversations start by entering the other person's emotional weather
- P3: use Whatzit, Whoozat?, and the Never the Naked cluster to show how context creates easy hooks
- P4: use Word Detective and Swivelling Spotlight to show how listening creates more material than performance does
- P5: use Parroting and Encore! to show how expansion prompts keep the other person talking naturally
- P6: preserve the limit that too much prompting becomes mechanical, then bridge to the next chapter's concern with status and polish

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter's mechanism is conversational fuel. Talk flows when one person notices hooks, offers context, and invites expansion instead of demanding originality from a blank start.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must preserve the line between generous follow-up and visible technique. Hard depth should ask when conversation support starts sounding processed.

Threshold Question:
How do you keep a conversation alive without making the other person feel worked?

Takeaway Count Lock:
- Easy: 3
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 5

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- Takeaway 1: conversation dies when people must generate from nothing
- Takeaway 2: mood matching lowers friction early
- Takeaway 3: context hooks reduce blank-start pressure
- Takeaway 4: spotlighting and parroting create expansion
- Takeaway 5: real interest must stay visible or the method turns mechanical
