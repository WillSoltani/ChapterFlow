Chapter Promise:
This chapter will make the reader see how a crowded room becomes easier once they treat movement, approach choice, openness, and recall as deliberate social tasks.

Opening Move:
Open on the difference between drifting through a crowded room and entering it with enough intention that faces, openings, and follow-through stop feeling random.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P2-P4
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P4-P6
- anchor 4 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: name the real problem: crowded rooms punish passivity and reward deliberate attention before approach
- P2: use munching or mingling and rubberneck the room to show why availability and scanning matter before conversation starts
- P3: use chooser-not-choosee and come-hither hands to show how approach choice and visible openness change the room's social traffic
- P4: use tracking and business card dossier to show how memory turns brief contacts into actual relationships
- P5: use eyeball selling to show how real interest is carried by visible attention, not just words or card exchange
- P6: preserve the limit that room-working becomes cold when it sounds transactional, then bridge to the final chapter's danger-and-tact problem

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter's mechanism is intentional room navigation. Strong room-workers read, choose, invite, remember, and follow through instead of wandering or waiting.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must preserve the line between deliberate room movement and social harvesting. Hard depth should ask when strategic movement starts making people feel processed.

Threshold Question:
How do you turn a noisy room into a sequence of real connections without making the room feel like a checklist?

Takeaway Count Lock:

- Hard: 5

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- Takeaway 1: availability changes who can approach you
- Takeaway 2: room-reading should happen before movement
- Takeaway 3: choosing whom to approach is stronger than passive waiting
- Takeaway 4: tracking and recall make crowded-room interest believable
- Takeaway 5: the whole method fails when curiosity disappears and transaction takes over
