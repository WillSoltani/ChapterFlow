## Frozen bundle summary
The official bundle supports a book organized into nine large parts rather than ninety-two full narrative chapters. Each part clusters named communication techniques around a specific social domain. The book's official positioning is practical and tactical: it focuses on first impressions, conversational recovery, status signaling, social belonging, rapport, praise, phone communication, party and networking behavior, and tact in delicate situations.
