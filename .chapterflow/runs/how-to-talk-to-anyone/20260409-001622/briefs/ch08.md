Core Claim:
This chapter argues that crowded social rooms reward deliberate movement, visible openness, good approach choices, and active recall rather than passive waiting or random chatter. The named techniques in this part all point toward one larger mechanism: a strong room-worker turns a noisy event into a sequence of intentional connections instead of letting the room choose for them.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the book's room-working chapter. Its distinct job is to show how entrances, approach strategy, invitation signals, and follow-through make a reception or party feel navigable and socially productive.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter shows that effective room-work starts before you speak, with whether you stay available and whether you read the room before moving.
Then it shows that choosing whom to approach and signaling openness changes your odds more than waiting to be chosen.
Then it shows that tracking details, using a simple dossier mindset, and selling with the eyes make future contact feel real instead of forgettable.
