Core Claim:
This chapter argues that rapport deepens when people hear their own rhythms, emotional language, imagery, and identity cues reflected back with real care. The named techniques in this part all point toward one larger mechanism: people relax when they feel recognized in their own style, but the effect breaks the instant reflection starts feeling copied, rushed, or strategic.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the book's likeness chapter. Its distinct job is to show how shared wording, empathic phrasing, and early identity signals make people feel "we" rather than merely "you and me."

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter shows that likeness begins with matching style and echoing key language rather than with generic friendliness.
Then it shows that empathizers, potent imaging, and anatomically correct empathizers make understanding feel more specific and embodied.
Then it shows that premature "we" and instant history can accelerate bonding only when they arise from real overlap rather than from fake intimacy.
