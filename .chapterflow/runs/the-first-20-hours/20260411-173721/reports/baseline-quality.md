# Baseline Quality

Chapters 1 and 2 now form the baseline quality floor for the rest of the run.

## Chapter 1 strengths
- strong opening pressure anchored in overloaded adult life
- clear mastery-versus-competence distinction
- visible threshold question around strategic realism versus excuse-making
- narrow enough to respect thin source support

## Chapter 2 strengths
- clear practice-architecture spine
- coherent sequence across the ten principles instead of listicle drift
- strong anti-theater boundary around setup, tools, and feedback
- examples stay operational and chapter-specific

## Baseline floor to preserve
- pressure-first openings
- chapter-specific mechanism rather than generic self-improvement prose
- hard depth must introduce a real limit or failure mode
- support surfaces must do different jobs instead of replaying the same sentence
- method chapters must avoid productivity sloganeering

## Watch items for later waves
- do not let example chapters become generic "practice works" summaries
- keep source-thin chapters narrower rather than compensating with invention
- watch for repeated reinforcement tails across recap, review cards, and implementation plans
