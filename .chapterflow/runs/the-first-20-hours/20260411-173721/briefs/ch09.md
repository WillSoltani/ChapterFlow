Core Claim:
Chapter 9 applies the rapid skill acquisition method to windsurfing. The frozen support frames the chapter through environment-shaped practice and staged isolation of subskills. The chapter's job is to show that the method still works when the learner cannot control all conditions directly, provided the skill is broken into smaller trainable components and practice respects equipment, weather, water, and risk.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the only core application chapter where the environment itself becomes part of the curriculum. The learner is not just managing complexity or repetition. The learner must practice in ways that respect unstable conditions and isolate subskills without pretending risk disappears.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author shows that windsurfing cannot be learned honestly by treating the whole live environment as one undifferentiated challenge.
Then he isolates smaller trainable parts such as stance, sail handling, balance, launch sequence, and recovery under safer conditions.
Then he concludes that progress comes from staged exposure that matches the learner's current control while still respecting weather, equipment, and environmental limits.
