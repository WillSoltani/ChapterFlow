Core Claim:
Chapter 8 applies the rapid skill acquisition method to ukulele. The frozen support frames the chapter through isolated repetition, deliberate narrowing, and public performance readiness. The chapter's job is to show that a daunting performance skill becomes learnable when the learner narrows the target, drills a few essential movements repeatedly, and prepares for a real public standard without pretending to master the whole instrument first.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the first case chapter where the learner is moving toward a public performance threshold rather than private competence alone. Its distinct pressure is the temptation to treat the whole instrument as the target instead of a narrow playable subset.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author shows how a musical instrument looks larger than the learner needs if every chord, strum, and song is treated as equally urgent.
Then he uses deliberate narrowing and isolated repetition to make the skill trainable.
Then he concludes that public readiness can come from a small reliable repertoire rather than from broad mastery.
