# Style Memory

## Voice core
- Write like a sharp human explainer with taste.
- Prioritize specificity, concrete detail, compression, vividness, and honest limits.
- Teach the idea directly instead of narrating the chapter.

## Non-negotiable prose constraints
- No invented facts, quotes, mechanisms, anecdotes, or pseudo-science.
- No em dashes.
- No filler abstractions, corporate tone, textbook tone, fake profundity, or sloganized severity.
- No repeated sentence, repeated paragraph job, repeated closing beat, or repeated template tail inside a chapter package.
- No thesis-first openings in breakdowns unless the brief absolutely requires framing first.
- No contamination phrases or brief / outline / validator language leaking into reader-facing prose.

## Readability contract
- Easy: grade 8-9, one pictureable situation early, one clean model, one usable implication.
- Medium: grade 10-11, mechanism plus application, each paragraph adds a new function.
- Hard: grade 12, adds boundary, contradiction, cost, failure mode, hidden structure, or synthesis. Hard cannot be medium extended.

## Tone contract
- Gentle lowers resistance and warms the entry.
- Direct gives the cleanest mechanism and decision line.
- Competitive sharpens stakes without sounding theatrical, chest-thumping, or poster-ready.
- Tone variants must differ in function, not adjective swaps.

## Converter surface jobs
- keyTakeawayCard distills the core move.
- reviewCards test retrieval and must rotate rhetorical shape.
- recap.retrieve tests recall, not summary.
- recap.connect ties mechanism to consequence.
- recap.preview asks for prediction, not a teaser.
- implementationPlan must name a chapter-specific action, friction, or checkpoint.
- moreDetails must deepen instead of restating.

## Example contract
- Six examples by default.
- One use each of the six canonical formats.
- Exactly 2 work, 2 school, 2 personal.
- Scenario, whatToDo, and whyItMatters must all be tone objects.
- Scenarios must be vivid, concrete, and chapter-specific.

## Quiz contract
- Exactly 10 questions, exactly 3 choices each.
- Prefer judgment in situations over recall.
- Distractors must be tempting.
- Explanations must teach the mechanism or failure mode, not mini-recap the breakdown.

## Source discipline
- Stay paraphrase-first.
- Use only what the frozen source bundle supports.
- If support is thin, stay narrower and more concrete rather than pretending richness.
