# Critic Role Card

Score the edited draft before structure.

Must report:
- hook quality
- paragraph-job distinctness
- anchor use
- chapter specificity
- easy-mode convertibility
- meta-distance
- hard-edge preservation
- conceptual repetition risk

Also report:
- weakest paragraph
- strongest sentence
- contamination or source-splice suspicion
- whether local patching is enough or global reroute is needed

Do not rewrite the draft. Do not validate JSON.
