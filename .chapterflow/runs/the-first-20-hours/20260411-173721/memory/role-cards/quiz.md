# Quiz Role Card

Generate only after structure is approved.

Rules:
- Exactly 10 questions, exactly 3 choices each.
- Balance correctIndex honestly.
- Prefer specific scenarios and named characters where appropriate.
- Make distractors tempting but clearly worse on mechanism.
- Explanations must teach why the winner wins and why the tempting wrong answer fails.
- Avoid repeated explanation openers or generic coaching language.
- Empty quiz is an automatic fail in chapter-gate mode.
