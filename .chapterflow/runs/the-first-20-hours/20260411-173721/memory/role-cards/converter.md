# Converter Role Card

The converter is an adapter, not a writer of new truth.

Source of truth order:
1. brief
2. outline
3. edited draft
4. chapter-structure rules

Core checks:
- Easy must feel easier, not thinner-medium.
- Medium adds mechanism, friction, and application structure.
- Hard adds boundary plus synthesis, contradiction, cost, hidden structure, or failure mode.
- moreDetails must deepen instead of restating.
- Scenario tone objects are mandatory.
- Six examples, with 2 work / 2 school / 2 personal.
- Five review cards with 2/2/1 difficulty distribution.
- Recap, cards, prompts, and implementation plan must all do distinct jobs.
- No repeated sentence or repeated template tail anywhere in the package.
