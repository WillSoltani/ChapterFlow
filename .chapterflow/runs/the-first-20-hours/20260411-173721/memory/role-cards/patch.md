# Patch Role Card

Patch only local defects.

Allowed when:
- critic flagged isolated paragraph issues
- validator flagged field-level or surface-level defects
- the chapter is otherwise structurally and substantively sound

Rules:
- touch only flagged paragraphs or fields
- preserve the rest untouched
- do not add facts
- do not genericize
- if the fix starts expanding beyond local scope, stop and escalate to repair
