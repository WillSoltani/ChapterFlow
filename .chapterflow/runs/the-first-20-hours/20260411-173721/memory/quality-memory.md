# Quality Memory

## Chapter gate minimum
- Required artifacts: brief, outline, quiz blueprint, source sidecar txt/json, canonical draft, edited draft, critic report, structured chapter json, quiz json, validation report, validated chapter json, validated review-package json, reading metrics json.
- Critic threshold: at least 10/12.
- Fail immediately on contamination phrases, plain-string scenarios, identical tone objects, empty quiz in generate mode, source-sidecar mismatch, or prose-audit hard fails.

## Writer / editor quality bar
- Opening must create curiosity from pressure, event, conflict, or a live question.
- Every paragraph needs one primary role: anchor, mechanism, tension, limit, implication, or bridge.
- Use at least two strong anchors when source support exists.
- Keep one real tension, limit, or failure mode alive.
- Make the chapter unmistakably this chapter, not a reusable self-improvement essay.

## Auto-fail signals
- Generic enough to fit another chapter.
- Paragraph-job repetition.
- Repeated sentence or repeated ending beat.
- Hard depth that only rephrases medium.
- moreDetails that merely paraphrase.
- Implementation plan, recap, cards, or prompts that sound reusable across chapters.
- Invented authority or unsupported detail.

## Validation posture
- Fix mechanics directly.
- Do not silently flatten prose to force a pass.
- Escalate to repair if specificity, tone differentiation, scenario quality, contamination cleanup, or support-surface distinctness is weak.
- Review wrapper must contain one full validated chapter, not a partial or loose blob.

## Wave guard
- Chapters 1 and 2 establish baseline quality.
- Continue in waves of 2 only after each active chapter fully clears gate and continuity seal.
- Run artifact guard and quality sentry after each completed wave.
