Chapter Promise:
By the end of this chapter, the reader can use STATE to put a hard message into the pool without collapsing into accusation, vagueness, or fake openness.

Opening Move:
Open with a project-milestone conversation where the speaker knows something is slipping but needs to say it without turning the concern into a verdict.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P3
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P4-P5
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: project conversation where the hard message is real but the wrong opening would trigger defense
- P2: explain Share your facts and why observable ground lowers heat without lowering honesty
- P3: explain Tell your story and why owning interpretation matters
- P4: explain Ask for others' paths and Talk tentatively as the moves that keep the message discussable
- P5: explain Encourage testing and the failure modes of fake invitation or early collapse
- P6: school or personal anchor showing STATE in a second setting
- P7: preserve the limit around genuine openness and bridge to listening for the other person's path

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter's non-generic mechanism is that the order matters. Facts create an entry point, story creates meaning, invitation opens the pool, and tentativeness plus testing keep the story from hardening into a verdict.

Hard-Depth Tension:
How does the reader stay candid about a real concern while signaling that their interpretation is still open to challenge?

Threshold Question:
What keeps a hard message honest without turning it into a prosecuting speech?

Takeaway Count Lock:
- Easy: 3 (always)
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 5

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- Takeaway 1: facts first lowers heat without erasing the message
- Takeaway 2: story must be owned as story, not smuggled in as fact
- Takeaway 3: asking for others' paths must be genuine
- Takeaway 4: tentativeness protects dialogue while preserving conviction
- Takeaway 5: encouraging testing requires real openness to correction
