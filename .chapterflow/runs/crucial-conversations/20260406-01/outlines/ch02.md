Chapter Promise:
By the end of this chapter, the reader can use the pool metaphor to diagnose why a conversation failed and can distinguish real dialogue from surface politeness.

Opening Move:
Open inside a hybrid meeting that looks efficient while the people with the most relevant objections stop contributing.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P3
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P4-P5
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: hybrid meeting scene where silence is mistaken for agreement
- P2: introduce the pool metaphor and why fuller contribution improves decisions
- P3: show that weak commitment later often begins with withheld information earlier
- P4: identify withholding as the enemy and safety as its usual cause
- P5: distinguish dialogue from politeness or venting
- P6: show the retrospective diagnostic line and why the metaphor sticks
- P7: bridge to the internal question of what the speaker is protecting when they hold back

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter's non-generic distinction is between a room that sounds calm and a room that is still filling with useful meaning.

Hard-Depth Tension:
How should someone think about the pool when they know the room is unsafe enough that contributing carries real cost?

Threshold Question:
If the pool shrinks whenever safety drops, what has to happen inside the speaker before they can keep contributing honestly?
