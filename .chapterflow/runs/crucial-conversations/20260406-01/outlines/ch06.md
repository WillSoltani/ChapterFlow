Chapter Promise:
By the end of this chapter, the reader can diagnose whether safety broke because of missing purpose or missing respect and can choose between apology, Contrasting, and CRIB without turning any of them into scripts.

Opening Move:
Open with a co-founder fight over hiring where the strategy argument is already hot and the missing shared purpose has not yet been named.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P3
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P4-P5
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: co-founder hiring fight that sounds like budget but is really a shared-purpose failure
- P2: introduce Mutual Purpose and Mutual Respect as the two conditions of safety
- P3: explain how to diagnose which condition is missing
- P4: explain apology as the repair when the speaker actually caused damage
- P5: explain Contrasting through a manager/direct-report misread
- P6: explain CRIB as a strategy-level repair that climbs to shared purpose
- P7: preserve the limit around sincerity and power asymmetry, then bridge to speaking hard truth

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The non-generic mechanism is diagnosing whether the break is in purpose or respect and matching the repair to that exact failure instead of reaching for one soothing phrase.

Hard-Depth Tension:
How does the reader use apology, Contrasting, and CRIB sincerely without pretending they can repair every unsafe room, especially across power gaps?

Threshold Question:
If a conversation is unsafe, what tells you whether the person thinks you are against them or simply beneath them?
