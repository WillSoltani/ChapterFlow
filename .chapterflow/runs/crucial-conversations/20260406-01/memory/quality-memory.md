# Quality Memory

- Factual truth comes from the brief and source sidecar. Do not add unsupported facts, quotes, or mechanisms.
- Prose comes before structure. Weak prose does not get converted just because the schema is waiting.
- Contamination is a hard failure. No outline language, validator language, calibration notes, or source-splice text in reader-facing content.
- No bulk generation. Scripts may slice, lint, compare, assemble, and report; they may not author prose, examples, quiz explanations, or learning surfaces.
- Scenario tone policy is required: `scenario`, `whatToDo`, and `whyItMatters` must all be tone objects with materially different variants.
- Easy has exactly 3 takeaways and no extra depth fields. Medium uses singular `selfCheckPrompt`. Hard uses exactly 2 `selfCheckPrompts` plus a `predictionPrompt`.
- Patch locally where possible. Escalate to repair only when the failure is truly prose quality, specificity, or contamination.
- Chapter gate and release gate are different. This run stops after validated Chapter 1 and waits for approval.
