Core Claim:
The framework does not disappear when the room gets unequal, hostile, asynchronous, or identity-charged. Chapter 10 argues that the same underlying move still applies: restore safety, then speak honestly. What changes is timing, order, scope, and emphasis. The chapter closes by pushing the reader out of abstraction and toward one or two specific avoided conversations.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This chapter handles the reader's exemptions. It is the place where "yeah, but my case is special" gets answered without pretending the special conditions are unreal. It also carries the book's strongest ethical warning: the framework fails when used to manipulate, extract compliance, or win.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author names the tough cases readers use to exempt themselves.
Then the author insists the framework remains the same while order and emphasis shift.
Then the author applies that claim to power gaps, subject-shifting, chronic complaint, text, and identity difference.
Then the author ends by requiring the reader to plan and have a real avoided conversation.

Required Anchors:
- Power-gap conversation: a student or employee has to raise a hard issue with someone who controls access, grades, or advancement.
- Text-based conflict: a message thread keeps hardening because tone and safety cues are stripped out.
- Hostile or evasive conversation: the other person either keeps changing the subject or keeps complaining without moving.

Specific Applications:

- moving a text conflict back toward conditions the framework can support
- planning one real avoided conversation instead of admiring the framework from a distance

Common Misreadings:
- Tough cases require a completely different framework.
- Honesty under power difference means bluntness.
- The method works as long as the wording sounds calm, even if the speaker is trying to win.

Counterarguments or Limits:
Power gaps can narrow how direct the first move can be. Text strips away climate cues and may require moving to a richer channel. Some hostile people will still refuse dialogue. Identity and cultural difference increase the cost of careless assumptions. None of those realities erase the framework, but they do change how carefully the reader has to apply it.

Moral Complexity:
The chapter must preserve that power, identity, and hostility are real conditions rather than rhetorical decoration. It also has to insist that the framework only works when the speaker is genuinely open to being changed rather than merely sounding reasonable.
