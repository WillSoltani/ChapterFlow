Core Claim:
The reverse of stating your own path is drawing out the other person's path when they are hiding it, compressing it, or escalating around it. Chapter 8 teaches AMPP to bring their meaning into the room and ABC to work with it once it arrives.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This chapter is not passive listening advice. It teaches active recovery of withheld meaning and then shows how to handle genuine disagreement without shrinking the conflict into "yes, but."

Author Logic Chain:
First the author teaches AMPP as the way to draw out another person's facts, feelings, and concerns.
Then the author explains Ask, Mirror, Paraphrase, and Prime in order of increasing intervention.
Then the author teaches ABC once the other person's path is visible.
Then the author shows how Agree, Build, and Compare shrink false disagreement while preserving true disagreement.

Required Anchors:
- Manager and disengaged direct report: one-word answers become a real conversation after asking and mirroring.
- School or lab tension: paraphrase shows understanding before disagreement is added.
- Personal conversation: priming is used carefully as a humble guess rather than a forced story.

Frameworks or Terms Introduced:
- AMPP: Ask, Mirror, Paraphrase, Prime.
- Ask: a genuine open question.
- Mirror: name the emotion that seems visible but unspoken.
- Paraphrase: play back the meaning in your own words.
- Prime: offer a humble guess about what the other person may be afraid to say.
- ABC: Agree, Build, Compare.

Specific Applications:
- drawing out a disengaged report in a 1:1
- helping a quiet teammate or student say the real concern
- disagreeing after listening without snapping back into "yes, but"

Common Misreadings:
- Mirroring is not amateur therapy.
- Priming is not putting words in the other person's mouth.
- Agree does not mean surrendering your own view.
- Compare is not a prettier version of "you're wrong."

Counterarguments or Limits:
Some people genuinely will not engage, some emotions are easy to misread, and priming can become intrusive if offered arrogantly. The chapter should preserve that AMPP escalates in risk and should only move upward as needed. It should also keep clear that listening does not require pretending all disagreements are small.

Moral Complexity:
The chapter should keep listening from becoming control. Priming is only ethical when offered as a tentative guess that the other person can reject.
