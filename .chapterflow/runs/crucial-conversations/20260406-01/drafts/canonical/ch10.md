Yusuf keeps rehearsing the sentence in the hallway outside office hours, and every version sounds wrong. If he speaks plainly, the professor may hear disrespect. If he stays vague, the grading policy he thinks is unfair will stay untouched. That is the feeling Chapter 10 starts with. Not the easy case where both people are thoughtful and equally free. The hard case. The reader's private objection to the whole book: yeah, but my situation is special.

The chapter takes that objection seriously. It lists the cases people use to exempt themselves from the framework: the person who will not engage, the one who keeps changing the subject, the chronic complainer, the person with much more power, the person with much less power, the conversation that has to happen over text, and the conversation that runs across identity or cultural difference. Then it makes its central claim. The framework does not change in those rooms. The order and emphasis change. The same underlying move still applies: restore safety, then speak honestly.

That claim matters most in power-gap conversations because power changes what the first honest move can safely look like. Yusuf may not open with accusation. He may need to narrow the ask, anchor it in observable facts, and show respect for the role before he names the concern. The chapter does not call that cowardice. It calls it fit. The principle is the same as everywhere else: make the room safer and tell the truth. What changes is scope, pacing, and what kind of ask the other person can realistically hear.

The same logic applies to text. Ramona and Kai can produce a full argument in twelve messages, but the medium strips out nearly every repair signal the framework depends on. Tone gets guessed. Delay gets interpreted. Shortness looks cold. Punctuation becomes climate. So the chapter treats text as a special case not because truth stops mattering there, but because the channel is bad at carrying safety. Sometimes the right move is not a cleverer message. It is a re-entry line that moves the conversation into a call, a walk, or at least a slower exchange where misunderstanding can be repaired before it hardens.

Hostility and evasion create a different distortion. Damian's coworker complains every week, but every attempt to solve the problem dissolves into a new grievance. Celeste's sibling answers every real question with a side topic. The temptation in those rooms is either to attack the pattern or to surrender to it. Chapter 10 keeps the middle path alive. Restore enough safety that the conversation can hold, then return to the actual issue instead of being dragged endlessly by the latest diversion. The framework does not promise instant cooperation. It simply refuses to let difficulty become a license for either domination or drift.

Identity and cultural difference raise the stakes further because assumptions become more expensive. A school discussion can turn brittle fast if one person mistakes a pause for agreement, or reads caution as bad faith, or speaks as if their own frame is the neutral one. The chapter's answer is not to avoid the conversation. It is to slow the assumptions, watch the conditions more carefully, and keep openness alive. Safety here is not softness. It is the condition that lets honesty land without collapsing immediately into defense.

Then the chapter makes its hardest claim. The framework is not a technique for winning arguments. Readers who use it to manipulate, extract compliance, or sound reasonable while steamrolling will find that it fails. That warning is stronger than a style note. It is the ethical test for everything in the book. The words are only useful when the speaker is genuinely open to being changed by what the other person says. Without that openness, the language becomes choreography in search of control.

That is why the final assignment is so specific. Do not admire the framework in abstraction. Choose one or two real conversations you have been avoiding. Plan them. Decide what safety requires, what honesty requires, what medium fits, what scope is realistic, and what you are actually willing to hear back. Then have them. Chapter 10 closes the book by taking away the last excuse. The hard room may still be hard. The framework may still need adjustment in order and emphasis. But the work is no longer to wait for a perfect case. The work is to walk into the real one.
