# Baseline Quality Floor

Baseline chapters: ch01 and ch02

- Chapter 1 establishes the quality floor for fundamentals prose that explains mechanism without turning into generic self-help. Its strongest recurring standard is systems logic with a real patience-versus-drift limit.
- Chapter 2 establishes the quality floor for conceptual depth that stays practical. Its strongest recurring standard is turning identity into evidence-through-repetition rather than affirmation language.
- Shared floor: curiosity-first opening, chapter-specific mechanism, one live tension, concrete examples across work / school / personal, and clean conversion into non-collapsed tone surfaces.
- Shared risk to watch in later waves: do not let concepts broaden into generic productivity talk or flatten into slogan-level recap.
