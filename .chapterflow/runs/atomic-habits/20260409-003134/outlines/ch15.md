Chapter Promise:
Readers will understand that immediate satisfaction strongly influences whether a behavior gets repeated.

Opening Move:
Open on a contrast where the bad habit feels good now while the good habit pays later, then show why repetition follows the immediate feeling.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 for the cardinal rule that rewarded behavior repeats
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P5 for immediate outcomes versus delayed outcomes
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P7 for designing some immediate satisfaction into good habits

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: shift from setup into immediate satisfaction
- P2: explain the cardinal rule of repeated behavior
- P3: show why immediate outcomes often beat delayed ones
- P4: show why bad habits often reward now and cost later
- P5: show why good habits need some immediate reinforcement
- P6: preserve the limit that satisfaction is not the whole story
- P7 if needed: bridge into Chapter 16 and tracking/visible progress

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter distinguishes long-term benefit from immediate payoff and argues that repetition follows what feels rewarding now.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Good habits often pay late while bad habits pay now. The chapter must preserve the problem of aligning immediate reinforcement with long-term benefit.

Hard Test:
What changes when a good habit stops feeling like pure delayed sacrifice and starts carrying some immediate sign of reward?

Takeaway Count Lock:
- Easy: 3 (always)
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 5

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- rewarded behavior gets repeated
- immediate outcomes often dominate delayed ones
- bad habits often win because they satisfy now
- good habits need some immediate reinforcement
- satisfaction helps repetition without replacing long-term meaning
