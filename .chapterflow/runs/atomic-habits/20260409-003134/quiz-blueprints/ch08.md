# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter: How to Make a Habit Irresistible
Core concepts to test:
- craving gives cues motivational force
- anticipation helps explain why cues feel attractive before action
- temptation bundling can make good habits more appealing
- attraction can be shaped through association without becoming magic
- Chapter 9 will widen attraction into social influence

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: analyze

Question plan:
- q01: identify the chapter's main shift from cue visibility to craving
- q02: define why craving matters in the habit loop
- q03: explain how anticipation changes attraction
- q04: work scenario about pairing a needed task with a wanted ritual
- q05: school scenario about a tempting cafeteria or social routine
- q06: personal scenario about bundling walks with a favorite podcast
- q07: diagnose what temptation bundling actually does
- q08: identify the chapter's limit on overclaiming attraction
- q09: connect Chapter 7 exposure logic to Chapter 8 attraction logic
- q10: explain why Chapter 9 would next examine social influence

Distractor notes:
- confuse attraction with reward after the behavior only
- overstate neuroscience claims
- treat bundling as unrelated bribery

Explanation diversity notes:
- vary explanation openings
- make each explanation teach a separate attraction or craving point

CorrectIndex plan:
- keep distribution roughly balanced across 0 / 1 / 2
