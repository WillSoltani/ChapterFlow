# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter: The Secret to Self-Control
Core concepts to test:
- apparent self-control as reduced temptation exposure
- repeated cue exposure keeps bad habits active
- removing cues can support better behavior
- structural avoidance is not the same as weakness
- Chapter 8 will widen from cue exposure to attraction and craving

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: analyze

Question plan:
- q01: identify the chapter's main reframing of self-control
- q02: define why temptation cues matter
- q03: explain why fewer tempting situations can help
- q04: work scenario about removing distracting tabs or alerts
- q05: school scenario about a repeated vending-machine or locker-route cue
- q06: personal scenario about moving the phone away from the bedside
- q07: diagnose why repeated exposure keeps the bad habit active
- q08: identify the chapter's limit on overclaiming cue removal
- q09: connect Chapter 6 environment design to Chapter 7 self-control
- q10: explain why Chapter 8 would next examine attraction instead of visibility

Distractor notes:
- confuse self-control with pure moral strength
- treat cue removal as weakness
- ignore exposure frequency

Explanation diversity notes:
- vary explanation openings
- make each explanation teach a separate exposure-design point

CorrectIndex plan:
- keep distribution roughly balanced across 0 / 1 / 2
