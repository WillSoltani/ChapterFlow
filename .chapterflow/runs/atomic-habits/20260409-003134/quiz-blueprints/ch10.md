# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter: How to Find and Fix the Causes of Your Bad Habits
Core concepts to test:
- bad habits often feel like solutions to immediate problems
- the underlying cause matters more than the surface behavior alone
- reinterpretation can weaken the pull of a bad habit
- better substitutes should address the real need underneath the habit
- Chapter 11 will next focus on making good habits easier

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: analyze

Question plan:
- q01: identify the chapter's shift from surface habit to underlying cause
- q02: define why bad habits can feel useful in the moment
- q03: explain why naming the real problem matters
- q04: work scenario about stress-snacking or inbox checking
- q05: school scenario about scrolling, boredom, or avoidance
- q06: personal scenario about restlessness or late-night browsing
- q07: diagnose what reinterpretation actually changes
- q08: identify the chapter's limit on overclaiming awareness
- q09: connect Chapter 9 social attraction to Chapter 10 cause diagnosis
- q10: explain why Chapter 11 would next emphasize ease

Distractor notes:
- treat bad habits as random weakness only
- overstate awareness as a total cure
- ignore the real need under the surface behavior

Explanation diversity notes:
- vary explanation openings
- make each explanation teach a separate cause-diagnosis point

CorrectIndex plan:
- keep distribution roughly balanced across 0 / 1 / 2
