# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter: How Your Habits Shape Your Identity (and Vice Versa)
Core concepts to test:
- outcomes versus processes versus identity
- identity-based habits
- repeated action as evidence
- old labels reinforcing old behavior
- identity as direction rather than prison

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: create

Question plan:
- q01: identify the deepest layer of change in the chapter
- q02: distinguish outcome goals from identity-based habits
- q03: explain why repeated action matters more than self-description alone
- q04: work scenario about choosing an identity standard over a deadline excuse
- q05: school scenario about a lab mistake and the danger of a fixed label
- q06: personal scenario about "not a morning person"
- q07: why identity-based change can last after a goal event ends
- q08: what the chapter warns against when using identity language
- q09: connect Chapter 1 systems logic to Chapter 2 identity logic
- q10: ask what Chapter 3 must explain if identity is built by repeated action

Distractor notes:
- treat identity as a slogan with no repeated behavior
- treat one success or failure as total proof of identity
- confuse process goals with identity change

Explanation diversity notes:
- vary explanation openers aggressively
- make each explanation teach a different piece of the identity mechanism

CorrectIndex plan:
- keep distribution roughly balanced across 0 / 1 / 2
