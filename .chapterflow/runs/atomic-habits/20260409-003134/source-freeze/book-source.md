### Systems framing

Source: `jamesclear_goals_systems`

- Reinforces the argument that results follow systems and processes, not only goals.

### Identity change

Source: `jamesclear_identity_based_habits`

- Reinforces the identity layer of change and the idea that repeated actions become evidence for a desired self.
- Primary official support for Chapter 2.

### Cue design and habit stacking

Source: `jamesclear_habit_stacking`

- Supports implementation intentions, pairing habits to current routines, and the need for highly specific cues.
- Primary official support for Chapter 5 and related cue design material.

### Environment design

Source: `jamesclear_power_of_environment`

- Supports the claim that environment design often beats raw motivation.
- Useful for Chapters 6 and 7 and for law-of-least-effort adjacency later.

### Motivation and boredom

Source: `jamesclear_goldilocks_rule`

- Supports the challenge-skill balance argument and boredom as a threat to long-term consistency.
- Primary official support for Chapter 19.

### App surface

Source: `atoms_app`
