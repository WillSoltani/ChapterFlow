Chapter 9 source focus: habits become more attractive when they align with belonging, social norms, and the people around us.

- Official support is strongest for the idea that people imitate the habits of the close, the many, and the powerful.
- Secondary support can reinforce that behavior feels easier to adopt when the surrounding group treats it as normal and desirable.
- Keep the claims narrow: the chapter is about social attraction and imitation, not total social determinism.
- The hard edge is interpretive. A behavior often feels attractive because it protects belonging, not only because of private desire.
- Avoid drifting into Chapter 10 cause-diagnosis language as the main explanation here.
