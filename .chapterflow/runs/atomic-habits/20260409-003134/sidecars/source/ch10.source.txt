- Official support is strongest for the idea that behavior is driven by the desire to solve problems.
- Secondary support can reinforce that cravings can be reinterpreted and that awareness of the real need can weaken a bad habit's pull.
- Keep the claims narrow: the chapter is about diagnosing perceived solutions and reframing responses, not about instant cure through insight.
- The hard edge is interpretive. A bad habit can be a flawed answer to something real.
- Avoid drifting into later ease/friction mechanics as the main explanation here.
