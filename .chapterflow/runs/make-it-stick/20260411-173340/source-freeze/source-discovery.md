# Source Discovery

## Goal

Freeze a lawful source bundle for *Make It Stick* before any chapter work.

## Discovery Result

- No public-domain full text exists.
- The dominant lawful anchor is the official Harvard University Press metadata as surfaced through Google Books and the De Gruyter/Harvard title page.
- The chapter map is stable across the authorized preview and Open Library catalog record.
- Official preview text is sufficient for title, framing, and chapter order, but too thin for quote-heavy chapter drafting.
- The run therefore uses a narrow secondary bundle only to support chapter interpretation where the authorized preview is sparse.

## Lock Decision

- Locked automatically to the 2014 English Belknap Press of Harvard University Press edition.
- No translation ambiguity is active.
- No abridged-vs-full ambiguity appears in the chosen lawful source surfaces.
- No evidence shows a materially different chapter order across the main English edition surfaces.

## Working Policy

- Use paraphrase-first writing.
- Treat Google Books and De Gruyter/Harvard as the authorized support surfaces.
- Use exact quotes only when the wording is directly supported by the frozen authorized bundle.
- Where support is thin, stay narrower instead of widening claims.
