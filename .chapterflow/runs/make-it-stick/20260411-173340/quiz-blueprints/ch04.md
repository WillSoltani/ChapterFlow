# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter: Embrace Difficulties

Core concepts to test:
- some difficulties are desirable because they strengthen retrieval, generation, or repair
- smooth errorless practice can misprice learning
- errors can help when correction follows
- overload and unsupported frustration are not desirable difficulties
- the chapter judges difficulty by mechanism, not by toughness theater

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: analyze

Question plan:
- q01: core recall of what desirable difficulty means
- q02: contrast between smooth practice and productive challenge
- q03: application to retrieval or generation during school review
- q04: workplace transfer involving over-guided training
- q05: why errors can still help learning
- q06: misreading that pain itself is the goal
- q07: personal-study scenario about using a miss as repair material
- q08: bridge question toward illusions of knowing
- q09: synthesis on support versus overload
- q10: threshold question about when difficulty stops teaching

Distractor notes:
- tempting wrong answer type 1: harder automatically means better
- tempting wrong answer type 2: error avoidance mistaken for stronger learning

Explanation diversity notes:
- avoid repeated openers
- one explanation should stress repair, another generation, another overload boundary
- at least one should reject macho interpretations of difficulty

CorrectIndex plan:
- keep distribution roughly balanced across 0 / 1 / 2
