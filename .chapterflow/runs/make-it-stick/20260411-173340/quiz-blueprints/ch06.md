# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter: Get Beyond Learning Styles

Core concepts to test:
- preferred style is not the same thing as stronger learning
- teaching choices should fit the material and the task
- prior knowledge often matters more than style labels
- active retrieval and explanation matter more than preference matching
- style beliefs can distract from evidence-backed design

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: analyze

Question plan:
- q01: core recall of what the chapter rejects about learning styles
- q02: preference versus evidence contrast
- q03: application to choosing representation for a science or geometry lesson
- q04: workplace transfer involving background knowledge and training design
- q05: why prior knowledge matters
- q06: misreading that learner differences do not matter at all
- q07: personal-study scenario about liking a format versus learning from it
- q08: bridge question toward beliefs about ability
- q09: synthesis on material fit and retrieval
- q10: threshold question about what should guide instruction

Distractor notes:
- tempting wrong answer type 1: preference mistaken for proof
- tempting wrong answer type 2: anti-style correction exaggerated into one-size-fits-all teaching

Explanation diversity notes:
- avoid repeated openers
- one explanation should stress task fit, another prior knowledge, another retrieval
- at least one should reject the idea that the chapter dismisses all learner differences

CorrectIndex plan:
- keep distribution roughly balanced across 0 / 1 / 2
