Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader understands that GTD needs usable operating conditions, not just agreement with the ideas.

Opening Move:
Open on the moment when a good system is abandoned because the capture tool, workspace, or setup time is inconvenient.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 for setup friction and the move from theory to practice
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 for time, space, and tools as the setup triad
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 for trusted use and decay when the environment stays awkward

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: show the failure of an inconvenient system
- P2: shift from theory into setup conditions
- P3: explain time as setup investment
- P4: explain space and tool readiness
- P5: explain friction and trusted use
- P6: land the consequence of poor setup and bridge to full collection

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The key distinction is between agreeing with the system conceptually and making the environment easy enough that the system is actually used.

Hard-Depth Tension:
A sound method can still fail if the environment makes every capture or review step annoying. Hard depth must keep this failure mode visible.

Threshold Question:
What happens to a trusted system when it is technically possible to use but practically annoying to use?

Takeaway Count Lock:
- Easy: 3 (always)
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 5

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- Takeaway 1: method quality is not enough without readiness
- Takeaway 2: setup time is an investment in future ease
- Takeaway 3: available tools reduce capture friction
- Takeaway 4: workspace conditions affect review trust
- Takeaway 5: practical annoyance is enough to break the system
