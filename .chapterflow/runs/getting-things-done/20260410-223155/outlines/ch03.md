Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader understands why project planning improves when it follows a natural sequence from purpose to next actions instead of jumping straight into scattered brainstorming.

Opening Move:
Open on the familiar feeling of a project meeting where everyone starts throwing out ideas before anyone has defined success.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 for the natural vs unnatural planning contrast
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P5 for the five planning phases
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P6 for the dining-out example and practical landing

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: show the noise of backward planning
- P2: introduce natural planning as the corrective sequence
- P3: explain purpose and principles plus outcome visioning
- P4: explain brainstorming after outcome clarity
- P5: explain organizing and next actions
- P6: land the dining-out example and relaxed control idea
- P7 if needed: bridge to physical setup and execution

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The core distinction is between natural planning, which starts with purpose and outcome, and unnatural planning, which starts with scattered brainstorming before success is defined.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter's planning model only works if outcome visioning stays grounded and brainstorming does not arrive too early. Hard depth must keep the risk of fantasy planning and idea sprawl visible.

Threshold Question:
What goes wrong when a project starts generating ideas before anyone has defined what done is supposed to look like?

- Hard: 5

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- Takeaway 1: natural planning starts with purpose, not tactics
- Takeaway 2: outcome visioning narrows attention usefully
- Takeaway 3: brainstorming works best after the outcome is visible
- Takeaway 4: organizing prevents idea sprawl from staying amorphous
- Takeaway 5: next actions reconnect project planning to movement
