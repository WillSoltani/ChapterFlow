Core Claim:
Doing in GTD depends on choosing the best available action from a trusted current system rather than reacting only to pressure or mood. Chapter 9 argues that action choices are filtered through context, time, energy, and priority. The system becomes practical at the moment of engagement by narrowing what makes sense now, while higher horizons remain above the ground-level choice.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the engagement chapter. It follows review by explaining how a current GTD system gets used to choose what to do next in real conditions.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author assumes a trusted, current system is available for action choice.
Then the author shows that context, time, energy, and priority shape what action makes sense now.
Then the author concludes that doing is a situated choice from the system, not a random reaction detached from it.
