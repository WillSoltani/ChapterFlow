Core Claim:
Review keeps the organized GTD system alive by revisiting it often enough that lists, buckets, and commitments do not go stale. Chapter 8 argues that a well-organized system can still fail if it is not refreshed. The Weekly Review is load-bearing because it restores trust, catches drift, and keeps the structure current enough to support engagement.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the maintenance chapter. It follows organizing by explaining how the system stays functional over time rather than quietly decaying into outdated lists and neglected buckets.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author shifts from system structure into system upkeep.
Then the author shows that review, especially Weekly Review, keeps the organized system current and trustworthy.
Then the author concludes that without regular review, even a well-built system becomes stale and harder to rely on.
