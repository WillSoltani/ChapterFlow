Core Claim:
Once the daily ground game of lists, review, and next-action choice is working, GTD can return to project control with more confidence and clarity. Chapter 10 argues that projects come back into focus when the lower workflow layers are reliable enough to support creative planning and regained project visibility. Project control is not a separate miracle above the system; it depends on the ground game functioning beneath it.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the return-to-projects chapter. It follows doing by lifting from live next-action choice back to project-level control and creative planning without redoing the full Chapter 3 planning architecture.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author assumes the ground game is functioning well enough to support wider control.
Then the author shows that project control becomes more available once daily execution is stable.
Then the author concludes that projects can be brought under control through renewed visibility and practical creative planning built on the trusted system.
