Core Claim:
The next-action decision matters because deciding the concrete next move changes how people relate to work and commitments. Chapter 12 argues that progress often shifts once a vague obligation is converted into an identified next action. The principle has power because it reduces stuckness and makes engagement more immediate.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the principles-side return to next actions. It follows collection by explaining why deciding the next physical move has force beneath the GTD system, not just inside the practical workflow chapter.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author treats vague commitments as a source of friction and avoidance.
Then the author shows that deciding the next action changes how work becomes approachable.
Then the author concludes that the next-action decision has principle-level power because it turns abstraction into movable engagement.
