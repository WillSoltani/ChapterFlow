Core Claim:
Outcome focusing matters because a clear picture of the desired result sharpens action, planning, and attention. Chapter 13 argues that people move more effectively when they know what done looks like. The principle has power because it gives direction to effort instead of leaving work as generic activity.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the principles-side return to outcomes. It follows the next-action chapter by explaining why seeing the desired result has force beneath the GTD system, not just inside project planning.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author treats fuzzy desired results as a source of scattered effort.
Then the author shows that outcome focusing clarifies what action and planning are for.
Then the author concludes that clear outcomes have principle-level power because they align movement with a visible end state.
