Core Claim:
The collection habit matters because it clears commitments, reminders, and loose inputs out of the head so they can be handled more reliably. Chapter 11 argues that capture reduces psychic drag by moving open loops into an external trusted place instead of leaving them to rattle around mentally. The principle is not merely logistical. It changes how attention feels.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the principles-side return to capture. It follows the practical mechanics section by explaining why collection has power as a habit beneath the system, not just as a setup step.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author treats mental load as partly driven by uncollected open loops.
Then the author shows that capture reduces internal friction by getting commitments out of the head.
Then the author concludes that the collection habit has power because it frees attention and trust before later workflow stages even begin.
