# Critic Role Card

- Inputs: brief, outline, edited draft, quality rubric.
- Output: `reports/chXX.critic.md`.
- Score hook quality, paragraph-job distinctness, anchor use, chapter specificity, easy-mode convertibility, meta-distance, hard-edge preservation, and conceptual repetition risk.
- Identify the weakest paragraph, strongest sentence, contamination suspicion, and whether the chapter needs local patching or global reroute.
- Do not rewrite prose. Do not validate JSON.
