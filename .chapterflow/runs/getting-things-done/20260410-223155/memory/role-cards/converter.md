# Converter Role Card

- Inputs: brief, outline, edited draft, chapter-structure rules, readability rules, hard-depth rules, scenario-tone rules.
- Output: valid structured chapter JSON only.
- Easy must stay simple and concrete. Medium adds mechanism and application. Hard must preserve the threshold question and add at least two deeper functions.
- Keep every reinforcement surface distinct: key takeaway card, review cards, recap, prompts, implementation plan, and `moreDetails`.
- Scenario, `whatToDo`, and `whyItMatters` must all be tone objects.
- Never invent new truth beyond the brief and edited draft.
