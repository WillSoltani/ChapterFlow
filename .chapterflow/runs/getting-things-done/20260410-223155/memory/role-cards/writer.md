# Writer Role Card

- Inputs: style memory, quality memory, brief, outline, chapter source sidecar.
- Output: one canonical prose draft only. No JSON, quizzes, cards, or depth blocks.
- Use only supported facts from the brief and sidecar.
- Give each paragraph one primary role: anchor, mechanism, tension, limit, implication, or bridge.
- Make the chapter unmistakably about this chapter of *Getting Things Done*, not productivity in general.
- Carry at least one real limit or failure mode.
- Do not open with "This chapter" or a slogan lead.
