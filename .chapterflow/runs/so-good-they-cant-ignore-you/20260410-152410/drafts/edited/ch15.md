Mission becomes public in this chapter because Newport argues that meaningful direction is not complete when it stays private. After Rule 4 showed that mission needs capital and then little bets, the final requirement is visibility. The chapter's answer is marketing. A clarified mission needs reach if it is going to matter beyond the person quietly holding it.

That claim matters because private conviction does not automatically create public force. A person can build support for a mission and sharpen it through exploration, yet still leave it too hidden to shape the world around them. Newport is not saying substance can be replaced by visibility. He is saying that substance without reach can remain limited in its effect. Marketing is what helps the mission travel.

The chapter therefore completes the sequence of Rule 4. Capital makes mission possible. Little bets help mission emerge. Marketing helps the mission gain force. That order matters because visibility is not being treated as a shortcut around weak work or unclear direction. It comes after support and discovery. Marketing amplifies something real rather than inventing value out of nothing.

That is why the chapter should not be read as an argument for manipulative self-promotion. Newport's point is narrower and more practical. If a mission is going to create impact, people need to be able to encounter it. Reach, attention, and amplification become part of the work of meaning because hidden direction has limited power no matter how sincere it may be.

The chapter also closes Rule 4 cleanly. It gathers the three requirements into one picture of meaningful work. Mission is not just a feeling. It needs support, it needs exploration, and it needs visibility. The rule becomes more demanding, but also more concrete. Meaning is no longer mystical. It is built, refined, and then made public enough to matter.

Missions require marketing because a meaningful direction needs more than private clarity. It needs enough reach to create consequence. Newport uses this chapter to finish the rule and prepare the book for its conclusion. Once support, discovery, and visibility are all in view, the final task is to integrate what the book has been arguing about work all along.
