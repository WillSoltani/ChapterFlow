## Rotation Plan For Examples

- Wave 1 should lean on early-career office and university settings because Rule 1 is about belief correction.
- Rule 2 should increase apprenticeship, training, and creative-work scenarios.
- Rule 3 should lean on negotiation, job design, and boss-market tension.
- Rule 4 should use adjacent-possible, exploration, and visibility scenarios.

## School-Setting Plan

- Use seminar, lab, advising office, student organization, and internship contexts.
- Avoid repeating professor-correction scenes across adjacent chapters.

## Vocabulary Watchlist

- Do not overuse: passion, craft, skill, control, mission, remarkable, leverage.
- Rotate mechanism words so later chapters do not sound templated.

## Motif And Callback Opportunities

- Rule 1 myth-busting can echo later when capital and mission are shown as earned, not discovered.
- The contrast between mindset and market should recur across Rules 2 and 3.
- Adjacent-possible logic can call back to early anti-passion arguments about uncertainty.

## Thin-Chapter Risks

- Chapter 1 and Chapter 16 risk sounding like book-summary glue if not anchored tightly.
- Chapter 3 and Chapter 9 risk generic career advice drift if the pressure and failure mode are not concrete.
