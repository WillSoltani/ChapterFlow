## Visible authorized description

The authorized preview/store copy states that Newport argues against the usual "follow your passion" advice and instead organizes the book around four rules:

1. Don't follow your passion
2. Be so good they can't ignore you
3. Turn down a promotion
4. Think small, act big

The visible description presents the book as a blueprint for building work you love through skill, control, and mission rather than passion-first searching.
