## Narrowly frozen support

- Newport describes career capital as the rare and valuable skills you accumulate through disciplined craft.
- The post contrasts people who build capital patiently with people who demand autonomy, mission, or ideal conditions before they have earned leverage.
- The concept is presented as the currency that later buys control, creativity, and compelling work.
