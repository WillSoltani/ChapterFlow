## Narrowly frozen support

- Newport describes the "passion mindset" as harmful for students because it can turn broad or idle interests into obligatory pursuits.
- He argues that early-stage attraction is often too immature to deserve decisive authority.
- He recommends treating college as a place to develop passions rather than follow ones that students assume already exist.
- The post explicitly links passion-first thinking with unnecessary stress and burnout.

## Downstream rule

Use this source narrowly for Chapter 3 support around the emotional and behavioral costs of passion-first thinking. Do not expand it into claims beyond student overload, stress, burnout, and premature identity pressure.
