# Style Memory

- Specific over generic. Concrete over abstract. Compression over restatement.
- Reader-facing prose should teach the idea directly, not narrate the chapter artifact.
- Open from pressure, event, conflict, or a live question. Avoid thesis-first or slogan-first starts.
- Every paragraph needs one primary job: anchor, mechanism, tension, limit, implication, or bridge.
- Easy must feel accessible, not like thinner medium. Medium explains mechanism and application. Hard must add boundary, contradiction, cost, or threshold.
- Competitive tone must stay sharp and credible, never theatrical.
- Avoid contamination phrases, pseudo-science, corporate tone, stock tails, repeated endings, and AI-sounding filler.
