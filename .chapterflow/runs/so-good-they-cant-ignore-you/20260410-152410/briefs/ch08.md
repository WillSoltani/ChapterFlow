Core Claim:
Rule 3 opens by arguing that control is one of the strongest traits of compelling work. Newport treats control as an "elixir" because it changes how a job feels, but the chapter must preserve that control is not simply claimed by desire. It becomes possible when leverage exists. This chapter should establish control as the next major reward that career capital can purchase.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the pivot from building leverage to spending leverage. It introduces control as the central payoff of Rule 3.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author identifies control as a defining trait of dream work.
Then the author links the appeal of control to the leverage built in Rule 2.
Then the author concludes that pursuing control requires a more careful strategy than merely wanting independence.

Required Anchors:
- control as the dream-job elixir
- the connection between control and compelling work
- the need for leverage before control can be claimed sustainably
