Core Claim:
This chapter should turn the two control traps into practical judgment. After showing that autonomy moves can be blocked by outside resistance or weakened by thin internal support, Newport now needs a narrow synthesis: people should seek control by reading resistance intelligently and testing support honestly. The point is not certainty. The point is to avoid naive freedom moves without abandoning control as a real prize.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the Rule 3 avoidance chapter. It converts the two trap chapters into a usable standard for pursuing control without romanticizing or fearing it.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author recalls that control can fail from outside resistance or internal fragility.
Then the author turns those failures into judgment criteria for evaluating a move toward more autonomy.
Then the author concludes that wise pursuit of control means respecting both leverage and durability.

Required Anchors:
