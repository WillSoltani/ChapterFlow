Core Claim:
After dismantling the passion mindset, this chapter introduces the alternative: the craftsman mindset. Newport argues that work becomes clearer when you focus on what value you can produce instead of demanding that work constantly confirm your calling. The core move is attentional. By turning outward toward craft, contribution, and improvement, you lower interpretive noise and create a more durable basis for meaning.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the pivot chapter. It is the first positive replacement for Rule 1's critique and introduces the book's central alternative lens.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author contrasts the passion mindset with the craftsman mindset.
Then the author uses the Jordan Tice example to show patient craft over self-absorption.
Then the author concludes that focusing on contribution creates more stable meaning than constant fit-monitoring.

Required Anchors:
- Newport's explicit contrast between passion mindset and craftsman mindset.
- Jordan Tice as an example of patient skill-building and modesty.
- The idea that asking what you can offer the world is a better evaluative lens than asking what the world offers you.

Common Misreadings:

Counterarguments or Limits:
Contribution focus can be exploited if the environment gives no path for growth or value recognition. The chapter should admit that outward focus is not blind servitude.
