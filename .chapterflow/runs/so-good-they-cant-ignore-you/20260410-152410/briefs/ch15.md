Core Claim:
This chapter should argue that a mission does not fully matter if it remains private. After capital supports mission and little bets help clarify it, Newport now needs to show that mission also requires visibility. Marketing should be framed as the process that gives a mission public force, reach, and consequence rather than leaving it as a private intention.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the public-force chapter for Rule 4. It completes the rule by showing that mission needs not just support and discovery, but also visibility.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author argues that a mission must move beyond private understanding.
Then the author shows that visibility and amplification help a mission matter in the world.
Then the author concludes that marketing is part of how meaningful direction creates impact rather than staying hidden.
