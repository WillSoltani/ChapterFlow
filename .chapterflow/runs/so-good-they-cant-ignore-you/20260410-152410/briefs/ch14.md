Core Claim:
This chapter should argue that mission does not usually arrive as a perfect, finished answer. Even when earlier capital exists, a meaningful direction still has to be explored and refined. Newport's logic should make little bets the method that helps a mission emerge through small experiments instead of through instant certainty.
