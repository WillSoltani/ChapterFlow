Core Claim:
After defining career capital and profiling the people who build it, this chapter turns to method. Newport argues that becoming a craftsman requires deliberate focus on improving rare and valuable skill rather than relying on vague enthusiasm. The chapter should make skill-building feel procedural and demanding without drifting into empty grind rhetoric.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the how-to chapter for Rule 2. It moves from leverage logic and builder posture to the actual process of developing the kind of craft that generates capital.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author shifts from builder profile to build process.
Then the author emphasizes disciplined improvement over diffuse ambition.
Then the author concludes that becoming a craftsman is how career capital is intentionally produced.

Required Anchors:
- the transition from career capitalist posture to craft-building method
- deliberate development of rare and valuable skill
- the idea that better work later depends on rigorous improvement now
