Core Claim:
Forecasting can improve, but only if people demand scorekeeping, feedback, and better institutional incentives. The chapter argues that the future of forecasting depends on accountability, smaller and better-structured questions, and partnerships between superforecasters and superquestioners rather than on any fantasy of final mastery.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the book's future-facing chapter. It asks what a healthier forecasting culture and institution would require after all the earlier lessons about skill, limits, and revision.

Author Logic Chain:
First the authors argue that forecasting is still a work in progress rather than a finished science.
Then they show how institutional incentives often reward persuasive or flattering forecasts over accurate ones.
Then they argue for scorekeeping, smaller component questions, and superquestioners as the path toward a better forecasting future.
