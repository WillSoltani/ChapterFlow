Core Claim:
The chapter tests whether superforecasters are truly special or merely impressive under limited conditions. Using critics like Kahneman and Taleb, it acknowledges black swan events, surprise, and the hard limits of long-range prediction while still defending forecasting as a disciplined way to improve judgment and planning.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the book's critique-and-limits chapter. It turns the superforecasting story against its own triumphalism and asks how much forecasting can honestly claim.

Author Logic Chain:
First the authors present serious criticism of forecasting ambition.
Then they use black swan and surprise arguments to show where prediction meets real limits.
Then they defend forecasting as still useful for judgment, preparedness, and better questions even when certainty is impossible.
