Core Claim:
Superforecasters are not mystics. They are people whose habits of updating, aggregation, humility, and repeated scored practice let them outperform more prestigious experts. The Iraq WMD failure shows the cost of overconfident question substitution, while the IARPA and Good Judgment Project material shows that disciplined ordinary forecasters can beat elite institutions. The chapter also insists that skill must be separated from luck, which is why Doug Lorch and regression-to-the-mean matter.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is where the book stops speaking in abstractions about method and starts presenting real outperformance under tournament conditions.
