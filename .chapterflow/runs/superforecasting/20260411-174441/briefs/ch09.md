Core Claim:
Strong forecasters can improve further in teams, but only when the team avoids groupthink and makes disagreement operational. The chapter presents superteams as groups that aggregate multiple views, protect psychologically safe pushback, and keep revision alive instead of mistaking surface harmony for intelligence.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the book's transition from individual forecasting traits to collective forecasting design. It asks what kind of team structure preserves the strengths of superforecasters instead of flattening them into consensus theater.

Author Logic Chain:
First the authors move from perpetual beta at the individual level to collaborative forecasting at the team level.
Then they show that smart groups can still fail through groupthink if dissent becomes socially expensive.
Then they argue that superteams work because disagreement, aggregation, and psychologically safe challenge remain active inside the group.
