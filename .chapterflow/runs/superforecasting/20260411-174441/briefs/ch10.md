Core Claim:
Leadership looks like it should conflict with superforecasting because leaders must project confidence while superforecasters cultivate doubt. The chapter argues that the real solution is balance: leaders need decisive action, intellectual humility, mission clarity, and enough decentralization that local judgment can respond to uncertainty.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the book's leadership chapter. It tests whether forecasting habits survive contact with command, speed, delegation, and public responsibility.

Author Logic Chain:
First the authors stage the tension between decisive leadership and forecasting humility.
Then they use Moltke-style decentralized initiative to show that uncertainty requires action plus delegated judgment.
Then they argue that leaders must combine confidence, humility, and local initiative rather than choosing one trait alone.
