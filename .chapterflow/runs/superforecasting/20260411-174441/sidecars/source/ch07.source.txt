Chapter 7 source sidecar

Heading: Supernewsjunkies?

Frozen support:
- Superforecasters update more frequently than average forecasters, but their advantage is not reducible to simply consuming more news.
- Tetlock and Gardner stress that their original forecasts were already stronger before later updates.
- Bill Flack's Arafat forecast shows careful update sizing: moving from 60 percent to 65 percent after a meaningful but not decisive signal.
- Forecasters can fail by underreacting because they are attached to a prior story or by overreacting to irrelevant information through the dilution effect.
- Tim Minto's pattern of many small updates shows that frequent revision works best when each move is proportionate.

Allowed support boundaries:
- Keep the chapter on update discipline, dilution effect, and proportionate movement. Do not expand into general media criticism beyond the frozen support.
