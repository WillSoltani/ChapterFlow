Heading: Superteams

Frozen support:
- The chapter shifts from individual improvement to what happens when strong forecasters work together.
- Groupthink remains a danger even among smart people, so the chapter values structures that keep disagreement alive.
- Superteams are not defined by harmony alone. They depend on psychologically safe pushback, active revision, and aggregation of differing views.
- The chapter treats teamwork as a way to improve judgment only when dissent, correction, and information sharing remain operational.
- The practical tension is that a team can become smarter than its members or more conformist than its members depending on how disagreement is handled.

Allowed support boundaries:
- Keep the chapter on superteams, groupthink risk, psychologically safe pushback, and collective forecasting discipline. Do not import generic teamwork slogans or unsupported management theory.
