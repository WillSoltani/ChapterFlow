Heading: Perpetual Beta

Frozen support:
- Superforecasters tend to show a growth mindset rather than a fixed mindset, treating improvement as ongoing rather than closed.
- The phrase perpetual beta matters because it frames self-improvement as endless trial, feedback, analysis, and revision.
- Ambiguous forecasting language and hindsight bias both make learning from mistakes harder.
- Superforecasters actively seek feedback and are unusually willing to inspect and improve their own technique.
- The chapter argues that perpetual beta may matter more than intelligence in explaining durable forecasting improvement.

Allowed support boundaries:
- Keep the chapter on growth mindset, feedback hunger, hindsight bias, and iterative improvement. Do not import extra self-help framing beyond the frozen support.
