Core Claim:
The chapter argues that changing the angle of interpretation can reveal possibility, leverage, or meaning that a narrower read kept hidden. Its job is to show that perspective alters the usable relationship to the obstacle without pretending the obstacle is unreal. The chapter should make reframing feel disciplined and reality-based, not upbeat or evasive. It should stay distinct from Chapter 4 by building on cleaner facts and then changing the angle, rather than cleaning the facts themselves.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Chapter 4 practiced objectivity. Chapter 5 asks what becomes possible once the facts are clearer and the angle can change without lying. This chapter is about re-seeing, not denial and not raw positive thinking.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author shows that one obstacle can produce very different readings depending on the angle.
Then the author shows that a changed angle can reveal opportunity or room that was previously hidden.
Then the author concludes that perspective is a disciplined interpretive move built on facts rather than against them.

Counterarguments or Limits:
Perspective cannot change every fact. It can only change the angle from which those facts are interpreted. The chapter should keep that boundary visible.
