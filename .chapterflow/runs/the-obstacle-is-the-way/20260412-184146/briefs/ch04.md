Core Claim:
The chapter argues that objectivity is a practice of stripping away distortion so the obstacle can be classified more accurately. Its job is to show that ego, fear, and wishful thinking all add false material to the read. The chapter should make objectivity feel disciplined and practical rather than cold or detached. It should stay distinct from Chapter 3 by focusing on cleaner seeing after emotion is governed, not on emotion itself.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Chapter 3 kept feelings from ruling the response. Chapter 4 asks what cleaner reading looks like after that governance is in place. It is not just "be calm." It is the active work of seeing without self-serving additions.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author shows that people rarely read obstacles cleanly on the first pass.
Then the author shows how ego, fear, and wishful thinking corrupt classification.
Then the author concludes that objectivity is a repeatable discipline that improves judgment and next moves.

Specific Applications:

Common Misreadings:

Counterarguments or Limits:
Objectivity is never perfect and does not erase involvement. The discipline is to reduce distortion, not to become a machine.
