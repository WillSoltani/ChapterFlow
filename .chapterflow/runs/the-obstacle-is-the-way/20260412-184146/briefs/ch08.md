Core Claim:
The chapter argues that many obstacles stay stuck because the reader keeps approaching them with the same default interpretation and the same stale response pattern. Its job is to show that different thinking can uncover workable routes hidden by habit. The chapter should make unconventional thought feel disciplined and reality-based rather than quirky or random. It should stay distinct from Chapter 7 by moving from present attention into deliberate pattern-breaking.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Chapter 7 stabilized attention on the live step. Chapter 8 asks what happens when the live step is visible but the old mental pattern is still running. It is not another presence chapter and not yet full opportunity-finding as its own chapter.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author shows that default thinking often reproduces the same blockage.
Then the author shows that a changed frame or unusual angle can reveal options the default pattern missed.
Then the author concludes that disciplined novelty is sometimes the only way to keep the obstacle from owning the route.
