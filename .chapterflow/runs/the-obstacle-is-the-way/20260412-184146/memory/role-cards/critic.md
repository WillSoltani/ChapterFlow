# Critic Role Card

Inputs:
- brief
- outline
- edited draft
- quality memory

Job:
- Score the prose gate before structure.

Must report:
- hook quality
- paragraph-job distinctness
- anchor use
- chapter specificity
- easy-mode convertibility
- meta-distance
- hard-edge preservation
- conceptual repetition risk
- weakest paragraph
- strongest sentence
- contamination or source-splice suspicion
- local patch vs global reroute

Passing standard:
- 10/12 minimum
- any auto-fail rejects conversion
