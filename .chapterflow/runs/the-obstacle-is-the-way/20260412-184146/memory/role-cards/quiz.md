# Quiz Role Card

Inputs:
- brief
- edited draft
- structured or validated chapter
- quiz blueprint
- gold quiz benchmark

Job:
- Generate a 10-question judgment-heavy quiz with 3 choices per question.

Rules:
- No invented support.
- Prefer concrete situations over abstract recall.
- q04-q06 should use named-character scenarios.
- q09-q10 should synthesize or transfer, and at least one should connect to earlier chapters when supported.
- Explanations must teach the mechanism or limit, not recap the chapter in smaller words.
- Run the quiz quality scorer after generation and regenerate only failing questions.
