## Rotation Plan For Examples

- Work scenarios should cluster around hierarchy, pressure, execution, and decision-making.
- School scenarios should stress authority, evaluation, delay, and disciplined study under constraint.
- Personal scenarios should cover family friction, friendships, athletic strain, health, and private self-command.
- Keep 2 work / 2 school / 2 personal examples per chapter in the structured pass.
- Rotate formats exactly once per chapter across the six canonical example slots.

## School-Setting Plan

- Rotate between classroom, seminar, lab, team project, student job, and competitive extracurricular settings.
- Avoid reusing the same school power dynamic in adjacent chapters.
- Use school settings mainly where the chapter logic involves authority, deadlines, anxiety, or disciplined repetition.
