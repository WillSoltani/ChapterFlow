Chapter Promise:
This chapter will show how work settings can borrow deliberate-practice logic without pretending that jobs offer the same clean standards as elite training fields.

Opening Move:
Open by admitting that most jobs are messy and that this mess changes how practice must be designed.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The mechanism is partial translation. Work practice improves when professionals isolate trainable subskills and design tighter review loops inside otherwise noisy environments.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must preserve the difference between improved workplace practice and full deliberate practice.

Threshold Question:
How do you make work more practice-rich when the job does not hand you a coach, a scoreboard, and a clean rep every time?
