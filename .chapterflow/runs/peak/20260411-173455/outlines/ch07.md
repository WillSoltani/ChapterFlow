Chapter Promise:
This chapter will show how bounded ordinary improvement can compound into extraordinary performance without pretending the road is easy, straight, or free of sacrifice.

Opening Move:
Open by rejecting the fantasy of a sudden leap into extraordinary performance.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P2-P4
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P4-P6
- anchor 4 used in paragraph(s): P6-P7

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: reject sudden-miracle framing
- P2: show compounding from accumulated deliberate development
- P3: explain rising standards and sequencing
- P4: explain longer commitment and plateau pressure
- P5: make sacrifice and narrowing filters visible
- P6: preserve the limit against vague inspiration
- P7: bridge to the talent chapter

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The mechanism is compounding under rising standards. Extraordinary performance emerges when deliberate development keeps intensifying instead of flattening into casual maintenance.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must preserve the difference between serious long-path development and generic ambition theater.

Threshold Question:
What changes when improvement no longer means getting a little better, but staying on a road whose standards and costs keep rising?
