Chapter Promise:
This chapter will make the reader see that practice works because bodies and minds adapt to repeated demand, not because effort creates magic.

Opening Move:
Open on the familiar fact that bodies change under training, then widen that intuition toward mental performance.

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: start from obvious physical adaptation and the reader's existing intuition
- P2: carry that logic toward mental training and skill growth
- P3: expose the mistake of treating exposure ceilings as natural destiny
- P4: explain that adaptation follows the demand pattern, not the wish
- P5: preserve the warning that bad drills also train the system
- P6: bridge toward mental representations as the next deeper mechanism

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter's mechanism is demand-led adaptation. Repeated, specific pressure reshapes capacity, but the direction of that change depends on what the drill is actually asking the system to do.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must preserve the difference between adaptive growth and misadaptation. More training is not automatically better training.

Threshold Question:
If the system adapts to what it repeatedly does, what exactly are you training it to become better at?
