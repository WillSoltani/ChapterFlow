Chapter Promise:
This chapter will define deliberate practice as the stricter training form that produces elite gains when a field has stable standards, informed feedback, and repeated correction.

Opening Move:
Open by separating ordinary repetition from a harsher, more precise kind of practice.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P2-P4
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P4-P5
- anchor 4 used in paragraph(s): P6-P7

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: distinguish ordinary practice from deliberate practice
- P2: define the ingredients of the gold standard
- P3: explain why standards and measurability matter
- P4: explain why coaching and feedback matter
- P5: explain why the method is effortful and often unpleasant
- P6: preserve the limit that some domains lack full deliberate-practice conditions
- P7: bridge to workplace adaptation

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The mechanism is a narrow correction loop inside a field with known standards. Deliberate practice works because it isolates weaknesses, stretches them under feedback, and repeats correction with precision.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must preserve the difference between intense effort and true deliberate practice.

Threshold Question:
What conditions have to exist before a hard training routine deserves the name deliberate practice?

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- Takeaway 1: deliberate practice is narrower than ordinary practice
- Takeaway 2: stable standards let feedback stay specific
- Takeaway 3: coaching or informed external correction matters
- Takeaway 4: the method is difficult because it lives near weakness, not comfort
- Takeaway 5: many domains need adaptation because they lack clean gold-standard conditions
