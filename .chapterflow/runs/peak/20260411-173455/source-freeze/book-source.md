## Secondary support kept in the frozen bundle

Readingraphics, Gistfist, and Probinism provide chapter-level summaries that align with the frozen chapter map. Those sources reinforce these recurring themes:

- naive practice plateaus while purposeful practice uses specific goals, feedback, and stretch
- human adaptability is broader than common talent stories suggest
- mental representations are central to expert performance
- deliberate practice depends on established training methods, feedback, and coaching
- the core principles can be adapted for work and ordinary life
- exceptional performance remains possible without assuming fixed natural limits do all the work
