Core Claim:
This chapter argues that natural talent is the strongest objection to the book's argument, but genetic influence does not rescue lazy fatalism; differences in starting conditions and response patterns matter, yet long development, selection effects, and training structure still explain far more than talent myths usually admit.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Chapter 7 described the long demanding road to extraordinary performance. Chapter 8 tests the hardest counterargument directly by separating real biological differences from the exaggerated social story that talent explains most outcomes by itself.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter states the talent objection clearly rather than dodging it.
Then it separates genetic influence from strong fatalist conclusions.
Then it explains how training, selection, and survivorship distort common talent stories.
Then it preserves the limit that biology matters in some ways without becoming the master explanation.

Required Anchors:
- natural talent is the strongest apparent challenge to deliberate-development arguments
