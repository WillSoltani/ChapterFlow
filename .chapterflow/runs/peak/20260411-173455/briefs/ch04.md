Core Claim:
This chapter argues that deliberate practice is the gold standard for building elite performance because it combines field-specific goals, stretch beyond comfort, immediate feedback, and informed correction under standards that are already known.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Earlier chapters explained why practice can work and what it builds. This chapter defines the stricter training form that actually builds those gains reliably at the highest level.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter separates ordinary repetition from deliberate practice.
Then it shows that deliberate practice requires a field with stable standards and informed coaching or feedback.
Then it explains why the method is effortful, narrow, and often unpleasant.
Then it preserves the limit that not every domain offers the full conditions needed for pure deliberate practice.

Required Anchors:
- deliberate practice is narrower and harsher than ordinary practice
- stretch, feedback, correction, and repeatable standards work together
- expert coaching or informed external evaluation matters
- the limit that some fields do not support the full gold-standard version cleanly
