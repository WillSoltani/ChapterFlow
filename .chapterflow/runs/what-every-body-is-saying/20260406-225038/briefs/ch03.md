Core Claim:
The body often reacts first because human beings carry older protective responses built to manage danger, discomfort, surprise, and relief quickly. The chapter should explain timing rather than claim precise science: early body shifts often reflect regulation, protection, or orientation before language catches up.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Chapter 1 sold the promise and Chapter 2 taught the method. Chapter 3 explains why the method works as early as it does. Its distinct job is to explain speed without turning into unsupported neuroscience.
