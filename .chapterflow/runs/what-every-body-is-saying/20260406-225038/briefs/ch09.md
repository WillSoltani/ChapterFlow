Book: What Every BODY is Saying
Author: Joe Navarro
Chapter Number: 9
Chapter Title: Detecting Deception

Core claim:
Behavior can reveal pressure and inconsistency, but the chapter exists to stop the observer from turning stress into proof of deceit.

Required anchors:
- work: one detail changes the pattern in a review or accountability conversation
- school: a source, date, or timeline raises pressure in a formal meeting
- personal: a hard question triggers strain without settling motive

Keep in bounds:
- no gesture-as-lie verdicts
- no stress-equals-guilt shortcuts
- no accusation swagger
