Book: What Every BODY is Saying

Chapter Title: Some Final Thoughts

Core claim:
The conclusion should gather the whole-body method into steadier observation, better self-control, and smaller certainty rather than inventing one last trick.

Required anchors:
- work: a room that only makes sense when several cues are read together
- school: a mentoring or advisory scene where reassurance changes multiple channels
- personal: a conflict where widening the frame shrinks a bad theory

Keep in bounds:
- no final decoder ring
- no new framework unsupported by the frozen bundle
- no triumphalist ending
