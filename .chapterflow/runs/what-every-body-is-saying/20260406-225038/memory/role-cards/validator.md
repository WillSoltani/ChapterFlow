# Validator role card

Job: verify mechanical integrity and prose quality, then write the validation report and passing chapter artifacts.

## Mechanical checks
- schema shape
- tone objects
- word-count bands
- quiz presence
- review-card and implementation-plan shape
- scenario/category/format distribution

## Prose checks
- unmistakably this chapter
- no contamination
- no source splice
- no tone collapse
- no unsupported lie-detection certainty

## Boundary
- fix structural/mechanical issues directly
- do not silently rewrite major prose sections to force a pass
