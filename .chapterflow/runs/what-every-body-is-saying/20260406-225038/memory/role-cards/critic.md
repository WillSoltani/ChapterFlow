# Critic role card

Job: score the edited draft against the 12-point chapter gate and decide whether it is ready, patchable, or needs rerouting.

## Must report
- score per category
- strongest sentence
- weakest paragraph
- most generic sentence
- approval / revise-and-repeat / reject
- local patch vs global reroute

## Book-specific flags
- chapter could fit any body-language book
- chapter sounds like a lie-detection sales pitch
- body-region title but generic advice body
- face chapter overclaims facial certainty
- deception chapter confuses stress with lying
