# Style Memory — What Every BODY is Saying

Operating cheat sheet for writer/editor/converter. Distilled from the v13 pack style files plus this run's source constraints.

## Voice
Sharp human explainer. Concrete, observant, authored. Teach the idea directly. Use specific rooms, bodies, objects, and stakes. Keep the tone grounded and pragmatic rather than mystical, therapeutic, or macho.

## Book-specific posture
- This book is about reading nonverbal behavior with discipline, not pretending to read minds.
- Whole-body observation beats face fixation.
- Confidence, discomfort, engagement, withdrawal, and hesitation are safer categories than motive certainty.
- Deception claims must stay cautious. Stress is not proof of lying.

## Non-negotiables
- Use only facts, concepts, and claims supported by the brief and chapter source sidecar.
- No em dashes.
- No pseudo-science, fake precision, or neuro-jargon unless the brief explicitly supports it.
- No raw source splices into reader-facing prose.
- No writer-note leakage, validator language, or prompt contamination.

## Banned phrasing
- Avoid generic self-help language, corporate pep talk tone, and thesis-first openers.
- Avoid "In this chapter," "Have you ever," "Imagine you," "This matters because," "the art of," and other canned scaffolding.
- Avoid sounding like a detection guru selling certainty.

## Depth contract
- Easy: one clear frame, concrete nouns, graspable early example.
- Medium: mechanism plus application, one or two live distinctions.
- Hard: real boundary condition, failure mode, or unresolved tension.

## Tone differentiation
- Gentle: lowers resistance and gives the reader room.
- Direct: gives the cleanest mechanism and operational distinction.
- Competitive: raises stakes and consequence without becoming theatrical.

## Book-specific craft checks
- Each chapter must feel body-region specific when the chapter title is body-region specific.
- Use invented modern scenarios only; do not import anecdotes from the book unless source support is explicit.
- Keep the ethics clean: observation as awareness, not control fantasy.
