Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader understands how hands and fingers reveal confidence, stress, soothing, concealment, and emphasis, and how to read those signals without oversimplifying them.

Opening Move:
Open in a work scene where the words stay calm while the hands quietly tighten or disappear.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1, P5
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3, P4
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P4, P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: work scene showing tense or hidden hands before verbal admission
- P2: explain why hands and fingers matter as expressive and regulatory tools
- P3: map palms, concealment, rubbing, interlacing, and hand emphasis
- P4: school and personal examples of confidence, stress, and soothing
- P5: practical observer payoff for pacing and follow-up
- P6: props, tables, style, and habit cautions
- P7: bridge into the face chapter

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Distinguish expressive or soothing hand signal from full motive certainty.

Hard-Depth Tension:
When do hands offer a real read on confidence, strain, or self-soothing, and when do props, habit, or style outrank the first impression?
