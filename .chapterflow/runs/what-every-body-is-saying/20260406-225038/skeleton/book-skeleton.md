## Chapter order and one-line intents
1. **I See What You're Thinking** — opens the book's promise and teaches the reader to watch the whole body instead of chasing verbal content alone.
2. **Mastering the Secrets of Nonverbal Communication** — establishes the operating rules for observation: context, baseline, clusters, comfort/discomfort, and restraint.
3. **Living Our Limbic Legacy** — explains why fast, physical, emotionally honest reactions show up before polished explanations do.
4. **Getting a Leg Up on Body Language** — shows why feet and legs often reveal direction, preference, readiness, and withdrawal earlier than the face.
5. **Torso Tips** — tracks torso, chest, hips, and shoulders as signals of openness, protection, comfort, and territorial tension.
6. **Knowledge Within Reach** — narrows to arms as barriers, invitations, restraint signals, and distance-management tools.
7. **Getting a Grip** — focuses on hands and fingers as confidence, stress, self-soothing, and emphasis signals.
8. **The Mind's Canvas** — re-frames the face as useful but dangerous when read alone; expressions need context and timing.
9. **Detecting Deception: Proceed with Caution!** — warns against certainty, teaching readers to flag discomfort and inconsistency rather than declare lies.
10. **Some Final Thoughts** — closes with disciplined observation, self-presentation, and ethical caution rather than a new framework.
