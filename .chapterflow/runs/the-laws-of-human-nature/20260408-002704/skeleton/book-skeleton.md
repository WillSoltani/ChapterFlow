## Vocabulary Watchlist

- Avoid broad self-help words like `mindset`, `journey`, `unlock`, `harness`, `navigate`, and `game-changer`.
- Avoid pseudo-scientific authority moves.
- Keep analytical nouns concrete: `pattern`, `pressure`, `status`, `signal`, `friction`, `sequence`, `projection`.

## Motif And Callback Opportunities

- The reader's own blind spots should remain visible across the book.
- Collective irrationality in chapter 2 should echo later group conformity and generational myopia.
- Empathy in chapter 3 should counterbalance the cold-read techniques in chapters 4, 8, 16, and 17.
- The final chapter should loop back to the opening demand for self-knowledge rather than collapse into abstraction.

## Thin-Chapter Risk Notes

- Introduction risks turning into generic book-preface prose unless the method, limit, and next-step pressure are sharply defined.
- Gender Rigidity and Generational Myopia need careful narrowing so they do not drift into broad social commentary.
- Covetousness and Fickleness need strong modern scenario specificity to avoid chapter overlap.
