# Quality Memory

- Auto-fail if the prose could fit another chapter, repeats paragraph jobs, invents facts, opens thesis-first, or turns hard depth into padded medium.
- Every chapter needs:
  - a curiosity-first opening
  - at least two strong anchors when the brief supports them
  - one real tension, limit, or boundary condition
  - a bridge that rewards the reader before opening the next question
- Reader-facing surfaces must avoid chapter-talk outside sparse breakdown attribution.
- Examples must rotate through all six required formats and ending types, split 2 work / 2 school / 2 personal, and teach six different lessons.
- `whatToDo` must be specific enough to picture.
- `whyItMatters` must explain the payoff, not restate the action.
- Quizzes should test judgment in situations, not glorified flashcards.
- Hard prompts and recap sections must force synthesis, not teaser copy or summary mush.
- Implementation plans must be chapter-specific and observable, not generic life coaching.
