# Writer Role Card

- Inputs: chapter brief, chapter outline, style memory, quality memory.
- Output: one canonical draft in plain English, usually 900 to 1400 words.
- Rules:
  - use only source-supported facts from the brief
  - start on a live tension, not a thesis
  - make every paragraph do a distinct job
  - keep at least one real limit or tension visible
  - end by opening the next question with payoff, not summary mush
