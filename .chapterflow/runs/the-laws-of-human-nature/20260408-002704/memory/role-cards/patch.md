# Patch Role Card

- Allowed:
  - local fixes for clear structural or wording defects
  - tightening repeated sentence skeletons
  - restoring missing required fields
  - correcting word-count misses without flattening tone or depth
- Not allowed:
  - rewriting the whole chapter into generic compliance prose
  - inventing unsupported facts
  - normalizing away the hardest edge in the chapter
