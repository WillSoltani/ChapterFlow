Chapter Title: Seize the Historical Moment: The Law of Generational Myopia
Position In Book: seventeenth law chapter, chapter 18 of 19

Core Claim:
Greene argues that every generation is shaped by a distinct emotional climate, or zeitgeist, that feels natural from the inside. People become myopic when they keep reading the present through the assumptions that formed them earlier and miss the moment when the larger mood has changed.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the timing-and-historical-mood chapter. It shifts scale from concealed hostility inside individuals to the wider generational forces that shape taste, ambition, rebellion, leadership, and reception.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author uses Georges-Jacques Danton and Robespierre to show that even inside one revolution the emotional climate can shift, rewarding one kind of leader and then another.
Then the author defines generational myopia as the tendency to mistake one's formative assumptions for permanent reality, emphasizing zeitgeist, timing, and the emotional imprint of youth.
Then the author concludes that readers must study emerging moods, notice when their own instincts are lagging, and adapt their style, message, and ambition to the moment they are actually in.

Common Misreadings:
- reducing the chapter to shallow generation stereotypes
- treating zeitgeist as destiny that removes individual agency
- assuming youth automatically sees the future more clearly

Counterarguments or Limits:
People are not reducible to age cohort alone, and institutions, class, technology, and local culture all matter. Greene's point is narrower: people repeatedly misread the present because they overtrust the assumptions formed in youth and underread shifts in the broader mood.

Previous Chapter Bridge:
Chapter 17 showed how hidden hostility can shape relationships and institutions from the inside. Chapter 18 widens the lens, asking how whole generations misread their moment and why timing decides which styles of leadership or ambition fit.
Next Chapter Bridge:
Once the reader can see historical timing and generational mood, the final chapter narrows the lens again to mortality, arguing that finitude clarifies urgency, meaning, and proportion.
