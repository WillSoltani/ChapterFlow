Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand gender rigidity as overidentification with a one-sided masculine or feminine script that produces projection, weakness, and lost range.

Opening Move:
Open on Caterina Sforza as a figure who breaks narrow feminine coding and immediately unsettles rigid expectations about strength.

Anchor Allocation:
- Caterina Sforza used in P1 and P2
- gender rigidity used in P2 and P3
- anima, animus, integration, and projection used in P3 through P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: hook with Caterina Sforza and the shock of a figure who resists easy gender coding
- P2: define gender rigidity as one-sided identification that narrows range
- P3: explain anima and animus as the disowned complement and show why repression breeds projection
- P4: show practical signs such as brittle authority, performative softness, and hostility toward the traits one disowns
- P5: explain integration as fuller range rather than collapse into sameness
- P6: bridge to aimlessness by showing that freed energy still needs purpose and direction

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The key distinction is between integration and caricature. Integration widens human flexibility. Rigidity performs one side while disowning the other.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The reader must avoid both rigid pride and vague flattening, preserving real difference while rejecting one-sided identity as maturity.
