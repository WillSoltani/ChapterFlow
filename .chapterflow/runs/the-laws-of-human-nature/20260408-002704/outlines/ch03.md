Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand that self-love is universal, that deep narcissism turns other people into mirrors or tools, and that empathy is the practical corrective.

Opening Move:
Open on Stalin's ability to switch from warmth to intimidation because he needed total control over the emotional field around him.

Anchor Allocation:
- Stalin used in P1 and P2
- Tolstoy and Sonya used in P3 and P4
- Shackleton used in P5 and P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: hook with the strange mix of charm and fear around a narcissistic leader
- P2: define ordinary versus deep narcissism and show why criticism wounds so deeply
- P3: move from politics to intimacy through Tolstoy and Sonya
- P4: explain how self-reference destroys reciprocity and listening
- P5: introduce Shackleton as healthy self-regard disciplined by empathy
- P6: make empathy practical and bridge to the problem of masks

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The central distinction is between self-regard that can still take in other minds and self-regard that can only use other minds as mirrors, props, or enemies.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Empathy cannot mean dissolving boundaries or trying to heal manipulators through endless understanding. Healthy narcissism keeps structure, purpose, and self-belief while still seeing others clearly.

Takeaway Count Lock:
- Easy: 3 (always)
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 6

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- Takeaway 1: ordinary narcissism as the baseline
- Takeaway 2: deep narcissism and the wound under the surface
- Takeaway 3: narcissistic leadership and control hunger
- Takeaway 4: toxic reciprocity failure in close relationships
- Takeaway 5: empathy as outward attention and practical discipline
- Takeaway 6: boundary condition that empathy is not self-erasure

Scenario Lesson Map:
- Scenario 1 (decision_point, work): notice when status pain is blocking listening in a meeting
- Scenario 2 (postmortem, personal): review a conversation and find where comfort became self-display
- Scenario 3 (dialogue, school): distinguish confidence from attention hunger in a student setting
- Scenario 4 (predict_reveal, work): see how charisma without reciprocity collapses after criticism
- Scenario 5 (dilemma, personal): help someone without taking over their crisis
- Scenario 6 (before_after, school): show leadership improving when attention shifts outward
