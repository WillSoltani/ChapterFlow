Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand that character is a durable pattern, that compulsion reveals itself through repeated behavior under stress, and that trust should be calibrated through long-range evidence rather than charm or promises.

Opening Move:
Open on Howard Hughes as a figure whose brilliance and glamour hid deep rigidity, compulsion, and weakening character.

Anchor Allocation:
- Howard Hughes used in P1 and P2
- repeated pattern under frustration used in P3
- relation to work, apology, and adaptability used in P4 and P5

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: hook with Hughes and the danger of misreading talent as strength
- P2: define character as repeated emotional and behavioral pattern
- P3: explain compulsion, adversity, and why small repeated behaviors matter
- P4: identify strong-character signals such as work ethic, resilience, and adaptability
- P5: warn against grading apologies instead of changed pattern
- P6: show how to strengthen one's own character and bridge toward desire in the next chapter

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter's core distinction is between surface appeal and durable character. Trust must be earned by pattern over time, not by isolated charm or declarations.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Compassion matters, but compassion that ignores stable pattern becomes self-deception and strategic weakness.

Takeaway Count Lock:
- Easy: 3 (always)
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 6

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- Takeaway 1: character as durable pattern
- Takeaway 2: Howard Hughes and hidden brittleness
- Takeaway 3: compulsion under adversity and frustration
- Takeaway 4: work ethic, adaptability, and collaboration
- Takeaway 5: apology versus changed pattern
- Takeaway 6: building stronger character in oneself

Scenario Lesson Map:
- Scenario 1 (decision_point, work): delay promotion until repeated blame-shifting and pressure behavior are weighed against talent
- Scenario 2 (postmortem, personal): realize apologies were overvalued while the damaging pattern stayed intact
- Scenario 3 (dialogue, school): debate whether brilliance without steadiness counts as strong character
- Scenario 4 (predict_reveal, work): watch a polished operator unravel when setback removes easy admiration
- Scenario 5 (dilemma, personal): decide whether help should ignore a long pattern of unreliability
- Scenario 6 (before_after, school): improve team selection by weighting consistency and adaptation over sparkle

Next-Chapter Bridge:
Once trust is calibrated by character, the next chapter turns to desire and why people chase what stays just out of reach.

Genericity Risks:

- sounding morally smug or fatalistic
- forgetting the chapter's emphasis on pattern, adversity, and work

Forbidden Drift:
- one-strike condemnation
- deterministic "people never change" language
- confusing compassion with strategic blindness
