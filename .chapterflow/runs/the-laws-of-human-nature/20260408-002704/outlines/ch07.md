Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand that shortsightedness comes from emotional heat and compressed time horizons, and that distance is the practical tool that restores better judgment.

Opening Move:
Open on the South Sea Bubble as a vivid example of crowd desire turning immediate greed into catastrophic short-range thinking.

Anchor Allocation:
- South Sea Bubble used in P1 and P2
- Maria Mitchell used in P3 and P4
- distance, time scale, and second-order consequences used in P5 and P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: hook with the South Sea Bubble and the danger of present-tense crowd fever
- P2: define shortsightedness as emotion plus narrowed time horizon
- P3: introduce Maria Mitchell as the countermodel of patient observation and distance
- P4: explain the practical tools of delay, reframing, and second-order thinking
- P5: show how urgency, conflict, and desire all compress perspective in daily life
- P6: bridge to defensiveness by showing that distorted perspective hardens self-protective reactions

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter's core distinction is between true urgency and emotional urgency. Perspective means restoring a wider horizon before acting, not avoiding action forever.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The reader must learn to create distance without becoming passive, smug, or disconnected from real stakes.
