Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand that desire grows around scarcity, mystery, and fantasy, and that oversupply often weakens value.

Open on Coco Chanel as someone who turned style, independence, and controlled absence into a stronger object of desire.

- scarcity and fantasy used in P3 and P4

Paragraph Job Map:
