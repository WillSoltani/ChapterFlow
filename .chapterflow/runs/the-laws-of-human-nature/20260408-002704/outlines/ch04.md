Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand that people wear social masks, that true attitudes leak through clusters of signals, and that good observation must stay probabilistic rather than paranoid.

Opening Move:
Open on Milton Erickson learning to notice contradiction between what people said and what their bodies could not fully hide.

- Milton Erickson used in P1 and P2
- childhood masking used in P3
- Othello caution used in P4 and P6

Paragraph Job Map:

- P2: define masks as social adaptation rather than rare deception
- P3: explain leakage, baseline, and clusters
- P4: warn against projection through Othello-style misreading
- P5: show how deliberate role-playing can be used without falseness
- P6: bridge to character by separating momentary signal from long-term pattern

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter's core distinction is between noticing signals and pretending certainty. The reader should become better at probability, not addicted to instant verdicts.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Observation can sharpen accuracy, but only if the observer also watches their own projection and waits for clusters, context, and repetition.
