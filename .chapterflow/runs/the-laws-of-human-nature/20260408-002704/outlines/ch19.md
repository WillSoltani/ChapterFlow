Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand death denial as a hidden source of vanity and drift and should see mortality awareness as a way to recover purpose, empathy, and proportion.

Opening Move:
Open on Flannery O'Connor so the closing law begins with mortal seriousness that sharpens life rather than shrinking it.

Anchor Allocation:
- Flannery O'Connor used in P1, P3, and P6
- mortality, death denial, purpose, empathy, and finitude used in P2 through P7
- vanity, urgency, proportion, gratitude, and triviality used in P3 through P7

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: hook with Flannery O'Connor as a model of sharpened seriousness under mortality
- P2: define death denial and explain why people push finitude out of awareness
- P3: show how denial quietly feeds vanity, pettiness, aggression, and drift
- P4: explain why mortality awareness can deepen empathy and gratitude instead of producing despair
- P5: show practical signs of time-wasting illusion in work, school, and personal life
- P6: explain how to practice steady mortality awareness without melodrama or nihilism
- P7: close the loop on the whole book by connecting self-knowledge, proportion, and seriousness

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The key distinction is between theatrical obsession with death and steady awareness of finitude. The chapter is about proportion and clarity, not morbidity.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The reader must avoid both denial and grand performance. Mortality should sharpen humility and urgency without collapsing into panic, sentimentality, or productivity theater.
