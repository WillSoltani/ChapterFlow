Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand generational myopia as the habit of reading the present with expired assumptions and should know how to study mood, timing, and reception more accurately.

Opening Move:
Open on Georges-Jacques Danton and Robespierre so the law begins with a changing revolutionary mood rather than abstract sociology.

Anchor Allocation:
- Georges-Jacques Danton and Robespierre used in P1, P3, and P5
- zeitgeist, generation, timing, and myopia used in P2 through P6
- formative imprint, lagging instincts, and adaptation used in P3 through P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: hook with Danton and Robespierre as figures fitted to different phases of one historical moment
- P2: define generational myopia, zeitgeist, and the emotional imprint of youth
- P3: explain why each generation rebels, forms identity, and later risks hardening into its own blind spot
- P4: show how leaders and institutions fail when they keep applying a fading style to a changed mood
- P5: give practical signs of lagging timing and show how to read emerging values without flattening people into stereotypes
- P6: explain how to adapt by studying younger energies, cross-checking one's instincts, and adjusting ambition to the real moment
- P7: bridge from concealed individual hostility to historical mood, then from timing into mortality and proportion

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The key distinction is between structural mood and individual preference. The chapter is not about labeling people by age. It is about how formative assumptions become default lenses that can go stale when the wider historical mood changes.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The reader must avoid both fatalism and vanity. Generational mood shapes what feels compelling or outdated, but agency still matters because readers can study timing and adapt.

Scenario Lesson Map:

Next-Chapter Bridge:
After widening the frame to generations and historical timing, the final law narrows the frame again to mortality. Greene closes by arguing that finitude clarifies seriousness, empathy, and purpose.
