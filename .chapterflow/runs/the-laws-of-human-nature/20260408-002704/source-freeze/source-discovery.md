# Source Discovery

## Search Goal

Freeze enough official or authorized material to:

- lock the English edition automatically
- confirm reader-facing chapter order
- identify a safe scope for reader-facing prose
- identify early anchor material for the baseline chapters

## Source Ladder Used

1. Official publisher metadata and description
2. Authorized Google Books preview and contents page
3. Bibliographic work-family records
4. Narrow secondary chapter summaries for early baseline chapters

## Support Boundaries

- Exact quotes are not approved beyond very short metadata snippets already visible in the frozen bundle.
- Reader-facing prose will stay paraphrase-first.
- Historical flourish not supported by the frozen bundle should not be invented.
- Early baseline chapters may use the following anchor areas because frozen support exists:
  - Introduction: self-blindness, motive-reading, strategic awareness without paranoia
  - Chapter 2 / irrationality: Pericles, Athens after his death, market-bubble irrationality, Milton Erickson as a counter-model
  - Chapter 3 / narcissism: healthy versus deep narcissism, Stalin, Tolstoy, Shackleton
