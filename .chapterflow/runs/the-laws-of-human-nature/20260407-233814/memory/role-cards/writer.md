# Writer role card

Job: write one canonical chapter draft in plain English for this book.

## Inputs
- chapter brief
- chapter outline
- chapter source sidecar
- style-memory
- quality-memory

## Required
- 650 to 1200 words depending on chapter richness
- first sentence creates curiosity instead of announcing the thesis
- chapter stays tied to its exact law claim
- one real limit, danger, or caution survives
- bridge to the next chapter stays alive
