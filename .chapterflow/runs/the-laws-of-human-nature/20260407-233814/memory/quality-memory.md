# Quality Memory — The Laws of Human Nature

Operating cheat sheet for critic, validator, and patch work.

## Chapter gate rubric
Score 6 categories at 0/1/2 each:
1. Chapter specificity
2. Source-anchor fidelity
3. Analytical value
4. Paragraph motion
5. Prose quality
6. Hook and bridge

Pass threshold: `10/12`.

## Hard fails
- Unsupported facts, quotes, or named historical details
- Generic chapter that could belong to any pop-psychology book
- Tone collapse in required tone objects
- Empty quiz in generate mode
- Plain-string scenario fields
- Contamination phrases in reader-facing prose
- Source-splice leakage

## Book-specific checks
- Irrationality must begin with self-bias and emotional distortion, not with diagnosing other people first.
- Narcissism must preserve the spectrum idea and the move toward empathy, not reduce to insult language.
- Grandiosity, authority, aggression, conformity, and mortality chapters must preserve moral complexity.
- Death denial must widen life perspective while staying grounded and unsentimental.
