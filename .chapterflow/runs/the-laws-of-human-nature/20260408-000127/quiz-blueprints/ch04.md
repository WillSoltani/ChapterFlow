# Quiz blueprint

Chapter Number: 4
Chapter Title: See Through People's Masks: The Law of Role-playing

Bloom Ceiling: evaluate-create on q09-q10
Concept Budget: 3 to 5 live concepts
Primary Tested Concepts:
- Social masks are standard operating equipment. Everyone curates their presentation. Treating this as abnormal or morally suspect prevents you from reading people accurately.
- Facial microexpressions bypass conscious control. They appear in roughly 1/25 of a second and represent the genuine emotional reaction before the social mask reasserts itself.
- Voice is a high-bandwidth emotional channel. Pitch rises under stress, flattens during suppressed anger, and introduces micro-pauses during constructed (as opposed to recalled) statements.
- Incongruence between verbal and nonverbal signals is your highest-value data point. When words and body language conflict, the body language is almost always closer to the person's real state.
- The trained-versus-spontaneous distinction applies to all expressions. Trained expressions appear slightly delayed, last slightly too long, and often miss ancillary muscle engagement (such as eye crinkling in genuine smiles).

Prediction Questions:
- Chapter 5 examines character as the stable layer beneath shifting masks. Given what you now know about how masks crack under pressure, predict: which types of involuntary signals are most likely to reveal character rather than just momentary emotion?
- Chapter 5 addresses character, the stable behavioral substrate beneath shifting masks. The detection skills from this chapter become the tools for identifying which traits persist across contexts, which is the definition of character.

Likely Misreadings:
- reducing the chapter to a slogan
- mistaking a single moment for the whole principle
- forgetting the stated boundary condition
