# Quiz blueprint

Chapter Number: 17
Chapter Title: See the Hostility Behind the Friendly Facade: The Law of Aggression

Bloom Ceiling: evaluate-create on q09-q10
Concept Budget: 3 to 5 live concepts
Primary Tested Concepts:
- Suppressed aggression finds indirect outlets. Social norms push hostile impulses below the surface, where they re-emerge as passive obstruction, territorial overreaction, chronic low-level hostility, and disproportionate eruptions. The suppression changes the form, not the force.
- Outcomes reveal intent more reliably than tone. When a person is reliably pleasant but things reliably go wrong after their involvement, the pleasantness is the cover story. Assess people by the trail of results they leave, not by the warmth of their delivery.
- Territorial responses scale with perceived threat to the domain, not with the size of the boundary crossing. A minor incursion into claimed territory triggers a defense response calibrated to protect the entire territory, which is why the reaction often looks disproportionate to the trigger.
- Aggressive energy directed at objectives produces persistence that passive motivation cannot match. The goal is not suppression but redirection. Channel the impulse toward building, and the same force that could destroy relationships becomes fuel for sustained execution.
- Passive aggressors use indirection. Chronic aggressors maintain steady low-level hostility. Territorial aggressors erupt at boundary violations. Each type produces a recognizable trail of behavior. Accurate classification determines your response.

Prediction Questions:
- Chapter 18 scales the aggression framework from individual to generational. Using the concealment and redirection concepts from this chapter, predict the process by which suppressed generational aggression erupts into historical change.
- Chapter 18 examines generational aggression cycles. The bridge: this chapter showed how suppression produces indirect expression at the individual level. Chapter 18 will argue the same process operates at the generational level, with the eruptions shaping historical turning points rather than personal conflicts.

Likely Misreadings:
- reducing the chapter to a slogan
- mistaking a single moment for the whole principle
- forgetting the stated boundary condition
