# Quiz blueprint

Chapter Number: 13
Chapter Title: Reconnect to the Masculine or Feminine Within You: The Law of Gender Rigidity

Bloom Ceiling: evaluate-create on q09-q10
Concept Budget: 3 to 5 live concepts
Primary Tested Concepts:
- Your default behavioral range was selected for you, not by you. Social environment determined which traits were reinforced and which were suppressed. Recognizing this as conditioning rather than identity is the first step toward expanding your options.
- Suppression creates distortion, not elimination. A capacity that is denied conscious expression will emerge unconsciously in dysfunctional forms. Passive aggression, emotional volatility, and sabotage are common distortions of suppressed assertiveness, vulnerability, and autonomy.
- Integration beats specialization in complex environments. Caterina Sforza's advantage was not superiority in any single trait. It was the breadth of her repertoire. She could deploy force or diplomacy as the situation demanded, which made her unpredictable to opponents who expected one or the other.
- Behavioral expansion follows a trainable sequence: identify the gap, practice in controlled conditions, then integrate for automatic access. The process is identical to any other skill acquisition. Discomfort during practice is expected and temporary.
- The chapter sits in a deliberate sequence. Chapter 12 identified rigid self-image as a limitation. Chapter 13 targets the deepest source of that rigidity: conditioned behavioral constraints. Chapter 14 will argue that sustained purpose requires the full repertoire this chapter builds.

Prediction Questions:
- Chapter 14 addresses sustained purposeful direction. Using the behavioral range architecture from this chapter, predict why Greene argues that purpose fails without integration, and what specific failure mode a rigid person encounters when pursuing long-term goals.
- Chapter 14 shifts from capability to deployment. The behavioral range you built in this chapter becomes the engine for sustained purposeful direction. Greene will argue that most failures of purpose are not failures of motivation but failures of flexibility, where the person could not adapt their approach when conditions changed. The integrated self from Chapter 13 is the solution to that failure mode.

Likely Misreadings:
- reducing the chapter to a slogan
- mistaking a single moment for the whole principle
- forgetting the stated boundary condition
