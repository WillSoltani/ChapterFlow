# Quiz blueprint

Chapter Number: 3
Chapter Title: Transform Self-Love into Empathy: The Law of Narcissism

Bloom Ceiling: evaluate-create on q09-q10
Concept Budget: 3 to 5 live concepts
Primary Tested Concepts:
- Narcissism operates on a spectrum from healthy to deep. Healthy narcissism produces confidence and risk tolerance. Deep narcissistic tendency produces an inability to perceive others as real. The position on this line is not fixed; it shifts under stress.
- Visceral empathy is nonverbal decoding. It focuses on posture, micro-expressions, breathing, and gesture to identify what someone actually feels, which often differs from what they verbally report.
- The four markers of deep narcissistic tendency are: control disguised as care, disproportionate reaction to criticism, idealize-devalue cycling, and empathy absence. These form a diagnostic checklist for interpersonal risk.
- Empathy is a trainable skill, not a fixed characteristic. Erickson's case demonstrates that extraordinary empathy developed from sustained, deliberate observation under constraint, not from innate disposition.
- Chapter 2's emotional regulation is the precondition for Chapter 3's empathy. Unmanaged internal reactivity consumes the attentional bandwidth needed to read nonverbal signals.

Prediction Questions:
- Chapter 4 addresses social masks. Based on the empathy skills from this chapter, predict what specific technique Greene will recommend for detecting when someone's public performance diverges from their actual state.
- Chapter 4 builds on visceral empathy to decode social masks. Masks are maintained through controlled channels; empathy reads the uncontrolled ones. The gap between the two reveals what the performance conceals.

Likely Misreadings:
- reducing the chapter to a slogan
- mistaking a single moment for the whole principle
- forgetting the stated boundary condition
