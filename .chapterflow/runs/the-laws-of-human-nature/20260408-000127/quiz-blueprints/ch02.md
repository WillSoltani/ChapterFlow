Concept Budget: 3 to 5 live concepts
Primary Tested Concepts:
- The affect heuristic substitutes feeling for analysis automatically. Your first response to any emotional trigger is not a thought. It is a sensation that your brain retroactively labels as a conclusion. Recognizing this substitution is the prerequisite for every other skill in this book.
- Inflaming factors are identifiable triggers that reliably degrade rational processing. Sudden stress overloads the system with urgency signals. Group pressure converts collective emotion into the sensation of independent judgment. Narcissistic wounds redirect cognitive resources toward self-image repair at the expense of accurate assessment.
- Delay and suppression are different processes with opposite outcomes. Suppression pushes emotion underground, where it accumulates and eventually distorts behavior. Delay acknowledges the emotion, holds the action, and allows the analytical process to produce a higher-quality response.
- Rationality is not the absence of emotion. It is emotion under the direction of a longer-term calculus. Greene uses Athena, the goddess of strategic warfare, to distinguish reactive intensity (Ares) from directed intensity (Athena). Both are engaged. Only one is effective.
- Rationality operates as a trainable skill, not a fixed trait. Each instance of successfully inserting a delay between trigger and response strengthens the neural pathway for future delay. The skill compounds with repetition, which means early investment produces accelerating returns.
