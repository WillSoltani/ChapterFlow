# Quiz blueprint

Chapter Number: 5
Chapter Title: Determine the Strength of People's Character: The Law of Compulsive Behavior

Bloom Ceiling: evaluate-create on q09-q10
Concept Budget: 3 to 5 live concepts
Primary Tested Concepts:
- Character formation happens in childhood and hardens through repetition. Adult behavior under stress is a replay of early coping strategies, not a fresh response to current conditions.
- Stress strips the performance layer and exposes default behavior. The person you see under pressure is closer to the real operating network than the person you see during calm, controlled conditions.
- Behavioral repetition under similar conditions is the most reliable character indicator. A single instance might be situational. Two instances form a signal. Three confirm a default.
- Strong character signals: delayed gratification, adaptability under disruption, consistent behavior across audiences. Weak character signals: impulsiveness, rigidity under stress, behavior that shifts depending on the observer.
- Red flags for weak character include extreme perfectionism (control substituting for adaptability), chronic excuse-making (externalization substituting for accountability), and never-wrong syndrome (rigidity substituting for learning). Each is a coping structure built on top of an unresolved early groove.
