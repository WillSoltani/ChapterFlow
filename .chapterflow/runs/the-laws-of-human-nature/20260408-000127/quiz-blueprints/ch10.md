# Quiz blueprint

Chapter Number: 10
Chapter Title: Confront Your Dark Side: The Law of Repression

Bloom Ceiling: evaluate-create on q09-q10
Concept Budget: 3 to 5 live concepts
Primary Tested Concepts:
- Your public self is a curated performance. The persona you present to the world leaves out the qualities you consider unacceptable. Those excluded traits do not vanish. They accumulate as the shadow, and the tighter you hold the persona, the more volatile the shadow becomes.
- Outsized judgment of others is a shadow signal. Projection takes the quality you refuse to own and attaches it to someone else. The emotional charge you feel when criticizing them is energy that actually belongs to your own internal conflict.
- The repression cycle has three stages. First, you deny the trait exists. Second, the denied trait gains influence because it is operating without oversight. Third, it erupts in behavior that feels alien to you but has been building for a long time.
- Nixon illustrates the full repression cycle at presidential scale. He built a persona of composed statesmanship while harboring deep suspicion and vindictiveness. Those traits shaped Watergate, the cover-up, and the decisions that ended his career, all while he believed he was acting rationally.
- Integration means recognition, not indulgence. Acknowledging that you have a capacity for manipulation does not mean you start manipulating. It means the impulse becomes visible, which is the prerequisite for choosing a different response.
