# Quiz blueprint

Chapter Number: 9
Chapter Title: Change Your Circumstances by Changing Your Attitude: The Law of Self-Sabotage

Bloom Ceiling: evaluate-create on q09-q10
Concept Budget: 3 to 5 live concepts
Primary Tested Concepts:
- Attitude operates as a data-selection filter. It determines which subset of available information reaches your conscious attention. Two people in the same room literally perceive different realities because their filters highlight different data. The filter, not the room, is the primary variable.
- The attitude-perception-behavior-response loop runs automatically until deliberately interrupted. Each stage feeds the next, creating a self-confirming cycle that feels like objective observation but is actually circular reasoning. The intervention point is between attitude and perception.
- Hostile, anxious, and avoidant are the three primary toxic filters. Each generates a distinct self-sabotage signature: manufactured conflict, manufactured crises, and manufactured isolation. Identifying which filter you default to is the first step in replacing it.
- The generous eye is a perceptual upgrade, not a softening. Chekhov saw more of reality than his bitter siblings, not less. By looking past the surface behavior to the driving emotion beneath it, he gained access to information that a resentful filter would have screened out.
- Default filters are inherited, not selected. Most people's baseline attitudes were shaped by early environments they did not control. Recognizing that your filter is a product of conditioning rather than accurate assessment of reality makes it available for conscious revision.
