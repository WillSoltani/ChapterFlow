# Quiz blueprint

Chapter Number: 8
Chapter Title: Soften People's Resistance by Confirming Their Self-Opinion: The Law of Defensiveness

Bloom Ceiling: evaluate-create on q09-q10
Concept Budget: 3 to 5 live concepts
Primary Tested Concepts:
- Defensiveness precedes evaluation. The identity protection system fires before the analytical system engages. By the time the person is consciously processing your argument, the emotional verdict has already been rendered.
- Flattery triggers skepticism. Validation triggers safety. Generic compliments activate the same suspicion filter that detects manipulation. Specific confirmation of a person's self-image bypasses that filter because it matches internal data the person already holds.
- Sequencing is non-negotiable. Confirm the self-image before introducing the argument. Reversing the order means the argument hits a defended mind, and the validation that follows reads as an apology rather than a genuine opening.
- Five dimensions govern the defensive response: autonomy, status, certainty, fairness, relatedness. Each represents a non-negotiable aspect of self-concept. Trigger one negatively and defenses activate. Confirm one positively and processing capacity opens.
- Sustainable influence requires alignment, not deception. Johnson's senators did not regret their votes because the legislation matched values they actually held. When influence exploits rather than aligns, the resulting agreement is brittle and collapses under review.

Prediction Questions:

- Chapter 9 moves the analysis upstream. If the defensive system monitors incoming information continuously, then the nonverbal signals you project before speaking are already being classified as safe or threatening. Greene will argue that attitude projection is the first variable in the influence sequence, preceding even the confirmation step.

Likely Misreadings:
