# Quiz blueprint

Chapter Number: 14
Chapter Title: Advance with a Sense of Purpose: The Law of Aimlessness

Bloom Ceiling: evaluate-create on q09-q10
Concept Budget: 3 to 5 live concepts
Primary Tested Concepts:
- The Life's Task is a discovery, not a decision. You are not choosing a purpose from a menu. You are excavating one that has been present since your earliest fascinations but was overwritten by external signals.
- Goals are consumable; purpose is renewable. A goal, once achieved or failed, is spent. Purpose survives both outcomes and produces the next goal automatically. Purpose-driven people sustain momentum that goal-driven people burn through and never recover.
- Unsupervised behavior is the most reliable data on primal inclination. What you choose to do when there is no audience, no grade, and no paycheck reveals the orientation that social performance obscures.
- Certainty is a product of commitment, not a prerequisite for it. King did not have a roadmap when he said yes to the boycott. He had a strong enough signal to commit, and the commitment itself revealed the next step.
- The Law of Aimlessness targets borrowed ambition, not low effort. Aimless people are often hardworking and accomplished. The problem is that their accomplishments serve a narrative written by someone else.

Prediction Questions:
- Chapter 15 addresses group conformity. Using the goal-versus-purpose method from this chapter, predict why purpose-driven individuals are fundamentally more resistant to group absorption than goal-driven individuals.
- Chapter 15 addresses group conformity and the forces that dissolve individual direction into collective identity. The Life's Task you define here is the variable that determines resistance. Consider: if purpose is the anchor, what specifically makes aimless people vulnerable to group absorption?

Likely Misreadings:
- reducing the chapter to a slogan
- mistaking a single moment for the whole principle
- forgetting the stated boundary condition
