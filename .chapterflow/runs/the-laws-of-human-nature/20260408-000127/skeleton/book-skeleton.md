## Moral complexity flags
- Keep strategic-awareness distance in chapters on role-playing, desire, envy, aggression, conformity, and leadership.
- Do not confuse diagnosis with endorsement.
- Introduction must warn against paranoia as clearly as it warns against naivete.

## Rotation plan for examples
- Every chapter uses 2 work, 2 school, and 2 personal examples.
- Work settings rotate among managers, client work, team meetings, hiring, and leadership scenes.
- School settings rotate among seminars, lab work, advising meetings, clubs, debate, newsroom, and project groups.
- Personal settings rotate among siblings, partners, roommates, friendships, family dinners, and group chats.

## School-setting plan
- Keep school scenes contemporary and concrete: seminar rooms, dorm kitchens, advising offices, labs, campus newsrooms, debate prep rooms.
