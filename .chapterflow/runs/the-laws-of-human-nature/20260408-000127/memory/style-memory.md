# Style Memory — The Laws of Human Nature

Operating cheat sheet for writer, editor, and converter. Distilled from the v14 segmented pack plus this run's source limits.

## Voice
Concrete, sharp, readable, and socially observant. Strong openings, distinct paragraph jobs, live modern rooms, and practical consequence over filler abstraction.

## Book-specific posture
- Keep the frame on hidden motive, self-deception, masks, status pressure, envy, aggression, and mortality.
- Read the book as strategic awareness, not moral endorsement.
- Use social realism over generic self-help.
- Make every chapter feel unmistakably tied to its named law.

## Non-negotiables
- Paraphrase-first. No invented facts, quotes, studies, or unsupported mechanisms.
- No em dashes.
- No fake neuroscience or prestige vocabulary not supported by the brief.
- No raw source splice into reader-facing prose.
- No chapter-talk in takeaways, prompts, examples, quiz explanations, or implementation plans.

## Depth contract
- Easy clarifies one clean frame.
- Medium explains mechanism and application.
- Hard must preserve a real tension, failure mode, or boundary condition.

## Tone differentiation
- Gentle lowers resistance.
- Direct diagnoses the mechanism cleanly.
- Competitive raises stakes without cartoon aggression.

## Book-specific craft checks
- Introduction must frame motive-reading, self-blindness, and precision without tipping into paranoia.
- Law chapters must keep one distinct social mechanism each.
- Later chapters should synthesize earlier ideas without flattening differences.
- Moral-complexity chapters need strategic distance rather than endorsement.
