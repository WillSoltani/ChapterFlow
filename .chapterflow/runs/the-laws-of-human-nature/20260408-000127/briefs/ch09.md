Chapter Title: Change Your Circumstances by Changing Your Attitude: The Law of Self-Sabotage
Position In Book: package chapter 9 of 19

Core Claim:
Attitude operates as a data-selection filter on sensory input. It determines which subset of available information reaches conscious attention and which gets discarded. The filter, not the environment, is the primary variable determining your experienced reality. The attitude-perception-behavior-response loop is the core architecture of self-sabotage. Each stage feeds the next in a self-confirming cycle that operates below conscious attention and produces outcomes that feel like objective proof of the attitude's accuracy. The generous eye is a perceptual upgrade that provides more data, not less. Chekhov's ability to see the vulnerability beneath hostility gave him a more complete model of every person and situation he encountered. Resentment is a low-resolution filter. Curiosity is a high-resolution one.

Counterarguments or Limits:
- Conscious filter replacement has a failure mode: stress-triggered reinstallation. Under pressure, the old filter reasserts itself because the repressed emotional material that generated it remains unprocessed. Sustainable change requires addressing both the filter and its source, which is the subject of Chapter 10.
