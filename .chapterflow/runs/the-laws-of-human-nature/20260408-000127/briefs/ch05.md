Chapter Title: Determine the Strength of People's Character: The Law of Compulsive Behavior

Core Claim:
Behavioral defaults are installed during early-childhood neural plasticity windows and automated through years of repetition. By adulthood, they fire faster than deliberate thought, making stress the most reliable exposure condition. Observed stressed-state behavior is a sample from a distribution, not an isolated data point. The same output has been generated across many prior stressful events. Your observation is late, not early. Among the three character strength indicators, delayed gratification has the highest predictive validity. It correlates with impulse control, long-term planning capacity, and the ability to sustain commitment through discomfort.
