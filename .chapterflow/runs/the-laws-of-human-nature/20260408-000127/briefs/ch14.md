Core Claim:
The Life's Task is a discovery, not a decision. You are not choosing a purpose from a menu. You are excavating one that has been present since your earliest fascinations but was overwritten by external signals. Goals operate on a discrete payoff structure; purpose operates on a continuous one. A goal, once achieved or failed, is spent. Purpose survives both outcomes and produces the next goal automatically. Certainty is a product of commitment, not a prerequisite. The Life's Task reveals itself iteratively. Each commitment generates data that informs the next commitment. Waiting for full clarity before acting guarantees you never receive it.
