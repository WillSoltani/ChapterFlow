Core Claim:
The narcissism spectrum is a situational variable, not a personality constant. Healthy narcissism provides internal stability. Deep narcissistic tendency produces perceptual closure. Stress, sleep debt, and social rejection all push the needle toward the deep end. Visceral empathy targets the nonverbal channel: posture, micro-expressions, breathing rate, gesture velocity, and proximity. This channel is less controlled than speech and leaks emotional data the speaker may not intend to share. Deep narcissistic tendency presents four diagnostic markers: control disguised as helpfulness, disproportionate reaction to feedback, idealize-devalue cycling, and empathy absence masked by learned social scripts.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
**Narcissism operates on a spectrum from healthy to deep.** Healthy narcissism produces confidence and risk tolerance. Deep narcissistic tendency produces an inability to perceive others as real. The position on this line is not fixed; it shifts under stress.
