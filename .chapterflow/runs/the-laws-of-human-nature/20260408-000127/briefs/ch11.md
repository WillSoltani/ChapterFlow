Core Claim:
The perceptual conversion from inferiority to moral conviction is the defining feature of envy. It happens preconsciously, which means the envious person's subsequent behavior is sincere. They genuinely believe their criticism is principled, making direct confrontation about the underlying envy largely futile. Envy intensity is a function of status proximity, domain overlap, and shared starting conditions. The closer two people are on these three variables, the more one person's advancement creates an involuntary comparison the other cannot neutralize without either honest self-examination or defensive distortion. The diagnostic value of the four signals lies in co-occurrence. Any single signal has innocent explanations. Two or more from the same person in a compressed timeline shifts the assessment from 'possible' to 'probable,' and three signals move it to 'actionable.'
