Core Claim:
The affect heuristic substitutes emotional output for analytical output below the threshold of conscious detection. Neuroimaging confirms that emotional processing centers activate 200-300ms before prefrontal deliberative regions. Your first response is, by definition, emotional, regardless of how rational it feels. Sudden stress compresses the decision timeline below the threshold for deliberative processing. The sympathetic nervous arrangement floods the body with urgency signals that are appropriate for physical emergencies but processatically degrade decision quality in social and professional contexts. Emotional contagion in group settings bypasses individual evaluation. Mirror neurons and conformity circuits cause the collective emotional state to be absorbed and processed as independently generated judgment, making group decisions particularly susceptible to emotional hijacking.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
