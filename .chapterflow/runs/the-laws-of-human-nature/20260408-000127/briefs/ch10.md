Chapter Title: Confront Your Dark Side: The Law of Repression

Core Claim:
The shadow is a structural feature of personality, not a moral failure. Every person develops a persona to function socially. The shadow is the remainder: whatever the persona excludes. The danger is not in having a shadow. The danger lives in pretending you do not have one. Nixon is a case study in shadow amplification. His political intelligence was genuine. So was his paranoia. The tragedy was that one operated in the light and the other in the dark, and the one in the dark eventually consumed everything the one in the light had built. Projection operates as both a symptom and a signal. It protects the ego by externalizing the disowned trait. It also exposes the ego by revealing exactly which traits have been disowned. The person who learns to read projections inward gains access to their own blind spots.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
**Your public self is a curated performance.** The persona you present to the world leaves out the qualities you consider unacceptable. Those excluded traits do not vanish. They accumulate as the shadow, and the tighter you hold the persona, the more volatile the shadow becomes.
