Chapter Title: Elevate Your Perspective: The Law of Shortsightedness

Core Claim:
The neural architecture for threat response does not distinguish between physical and social or economic threats. High arousal activates rapid-response circuits that compress temporal focus regardless of context. Intelligence is downstream of this system, not upstream. You cannot think your way past a response that fires before thinking begins. The four markers form a reinforcing cycle, not independent symptoms. Emotional reactivity feeds quick-fix behavior. Quick-fix orientation prevents trend analysis. Absence of trend analysis increases reliance on social consensus. Social consensus amplifies emotional reactivity. Disrupting any single link weakens the entire chain. Consequential depth is trainable. The discipline of extending your analysis from first to second to third-order consequences is not innate. Like any trained capacity, the skill sharpens with structured repetition and atrophies the moment you stop practicing.
