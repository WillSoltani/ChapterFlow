Core Claim:
Defensive activation outpaces rational processing. The neural pathway for identity threat detection operates faster than the pathway for deliberate analysis. Arguments that trigger identity defense are emotionally classified before they are rationally evaluated, which means the distortion is invisible to the person experiencing it. The authenticity filter distinguishes flattery from validation at the neural level. Generic praise carries no targeting data and fails to match internal self-model content. Specific validation references actual self-concept investments and produces a match that the brain registers as genuine recognition. Three stages: confirm, mirror, redirect. Confirmation lowers the defensive gate. Mirroring deepens rapport to a level that generic acknowledgment cannot reach. Redirection introduces the desired outcome as self-expression rather than compliance. Skipping a stage weakens the entire sequence.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
**Defensiveness precedes evaluation.** The identity protection system fires before the analytical system engages. By the time the person is consciously processing your argument, the emotional verdict has already been rendered.
