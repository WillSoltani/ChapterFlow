Core Claim:
The desire for leadership and the resistance to unearned authority are paired responses. Followers run a continuous evaluation of whether the leader deserves the role. Passing the evaluation accumulates trust. Failing it accumulates resentment. Both compound over time. The four pillars are interdependent. Competence without empathy produces cold efficiency. Vision without competence produces empty enthusiasm. Empathy without consistency produces warmth that people enjoy but do not trust. Consistency without vision produces reliable directionlessness. Single-style leadership has a predictable failure condition. Each style is optimal for specific circumstances and suboptimal for others. The Adaptive leader who has developed genuine capability in all styles cannot be defeated by a change in conditions that invalidates any single approach.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
**Positional authority produces compliance but not commitment.** The gap between the two is the gap between claimed authority and demonstrated authority. Closing it requires visible proof, not verbal assertion.
