Chapter Title: Seize the Historical Moment: The Law of Generational Myopia

Core Claim:
Generational conditioning functions as a perceptual filter, not a set of beliefs. Beliefs can be examined and updated. Filters shape what you notice in the first place. The formative conditions of your youth installed a filter that determines which information registers as important and which gets dismissed before conscious evaluation begins. The generational cycle is self-generating. Each archetype's defining achievement creates the environment that shapes the next archetype's defining reaction. This means the cycle is not driven by random events but by the predictable consequences of each generation's collective behavior. Zeitgeist gaps peak at generational transition points. The gap between what the outgoing generation built and what the incoming generation demands is widest when the old order is visibly straining but the new order has not yet formed. Positioning at this moment produces disproportionate returns.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
**Generational personality is installed, not chosen.** The conditions of your formative years produce a set of default assumptions that you carry forward as 'common sense.' Recognizing these defaults as products of timing rather than truth is the prerequisite for seeing beyond them.
