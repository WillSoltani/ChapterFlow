# Style Memory

- Write like a sharp human explainer: concrete, selective, readable, and unmistakably authored.
- Open on friction, novelty, status pressure, or a live pitch mistake instead of a thesis-first abstract claim.
- Keep each chapter tied to the specific mechanism in `Pitch Anything`, not to generic sales or communication advice.
- Easy should feel accessible and vivid, medium should explain mechanism and application, and hard should hold onto a real limit or tension.
- Tone differences must do different jobs: gentle lowers resistance, direct clarifies the move, competitive sharpens leverage and consequences.
- Cut corporate polish, neuroscience theater, pseudo-precision, contamination phrases, and reusable podcast-summary prose.
