# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter: Telling the Story
Core concepts to test:
- why story follows frame in the STRONG method
- how story transfers meaning faster than a pile of facts
- why sequence and stakes matter more than full chronology
- how rambling exposition weakens attention and status
- the limit against manipulative or indulgent storytelling

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: evaluate / transfer

Question plan:
- q01: core definition of story in practical pitch terms
- q02: why story comes after frame and before intrigue
- q03: how sequence helps an audience follow consequence
- q04: work scenario with a founder choosing story over fact-dump
- q05: trap between concise story and autobiography
- q06: school postmortem on a recommendation that lacked narrative setup
- q07: personal ask where a turning point matters more than a resume list
- q08: predict-reveal on compressing a long backstory into a pointed sequence
- q09: deeper synthesis on the limit of story as a persuasion tool
- q10: bridge toward intrigue as the next move in the method

Distractor notes:
- tempting wrong answer type 1: answers that define story as decoration or emotional fluff
- tempting wrong answer type 2: answers that treat full chronology as automatically superior

Explanation diversity notes:
- avoid repeated openers
- keep explanations tied to sequence, stakes, attention, and disciplined compression

CorrectIndex plan:
- keep distribution roughly balanced across 0 / 1 / 2
