# Baseline Quality Floor

Baseline chapters: Chapter 1 and Chapter 2

## Shared strengths

- Both chapters open with a concrete room-level failure before abstracting into method.
- Both stay tightly bounded to the book's own mechanics instead of drifting into generic presentation advice.
- Both preserve the anti-fake-science / anti-magic boundary that keeps the promise credible.
- Both validated chapters keep the full EMH stack, six distributed examples, ten quiz questions, five review cards, and clean review-package wrappers.

## Chapter-specific strengths

- Chapter 1 sets the book-wide promise clearly: effort is not the same thing as method, and better pitch control cannot counterfeit weak substance forever.
- Chapter 2 sharpens the audience-processing mechanism clearly: the room filters for salience before it grants deeper analysis full access.

## Floor for later waves

- No later chapter should fall below critic 10/12 without rerouting through stronger prose passes.
- Hooks should stay vivid and room-specific, not generic or slogan-like.
- Reader-facing structured surfaces must teach directly and avoid meta-distance.
- Claims must stay bounded by the frozen source bundle and avoid inflated neuroscience or unsupported generalization.
- Later chapters must preserve usable bridges into the next chapter rather than ending as isolated summaries.
- Validation minima remain mandatory: full artifact chain, clean chapter-gate lint, full review-package wrapper match, and word-floor compliance across EMH breakdowns.

- Treat Chapter 1 and Chapter 2 as the reference pair for later wave checks under `qualityDecayStopDelta = 1.5`.
