Core book thesis:
The book argues that most pitches fail because they aim at rational explanation before they win attention, status, and frame control. Klaff presents pitching as a repeatable method built around how people process threat, novelty, status, and desire. The book's core operating sequence is the STRONG method: set and own the frame, tell the story, reveal intrigue, offer the prize, nail the hookpoint, and get a decision without showing neediness.
