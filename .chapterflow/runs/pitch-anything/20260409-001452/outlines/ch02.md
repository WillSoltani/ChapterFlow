Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter is not anti-logic. It is arguing that logic must survive a first filter for relevance and salience before deeper reasoning can even engage.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Simplifying for a fast first filter can help truth get through, but used badly it can turn into oversimplification that makes the audience react to theater instead of substance.

Threshold Question:
When does simplifying for the audience's first filter help a strong idea survive, and when does it become an excuse to oversimplify reality until the listener is reacting to theater instead of truth?
