Surprise!

Supported by frozen bundle sources: Harriman House official contents page, Google Books bibliographic preview, and catalog listings where needed.

Focus claims:
- History is steered by surprises so often that planning as if surprise were rare is itself a major forecasting mistake.
- People overweight what is recent and visible, then build narratives that assume tomorrow will mostly resemble the range they already know.
- The chapter asks readers to plan with humility, widen ranges, and stop treating the absence of recent shocks as proof that shocks are unlikely.
