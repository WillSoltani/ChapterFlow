You'll Change

Supported by frozen bundle sources: Harriman House official contents page, Google Books bibliographic preview, and catalog listings where needed.

Focus claims:
- People underestimate how much their future priorities will differ from the priorities they defend most confidently today.
- Identity drift makes extreme long-term commitments dangerous because the self who bears the cost may not share the same goals as the self who made the promise.
- The practical response is to avoid locking life around one forecast of who you will become and to leave some room to revise without humiliation.
