Focus claims:
- Good returns always charge a fee, and in investing the fee is usually emotional discomfort rather than a line item on a statement.
- People want the upside of volatility without the experience of volatility, which leads them to abandon good assets at the moment the fee comes due.
- The practical move is to choose a level of uncertainty you can actually hold through and to stop confusing price fluctuation with proof of failure.
