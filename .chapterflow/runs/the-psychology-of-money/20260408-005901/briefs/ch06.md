Chapter Title: Getting Wealthy vs. Staying Wealthy
Position In Book: 6 of 22

Core Claim:
The traits that help create wealth are not the same traits that preserve it. Accumulation may reward boldness and optimism, but staying wealthy requires survival, humility, and room for error.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This chapter sharpens the book's survival theme into a direct contrast between two different games. It separates the excitement of building from the discipline of not losing what was built.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author distinguishes the skill of accumulation from the skill of preservation.
Then the author shows why getting wealthy often involves risk-taking, but staying wealthy requires fear of ruin.
Then the author argues that survival is the central priority once compounding is underway.
Then the author frames room for error and humility as practical tools rather than timid habits.
Then the author bridges to tails and asymmetric outcomes by showing that long-run success tolerates many misses if survival is preserved.
