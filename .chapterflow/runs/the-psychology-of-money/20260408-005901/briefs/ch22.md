- Chapter: 22
- Title: `Postscript: A Brief History of Why the U.S. Consumer Thinks the Way They Do`
- Objective: Explain how postwar prosperity, rising security, expanding opportunity, easy consumer abundance, and credit culture helped shape a consumer mindset that expects growth, access, comfort, and improvement.
- Continuity bridge in: Chapter 21 narrowed the framework to one person's settings; this postscript widens to a broader historical explanation of how a whole consumer culture was formed.
- Continuity bridge out: This is the final reader-facing unit and should land the book with context rather than a new behavioral rule.
- Core risks to avoid: overclaiming historical specifics, drifting into unsupported economic detail, or losing the connection between lived consumer expectations and money psychology.
