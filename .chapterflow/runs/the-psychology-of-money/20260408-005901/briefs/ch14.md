# Chapter 14 Brief

- Chapter: 14
- Title: `Room for Error`
- Objective: Show that margin is not wasted space in a plan but a deliberate way to survive uncertainty, ordinary volatility, and your own imperfect judgment.
- Continuity bridge in: Chapter 13 argued that forecasts repeatedly miss from the edges; this chapter turns that humility into a design rule by building slack before pressure arrives.
- Continuity bridge out: Prepare Chapter 15 by showing that even robust plans still face another uncertainty source: the person making them will change over time.
- Core risks to avoid: making margin sound like cowardice, flattening the chapter into generic safety advice, or ignoring the tradeoff that too much slack can become stagnation.
