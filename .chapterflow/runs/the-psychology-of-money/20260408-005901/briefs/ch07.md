# Chapter 7 Brief

- Chapter: 7
- Title: `Tails, You Win`
- Objective: Explain why a small number of outsized outcomes often drive most long-run success, and why survival matters because it keeps a person available for those tails.
- Continuity bridge in: Chapter 6 established preservation and anti-ruin logic.
- Continuity bridge out: Lead into freedom by showing that enough wealth matters because it can buy control over time.
- Core risks to avoid: statistical jargon without behavior, lottery-talk flattening, glamorizing randomness, implying skill is irrelevant.
