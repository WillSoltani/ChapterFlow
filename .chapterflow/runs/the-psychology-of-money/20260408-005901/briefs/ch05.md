Chapter Title: Confounding Compounding
Position In Book: 5 of 22

Core Claim:
Compounding matters more than people feel because its power hides inside time. Extraordinary outcomes often look like brilliance when they are partly the product of long duration.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This chapter shifts from appetite and stopping rules to the quiet force of long horizons. It shows that time can do more than intensity, and that patience is often mistaken for ordinary performance until the late stages.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author explains that compounding is hard to respect because its curve is unintuitive.
Then the author shows that long duration often matters as much as raw talent or dramatic action.
Then the author argues that people over-credit extreme outcomes to genius and under-credit time.
Then the author reframes patience as a major edge rather than passive waiting.
Then the author bridges to staying wealthy by showing that compounding only works if survival protects the runway.

Required Anchors:
- compounding is mathematically powerful but emotionally under-felt
- time is often the hidden ingredient in extraordinary outcomes
- long duration is easy to misread as exceptional genius
- patience and survival keep compounding alive
- dramatic action is often less important than uninterrupted time

Counterarguments or Limits:
