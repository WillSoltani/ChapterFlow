# Chapter 12 Brief

- Chapter: 12
- Title: `Reasonable > Rational`
- Objective: Show that the best money plan is often the one a person can actually follow consistently, even if a cleaner model points to something more efficient on paper.
- Continuity bridge in: Chapter 11 treated saving as a human response to uncertainty, which opens the question of what kind of money behavior fits real life.
- Continuity bridge out: Prepare Chapter 13 by showing that durable plans still live inside a world shaped by surprise rather than perfect forecast accuracy.
- Core risks to avoid: turning `reasonable` into an excuse for laziness, sounding anti-math, or flattening the chapter into generic advice about feelings.
