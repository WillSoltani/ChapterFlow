# Chapter 17 Brief

- Chapter: 17
- Title: `You & Me`
- Objective: Show that people in markets often look irrational to one another because they are playing different games with different time horizons, incentives, and definitions of success.
- Continuity bridge in: Chapter 16 established that every worthwhile outcome has a different emotional price; this chapter widens that insight by showing that different players are paying for different goals in the same market.
- Continuity bridge out: Prepare Chapter 18 by showing how confusing, conflicting market behavior leaves room for especially persuasive pessimistic stories.
- Core risks to avoid: making the chapter sound like relativism with no standards, flattening all disagreement into harmless difference, or losing the point that game mismatch causes many apparent contradictions.
