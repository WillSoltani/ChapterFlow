# Chapter 10 Brief

- Chapter: 10
- Title: `Wealth is What You Don't See`
- Objective: Show that wealth is the money and flexibility not converted into visible consumption, which makes real financial strength harder to notice from the outside.
- Continuity bridge in: Chapter 9 weakened the credibility of visible status signals by showing how easily they are misread.
- Continuity bridge out: Prepare Chapter 11 by making unspent assets and restraint feel like active financial behavior rather than absence.
- Core risks to avoid: turning the chapter into anti-spending absolutism, confusing wealth with income, and making invisibility sound mystical instead of concrete.
