# Chapter 11 Brief

- Chapter: 11
- Title: `Save Money`
- Objective: Show that saving is valuable because it creates independence, resilience, and room for uncertainty even when there is no specific purchase goal attached to it.
- Continuity bridge in: Chapter 10 defined wealth as retained, invisible capacity rather than visible lifestyle.
- Continuity bridge out: Prepare Chapter 12 by making saving a behavioral and emotional choice, not a purely mathematical one.
- Core risks to avoid: making saving sound like fear, reducing it to budgeting tips, or acting as if everyone saves for the same reason.
