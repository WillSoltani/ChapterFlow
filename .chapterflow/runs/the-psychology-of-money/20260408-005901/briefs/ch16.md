# Chapter 16 Brief

- Chapter: 16
- Title: `Nothing's Free`
- Objective: Show that every worthwhile investing outcome carries a behavioral price, and the real challenge is learning to treat volatility, uncertainty, and regret as fees worth paying instead of as proof that something has gone wrong.
- Continuity bridge in: Chapter 15 argued that plans should leave room for a changing self; this chapter says even a flexible plan still demands emotional payment when returns come attached to discomfort.
- Continuity bridge out: Prepare Chapter 17 by showing that people often misread others because they assume everyone is paying for the same thing in markets, even when they are playing different games.
- Core risks to avoid: making the chapter sound like blind stoicism, treating all pain as wise by default, or confusing avoidable self-inflicted chaos with the necessary fee attached to good long-term outcomes.
