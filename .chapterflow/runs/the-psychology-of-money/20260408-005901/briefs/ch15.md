# Chapter 15 Brief

- Chapter: 15
- Title: `You'll Change`
- Objective: Show that people underestimate how much their future priorities will differ from the ones they defend most confidently today, so good planning should leave room to revise without humiliation.
- Continuity bridge in: Chapter 14 built room for external error; this chapter adds that long plans also face internal change because the planner's identity and preferences drift over time.
- Continuity bridge out: Prepare Chapter 16 by showing that adaptable plans still require paying the emotional price attached to worthwhile returns.
- Core risks to avoid: sounding anti-commitment, reducing the chapter to vague self-help about growth, or treating every change of mind as wise by default.
