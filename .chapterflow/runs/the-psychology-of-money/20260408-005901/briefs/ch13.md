# Chapter 13 Brief

- Chapter: 13
- Title: `Surprise!`
- Objective: Show that history is shaped by surprises often enough that planning as if the future will mostly stay inside the recent visible range is itself a serious mistake.
- Continuity bridge in: Chapter 12 argued that money plans must fit real humans; this chapter widens the challenge by showing that even durable humans still face an unpredictable world.
- Continuity bridge out: Prepare Chapter 14 by turning humility about surprise into a design rule: leave room for error.
- Core risks to avoid: sounding nihilistic about planning, reducing the chapter to vague anti-forecasting advice, or treating surprise as permission to ignore obvious risks and probabilities.
