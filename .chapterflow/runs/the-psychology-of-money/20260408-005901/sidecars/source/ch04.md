# CH04 Source Sidecar

Chapter: Never Enough

## Frozen-support facts

- Stable public summaries describe this chapter as a warning about greed, comparison, and the inability to stop.
- The practical lesson is that enough functions as a limit against ruin.
- Public discussion of the book consistently ties this chapter to status and the danger of risking what matters for what matters less.

## Safe paraphrase zones

- "more" can become an endless comparison game
- success without a stopping point can become unstable
- the most dangerous risk may be unnecessary risk taken after enough already existed
- enough is a protection, not a sign of weakness

## Avoid

- exact quotations
- unsupported biographical specifics about cautionary figures
- framing the chapter as anti-ambition in general
