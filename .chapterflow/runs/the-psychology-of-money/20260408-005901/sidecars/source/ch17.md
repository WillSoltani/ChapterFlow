# Chapter 17 Source Sidecar

- Frozen source base: 2020 original English 1st edition source bundle.
- Reader-facing focus: different players use money for different purposes, so market behavior cannot be judged well without knowing the game, incentive, and timeline behind it.
- Guardrail: do not imply that every behavior is equally wise; preserve the distinction between internal rationality within a game and the danger of borrowing a game that does not fit your own goals.
- Continuity note: Chapter 16 explained the emotional price of a strategy; Chapter 17 explains why players tolerate different prices because they are playing different games before Chapter 18 shows how pessimistic stories exploit confusion and emotional asymmetry.
