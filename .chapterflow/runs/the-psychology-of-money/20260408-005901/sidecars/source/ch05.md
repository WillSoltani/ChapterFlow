Chapter: Confounding Compounding

## Frozen-support facts

- Stable public summaries describe this chapter as a lesson in how extraordinary financial outcomes are often driven by time more than people initially notice.
- Public discussion of the book regularly ties compounding to patience, survival, and the hidden effect of uninterrupted duration.
- Safe chapter framing emphasizes that dramatic results often overstate brilliance and understate time.

## Safe paraphrase zones

- compounding hides its force until later stages
- long duration is frequently under-credited
- patience protects the runway that compounding needs
- interruption can do more damage than slow progress feels like it should

## Avoid

- exact quotations
- unsupported numerical claims from outside the frozen bundle
- framing compounding as automatic or guaranteed
