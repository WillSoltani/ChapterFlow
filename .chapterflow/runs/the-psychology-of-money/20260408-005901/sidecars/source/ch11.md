# Chapter 11 Source Sidecar

- Frozen source base: 2020 original English 1st edition source bundle.
- Reader-facing focus: saving matters because it preserves flexibility and reduces dependence even when there is no specific goal attached to the money yet.
- Guardrail: do not reduce the chapter to generic budgeting advice or moral virtue language.
- Continuity note: Chapter 10 defined wealth as unspent capacity; Chapter 11 turns that into the deliberate practice of saving before Chapter 12 broadens the standard from rational to reasonable.
