# CH01 Source Sidecar

Chapter: Introduction: The Greatest Show On Earth

## Frozen-support facts

- The book is framed as a set of short chapters about timeless lessons on wealth, greed, and happiness.
- The opening chapter establishes the book's behavioral frame.
- Public discussion of the book consistently references the contrast between Ronald Read and Richard Fuscone as the opening illustration.
- The core lesson supported by the official book description is that doing well with money is not necessarily about what you know, but how you behave.

## Safe paraphrase zones

- Ronald Read represents modest means plus long-run patience.
- Richard Fuscone represents elite credentials plus behavior that could not survive pressure.
- Money decisions happen inside pride, status, fear, and incentives.
- Stories are an appropriate teaching format because they preserve the human messiness of money.

## Avoid

- exact quotations
- unsupported statistics
- page-specific claims not visible in the frozen public bundle
- adding technical details about either man's balance sheet beyond the broad contrast
