## Motifs And Callback Opportunities

- Money behavior is shaped by memory, incentives, and status pressure.
- Survival keeps showing up as the hidden requirement of every good-looking plan.
- Freedom beats visible display.
- Later chapters should fold earlier tensions back in, especially luck, enough, room for error, and different games.
- The final postscript should widen the lens without breaking the reader-facing loop back to Chapter 1.
