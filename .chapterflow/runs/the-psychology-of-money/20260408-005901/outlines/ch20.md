# Chapter 20 Outline

1. Money success is driven less by perfect formulas than by a durable temperament.
2. Reasonable beats hyper-rational because survival, saving, and room for error matter more than optimization.
3. Playing your own game and valuing freedom protect judgment from envy, status, and comparison.
4. Humility about luck, risk, and uncertainty supports patience, compounding, and long-term staying power.
5. Bridge to `Confessions` by shifting from the book's universal frame to the author's personal weighting of those rules.
