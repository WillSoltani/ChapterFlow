# Chapter 17 Outline

1. People in the same market are often pursuing different goals on different clocks.
2. Behavior that looks irrational from one game can be rational inside another.
3. Confusion grows when investors borrow signals, rules, or emotions from players in a different game.
4. The answer is not isolation but clarity about which game you are playing and which voices actually matter to it.
5. Bridge to `The Seduction of Pessimism` by showing that once the market feels confusing and conflict-filled, dark explanations become especially appealing.
