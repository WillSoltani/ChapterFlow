# Chapter 9 Outline

1. People assume visible luxury transfers admiration to the owner.
2. The paradox is that viewers often imagine themselves in the object instead.
3. Status chasing therefore delivers less social payoff than expected.
4. The desire underneath is usually respect, attention, or approval, not the object itself.
5. Bridge to `Wealth is What You Don't See` by showing that visible evidence is noisy and invisible reserves matter more.
