# CH04 Outline

## Hook

- Open on the paradox that more can feel worse, not better.
- Show how success can expand appetite instead of ending it.

## Build

- Explain enough as a boundary, not a mood.
- Show status comparison as the engine of endless more.
- Emphasize the danger of taking irreversible downside for optional upside.
- Keep ambition distinct from insatiability.

## Deliver

- Land the central rule: never risk what matters and is hard to replace for what matters less and can be replaced.
- Show enough as a protective skill.
- Explain why stopping points create durability.

## Bridge

- Reward the reader with a clean filter: if the next gain requires risking your survival or peace, it may already be too expensive.
- Open the next chapter by showing that time and compounding reward staying in the game.

## Distinct Scenario Lesson Map

1. Work / decision_point:
   reject a prestige-driven risk that threatens stability
2. School / postmortem:
   review how comparison pushed a project past wise limits
3. Personal / dialogue:
   define enough in a household before lifestyle creep expands
4. Work / predict_reveal:
   show a top performer stretching too far for status validation
5. School / dilemma:
   choose between a visible win and a durable path
6. Personal / before_after:
   reframe success from endless upgrade to protected sufficiency

## Takeaway Count Lock

- Easy: 3
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 6

## Implementation Plan Direction

- Core skill: defining enough before status pressure takes over
- Work plan: identify risks taken for prestige rather than need
- School plan: separate growth goals from comparison goals
- Personal plan: write down non-negotiables you will not risk for "more"
