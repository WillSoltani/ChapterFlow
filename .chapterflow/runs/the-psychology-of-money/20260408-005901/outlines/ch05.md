# CH05 Outline

## Hook

- Open on the fact that huge outcomes often look sudden after being quiet for a long time.
- Show why compounding is hard to feel before it becomes dramatic.

## Build

- Explain time as the hidden ingredient in many extraordinary results.
- Contrast patient duration with flashy but interrupted effort.
- Emphasize that people often over-credit genius and under-credit survival plus time.
- Keep the chapter behavioral, not purely mathematical.

## Deliver

- Land the rule: the biggest edge is often staying in a sound process long enough.
- Frame patience as active protection of runway.
- Show why interruption is the enemy of compounding.

## Bridge

- Connect compounding to survival and the need to stay in the game.
- Open Chapter 6 by distinguishing accumulation skill from preservation skill.

## Distinct Scenario Lesson Map

1. Work / decision_point:
   resist cutting a long-building project just before patient gains become visible
2. School / postmortem:
   review how impatience broke a compounding experiment
3. Personal / dialogue:
   compare a slow-growing plan with the urge for dramatic moves
4. Work / predict_reveal:
   show a star track record owed partly to long uninterrupted duration
5. School / dilemma:
   choose between fast-looking wins and durable accumulation
6. Personal / before_after:
   reframe progress from speed to uninterrupted time

## Takeaway Count Lock

- Easy: 3
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 6

## Implementation Plan Direction

- Core skill: protect duration
- Work plan: avoid interrupting long-run processes for short-run vanity
- School plan: track what repeated steady gains are doing over time
- Personal plan: identify one place where impatience keeps breaking compounding
