# Chapter 10 Outline

1. What people see is spending, not the assets that remain unspent.
2. Wealth is the reserve, margin, and optionality that outsiders usually cannot observe.
3. Visible lifestyle and real net strength are not the same thing.
4. Restraint is productive because it preserves future choice.
5. Bridge to `Save Money` by reframing non-spending as active behavior.
