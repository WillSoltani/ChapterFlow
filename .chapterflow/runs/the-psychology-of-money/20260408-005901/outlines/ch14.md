# Chapter 14 Outline

1. Margin is a survival design, not dead weight.
2. Tight assumptions make ordinary mistakes and volatility far more destructive than they first appear.
3. Cash, lower leverage, slower timelines, and conservative assumptions act as shock absorbers.
4. Margin feels inefficient during calm periods, which is why people underinvest in it before they need it.
5. Bridge to `You'll Change` by showing that robust plans must also survive changes in the planner, not only shocks in the world.
