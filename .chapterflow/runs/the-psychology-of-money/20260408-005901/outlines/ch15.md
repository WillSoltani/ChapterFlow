# Chapter 15 Outline

1. People are overconfident that their future priorities will closely match their current ones.
2. Identity drift makes extreme long-term commitments dangerous when the future self inherits promises it would not have made.
3. The answer is not aimlessness but more reversible commitments and less humiliation around revision.
4. Flexibility is different from drift because some commitments still deserve to be honored.
5. Bridge to `Nothing's Free` by showing that adaptable plans still demand emotional endurance when real volatility arrives.
