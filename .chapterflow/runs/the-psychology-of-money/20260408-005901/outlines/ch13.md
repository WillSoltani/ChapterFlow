# Chapter 13 Outline

1. Big turning points often arrive from outside the range people were recently treating as normal.
2. Recency bias narrows what feels possible, so forecasts quietly inherit short memories.
3. The lesson is not to stop planning, but to plan with wider ranges and more humility.
4. Good planning manages exposure to being wrong instead of pretending wrongness is unlikely.
5. Bridge to `Room for Error` by showing that surprise becomes a practical reason to build margin on purpose.
