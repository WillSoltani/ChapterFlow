# Chapter 12 Outline

1. A mathematically elegant plan can fail if the person using it cannot live with the stress it creates.
2. Good money behavior must fit temperament, sleep, attention, and risk tolerance, not only abstract efficiency.
3. Durability often beats brilliance because a workable plan survives long enough to matter.
4. `Reasonable` does not mean sloppy or self-protective fiction; it still requires honest math and honest self-awareness.
5. Bridge to `Surprise!` by showing that even durable plans must expect a future that will not unfold exactly as modeled.
