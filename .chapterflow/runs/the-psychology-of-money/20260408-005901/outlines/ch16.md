# Chapter 16 Outline

1. Good investing results are never truly free because returns come bundled with volatility, doubt, and regret.
2. People fail when they misread those costs as fines that signal a mistake instead of fees required to access worthwhile outcomes.
3. The real edge is behavioral: deciding in advance which emotional prices are worth paying and which risks are not.
4. Paying the right fee is different from tolerating chaos for its own sake because some costs are unnecessary while others are structural.
5. Bridge to `You & Me` by showing that confusion grows when people assume everyone in the market is paying for the same thing on the same time horizon.
