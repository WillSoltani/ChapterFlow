# CH03 Outline

## Hook

- Open on the emotional unfairness of outcomes.
- A good result can hide a reckless process; a bad result can hide disciplined judgment.

## Build

- Define luck and risk as paired distortions around outcome reading.
- Show why people naturally anchor on visible winners and losers.
- Explain why narrative compresses randomness into character.
- Emphasize humility in assigning praise and blame.

## Deliver

- Land the rule: judge process more carefully when randomness is load-bearing.
- Show that hero worship and self-condemnation both become weaker under this lens.
- Keep responsibility in view: luck exists, but that does not excuse bad process.

## Bridge

- Reward the reader with a usable filter: ask what part of the result belonged to process and what part belonged to chance.
- Open the next chapter by showing how misread success can turn into destructive appetite for more.

## Distinct Scenario Lesson Map

1. Work / decision_point:
   separate a lucky win from a repeatable process
2. School / postmortem:
   review a disciplined loss without abandoning good method
3. Personal / dialogue:
   reduce shame by naming risk after a setback
4. Work / predict_reveal:
   reveal that a star performer was also riding a favorable environment
5. School / dilemma:
   choose between copying a flashy winner and trusting a boring process
6. Personal / before_after:
   shift from outcome-only self-judgment to process review

## Takeaway Count Lock

- Easy: 3
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 6

## Implementation Plan Direction

- Core skill: outcome humility
- Work plan: evaluate process after wins and losses
- School plan: separate learning from scoreboard emotion
- Personal plan: write down controllable choices before reviewing results
