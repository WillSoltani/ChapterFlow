# Chapter 19 Outline

1. Fear and uncertainty increase the desire for simple, confident explanations.
2. When people feel exposed, explanatory certainty can become more attractive than evidentiary quality.
3. Weak stories gain power when they offer emotional relief, order, and someone to blame.
4. The answer is not cynicism but noticing when emotional need is doing too much of the believing.
5. Bridge to `All Together Now` by showing the need to restate a balanced framework after fear, noise, and narrative distortion.
