# Chapter 11 Outline

1. Saving is useful even without a precise goal because life is uncertain.
2. Saved money buys time, options, and tolerance for error.
3. The point is not only income level but the gap between what is earned and what is spent.
4. Saving supports independence more than visible consumption does.
5. Bridge to `Reasonable > Rational` by showing that money behavior depends on temperament and context, not pure optimization.
