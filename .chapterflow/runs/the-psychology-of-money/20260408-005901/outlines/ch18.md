# Chapter 18 Outline

1. Pessimistic stories attract attention because danger feels urgent and vivid.
2. Optimistic progress is often slow, quiet, and statistically real but emotionally unimpressive.
3. People mistake the emotional seriousness of bad news for deeper truth.
4. The answer is not blind optimism but learning to separate emotional force from evidentiary strength.
5. Bridge to `When You'll Believe Anything` by showing how fear and confusion lower the standard for what stories people accept.
