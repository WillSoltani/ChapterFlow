# CH02 Quiz Blueprint

Goal: test whether the reader can use history and lived experience to interpret money behavior without slipping into "anything goes."

Coverage:
- 2 recall questions on the chapter's central claim
- 3 understanding questions on experience-shaped risk perception and historical recency
- 3 application questions using work, school, and family scenes
- 2 analysis questions on explanation versus endorsement and the bridge to luck and risk

Required reminders:
- explanations should reinforce humility without erasing standards
- include at least one question on the recency of modern retirement norms
- include at least one question on the danger of copying advice from people with different histories
