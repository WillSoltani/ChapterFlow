# Quiz Blueprint

Chapter Number: 1
Chapter Title: The Characters of the Story

Bloom Ceiling: analyze
Concept Budget: focused
Primary Tested Concepts:
- System 1 as the fast source of impressions and default answers
- System 2 as the slower layer for checking, comparison, and explicit review
- the hidden handoff between fluent first answers and later inspection
- selective correction rather than permanent suspicion
- the bridge from dual-process language into the resource question of Chapter 2

Prediction Questions:
- predict when a quick answer deserves a slower pass
- predict how fluency can be mistaken for proof in a live situation
- predict why the next chapter must explain when review is available at all

Likely Misreadings:
- treating the two systems as literal anatomical boxes
- assuming slow thinking is always better than fast thinking
- turning the chapter into generic skepticism instead of calibrated review
