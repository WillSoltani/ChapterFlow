Core Claim:
Kahneman shows how intuitive forecasts improve when base rates and regression are given explicit weight. Better prediction separates the shared signal from noise and shrinkage toward the norm. The chapter matters because intuitive predictions stay too extreme when they fail to shrink toward what usually happens. Its practical move is to start with the base rate, then adjust for the case-specific information without forgetting regression.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Taming Intuitive Predictions sits between Regression to the Mean and The Illusion of Understanding. It is the chapter that explains how intuitive predictions improve when base rates and regression are given explicit weight. Its distinctive value is that it turns a fast cue into a concrete audit point for later judgment errors.

Author Logic Chain:
