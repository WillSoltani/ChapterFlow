Core Claim:
Kahneman uses the conjunction fallacy to show that richer stories can feel more probable than broader truths. Specific detail can raise plausibility while lowering actual probability. The chapter matters because the more vivid option can feel likelier even when it is logically narrower. Its practical move is to check whether one option is a subset of another before trusting the more detailed story.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Linda: Less Is More sits between Tom W's Specialty and Causes Trump Statistics. It is the chapter that explains how richer stories can feel more probable than broader truths. Its distinctive value is that it turns a fast cue into a concrete audit point for later judgment errors.

Author Logic Chain:
First Kahneman identifies how specific detail raises plausibility while lowering actual probability.
Then he shows how that cue bends judgment before stronger checking arrives.
Then he concludes that better judgment requires a deliberate check on whether the confidence came from evidence or from the chapter mechanism.
