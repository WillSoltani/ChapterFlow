Chapter Title: Life as a Story
Position In Book: package chapter 36 of 38

Core Claim:
Kahneman shows that remembered lives are shaped by peaks and endings more than by duration. Memory compresses experience into a narrative with highlights and conclusions. The chapter matters because long stretches of lived time often lose influence once memory constructs a story around standout moments and the ending. Its practical move is to separate what an episode felt like while living it from what the memory of it now emphasizes.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Life as a Story sits between Two Selves and Experienced Well-Being. It is the chapter that explains how the remembering self builds summaries that are not faithful measures of duration. Its distinctive value is that it shows why memory is story-shaped rather than time-shaped and why that matters for evaluating happiness, pain, and choice.

Author Logic Chain:
First Kahneman shows that memory compresses episodes around peaks and endings.
Then he shows that duration often counts less in remembered evaluation than people expect.
Then he concludes that better judgment requires separating the story a memory tells from the full span of lived time.
