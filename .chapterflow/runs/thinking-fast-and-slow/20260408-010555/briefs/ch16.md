Chapter Title: Causes Trump Statistics
Position In Book: package chapter 16 of 38

Core Claim:
Kahneman shows that causal stories and stereotypes often overpower clean statistical reasoning. The mind prefers an explanatory narrative to a dry distribution. The chapter matters because statistics get sidelined once a story explains the scene well enough. Its practical move is to ask what the numbers say before allowing the causal account to close the case.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Causes Trump Statistics sits between Linda: Less Is More and Regression to the Mean. It is the chapter that explains how causal stories and stereotypes overpower dry statistical reasoning. Its distinctive value is that it turns a fast cue into a concrete audit point for later judgment errors.

Author Logic Chain:
First Kahneman identifies how the mind prefers an explanatory narrative to a clean distribution.
Then he shows how that cue bends judgment before stronger checking arrives.
Then he concludes that better judgment requires a deliberate check on whether the confidence came from evidence or from the chapter mechanism.
