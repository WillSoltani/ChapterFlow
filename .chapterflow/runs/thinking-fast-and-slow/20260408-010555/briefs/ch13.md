Core Claim:
Kahneman shows that vivid emotion amplifies availability and bends judgments about danger and safety. Fear and imagery can make some risks loom larger than their actual frequency warrants. The chapter matters because dramatic threats get overweighted when they are easy to picture and hard to forget. Its practical move is to compare the vivid scenario with actual frequency before treating the fear response as a probability estimate.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Availability, Emotion, and Risk sits between The Science of Availability and Tom W's Specialty. It is the chapter that explains how vivid emotion makes some risks loom larger than their frequency warrants. Its distinctive value is that it turns a fast cue into a concrete audit point for later judgment errors.

Author Logic Chain:
First Kahneman identifies how fear and imagery amplify availability and bend judgments about danger and safety.
Then he shows how that cue bends judgment before stronger checking arrives.
Then he concludes that better judgment requires a deliberate check on whether the confidence came from evidence or from the chapter mechanism.
