Chapter Promise:
The reader will understand why equivalent outcomes produce different choices under different wording, how gain and loss frames shift judgment, and what practical reframing looks like.

Opening Move:
Start with a policy or treatment description that feels acceptable in one frame and harsh in another even though the outcome is unchanged.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: show a live framing shift around a work or policy decision
- P2: explain equivalent reality versus different wording
- P3: deepen the pattern with school and family/treatment examples
- P4: show why people defend opposite verdicts under changed wording
- P5: state the practical correction of restating the choice
- P6: bridge toward Two Selves

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
equivalent realities can feel different once they are described as gains or losses.

Hard-Depth Tension:
frames can surface real concerns, but wording alone often moves judgment much more than people admit.
