Chapter Promise:
The reader will understand why surprise so quickly becomes a search for cause, and how to resist granting the first tidy explanation more authority than it deserves.

Opening Move:
Open with Maya in a service review where one failure makes the room assign blame before it has mapped the chain of events.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: show how violated expectation changes the emotional and interpretive climate of a scene
- P2: define norms, surprise, and the shift into causal search
- P3: explain why broken norms invite stories about intention and cause
- P4: use school and personal anchors to show how quickly motive and blame get assigned
- P5: state the limit that causal search is useful even though the first story can mislead
- P6: bridge toward A Machine for Jumping to Conclusions

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Surprise is not just noticing something odd. It is noticing that a norm or expectation has been broken and then rushing to restore order with a cause.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The hard version must preserve the usefulness of fast causal search while showing how the first satisfying explanation becomes sticky before alternatives are tested.
