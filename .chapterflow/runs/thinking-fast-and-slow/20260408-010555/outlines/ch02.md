Chapter Promise:
The reader will understand that careful thought requires scarce attention, that effortful tasks interfere with each other, and that cognitive load changes judgment in predictable ways.

Opening Move:
Open with a hard task that the reader can feel in the body as effort rather than just understand abstractly.

- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: show what effort feels like in contrast to automatic activity
- P2: define attention as the scarce resource behind System 2
- P3: explain why two effortful tasks collide
- P4: connect mental load to poorer monitoring and self-control
- P5: clarify that effort signals cost, not guaranteed correctness
- P6: bridge toward The Lazy Controller by showing why the checking system often fails to engage

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Effortful tasks share a limited attentional budget.

Hard-Depth Tension:
