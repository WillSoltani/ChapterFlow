Chapter Promise:
The reader will understand how fluency becomes a cue for truth and safety, and how to interrupt that cue without becoming suspicious of every clear or familiar thing.

Opening Move:
Open with Maya watching a polished proposal calm a room before anyone has checked what the proposal actually assumes.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: show a work scene where ease quietly changes the standard of scrutiny
- P2: define cognitive ease and name the mechanism clearly
- P3: explain why familiarity, repetition, and fluency feel like signs of truth
- P4: move to school and personal anchors to show how the cue distorts ordinary judgment
- P5: state the boundary condition that ease is often useful and not automatically deceptive
- P6: bridge toward the next chapter by showing what happens when ease is broken by surprise

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The mind often treats ease of processing as if it were evidence about truth, safety, or quality.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The hard version must show that fluency can become counterfeit credibility, while preserving the limit that clarity and familiarity are often useful.

- Takeaway 1: what cognitive ease is doing

Scenario Lesson Map:

- Scenario 6 (before_after, personal): teaches what changes once rehearsal is separated from proof
