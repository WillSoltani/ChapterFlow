## Chapter Order

1. Animal Behavior
Intent: use non-human analogues to show that apparently prosocial behavior can hide prestige or political motives.

2. Competition
Intent: show the three competitive games that make signaling central to human social life.

3. Norms
Intent: explain how norms suppress selfishness while also creating new ways to hide selfish motives.

4. Cheating
Intent: show how people exploit weak enforcement, ambiguity, and fig leaves to violate norms in public.

5. Self-Deception
Intent: explain why minds hide truths from themselves in order to mislead other people more effectively.

6. Counterfeit Reasons
Intent: show how confabulation and rationalization manufacture clean explanations after the fact.

7. Body Language
Intent: read nonverbal behavior as honest signaling and discreet coordination.

8. Laughter
Intent: explain laughter as a play signal that hides social aggression under deniability.

9. Conversation
Intent: argue that conversation often advertises mental fitness more than it exchanges value fairly.

10. Consumption
Intent: show how buying behavior signals wealth, taste, conscientiousness, and identity.

11. Art
Intent: explain art through costly signaling, waste, and impressive production.

12. Charity
Intent: show that giving often signals prosociality and status more than impartial effectiveness.

13. Education
Intent: argue that schooling signals work traits and serves social functions beyond learning.

14. Medicine
Intent: reframe medicine partly as conspicuous caring and public sacrifice.

15. Religion
Intent: explain religion as group bonding, costly loyalty display, and norm enforcement.

16. Politics
Intent: show political participation as group loyalty signaling more than outcome optimization.
