Core Claim:
The chapter argues that self-deception is not only a defensive quirk but an evolved social strategy. Hidden truths may be concealed from the self in order to mislead other people more effectively. Older defensive models matter as background, but the chapter's distinct move is to connect self-deception to social signaling and modular mental processes.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the chapter where cheating turns inward. Its distinctness comes from comparing older defensive theories with newer evolutionary ones, then using modularity and self-discretion to explain how people can hide motives even from themselves.
