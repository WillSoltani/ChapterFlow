Core Claim:
The chapter argues that people are often strategically ignorant about their own motives and can fabricate explanations after the fact. Split-brain and confabulation evidence support the claim that the mind can generate counterfeit reasons, while the ego behaves more like a press secretary than a central executive.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the chapter where self-deception becomes visible as explanation-making. Its distinctness comes from strategic ignorance, confabulation, and the image of the ego as a press secretary rather than a command center.
