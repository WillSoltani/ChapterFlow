Core Claim:
The chapter argues that once norms exist, people still have incentives to cheat when they think they can get away with it. The most interesting cheating is not blatant secret violation but broad-daylight violation under thin fig leaves. Weak norms, lazy enforcement, discreet communication, and ready-made excuses allow people to violate norms while making enforcement costly or awkward for others.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the chapter where hidden motives move from competition and norms into public cheating under plausible deniability. Its distinctness comes from thin fig leaves, broad-daylight violations, and enforcement friction.

Author Logic Chain:
First the authors argue that norms create incentives to cheat.
Then they distinguish blatant hidden cheating from public cheating under cover.
Then they explain how weak norms, lazy enforcement, discreet communication, and excuses protect violators.
