Core Claim:
The chapter argues that art can be understood through costly signaling and the handicap principle. Impracticality and extrinsic features help explain how art signals value.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the chapter where signaling becomes conspicuously aesthetic. Its distinctness comes from explaining artistic value through cost, impracticality, and visible excess rather than through function alone.
