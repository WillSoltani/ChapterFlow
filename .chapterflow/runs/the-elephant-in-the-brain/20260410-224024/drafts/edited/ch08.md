A laugh can make a risky moment feel safer without making it empty. Chapter 8 starts there. After body language coordinated sensitive interaction, the book turns to laughter and asks why this signal changes the stakes of a moment so quickly.

The chapter says laughter is a play signal. It does not only express joy. It marks a move as less dangerous than it might otherwise seem and gives the interaction more room to continue without immediate escalation.

This matters for physical danger, but the chapter pushes even harder on social danger. A tease, challenge, flirtation, or risky remark can carry real content while laughter softens the edge. The point is not that the meaning disappears. It is that the threat level gets lowered enough for the interaction to continue.

That is why laughter is tied to deniability. A person can say something risky and then laugh. If it works, the message travels. If it fails, the laugh gives room to retreat and claim the move was only playful. The social content stays alive while commitment to it gets softened.

So laughter becomes a flexible tool. It can reduce danger, test boundaries, preserve connection, and carry real meaning at the same time. The next chapter turns to conversation, where signaling becomes a longer display of what people know and how they learn.
