Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand why people often violate norms in ways that stay publicly visible yet hard to punish.

Opening Move:
Open from the frustration of seeing a violation everyone notices but nobody cleanly prosecutes.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 incentive to cheat under norms
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 thin fig leaves and common knowledge problems
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 discreet communication, excuses, and lazy enforcement

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: norms create pressure and temptation together
- P2: cheating is often public rather than hidden
- P3: weak norms and common knowledge problem
- P4: thin fig leaves
- P5: discreet communication and excuses
- P6: social laziness and enforcement cost
- P7 if needed: bridge into self-deception

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The mechanism is not only secret violation. It is enforceable ambiguity: behavior that looks bad enough to suspect yet not cleanly enough to punish.

Hard-Depth Tension:
How much cheating survives because violators are bold, and how much survives because observers do not want the cost of turning suspicion into common knowledge?

Threshold Question:
When does a norm fail because people do not care, and when does it fail because they care but cannot coordinate proof and enforcement?
