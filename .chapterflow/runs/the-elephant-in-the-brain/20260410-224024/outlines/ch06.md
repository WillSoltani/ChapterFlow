Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand why people can produce sincere-sounding explanations without reliable access to their real motives.

Opening Move:
Open from the discomfort of hearing a clean explanation that may have been assembled after the action rather than before it.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 strategic ignorance about motives
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 split-brain and confabulation evidence
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 ego as press secretary rather than central executive

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: self-deception turns into explanation-making
- P2: strategic ignorance as setup
- P3: confabulation and fabricated explanation
- P4: split-brain evidence as support
- P5: ego as press secretary
- P6: limits of the central-executive model
- P7 if needed: bridge into body language

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The mechanism is not simple lying. It is explanation after the fact from a mind that does not fully see its own causes.

Hard-Depth Tension:
How can a person sound sincere and coherent while still functioning more like a spokesperson than a fully informed decision-maker?

Threshold Question:
When does a reason reflect genuine access to motive, and when is it mainly a polished story produced for social or internal consumption?
