Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand why art can signal value through costliness, impracticality, and extrinsic features rather than through direct utility alone.

Opening Move:
Open from the strange fact that some artistic value seems to rise when the work looks difficult, wasteful, or impractical.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 art through costly signaling
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 handicap principle and impracticality
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 extrinsic features signaling value

- P1: direct-use explanation feels too small

- P3: handicap principle

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The mechanism is not that beauty or enjoyment disappears. It is that visible cost, burden, and impracticality can help make artistic value credible as a signal.

Hard-Depth Tension:
How can art be genuinely appreciated while still drawing part of its value from costliness, handicap, and extrinsic social signals?

Threshold Question:
When is artistic value mainly about the experience of the work, and when is it also about what the work's costliness or impracticality communicates socially?
