Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand why charity can signal wealth, empathy, and prosocial orientation rather than reflecting pure effectiveness motives alone.

Opening Move:
Open from the uncomfortable fact that giving often seems to follow visibility and recognition more than maximum impact.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 charity signaling wealth, empathy, and prosocial orientation
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 giving patterns fitting social-display motives
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 effectiveness tension and genuine help

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: pure altruism picture feels incomplete
- P2: charity as social signal
- P3: wealth, empathy, and prosocial signals
- P4: visibility and recognition patterns
- P5: effectiveness tension
- P6: genuine benefit plus display
- P7 if needed: bridge into education

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The mechanism is not that charity never helps. It is that giving patterns often follow visibility and social recognition in ways that fit display motives better than pure effectiveness alone.

Hard-Depth Tension:
How can charity produce real benefit while still being shaped by signals about wealth, empathy, and moral orientation?

Threshold Question:
When is giving mainly about helping effectively, and when is it also about what the act communicates about the giver?
