Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The mechanism is not that laughter erases threat. It marks behavior as more safely exploratory while preserving room to retreat from full seriousness.

Hard-Depth Tension:
How can laughter reduce danger while still carrying real social content that can wound, test, or signal?

Threshold Question:
When is laughter mainly softening a risky interaction, and when is it also protecting the speaker with deniability while leaving the message intact?
