Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand why medicine can function partly as conspicuous caring, with visible sacrifice and care signals mattering alongside health claims.

Opening Move:
Open from the fact that some treatments feel reassuring partly because they look effortful, costly, or caring, not only because of their health effect.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 medicine as conspicuous caring
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 visible sacrifice and public care signals
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 health claims plus care-signal tension

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: pure health-optimization story feels incomplete

- P3: visible sacrifice as care signal

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The mechanism is not that medicine never heals. It is that visible sacrifice and public signals of care can shape treatment choices alongside health claims.

Hard-Depth Tension:
How can medical treatment produce real benefit while still functioning as a public signal of caring through visible sacrifice and effort?

Threshold Question:
When is medicine mainly about health improvement, and when is it also about making care publicly legible?
