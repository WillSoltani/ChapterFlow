Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand why conversation often serves as a display of the ability to know and learn rather than as mere information transfer.

Opening Move:
Open from the odd fact that people often keep talking long after the strictly useful information has already been exchanged.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 conversation as more than information exchange
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 showing off ability to know and learn
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 news and academic research as application zones

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: narrow information-transfer model feels incomplete
- P2: conversation as social display
- P3: showing ability to know
- P4: showing ability to learn
- P5: news as conversation-display arena
- P6: academic research as prestige display arena
- P7 if needed: bridge into consumption

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The mechanism is not that conversation stops conveying information. It is that talk also lets people display awareness, curiosity, memory, explanation skill, and intellectual range.

Hard-Depth Tension:
How can conversation be genuinely informative while still operating as a status display about who knows, notices, and learns well?

Threshold Question:
When is a conversation mainly solving an information problem, and when is it also functioning as a display of intellectual ability and social worth?
