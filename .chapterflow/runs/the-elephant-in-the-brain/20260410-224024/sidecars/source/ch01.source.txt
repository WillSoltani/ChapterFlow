Primary heading summary:
The chapter surveys two examples of hidden-motive-type behaviors in non-human species: social grooming among primates and competitive altruism in the Arabian babbler bird. Grooming looks hygienic at first, but closer inspection suggests it functions politically. Babbler helping looks group-serving, but the behavior can be read as selfish prestige jockeying. These examples are presented as analogues for later human behavior.
