Primary heading summary:
The chapter argues that important parts of human physiology and behavior were shaped by intra-group competition, not only by physical environment. It identifies three central competitive games: sex, social status, and politics. In the sexual game, people compete for mates. In the status game, they compete for friends. In the political game, they compete for power through coalitions and coordinated allies. All three games involve sending and receiving signals about qualities as a potential partner.
