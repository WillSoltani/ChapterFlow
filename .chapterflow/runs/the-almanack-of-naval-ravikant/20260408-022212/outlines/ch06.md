# Chapter Outline

Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand that this appendix is really Naval's reading philosophy: curiosity-first, signal-hungry, medium-agnostic, and oriented toward theory, concepts, judgment, and rereading rather than prestige display.

Opening Move:
Open with the tension that Naval explicitly says he does not read for self-improvement, then land on the sharper rule that the best book is the one you will devour.

Anchor Allocation:
- Anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 for curiosity, devouring, connoisseurship, and taste formation.
- Anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 for the category map across nonfiction, philosophy/spirituality, science fiction, blogs, essays, and other recommendations.
- Anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 for rereading, templates, embarrassing reading, investment, and the appendix as upstream input stream.

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: Hook with anti-self-improvement reading and explain why curiosity matters more than reading as duty.
- P2: Explain connoisseurship and why enough reading moves a person toward theory, concepts, and nonfiction.
- P3: Show what the category spread reveals, especially nonfiction and philosophy/spirituality as framework and self-observation inputs.
- P4: Extend the input stream through science fiction, blogs, essays, graphic novels, television, and other recommendations to show signal over medium.
- P5: Explain rereading, gifting, template use, embarrassing books, and treating books as investment rather than expense.
- P6: Close by framing the list as Naval's upstream input stream for judgment and worldview, not as a canon to obey.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter's real distinction is that reading is treated as input design, not as moral self-improvement or status signaling. Curiosity gets you to devour, enough volume creates taste, taste pushes you toward denser signal, and that signal can arrive through any medium worth rereading or revisiting.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard mode must preserve the boundary between curiosity and randomness. It also must preserve the distinction between reading a source and endorsing it, and between using a list as raw material versus treating it as a canon. The appendix only deepens if it keeps medium-agnostic openness under taste discipline.
