# Book Skeleton

## Metadata
- Book: The Almanack of Naval Ravikant
- Author: Eric Jorgenson
- Locked edition: 2020 Magrathea public-service PDF/EPUB family
- Package shape: 6 reader-facing units built from the major section structure of the locked edition
- Wave size: 2 by default under `serial_safe`
- Premium / solo routing candidates: ch01, ch02, ch04

## Rotation Plan For Examples
- Maintain the canonical six-format rotation once per chapter: `decision_point`, `postmortem`, `dialogue`, `predict_reveal`, `dilemma`, `before_after`.
- Maintain `2 work / 2 school / 2 personal` examples in every chapter package.
- Rotate endings across `broader_principle`, `self_directed_question`, `surprising_implication`, `cross_domain`, `common_trap`, and `perspective_reframe`.

## Vocabulary Watchlist
- Keep wealth, specific knowledge, leverage, accountability, judgment, mental models, desire, envy, meditation, freedom, values, and presence precise and chapter-bound.
- Avoid startup cliches, vague self-optimization jargon, pseudo-spiritual filler, and generic "follow your passion" language.

## Motif / Callback Opportunities
- ch01-ch02 establish the operating logic: wealth comes from leverage and judgment, not status performance.
- ch03-ch04 turn outward success inward by asking what desires, habits, and self-deception do to peace.
- ch05 condenses the worldview into values, attention, and time.
- ch06 works best as a capstone appendix: reading is not a random list but Naval's upstream input stream for judgment and worldview.

## Chapter Plan
### ch01 Building Wealth
- Intent: Show that wealth is built through specific knowledge, leverage, accountability, patience, and long-term reputation rather than short-term hustling.
- Source richness: high
- Concept density: high
- Moral complexity: medium
- Thin-chapter risk notes: keep specific knowledge, leverage, luck, and patience distinct; do not flatten the section into generic career advice.
- Wave routing: calibration 1

### ch02 Building Judgment
- Intent: Show that better decisions come from clearer thinking, identity reduction, durable mental models, and serious reading.
- Source richness: high
- Concept density: high
- Moral complexity: medium
- Thin-chapter risk notes: keep the mental-model material concrete and avoid making judgment sound like vague intelligence worship.
- Wave routing: calibration 2

### ch03 Learning Happiness
- Intent: Reframe happiness as a trainable condition shaped by desire, presence, peace, habits, and acceptance rather than by achievement.
- Source richness: medium_high
- Concept density: medium
- Moral complexity: medium
- Thin-chapter risk notes: avoid soft inspirational language; keep envy, desire, and acceptance tied to practical tradeoffs.
- Wave routing: wave 3

### ch04 Saving Yourself
- Intent: Turn the lens onto self-governance by linking authenticity, health, meditation, growth, and freedom from internal traps.
- Source richness: high
- Concept density: high
- Moral complexity: high
- Thin-chapter risk notes: do not let the section dissolve into lifestyle advice; preserve the through-line of self-authorship and liberation.
- Wave routing: solo/premium

### ch05 Philosophy
- Intent: Compress Naval's worldview into meaning, values, rational Buddhism, and present-focused attention.
- Source richness: medium
- Concept density: medium_high
- Moral complexity: medium
- Thin-chapter risk notes: keep the alias `How to Live` visible and resist fake-deep paraphrase around spirituality.
- Wave routing: wave 4

### ch06 Naval's Recommended Reading
- Intent: Treat the reading appendix as a map of upstream influences that sharpen judgment, taste, and long-horizon thinking.
- Source richness: medium
- Concept density: low_medium
- Moral complexity: low
- Thin-chapter risk notes: this section is list-heavy, so extract organizing logic from the categories instead of padding with invented commentary.
- Wave routing: wave 4
