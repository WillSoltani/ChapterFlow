# Quiz Blueprint

Chapter Number: 3
Chapter Title: Learning Happiness

Core Concepts To Test:
- happiness as a learned baseline rather than an achievement reward
- presence, peace, and the feeling of nothing missing
- desire contract and success dissatisfaction
- envy, internal scorecards, and comparison traps
- happiness habits plus change/accept/leave

Bloom Ceiling:
- evaluate

Question Budget:
- q01-q03 simple / remember-understand
- q04-q08 standard / apply-analyze
- q09-q10 deeper / evaluate-create

Named-character Scenario Slots:
- q04: Leila getting the promotion she wanted and immediately creating a new problem from it
- q05: Sana deciding whether envy means she wants another student's life or just a small slice of it
- q06: Jules stuck between changing, accepting, or leaving a draining personal situation

Cross-Chapter Links:
- q10 can connect lightly to Chapter 2 by asking why better judgment still does not guarantee peace

Wrong-Answer Temptations:
- equating happiness with excitement or constant positive emotion
- treating acceptance as passive surrender
- assuming external success should remove envy or anxiety by itself
- mistaking one habit hack for a universal cure

Prediction Link:
- If happiness requires lowering identity, reshaping habits, and refusing self-created suffering, the next chapter can ask how to save yourself from the parts of you that keep rebuilding the trap.

Do not write the final questions here.
