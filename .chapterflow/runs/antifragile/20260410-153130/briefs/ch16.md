Core Claim:
This chapter should argue that disorder, trial, and self-discovery are not failures of rigor in uncertain life. Taleb is pushing the reader to see a certain kind of mess as informative and productive when downside is bounded. The reader should leave seeing disorder not as a romantic slogan but as a disciplined environment where discovery happens.
