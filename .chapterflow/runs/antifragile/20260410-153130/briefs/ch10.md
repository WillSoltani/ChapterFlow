Core Claim:
This chapter should argue that the key to uncertainty is not average-case comfort but asymmetry between upside and downside. Taleb uses Seneca to show that one should arrange life and decisions to limit ruin while keeping gains available. The reader should leave thinking in payoff asymmetries instead of smooth average expectations.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the asymmetry chapter. It must stay centered on upside versus downside and not collapse into generic stoicism or lifestyle advice.

Author Logic Chain:
First Taleb introduces Seneca as a way to think about downside and upside. Then he shows why survival and limited downside matter under uncertainty. Then he shifts the reader toward decisions that preserve gains without courting ruin.
