Core Claim:
This chapter should argue that in nonlinear domains, interventions can transform inputs in surprising ways and sometimes reverse their intended effect. Taleb is pushing the reader to see that the same action can heal, harm, or invert depending on response shape, concentration, and context. The reader should leave understanding that transformation and inversion are central risks wherever nonlinear structure is opaque.
