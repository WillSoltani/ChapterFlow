Core Claim:
This chapter should argue that a single large impact is not equivalent to many small impacts that sum to the same total, because harm and benefit often scale nonlinearly. Taleb is pushing the reader to see that aggregation can mislead when dose, concentration, and convexity matter. The reader should leave understanding that equal arithmetic totals can produce very unequal real-world consequences.
