Core Claim:
This chapter should argue that under uncertainty one should avoid total exposure to one flashy source of upside and instead structure life with strong protection on one side and selective open upside on the other. Taleb is not preaching dullness. He is arguing for the barbell as a practical way to preserve survival while keeping optional gains alive.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the barbell chapter. It must stay structural and payoff-based rather than turning into generic career or relationship advice.

Author Logic Chain:
First Taleb explains why single-source exposure can be fragile. Then he frames the barbell as extreme safety plus selective upside. Then he shows how that structure preserves survival while keeping opportunity alive.
