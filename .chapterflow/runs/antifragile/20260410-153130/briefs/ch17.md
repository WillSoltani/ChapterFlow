Core Claim:
This chapter should argue that practical judgment grounded in exposure, consequence, and lived payoff can outperform verbally elegant but disembodied reasoning in uncertain life. Taleb is using Fat Tony as the character who sees reality through incentives and skin in the game, while Socratic or overly abstract discourse risks sounding smarter than it is. The reader should leave seeing that in uncertain domains, argument must answer to consequences, not verbal polish alone.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
