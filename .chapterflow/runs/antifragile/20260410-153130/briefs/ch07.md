Core Claim:
This chapter should argue that many interventions look compassionate or intelligent because they suppress immediate discomfort, yet they often block adaptation and increase hidden fragility. Taleb is not rejecting all intervention. He is targeting intervention that mistakes visible smoothness for improvement. The reader should leave seeing that helping can be harmful when it removes signal, delays correction, or shifts harm into a larger later form.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the intervention chapter. It must stay precise about signal removal, delayed harm, and hidden costs rather than becoming a blanket anti-help slogan.

Author Logic Chain:
First Taleb distinguishes real help from intervention that suppresses corrective stress. Then he shows how smoothing visible pain can conceal larger fragility. Then he asks what kinds of intervention preserve learning instead of destroying it.
