Core Claim:
This chapter should argue that prediction gains prestige in modern systems even when the domain is too complex, nonlinear, or opaque for confident forecasting. Taleb is not rejecting all estimation. He is attacking forecast arrogance and the social status built around it. The reader should leave seeing that payoff awareness and humility often matter more than polished prediction in uncertain domains.
