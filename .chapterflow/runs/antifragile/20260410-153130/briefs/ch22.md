This chapter should argue that long life is often supported more by removing harms and avoiding fragile-making excess than by aggressively adding complicated interventions. Taleb is pushing the reader to see longevity through subtraction, restraint, and respect for what time has already taught about fragility. The reader should leave understanding that not making things worse can be a deeper principle than constant optimization.
