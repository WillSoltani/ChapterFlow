Core Claim:
This chapter should argue that practical people who price payoff and exposure well can understand uncertainty better than polished experts who rely on elegant theory detached from consequences. Taleb is not glorifying ignorance. He is contrasting grounded risk realism with prestige abstraction. The reader should leave seeing that being right about payoff often matters more than sounding refined about models.
