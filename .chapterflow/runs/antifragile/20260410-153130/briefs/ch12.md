Core Claim:
This chapter should argue that optionality is the ability to benefit from favorable outcomes without needing to predict them precisely. Taleb uses Thales to show that freedom, flexibility, and asymmetry can be more valuable than forecast accuracy. The reader should leave seeing optionality as a concrete advantage in uncertainty, not a vague love of keeping options open forever.
