Chapter 2 source notes

- Heading: Overcompensation and Overreaction Everywhere
- Frozen support says this chapter treats setbacks, redundancy, and strong responses to shocks as sources of innovation and recovery.
- Taleb emphasizes abundance and redundancy in natural systems as protection and adaptive capacity, not waste.
- He also uses information as an antifragile object: attempts to suppress or ban information can increase attention to it.
- Keep the chapter on bounded overreaction, redundancy, and upside-from-suppression. Avoid drifting into broad "all conflict is good" language.
