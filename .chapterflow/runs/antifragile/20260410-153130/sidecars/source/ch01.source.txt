Chapter 1 source notes

- Heading: Between Damocles and Hydra
- Frozen support says this chapter introduces the missing opposite of fragile and distinguishes fragile, robust, and antifragile.
- Hydra is the positive image: cut one head and more grow back.
- Damocles represents exposed vulnerability under apparent success or power.
- The chapter argues that resilience and robustness are not enough because they imply resistance, not improvement through stress.
- The working chapter should stay on naming, distinction, and the first usable map of the triad.
- Keep later technical expansions out. The chapter's job is conceptual installation, not the full system.
