Core Claim:
Chapter 8 argues that verbal agreement is a weak predictor of follow-through, so negotiators must test commitment quality, detect hidden resistance, and distinguish talk from real execution.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the execution chapter. It must focus on commitment quality, hidden resistance, and follow-through rather than on persuasion or price tactics alone.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter challenges the idea that agreement guarantees action.
Then it explores signals of weak commitment or hidden resistance.
Then it shows how execution depends on what the other side will actually do after the conversation.
Then it warns that overconfidence in verbal yes leads to preventable failure.

Required Anchors:
- source-heading support that this chapter centers on implementation, liar detection, follow-through, and commitment

Counterarguments or Limits:
