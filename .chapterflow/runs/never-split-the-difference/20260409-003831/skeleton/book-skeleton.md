## Chapter Order And Intent
1. `ch01` The New Rules
   Intent: reset negotiation from rational argument to information gathering, emotional leverage, and tactical empathy under pressure.
2. `ch02` Be a Mirror
   Intent: show how mirroring slows conflict, opens the other side up, and creates working rapport.
3. `ch03` Don't Feel Their Pain, Label It
   Intent: explain why naming emotions cleanly creates trust without collapsing into sympathy.
4. `ch04` Beware "Yes", Master "No"
   Intent: reframe agreement-seeking by treating a safe "no" as the door to real disclosure and momentum.
5. `ch05` Trigger the Two Words That Immediately Transform Any Negotiation
   Intent: show how deep understanding and accurate summaries create the "that's right" moment that unlocks movement.
6. `ch06` Bend Their Reality
   Intent: explore how fairness, loss framing, and perception shape what people experience as reasonable.
7. `ch07` Create the Illusion of Control
   Intent: turn calibrated questions into a way of steering conflict while letting the other side feel agency.
8. `ch08` Guarantee Execution
   Intent: focus on commitment quality, hidden resistance, and the difference between verbal agreement and real follow-through.
9. `ch09` Bargain Hard
   Intent: bring the method into explicit price negotiation, late-stage haggling, and disciplined concession behavior.
10. `ch10` Find the Black Swan
   Intent: close on hidden information, leverage from unknown unknowns, and breakthrough conditions in stuck negotiations.

## Moral Complexity Flags
- ch01: negotiation skill can drift from disciplined empathy into manipulation if detached from truth-testing.
- ch03: emotional labeling can be misused if it becomes performance without real listening.
- ch06: fairness framing and loss pressure require strategic-distance framing rather than endorsement.
- ch08: commitment detection can slide into cynicism if every person is treated as deceptive by default.
- ch10: black-swan hunting can encourage over-reading weak signals if not tied to evidence.
