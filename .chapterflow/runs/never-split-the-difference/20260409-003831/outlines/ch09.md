Chapter Promise:
Show how the book's negotiation method behaves when the conversation turns into explicit price bargaining, concessions, and pressure around numbers.

Opening Move:
Open on the moment when a negotiation stops sounding conceptual and becomes a live haggle over money, terms, or a final range.

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: shift from general negotiation method into explicit bargaining pressure
- P2: show why splitting the difference can signal weak discipline
- P3: introduce non-round numbers and concession structure
- P4: explain strategic umbrage as a controlled emotional bargaining signal
- P5: transfer the logic across work, school, and personal money or term disputes
- P6: preserve the limit that no single bargaining pattern guarantees the outcome

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter is not about theatrical aggression. It is about disciplined bargaining behavior that changes how the other side reads value, pressure, and concession movement.

Threshold Question:
When does bargaining stay structured and strategic, and when does it become lazy splitting that gives away leverage too cheaply?
