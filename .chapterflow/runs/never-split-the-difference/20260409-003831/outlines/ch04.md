Chapter Promise:
Show why a safe no can produce more truth than a pressured yes, and why no-oriented questions keep the other side engaged without trapping them.

Opening Move:
Open on the misleading relief of hearing yes and later discovering that nothing real moved.

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: show why yes often flatters the negotiator too early
- P2: explain the false-yes problem
- P3: show how no preserves autonomy and lowers threat
- P4: introduce no-oriented framing as a practical move
- P5: transfer the idea across work, school, and personal negotiations
- P6: preserve the limit that no needs relationship and context to keep the conversation alive

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Yes can signal politeness, avoidance, or escape. No often signals enough safety for the other side to tell the truth.

Threshold Question:
When does no create useful honesty, and when is it simply the endpoint of a conversation that never became safe enough to continue?
