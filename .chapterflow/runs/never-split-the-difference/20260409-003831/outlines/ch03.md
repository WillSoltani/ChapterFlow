Chapter Promise:
Show how labeling turns emotional recognition into trust and disclosure without requiring the negotiator to become emotionally flooded.

Opening Move:
Open on the familiar moment when someone calms down not because they were solved, but because they finally felt accurately recognized.

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: show why being told facts while upset rarely works
- P2: distinguish absorbing feelings from naming them
- P3: introduce labeling as the active move
- P4: explain why labels lower fear and invite correction
- P5: transfer the skill across work, school, and personal negotiations
- P6: preserve the limit that bad labels feel fake and bridge to `no`

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The negotiator does not need to feel the other side's pain to use it well. The negotiator needs to identify and verbalize it accurately enough that the other side feels seen.

Threshold Question:
When does a label deepen trust, and when does it become an overconfident guess that makes the other side feel misread?
