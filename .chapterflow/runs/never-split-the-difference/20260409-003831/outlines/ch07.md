Chapter Promise:
Show how calibrated questions steer the conversation while letting the other side feel in control, and why that feeling of agency often produces better cooperation than direct pressure.

Opening Move:
Open on the odd moment when the other side feels like they are deciding the path even while you are shaping the structure of the conversation.

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: show why direct demands trigger resistance
- P2: introduce calibrated questions as a steering move
- P3: explain why how/what questions create collaboration pressure
- P4: show how the other side starts solving the problem for you
- P5: transfer the move across work, school, and personal negotiations
- P6: preserve the limit that hostile or fake questions collapse the effect

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter is not about surrendering control. It is about guiding the exchange through questions that preserve the other side's sense of agency.

Threshold Question:
When do calibrated questions create useful agency and problem-solving, and when do they reveal themselves as disguised pressure that makes the other side feel steered anyway?
