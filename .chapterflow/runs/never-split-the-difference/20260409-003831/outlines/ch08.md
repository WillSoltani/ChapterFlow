Chapter Promise:
Show why verbal agreement is not enough, and how commitment quality and hidden resistance determine whether anything real gets executed after the negotiation.

Opening Move:
Open on the familiar moment when a conversation seemed successful until nothing actually happened afterward.

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: show why agreement often gets mistaken for execution
- P2: introduce hidden resistance and weak commitment
- P3: explain why implementation quality is the real test
- P4: show how to look for stronger evidence of follow-through
- P5: transfer the move across work, school, and personal negotiations
- P6: preserve the limit that commitment cannot be read perfectly

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter is not about distrusting everyone. It is about distinguishing surface cooperation from real execution readiness.

Threshold Question:
When does apparent agreement become real follow-through, and when is it only smooth language that hides weak commitment or unresolved resistance?
