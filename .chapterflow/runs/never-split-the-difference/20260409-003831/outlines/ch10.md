Chapter Promise:
Show how hidden information can overturn apparent leverage, reshape stalled negotiations, and create breakthrough movement late in the process.

Opening Move:
Open on the feeling that a negotiation is stuck until one previously unknown fact suddenly changes what the whole room means.

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: frame deadlock as potentially incomplete information rather than final reality
- P2: define black swans as hidden facts that materially change leverage
- P3: explain why unknown unknowns matter more than visible arguments alone
- P4: show how discovery can reset options, pressure, and bargaining power
- P5: transfer the search logic across work, school, and personal negotiations
- P6: preserve the limit that not every odd detail is a real black swan

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter is not about guessing wildly. It is about disciplined discovery of information asymmetries that can change the negotiation's structure.

Threshold Question:
When does hidden information genuinely change leverage, and when is the negotiator only projecting meaning onto noise?
