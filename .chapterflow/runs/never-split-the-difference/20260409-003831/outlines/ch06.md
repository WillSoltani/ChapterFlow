Chapter Promise:
Show how fairness, loss, and anchoring shape what feels reasonable, and why perception can be influenced only if trust and context are still intact.

Opening Move:
Open on the moment when two sides look at the same offer and experience it as completely different realities.

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: show that offers are judged relative to perception, not in a vacuum
- P2: introduce fairness as an emotional frame
- P3: explain loss pressure and reference points
- P4: show how anchoring bends perceived reasonableness
- P5: transfer the idea across work, school, and personal negotiations
- P6: preserve the limit that crude framing destroys trust

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter is not about inventing reality from nothing. It is about understanding that reasonableness is experienced through fairness, loss, and comparison.

Threshold Question:
When does shaping perception clarify risk and value, and when does it become manipulative pressure that makes the other side stop trusting you?
