Chapter Promise:
Show why pressure-first, logic-first negotiation fails in real human situations, and why the opening move of the book is to replace that instinct with listening, safety, and tactical empathy.

Opening Move:
Open on the familiar mistake of pushing harder in a tense conversation and feeling the other person close up instead of come along.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): 1-2 -> Voss's FBI hostage-negotiator credibility and high-stakes origin
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): 2-4 -> negotiation shaped by emotion, safety, and perception rather than logic alone
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): 4-6 -> tactical empathy, no, and later techniques as the better path

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: picture the common failure of arguing harder and getting less
- P2: establish why Voss's hostage-negotiation background makes the reset worth hearing
- P3: argue that people do not negotiate from pure logic when stakes or fear are live
- P4: introduce tactical empathy and listening as the opening correction
- P5: show why everyday negotiations also need safety and discovery rather than pressure
- P6: preserve the limit that empathy is not magic and bridge to mirroring

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The old rules treat negotiation as convincing through forceful logic. The new rules treat negotiation as discovering what the other side is feeling, fearing, and protecting so movement becomes possible.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Emotion-aware negotiation creates leverage, but that leverage becomes morally thin if empathy is performed as a soft weapon instead of as a real route to understanding.

Threshold Question:
When does tactical empathy open a real path to movement, and when is someone using the language of understanding to disguise a pressure move?

- Takeaway 1: old negotiation instincts fail because pressure triggers defense
- Takeaway 2: high-stakes negotiation forced Voss to learn a more human model

- Takeaway 4: safety and "no" matter because control affects disclosure
- Takeaway 5: empathy clarifies truth but cannot rescue a bad underlying offer
