# Critic Card

- Inputs: brief, outline, edited draft, quality gate.
- Score hook quality, anchor use, paragraph distinctness, chapter specificity, easy-mode convertibility, meta-distance, hard-edge preservation, and repetition risk.
- Flag contamination phrases, source-splice risk, weakest paragraph, and strongest sentence.
- Decide whether local patching is enough or a broader reroute is needed.
- Do not rewrite prose or validate JSON.
