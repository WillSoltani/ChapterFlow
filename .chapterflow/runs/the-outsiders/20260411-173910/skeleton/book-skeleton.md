# Book Skeleton

Metadata:
- bookId: the-outsiders
- title: The Outsiders: Eight Unconventional CEOs and Their Radically Rational Blueprint for Success
- author: William N. Thorndike Jr.
- edition: 2012 English Harvard Business Review Press trade-family edition
- wave size: 2

Chapter order and intent:
1. A Perpetual Motion Machine for Returns
   - intent: show how Tom Murphy built extraordinary value through disciplined capital allocation, lean headquarters, local autonomy, and selective large bets
   - source richness: medium
   - concept density: medium
   - moral complexity: low
2. An Unconventional Conglomerateur
   - intent: show how Henry Singleton treated capital allocation as a changing game rather than a fixed doctrine
   - source richness: low-medium
   - concept density: high
   - moral complexity: medium
3. The Turnaround
   - intent: show how Bill Anders reset General Dynamics through portfolio triage and rational downsizing
   - source richness: low-medium
   - concept density: medium-high
   - moral complexity: medium
4. Value Creation in a Fast-Moving Stream
   - intent: show John Malone's unusual skill with cable economics, leverage, and tax-aware value creation
   - source richness: low-medium
   - concept density: high
   - moral complexity: high
5. The Widow Takes the Helm
   - intent: show how Katharine Graham grew from reluctant successor into a disciplined capital allocator
   - source richness: low-medium
   - concept density: medium
   - moral complexity: medium-high
6. A Public LBO
   - intent: show Bill Stiritz using operating rigor and deal discipline to create private-equity-like economics inside a public company
   - source richness: low-medium
   - concept density: high
   - moral complexity: medium
7. Optimizing the Family Firm
   - intent: show Dick Smith balancing family-control realities with outsider-style capital allocation discipline
   - source richness: low-medium
   - concept density: medium
   - moral complexity: medium
8. The Investor as CEO
   - intent: show Buffett as the clearest fusion of operator and investor logic
   - source richness: medium
   - concept density: high
   - moral complexity: medium
9. Radical Rationality
   - intent: distill the outsider mind-set into reusable operating principles without losing case-study specificity
   - source richness: medium
   - concept density: high
   - moral complexity: high
