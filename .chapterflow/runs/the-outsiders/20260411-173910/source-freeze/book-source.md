## High-signal support notes

- The book's through-line is capital allocation, not celebrity CEO mythology.
- The chapter titles and pairings indicate a case-study structure rather than a theory-first structure.
- Chapter 1 centers on Tom Murphy and Capital Cities Broadcasting, with the later ABC acquisition serving as a visible payoff to Murphy and Dan Burke's discipline.
- Reputable secondary support confirms Capital Cities was known for efficient management, lean headquarters, and the surprising scale of the ABC acquisition.
