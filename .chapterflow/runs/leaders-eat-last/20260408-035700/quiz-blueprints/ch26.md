# Chapter 26 Quiz Blueprint — Shared Struggle

Chapter Core:
This chapter most safely argues that shared, meaningful hardship can deepen trust and solidarity more than comfort alone, while rejecting the idea that suffering is good in itself. The hard limit is mutual, bounded struggle rather than romanticized pain.

Question Targets:
1. Define the chapter's shared-burden claim safely.
2. Distinguish shared struggle from glorified suffering.
3. Explain the bridge from Chapter 25 to Chapter 26.
4. Apply the idea that mutual burden builds trust.
5. Contrast shared hardship with outsourced or isolated burden.
6. Recognize why comfort alone does not create the same bond.
7. Preserve the limit against abuse or needless hardship.
8. Distinguish meaningful challenge from pointless pain.
9. Apply the lesson to school or personal settings.
10. Predict why `We Need More Leaders` follows next.

Difficulty Spread:
- easy: title meaning, bridge, hard limit
- medium: trust mechanism, shared cost, application
- hard: mutual protection, non-romanticized struggle, Chapter 27 prediction
