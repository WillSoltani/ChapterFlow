# Chapter 20 Quiz Blueprint — Friends Matter

Chapter Core:
This chapter most safely argues that friendship means real human loyalty inside a group, not sentimentality. The hard limit is that friendship must support truth, sacrifice, and accountability rather than favoritism or lowered standards.

Question Targets:
1. Define friendship safely for this chapter.
2. Explain the bridge from integrity to friendship.
3. Distinguish bond from transaction.
4. Apply the idea that people endure more for people than for abstractions.
5. Connect friendship to trust and Circle-of-Safety conditions.
6. Connect abstraction to cynicism, paranoia, and self-protection.
7. Preserve the limit that friendship is not favoritism.
8. Recognize accountability inside real care.
9. Apply the chapter to school or personal settings.
10. Predict why `Lead the People, Not the Numbers` comes next.

Distractor Rules:
- Wrong choices should reduce friendship to social perk culture or office niceness.
- Wrong choices can overcorrect into saying professionalism requires emotional distance.
- Avoid invented case studies or sentimental slogans.

Transfer Scenarios:
- teams that carry hard weeks differently depending on whether people feel known
- school groups choosing between protecting a friend and protecting fairness
- personal relationships where care makes candor more possible

Difficulty Spread:
- easy: definition, bridge, bond versus transaction
- medium: trust, sacrifice, abstraction, accountability
- hard: cynicism, favoritism limit, people-versus-numbers bridge
