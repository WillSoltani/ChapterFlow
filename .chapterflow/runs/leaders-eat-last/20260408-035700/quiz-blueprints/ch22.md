# Chapter 22 Quiz Blueprint — At the Center of All Our Problems Is Us

Chapter Core:
This chapter most safely argues that broken systems persist partly because human appetites and rationalizations keep rewarding the easier option. The hard limit is that the chapter names participation without denying structural pressure.

Question Targets:
1. Define the chapter's inward-accountability claim safely.
2. Distinguish participation from total blame.
3. Explain the bridge from Chapter 21 to Chapter 22.
4. Apply the idea of preferring the easier abstraction or shortcut.
5. Connect those habits to trust and self-protection.
6. Recognize rationalization as a sustaining mechanism.
7. Preserve the limit that systems still matter.
8. Distinguish explanation from excuse in a concrete scenario.
9. Apply the lesson to school or personal settings.
10. Predict why `At Any Expense` comes next.

Distractor Rules:
- Wrong choices should either deny any personal role or collapse everything into individual blame.
- Wrong choices can drift into unsupported addiction or neuroscience language.
- Avoid named public examples or moral-theater slogans.

Transfer Scenarios:
- choosing the cleaner score over the messier truth
- group habits that keep rewarding short-term comfort over honesty
- personal explanations that quietly function as excuses

Difficulty Spread:
- easy: definition, bridge, participation-versus-blame
- medium: rationalization, trust cost, concrete application
- hard: system-plus-self balance, explanation-versus-excuse, Chapter 23 prediction
