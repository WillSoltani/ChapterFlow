# Chapter 19 Quiz Blueprint — Integrity Matters

Chapter Core:
This chapter most safely argues that integrity is operational coherence under pressure. The hard limit is that integrity is not perfection or image-management; it is whether standards stay real when compromise becomes attractive.

Question Targets:
1. Define integrity safely for this chapter.
2. Explain the bridge from leader example to integrity.
3. Distinguish integrity from branding or private sincerity.
4. Apply the coherence-under-pressure frame.
5. Connect integrity to trust and safety.
6. Connect broken integrity to cynicism and self-protection.
7. Preserve the limit that integrity is not perfection.
8. Recognize selective enforcement as integrity failure.
9. Apply the chapter to school or personal settings.
10. Predict why friendship comes next.

Distractor Rules:
- Wrong choices should reduce integrity to image, reputation, or generic honesty.
- Wrong choices can overcorrect into treating any mistake as total failure.
- Avoid invented scandals or public examples.

Transfer Scenarios:
- leaders who bend rules when the cost reaches them
- school groups where stated standards apply selectively
- personal promises that are tested when keeping them becomes inconvenient

Difficulty Spread:
- easy: title, definition, bridge
- medium: pressure test, trust, selective enforcement
- hard: limits, cynicism, friendship bridge
