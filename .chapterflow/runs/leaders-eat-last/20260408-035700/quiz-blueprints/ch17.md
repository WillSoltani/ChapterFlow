# Chapter 17 Quiz Blueprint — Leadership Lesson 1: So Goes the Culture, So Goes the Company

Chapter Core:
This chapter most safely argues that culture is the repeated pattern of signals, tolerated tradeoffs, and expected behavior that determines how the company actually operates. The hard limit is that culture is not mystical and does not replace structure, but it does shape how structure is lived and copied.

Question Targets:
1. Define culture safely for this chapter.
2. Explain the bridge from imbalance to culture.
3. Distinguish culture from branding or stated values.
4. Apply the repeated-signals frame to a real scenario.
5. Connect culture to trust and cooperation.
6. Connect unhealthy culture to cynicism and self-protection.
7. Preserve the limit that culture is causal without being magic.
8. Recognize tolerated tradeoffs as culture teaching.
9. Apply the chapter to a school or personal setting.
10. Predict why the next lesson turns to leaders.

Distractor Rules:
- Wrong choices should collapse culture into slogans, perks, or abstract values language.
- Wrong choices can overcorrect into claiming culture alone explains everything.
- Avoid invented case studies or management frameworks.

Transfer Scenarios:
- teams that say collaboration matters while rewarding individual rescue behavior
- school groups that claim fairness but repeatedly tolerate the same exclusion pattern
- households where logistics or pressure teach stronger norms than spoken intentions

Difficulty Spread:
- easy: title, definition, and bridge
- medium: repeated signals, tolerated tradeoffs, trust climate
- hard: limits, causal logic, and leader bridge
