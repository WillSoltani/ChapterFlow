# Chapter 18 Quiz Blueprint — Leadership Lesson 2: So Goes the Leader, So Goes the Culture

Chapter Core:
This chapter most safely argues that leaders shape culture because their behavior carries unusual teaching power inside a group. The hard limit is that leaders are not the only force in culture, but they strongly set what feels safe, rewarded, tolerated, and real.

Question Targets:
1. Define the safest reading of the title.
2. Explain the bridge from culture to leader.
3. Distinguish leader behavior from leader rhetoric.
4. Apply the idea of leader as signal source.
5. Connect leader behavior to trust and safety.
6. Connect leader behavior to cynicism or self-protection.
7. Preserve the limit that leaders are powerful without being magical.
8. Recognize tolerated behavior from above as cultural teaching.
9. Apply the chapter in school or personal settings.
10. Predict why integrity comes next.

Distractor Rules:
- Wrong choices should turn the chapter into speech-making, charisma, or generic top-down control.
- Wrong choices can overcorrect into claiming leaders do not matter because systems do.
- Avoid invented leader examples or named executive lore.

Transfer Scenarios:
- manager reactions teaching whether honesty is safe
- team captains or teachers setting tone through what they tolerate
- household authority normalizing either blame or protection

Difficulty Spread:
- easy: title, definition, bridge
- medium: model-and-imitation logic, safety, tolerated behavior
- hard: limits, self-protection, integrity bridge
