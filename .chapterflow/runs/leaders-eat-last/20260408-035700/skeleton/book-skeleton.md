# Book Skeleton

## Metadata
- Book: Leaders Eat Last
- Author: Simon Sinek
- Locked edition: 2017 Portfolio / Penguin revised U.S. trade family
- Package shape: 27 reader-facing units built from the numbered chapter structure of the locked revised edition
- Source posture: coverage-only; later chapter briefs must stay paraphrase-first and claim-light
- Calibration routing: ch01 and ch02
- High-attention routing candidates: ch01, ch02, ch13, ch21, ch24, ch27

## Rotation Plan For Examples
- Maintain the canonical six-format rotation once per chapter: `decision_point`, `postmortem`, `dialogue`, `predict_reveal`, `dilemma`, `before_after`.
- Maintain `2 work / 2 school / 2 personal` examples in every chapter package.
- Rotate endings across `broader_principle`, `self_directed_question`, `surprising_implication`, `cross_domain`, `common_trap`, and `perspective_reframe`.

## Vocabulary Watchlist
- Keep leadership, Circle of Safety, trust, cooperation, empathy, belonging, abstraction, addiction, and responsibility precise and chapter-bound.
- Use biology language only when the brief supports it directly; do not let chemistry terms become fake authority signals.
- Avoid generic workplace inspiration, military-romance cliches, and loose "be authentic" coaching.

## Motif / Callback Opportunities
- ch01-ch04 establish the protection logic: people perform differently when they feel covered rather than exposed.
- ch05-ch10 widen that logic from empathy and leadership into pressure, status, and environmental fit.
- ch11-ch16 explain how modern systems drifted away from human-scale trust and toward abstraction.
- ch17-ch21 translate the diagnosis into leadership lessons about culture, integrity, friendship, and metrics.
- ch22-ch24 frame the social cost as addiction to short-term rewards and disembodied thinking, with ch24 as the revised-edition hinge.
- ch25-ch27 end on practice: recovery through discipline, shared struggle, and a broader definition of leadership.
