# Chapter 27 Outline — We Need More Leaders

Chapter Promise:
The reader should leave seeing leadership as a broader responsibility to protect people and humanize institutions where they stand, not as a role reserved for a few people above them.

Opening Move:
Open with a group waiting for someone higher up to fix a problem that is already hurting people.

Anchor Allocation:
- title / Part 8 frame used in P1 and P6
- Chapter 26 bridge used in P1 and P4
- trust / Circle-of-Safety framing used in P3 and P5
- anti-abstraction / human-cost logic used in P2 and P5

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: move from shared burden to widened leadership
- P2: explain why outsourced responsibility leaves institutions less human
- P3: define leadership as protection and local responsibility, not merely title
- P4: show that more leaders means more people willing to share cost where they are
- P5: preserve limits: titles still matter, but titles alone are insufficient
- P6: close with a live challenge rather than motivational summary

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Differentiate leadership as role from leadership as responsibility. The mechanism is that trust survives when more people take local responsibility for human consequences instead of waiting for formal authority to absorb all moral work.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard mode must hold the tension that institutions still need formal leaders, but those leaders cannot humanize the system alone if everyone else treats responsibility as somebody else's assignment.

- Takeaway 2: responsibility cannot be outsourced upward
- Takeaway 3: leadership as protection in a local sphere
- Takeaway 4: titles matter but are insufficient
- Takeaway 5: closing challenge to act without waiting for ideal authority
