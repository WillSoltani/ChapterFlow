# Chapter 22 Outline — At the Center of All Our Problems Is Us

Chapter Promise:
The reader should leave understanding that broken systems persist partly because human appetites and rationalizations keep rewarding the easier, thinner, or more flattering choice.

Opening Move:
Open with a moment where someone can already see the honest action but still reaches for the cleaner, faster, or safer alternative.

Anchor Allocation:
- title / part placement used in P1 and P6
- Chapter 21 bridge used in P1 and P2
- trust / Circle-of-Safety framing used in P3 and P4
- cynicism / self-interest framing used in P4 and P5
- designed-versus-current-conditions framing used in P5 and P6

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: live inward-turn moment and chapter question
- P2: define the chapter as participation, not system denial
- P3: show how appetites for convenience, relief, and validation feed the broken pattern
- P4: connect those habits to cynicism, self-protection, and thinning trust
- P5: preserve the limit that systems still matter, but our rationalizations keep helping them win
- P6: hard edge: inward accountability without contempt or blame-the-victim drift; bridge to short-term wins at any expense

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Differentiate explanation from excuse. The mechanism is that people often prefer the faster, clearer, more rewarding abstraction or shortcut, then rationalize that preference as necessity or realism.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must preserve that systems shape us while still saying we participate in them. Hard mode only works if it argues that our attraction to convenience, status, distance, or short-term relief helps keep the distortion alive even when we recognize it.

Scenario Lesson Map:

Next-Chapter Bridge:
