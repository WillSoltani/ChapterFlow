# Chapter 7 Outline — The Big C

## Shape
- P1: Scene hook. Late-night alerts, flinching team, constant bracing.
- P2: Narrow chapter read. Five-chemical frame plus `E.D.S.O.` makes this the likely remaining `C` force.
- P3: Define the force as chronic alarm, vigilance, or threat pressure that can dominate a room.
- P4: Show the social cost: guardedness, blame, concealment, paranoia, and self-interest.
- P5: Make it managerial. Ask what practices keep people braced all the time.
- P6: Bridge ahead. Later leadership chapters will require people who can absorb pressure instead of spreading it.

## Guardrails
- Keep naming cautious; over-explain the social effects, under-explain the biology.
- Use concrete practices: emergency mode, public shaming, rumor, unstable priorities, constant alerts.
- Do not turn the chapter into generic anti-stress advice.
