# Chapter 8 Outline — Why We Have Leaders

## Shape
- P1: Hook with pressure and confusion: the team asks what the boss is actually for when things get hard.
- P2: Use the frozen FAQ anchor: leadership is a choice, not a rank.
- P3: Connect that choice to the human need for safety and trusted networks.
- P4: Use `leaders eat last` as the clearest concrete image of responsibility before privilege.
- P5: Translate into workplace design: trust, cooperation, collaboration, and employee well-being.
- P6: Close with the limit case: title without sacrifice is empty; Chapter 9 should force cost and courage.

## Tensions
- rank versus responsibility
- authority versus protection
- privilege versus obligation
- fast personal gain versus durable group trust
- symbolic leadership versus costly leadership

## Avoid
- abstract “great leaders inspire” language
- military hero worship beyond the care-before-privilege symbol
- generic servant-leadership jargon without concrete consequence
