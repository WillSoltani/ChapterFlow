# Chapter 23 Outline — At Any Expense

Chapter Promise:
The reader should leave seeing that the dangerous part of a short-term win is not only its speed but the way it can hide who pays for it.

Opening Move:
Open with a result that looks clean to the decision-maker while the human bill has been pushed out of sight.

Anchor Allocation:
- title / part placement used in P1 and P6
- Chapter 22 bridge used in P1 and P2
- trust / Circle-of-Safety framing used in P3 and P4
- cynicism / self-interest framing used in P4 and P5
- abstraction / numbers sequence used in P2 and P5

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: bridge from inward participation to hidden human cost
- P2: define the chapter as cost shifting, not anti-ambition
- P3: show how distance and abstraction make the cost easier to ignore
- P4: connect hidden cost to trust erosion and self-interest
- P5: preserve the limit that not every tradeoff is corrupt, but the chapter targets wins purchased with someone else's burden
- P6: hard edge: a result is morally thinner when the bill is displaced; bridge to `The Abstract Generation`

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Differentiate achievement from success purchased by invisible human cost. The mechanism is that abstraction and reward structure make it easier to accept harm when the harmed person, team, or future consequence is less visible than the result.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must keep ambition and growth from becoming the enemy. Hard mode only works if it argues that the real issue is detached success, where the chooser feels the benefit while others absorb the strain, silence, or cleanup.

Scenario Lesson Map:

- Scenario 6 (before_after, personal): teaches how naming the hidden bill changes what counts as success

Next-Chapter Bridge:
If abstract systems make it easier to hide who pays, what happens to people formed inside those systems from the start?
