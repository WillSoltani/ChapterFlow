Chapter Title: Leadership Lesson 2: So Goes the Leader, So Goes the Culture
Position In Book: Part 6 follow-through after culture lesson, moving upstream from company behavior to leader behavior

Core Claim:
This chapter most safely argues that leaders shape culture because their behavior carries unusual teaching power inside a group. `So Goes the Leader, So Goes the Culture` should therefore read as a concrete chain chapter, not as hero worship. The narrow claim is that people watch authority closely and learn from what leaders model, reward, excuse, and punish, which means the leader sets the ceiling and tone for what culture becomes under pressure.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
The chapter is not simply repeating that culture matters. Its distinct move is to identify the leader as culture's strongest upstream signal. If Chapter 17 says culture drives the company, Chapter 18 says leader behavior is a major reason culture becomes what it becomes.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author uses the lesson title to make leader behavior upstream of culture.
Then the author likely builds from Chapter 17: culture is repeated teaching, and leaders are unusually visible teachers.
Then the author reconnects this chain to the book's protection logic, trust-versus-cynicism framing, and Circle-of-Safety conditions.
Then the author concludes that culture cannot outrun the example, tolerances, and tradeoffs taught by those with authority.
