# Chapter 27 Brief — We Need More Leaders

Book: Leaders Eat Last
Author: Simon Sinek
Publication Year: 2017
Book ID: leaders-eat-last

Chapter Number: 27
Chapter Title: We Need More Leaders
Position In Book: Part 8 closing chapter that widens leadership from role to responsibility

Core Claim:
This chapter most safely argues that leadership cannot remain the job of a few people at the top if institutions are supposed to stay human. `We Need More Leaders` should close by widening the definition of leadership into a willingness to protect people, share burden, and accept responsibility where you are rather than waiting for rescue from above. The narrow claim is not that hierarchy disappears. It is that humane institutions depend on more people acting like leaders inside their local sphere of influence.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
The chapter is not a generic call for confidence or ambition. Its distinct move is to convert the book's earlier protection-and-burden logic into a social requirement: responsibility for trust, dignity, and mutual protection cannot be outsourced upward forever. If Chapter 26 says real leaders share burden, Chapter 27 says more people must become the kind of person who does that.

Author Logic Chain:
First the title widens the frame from one leader to a larger shortage of leadership.
Then the chapter likely argues that waiting for authority alone leaves institutions abstract and under-protective.
Then it shows that leadership is a practice of taking responsibility for people, cost, and culture where you stand.
Then the author closes by turning the book's themes into a live challenge rather than a motivational wrap-up.
