## Working thesis
`Why We Have Leaders` likely answers the question raised by the earlier chemistry and threat chapters: if groups are shaped by reward, fear, and trust, what is leadership for in the first place? The strongest source-bound answer is that leaders exist to create and protect the conditions in which other people can work, speak, cooperate, and survive together. Rank may indicate authority, but leadership is the choice to spend that authority on protection rather than self-advantage.
