Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand that fit within a system often depends on occupying a distinct role, and that overlap or role confusion can weaken the larger environment.

Opening Move:
Open on two people or teams stepping on each other's work because the roles in the shared system are poorly differentiated.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 niche as differentiated role
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 overlap friction and specialization
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 flexibility limits and practical use

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: live scene of duplicated effort or role confusion
- P2: define niche as distinct role within a larger system
- P3: show how specialization can reduce friction
- P4: explain why total overlap weakens fit
- P5: preserve flexibility and warn against brittle over-specialization
- P6: practical rule for role clarity and fit
- P7 if needed: bridge to self-preservation

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter must separate useful specialization from rigid identity or frozen role assignment.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must keep the balance between differentiation that helps the system and over-specialization that reduces resilience.

Threshold Question:
What distinct role does this person, team, or habit serve in the larger environment, and where is overlap creating waste?

Takeaway Count Lock:
- Easy: 3 (always)

- Hard: 5

- Takeaway 1: niches are differentiated roles within a system
- Takeaway 2: role fit improves performance inside a shared environment
- Takeaway 3: overlap friction creates waste and conflict
- Takeaway 4: specialization helps until it becomes brittle or isolated
- Takeaway 5: wise judgment clarifies distinct value without freezing adaptation
