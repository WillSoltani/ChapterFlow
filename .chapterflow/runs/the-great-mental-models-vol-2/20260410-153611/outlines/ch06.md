- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 fast movement hiding directional error
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 speed versus heading in personal or team settings
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 corrective slowdown and aligned acceleration

- P1: live scene of busy but misaligned movement

The chapter must separate raw speed from meaningful movement toward the right destination.
