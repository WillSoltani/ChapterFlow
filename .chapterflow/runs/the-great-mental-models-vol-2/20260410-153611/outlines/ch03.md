By the end, the reader should understand that order requires ongoing energy, and deferred maintenance quietly turns into larger disorder later.

- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 drift and accumulating disorder
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 disorder displacement and overcontrol limit

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
