Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand why the right combination can outperform purity, and how complementary strengths create better properties than isolated elements alone.

Opening Move:
Open on a system or team that improves once a balancing element is added that changes the whole mix.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 better properties from the right mix
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 complementary strengths versus purity trap
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 deliberate combination versus incoherent mixture

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: live scene of weak purity or incomplete strength
- P2: reveal how a combination improves the system
- P3: explain alloying and complementary strengths
- P4: show mixture improving resilience or versatility
- P5: separate good combination from random variety
- P6: practical rule for combination design
- P7 if needed: bridge to biology section

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter must separate thoughtful alloying from random mixture or variety for its own sake.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must keep alive the boundary between beneficial complementary design and bad combinations that weaken the system.

Threshold Question:
What strengths need to be combined so the whole system gains properties none of the parts could produce alone?

Takeaway Count Lock:
- Easy: 3 (always)
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 5

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- Takeaway 1: combinations can create better properties than purity
- Takeaway 2: complementary strengths matter more than isolated strength
- Takeaway 3: purity can become a weakness in real systems
- Takeaway 4: not every mix is good; design matters
- Takeaway 5: resilient systems often come from thoughtful combination
