Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand that incentives steer behavior through practical consequences, and that wise judgment studies what a system actually rewards rather than what it merely says.

Opening Move:
Open on a setting where a reward structure predictably changes behavior.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 incentives and behavior direction
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 signal mismatch and distorted response
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 adaptive response and practical use

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: live scene of behavior shifting under an incentive
- P2: define incentives as behavior-shaping consequences
- P3: show mismatch between stated aim and rewarded behavior
- P4: explain how people adapt to the real signal
- P5: show limit where narrow incentives distort judgment
- P6: practical rule for reading what a system really rewards
- P7 if needed: bridge to energy minimization

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter must separate official goals from actual consequences.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must keep both truths alive: incentives are powerful, and they can misfire when the rewarded behavior is narrower than the intended result.

Threshold Question:
What behavior is this system truly paying for, and what secondary behavior will that invite?

Takeaway Count Lock:

- Hard: 5

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- Takeaway 1: incentives steer behavior
- Takeaway 2: real reward structures matter more than stated values
- Takeaway 3: people adapt to the actual signal
- Takeaway 4: narrow incentives can distort outcomes
- Takeaway 5: wise judgment reads reward structures before judging responses
