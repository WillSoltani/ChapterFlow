# Source Discovery

## Objective

Freeze a lawful source bundle for *The Great Mental Models, Volume 2: Physics, Chemistry, and Biology* before any chapter work begins.

## Discovery result

No public-domain full text exists for this book. The usable bundle is therefore:

1. current official publisher metadata from Penguin Random House
2. authorized preview metadata and partial contents from Google Books
3. complete chapter map from Ingram Syndetics syndication surfaced on Better World Books
4. edition-history confirmation from Open Library
5. narrow secondary chapter notes from Scott Vejdani only where the authorized preview does not expose enough interior text

## Dominant edition call

The working edition is the 2024 Penguin edition. The older 2020 Latticework hardcover appears to be the original lineage, but the frozen evidence does not show chapter-level structural divergence. Because the current Penguin edition has official publisher metadata and accessible preview support, it is the most stable edition lock for this run.

## Use constraints

- Paraphrase-first downstream.
- No exact quotation unless directly supported by the frozen authorized preview bundle.
- Stay narrower than unsupported detail when a chapter interior is thin.
- Secondary notes can sharpen chapter intent, but they cannot override the authorized bundle.
