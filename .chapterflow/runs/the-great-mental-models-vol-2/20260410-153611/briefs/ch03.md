Core Claim:
Thermodynamics is the model that reminds you order costs energy. Systems do not stay clean, organized, trustworthy, or effective by default. If energy is not applied to maintenance, correction, and renewal, drift sets in and disorder grows. Strong judgment therefore looks for hidden upkeep costs and recognizes when visible order is only being sustained by shifting chaos somewhere else.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This chapter is about maintenance pressure and disorder drift, not feedback loops or perspective. It should feel impossible to confuse with Chapter 2 because the core move is not response training but the ongoing energy required to keep a system ordered.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author establishes that order is not free.
Then the author shows how deferred maintenance lets disorder accumulate quietly.
Then the author concludes that wise action budgets for upkeep and checks where disorder is being displaced.

Required Anchors:

Specific Applications:
