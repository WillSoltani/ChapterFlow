   - concept density: medium
   - moral complexity: low

   - concept density: medium
   - moral complexity: low

   - concept density: medium-high

## Rotation Plan For Examples

- keep two work, two school, and two personal examples in each structured chapter
- rotate the six canonical formats exactly once per chapter
- avoid repeating the same ultralearning domain across adjacent chapters

## School-Setting Plan

- rotate among seminar, coding club, language lab, thesis meeting, design review, and exam-prep environments

## Vocabulary Watchlist

- avoid hustle culture filler: hack, crush it, grind, optimize everything, genius, limitless
- prefer operational verbs: map, practice, test, isolate, revise, transfer, retain

## Motif / Callback Opportunities

- self-direction versus institutional defaults
- learning as designed practice rather than passive consumption
- intensity with boundaries
- real-world transfer over comfortable simulation

## Thin-Chapter Risk Notes

- chapter 2 can drift into generic future-of-work rhetoric if it loses the book's concrete self-education pressure
- principle chapters can collapse into advice-list summaries if the anchor examples and failure modes are not kept visible
- chapter 14 can drift into inspirational closure unless it preserves the book's unconventional-education tension

## Premium-Routing Candidates

- Chapter 4 because metalearning can become abstract if it loses concrete project design pressure
- Chapter 6 because directness can blur into generic "practice more" advice
- Chapter 11 because intuition can invite unsupported cognitive-language filler
