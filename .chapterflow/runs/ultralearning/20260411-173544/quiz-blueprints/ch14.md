# Chapter 14 Quiz Blueprint

Core Concepts:
- unconventional education widens the book from one project to a broader learning path
- ultralearning is deep self-education rather than passive dependence on fixed routes
- self-direction and adaptation are valuable, but they still require structure and effort
- the closing reflection is grounded, not utopian or anti-institution
- project-based learning can shape how a learner thinks about education more broadly

Question Targets:
1. Define unconventional education in the chapter's bounded sense.
2. Explain how the chapter widens from project to broader educational meaning.
3. Apply the principle to a learner deciding whether to keep building self-directed projects.
4. Distinguish grounded self-direction from structure-free fantasy.
5. Connect the chapter back to the whole book.
