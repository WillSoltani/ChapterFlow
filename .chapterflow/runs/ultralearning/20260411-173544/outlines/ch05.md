Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Focus is a practical condition for deep work on the target, not a lucky mood.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Protection helps concentration survive, but protective rituals can become avoidance if they replace actual contact with the task.

Threshold Question:
How much protection is enough to keep attention stable before focus routines become another detour around the work?

- Takeaway 4: focus should be structured, not romanticized
