Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Feedback matters when the learner puts real work in front of corrective response rather than staying protected inside private preparation.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Correction can sharpen learning, but not every comment is useful; the learner has to face real signal without dodging it and without surrendering to noise.

Threshold Question:
How do you seek correction without either hiding from it or treating every reaction as equally valuable?
