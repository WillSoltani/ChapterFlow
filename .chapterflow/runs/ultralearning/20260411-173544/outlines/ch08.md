Chapter Promise:
Show that learning gets stronger when the learner tests recall instead of only rereading material passively.

Opening Move:
Open on the false comfort of recognizing a page and mistaking that familiarity for usable memory.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Retrieval asks the learner to produce knowledge from memory, while passive rereading can leave the answer in sight and disguise weak recall.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Testing helps when it genuinely demands recall, but it can become fake retrieval if the cue is too leading or the answer remains practically visible.

Threshold Question:
How do you tell whether a study move is actually forcing recall rather than just creating a feeling of recognition?
