Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Ultralearning matters because commitment changes the rate and depth of progress. Dabbling is exploratory; ultralearning is designed for breakthroughs.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Ultralearning is powerful, but it is not automatically the right mode for every goal. Hard depth must keep the tradeoff between leverage and over-commitment visible.

Threshold Question:
When does a goal deserve the cost of ultralearning instead of the lighter touch of dabbling?
