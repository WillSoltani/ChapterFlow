Title: How to become an ultralearner

Frozen support:
- first projects should focus on one thing rather than a pile of unrelated ambitions
- smaller projects need tighter constraints so progress can become visible
- common project formats include full-time, fixed-schedule, and fixed-hour approaches
- preparation should include learning-method research, gathering materials, building a simple attack plan, and a pilot week
- pilot testing is useful because it reveals unrealistic schedules before full commitment

Allowed exact lines:
- none; paraphrase only

Practical support boundary:
- safe to argue for one target, realistic formats, preparation, and pilot testing
- not safe to invent detailed time formulas or universal scheduling prescriptions
