CHAPTER 8 SOURCE SIDECAR

Frozen authorized support for Chapter 8:
- Principle 5 title from frozen table of contents: Retrieval: test to learn.
- Narrow principle support from frozen Scott H. Young materials: retrieval means testing to learn rather than rereading passively.

Safe extensions permitted by the frozen support:
- Retrieval asks the learner to try producing knowledge rather than only reviewing it again in view.
- Passive rereading can feel smoother than recall because the source remains present.
- Testing can be used as part of learning rather than only as a final judgment after learning.

Safe limits:
- Do not claim laboratory memory results, percentages, or neuroscience mechanisms.
- Do not claim rereading is always useless.
- Keep the chapter centered on recall versus passive review, not on formal exam systems.
