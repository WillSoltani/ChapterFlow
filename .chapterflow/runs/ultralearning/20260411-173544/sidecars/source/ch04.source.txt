Title: Principle 1: Metalearning: first draw a map

Frozen support:
- metalearning centers on research into why, what, and how to learn before and during a project
- the chapter title frames the principle as drawing a map first
- safe extension: why refers to purpose or stakes, what refers to the parts of the skill, and how refers to learning methods matched to the target
- safe limit: the map should guide action and remain revisable during the project

Allowed exact lines:
- none; paraphrase only

Practical support boundary:
- safe to argue for orientation before sprinting, for mapping motive, scope, and method, and for revising the map when the project teaches back
- not safe to invent named subcategories, fixed percentages, or detailed learning recipes absent from the frozen bundle
