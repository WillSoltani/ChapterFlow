Core Claim:
Drill means isolating a rate-determining weakness and attacking it directly instead of always practicing the whole skill in one piece. Scott Young uses this principle to solve bottlenecks that keep broader practice from compounding. The chapter matters because whole-task practice can stay too blunt when one weak link keeps slowing everything else down.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the weakness-isolation chapter. If it loses the bottleneck logic, it collapses into generic repetition advice.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author argues that some weak points hold back the whole performance disproportionately.
Then the author shows why isolating and attacking that weak point can speed overall improvement.
Then the author keeps the principle bounded by tying drills back to the larger target skill instead of letting them become disconnected exercises.
