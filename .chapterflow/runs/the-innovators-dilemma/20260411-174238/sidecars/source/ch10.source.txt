Managing the Strategy Development Process appears near the end of Part 2 in the frozen 2013 Harvard Business Review Press table of contents. The authorized bundle frames Part 2 as managerial response after structural fit, discovery-driven learning, and organizational capabilities. Combined with the chapter title and sequence after Chapter 9, the safe reading is that Christensen now turns from market and organization fit to the process by which strategy should be formed. The likely boundary is that strategy in disruptive settings cannot be treated as a purely top-down finished plan detached from discovery, capabilities, and organizational conditions. Managers need a strategy development process that works with emergence, learning, and the organization's actual limits. The frozen boundary supports strategy development as a process question, but not a detailed named framework or specific case account for this chapter.
