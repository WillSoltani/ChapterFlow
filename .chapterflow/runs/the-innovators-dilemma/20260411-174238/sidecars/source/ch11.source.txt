The Dilemmas of Innovation: A Summary is the final chapter in the frozen 2013 Harvard Business Review Press table of contents. The frozen source bundle also states that the final section synthesizes the recurring dilemmas rather than adding a separate unrelated framework. That creates a clear boundary for this chapter. It should gather the book's repeated tensions into a summary pattern: strong incumbents behaving rationally yet failing under disruption, customer and value-network dependence, mismatch between organization and emerging market, small-market unattractiveness, capability limits, and strategy that must develop under uncertainty. The safe reading is synthesis and naming of the pattern, not a fresh case study chapter or a new proprietary model. The chapter should sound like a conclusion that compresses the argument without flattening its contradictions.
