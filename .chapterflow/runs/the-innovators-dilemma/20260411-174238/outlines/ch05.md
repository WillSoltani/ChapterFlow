Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Managing disruptive change does not mean abandoning discipline. It means refusing to use the established business's near-term proof tests as the only way to judge an immature market.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The incumbent can manage seriously and still destroy the disruptive effort by demanding reliable forecasts, margins, or customer validation that only a mature market can produce.

Threshold Question:
What should a manager do when the new effort cannot satisfy the old business's standards yet, but killing it early may guarantee the firm never learns whether the market is real?
