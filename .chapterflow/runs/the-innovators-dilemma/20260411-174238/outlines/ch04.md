Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Firms do not resist low-end markets only because of attitude; their financial logic and cost structure make those markets hard to justify.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The same move that raises profits in the present can create the unattended low-end gap that gives entrants room to climb.

Threshold Question:
