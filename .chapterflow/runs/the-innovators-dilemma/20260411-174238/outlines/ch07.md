Show that the right organization must also be the right size for the market, because a small emerging market may not matter enough to a large unit.

Open on the mismatch between a real market opportunity and a large organization's need for larger wins.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The same market can be too small to matter to one organization and large enough to matter to another. Strategic value is relative to scale.

Hard-Depth Tension:
A large organization can correctly see a market as real and still reject it because the market is too small to affect its growth, while a smaller unit could rationally pursue it.

Threshold Question:
What should managers do when the market is real but still too small to matter inside the organization that currently owns it?
