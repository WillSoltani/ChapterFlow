Chapter Promise:
Show that strategy in disruptive settings must be managed as a development process rather than treated as a finished top-down answer alone.

Opening Move:
Open on the tension between the need for strategic guidance and the reality that key answers still emerge during action.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The issue is not strategy versus no strategy. The issue is whether managers treat strategy as a process that can develop through learning or as a prematurely fixed answer.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Managers must guide strategic direction seriously while accepting that the final strategy may emerge through discovery and organizational constraints rather than complete advance design.

Threshold Question:
How should leaders manage strategy when the market, the organization's fit, and the important lessons are still developing?
