Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The problem is not only executive will. Organizations are shaped by the customers they serve, so a unit whose customers do not need the disruptive technology is structurally unlikely to make it a real priority.

Hard-Depth Tension:
A competent incumbent unit can sincerely support the disruptive effort in words and still under-serve it in practice because its real customers keep pulling attention elsewhere.

Threshold Question:
Where should responsibility live when the people who currently define success for the incumbent unit do not yet value the disruptive offering?
