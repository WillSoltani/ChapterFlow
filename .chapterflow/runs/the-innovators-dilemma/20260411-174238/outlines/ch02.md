Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Product strength is relative to the network that values it, not an absolute property.

Hard-Depth Tension:
A firm can evaluate correctly by today's standards and still miss tomorrow's market because its standards are network-bound.

Threshold Question:
How do you judge a weak-looking technology when your current market is training you to ignore exactly the customers who want it?
