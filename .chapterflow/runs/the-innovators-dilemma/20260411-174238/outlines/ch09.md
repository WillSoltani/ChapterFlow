Show that organizational capabilities are context-bound, so strengths in one market can become disabilities in another.

Opening Move:

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Capabilities are not universal assets. They depend on the match between organizational routines and the task at hand.

Hard-Depth Tension:
An organization can be strong, disciplined, and admired while still being poorly fitted for the new market it is trying to enter.

Threshold Question:
How should managers judge whether the routines that made them successful are helping or hurting their response to a new market?
