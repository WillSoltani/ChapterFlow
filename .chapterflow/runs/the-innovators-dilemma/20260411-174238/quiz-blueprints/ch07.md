# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter: Match the Size of the Organization to the Size of the Market
Core concepts to test:
- market significance is relative to organizational size
- a small real market may not matter enough to a large unit
- growth threshold mismatch can create rational dismissal
- a smaller organization can care about the same market more seriously
- scale fit is not the same as proving the market fully discovered

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: evaluate

Question plan:
- q01: identify the chapter's scale-fit rule
- q02: explain why large units dismiss small real markets
- q03: apply the relative-importance idea
- q04: named work scenario about moving a niche opportunity to a smaller unit
- q05: distinguish strategic importance from immediate materiality
- q06: reject the misreading that all small markets are good
- q07: school transfer about assigning a small program to the right-sized team
- q08: personal scenario about revisiting an opportunity rejected as too small
- q09: deeper synthesis on rational dismissal inside a large unit
- q10: bridge toward Chapter 8's market-discovery problem

Distractor notes:
- tempting wrong answer type 1: if a market is small, it must be irrelevant
- tempting wrong answer type 2: small size alone guarantees success

Explanation diversity notes:
- avoid repeated openers
- each direct explanation must begin differently

CorrectIndex plan:
- keep distribution roughly balanced across 0 / 1 / 2
