# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter: How to Appraise Your Organization's Capabilities and Disabilities
Core concepts to test:
- capabilities are context-bound rather than universally transferable
- routines and strengths can become disabilities in a new market
- self-appraisal should judge fit, not celebrate excellence in the abstract
- a strong organization can still be the wrong instrument for a new task
- the chapter bridges toward the strategy development process

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: evaluate

Question plan:
- q01: identify the chapter's main capability claim
- q02: explain why strengths can become disabilities
- q03: apply the routine-misfit idea
- q04: named work scenario about a strong process blocking a new effort
- q05: distinguish organizational excellence from contextual fit
- q06: reject the misreading that every routine becomes harmful
- q07: school transfer about whether top-program habits fit a different learner group
- q08: personal scenario about over-trusting familiar strengths
- q09: deeper synthesis on why admired organizations can still fail new tasks
- q10: bridge toward strategy development process

Distractor notes:
- tempting wrong answer type 1: past excellence guarantees future fit
- tempting wrong answer type 2: disruption means all routines are bad

Explanation diversity notes:
- avoid repeated openers
- each direct explanation must begin differently

CorrectIndex plan:
- keep distribution roughly balanced across 0 / 1 / 2
