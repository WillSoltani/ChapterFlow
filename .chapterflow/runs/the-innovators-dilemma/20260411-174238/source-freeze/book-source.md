## Locked Edition
- Title: The Innovator's Dilemma: When New Technologies Cause Great Firms to Fail
- Author: Clayton M. Christensen
- Publisher: Harvard Business Review Press
- Published: 2013 reprint
- Original publication provenance: Harvard Business School Press, 1997

## Official High-Level Framing
From the official HBS book page, frozen as paraphrase:
- Christensen argues that successful companies can still lose market leadership when disruptive innovations emerge.
- The book distinguishes between doing the established-business logic well and learning when that same logic becomes a trap.
- The book offers case-based guidance for recognizing and responding to disruptive innovation.

## Secondary Chapter Support
SuperSummary confirms the broad movement of the book:
- Part 1 uses the disk-drive and excavator industries to show how strong incumbents fail.
- Part 2 shifts from diagnosis to managerial response: autonomous organizations, small-market fit, discovery-driven learning, and organizational capabilities.
- The final section synthesizes the recurring dilemmas rather than adding a separate unrelated framework.
