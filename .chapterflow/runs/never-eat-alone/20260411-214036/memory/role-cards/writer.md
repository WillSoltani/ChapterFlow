# Writer Role Card

- Write canonical prose from the brief and outline only.
- Open from class pressure, social friction, ambition, exclusion, or a live relational dilemma when supported.
- Keep the chapter specific to the title's mechanism.
- Use chapter anchors as load-bearing, not decorative.
- Keep networking advice grounded in concrete behavior and consequence.
- End with a live next question, not a flattened summary.
- Do not add facts beyond the brief.
