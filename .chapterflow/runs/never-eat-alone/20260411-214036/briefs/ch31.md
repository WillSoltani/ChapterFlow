Core Claim:
This chapter argues that a healthy long-game network grows through repeated reciprocal guidance, not only through one-way advice seeking. Within the frozen support boundary, Ferrazzi's point is that mentorship and menteeship strengthen development when both become recurring relational practices rather than isolated transactions.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the book's reciprocal-guidance chapter. If it goes generic, it becomes vague mentoring advice or hero-worship. It has to show why growth becomes more humane and durable when people both receive guidance and extend it.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author moves from disciplined success into the question of how growth stays renewable across relationships.
Then the author shows that mentors and mentees create a repeating cycle of learning, perspective, and reciprocity.
Then the author concludes that networks stay healthy when guidance flows in more than one direction over time.
