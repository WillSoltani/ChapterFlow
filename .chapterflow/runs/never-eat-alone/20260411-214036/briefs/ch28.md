Core Claim:
This chapter argues that approaching influence and powerful people works best through contribution, strategic humility, and clarity about what matters rather than through flattery, status chasing, or awe. Within the frozen support boundary, Ferrazzi's point is that closeness to power should be handled as a disciplined relational skill rather than as mere social climbing.
