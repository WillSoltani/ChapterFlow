Core Claim:
This chapter argues that high-leverage people are best approached through contribution, usefulness, and relational fit rather than through naked access-seeking. Within the frozen support boundary, Ferrazzi's point is that connectors respond better to value-bearing approach than to needy chase.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the book's connector chapter. If it goes generic, it becomes cynical access-hacking advice. It has to show why approach quality matters when the target person has many demands on their attention.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author turns from deliberate conference use to what happens when a useful room contains high-leverage people.
Then the author shows that chasing access directly is weaker than approaching with contribution.
Then the author concludes that connectors are best engaged through value, fit, and relational usefulness rather than pressure.
