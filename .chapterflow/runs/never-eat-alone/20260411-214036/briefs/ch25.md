Core Claim:
This chapter argues that stronger connections often come from having a richer inner life, broader interests, and more substance to bring into conversation. Within the frozen support boundary, Ferrazzi's point is that interestingness is not performance alone; it comes from what a person genuinely notices, learns, and cares about.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the book's substance-and-curiosity chapter. If it goes generic, it becomes charisma advice or shallow personality optimization. It has to show why richer inputs make encounters more memorable without turning interestingness into pure performance.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author moves from better encounters and overlap to the question of what makes those encounters worth having.
Then the author shows that richer interests, experiences, and curiosity create more conversational and relational depth.
Then the author concludes that interesting people are usually bringing more life, range, and attention into the room rather than just better social technique.
