Core Claim:
This chapter argues that ideas, curation, and useful sharing can keep a person visible and valuable across a broader network. Within the frozen support boundary, Ferrazzi's point is that content can function as ongoing usefulness when it helps people think, notice, or connect more clearly.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the book's content-and-curation chapter. If it goes generic, it becomes shallow personal-brand advice or noisy posting doctrine. It has to show why useful ideas can extend presence without rewarding empty broadcast behavior.
