Core Claim:
This chapter argues that intermediaries and gatekeepers should be treated as relationships to honor rather than barriers to bulldoze. Within the frozen support boundary, Ferrazzi's point is that access improves when the person in between is treated with respect, relevance, and patience instead of as an obstacle.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the book's intermediary chapter. If it goes generic, it becomes etiquette advice. It has to show why gatekeepers matter strategically and relationally.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author turns from warmer first contact to the people who control access.
Then the author shows that gatekeepers are not props but real participants in the access path.
Then the author concludes that respectful treatment of intermediaries creates better access than bulldozing or dismissing them.
