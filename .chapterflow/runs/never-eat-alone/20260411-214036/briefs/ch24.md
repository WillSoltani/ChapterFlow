Core Claim:
This chapter argues that useful chance encounters become more likely when a person deliberately designs for exposure, circulation, and overlap. Within the frozen support boundary, Ferrazzi's point is that serendipity is not pure luck; it can be encouraged by how people gather, move, host, and share.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the book's designed-luck chapter. If it goes generic, it becomes magical thinking or vague "be open" advice. It has to show why useful chance can be made more likely through environment and behavior.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author moves from useful visibility into the question of where surprising opportunities actually happen.
Then the author shows that structure, exposure, and overlap can increase the odds of meaningful encounters.
Then the author concludes that serendipity is strengthened when people design their routines and environments to create more useful crossings.
