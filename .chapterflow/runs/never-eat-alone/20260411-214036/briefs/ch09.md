Core Claim:
This chapter argues that first contact does not have to stay as cold, abrupt, or socially costly as people fear. Within the frozen support boundary, Ferrazzi's point is that thoughtful context, warmer framing, and relevance can reduce the friction of initial outreach without pretending the contact is already close.
