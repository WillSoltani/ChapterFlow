Core Claim:
This chapter argues that relationships compound when a person does not only enter rooms but also creates rooms, gatherings, and recurring points of connection for other people. Within the frozen support boundary, Ferrazzi's point is that hosting and convening can make a network stronger by turning usefulness into a shared environment rather than a one-off interaction.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the book's hosting-and-convening chapter. If it goes generic, it becomes event-planning advice or vague community rhetoric. It has to show why building a room can strengthen a network more deeply than only attending other people's rooms.
