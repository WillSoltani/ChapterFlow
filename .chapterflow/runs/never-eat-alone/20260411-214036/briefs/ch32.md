Core Claim:
This chapter argues that neat balance myths often hide the real tradeoffs required by ambitious, relationally serious lives. Within the frozen support boundary, Ferrazzi's point is that priorities have to be integrated and negotiated rather than forced into a static formula of equal distribution.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the book's anti-balance-myth chapter. If it goes generic, it becomes productivity advice or vague work-life commentary. It has to show why relational and professional priorities require honest tradeoffs rather than clean symmetry.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author moves from humane growth into the practical strain of carrying ambition, relationships, and commitments at once.
Then the author shows that static balance formulas often fail because life is shaped by changing priorities and tradeoffs.
Then the author concludes that integration and honest negotiation are stronger than pretending everything can always be evenly balanced.
