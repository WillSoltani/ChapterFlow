Core Claim:
This chapter argues that a recognizable reputation becomes more useful when it is extended across more channels in a way that stays coherent, valuable, and non-noisy. Within the frozen support boundary, Ferrazzi's point is that broadcasting a brand should amplify real value rather than dissolve it into vanity or repetition.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
