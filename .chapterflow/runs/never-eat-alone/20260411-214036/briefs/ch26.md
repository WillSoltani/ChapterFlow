Core Claim:
This chapter argues that a recognizable reputation can be shaped around real value, strengths, and substance rather than left entirely to accident. Within the frozen support boundary, Ferrazzi's point is that a brand is strongest when it reflects consistent usefulness rather than cosmetic self-invention.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
