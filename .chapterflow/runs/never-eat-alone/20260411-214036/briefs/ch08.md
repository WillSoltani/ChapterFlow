Core Claim:
This chapter argues that remembering names and personal details is part of relationship-building rather than a superficial trick. Ferrazzi's point, within the frozen support boundary, is that memory, note-taking, and personal detail retention can function as proof of care when they are used to strengthen real recall and follow-through rather than to fake intimacy.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the book's memory chapter. If it goes soft, it becomes generic "remember names" advice. It has to show why recall matters strategically and relationally without becoming manipulative.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author moves from homework before contact to memory after contact.
Then the author shows that names and personal details help people feel recognized rather than interchangeable.
Then the author concludes that recall is one of the ways care becomes visible over time.
