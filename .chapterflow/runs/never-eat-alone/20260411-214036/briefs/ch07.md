Core Claim:
This chapter argues that preparation is not optional decoration around networking. Ferrazzi's point, as far as the frozen bundle supports, is that useful connection depends on doing enough research and homework that the interaction feels relevant, respectful, and informed. Preparation proves interest and reduces the risk of wasting the other person's attention.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the book's prep chapter. If it goes vague, it becomes generic "be prepared" advice. It has to connect homework to relationship quality, not just to personal professionalism.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author turns from anti-jerk warning to the need for better preparation.
Then the author shows that research and context make conversations more specific and less self-centered.
Then the author concludes that homework is part of respect because it helps a contact feel purposeful rather than lazy or extractive.
