## Strengths

- Official current-edition metadata is stable across Penguin Random House and Google Books.
- The official Chapter 1 excerpt provides lawful direct support for the opening chapter.
- The Google Books contents listing exposes a substantial portion of the chapter order and anchors the page sequence.
- Secondary chapter-map cross-checks line up with the visible authorized contents and fill only the truncated tail of the chapter list.

## Limits

- No full authorized text was available during the freeze window.
- Chapter-by-chapter factual support will be uneven. Early chapters have stronger official support than some later chapters.
- Exact quotes should be used sparingly and only where the official excerpt or another frozen authorized preview explicitly supports them.

## Writing posture required by this freeze

- Stay paraphrase-first.
- Prefer narrower claims over speculative expansion.
- Tie chapter claims to the frozen chapter map, official description, and chapter-local sidecars.
- Treat unsupported specifics as out of bounds even if they are tempting or broadly plausible.
