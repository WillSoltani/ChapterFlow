## Authorized source coverage

### Official publisher positioning

The current publisher materials frame the book as a relationship-building and career-success guide built around generosity, proactive outreach, and long-term connection maintenance. The expanded edition explicitly adds digital-age advice about social media, content, and online relationship-building.

### Authorized Chapter 1 support

The official Chapter 1 excerpt supports these chapter-1 facts and anchors:

- Ferrazzi opens from feeling socially and professionally outclassed at Harvard Business School.
- He ties that insecurity back to a working-class upbringing in southwestern Pennsylvania and to childhood experiences of exclusion around class markers.
- He presents caddying at a country club and his closeness to the Pohland family as formative experiences in learning generosity, reciprocity, and the practical power of helping others.
- He contrasts relationship-building with hyper-competitive individualism and argues that success comes through working with people rather than against them.
- He uses an early Deloitte consulting failure to show that relationships and help from others matter when pure technical performance is not enough.
