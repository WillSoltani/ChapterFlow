Readers will understand why learning well matters more now: the environment floods them with information, competes for attention, and rewards the people who can filter, retain, and adapt.

Opening Move:

- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 for the gap between access to information and usable understanding
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 for learning as adaptive leverage in current work and life
