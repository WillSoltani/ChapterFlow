Chapter Promise:
Readers will understand that motivation becomes more durable when learning is tied to a meaningful reason rather than to mood, pressure, or shallow performance alone.

Opening Move:
Open on the difference between doing a task because it matters and doing it only because it is scheduled or socially visible.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 for purpose as a stabilizer of motivation
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 for meaning beyond mood and raw discomfort
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 for purpose as preparation for the rest of the motivation section

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: expose the weakness of mood-based motivation
- P2: show how purpose changes the meaning of effort
- P3: distinguish purpose from shallow urgency or productivity theater
- P4: guard against treating purpose as magic
- P5: connect purpose to endurance and chosen direction
- P6: bridge into energy as the next motivational constraint
- P7 if needed: preserve nuance for unclear or evolving purpose

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter distinguishes purpose-driven effort from mood-driven or performance-theater effort.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Purpose should strengthen endurance without becoming slogan language or fake certainty.

Threshold Question:
How do you use purpose as a real source of endurance without turning it into a slogan?

Takeaway Count Lock:

- Hard: 5

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- purpose as stabilizer

Scenario Lesson Map:
- Scenario 1 (decision_point, school): whether to keep studying when the task matters beyond the grade
- Scenario 2 (postmortem, work): a project stalls because the purpose stayed vague
- Scenario 3 (dialogue, personal): challenge the difference between obligation and meaning
- Scenario 4 (predict_reveal, school): purpose increases endurance before enjoyment rises
- Scenario 5 (dilemma, work): chase visible productivity or reconnect to the real reason for the task
- Scenario 6 (before_after, personal): from pressure-only effort to purpose-linked effort
