Chapter Promise:
Readers will understand speed reading as cleaner, lower-friction reading rather than as reckless skimming.

Opening Move:
Open on the false choice between reading slowly forever and skimming carelessly.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 for faster reading as cleaner process rather than blind skimming
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 for better speed coming from reduced friction, fewer regressions, or steadier attention
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 for speed reading as the next practical step after memory method

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: expose the speed-versus-comprehension false choice
- P2: distinguish slow reading from friction-heavy reading habits
- P3: define speed gains as reduced regressions and cleaner pacing
- P4: show how attention and visual pacing can preserve comprehension while increasing efficiency
- P5: guard against blind-skimming hype or universal speed claims
- P6: bridge from memory into speed reading and forward into thinking
- P7 if needed: preserve nuance around text fit and reading goals

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter distinguishes faster intake built on cleaner reading behavior from careless skimming that sacrifices understanding.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must make reading speed feel trainable without turning comprehension into collateral damage.

Threshold Question:
How do you reduce reading friction and increase pace without flattening comprehension or pretending every text should be rushed?
