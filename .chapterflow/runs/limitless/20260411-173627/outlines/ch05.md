Chapter Promise:
Readers will understand that beliefs about intelligence, memory, and identity can function like hidden ceilings by shaping how they interpret difficulty and what effort they permit themselves to sustain.

Opening Move:
Open on a self-description that feels factual but has never been tested carefully.

- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 for belief as hidden ceiling
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 for self-description versus tested reality
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 for how beliefs govern effort, persistence, and method use

- P1: expose the apparent certainty of a limiting self-story
- P2: show how belief changes interpretation before ability is tested
- P3: separate repeated assumption from settled reality
- P4: guard against fake invincibility or blame logic
- P5: connect belief ceilings to learning behavior and method use
- P6: bridge into the specific lies of learning
