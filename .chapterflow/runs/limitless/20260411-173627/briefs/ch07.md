Core Claim:
Clearing false beliefs is not enough if the learner still lacks a reason worth sustaining. This chapter should argue that motivation becomes more durable when action is tied to purpose rather than to mood, pressure, or shallow productivity alone. Its job is to make purpose feel like a stabilizing force for effort without pretending that purpose replaces energy, method, or structure.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the purpose chapter. It should feel impossible to confuse with Chapter 6 because it moves from stripping away false rules to naming the deeper reason that can keep effort alive.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author shifts from myth-clearing to the question of what actually sustains action.
Then the author shows how purpose changes the meaning of effort and difficulty.
Then the author concludes that learning lasts longer when it serves a reason larger than momentary mood.

Counterarguments or Limits:
The chapter should not claim that purpose replaces energy, skill, or practical structure. The narrower claim is that purpose makes sustained effort more rational by giving difficulty a reason rather than leaving it as raw discomfort.

Previous Chapter Bridge: Chapter 6 cleared false rules that poison effort; this chapter asks what positive force can now sustain that effort.
Next Chapter Bridge: once purpose is named, the next question is whether the learner has the energy to carry that purpose consistently.
