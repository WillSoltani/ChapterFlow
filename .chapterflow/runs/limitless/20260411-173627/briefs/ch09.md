Core Claim:
Motivation becomes usable when action is made behaviorally easy to begin. This chapter should argue that learners stall not only because they lack purpose or energy, but because the opening cost of action is too high. Its job is to make small simple steps feel like a serious reduction-of-resistance strategy rather than a cute productivity slogan.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the action-threshold chapter. It should feel impossible to confuse with Chapter 8 because it moves from whether the learner has enough fuel to whether the first move has been made small enough to start with the fuel available.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author shifts from energy into the practical problem of beginning.
Then the author shows how oversized starting conditions create avoidable resistance.
Then the author concludes that shrinking the first move makes momentum more available without pretending that tiny steps solve everything.

Required Anchors:

- reducing resistance by shrinking the start

Specific Applications:

Common Misreadings:
- reducing the chapter to cute habit tips
- implying that tiny steps are enough without repetition or follow-through
- pretending that small starts remove the need for purpose, energy, or method

Counterarguments or Limits:
The chapter should not claim that small steps make hard work easy forever. The narrower claim is that smaller starts reduce friction at the point of initiation, which makes effort more likely to begin and easier to repeat.

Previous Chapter Bridge: Chapter 8 asked whether the learner had enough fuel to honor purpose; this chapter asks how to start action without demanding a dramatic burst of force.
Next Chapter Bridge: once action is easier to begin, the next question is how to deepen engagement into flow.

Cross-Chapter Tensions:
- serious progress versus tiny-entry framing
- starting easier versus trivializing the work

Moral Complexity:
Low to medium. The chapter should avoid insulting stalled learners by treating every failure to start as laziness.

Hard-Depth Minimum:
Preserve the tension that small simple steps are powerful because they reduce initiation cost, not because the work is secretly small. Hard depth must leave alive the question: how do you respect tiny starts without turning ambition into permanent avoidance of difficulty?
