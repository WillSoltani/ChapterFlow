Core Claim:
Purpose is not enough if the learner is chronically under-fueled. This chapter should argue that cognitive performance and sustained effort depend on energy, not inspiration or willpower theater alone. Its job is to make energy feel like a practical learning constraint without drifting into unsupported health specificity or pretending that energy is the whole answer.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the energy chapter. It should feel impossible to confuse with Chapter 7 because it moves from why effort matters to whether the learner has the fuel to keep honoring that why.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author shifts from purpose to the practical fuel required to carry purpose.
Then the author shows how low energy distorts attention, patience, and follow-through.
Then the author concludes that sustainable learning requires respecting energy as a real constraint rather than moralizing exhaustion.

Required Anchors:
- energy as fuel for motivation

- energy as preparation for practical action in the rest of the motivation section

Counterarguments or Limits:
The chapter should not claim that energy replaces purpose, method, or structure. The narrower claim is that energy sets practical limits on how much effort and attention can be sustained, so ignoring it makes good intentions less usable.

Previous Chapter Bridge: Chapter 7 named the reason for effort; this chapter asks whether the learner has the energy to keep paying for that effort.
Next Chapter Bridge: once fuel is respected, the next question is how to reduce resistance by turning effort into smaller simple steps.
