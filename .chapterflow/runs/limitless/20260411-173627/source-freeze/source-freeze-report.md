# Source Freeze Report

- `book-source.md`: frozen source bundle summary with metadata, chapter map, and source notes

## Authority order for chapter work

1. Locked 2020 Hay House base edition chapter order and metadata
2. Frozen preview bundle and heading index
3. Secondary chapter summaries only where preview support is thin

## Guardrails applied

- No pirate mirrors or unauthorized reposts
- No use of expanded-edition-only chapters
- No exact quotation unless directly supported by the frozen preview
- No chapter invention beyond frozen sources

## Risk notes

- Preview support is strongest for chapter order, headings, and book-level framing, and thinner for paragraph-level prose details.
- Later chapter briefs should prefer mechanism and practical scope over aggressive anecdotal detail unless corroborated in the frozen bundle.
- Chapter 1 and Chapter 2 can still be written with strong specificity because the memoir setup and the modern-learning-pressure framing are broadly supported in the frozen bundle.
