        Core claim:
        - The leader's first job in uncertainty is to stay psychologically functional enough to create clarity and momentum for others without pretending certainty they do not possess.

        Distinct mechanism:
        - Horowitz combines CEO psychology, communication, and directional leadership into one principle: the company takes cues from how the leader handles ambiguity, not from whether ambiguity disappears.

        Required anchors:
        - The most difficult CEO skill is psychological, because the role offers little training and constant pressure.
- Leadership under uncertainty requires directional clarity without fake omniscience.
- Followers read the leader's steadiness, communication, and standards when the route is still unresolved.

        Common misreadings:
        - Treating the chapter as license for bluffing certainty instead of leading honestly under ambiguity.
- Reducing psychological management to positive thinking rather than disciplined emotional control.

        Specific applications:
        - Use self-management to keep ambiguity from turning into organizational panic.
- Communicate what is known, unknown, and next without posturing.
- Lead with direction and standards even when the route is still unfolding.

        Hard-edge requirement:
        - Hard mode must show how a leader's unmanaged fear or confusion spreads through the company faster than any official message.

        Threshold question:
        - What must a leader make clear when they cannot yet make everything certain?
