# Quiz Blueprint - ch06

## Blueprint Goal
- Test mechanism and application of the chapter principle, not quote recall.
- Reinforce the operating order: people -> product -> profit.
- Stress-test misreadings around anti-profit framing and perk-only people care.

## Core Knowledge Checks (Multiple Choice)

1. Mechanism Priority Check
- Stem: A founder says profit pressure means training can wait until next year. Which chapter-consistent response is strongest?
- Correct concept: Delaying people-system development creates management debt that later damages product and profit.
- Distractor pattern to include: "Training is useful only after scale is stable."
- Explanation target: Profit durability depends on sequencing, not on skipping people investment.

2. Culture Definition Check
- Stem: Which culture choice fits "a good place to work" in this chapter?
- Correct concept: Culture supports performance and accountability, not comfort-only perks.
- Distractor pattern to include: "High morale perks are sufficient if output is currently fine."
- Explanation target: Care without standards becomes execution drift.

3. Management Debt Recognition Check
- Stem: Which early signal most strongly indicates management debt is forming?
- Correct concept: Growing role ambiguity, inconsistent management quality, and repeated coordination failures.
- Distractor pattern to include: "Short-term revenue is still up, so no debt risk."
- Explanation target: Debt appears in people-system strain before financial decline is obvious.

4. Hiring/Training Relationship Check
- Stem: A startup hired strong executives. Which claim aligns with the chapter?
- Correct concept: Executive hiring does not remove responsibility to build internal management capability.
- Distractor pattern to include: "Senior hires fully replace manager development."
- Explanation target: Leadership system quality requires ongoing development, not one-time hiring.

5. Misreading Guard Check
- Stem: Which statement is a misreading of the chapter principle?
- Correct concept: "People first" is not anti-profit; it defines causal order for durable profit.
- Distractor pattern to include: "Profit matters, but it is downstream of people and product quality."
- Explanation target: The chapter argues sequencing discipline, not financial indifference.

6. Threshold Question Application Check
- Stem: A company doubles headcount quickly with no management-system updates. What breaks first?
- Correct concept: People-system consistency breaks first, then product reliability and financial durability weaken.
- Distractor pattern to include: "Profit is always first to fail before team/process issues appear."
- Explanation target: Failure sequence follows the neglected layer.

## Scenario-Based Transfer Items (Short Answer)

1. Work Transfer
- Prompt: In a time-pressured team setting, identify one decision that protects people-system quality before short-term output optics.
- Expected evidence: Names a specific tradeoff, consequence, and link to product/profit durability.

2. School Transfer
- Prompt: In a confused school team, propose one intervention that converts surface alignment into real ownership.
- Expected evidence: Role clarity, management behavior, and accountability mechanism.

3. Personal Transfer
- Prompt: Describe one personal avoidance loop that resembles management debt and how to break it.
- Expected evidence: Delayed discipline pattern, compounding cost, concrete corrective action.

## Structured Recap Prompts
- Retrieve: What does "people, products, profits in that order" prevent that a profit-first shortcut misses?
- Connect: How do training and management quality assurance influence product outcomes before they appear in financial metrics?
- Preview: As systems mature, what new scaling problems become central in the next chapter's going-concern focus?

## Medium Activation Prompt
- Prompt: Identify one current team habit that improves short-term speed but likely creates management debt. Replace it with one higher-discipline practice you can start this week.
- Check: Must require immediate action, not chapter summary.

## Medium Self-Check Prompt
- Prompt: Where are you currently mistaking comfort signals for performance-culture signals?
- Check: Must remain a question.

## Hard Self-Check Prompts (Two Tone Slots)
- Slot A (direct pressure): Which management discipline are you delaying because the team still feels "small," and what specific cost will appear first if you keep delaying?
- Slot B (competitive pressure): If a stronger operator took over your team tomorrow, which people-system gap would they fix first to protect product and profit?

## Hard Prediction Prompt
- Prompt: If leadership keeps postponing manager development for two quarters while headcount grows, predict the next operational failure and its downstream effect on product and profit.
- Check: Must ask for anticipated move/consequence, not vague teaser.

## Review Card Set (Applied Recall)

1. Card: Principle-to-Mechanism
- Front: Why is profit third in the chapter order?
- Back target: Because people-system and product-system quality causally support durable profit.

2. Card: Misreading Correction
- Front: What is wrong with equating "people care" with perks?
- Back target: It ignores training, management quality, role clarity, and accountability.

3. Card: Debt Detection
- Front: Name one early indicator of management debt.
- Back target: Role confusion, inconsistent manager behavior, or repeated coordination failures.

4. Card: Leadership Obligation
- Front: Why doesn't executive hiring end management development work?
- Back target: Internal management quality must be built and maintained continuously.
