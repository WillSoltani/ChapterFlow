# Chapter 8 Quiz Blueprint

## Blueprint Intent
Test applied understanding of uncertainty leadership mechanics, not quote recall.

## Coverage Targets
- Core claim coverage: leader psychological function drives team clarity and momentum.
- Mechanism coverage: emotional control, communication structure, directional standards.
- Boundary coverage: confidence versus denial.
- Misreading coverage: no bluffing certainty, no shallow positivity framing.

## Question Set Plan
1. Mechanism Check (MCQ)
- Prompt shape: choose the response that preserves momentum without fake certainty.
- Correct-answer signal: names known/unknown/next action and maintains standards.
- Distractor pattern: confident-sounding but evasive or purely motivational language.

2. Application Check (Scenario MCQ)
- Prompt shape: deadline pressure with incomplete data.
- Correct-answer signal: disciplined next step plus transparent uncertainty boundary.
- Distractor pattern: delay until certainty or overpromise timeline.

3. Signal Reading Check (Multi-select)
- Prompt shape: identify behaviors teams read under ambiguity.
- Correct-answer signal: steadiness, communication cadence, standards follow-through.
- Distractor pattern: charisma-only or optimism-only cues.

4. Failure Mode Check (MCQ)
- Prompt shape: leader anxiety leak and resulting team behavior.
- Correct-answer signal: unmanaged fear spreads faster than official message.
- Distractor pattern: assumes formal memo neutralizes emotional contagion.

5. Misreading Guard Check (MCQ)
- Prompt shape: pick statement consistent with chapter boundary.
- Correct-answer signal: confidence with explicit unknowns.
- Distractor pattern: bluff certainty to preserve authority.

6. Transfer Check (Short answer)
- Prompt shape: apply principle to a new domain with partial information.
- Expected elements: emotional control move, communication frame, next operational step.

## Scenario-Linked Items
1. Scenario 1 (postmortem/school)
- Ask which earlier leadership decision most affected newsroom stability.
- Explanation must show surprising implication of unclear versus clear direction.

2. Scenario 3 (predict_reveal/work)
- Ask reader to predict consequence of one avoidance move, then evaluate reveal.
- Explanation must name the common trap and why it feels attractive.

3. Scenario 4 (dilemma/school)
- Ask which option prevents ambiguity from degrading standards.
- Explanation must reframe dilemma from comfort-seeking to responsibility under uncertainty.

4. Scenario 6 (decision_point/work)
- Ask which decision creates repeatable trust under uncertainty.
- Explanation must link to self-directed leadership question.

## Explanation Rules (All Scored Items)
- Explain why the correct option matches chapter mechanism.
- Explain why each distractor fails operationally under uncertainty.
- Keep explanations principle-bound, no trivia or source-splice language.

## Medium Prompt Blueprints
1. Activation Prompt A
- Ask for one live uncertainty the reader is currently leading through.
- Require immediate action statement for next 24 hours.

2. Activation Prompt B
- Ask reader to rewrite one unclear team message into known/unknown/next format.

3. Self-Check Prompt A
- Question form only: What unknown are you hiding to appear in control?

4. Self-Check Prompt B
- Question form only: Which standard will your team assume is optional this week unless you restate it?

## Hard Prompt Blueprints
1. Hard Self-Check Tone Slot: Direct
- Prompt: Where does your current confidence claim exceed your evidence, and what is the cost if your team copies that behavior?

2. Hard Self-Check Tone Slot: Competitive
- Prompt: If your fear leaked into every decision this week, which execution metric would break first?

3. Hard Prediction Prompt A
- Prompt: If you delay communication until certainty, what is the most likely team-level consequence by the next decision cycle?

4. Hard Prediction Prompt B
- Prompt: If you admit uncertainty but set no standards, what behavior pattern appears next?

## Structured Recap Blueprint
1. Retrieve
- Prompt: Name the three-part communication frame for leading under uncertainty.

2. Connect
- Prompt: How does leader emotional control change decision quality across the team?

3. Preview
- Prompt: When no fixed rule resolves a tradeoff, what judgment habit must lead next?

## Review Card Blueprint
1. Applied Recall Card
- Front: You know only 60 percent of the path and team anxiety is rising. What do you communicate now?
- Back rubric: known/unknown/next action plus explicit standard.

2. Transfer Card
- Front: In a non-startup setting, how do you show confidence without denial?
- Back rubric: boundary statement, direction signal, accountability signal.
