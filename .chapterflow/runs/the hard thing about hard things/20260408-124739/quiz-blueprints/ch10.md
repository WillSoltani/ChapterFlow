# Chapter 10 Quiz Blueprint

## Blueprint Intent
Test applied understanding of transition, closure, and durable lessons, not plot recall.

## Coverage Targets
- Core claim coverage: the exit reframes struggle instead of erasing it.
- Mechanism coverage: reflection turns lived pressure into durable operating principles.
- Boundary coverage: closure versus sentimentality, success versus resolution.
- Misreading coverage: no triumph-only reading, no afterthought reading.

## Question Set Plan
1. Transition Check (MCQ)
- Prompt shape: choose the interpretation that treats the exit as a reframing event.
- Correct-answer signal: acknowledges retained scars and lessons.
- Distractor pattern: treats success as total erasure of the struggle.

2. Reflection Check (Scenario MCQ)
- Prompt shape: founder-panel or handoff scenario where someone wants a neat victory narrative.
- Correct-answer signal: separates outcome from the lessons the hard years produced.
- Distractor pattern: ceremonial closure language without any operating lesson.

3. Durable Lesson Check (Multi-select)
- Prompt shape: identify which lessons survive beyond the immediate survival phase.
- Correct-answer signal: names principles that travel across contexts and timelines.
- Distractor pattern: picks only emotionally satisfying or story-shaped claims.

4. Identity Shift Check (MCQ)
- Prompt shape: leader feels relieved but also dislocated after the exit.
- Correct-answer signal: recognizes unresolved identity and responsibility shifts.
- Distractor pattern: assumes the transition automatically resolves the role change.

5. Misreading Guard Check (MCQ)
- Prompt shape: which statement avoids reducing the ending to an afterthought.
- Correct-answer signal: treats Chapter 10 as the consolidation point for the book's logic.
- Distractor pattern: frames the chapter as optional epilogue material.

6. Transfer Check (Short answer)
- Prompt shape: apply the chapter to a non-startup ending, handoff, or completion.
- Expected elements: what remains true, what costs remain, what lesson travels forward.

## Scenario-Linked Items
1. Scenario 1 (predict_reveal/work)
- Ask what the leader should realize about the exit before the reveal of its lasting meaning.
- Explanation must show how the broader principle emerges from transition.

2. Scenario 2 (dilemma/school)
- Ask which response best handles a founder-panel conversation about closure and next steps.
- Explanation must distinguish real closure from procedural wrapping up.

3. Scenario 4 (decision_point/work)
- Ask which decision keeps the leader honest about unresolved tradeoffs after success.
- Explanation must name the cross-domain transfer from crisis to reflection.

4. Scenario 6 (dialogue/personal)
- Ask how one conversation can reframe an ending without turning it into self-congratulation.
- Explanation must show the perspective shift rather than triumph language.

## Explanation Rules (All Scored Items)
- Explain why the correct option matches Chapter 10's reframing mechanism.
- Explain why each distractor fails operationally or morally.
- Keep explanations principle-bound, no trivia or source-splice language.

## Medium Prompt Blueprints
1. Activation Prompt A
- Ask for one real ending, handoff, or transition the reader is facing.
- Require a sentence naming what lesson survives after the ending.

2. Activation Prompt B
- Ask reader to separate what the outcome changed from what the hard years still mean.

3. Self-Check Prompt A
- Question form only: What do you keep calling closure that is really just the end of one visible struggle?

4. Self-Check Prompt B
- Question form only: Which lesson from your hard season still applies even though the original crisis is over?

## Hard Prompt Blueprints
1. Hard Self-Check Tone Slot: Direct
- Prompt: Which unresolved cost are you trying to hide by calling the transition a win?

2. Hard Self-Check Tone Slot: Competitive
- Prompt: What part of the story still has leverage over your identity after the exit is complete?

3. Hard Prediction Prompt A
- Prompt: If you treat the ending as total resolution, what lesson will you fail to carry into the next role?

4. Hard Prediction Prompt B
- Prompt: If you rush to celebrate without reflection, what tradeoff will you miss?

## Structured Recap Blueprint
1. Retrieve
- Prompt: Name the chapter's move from exit event to durable lesson.

2. Connect
- Prompt: How does transition force the leader to reinterpret earlier struggle?

3. Preview
- Prompt: What does this ending teach about carrying lessons forward after the original crisis is gone?

## Review Card Blueprint
1. Applied Recall Card
- Front: The company is sold and people want a simple victory story. What does Chapter 10 ask you to preserve?
- Back rubric: retained scars, durable lessons, and transition as reflection.

2. Transfer Card
- Front: In a non-startup ending, how do you close without erasing what the hard season taught?
- Back rubric: outcome versus lesson distinction, unresolved cost acknowledgment, forward-facing principle.
