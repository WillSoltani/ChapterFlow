# Chapter 5 Quiz Blueprint Lock

## Quiz Purpose
- Test mechanism and application of Chapter 5 crisis doctrine.
- Enforce distinction between disciplined truth/responsibility and macho or brutal misreadings.
- Ensure readers can choose actions under conditions where every option harms someone.

## Competency Targets
- Diagnose collapse conditions without denial language.
- Apply tell-it-like-it-is communication under fear.
- Choose layoff and executive decisions with clarity, speed, and respect.
- Detect the cost of delay framed as kindness.
- Predict consequences of truth-first leadership over time.

## Item Set Blueprint

### Easy Tier (grade 8-9)
- Target length envelope for later writer: 140-175 words per tone context.
- Item types:
| ID | Type | Cognitive Role | Blueprint Prompt |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | multiple choice | retrieve | Which action best matches "tell it like it is" during crisis? |
| E2 | multiple choice | retrieve | Which choice shows urgency without panic? |
| E3 | multiple choice | connect | Which delay pattern causes extra harm in layoffs? |
| E4 | short answer | connect | Why is polished reassurance dangerous when fear is high? |
| E5 | scenario pick | application | Choose the better first move in an executive role-fit failure. |
| E6 | scenario pick | application | Identify the response that turns struggle into action. |

### Medium Tier (grade 10-11)
- Target length envelope for later writer: 330-420 words per tone context.
- Required prompt classes:
| ID | Type | Requirement Lock | Blueprint Prompt |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M1 | activation | immediate use | What hard truth do you need to communicate in the next 24 hours, and to whom? |
| M2 | self-check | question form only | Where are you delaying a painful people decision, and what damage is accumulating? |
| M3 | mechanism explain | why correct answer fits principle | Explain why direct truth improves execution alignment in fear conditions. |
| M4 | case analysis | application | Given a role-fit miss, choose fire, demote, or support plan and justify with chapter mechanism. |
| M5 | error diagnosis | misreading guard | Distinguish disciplined urgency from panic behavior in a short case. |
| M6 | transfer | cross-domain | Apply the chapter rule set to a non-company team breakdown. |

### Hard Tier (grade 12)
- Target length envelope for later writer: 490-600 words per tone context.
- Boundary condition lock: every hard case must include a contradiction, downside, or moral cost.
- Required prompt classes:
| ID | Type | Requirement Lock | Blueprint Prompt |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | self-check (gentle slot) | distinct tone object | What truth are you softening to protect relationships, and what is the operational cost? |
| H2 | self-check (competitive slot) | distinct tone object | What hard call are you avoiding because you want to look humane, and what will that avoidance cost next month? |
| H3 | prediction | anticipate next move or consequence | If a leader communicates truth but delays role decisions, what consequence appears next and why? |
| H4 | dilemma resolution | threshold question | Choose between two harmful options and defend obligations that remain. |
| H5 | postmortem rebuild | failure mode | Reconstruct a collapse sequence caused by staggered half-decisions. |
| H6 | doctrine synthesis | transfer | Build a step sequence for a new crisis case using the chapter operating loop. |

## Answer-Key and Explanation Rules
- Every keyed answer must include a "why this fits Chapter 5 mechanism" explanation.
- Explanations must reference truth, people decisions, and consequence timing where relevant.
- Wrong-option rationales should expose common misreadings: macho framing, cruelty framing, delay-as-kindness framing.

## Structured Recap Blueprint
- `retrieve`: recall the three required anchors from memory in applied wording.
- `connect`: link one anchor to a specific mechanism and tradeoff in a case.
- `preview`: ask what changes when moving from collapse management to people-product-profit ordering in the next chapter.
