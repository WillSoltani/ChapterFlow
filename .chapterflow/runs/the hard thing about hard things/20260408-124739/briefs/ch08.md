## Principle Lock
- Core claim: The leader's first job in uncertainty is to stay psychologically functional enough to create clarity and momentum for others without pretending certainty they do not possess.
- Distinct mechanism: CEO psychology, communication, and directional leadership operate as one system; the company responds to leader behavior under ambiguity more than to perfect forecasts.
- Threshold question: What must a leader make clear when they cannot yet make everything certain?
- Limit boundary: Confidence is required; denial is forbidden.
- Hard-edge tension: Unmanaged fear or confusion in leadership spreads through the company faster than official messaging.

## Required Anchors
1. The hardest CEO skill is psychological because pressure is constant and training is limited.
2. Leadership under uncertainty requires directional clarity without fake omniscience.
3. Teams read steadiness, communication quality, and standards while the route remains unresolved.

## Application Targets
- Keep personal emotional control so ambiguity does not become organizational panic.
- Communicate three lanes clearly: known, unknown, and next action.
- Set direction and standards before full certainty exists.

## Misreading Guards
- Reject bluffing certainty as a leadership tactic.
- Reject reducing psychological control to generic positivity.
- Reject chapter-generic startup advice detached from uncertainty mechanics.
