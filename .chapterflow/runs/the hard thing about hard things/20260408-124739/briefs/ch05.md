# Chapter 5 Brief Lock

## Chapter Identity
- Chapter: `ch05`
- Title: `When Things Fall Apart`
- Core claim: when the company is breaking, the CEO earns the role by facing ugly reality directly and making painful people decisions without false comfort or performative softness.
- Distinct mechanism: one crisis doctrine across situations, tell it like it is, execute layoffs cleanly, fire or demote decisively when needed, and stop expecting rescue.
- Quote posture: paraphrase first.

## Anchor Lock
- The struggle: the private psychological burden of knowing the company can fail on your watch.
- Tell it like it is: truthful communication matters more in fear than polished reassurance.
- Layoffs and executive decisions: hard people calls must be made clearly, quickly, and without self-protective delay.

## Moral Tension Lock
- Threshold question: what obligations remain when every available option harms someone and the leader still has to choose?
- Hard-edge requirement: show why delaying painful decisions to feel kinder often increases damage for everyone involved.
- Limit condition: keep honesty distinct from brutality and urgency distinct from panic.

## Misreading Guards
- Reject macho-suffering framing. Keep focus on disciplined truth and responsibility.
- Reject cruelty framing. Tough calls are for company survival and role clarity, not punishment.
- Keep truth-telling operational: communication must reduce confusion and increase execution clarity.

## Continuity and Positioning
- Previous chapter bridge: `This Time with Feeling` established compounded pressure; this chapter turns pressure into a concrete crisis operating playbook.
- Next chapter bridge: `Take Care of the People, the Products, and the Profits, In That Order` shifts from collapse management to durable company building.
- Distinction in book: this is the most operational CEO crisis chapter and must read as a decision manual under failure pressure.
