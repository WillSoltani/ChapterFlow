# Chapter Brief: ch04 "This Time with Feeling"

## Packet Purpose
Build Chapter 4 around one pressure pattern: leadership suffering repeats, and prior pain does not reduce the need for the next clean decision.

## Source Lock
- Edition anchor: 2014 first edition, HarperBusiness, ISBN 9780062273208.
- Facts stay inside the frozen source bundle and chapter sidecar.
- Quote posture: paraphrase-first.
- No added chronology, no extra company details, no external examples.

## Core Principle
- Core claim: A leader cannot spend prior suffering as a coupon against future hard decisions.
- Distinct mechanism: Repeated operational and financing shocks test whether the leader can keep deciding under recurring strain.
- Threshold question: How do you keep making clean decisions when the cost of the last round is still in your system?

## Required Anchors (Locked)
1. Turnaround pressure continues after the first survival phase rather than ending cleanly.
2. Strategic shifts carry emotional cost because the leader knows how painful the next round may be.
3. Repetition itself is one of the hard things, not just the content of one crisis.

## Hard Edge And Moral Limit (Locked)
- Hard edge requirement: Show how repeated pressure distorts judgment when numbness is mistaken for resilience.
- Moral complexity limit: Preserve fatigue and burden without framing leaders as helpless victims.

## Common Misreadings To Block
- "This is just another crisis recap."
- "Experience makes later hard calls emotionally easy."

- Bridge to ch05 ("When Things Fall Apart"): prepare operational crisis doctrine by first clarifying cumulative psychological and decision pressure.
