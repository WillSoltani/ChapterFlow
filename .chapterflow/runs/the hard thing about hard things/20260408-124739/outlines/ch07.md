# Chapter Outline: ch07 - Concerning the Going Concern

## Outline Purpose
- Lock section flow, anchor placement, and scenario insertion before prose drafting.
- Keep all claims tied to Chapter 7 frozen facts only.

## Section 1 - Scaling Breakpoint (Opening Pressure)
- Objective: open on the leadership pressure created when informal coordination no longer works.
- Must establish: growth changes which management rules are viable.
- Must frame: failure to design the organization is an active decision with consequences.
- Transition cue: move from symptom level (confusion, drift, politics) to design level (systems, roles, cadence).

## Section 2 - Politics Versus Productive Process
- Objective: separate "bad bureaucracy" from "intentional process that lowers political behavior."
- Anchor: growth makes informal signals and incentives more dangerous.
- Required contrast: preserving old informality can increase hidden politics.
- Limitation note: process must serve mission, not administrative vanity.

## Section 3 - Executive Fit Is Stage-Specific
- Objective: define executive quality as contextual to company stage and immediate need.
- Anchor: no universally great executive independent of stage.
- Application lock: evaluate role fit against current constraints, not brand reputation.
- Misreading guard: do not frame this as anti-executive or anti-experience.

## Section 4 - Programming Culture And Cadence
- Objective: show that culture and operating rhythm require explicit design as scale rises.
- Anchor: founder sincerity is insufficient without repeated operating mechanisms.
- Required mechanism framing: cadence, expectations, and accountability loops reduce ambiguity.
- Hard-edge cue: no-program culture defaults to inconsistency across teams.

## Section 5 - Integrated Decision Standard
- Objective: provide one integrated check for Chapter 7 decisions.
- Decision standard:
- What has outgrown informal coordination?
- Which process reduces ambiguity without adding vanity overhead?
- Which executive profiles match current stage constraints?
- Which cultural behaviors need explicit reinforcement through cadence?
- Threshold question insertion: which early-stage habit is now dangerous?

## Section 7 - Bridge Forward
- Backward bridge: connect to Chapter 6 priority order (people, product, profit) as prerequisite context.
- Forward bridge: handoff to Chapter 8 uncertainty leadership by showing that credible leadership under uncertainty requires designed operating systems.
