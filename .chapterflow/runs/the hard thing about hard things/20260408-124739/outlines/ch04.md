# Chapter Outline: ch04 "This Time with Feeling"

## Outline Intent
Show that leadership pressure compounds. The chapter must demonstrate repeated decision burden, not a one-time heroic recovery.

## Section 1: Re-entry Into Pressure
- Objective: Establish that the first survival phase did not end the hard part.
- Must-carry point: New hard decisions arrive before emotional recovery is complete.
- Mechanism to foreground: emotional residue reduces decision margin while stakes remain high.
- Guardrail: Do not present this as a clean "after crisis" chapter.

## Section 2: Repetition As The Hard Thing
- Objective: Reframe repetition itself as a central leadership burden.
- Must-carry point: The same leader must act again with less emotional reserve.
- Mechanism to foreground: cumulative stress changes how tradeoffs are evaluated.
- Guardrail: Avoid generic resilience framing that treats endurance as identity performance.

## Section 3: Clean Decisions Under Recurring Strain
- Objective: Define what "clean decision" means in this chapter.
- Must-carry point: Clarity requires method and accountability, not adrenaline carryover from prior wins.
- Mechanism to foreground: distinguish signal from fatigue-driven urgency.
- Guardrail: No abstractions detached from concrete pressure and consequence.

## Section 4: Distortion Risk
- Objective: Surface the hard-edge contradiction.
- Must-carry point: Numbness can look like toughness while actually degrading judgment.
- Mechanism to foreground: repeated pressure can cause underreaction, overreaction, or emotional detachment from consequence.
- Guardrail: Preserve fatigue without portraying the leader as powerless.

## Section 5: Forward Bridge To Chapter 5
- Objective: Hand off to operational crisis practice.
- Must-carry point: Before learning specific crisis plays, the reader must grasp cumulative burden and decision hygiene.
- Mechanism to foreground: repeated hard calls require repeatable operating discipline.
- Guardrail: End with unresolved pressure that naturally points to "When Things Fall Apart."

## Anchor Lock Checklist
1. Turnaround pressure persists after initial survival.
2. Strategic shifts carry emotional cost because prior pain is remembered.
3. Repetition is itself a core hard thing.
4. Threshold question is answered through decision hygiene, not inspiration.
5. Hard mode shows numbness-versus-resilience confusion as a real failure mode.
