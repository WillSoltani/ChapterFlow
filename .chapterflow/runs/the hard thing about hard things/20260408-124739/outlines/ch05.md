# Chapter 5 Outline Lock

## Scope
- Chapter: `When Things Fall Apart`
- Objective: provide an operational crisis doctrine for CEOs under collapse pressure.
- Source boundary: frozen chapter bundle only.

## Section Sequence

1. **Pressure Opening: CEO Job Under Collapse**
- Function: open on immediate pressure, not abstract thesis.
- Must establish: company can fail on the leader's watch; responsibility cannot be delegated.
- Constraint: no heroic posturing.

2. **The Struggle as Leadership Burden**
- Function: define the struggle as private load that must convert to action.
- Must establish: suffering alone has no value unless translated into decisions and execution.
- Transition cue: internal burden becomes external communication.

3. **Tell It Like It Is**
- Function: codify truthful communication during fear conditions.
- Must establish: fear amplifies rumor; polished reassurance erodes trust.
- Include mechanism: truth aligns team interpretation, then action.
- Constraint: honesty is not permission for cruelty.

4. **Layoffs: Clarity, Speed, Respect**
- Function: operationalize painful workforce decisions.
- Must establish: delay in the name of kindness usually increases total harm.
- Include mechanism: clear rationale, decisive timing, dignity in execution.
- Failure mode to name: serial half-measures that prolong uncertainty.

5. **Executive Decisions: Fire or Demote When Needed**
- Function: address role-fit breakdowns under crisis.
- Must establish: protecting executive comfort can cost company survival.
- Include mechanism: role requirements, decision clarity, no self-protective drift.

6. **No Rescue Doctrine**
- Function: reject fantasy that outside forces will save the company.
- Must establish: responsibility sits with current leadership choices.
- Link back to anchor: truth, people calls, and execution discipline are the only path.

7. **Integrated Crisis Operating Loop**
- Function: synthesize the chapter into repeatable sequence.
- Sequence lock: face facts -> communicate truth -> decide people changes -> execute cleanly -> reassess reality.
- Constraint: urgency without panic.

8. **Bridge Forward**
- Function: prepare move from survival logic to company-building logic in next chapter.
- Bridge line intent: after truth and hard calls stabilize reality, leadership can re-center on people, product, and profit order.
