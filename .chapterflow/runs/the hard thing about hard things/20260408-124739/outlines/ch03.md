# Chapter 03 Outline: "I Will Survive"

## Outline Goal
Provide a prose-ready structure for Chapter 3 that is source-bound, scenario-locked, and explicit about survival mechanism.

## Section Flow

### 1) Pressure Setup: When Growth Logic Breaks
- Establish crash conditions: external market shift makes prior growth and funding assumptions unreliable.
- Name the operating change: this is no longer a scaling chapter; it is a survival chapter.
- Open on conflict/decision pressure, not thesis narration.

### 2) Redefine The Job: Survival As Primary Metric
- Convert success definition from expansion to continuation.
- Clarify that "keep going" means active management decisions, not emotional performance.
- Mark core danger: time and cash constraints reduce optionality each cycle.

### 3) Mechanism Block A: Financing Under Contraction
- Show financing decisions as repeated, constrained, and often unattractive.
- Keep focus on tradeoffs: dilution/risk/control vs runway extension.
- Tie each financing move to one rule: buy time for another disciplined decision cycle.

### 4) Mechanism Block B: Communication Under Fear
- Show truth-telling as operational necessity, not culture branding.
- Distinguish helpful confidence from misleading reassurance.
- Make explicit that communication quality affects execution accuracy during crisis.

### 5) Mechanism Block C: Endurance With Judgment
- Separate useful persistence from stubborn denial.
- Show repeated decision fatigue as the hidden leadership tax.
- Preserve hard edge: leader chooses without certainty and is judged by outcomes anyway.

### 6) Integration: Survival Is A Sequence, Not A Save
- Synthesize three mechanism blocks into a recurring loop:
  - face current reality
  - make best constrained call
  - communicate and execute
  - reassess under new pressure
- Reject heroic single-moment framing.

### 7) Bridge
- Backward bridge (ch02): biography gave credibility; this chapter tests it under collapse.
- Forward bridge (ch04): pressure does not reset after one survival cycle; emotional margin narrows.

## Tone-Object Requirements For Later Drafting
- gentle: lower resistance, keep pressure visible, avoid therapeutic language.
- direct: state mechanism and consequence plainly.
- competitive: sharpen stakes without swagger; pressure comes from tradeoffs, not bravado.
- Hard mode across all tones must include a contradiction, boundary, or downside.
