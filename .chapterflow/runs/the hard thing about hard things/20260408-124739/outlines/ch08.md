# Chapter 8 Outline

## Outline Objective
Lock chapter flow so writer can draft without inventing facts or structure.

## Section Flow
1. Pressure Open
- Start inside uncertainty pressure, not abstract leadership theory.
- Show immediate consequence risk if leader projects fear or confusion.
- Frame the chapter question: what must be made clear before full certainty exists?

2. Why This Skill Is Hard
- Establish that psychological discipline is the CEO's hardest recurring skill.
- Explain role conditions: high pressure, weak training signal, constant social amplification.
- Tie mechanism to organizational effect, not private emotion only.

3. Direction Without Fake Certainty
- Define the leadership communication contract:
- What is known.
- What is not known.
- What happens next.
- Make clear that credibility comes from honest boundaries plus forward movement.

4. Signal Leadership Under Ambiguity
- Clarify that teams watch leader steadiness, communication cadence, and standards enforcement.
- Show how mixed signals create rumor loops and decision drag.
- Keep confidence separate from denial.

5. Scenario Block Sequence (Locked)
- Scenario 1: postmortem / school / surprising_implication
- Scenario 2: dialogue / personal / cross_domain
- Scenario 3: predict_reveal / work / common_trap
- Scenario 4: dilemma / school / perspective_reframe
- Scenario 5: before_after / personal / broader_principle
- Scenario 6: decision_point / work / self_directed_question

6. Synthesis And Transition
- Re-state mechanism as operating rule: leader psychology sets organizational temperature.
- Reconfirm boundary: no bluffing certainty, no emotional spillover.
- Bridge forward to Chapter 9 paradox logic where rulebooks fail.

## Tone Control Notes For Later Drafters
- Gentle: reduce resistance while preserving accountability and consequence.
- Direct: emphasize mechanism, sequence, and operational tradeoff.
- Competitive: heighten stakes and execution pressure without bravado.

## Hard-Mode Injection Points
- Section 1: show fear contagion before any formal announcement.
- Section 3: force explicit admission of unknowns while still setting a next step.
- Section 4: demonstrate contradiction between confident tone and weak standards.
- Scenario 4 or 6: include a downside that remains even after a better decision.
