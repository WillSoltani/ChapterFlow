Chapter Promise:
Show that this book is written for the struggle: moments when the leader is under pressure, clean advice breaks down, and judgment still has to continue.

Opening Move:
Start with the contrast between polished management advice and the reality that the hardest parts of the job arrive without a recipe.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1, P2
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P2, P3
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P3, P4

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: Open on the gap between elegant leadership advice and ugly company reality.
- P2: Define the struggle and make the CEO's burden concrete.
- P3: Explain why the book chooses lived operator lessons over theory-first instruction.
- P4: Draw the line between realism and startup melodrama.
- P5: Close by setting up the next chapter's biographical grounding and the later crisis chapters.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter must separate useful realism from performative toughness.
The mechanism is not inspiration. It is judgment under pressure: leaders keep choosing even when no option feels clean.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard mode needs a real cost condition.
Show that leadership remains painful even when the leader knows the framework, because frameworks do not absorb consequences, layoffs, fear, or uncertainty on the leader's behalf.
