## Outline Lock
- Chapter claim and mechanism are fixed to the brief.
- Required anchors and hard-edge tension are mandatory in final prose.

## Section Blueprint

## 1) Pressure Opening: Growth Outruns Management
- Function: Establish the threshold question under immediate operating pressure.
- Include:
- Growth is increasing complexity faster than existing management habits.
- First visible cracks appear in people system execution (role confusion, weak management consistency, training gaps).
- Lock line of inquiry: what fails first when management system lags scale.

## 2) Principle Definition: People -> Product -> Profit
- Function: Define the ordering rule precisely.
- Include:
- Clarify that order is causal, not sentimental.
- State durable-profit logic: people quality and product integrity are prerequisites.
- Set explicit boundary against anti-profit misread.

## 3) Anchor A: Good Place To Work Means Performance Culture
- Function: Separate comfort culture from performance-supporting culture.
- Include:
- Culture standard must raise execution quality.
- Accountability is part of care, not opposed to it.
- Failure mode if skipped: culture drifts into low-clarity, low-ownership norms.

## 4) Anchor B: Training And Executive Hiring Are Core Startup Work
- Function: Show leadership obligation for management development.
- Include:
- Training is a force multiplier, not optional overhead.
- Executive hiring does not replace internal managerial development.
- Failure mode if skipped: leadership bandwidth collapse and inconsistent management quality.

## 5) Anchor C: Management Debt As Delayed Compounding Cost
- Function: Name and explain mechanism of debt accumulation.
- Include:
- Debt source: postponing management discipline while small.
- Debt expression later: product slippage, coordination failures, profit pressure.
- Link debt directly to people-system neglect.

## 6) Integration Section: One Operating System, Not Three Separate Priorities
- Function: Connect culture, training, hiring, and QA into unified mechanism.
- Include:
- People system quality feeds product decisions and execution reliability.
- Product reliability supports durable financial outcomes.
- Misread trap: treating people/product/profit as competing lanes instead of ordered dependencies.

## 7) Hard-Edge Section: The Expensive Delay Pattern
- Function: Fulfill hard-edge requirement with contradiction and consequence.
- Include:
- Why founders delay discipline (speed, identity as "still small").
- Short-term gain vs long-term operating cost.
- Boundary: show concrete downside, not abstract warning.

## 8) Bridge Forward: Going Concern Conditions
- Function: Hand off to Chapter 7 scope.
- Include:
- Once management system exists, scaling introduces politics/process/executive-fit issues.
- Keep continuity from crisis chapter to scale chapter explicit.
