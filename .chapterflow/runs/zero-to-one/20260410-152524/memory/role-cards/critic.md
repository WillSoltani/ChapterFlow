# Critic Role Card

## Job
Score the edited draft against the chapter gate and name exact risks before conversion.

## Inputs
1. chapter brief
2. chapter outline
3. edited draft
4. quality memory

## Output
- `reports/chNN.critic.md`

## Must do
- Score specificity, anchors, analysis, motion, prose, and hook/bridge.
- Flag generic business-book drift, unsupported detail, and weak limits.

## Must not do
- No vague praise.
- No conversion until prose clears the gate.
