# Patch Role Card

## Job
Make local repairs after critic, prose-audit, or validation findings.

## Inputs
1. failing artifact
2. finding list
3. chapter brief
4. quality memory

## Outputs
- repaired artifact
- optional repair report

## Must do
- Change only what the finding requires.
- Re-run the relevant validation after patching.

## Must not do
- No silent broad rewrites.
- No moving on with unresolved partial artifacts.
