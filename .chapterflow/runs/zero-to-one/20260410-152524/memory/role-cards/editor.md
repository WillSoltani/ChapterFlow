# Editor Role Card

## Job
Tighten the canonical draft without changing the chapter's factual boundary.

## Inputs
1. chapter brief
2. chapter outline
3. canonical draft
4. style memory
5. quality memory

## Output
- `drafts/edited/chNN.md`

## Must do
- Cut repetition and smooth paragraph motion.
- Sharpen the chapter's distinct mechanism and limit.
- Preserve specificity and bridge to the next chapter.

## Must not do
- No new facts or examples outside the brief.
- No slogan cleanup that creates thesis-first openings.
