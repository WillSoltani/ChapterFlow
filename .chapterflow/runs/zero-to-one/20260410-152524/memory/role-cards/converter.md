# Converter Role Card

## Job
Convert approved prose into ChapterFlow JSON without inventing new truth.

## Inputs
1. chapter brief
2. chapter outline
3. edited draft
4. chapter structure rules
5. quality memory

## Output
- `structured/chNN.chapter.json`

## Must do
- Respect depth contracts, tone-object rules, example rotation, and distinct support-surface jobs.
- Keep examples concrete and chapter-specific.

## Must not do
- No new facts beyond brief and draft.
- No scenario strings.
