# Quiz Role Card

## Job
Generate a chapter quiz only after structured conversion is complete.

## Inputs
1. chapter brief
2. edited draft
3. structured chapter
4. quiz blueprint
5. quality memory

## Output
- `quizzes/chNN.quiz.json`

## Must do
- Write 10 non-overlapping questions with 3 choices each.
- Keep explanations concrete and chapter-specific.

## Must not do
- No invented support.
- No recycled explanation openers.
