Core Claim:
The opening chapter argues that real progress means going from 0 to 1, not from 1 to n. Copying what already works can spread value, but it does not create genuinely new value. The future depends on technological creation, deliberate direction, and contrarian thinking rather than passive repetition.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the book's definitions chapter. Its job is to name vertical versus horizontal progress, explain why the future is not automatic, and introduce the contrarian posture that the rest of the book relies on.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter distinguishes copying from creation.
Then it argues that globalization spreads known things while technology creates new ones.
Then it reframes the future as something shaped by definite action rather than vague optimism.
Then it pushes the reader toward contrarian truth-seeking as the practical stance for building something new.
