Core Claim:
This chapter argues that business value depends less on being first than on creating an advantage durable enough to keep producing value far into the future.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the durability chapter. Its job is to move the book from escaping rivalry to understanding why long-term endurance and future cash flow matter more than early novelty or short-lived excitement.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter rejects first-mover glamour as a shallow standard.
Then it ties company value to future cash flow rather than immediate headlines.
Then it explains why durable advantage matters more than arriving early.
Then it preserves the limit that endurance comes from strength, not from passive waiting.

Required Anchors:
- the chapter's reframe from first-mover advantage toward last-mover advantage
- value depends on future cash flow and staying power
- short-lived lead is weaker than durable position
- the chapter asks what kind of company can still dominate at the end of the market's development
