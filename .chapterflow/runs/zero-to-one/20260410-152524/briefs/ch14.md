Core Claim:
This chapter argues that the unusual intensity that can make founders powerful can also make them unstable, so founder energy has both upside and danger.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the founder-paradox chapter. Its job is to keep founder exceptionalism and founder instability in the same frame rather than collapsing into hero worship or critique by stereotype.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter rejects simple founder myths, whether celebratory or hostile.
Then it argues that unusual founder intensity can create real advantages.
Then it shows why that same intensity can destabilize judgment, culture, or organization.
Then it preserves the limit that the chapter is about paradox and tension, not moral caricature.
