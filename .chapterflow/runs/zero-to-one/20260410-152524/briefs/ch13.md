Core Claim:
This chapter argues that ambitious technology sectors can still fail when timing, scale, and business design are misjudged, so technological ambition alone is not enough.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the clean-tech failure chapter. Its job is to diagnose why a promising technology wave can collapse without turning into generic hindsight sneering or anti-technology rhetoric.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter rejects the idea that large technological ambition automatically produces strong businesses.
Then it argues that timing, scale, and business design matter alongside technical promise.
Then it shows why failure can come from bad sequencing or weak underlying economics rather than from technology being worthless in principle.
Then it preserves the limit that the chapter is practical failure analysis, not mockery or prophecy.
