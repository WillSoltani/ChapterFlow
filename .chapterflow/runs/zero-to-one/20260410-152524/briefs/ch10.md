Core Claim:
This chapter argues that strong startups are built by tightly aligned teams with shared mission, high trust, and role fit rather than by loose collections of talented strangers.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the dense-culture chapter. Its job is to show that company output compounds when the team is mission-matched and internally coherent, while avoiding romance about culture as perks or vibes.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter argues that company culture is built intentionally rather than accidentally.
Then it treats close alignment and trust as productive mechanisms rather than sentimental extras.
Then it shows why selecting for fit and shared mission changes how people work together.
Then it preserves the limit that culture is not fluff, cult theater, or generic friendliness.

Required Anchors:
