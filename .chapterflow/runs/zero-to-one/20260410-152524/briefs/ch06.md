Core Claim:
This chapter argues that uncertainty does not justify surrendering to luck-only thinking. Planning still matters, and people can shape outcomes more than lottery-ticket thinking admits.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the anti-fatalism chapter. Its job is to defend agency and planning without pretending that uncertainty disappears.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter identifies lottery-ticket thinking as a cultural posture.
Then it argues that luck-only explanations weaken planning.
Then it restores a stronger role for agency under uncertainty.
Then it preserves the limit that planning is not prophecy or total control.
