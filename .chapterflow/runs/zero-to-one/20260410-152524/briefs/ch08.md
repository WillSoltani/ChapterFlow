Core Claim:
This chapter argues that important truths remain hidden or underbelieved, and that valuable companies are often built by acting on such secrets before they become obvious.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is the hidden-truth chapter. Its job is to show where outlier value can come from: not from copying what everyone already sees, but from discovering something real that others have missed.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter challenges the belief that all important truths are already known.
Then it argues that hidden or neglected truths still exist.
Then it links those truths to unusual opportunity and company creation.
Then it preserves the limit that contrarianism without truth is empty.

Required Anchors:
