# Chapter Brief: ch03

- chapterId: ch03
- number: 3
- title: The Speculator and the Prostitute
- bookId: the-black-swan

Anchor facts:
- The chapter contrasts two payoff structures.
- The speculator can survive many small misses and still be transformed by one large hit.
- The prostitute stands for linear, repeatedly paid labor where each encounter settles the score immediately.
- The chapter uses this contrast to introduce scalability and asymmetric reward.

Governing mechanism:
- In scalable domains, one rare success can outweigh a long run of ordinary outcomes.
