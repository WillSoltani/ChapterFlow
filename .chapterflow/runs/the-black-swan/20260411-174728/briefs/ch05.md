Chapter: 5
Title: Confirmation Shmonfirmation!

- keep the chapter concrete through decisions, forecasts, and ordinary argument habits

Non-negotiables:
- stress disconfirming evidence rather than generic open-mindedness

- end by setting up the narrative machinery in Chapter 6
