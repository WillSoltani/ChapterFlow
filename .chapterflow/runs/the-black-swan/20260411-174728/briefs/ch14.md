# Brief: ch14

Chapter: 14
Title: From Mediocristan to Extremistan, and Back

Purpose:
- introduce the core distinction between bounded and outlier-dominated domains
- show when averages and ordinary variation work and when they fail badly
- make the concept concrete through body traits, wealth, cities, markets, and cultural hits

Non-negotiables:
- keep the distinction precise rather than merely dramatic
- avoid flattening all domains into pure chaos
- bridge toward the anti-Gaussian critique in Chapter 15
