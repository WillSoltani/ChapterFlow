# Chapter Brief: ch04

- chapterId: ch04
- number: 4
- title: One Thousand and One Days, or How Not to Be a Sucker
- bookId: the-black-swan

Anchor facts:
- The chapter's central story is the Thanksgiving turkey.
- The turkey is fed for a thousand days and therefore grows more confident in the farmer's goodwill.
- The very day confidence peaks is the day danger peaks.
- The chapter uses the story to attack induction and false safety built from repeated confirmation.

Governing mechanism:
- A long run of confirming evidence can coexist with catastrophic hidden risk.
