# Canonical Draft: ch11

Taleb now pivots from exposing prediction vanity to asking what a sane posture looks like when the future stays opaque. The answer is less like prophecy and more like fieldwork. If the sky cannot be read cleanly, stop pretending it can and look for the traces that reality actually leaves behind.

That is the point of the bird-poop image. You do not need a grand theory of the bird's flight to notice where evidence has landed. Under uncertainty, practical progress often comes from searching, probing, noticing, and preserving room to act when something real appears.

The chapter therefore favors scouts over prophets. It prefers exploratory contact to polished anticipation. This is not cynicism and not passivity. It is disciplined openness: move through the world in ways that let luck, evidence, and surprise become legible before you speak too confidently about them.
