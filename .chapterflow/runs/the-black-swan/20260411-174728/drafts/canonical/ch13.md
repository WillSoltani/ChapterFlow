# Canonical Draft: ch13

Chapter 13 asks the obvious practical question after prediction has been humbled: what do you do instead? Taleb's answer is not paralysis. It is to build positions that can live with uncertainty better than your forecasts can explain it.

That means shifting from prophecy to structure. Preserve room to respond. Avoid fragility. Limit ruin. Seek asymmetries where the downside is survivable and the upside can remain open. The posture is less about seeing far and more about arranging yourself so that surprise does not immediately destroy you.

Appelles the Painter becomes a chapter about design under opacity. You do not need a perfect map if your position is hard to kill and able to exploit favorable deviation. That sets up the next chapter's domain split: some worlds are tame enough for averages, and some are ruled by outliers.
