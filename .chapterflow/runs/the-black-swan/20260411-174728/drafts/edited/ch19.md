# Edited Draft: ch19

Chapter 19 closes with posture, not prediction. Taleb's answer to uncertainty is asymmetry: cap ruin, keep options open, and stay available for favorable surprise.

The ending rejects both paralysis and false certainty. You do not need to know the future cleanly if your exposure is disciplined enough to survive error and use volatility well.

That is how the book ends practically. Do not kneel to ornate certainty. Build a life that can take a hit, learn, and still catch upside when the world turns unexpectedly generous.
