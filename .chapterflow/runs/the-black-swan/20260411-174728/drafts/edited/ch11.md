# Edited Draft: ch11

Chapter 11 replaces prediction with search. Taleb's image is practical: if the sky is opaque, do not pretend to map every flight path. Look for the traces that actually hit the ground.

The lesson is exploratory action. Search, probe, notice, and preserve room to move when reality reveals something. Scouts beat prophets because contact with the world outranks theatrical foresight.

The chapter stays anti-cynical. Pragmatism here means letting evidence arrive before confidence hardens. That prepares the next question: who deserves authority when visibility is weak?
