# Edited Draft: ch16

Chapter 16 says randomness is often misread because order is more attractive than noise. The mind beautifies chance into patterns, shapes, and stories that feel cleaner than the messy truth.

Taleb is exposing an aesthetic weakness as much as an analytic one. Smoothness can seduce judgment even when reality is rougher.

That is why bad models survive. They often look better than randomness feels. Chapter 17 will show the damage when neat curves are forced into places they do not belong.
