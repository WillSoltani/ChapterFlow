# Edited Draft: ch13

Chapter 13 turns uncertainty into a design problem. If prediction is weak, the question is not how to guess harder. The question is how to stand so that surprise does not crush you.

Taleb's answer is robust action: preserve options, cap ruin, and prefer asymmetries where loss is limited but gain can stay open. This is a structural posture, not a visionary one.

The chapter prepares the next move. To know what kind of robustness you need, you have to know what kind of domain you are in. That is where Mediocristan and Extremistan arrive.
