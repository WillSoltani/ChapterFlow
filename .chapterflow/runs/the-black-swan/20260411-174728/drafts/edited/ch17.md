# Edited Draft: ch17

Chapter 17 is about misplacement. A neat curve can become dangerous simply by being installed where the world does not support it.

Taleb is not attacking mathematics itself. He is attacking category error: using a tool of bounded order in a domain that is rougher, wilder, or less measurable than the method assumes.

That is why the chapter leads to the phony expert. Once the wrong tool sounds technical enough, someone will sell it with borrowed authority. Chapter 18 takes that step.
