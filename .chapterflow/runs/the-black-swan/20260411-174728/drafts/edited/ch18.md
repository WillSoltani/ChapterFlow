# Edited Draft: ch18

Chapter 18 turns the book's abstract critique into a recognizable character: the phony expert. This is the person who performs certainty beyond real visibility and sells precision that the domain never authorized.

Taleb is not condemning knowledge. He is attacking counterfeit authority that uses method theater, status, and confidence to hide uncertainty.

The chapter leads directly to the ending. Once the reader stops admiring the phony, the question becomes how to live and act without surrendering to false expertise. Chapter 19 answers that practically.
