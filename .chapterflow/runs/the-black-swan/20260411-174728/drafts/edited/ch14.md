# Edited Draft: ch14

Chapter 14 splits reality into two domain types. Mediocristan is the bounded world where no single case can overwhelm the whole. Extremistan is the wild world where one case can dominate the total.

That difference changes everything. Averages, ordinary variation, and bell-curve instincts can work in tame domains and fail badly in outlier-driven ones.

Taleb is not saying every field is wild. He is demanding that the domain be identified before the math gets trusted. Chapter 15 will press that point against Gaussian fraud directly.
