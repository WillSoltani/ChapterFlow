Chapter 15 turns the domain split into a direct attack on Gaussian overreach. Frozen sources support a narrow paraphrase: the bell curve becomes intellectually fraudulent when it is carried from bounded domains into Extremistan, where outliers and fat tails dominate outcomes. Taleb is not abolishing mathematics. He is attacking a false sense of safety created by the wrong distributional assumptions. The chapter prepares the next move toward the mind's attraction to patterns, elegance, and fake order.
