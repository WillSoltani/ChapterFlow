Title: One Thousand and One Days, or How Not to Be a Sucker

Support carried from the frozen bundle:
- The turkey is fed and cared for over a thousand days.
- That repeated confirmation increases the turkey's confidence.
- On the day confidence is highest, the turkey is slaughtered for Thanksgiving.
- The chapter uses this to expose the weakness of induction and the illusion that repeated support guarantees future safety.
