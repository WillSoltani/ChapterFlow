Chapter 12 challenges who gets to speak with authority when the world is opaque. Frozen sources support a narrow paraphrase: expertise deserves less deference when prediction is weak, incentives are distorted, and status outruns accountability. Taleb is not rejecting knowledge. He is pressuring prestige systems that grant authority without enough exposure to consequences. The chapter points toward a tougher standard: trust should lean toward those with real contact, constraint, and skin in the game rather than polished distance.
