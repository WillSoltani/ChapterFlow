Chapter 8 sharpens the book's attack on visible success by introducing silent evidence: the missing cases that never make it into the record people use for judgment. Frozen sources support a narrow paraphrase: the visible survivor can be deeply misleading when the hidden field of failures is absent. The point is not that winners teach nothing. It is that they teach dangerously incomplete lessons when denominator reality is missing. This chapter prepares the move toward the ludic fallacy by showing how clean samples can already be broken before formal modeling even begins.
