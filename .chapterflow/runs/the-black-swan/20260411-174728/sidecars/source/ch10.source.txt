Chapter 10 turns the earlier critiques into a direct attack on prediction culture. Frozen sources support a narrow paraphrase: the scandal is not merely that forecasts fail, but that they are delivered with unwarranted confidence and social authority. Taleb's concern is institutional as much as intellectual. Prediction theater survives because confidence is rewarded more visibly than calibrated ignorance. The chapter prepares the next move toward pragmatic searching and action without false foresight.
