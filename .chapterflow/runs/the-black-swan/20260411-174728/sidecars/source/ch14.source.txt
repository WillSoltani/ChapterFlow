Chapter 14 introduces one of the book's central distinctions. Frozen sources support a narrow paraphrase: some domains are Mediocristan, where individual variation is bounded and aggregates are stable, while others are Extremistan, where single observations can dominate the total. Taleb uses this split to explain why averages and Gaussian intuitions work in some places and become dangerous in others. The chapter prepares the direct attack on bell-curve thinking in Chapter 15.
