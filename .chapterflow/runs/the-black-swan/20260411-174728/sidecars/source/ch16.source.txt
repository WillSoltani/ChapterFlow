Chapter 16 shifts from wrong distributions to the mind's attraction to order. Frozen sources support a narrow paraphrase: people beautify randomness into patterns, stories, and smooth structures because elegance feels more satisfying than noise. Taleb is pressing on the aesthetic side of error. The problem is not only analytic weakness but also the emotional and visual appeal of order. This prepares the next chapter's critique of putting bell curves and neat statistics where the world does not justify them.
