## Early-chapter factual notes used for Chapters 1-2

Chapter 1 support:
- Taleb opens with his own formation as an empirical skeptic.
- Lebanon's civil-war rupture is a load-bearing example for discontinuity and the failure of smooth extrapolation.
- History is treated as opaque, with causes and consequences harder to map than retrospective stories suggest.

Chapter 2 support:
- Yevgenia Krasnova is Taleb's fictional novelist used to show how a wildly successful creative outcome looks inevitable only after it arrives.
- The story is built to show how one success can rewrite the perceived probability of that success.
- The chapter presses against categorization and hindsight explanation.
