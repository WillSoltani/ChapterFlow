# Book Skeleton

Metadata:
- Title: The Black Swan
- Author: Nassim Nicholas Taleb
- Edition: 2007 Random House first edition
- Chapter count: 19
- Variant family: EMH

Chapter order and one-line intent:
1. The Apprenticeship of an Empirical Skeptic: open with rupture and show why history jumps instead of gliding.
2. Yevgenia's Black Swan: show how success is reverse-engineered into fake inevitability.
3. The Speculator and the Prostitute: separate asymmetrical payoff from linear labor logic.
4. One Thousand and One Days: attack induction through the turkey's false security.
5. Confirmation Shmonfirmation!: show how minds defend prior belief by filtering evidence.
6. The Narrative Fallacy: explain how storytelling compresses uncertainty into fake order.
7. Living in the Antechamber of Hope: show how lottery-style hope distorts perception.
8. Giacomo Casanova's Unfailing Luck: expose silent evidence and survivor distortion.
9. The Ludic Fallacy: attack neat game-like models in messy reality.
10. The Scandal of Prediction: show why forecasters talk with more confidence than signal.
11. How to Look for Bird Poop: build pragmatic searching rather than precise prediction.
12. Epistemocracy, a Dream: challenge expert authority under deep opacity.
13. Appelles the Painter: shift from forecasting to robust action under uncertainty.
14. From Mediocristan to Extremistan, and Back: distinguish domains where averages work from domains where outliers dominate.
15. The Bell Curve, That Great Intellectual Fraud: attack Gaussian misuse in fat-tail domains.
16. The Aesthetics of Randomness: show how pattern-hunger misreads noise.
17. Locke's Madmen: show what breaks when neat statistics are forced onto wild systems.
18. The Uncertainty of the Phony: expose experts who sell precision they cannot own.
19. Half and Half: move from diagnosis to asymmetric practical posture.
