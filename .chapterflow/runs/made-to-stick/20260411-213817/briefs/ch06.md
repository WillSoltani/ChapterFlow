Core Claim:
Stories move ideas farther because they carry lessons, cues, and meaning inside a sequence people can replay. The chapter's point is not that stories are automatically powerful. It is that narrative can smuggle a principle into memory and action when the message is embedded in the story rather than stapled on as a moral.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This chapter closes the SUCCESs sequence by treating story as a delivery vessel for earlier strengths. The support here is narrower than earlier chapters, so the chapter should stay focused on stories as carriers, embedded messages, and the challenge / connection / creativity plot taxonomy.

Author Logic Chain:
First the authors argue that stories carry practical lessons farther than explanation alone.
Then they argue that the lesson must live inside the narrative instead of being pasted on afterward.
Then they sort recurring story power into challenge, connection, and creativity plots.
