- embedded lessons work better than morals stapled onto the end
- challenge, connection, and creativity plots offer a narrow taxonomy
- stories can package earlier sticky traits into one delivery vessel
- vivid narrative can distort as well as transmit

- highest justified level for this chapter: evaluate

- q06: school retelling scenario
- q07: transfer to peer teaching choice
- q08: personal explanation shift judgment
- q09: deeper synthesis on distortion risk
- q10: connection to epilogue / mutation in retelling

- each direct explanation must begin differently
