Chapter Promise:
Show that stories move ideas farther when the lesson is embedded in the narrative rather than attached after the fact.

Opening Move:
Open on the difference between a principle that sounds correct and a short story people can replay later when action is required.

- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 stories as carriers for lessons and practical meaning
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 embedded lesson versus stapled moral
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 challenge / connection / creativity plots and limit

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Stories work because they package meaning in sequence, character, and consequence so the lesson can travel with the narrative.

Hard-Depth Tension:
A story can transmit truth or distortion depending on whether the embedded lesson stays faithful to the principle it is supposed to carry.

Threshold Question:
How do you use a story as a carrier without letting the plot overshadow or warp the lesson?

Takeaway Count Lock:

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- Takeaway 1: stories carry action cues farther than explanation alone
- Takeaway 2: embedded lessons travel better than stapled morals
- Takeaway 3: challenge, connection, and creativity plots organize story power
- Takeaway 4: stories can deliver earlier chapter strengths in one vessel
- Takeaway 5: vivid narrative can distort as easily as it can transmit
