Chapter Promise:
Show why a short checklist can outperform unaided expertise on routine critical steps in complex work.

Opening Move:
Open on the B-17 flight-test failure so the reader feels how one forgotten basic can defeat a room full of expertise.

Paragraph Job Map:

- P2: identify memory and attention failure plus deliberate skipping as the chapter's two pressures
- P3: define what a checklist actually does

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter distinguishes long procedural complexity from a short list of minimum necessary checks. The checklist does not try to contain all expertise. It protects the vital basics most likely to be lost.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Keep alive the boundary between useful verification and dead compliance. Hard depth should preserve the question of how checklists help without shrinking judgment.

Threshold Question:
How do you keep a checklist short and forceful enough to prevent omission without making professionals stop thinking?
