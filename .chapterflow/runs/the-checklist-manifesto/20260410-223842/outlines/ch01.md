Show why modern high-skill work fails through overload and coordination strain even when the people involved are competent.

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: the drowning rescue as a concrete scene of many moving parts under pressure
- P2: name the era of superspecialists and why that expertise genuinely matters
- P3: show how complexity exceeds individual grasp even when skill is real
- P4: move from individual expertise to system failure and omission pressure
- P5: state why the old ideal of the self-sufficient expert is no longer enough

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter distinguishes lack of expertise from overload created by extreme complexity. The failure mode is not ignorance first. It is omission, handoff loss, and system strain inside expert work.
