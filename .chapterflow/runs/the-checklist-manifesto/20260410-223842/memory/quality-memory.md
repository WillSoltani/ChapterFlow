# Quality Memory — compact

Compiled from the v13 rules and frozen for this run.

## Chapter-quality rubric (must score >= 10/12)
1. Chapter specificity
2. Anchor use
3. Analytical value
4. Paragraph motion
5. Prose quality
6. Hook and bridge

## Auto-fails
- Invented medical detail, unsupported stats, or unsupported case embellishment.
- Generic productivity prose that could fit any business book.
- Thesis-first opening, contamination leakage, or source-splice drift.
- Hard depth that repeats medium.
- Empty quiz in chapter-gate mode.
- Plain-string scenarios, tone collapse, or wrapper payload drift.

## Structure contract
- Easy: 140-175 words per tone, exactly 3 takeaways, no `moreDetails`.
- Medium: 330-420 words per tone, 5-6 takeaways, `activationPrompt`, singular `selfCheckPrompt`, recap object.
- Hard: 490-600 words per tone, 5-7 takeaways, `activationPrompt`, exactly 2 `selfCheckPrompts`, `predictionPrompt`, recap object.

## Example and quiz contract
- 6 examples with canonical format rotation and 2 work / 2 school / 2 personal split.
- 5 review cards with 2/2/1 difficulty split.
- 10 quiz questions, 3 choices each, varying opener shapes and non-redundant principles.

## Source contract
- Every chapter needs sidecars before writer work.
- Sidecars must trace back to the frozen bundle.
- Keep checklists tied to concrete failure modes, communication points, or decision discipline.
- If the source only supports the chapter narrowly, keep the prose narrow too.
