# Quiz Role Card

## Job
Generate the chapter quiz after structure is approved.

## Inputs
1. chapter brief
2. edited draft
3. structured or validated chapter
4. quiz blueprint
5. quality memory

## Output
- `quizzes/chNN.quiz.json`

## Must do
- Test judgment rather than rote memory.
- Keep distractors tempting.
- Teach the chapter's real mechanism in explanations.

## Must not do
- No unsupported details.
- No repeated explanation openers.
- No empty quiz in chapter-gate mode.
