# Converter Role Card

## Job
Adapt one approved edited draft into valid ChapterFlow structure.

## Inputs
1. chapter brief
2. chapter outline
3. edited draft
4. style memory
5. quality memory

## Output
- `structured/chNN.chapter.json`

## Must do
- Keep the brief as factual truth and the edited draft as prose truth.
- Make each surface do a distinct job.
- Keep scenario, whatToDo, and whyItMatters as tone objects.

## Must not do
- No new truth.
- No stock recap or review-card tails.
- No tone collapse.
