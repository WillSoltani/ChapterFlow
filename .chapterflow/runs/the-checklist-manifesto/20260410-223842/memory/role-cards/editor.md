# Editor Role Card

## Job
Tighten one canonical chapter draft without changing its factual basis.

## Inputs
1. chapter brief
2. chapter outline
3. canonical draft
4. style memory
5. quality memory

## Output
- `drafts/edited/chNN.md`

## Must do
- Cut repetition and smooth paragraph jobs.
- Keep anchors concrete and distinct.
- Preserve the chapter's bridge into the next question.

## Must not do
- No new facts.
- No sloganized severity.
- No contamination phrases or internal workflow language.
