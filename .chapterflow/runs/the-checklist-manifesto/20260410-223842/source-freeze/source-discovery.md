# Source Discovery — The Checklist Manifesto

## Goal
Freeze a lawful, high-signal source bundle before any chapter work begins.

## Discovery outcome
- Locked the dominant English first-edition family for `The Checklist Manifesto: How to Get Things Right`.
- Confirmed the nine-chapter sequence and page starts from authorized preview/catalog sources.
- Confirmed that no translation or revised-edition ambiguity materially changes chapter structure for this run.
- Established a paraphrase-first source posture because the frozen bundle contains only limited preview text, not a full authorized text file.

2. Google Books preview for chapter order and page starts.
3. Open Library edition page for first-edition identifiers and chapter-title cross-check.
4. CCLS catalog / Syndetics for chapter list with page numbers and a short chapter-1 excerpt.
5. SuperSummary chapter bundles for chapter-level interpretation across the full book.
6. Graham Mann notes as a secondary cross-check for emphasis and recurring examples.

## Why no user question was needed
- The visible English edition family is stable at the chapter-map level.
- No translation choice is in play.
- No revised edition with added or removed chapters surfaced in the frozen sources.

## Operating limits for downstream chapters
- Exact quotes are allowed only when directly supported by the frozen preview or catalog excerpt.
- Otherwise use paraphrase-first prose and keep unsupported details out.
- When a secondary source adds nuance not visible in the authorized preview, keep it narrow and marked as interpretation rather than as direct textual proof.
