Core Claim:
The chapter argues that modern work outgrew the old master-builder model because many serious tasks are now too distributed for one expert to hold completely. Gawande distinguishes simple, complicated, and complex problems to show why elite knowledge alone no longer secures reliable results. In settings like major construction, what matters is not one mind commanding everything but a system of coordination, communication, and shared execution. The chapter widens the book from the checklist as a tool toward the organizational reality that makes such tools necessary.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
Unlike Chapter 2, which argues for checklists against omission, Chapter 3 changes the picture of expertise itself. Its pressure is structural and organizational. It explains why no modern master builder can safely carry the whole field alone and why disciplined coordination becomes the new professional demand.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author distinguishes simple, complicated, and complex problems so the reader can see why some work exceeds lone command.
Then the author uses the Russia Wharf construction example to show how modern achievement depends on many distributed specialists, constant coordination, and visible communication.
Then the author reframes professional excellence around reliable collaboration rather than around the fantasy of one person holding the entire system.

Frameworks or Terms Introduced:
- simple problem: a task with known steps and limited uncertainty
- complicated problem: a task that can be difficult yet still yields to expertise and planning
- complex problem: a task whose moving parts, handoffs, and changing conditions exceed what one controller can fully hold
- master-builder model: the old ideal that one expert can command the whole undertaking
- coordination discipline: shared communication and visible alignment across specialties

Specific Applications:
- major construction and project delivery
- medicine and surgery once multiple specialists must coordinate
- any expert environment where no single actor can safely hold every dependency alone
