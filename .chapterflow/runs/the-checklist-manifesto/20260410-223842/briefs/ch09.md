Core Claim:
The chapter argues that checklist logic reaches beyond surgery into other domains and closes the book on the practical value of routine discipline. Gawande uses investing and broader-domain adoption, especially Mohnish Pabrai, to show that checklists are not a medical curiosity but a general response to avoidable error in complex judgment work. The deeper point is that judgment grows stronger when it accepts routine safeguards instead of treating them as beneath serious expertise.
