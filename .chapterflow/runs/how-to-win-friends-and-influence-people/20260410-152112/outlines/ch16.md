# Chapter 16 Outline

## Promise

People cooperate more fully when they feel the action is partly theirs rather than something merely imposed on them.

## Chapter Moves

1. Reframe cooperation as shared agency rather than obedience.
2. Explain why compliance pressure can get surface movement without real buy-in.
3. Show how ownership changes effort, tone, and durability.
4. Draw the limit: staged ownership becomes manipulation theater.
5. Bridge forward to Chapter 17, where motives and perspective become more legible.

## Guardrails

- do not make the chapter sound like generic collaboration advice
- keep ownership distinct from fake empowerment
- preserve the difference between participation and pressured compliance
