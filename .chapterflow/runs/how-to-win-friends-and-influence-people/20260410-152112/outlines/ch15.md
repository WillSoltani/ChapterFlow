# Chapter 15 Outline

## Promise

Allowing someone to air a complaint can release pressure that would otherwise harden the exchange before any correction has a chance to work.

## Chapter Moves

1. Reframe the complaint as pressure, not just content.
2. Explain the safety-valve idea: letting the person speak can reduce immediate heat.
3. Show the risk of interrupting, correcting, or defending too early.
4. Draw the limit: hearing a complaint is not surrendering to it.
5. Bridge forward to cooperation once pressure has dropped.

## Guardrails

- do not romanticize complaint spirals
- do not imply every complaint is valid because it was heard
- keep the chapter about de-escalation before persuasion, not passive agreement
