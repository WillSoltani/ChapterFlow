# Chapter 19 Outline

## Promise

An appeal to nobler motives can change the story people tell themselves about their choice, making the better action easier to inhabit.

## Chapter Moves

1. Reframe persuasion as a contest of self-story.
2. Explain why people like to see themselves in a better light.
3. Show how a nobler-motive appeal gives the choice a different internal meaning.
4. Draw the limit: do not moralize or pretend motives are pure.
5. Bridge forward to dramatizing ideas in Chapter 20.

## Guardrails

- do not turn the chapter into sermonizing
- keep nobler motives tied to believable self-understanding
- preserve mixed-motive realism
