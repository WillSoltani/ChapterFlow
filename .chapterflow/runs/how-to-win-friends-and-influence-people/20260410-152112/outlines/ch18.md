# Chapter 18 Outline

## Promise

People lower their guard when their ideas and desires are met with real sympathy rather than immediate dismissal, but sympathy does not require agreement.

## Chapter Moves

1. Reframe sympathy as a reduction of defensiveness.
2. Show that people want their ideas and desires to be understood.
3. Distinguish sympathy from capitulation or endorsement.
4. Explain why precise sympathy works better than vague validation.
5. Bridge forward to appeals to nobler motives.

## Guardrails

- do not turn the chapter into generic emotional validation
- preserve the distinction between sympathy and agreement
- keep sympathy strategic, specific, and judgment-compatible
