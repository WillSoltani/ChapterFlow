# Chapter 22 Outline

## Promise

Praise before criticism can change the emotional frame of feedback so the person hears correction with less resentment.

## Chapter Moves

1. Reframe criticism as an emotional opening problem, not only a content problem.
2. Explain why sincere praise lowers defensiveness.
3. Distinguish specific honest praise from manipulative flattery.
4. Show that correction still has to remain real and clear.
5. Bridge forward to Chapter 23 on indirect criticism and dignity.

## Guardrails

- do not endorse fake compliment sandwiches
- keep praise sincere, specific, and relevant
- preserve the reality of the corrective message
