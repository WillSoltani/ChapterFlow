Chapter Promise:
Show that superiority and blunt certainty are reliable ways to create enemies because they make disagreement feel like a dignity attack.

Opening Move:
Open on the moment when being obviously certain stops sounding clear and starts sounding like contempt.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 -> title claim and superiority signal
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 -> dignity threat and why resistance hardens
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 -> keep humility non-passive and bridge into Chapter 12

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: frame enemy-making as a consequence of how correctness is carried
- P2: show how superiority turns disagreement into status injury
- P3: name dignity threat as the chapter's main mechanism
- P4: explain why contempt blocks influence even when the claim is right
- P5: keep the chapter from collapsing into fake uncertainty or passivity
- P6: bridge into Chapter 12 on admitting fault quickly

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter works by separating conviction from contempt, but it fails if humility sounds like hiding judgment rather than removing superiority.

Hard-Depth Tension:
How do you keep confidence intact while removing the signals that make another person feel belittled?

Threshold Question:
When does certainty stop clarifying and start humiliating?

Takeaway Count Lock:
- Easy: 3 (always)
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 5

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- superiority creates enemies faster than disagreement alone
- dignity threat hardens resistance
- certainty can become a weapon
- humility is different from passivity
- contempt blocks influence even when facts are solid

Scenario Lesson Map:
- Scenario 1 (decision_point, work): manager decides whether to correct with dominance or with plain respect
- Scenario 2 (postmortem, school): seminar discussion went badly because one student sounded superior
- Scenario 3 (dialogue, personal): personal correction softens once contempt leaves the tone
- Scenario 4 (predict_reveal, work): feedback moment improves when certainty stops performing status
- Scenario 5 (dilemma, school): tutor wants to be clear without making a classmate feel small
- Scenario 6 (before_after, personal): family disagreement shifts when confidence loses its superiority edge

Scenario Vividness Targets:
For at least 4 of 6 scenarios, include a laptop, seminar notes, coffee cup, shared doc, notebook margin, or kitchen chair.

Preview Constraint:
The preview must point toward Chapter 12 by asking what happens when the fault is yours and quick admission removes the other person's appetite for attack.
