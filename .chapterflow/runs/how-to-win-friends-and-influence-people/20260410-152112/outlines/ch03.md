Chapter Promise:
Show that influence begins when you frame the action through what the other person wants instead of through your own pressure alone.

Opening Move:
Open on the reader's usual mistake: asking from the speaker's side and wondering why the other person stays flat.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1-P2 -> self-centered appeals fail because people filter through their own wants
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P3-P4 -> motive translation as the practical move
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P5-P6 -> limit against pandering, surrender, or manipulation

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: announce the chapter's perspective shift
- P2: show why self-centered requests are weak
- P3: define motive translation
- P4: show willing cooperation versus dragged compliance
- P5: keep the principle from collapsing into people-pleasing
- P6: bridge into social interest and the next chapter

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
People do not naturally organize around your wants first. Stronger persuasion translates the ask into something the other person already values, wants, protects, or can feel proud of.

Hard-Depth Tension:
How do you begin from the other person's wants without becoming manipulative or erasing your own aims?

Threshold Question:
When is other-person framing intelligent empathy, and when does it become fake packaging?

Takeaway Count Lock:
- Easy: 3 (always)
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 5

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- people hear through self-interest first
- self-centered requests are structurally weak
- motive translation creates better willingness
- cooperation strengthens when the action feels worthwhile on the other side
- other-person framing is not the same as surrender or pandering

Scenario Lesson Map:
- Scenario 1 (decision_point, work): manager reframes a request around what the teammate wants from the project
- Scenario 2 (postmortem, school): student team sees why a self-centered pitch failed in a case competition room
- Scenario 3 (dialogue, personal): home request changes once the speaker stops leading with irritation
- Scenario 4 (predict_reveal, work): sales or proposal framing through client benefit instead of quota pressure
- Scenario 5 (dilemma, school): student leader tries to motivate without sounding manipulative
- Scenario 6 (before_after, personal): relationship atmosphere shifts when requests begin from shared benefit instead of grievance

Preview Constraint:
