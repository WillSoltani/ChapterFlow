# Chapter 30 Outline

## Promise

Influence lasts better when the other person can feel good about taking the action.

3. Distinguish constructive alignment from manipulation doctrine.
4. Show how pride, motive, and identity can support action.
5. Close the book by synthesizing the broader influence ethic.
