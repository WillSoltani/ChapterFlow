# Chapter 23 Outline

## Promise

Indirect criticism can reduce offense and preserve dignity if the problem still remains clear.

## Chapter Moves

1. Reframe criticism as partly a dignity-management problem.
2. Explain why indirect delivery can lower resistance.
3. Draw the limit: indirectness cannot become vagueness or evasion.
4. Show that clarity and tact have to coexist.
5. Bridge forward to Chapter 24 on admitting your own mistakes first.

## Guardrails

- do not glorify cowardly hinting
- keep the problem legible and real
- preserve indirectness as tact, not avoidance
