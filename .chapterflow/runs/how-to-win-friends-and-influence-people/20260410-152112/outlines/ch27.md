# Chapter 27 Outline

## Promise

Praising real small gains can make improvement feel possible and turn effort into momentum.

## Chapter Moves

1. Reframe praise as reinforcement of progress rather than flattery.
2. Explain why small gains change a person's sense of what is possible.
3. Distinguish real praise from empty positivity.
4. Show how noticed progress can produce momentum for continued effort.
5. Bridge forward to Chapter 28 on giving people a good name to live up to.

## Guardrails

- avoid generic positivity slogans
- keep praise tied to visible progress
- preserve credibility by refusing false encouragement
