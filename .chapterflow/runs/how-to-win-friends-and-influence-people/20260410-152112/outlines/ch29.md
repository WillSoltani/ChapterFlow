# Chapter 29 Outline

## Promise

When a fault feels workable instead of crushing, people can stay engaged long enough to repair it.

## Chapter Moves

1. Reframe correction as something repairable rather than hopeless.
2. Explain why manageable framing preserves effort and hope.
3. Distinguish realistic ease from false minimization.
4. Show how discouragement can stall or collapse improvement.
5. Bridge forward to Chapter 30 on making people glad to do what you want.

## Guardrails

- do not erase the effort or cost of change
- keep hope grounded in reality
- avoid pretending hard correction is effortless
