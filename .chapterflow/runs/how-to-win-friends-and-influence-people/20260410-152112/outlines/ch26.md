# Chapter 26 Outline

## Promise

Letting the other person save face preserves dignity and keeps cooperation possible even when correction or loss is deserved.

## Chapter Moves

1. Reframe correction as a dignity-and-cooperation problem.
2. Explain why humiliation poisons future cooperation.
3. Distinguish saving face from excusing failure or removing accountability.
4. Show how standards and dignity can coexist.
5. Bridge forward to Chapter 27 on turning improvement into momentum through praise.

## Guardrails

- do not confuse face-saving with avoiding consequences
- keep accountability visible
- preserve dignity as a practical necessity, not soft evasion
