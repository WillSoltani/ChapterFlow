# Chapter 21 Outline

## Promise

When softer methods fail, a clean challenge can sometimes move people by activating pride, standards, or the desire to prove themselves.

## Chapter Moves

1. Frame challenge as an escalation, not a starting habit.
2. Explain why some people move when their pride or standard is called forward.
3. Distinguish bounded challenge from insult, humiliation, or contempt.
4. Show the risk: challenge can harden resistance if it becomes abusive.
5. Bridge forward to Chapter 22, where correction must begin without arousing resentment.

## Guardrails

- do not romanticize aggression
- keep the challenge tied to standards, not humiliation
- preserve the last-resort logic throughout
