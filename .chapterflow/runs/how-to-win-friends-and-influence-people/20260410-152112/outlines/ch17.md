# Chapter 17 Outline

## Promise

The other person's position becomes easier to work with when you ask what they think they are protecting or pursuing instead of reacting only to the surface argument.

## Chapter Moves

1. Reframe perspective-taking as motive-reading.
2. Ask what the person believes they are protecting.
3. Ask what the person believes they are pursuing.
4. Draw the limit: understanding the motive is not the same as approving it.
5. Bridge forward to sympathy for ideas and desires in Chapter 18.

## Guardrails

- do not reduce the chapter to vague empathy language
- keep the formula operational and specific
- preserve the line between legibility and agreement
