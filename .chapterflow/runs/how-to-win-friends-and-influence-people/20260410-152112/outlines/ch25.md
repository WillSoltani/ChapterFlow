# Chapter 25 Outline

## Promise

Questions often create more dignity and buy-in than direct orders because they preserve agency and invite participation.

## Chapter Moves

1. Reframe orders as an agency and dignity problem.
2. Explain why questions create ownership and cooperation.
3. Distinguish real inquiry from fake rhetorical control.
4. Keep the authority trade-off visible: some moments still need speed and clear direction.
5. Bridge forward to Chapter 26 on letting the other person save face.

## Guardrails

- do not pretend questions solve every urgent situation
- keep leadership and clarity visible
- preserve the difference between genuine inquiry and disguised commands
