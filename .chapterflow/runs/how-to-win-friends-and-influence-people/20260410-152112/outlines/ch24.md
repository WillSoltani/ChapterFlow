# Chapter 24 Outline

## Promise

Admitting your own mistakes first can change correction from attack into shared fallibility and make feedback easier to hear.

## Chapter Moves

1. Reframe correction as a superiority-pressure problem.
2. Explain why admitting your own failures lowers defensiveness.
3. Distinguish functional humility from confessional filler.
4. Show that the real issue still has to remain visible.
5. Bridge forward to Chapter 25 on questions instead of orders.

## Guardrails

- keep self-disclosure brief and relevant
- do not let humility become performance
- preserve the clarity of the correction itself
