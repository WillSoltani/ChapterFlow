# Chapter 28 Outline

## Promise

Giving someone a credible good name can help them act in ways that match it.

## Chapter Moves

1. Reframe reputation as a scaffold for behavior.
2. Explain why people often try to live up to an offered identity.
3. Distinguish constructive reputation from fake branding or manipulation.
4. Show how a good name can stabilize improvement already underway.
5. Bridge forward to Chapter 29 on making faults seem easier to correct.

## Guardrails

- keep the reputation effect concrete rather than mystical
- avoid fake labeling detached from evidence
- preserve the manipulation risk if the identity offer is dishonest
