# Chapter 20 Outline

## Promise

An idea moves more strongly when it is dramatized vividly enough to be felt, as long as the dramatization stays honest.

## Chapter Moves

1. Reframe persuasion as an issue of felt vividness.
2. Explain why plain explanation can stay too abstract.
3. Show how dramatization makes an idea perceptible and memorable.
4. Draw the limit: vividness is not hype, inflation, or deception.
5. Bridge forward to challenge as a last-resort move.

## Guardrails

- do not turn the chapter into advertising hype
- keep dramatization tied to truthful presentation
- preserve the difference between vividness and fabrication
