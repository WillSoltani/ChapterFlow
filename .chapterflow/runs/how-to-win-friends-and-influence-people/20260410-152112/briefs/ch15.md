# Chapter 15 Brief

- title: The Safety Valve in Handling Complaints
- section: Part Three: How to Win People to Your Way of Thinking
- core claim: complaints lose some of their pressure when people are allowed to air them before they are interrupted, corrected, or argued down
- bridge from prior chapter: Chapter 14 showed that early agreement can shape momentum; this chapter explains what to do when momentum is blocked by grievance pressure
- bridge to next chapter: once pressure is released, cooperation has a better chance to form
- moral complexity: medium

## Required Emphases

- keep the valve metaphor concrete: release pressure before trying to redirect the exchange
- distinguish hearing from endorsing
- show that premature correction can intensify the complaint
- avoid generic "just let them vent" framing
