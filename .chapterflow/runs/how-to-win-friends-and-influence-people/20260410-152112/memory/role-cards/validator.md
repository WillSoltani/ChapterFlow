# Validator Role Card

- Fix mechanical issues directly, but do not silently rewrite major prose to force a pass.
- Validate schema, depth contracts, example rotation, quiz schema, wrapper shape, and reading surfaces.
- Fail immediately on empty quiz output, plain-string scenarios, identical tone objects, contamination leakage, or source-splice leakage.
- Write the validated chapter, full one-chapter review wrapper, reading metrics, and truthful validation report only after the chapter gate actually passes.
