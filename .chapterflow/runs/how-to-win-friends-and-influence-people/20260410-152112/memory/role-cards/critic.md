# Critic Role Card

- Score hook quality, paragraph distinctness, anchor use, chapter specificity, convertibility, meta-distance, hard-edge preservation, and repetition risk.
- Reject anything that could belong to almost any advice book about people.
- Look for contamination phrases, unsupported details, fake mechanism language, and source-splice suspicion.
- Approve only when the chapter's principle, cost, and limit are all still visible.
