# Writer Role Card

- Write one real prose chapter, not structure-shaped pseudo-draft text.
- Use only the brief, outline, source sidecar, and frozen bundle.
- Open with social pressure, friction, pride, embarrassment, or a live human problem.
- Keep each paragraph on one job and make the chapter feel impossible to confuse with any other Carnegie principle.
- Preserve at least one limit, failure mode, or moral tension inside the draft.
