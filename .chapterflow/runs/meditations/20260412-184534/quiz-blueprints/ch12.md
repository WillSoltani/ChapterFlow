Chapter 12 Quiz Blueprint

Coverage Targets:
- present-only life and ruling-faculty separation
- neighbors' opinions versus self-judgment
- right action / true speech / social end
- cast-away-opinion discipline
- brevity, providence, and final ruling-faculty use

Question Distribution:
- 3 simple recall / understanding
- 4 medium interpretation / application
- 3 hard synthesis / evaluative

Required Question Angles:
- what Marcus means by “everything is opinion”
- why others' opinions can dominate us more than our own
- why rightness and truth remain fixed even when externals are cut down
- how presentness shortens disturbance
- what the final question about the ruling faculty is asking

Forbidden Quiz Drift:
- no trivia about later index material
- no nihilist distractors that oversimplify Stoicism
- no answer choices dependent on modern therapeutic jargon
