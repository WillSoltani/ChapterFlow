# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter: Book VI
Core concepts to test:
- governing reason and duty under all conditions
- looking within and stripping appearances
- rational soul fitted for political and social life
- correction without anger and without imitation of vice
- common-good scale through the swarm and the bee

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: evaluate

Question plan:
- q01: meaning of duty under praise, blame, fatigue, and death
- q02: why Marcus strips admired things down to raw substance
- q03: work scenario about duty under discomfort
- q04: practical meaning of not becoming like the wrongdoer
- q05: personal scenario about resentment and imitation
- q06: school scenario about civic usefulness versus image management
- q07: why the rational soul fitted for political life ranks above fame
- q08: meaning of the Caesar warning
- q09: deeper judgment about what is good for the swarm and the bee
- q10: connect Book VI's wider civic and cosmic order to Book VII's coming focus on judgment and pain

Distractor notes:
- confuse world citizenship with people-pleasing
- confuse appearance stripping with disgust or nihilism
- confuse correction without anger with tolerance of wrongdoing

Explanation diversity notes:
- avoid repeated openers
- keep explanations tied to reason, duty, public usefulness, or common-good scale

CorrectIndex plan:
- keep distribution roughly balanced across 0 / 1 / 2
