Chapter 9 Quiz Blueprint

Coverage Targets:
- injustice and falsehood as impiety against universal nature
- present opinion / present conduct / present contentment sufficiency
- one intelligent soul and rational creatures made for one another
- ruling faculty, opinions, and trouble-casting
- anti-anger logic, mildness, and mortality scale

Question Distribution:
- 3 simple recall / understanding
- 4 medium interpretation / application
- 3 hard synthesis / evaluative

Required Question Angles:
- why injustice and lying count as violations of shared reality
- what Marcus means by saying trouble is within opinions
- how social duty survives without rage
- why mildness is stronger than anger in this chapter
- how mortality and ephemerality shrink grievance

Forbidden Quiz Drift:
- no trivia on Roman names or commentators
- no purely theological questions detached from ethics
- no answer choices that depend on unstated modern psychology
