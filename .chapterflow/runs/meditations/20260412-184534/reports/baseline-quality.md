# Baseline Quality

## Chapters Used

- Chapter 1: Book I
- Chapter 2: Book II

## Shared Floor

- The prose remains unmistakably chapter-specific instead of drifting into generic Stoic slogans.
- Easy stays concrete and accessible while medium and hard add real mechanism and tension.
- Competitive tone stays severe without becoming theatrical.
- Support surfaces keep the governing distinction alive instead of replaying the breakdown in smaller words.

## Chapter 1 Strengths

- Strong inherited-standard frame with anti-display seriousness.
- Gratitude works as moral accounting rather than sentiment.
- Clear bridge from admired examples to unproven conduct.

## Chapter 2 Strengths

- Strong dawn-pressure frame with clean social friction.
- Cooperation remains disciplined rather than soft.
- Mortality sharpens purpose without collapsing into panic.

## Quality Floor For Later Waves

- Later chapters must keep this level of chapter specificity, tension, and tone discipline.
- Do not let later Stoic books flatten into interchangeable calmness advice.
- Preserve one central governing distinction per chapter and keep examples attached to that distinction.
