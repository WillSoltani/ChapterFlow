Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The chapter distinguishes external irritation from inner corruption. Other people can obstruct comfort, but judgment decides whether they rule the soul.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Cooperation is required, but cooperation does not erase the ugliness of vice or the exhaustion of dealing with it. Hard depth must keep that tension alive.
