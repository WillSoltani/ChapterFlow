# Style Memory

- Teach directly. Do not narrate the chapter.
- Open from pressure, conflict, mortality, or an examined choice, not thesis-first summary.
- Keep paragraph jobs distinct: anchor, mechanism, tension, limit, implication, bridge.
- Easy must stay accessible and concrete. Medium must explain mechanism. Hard must add contradiction, limit, cost, or threshold.
- Competitive tone should sound severe and credible, not theatrical.
- No em dashes, fake profundity, pseudo-science, or contamination phrases.
- Reinforcement surfaces must do different jobs instead of replaying the same sentence.
- For Marcus Aurelius, keep Stoic claims attached to lived pressure, duty, mortality, self-command, and social obligation rather than floating abstractions.
