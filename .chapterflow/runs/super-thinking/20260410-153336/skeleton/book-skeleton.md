## Rotation Plan For Examples
- Rotate work, school, and personal contexts in every chapter.
- Avoid defaulting all strategy models to office politics.
- Use family, class, team, budgeting, and negotiation settings where the chapter pressure fits.

## School-Setting Plan
- Chapter 1: assignment design, exam prep, science-fair testing
- Chapter 2: project failure modes, group deadlines
- Chapter 3: study scheduling, attention allocation
- Chapter 4: classroom dynamics, campus networks
- Chapter 5: survey results, grading statistics
- Chapter 6: college choice, major choice, activity tradeoffs
- Chapter 7: debate teams, roommate disputes, student leadership

## Vocabulary Watchlist
- Avoid jargon drift, pseudo-science, and abstract filler.
- Watch reusable stock tails around mindset, consistency, leverage, and strategic thinking.
- Keep statistical chapters from becoming textbook recaps.

## Motif / Callback Opportunities
- Return to uncertainty vs certainty from Chapter 1 in later statistics and decision chapters.
- Reuse avoidable-error framing when later chapters discuss downside and conflict escalation.
- Let compounding and chain reaction concepts echo across time, nature, and network chapters.
