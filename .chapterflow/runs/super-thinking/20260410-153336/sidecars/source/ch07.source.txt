Chapter 7 title: Dealing With Conflict

Frozen authorized support:

1. Game Theory
- Supports treating conflict as strategic interaction rather than pure self-expression.
- Safe use: other people have incentives, responses, and counter-moves that change what counts as a good choice.
- Section tag: Playing The Game.

2. Authority
- Supports the role of status, credibility, and perceived legitimacy in shaping compliance or persuasion inside conflict.
- Safe use: conflict is not only about forceful argument; it is also about who is treated as worth listening to.
- Section tag: Nudge Nudge Wink Wink.

3. Loss Leader Strategy
- Supports the idea that not every apparent contest should be won directly and that giving ground in one place can change the larger strategic position.
- Safe use: sometimes the best move in conflict is to avoid the wrong fight, redirect the exchange, or win on a broader timescale.
- Section tag: The Only Winning Move Is To Not Play.

Authorized factual boundaries for Chapter 7:
- The chapter is about conflict, persuasion, and adversarial/social strategy under interaction.
- It can safely frame conflict as a situation where other people respond, incentives matter, and authority changes how moves land.
- It can safely teach that some conflicts are better redirected, reframed, or declined than fought head-on.
- Stay narrower rather than speculative on formal game-theory math, military doctrine, political theory, or negotiation systems not directly supported by the frozen preview family.
- Do not import unsupported named frameworks, payoff matrices, or broad behavioral claims as if they came from the frozen support.
