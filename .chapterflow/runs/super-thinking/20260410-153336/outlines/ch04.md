# Chapter Outline

## Hook
Open on a system that feels stuck until one threshold passes and the whole pattern starts moving differently.

## Build
1. Introduce natural selection as adaptation through fit.
2. Use flywheel to explain heavy starts and easier continuation.
3. Move to tipping point and network effects as acceleration mechanisms.

## Deliver
1. Show why some systems resist brute force and reward alignment instead.
2. Explain how early effort can prepare a later payoff that looks sudden.
3. Use tipping point and network effects to show chain reactions.
4. Land on the chapter's practical move:
   - read the environment
   - identify the threshold
   - look for reinforcing participation

## Bridge
Close by setting up later chapters that read systems more analytically rather than through growth and adaptation alone.

## Threshold Question For Hard
When should you respect a system's natural dynamics, and when does that become an excuse for passivity?
