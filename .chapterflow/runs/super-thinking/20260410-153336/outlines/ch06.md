# Chapter Outline

## Hook
Open on a choice that feels overwhelming until the reader sees it split into branches that can actually be inspected.

## Build
1. Introduce decision tree as the chapter's way of taming complexity.
2. Show why vague intuition struggles when multiple outcomes stay bundled together.
3. Use branching to make consequences visible enough to compare.

## Deliver
1. Teach the reader to separate options into paths and outcomes.
2. Show how decomposition reduces confusion without pretending uncertainty disappears.
3. Land on the chapter's practical move:
   - name the main branches
   - inspect likely consequences
   - compare paths before committing

## Bridge
Close by setting up the conflict chapter as the next step where other people, incentives, and strategic interaction complicate choice further.

## Threshold Question For Hard
When does structured decomposition sharpen judgment, and when does it become fake precision that hides what you still do not know?
