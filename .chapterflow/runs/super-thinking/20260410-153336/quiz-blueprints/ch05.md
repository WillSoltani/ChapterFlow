# Quiz Blueprint

- chapterId: ch05
- chapterTitle: Lies, Damned Lies, and Statistics

## Core skills to test
1. Recognizing why base rates matter before vivid examples
2. Spotting missing context in number-driven claims
3. Distinguishing useful skepticism from cynical dismissal
4. Applying context-first reading to everyday evidence

## Question distribution targets
- q01-q03: base-rate recall and recognition
- q04-q06: context and framing application
- q07-q08: everyday statistical-misread scenarios
- q09-q10: deeper synthesis and bridge to decision-making

## Scenario pool
- Work: a dashboard metric or conversion claim without comparison context
- School: survey or grade-distribution claims read too quickly
- Personal: a health or risk story that sounds decisive but lacks background frequency

## Distractor rules
- Tempt the reader with vivid single-case thinking
- Use context-free numbers as the misleading option
- Avoid unsupported formulas or fake data
