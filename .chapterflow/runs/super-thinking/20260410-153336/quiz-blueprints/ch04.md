# Quiz Blueprint

- chapterId: ch04
- chapterTitle: Becoming One with Nature

## Core skills to test
1. Recognizing adaptation through fit rather than force
2. Applying flywheel logic to hard starts and later momentum
3. Spotting threshold and tipping-point conditions
4. Identifying when network effects reinforce growth

## Question distribution targets
- q01-q03: adaptation and momentum recall
- q04-q06: threshold and network-effect application
- q07-q08: chain-reaction scenarios
- q09-q10: deeper synthesis and chapter bridge

## Scenario pool
- Work: a product or practice that only becomes easier after a threshold
- School: group participation that improves as more students engage
- Personal: habit momentum versus forcing an unsustainable routine

## Distractor rules
- Tempt the reader with linear thinking
- Use force-without-fit as the misleading option
- Avoid unsupported biological or startup lore
