# Quiz Blueprint

- chapterId: ch06
- chapterTitle: Decisions, Decisions

## Core skills to test
1. Recognizing why decision trees reduce confusion in complex choices
2. Splitting one big choice into branches and consequences
3. Applying decomposition to uncertainty without pretending certainty exists
4. Distinguishing structured judgment from vague intuition

## Question distribution targets
- q01-q03: decision-tree recall and recognition
- q04-q06: branch-and-outcome application
- q07-q08: messy-choice decomposition scenarios
- q09-q10: deeper synthesis and bridge to strategic interaction

## Scenario pool
- Work: choosing between project paths with different downstream consequences
- School: selecting courses or commitments with branching tradeoffs
- Personal: a multi-option life choice that feels simpler once decomposed

## Distractor rules
- Tempt the reader with all-at-once vague feeling as the misleading option
- Use fake precision as the other misleading option
- Avoid unsupported formulas or invented probability math
