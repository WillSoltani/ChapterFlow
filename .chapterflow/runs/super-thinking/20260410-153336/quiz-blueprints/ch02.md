# Quiz Blueprint

- chapterId: ch02
- chapterTitle: Anything That Can Go Wrong, Will

## Core skills to test
1. Recognizing Murphy's Law as a preparedness rule instead of fatalism
2. Identifying when incentives distort behavior away from the real goal
3. Distinguishing cobra effect from a merely imperfect outcome
4. Spotting short-term wins that undermine long-term strength
5. Extending local unintended-consequence logic to system-level failure

## Question distribution targets
- q01-q02: chapter frame and anchor recall
- q03-q05: incentives and cobra-effect application
- q06-q08: short-termism and preparedness scenarios
- q09-q10: system-level synthesis and chapter bridge

## Scenario pool
- Work: a team gets rewarded for a visible metric and starts gaming it
- School: a grading or attendance target changes student and teacher behavior
- Personal: someone fixes a problem in a way that creates a bigger one
- Civic/business: a short-term financial decision weakens a system later

## Distractor rules
- Tempt the reader with stated intentions instead of actual incentives
- Use plausible short-term wins that ignore downstream cost
- Avoid joke answers and fake villains
- Wrong answers should fail because they confuse goals with incentives, preparation with pessimism, or speed with robustness
