# Quiz Blueprint

- chapterId: ch03
- chapterTitle: Spend Your Time Wisely

## Core skills to test
1. Recognizing compounding in repeated small decisions
2. Applying opportunity cost to overcommitment
3. Spotting present bias in procrastination and short-term relief
4. Synthesizing growth, tradeoff, and temptation into one decision rule

## Question distribution targets
- q01-q03: compounding and tradeoff recall
- q04-q06: opportunity cost and present-bias application
- q07-q08: design-against-temptation scenarios
- q09-q10: deeper synthesis and chapter bridge

## Scenario pool
- Work: repeated small investments in skill or process
- School: choosing one deep priority over scattered commitments
- Personal: gym, savings, or habit consistency versus immediate comfort

## Distractor rules
- Tempt the reader with doing everything at once
- Use immediate relief as the misleading attractive option
- Avoid joke answers and unsupported productivity dogma
