# Quiz Blueprint

- chapterId: ch07
- chapterTitle: Dealing With Conflict

## Core skills to test
1. Recognizing conflict as responsive interaction rather than isolated expression
2. Identifying how incentives and counter-moves change a conflict
3. Understanding why authority changes how pressure or persuasion lands
4. Applying the chapter's rule about redirecting or refusing the wrong fight

## Question distribution targets
- q01-q03: conflict-as-interaction recall and recognition
- q04-q06: authority and response-loop application
- q07-q08: redirect-or-refuse scenarios
- q09-q10: deeper synthesis about strategic awareness versus manipulation

## Scenario pool
- Work: team dispute where direct escalation keeps strengthening the wrong game
- School: leadership or roommate conflict shaped by status and perceived legitimacy
- Personal: argument where walking away or reframing changes the larger outcome

## Distractor rules
- Tempt the reader with brute-force escalation as the misleading option
- Use abstract cleverness without incentive awareness as the other misleading option
- Avoid unsupported formal game-theory jargon or payoff math
