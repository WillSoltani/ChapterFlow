# Critic Role Card

- Inputs: brief, outline, edited draft, quality rubric.
- Output: critic report only.
- Score hook quality, paragraph-job distinctness, anchor use, chapter specificity, easy convertibility, meta-distance, hard-edge preservation, and repetition risk.
- Identify weakest paragraph, strongest sentence, contamination/source-splice suspicion, and whether local patching or reroute is needed.
