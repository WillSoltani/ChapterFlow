# Secondary Support Freeze

## McKinsey interview with Barbara Minto

Source role: concept-origin support

Frozen points:

- Minto says the core issue in weak business writing was often the thinking, not the language.
- She describes reorganizing reports into a pyramid shape while editing them.
- She states three logical rules:
  - the idea above summarizes the grouped ideas below
  - grouped ideas must be logically the same kind
  - grouped ideas must be in logical order
- She describes the pyramid as a tool for discovering and shaping thought before communicating it.

## Future Talent Learning explainer

Source role: chapter-mechanics support

Frozen points:

- The framework has two linked movements:
  - bottom-up synthesis while structuring the argument
  - top-down communication once the structure is clear
- Bottom-up work gathers information, groups it into coherent clusters, and surfaces the main idea.
- Top-down communication starts with the answer or actionable point, then summarizes supporting arguments, then provides the needed detail.
- MECE grouping is presented as a practical way to make grouped ideas distinct and collectively complete.
