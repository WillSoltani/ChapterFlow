## Chapter Order And Intent

1. Chapter 1: Why a pyramid structure
   Intent: establish why idea-first structure beats data-dump communication and why readers need the answer before the evidence.
   Source richness: medium-high
   Concept density: medium
   Moral complexity: low
   Thin-risk: low

2. Chapter 2: The substructures within the pyramid
   Intent: explain the internal logic that makes grouped ideas cohere and support a single higher-order point.
   Source richness: medium
   Concept density: medium-high
   Moral complexity: low
   Thin-risk: medium

3. Chapter 3: How to build a pyramid structure
   Intent: move from principle to construction method, including bottom-up grouping and beginner pitfalls.
   Source richness: medium-high
   Concept density: high
   Moral complexity: low
   Thin-risk: medium

4. Chapter 4: Fine points of introductions
   Intent: show how situation-complication-question framing sets up the top-down answer cleanly.
   Source richness: high
   Concept density: high
   Moral complexity: low
   Thin-risk: low

5. Chapter 5: Deduction and induction: the difference
   Intent: separate two forms of reasoning so writers know which pattern they are using and why it matters.
   Source richness: medium-high
   Concept density: high
   Moral complexity: low
   Thin-risk: low

6. Chapter 6: How to highlight the structure
   Intent: translate invisible logic into visible document signals such as numbering, indentation, and emphasis.
   Source richness: medium
   Concept density: medium
   Moral complexity: low
   Thin-risk: medium

7. Chapter 7: Questioning the order of a grouping
   Intent: test whether grouped ideas are actually in the right order and category rather than just adjacent.
   Source richness: medium-high
   Concept density: high
   Moral complexity: medium
   Thin-risk: medium

8. Chapter 8: Questioning the problem-solving process
   Intent: connect pyramid logic to analytical problem solving, diagnosis, and logic-tree thinking.
   Source richness: high
   Concept density: high
   Moral complexity: medium
   Thin-risk: low

9. Chapter 9: Questioning the summary statement
   Intent: show how to state a synthesis precisely enough that it earns the summary role instead of sounding vague.
   Source richness: medium-high
   Concept density: high
   Moral complexity: medium
   Thin-risk: medium

10. Chapter 10: Putting it into readable words
   Intent: convert the underlying idea structure into prose that people can scan, follow, and remember.
   Source richness: medium
   Concept density: medium
   Moral complexity: low
   Thin-risk: medium

Appendix: Problem Solving in Structureless Situations
Intent: extend the framework into messier contexts where the structure must be discovered before it can be stated.
