# Baseline Quality

Chapters compared: 1 and 2

## Shared floor

- both chapters open from live pressure rather than thesis summary
- both stay anchored to frozen source material and avoid speculative expansion
- both keep one governing mechanism visible from breakdown through reinforcement surfaces
- both maintain functional tone differences instead of adjective-swapped variants
- both preserve a real hard-depth tension rather than stretching medium prose

## Chapter 1 strengths

- strongest on map-versus-territory specificity
- strongest use of multiple anchors doing real analytical work
- clean operational bridge from abstraction to distortion

## Chapter 2 strengths

- strongest on boundary language and transfer-failure pressure
- sharper personal and strategic consequence around overreach
- clear tension between honest growth and ego-driven pretense

## Quality floor for later waves

- opening paragraph must create pressure before explanation
- at least two load-bearing anchors where support exists
- one non-generic mechanism per chapter must stay visible across all surfaces
- hard depth must preserve a live unresolved limit or threshold question
- implementation surfaces must remain chapter-specific and anti-template

## Watch items for later waves

- thin-support chapters must stay narrower than these two baseline chapters
- supporting-idea chapters must avoid padded abstraction when frozen source support is thinner
