Concept density: medium

Source richness: low

Source richness: high

Source richness: high

Source richness: low

10. Inversion

11. Occam's Razor

Source richness: medium

12. Hanlon's Razor

Source richness: medium
Concept density: medium

## Rotation Plan For Examples

- Start work-heavy for ch01 and ch02 because the models map easily onto resumes, reports, dashboards, and authority claims.

- Reserve high-stakes strategic work examples for second-order thinking, probabilistic thinking, and inversion.
