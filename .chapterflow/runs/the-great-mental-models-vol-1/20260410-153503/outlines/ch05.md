By the end, the reader should understand how to use structured imagination to test assumptions and consequences before reality makes the lesson expensive.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
The key distinction is between disciplined simulation that strips away noise to test implications and free-floating fantasy that loses contact with evidence.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Hard depth must keep the tension that thought experiments are powerful because they simplify reality, while that same simplification can mislead if the wrong variables are stripped out.

Threshold Question:
How do you simplify enough to see the principle without simplifying so much that the simulation stops resembling the real problem?
