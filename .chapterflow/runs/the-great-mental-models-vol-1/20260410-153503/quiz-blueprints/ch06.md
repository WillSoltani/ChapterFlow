# Chapter Quiz Blueprint

Chapter: Necessity and Sufficiency
Core concepts to test:
- necessary condition means required, not guaranteed
- sufficient condition means enough, not exclusive
- confusion between the two creates reasoning errors
- practical use in plans, credentials, and explanations
- bridge to cleaner consequence reasoning

Bloom ceiling:
- highest justified level for this chapter: evaluate

Question plan:
- q01: basic recall on necessary versus sufficient
- q02: identify a necessary-but-not-sufficient condition
- q03: identify a sufficient-but-not-necessarily-required condition
- q04: named work scenario on confusing approval with a complete plan
- q05: school transfer on study habits and grade outcomes
- q06: common trap of treating one successful route as the only route
- q07: personal dialogue transfer
- q08: scenario judgment on an applicant over-focusing on one credential
- q09: deeper synthesis on why the distinction sharpens diagnosis
- q10: bridge to second-order thinking

Distractor notes:
- tempting wrong answer type 1: equate required with guaranteed
- tempting wrong answer type 2: equate enough with only possible path

Explanation diversity notes:
- avoid repeated openers
- each direct explanation must begin differently

CorrectIndex plan:
- keep distribution roughly balanced across 0 / 1 / 2
