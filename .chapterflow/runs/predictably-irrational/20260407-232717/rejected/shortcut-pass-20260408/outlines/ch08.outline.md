## Core Moves
- Ariely's main mechanism is that people waste effort preserving options long after those options stop serving their real goals.
- Ariely makes the claim visible through the shrinking-door game and examples of option hoarding that sacrifice progress for imagined flexibility.
- The chapter's warning is that fear of closing doors creates distraction, switching, and abandonment of the path that matters most.
- Ariely's practical response is to identify the option that truly deserves commitment and stop paying to preserve every possibility.
- Its important limit is that the chapter is not against flexibility itself; it attacks compulsive option preservation.
