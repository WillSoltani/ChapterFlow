## Core Moves
- Ariely's main mechanism is that price can signal effectiveness so strongly that it changes felt results.
- Ariely makes the claim visible through the placebo study where a more expensive painkiller worked better than a discounted version.
- The chapter's warning is that people mistake price for proof and then feel the product through the signal.
- Ariely's practical response is to treat price as information to inspect, not as direct evidence of underlying power.
- Its important limit is that the chapter is not only about medicine; it is about price labels changing experience.
