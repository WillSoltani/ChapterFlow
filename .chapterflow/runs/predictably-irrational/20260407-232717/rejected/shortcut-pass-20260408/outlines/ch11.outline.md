## Opening
- The Context of Our Character, Part 1 says that ordinary people cheat a little when temptation is near and moral reminders are weak. Ariely uses the matrix-task experiments and the drop in cheating after honor-code or Ten Commandments reminders to prove that the pattern is not just intuition.

## Core Moves
- Ariely's main mechanism is that ordinary people cheat a little when temptation is near and moral reminders are weak.
- Ariely makes the claim visible through the matrix-task experiments and the drop in cheating after honor-code or Ten Commandments reminders.
- The chapter's warning is that people protect a self-image of honesty while still taking small dishonest gains.
- Ariely's practical response is to strengthen reminders, transparency, and friction before temptation appears.
- Its important limit is that the chapter is not that people are monsters; it is that context shapes how much honesty gets expressed.
