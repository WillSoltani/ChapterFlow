## Core Moves
- Ariely's main mechanism is that free is psychologically different from merely cheap.
- Ariely makes the claim visible through the truffle-versus-Kiss candy experiment and the pull of free shipping, trials, and bonus offers.
- The chapter's warning is that zero removes the felt downside and makes people ignore total value, future cost, and clutter.
- Ariely's practical response is to run the one-cent test and recalculate the full outcome before accepting the free option.
- Its important limit is that the chapter does not say every free offer is bad, only that free deserves more comparison, not less.
