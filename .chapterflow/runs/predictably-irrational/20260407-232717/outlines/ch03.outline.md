# Chapter Outline

## Opening
- Ariely starts with the chocolate experiment because it shows that free changes the kind of decision people think they are making. When both candies cost something, quality matters. When the cheaper option becomes free, the feeling of zero price overwhelms the small quality gap and flips many choices.

## Core Moves
- Zero price creates an emotional bonus that ordinary discounts do not.
- The one-cent change matters because it removes the felt possibility of loss.
- Free shipping, bonus items, and trial offers exploit the same distortion.
- The hidden costs are opportunity cost, extra clutter, future lock-in, and wasted attention.
- The practical defense is to restore comparison by asking what you would do if the price were one cent higher.
