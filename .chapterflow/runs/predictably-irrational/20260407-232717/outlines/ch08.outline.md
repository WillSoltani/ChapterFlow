# Chapter Outline

## Opening
- Ariely uses disappearing doors because they reveal how strongly people work to preserve options they are barely using.

## Core Moves
- Imminent closure makes options feel more valuable than they really are.
- Maintaining multiple doors can cost focus and performance.
- The pain of closing an option can exceed the value the option was actually delivering.
- Option-hoarding can become a hidden tax on commitment and attention.
- The practical lesson is to shut low-value doors deliberately so the chosen path can deepen.

## Reinforcement
- The Side Project Portfolio That Kept Stealing Focus From the Main Roadmap (decision_point, work)
- The Student Who Kept Too Many Clubs Alive to Lead Any of Them Well (postmortem, school)
- The Conversation About Why Every Weekend Plan Could Not Stay Open (dialogue, personal)

- The Course Schedule With One Too Many Backup Paths (dilemma, school)

## Exit
- If open doors become costly because possibility is overweighted, what happens when expectations change not just choice but the experience itself?
