# Chapter Outline

## Opening
- Ariely starts with the paper-deadline experiment because it shows that people often choose more flexibility than their future performance can safely handle.

## Core Moves
- Present bias makes near-term comfort overpower distant goals.
- Externally imposed intermediate deadlines outperform complete freedom.
- Self-imposed deadlines help somewhat because people understand their problem, but they are usually too weak.
- Freedom without structure can be a temptation, not a gift.
- The practical answer is to build binding milestones and consequences before delay starts compounding.
