# Chapter Outline

## Opening
- Ariely uses cheating experiments to show that many people cheat a little while still wanting to think of themselves as honest.

## Core Moves
- The chapter is about ordinary dishonesty, not obvious criminality.
- People balance gain against self-image and therefore often cheat by a little rather than by a lot.
- Moral reminders and honor-code cues can reduce cheating before the act.
- Context matters more than flattering stories about stable character admit.
- The practical lesson is to design honesty earlier in the process rather than trusting virtue after temptation appears.

## Exit
- If ordinary dishonesty is shaped by context and reminders, what happens when people become one step further removed from cash itself?
