- Objective: Ariely uses cheating experiments to show that ordinary people will often cheat a little when temptation is near, monitoring is weak, and rationalization is easy. The chapter is not about a small group of villains. It is about the tension between wanting benefit and wanting to keep a good self-image. Keep moral reminders, honor-code style prompts, and the small-cheating pattern central. The practical lesson is to strengthen honesty before temptation, not after. The limit is crucial: the chapter does not say people are simply bad. It says context can loosen honesty while self-concept keeps the cheating from growing without limit.

## Keep Visible
