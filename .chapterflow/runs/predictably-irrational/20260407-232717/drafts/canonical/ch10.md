# The Power of Price

Chapter 10 works because Ariely takes expectation one step closer to the body. Price is not only a number here. It can become a signal of potency. When people expect a higher-priced treatment or product to work better, the felt effect can change even if the underlying substance stays the same. That is why the chapter lives at an ethical edge. The same mechanism can comfort people, sell them, or mislead them. Ariely is not simply repeating the anchoring chapter. He is showing that price can become part of experienced effectiveness. The practical lesson is to inspect when the tag is doing more of the healing or persuading than the thing itself.

## Key Takeaway Card
- The chapter's key move is to treat price as a possible placebo signal, not just as a market fact.
