By the end, the reader should understand why the order in which information is seen can change judgment quality and why forensic science makes that visible.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Show that sequence is a process-design choice that frames later evidence rather than a superficial presentation detail.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must preserve that ordering information can alter judgment in consequential settings without claiming sequence alone solves the whole problem.

Threshold Question:
What changes when judgment is shaped not only by what information appears, but by the order in which it arrives?
