Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand why error inside judgment has to be made visible before noise can be analyzed seriously.

Opening Move:
Open from one judgment that feels debatable until the reader sees that a miss can still be inspected even without perfect certainty.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1 visible miss
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P2 defensible reading
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P3 bridge into noise analysis

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: anchor
- P2: mechanism

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Separate uncertainty that remains from error that still deserves measurement.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must preserve that measurement does not remove ambiguity, but ambiguity does not erase the duty to inspect error.

Threshold Question:
How can a judgment miss be measured seriously when the domain is not perfectly mechanical?
