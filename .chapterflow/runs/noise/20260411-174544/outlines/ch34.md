Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand the governance tradeoff between rules and standards when institutions try to reduce noise without losing judgment.

Opening Move:
Open from a system designer choosing between a tighter rule and a looser standard for the same class of decisions.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1 governance tradeoff
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P2 what rules gain and lose
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P3 what standards gain and lose

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: anchor
- P2: mechanism
- P3: distinction
- P4: implication
- P5: limit
- P6: bridge

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Show that rules and standards distribute consistency, flexibility, and human judgment differently.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must preserve that rules can reduce variation and that standards can preserve context, while neither option removes cost.

Threshold Question:
How much discretion should a system leave once noise becomes a serious concern?

- Takeaway 2: standards preserve context
- Takeaway 3: neither governance style is free
- Takeaway 4: institutions choose tradeoffs, not perfection
- Takeaway 5: bridge to taking noise seriously
