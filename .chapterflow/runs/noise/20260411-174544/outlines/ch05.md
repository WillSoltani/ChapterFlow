Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand why the mind can be treated as a measuring instrument whose reliability affects judgment quality.

Opening Move:
Open from one assessment that feels final until the reader realizes the reading came from an instrument that could have shifted.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): P1 instrument metaphor
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): P2 instrument shift
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P3 case-versus-judge distinction

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: anchor
- P2: mechanism
- P3: implication
- P4: tension
- P5: limit
- P6: bridge

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Separate trust in the case from trust in the instrument reading the case.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must preserve how a person should judge responsibly once they admit the instrument is not perfectly steady.

Threshold Question:
How should confidence change when the mind producing the answer is itself part of the uncertainty?

Takeaway Count Lock:
- Easy: 3 (always)
- Medium: 5
- Hard: 5

Hard Takeaway Topics:
- Takeaway 1: mind-as-instrument metaphor
- Takeaway 2: instability can live in the instrument
- Takeaway 3: reliability matters for trust in judgment
- Takeaway 4: humility without self-erasure
- Takeaway 5: bridge into what counts as judgment

Scenario Lesson Map:

Preview Constraint:

- slipping into anti-human rhetoric

- brief-language leak 1: threshold question wording in reader-facing prose
