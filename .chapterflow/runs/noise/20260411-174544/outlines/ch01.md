Chapter Promise:
By the end, the reader should understand that disagreement in professional judgment is often a hidden production problem, not harmless background variation.

Opening Move:
Open from the shock of an institution discovering that supposedly comparable judgments spread far wider than expected.

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Noise is not average directional bias. It is unwanted spread across judgments that should be closer.

Hard-Depth Tension:
The chapter must admit that not every disagreement is failure. The hard layer needs to ask where discretion becomes noise and what institutions lose when they flatten all judgment into rules.

Threshold Question:
When does variation signal humane discretion or real uncertainty, and when does it reveal a system that has stopped noticing its own inconsistency?
