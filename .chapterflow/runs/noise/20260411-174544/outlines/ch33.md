By the end, the reader should understand that noise reduction can carry a human cost when standardization starts to undermine dignity.

- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): P3 lower noise does not excuse degrading treatment

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Show that a system can become more consistent while becoming less humane in the experience it creates.

The chapter must preserve both the value of standardization and the claim that people can be diminished by rigid treatment.
