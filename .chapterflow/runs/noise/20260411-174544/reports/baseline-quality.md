# Baseline Quality

Baseline chapters:
- Chapter 1: `Finding Noise`
- Chapter 2: `Crime and Noisy Punishment`

Shared strengths
- Both chapters open from concrete institutional pressure instead of abstract thesis.
- Both preserve a live discretion boundary instead of collapsing into rigid anti-human conclusions.
- Both convert cleanly into chapter-specific support surfaces without schema drift after local patching.

Chapter 1 strength
- Best at making hidden variability visible through clean cross-domain framing.

Chapter 2 strength
- Best at making the moral and legitimacy cost of noise unavoidable.

Quality floor for later waves
- Keep Chapter 1's clarity of distinction between bias/noise and earned/unearned spread.
- Keep Chapter 2's moral seriousness without drifting into sermon or policy caricature.
- Preserve short, concrete openings and avoid generic system-talk unless the chapter actually widens to system structure.
