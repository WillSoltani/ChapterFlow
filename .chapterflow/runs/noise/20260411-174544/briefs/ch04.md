Core Claim:
After widening to noisy systems, the book narrows to singular decisions, where the problem of noise becomes harder to see because there is no obvious side-by-side lineup. The chapter's job is to show how unstable judgment still matters even when one case or one forecast arrives alone. It should make the reader feel the tension between the absence of direct comparison and the continued need for disciplined judgment.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It examines how noise operates when a decision does not arrive inside a visible batch or system comparison.
