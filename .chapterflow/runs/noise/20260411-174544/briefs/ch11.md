Core Claim:
After bringing the judges themselves into view, the book turns toward practical disciplines that help judgments become less biased and less noisy. The chapter's job is to introduce decision hygiene as a process-oriented response that works by improving how judgments are made rather than by trusting better intentions alone.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
It is the first chapter to present a named family of practical remedies, while keeping the distinction between bias reduction and noise reduction visible.

Author Logic Chain:
First the author moves from better judges toward methods that support better judging.
Then the author distinguishes debiasing from broader hygiene-oriented process design.
Then the author concludes that judgment quality improves through disciplined procedures, not only through personal effort.
