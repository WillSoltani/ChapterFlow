Core Claim:
After showing how guidelines can support professional judgment, the book narrows to the scale itself and asks how performance ratings are shaped by the structure used to express them. The chapter's job is to show that scales are not neutral containers: how they are defined can reduce some avoidable variation while still leaving judgment and tradeoffs in place.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
