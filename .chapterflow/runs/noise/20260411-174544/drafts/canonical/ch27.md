Once the matching operation is clearer, the book can narrow the frame again. The chapter turns to scales.

That move matters because judgments do not come out of raw cases alone. They also depend on the bands, levels, or ranges that receive the case.

This is where the chapter gets more specific. A poor scale can distort the judgment before anyone notices that the scale itself is part of the problem.

The chapter therefore treats scale design as part of judgment, not as neutral packaging. The structure of the scale helps shape what the judgment becomes.

That does not mean a scale turns judgment into certainty. A scale can guide or distort without becoming a perfect measuring device.

Once that is visible, the next move can follow the people using those scales. The book can turn to patterns.
