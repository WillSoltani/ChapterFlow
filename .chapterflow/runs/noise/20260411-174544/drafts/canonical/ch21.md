Once models are in view, the book can press harder on consistency. The next move is toward noiseless rules.

That move matters because rules promise a stronger answer to variation than ordinary discretionary judgment. If a rule removes room for individual drift, it can also reduce noise.

This is where the chapter has to stay balanced. A noiseless rule gains steadiness by narrowing discretion, but that gain is not free. What the rule wins in consistency, it can also lose in flexibility or responsiveness.

The chapter therefore does not treat rules as magic. It treats them as another response to variation, one that works by limiting judgment rather than refining it.

That is why the chapter belongs here. After judgments and models, the book can ask what happens when the system tries to suppress variation even more aggressively.

Once that tradeoff is visible, the next move can turn toward a harder limit. The book can ask what remains unknown even after structure improves.
