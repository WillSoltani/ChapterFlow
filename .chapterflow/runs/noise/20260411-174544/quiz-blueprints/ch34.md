Quiz Blueprint

Core checks:
- identify rules and standards as contrasting governance tools
- recognize that rules can reduce variation while standards preserve discretion
- reject the misreading that one governance form wins everywhere
- bridge from rules or standards into taking noise seriously

Question distribution:
- 3 easy understanding
- 5 medium apply/analyze
- 2 hard evaluate/bridge

Scenario coverage targets:
- hiring rubric versus principle example
- school cutoff versus discretionary standard example
- comparison of rule-heavy and standards-heavy systems
