Quiz Blueprint

Core checks:
- identify dignity as a human stake in judgment design
- recognize that standardization can reduce noise while still harming how people are treated
- reject the misreading that dignity cancels the value of structure
- bridge from dignity into rules or standards

Question distribution:
- 3 easy understanding
- 5 medium apply/analyze
- 2 hard evaluate/bridge

Scenario coverage targets:
- respectful versus scripted hearing example
- school placement meeting example
- comparison of consistent process versus humane treatment
