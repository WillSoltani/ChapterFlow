Quiz Blueprint

Core checks:
- identify that noise reduction can impose explicit costs and frictions
- recognize that tighter process is not free even when useful
- reject the misreading that costs automatically defeat reduction
- bridge from cost analysis into dignity

Question distribution:
- 3 easy understanding
- 5 medium apply/analyze
- 2 hard evaluate/bridge

Scenario coverage targets:
- extra-review burden example
- school friction example
- comparison of lower noise versus higher burden
