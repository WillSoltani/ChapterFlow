Quiz Blueprint

Core checks:
- identify patterns as recurring variation in judgments
- recognize stable personal differences as relevant to noise
- reject the misreading that repeated tendency explains everything
- bridge from patterns into the sources of noise

Question distribution:
- 3 easy understanding
- 5 medium apply/analyze
- 2 hard evaluate/bridge

Scenario coverage targets:
- recurring rating-style example
- school review-habit example
- comparison of two repeating judgment tendencies
