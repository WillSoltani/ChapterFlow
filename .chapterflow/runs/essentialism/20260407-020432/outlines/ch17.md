# Outline: Progress: The Power of Small Wins

1. Open with the chapter's central claim:
- small wins create momentum, and essentialists use minimum viable progress instead of waiting for dramatic breakthroughs

2. Diagnose the weak pattern:
- people aim so big, so fast, that they burn out before meaningful movement ever begins

3. Introduce the chapter's move:
- shrink the step, reward progress, and build confidence through repeated movement on meaningful work

4. Explain why it matters:
- without visible progress, motivation collapses and the essential task starts to feel heavier than it is

5. Keep the book-level bridge visible:
- the chapter gives execution a sustainable pace that compounds instead of flaring out

6. End with the live tension:
- small steps can feel unserious to ambitious people even though they are often the only steps that keep going
