# Outline: Select: The Power of Extreme Criteria

1. Open with the chapter's central claim:
- weak criteria produce too many yeses, while extreme criteria protect the few options that truly fit

2. Diagnose the weak pattern:
- people call options good enough too quickly and then wonder why their calendars fill with near-matches

3. Introduce the chapter's move:
- use minimum criteria and extreme criteria, including the 90 percent rule, to reject merely decent options

4. Explain why it matters:
- if standards stay soft, every attractive possibility gets mistaken for the essential one

5. Keep the book-level bridge visible:
- the chapter closes the explore section by turning discernment into a repeatable selection rule

6. End with the live tension:
- extreme criteria feel risky because they require saying no before certainty arrives
