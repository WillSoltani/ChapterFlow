# Outline: Discern: The Unimportance of Practically Everything

1. Open with the chapter's central claim:
- almost everything is noise and only a vital few options deserve concentrated effort

2. Diagnose the weak pattern:
- capable people keep treating many decent options as if they were equally important

3. Introduce the chapter's move:
- use the law of the vital few to rank sharply instead of spreading effort evenly

4. Explain why it matters:
- without discernment, energy gets diluted before any real trade-off is named

5. Keep the book-level bridge visible:
- this chapter makes Chapter 2's recovered agency useful by showing what to do with it

6. End with the live tension:
- discernment feels harsh because it rejects many good options before they become commitments
