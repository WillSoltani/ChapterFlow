# Outline: Sleep: Protect the Asset

1. Open with the chapter's central claim:
- sleep is not a luxury reward after important work; it is what protects the human asset required for important work

2. Diagnose the weak pattern:
- ambitious people often underinvest in themselves and damage the very instrument through which they contribute

3. Introduce the chapter's move:
- treat sleep as strategic protection for judgment, connection, and contribution

4. Explain why it matters:
- when the asset is depleted, discernment, creativity, and execution all decline together

5. Keep the book-level bridge visible:
- the chapter widens exploration from information and space to the condition of the person doing the choosing

6. End with the live tension:
- high-achievement cultures admire sacrifice even when the sacrifice quietly ruins performance
