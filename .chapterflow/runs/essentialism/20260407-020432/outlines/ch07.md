# Outline: Play: Embrace the Wisdom of Your Inner Child

1. Open with the chapter's central claim:
- play is not a childish extra; it expands options, lowers stress, and strengthens the mind used for discernment

2. Diagnose the weak pattern:
- adults often strip play out of life first, then wonder why creativity and resilience begin to collapse

3. Introduce the chapter's move:
- treat joyful exploration as essential fuel for better thinking rather than as wasted time

4. Explain why it matters:
- without play, exploration narrows and the mind becomes too rigid to notice better possibilities

5. Keep the book-level bridge visible:
- the chapter argues that better discernment depends on a mind that is not permanently clenched

6. End with the live tension:
- play sounds nonessential in achievement cultures, which is exactly why the chapter has to defend it so directly
