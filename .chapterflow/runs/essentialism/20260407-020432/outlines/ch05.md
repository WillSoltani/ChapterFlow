# Outline: Escape: The Perks of Being Unavailable

1. Open with the chapter's central claim:
- serious discernment needs protected space, and availability to everything destroys the conditions for thinking

2. Diagnose the weak pattern:
- people stay so reachable and reactive that they never create the silence needed to see what matters

3. Introduce the chapter's move:
- carve solitude, reading time, and reflective distance into ordinary life

4. Explain why it matters:
- without space, the loudest input wins by default and the essential remains hidden

5. Keep the book-level bridge visible:
- the chapter opens the explore section by defending unavailability as a tool for better selection

6. End with the live tension:
- space looks indulgent in busy systems even though it is what protects serious judgment
