# Outline: Edit: The Invisible Art

1. Open with the chapter's central claim:
- editing is not loss for loss's sake; it is a creative act that makes the important clearer, cleaner, and stronger

2. Diagnose the weak pattern:
- people keep treating subtraction as damage when it is often the reason meaning becomes visible

3. Introduce the chapter's move:
- cut, condense, and correct until the essential has room to stand out

4. Explain why it matters:
- without disciplined editing, life fills with clutter and the best work never gets enough space to breathe

5. Keep the book-level bridge visible:
- the chapter reframes elimination as an art rather than a punishment

6. End with the live tension:
- editing looks invisible precisely because success is often experienced as what remains, not what was removed
