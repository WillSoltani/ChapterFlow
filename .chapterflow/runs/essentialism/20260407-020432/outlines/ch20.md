# Outline: Be: The Essentialist Life

1. Open with the chapter's central claim:
- essentialism ultimately has to become an identity and way of life, not a periodic tactic added onto a crowded schedule

2. Diagnose the weak pattern:
- people treat essentialism like one more productivity move instead of a total shift in how life is chosen and lived

3. Introduce the chapter's move:
- become the kind of person who repeatedly asks what is essential and eliminates everything else

4. Explain why it matters:
- if essentialism stays occasional, the old default always returns and regret reclaims the calendar

5. Keep the book-level bridge visible:
- the closing chapter lifts the whole book from tactics into character, meaning, and life design

6. End with the live tension:
- a life organized around fewer things will often look strange to a culture that praises visible busyness
