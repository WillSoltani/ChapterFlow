# Outline: Flow: The Genius of Routine

1. Open with the chapter's central claim:
- routine is not the enemy of freedom here; it is the structure that makes the essential the default instead of a daily negotiation

2. Diagnose the weak pattern:
- without routine, nonessential distractions keep winning each time the person must choose from scratch

3. Introduce the chapter's move:
- design routines that remove friction and automate the essential path

4. Explain why it matters:
- if the default stays noisy, essential action requires unnecessary willpower every single day

5. Keep the book-level bridge visible:
- the chapter makes flow a systems question rather than a mood or accidental streak

6. End with the live tension:
- routine sounds dull to people who confuse novelty with freedom, even while chaos keeps draining their best work
