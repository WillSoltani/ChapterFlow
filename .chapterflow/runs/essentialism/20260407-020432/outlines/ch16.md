# Outline: Subtract: Bring Forth More by Removing Obstacles

1. Open with the chapter's central claim:
- essentialists make progress by removing the main constraint instead of blindly adding more effort everywhere

2. Diagnose the weak pattern:
- people keep pouring energy into the system without identifying the bottleneck that actually sets the pace

3. Introduce the chapter's move:
- find the slowest hiker and remove the obstacle that is holding the rest back

4. Explain why it matters:
- until the right obstacle moves, extra effort elsewhere mostly creates motion without throughput

5. Keep the book-level bridge visible:
- the chapter replaces heroic strain with targeted obstacle removal

6. End with the live tension:
- removing the main obstacle often means admitting that some impressive-looking work is actually the delay
