Chapter Promise:
Show that essentialism begins by reclaiming the act of choice and resisting the slow drift into helpless compliance.

Opening Move:
Open on the difference between having options on paper and actually exercising choice in real life.

Anchor Allocation:
- anchor 1 used in paragraph(s): 1-2 -> choice as action
- anchor 2 used in paragraph(s): 3-4 -> learned helplessness drift
- anchor 3 used in paragraph(s): 5-6 -> choosing to choose under constraint

Paragraph Job Map:
- P1: establish the mistaken view of choice as a thing
- P2: reframe choice as an action
- P3: explain how helplessness grows drip by drip
- P4: show what surrendered agency looks like in practice
- P5: recover the Essentialist discipline of choosing
- P6: preserve the cost question and bridge to discernment

Required Distinction or Mechanism:
Options are external; choosing is internal action.

Hard-Depth Tension:
Agency is real, but choices carry uneven costs and are not equally easy.

Threshold Question:
If every real choice carries costs, what does it mean to say choice is invincible without sounding dishonest?
