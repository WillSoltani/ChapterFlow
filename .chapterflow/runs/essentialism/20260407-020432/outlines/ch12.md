# Outline: Uncommit: Win Big by Cutting Your Losses

1. Open with the chapter's central claim:
- past investment is not a reason to keep funding the nonessential, and essentialists learn to cut sunk costs cleanly

2. Diagnose the weak pattern:
- people keep feeding dead commitments because walking away feels like admitting waste or failure

3. Introduce the chapter's move:
- use zero-based thinking, reverse pilots, and slower commitments to stop inertia from governing the future

4. Explain why it matters:
- until old commitments are reviewed honestly, the calendar remains owned by yesterday's bad decisions

5. Keep the book-level bridge visible:
- the chapter sharpens elimination by showing how to withdraw from work that no longer fits

6. End with the live tension:
- cutting losses hurts because it turns previous investment into a visible mistake
