Title: Sleep: Protect the Asset
Section Heading: Explore: How can we discern the trivial many from the vital few?

Objective
Write a chapter package that explains why sleep is not a luxury reward after important work; it is what protects the human asset required for important work.

Strict Intent
The chapter must show how ambitious people often underinvest in themselves and damage the very instrument through which they contribute. It must then guide the reader to treat sleep as strategic protection for judgment, connection, and contribution. Keep the stakes visible: when the asset is depleted, discernment, creativity, and execution all decline together.

Source Notes
- The best asset people have for contribution is themselves.
- Lack of sleep damages that asset, especially in successful people who keep pushing.
- Sleep improves exploration, connection-making, and the ability to do less but better.

Required Emphasis
- Skill: protecting personal capacity
- Bridge: the chapter widens exploration from information and space to the condition of the person doing the choosing
- Live tension: high-achievement cultures admire sacrifice even when the sacrifice quietly ruins performance
