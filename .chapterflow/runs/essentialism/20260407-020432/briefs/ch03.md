Book: Essentialism
Author: Greg McKeown
Chapter: 3
Title: Discern: The Unimportance of Practically Everything
Section Heading: Essence: What is the core mind-set of an Essentialist?

Objective
Write a chapter package that explains why almost everything is noise and only a vital few options deserve concentrated effort.

Strict Intent
The chapter must show how capable people keep treating many decent options as if they were equally important. It must then guide the reader to use the law of the vital few to rank sharply instead of spreading effort evenly. Keep the stakes visible: without discernment, energy gets diluted before any real trade-off is named.

Source Notes
- Joseph Moses Juran's law of the vital few explains why a small fraction of issues often drives most of the result.
- Many capable people stall their next level of contribution because they cannot release the idea that everything matters.
- Discernment starts with everyday choices, then expands to bigger areas of work and life.

Required Emphasis
- Skill: ranking by relative importance
- Bridge: this chapter makes Chapter 2's recovered agency useful by showing what to do with it
- Live tension: discernment feels harsh because it rejects many good options before they become commitments
