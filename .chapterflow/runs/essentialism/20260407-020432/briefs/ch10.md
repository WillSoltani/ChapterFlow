Book: Essentialism
Author: Greg McKeown
Chapter: 10
Title: Clarify: One Decision That Makes a Thousand
Section Heading: Eliminate: How can we cut out the trivial many?

Objective
Write a chapter package that explains why clarity of essential intent eliminates noise because one real decision governs hundreds of later ones.

Strict Intent
The chapter must show how teams and individuals drift into politics or scattered effort when the real purpose stays vague. It must then guide the reader to replace pretty clear direction with essential intent that is specific enough to organize behavior. Keep the stakes visible: without clear intent, people chase short-term wins that do not add up to meaningful progress.

Source Notes
- Lack of clarity creates politics or a leaderless 'it's all good' drift.
- Essential intent is both inspirational and concrete.
- A strong intent asks what one thing the group or person wants to be truly excellent at and how success will be recognized.

Required Emphasis
- Skill: defining essential intent
- Bridge: the eliminate section starts by naming the target precisely enough that later noes and cuts become easier
- Live tension: people love wordsmithing around goals because it feels safer than making a hard commitment
