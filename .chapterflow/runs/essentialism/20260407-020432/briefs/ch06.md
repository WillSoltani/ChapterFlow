Title: Look: See What Really Matters
Section Heading: Explore: How can we discern the trivial many from the vital few?

Objective
Write a chapter package that explains why essentialists do not inspect everything equally; they learn to scan the field and filter for what is actually significant.

Strict Intent
The chapter must show how too much information makes people feel informed while leaving them unable to distinguish signal from noise. It must then guide the reader to filter for the fascinating, journal selectively, and look for patterns instead of drowning in inputs. Keep the stakes visible: if attention is spent on every fact and opinion, the reader never sees the few clues that matter most.

Source Notes
- Discernment requires disciplined scanning and filtering of facts, options, and opinions.
- The journal practice matters because it captures patterns without turning the person into a stenographer of life.
- Writing less than you feel like writing sharpens attention to what is worth keeping.

Required Emphasis
- Skill: selective perception and signal detection
- Bridge: the chapter turns protected space into a method for seeing what is important inside a cluttered field
- Live tension: looking well requires saying no to most available information even when collecting it feels productive
