Title: Subtract: Bring Forth More by Removing Obstacles
Section Heading: Execute: How can we make doing the vital few things almost effortless?

Objective
Write a chapter package that explains why essentialists make progress by removing the main constraint instead of blindly adding more effort everywhere.

Strict Intent
The chapter must show how people keep pouring energy into the system without identifying the bottleneck that actually sets the pace. It must then guide the reader to find the slowest hiker and remove the obstacle that is holding the rest back. Keep the stakes visible: until the right obstacle moves, extra effort elsewhere mostly creates motion without throughput.

Source Notes
- Lao-tzu is invoked to frame wisdom as subtraction.
- Constraints are the obstacles holding the whole system back.
- The main question is which obstacle, if removed, would make the majority of the others matter less.

Required Emphasis
- Skill: constraint removal
- Bridge: the chapter replaces heroic strain with targeted obstacle removal
- Live tension: removing the main obstacle often means admitting that some impressive-looking work is actually the delay
