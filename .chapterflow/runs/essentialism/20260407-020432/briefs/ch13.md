Title: Edit: The Invisible Art
Section Heading: Eliminate: How can we cut out the trivial many?

Objective
Write a chapter package that explains why editing is not loss for loss's sake; it is a creative act that makes the important clearer, cleaner, and stronger.

Strict Intent
The chapter must show how people keep treating subtraction as damage when it is often the reason meaning becomes visible. It must then guide the reader to cut, condense, and correct until the essential has room to stand out. Keep the stakes visible: without disciplined editing, life fills with clutter and the best work never gets enough space to breathe.

Source Notes
- Disciplined editing increases contribution by creating more room for what matters.
- Editors ask whether the point is clear and whether it is being said as concisely as possible.
- The life version of editing includes cutting options, condensing effort, and correcting course.

Required Emphasis
- Skill: subtractive refinement
- Bridge: the chapter reframes elimination as an art rather than a punishment
- Live tension: editing looks invisible precisely because success is often experienced as what remains, not what was removed
