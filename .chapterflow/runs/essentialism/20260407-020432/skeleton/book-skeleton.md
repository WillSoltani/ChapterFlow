# Book Skeleton

## Metadata
- Book: Essentialism
- Author: Greg McKeown
- Locked edition: English Crown trade family (2014 core structure; 2020 paperback same family; 2024 anniversary adds intro and challenge extras)
- Numbered chapter range: 1-20
- Wave size: 2 by default
- Premium / solo routing candidates: ch10, ch11, ch14, ch18, ch19, ch20

## Rotation Plan For Examples
- Maintain the canonical six-format rotation once per chapter: decision_point, postmortem, dialogue, predict_reveal, dilemma, before_after.
- Maintain 2 work / 2 school / 2 personal examples in every chapter package.
- Rotate endings across broader_principle, self_directed_question, surprising_implication, cross_domain, common_trap, perspective_reframe.

## School-Setting Plan
- Vary school contexts across study blocks, team projects, clubs, lab work, college applications, and classroom leadership.
- Keep school examples concrete and bounded; avoid generic "student life" filler.

## Vocabulary Watchlist
- Keep essential, nonessential, trade-off, choice, criteria, buffer, boundary, progress, routine, and focus precise and chapter-bound.
- Avoid vague hustle talk, generic productivity slogans, and overclaiming self-mastery.

## Motif / Callback Opportunities
- The paradox of success from ch01 should echo in discernment, uncommitment, and later execution chapters.
- Choice, criteria, and trade-off language should recur without flattening into sameness.
- Buffer, routine, and focus should call back to earlier elimination work so the book feels cumulative.
- ch20 should return to the whole-life identity question rather than merely recap productivity tactics.
