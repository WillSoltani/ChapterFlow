Progress: The Power of Small Wins sharpens the book by attacking a pattern most people keep excusing. people aim so big, so fast, that they burn out before meaningful movement ever begins. McKeown's counterpunch is to shrink the step, reward progress, and build confidence through repeated movement on meaningful work. The chapter insists that small wins create momentum, and essentialists use minimum viable progress instead of waiting for dramatic breakthroughs.
