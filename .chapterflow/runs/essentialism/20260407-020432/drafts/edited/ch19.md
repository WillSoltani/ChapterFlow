Focus: What's Important Now? sharpens the book by attacking a pattern most people keep excusing. people confuse multitasking with real concentration and keep fragmenting attention across incompatible demands. McKeown's counterpunch is to replace broad scattered attention with a present-tense question about what is important now. The chapter insists that essential focus is not fake multifocus; it is the discipline of naming what matters now and giving it undivided attention.
