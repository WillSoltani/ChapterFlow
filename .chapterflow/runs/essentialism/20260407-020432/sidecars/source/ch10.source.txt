Chapter 10: Clarify: One Decision That Makes a Thousand
Section heading: Eliminate: How can we cut out the trivial many?

Rights-safe chapter framing
- Thesis: clarity of essential intent eliminates noise because one real decision governs hundreds of later ones
- Problem: teams and individuals drift into politics or scattered effort when the real purpose stays vague
- Move: replace pretty clear direction with essential intent that is specific enough to organize behavior
- Stakes: without clear intent, people chase short-term wins that do not add up to meaningful progress
- Bridge: the eliminate section starts by naming the target precisely enough that later noes and cuts become easier
- Tension: people love wordsmithing around goals because it feels safer than making a hard commitment

Supporting notes
- Lack of clarity creates politics or a leaderless 'it's all good' drift.
- Essential intent is both inspirational and concrete.
- A strong intent asks what one thing the group or person wants to be truly excellent at and how success will be recognized.
