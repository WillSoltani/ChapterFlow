Chapter 16: Subtract: Bring Forth More by Removing Obstacles
Section heading: Execute: How can we make doing the vital few things almost effortless?

Rights-safe chapter framing
- Thesis: essentialists make progress by removing the main constraint instead of blindly adding more effort everywhere
- Problem: people keep pouring energy into the system without identifying the bottleneck that actually sets the pace
- Move: find the slowest hiker and remove the obstacle that is holding the rest back
- Stakes: until the right obstacle moves, extra effort elsewhere mostly creates motion without throughput
- Bridge: the chapter replaces heroic strain with targeted obstacle removal
- Tension: removing the main obstacle often means admitting that some impressive-looking work is actually the delay

Supporting notes
- Lao-tzu is invoked to frame wisdom as subtraction.
- Constraints are the obstacles holding the whole system back.
- The main question is which obstacle, if removed, would make the majority of the others matter less.
