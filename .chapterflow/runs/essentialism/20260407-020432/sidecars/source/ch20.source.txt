Chapter 20: Be: The Essentialist Life
Section heading: Execute: How can we make doing the vital few things almost effortless?

Rights-safe chapter framing
- Thesis: essentialism ultimately has to become an identity and way of life, not a periodic tactic added onto a crowded schedule
- Problem: people treat essentialism like one more productivity move instead of a total shift in how life is chosen and lived
- Move: become the kind of person who repeatedly asks what is essential and eliminates everything else
- Stakes: if essentialism stays occasional, the old default always returns and regret reclaims the calendar
- Bridge: the closing chapter lifts the whole book from tactics into character, meaning, and life design
- Tension: a life organized around fewer things will often look strange to a culture that praises visible busyness

Supporting notes
- The chapter contrasts doing essentialism occasionally with being an essentialist.
- The essentialist life is described as a quiet revolution against busy barrenness.
- The book closes on clarity, control, joy, meaning, and the question of regret.
