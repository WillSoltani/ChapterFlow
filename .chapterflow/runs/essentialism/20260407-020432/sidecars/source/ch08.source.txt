Chapter 8: Sleep: Protect the Asset
Section heading: Explore: How can we discern the trivial many from the vital few?

Rights-safe chapter framing
- Thesis: sleep is not a luxury reward after important work; it is what protects the human asset required for important work
- Problem: ambitious people often underinvest in themselves and damage the very instrument through which they contribute
- Move: treat sleep as strategic protection for judgment, connection, and contribution
- Stakes: when the asset is depleted, discernment, creativity, and execution all decline together
- Bridge: the chapter widens exploration from information and space to the condition of the person doing the choosing
- Tension: high-achievement cultures admire sacrifice even when the sacrifice quietly ruins performance

Supporting notes
- The best asset people have for contribution is themselves.
- Lack of sleep damages that asset, especially in successful people who keep pushing.
- Sleep improves exploration, connection-making, and the ability to do less but better.
