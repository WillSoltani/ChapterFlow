Chapter 18: Flow: The Genius of Routine
Section heading: Execute: How can we make doing the vital few things almost effortless?

Rights-safe chapter framing
- Thesis: routine is not the enemy of freedom here; it is the structure that makes the essential the default instead of a daily negotiation
- Problem: without routine, nonessential distractions keep winning each time the person must choose from scratch
- Move: design routines that remove friction and automate the essential path
- Stakes: if the default stays noisy, essential action requires unnecessary willpower every single day
- Bridge: the chapter makes flow a systems question rather than a mood or accidental streak
- Tension: routine sounds dull to people who confuse novelty with freedom, even while chaos keeps draining their best work

Supporting notes
- W. H. Auden is quoted on routine as a sign of ambition in an intelligent person.
- The essentialist designs routine so essential work becomes the default position.
- Routine removes obstacles by reducing repeated decision fatigue.
