# Style Memory

- Write like an authored explainer, not a summary machine.
- Open on pressure, temptation, or a vivid anchor rather than announcing the thesis.
- Keep paragraph jobs distinct: anchor, mechanism, tension, limit, implication, bridge.
- Stay chapter-specific; if a sentence could fit another Ryan Holiday chapter, cut it.
- Easy opens the door, medium explains the mechanism, hard keeps a live limit or threshold alive.
- Competitive tone must feel severe and credible, not sloganized.
- Avoid generic self-help uplift, fake profundity, and polished-but-prefabricated sentence skeletons.
- No em dashes, no pseudo-science, no contamination phrases, and no meta narration about "the chapter" unless truly needed.
