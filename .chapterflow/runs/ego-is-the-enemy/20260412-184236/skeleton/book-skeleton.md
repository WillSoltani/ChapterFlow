## Rotation Plan For Examples

- keep two work, two school, and two personal examples per chapter
- rotate across decision_point, dialogue, dilemma, postmortem, predict_reveal, and before_after
- avoid repeating the same named character in adjacent chapters

## School-Setting Plan

- rotate among debate team, student newspaper, campus club board, lab team, seminar room, sports team film review, and exam-prep settings

## Vocabulary Watchlist

- avoid generic hustle language: grindset, winner, domination, mindset, authenticity theater
- prefer concrete verbs: work, defer, absorb, choose, practice, restrain, recover, refuse

## Motif / Callback Opportunities

- appearance versus contribution
- validation hunger versus private labor
- earned confidence versus identity inflation
- humility as realism rather than softness
- scorecards, standards, and self-governance

## Thin-Chapter Risk Notes

- Chapters 10, 19, and 26 are interstitial closers and must stay tight rather than pretending to be full standalone systems
- later success and failure chapters can drift into generic stoic advice if they lose the ego-specific mechanism
- Chapters 17 and 25 need care to avoid uplift language that outruns the frozen support

## Premium-Routing Candidates

- Chapter 2 because identity-versus-contribution can become generic ambition advice
- Chapter 14 because paranoia and entitlement invite unsupported psychology language
- Chapter 20 because failure-stage framing can slide into motivational recovery talk
- Chapter 24 because scorecard language can become abstract if not tied to a concrete standard
