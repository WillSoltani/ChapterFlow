- ch01 `Talk, talk, talk`
- ch02 `To be or to do?`

- both chapters hold a clear governing fork instead of drifting into generic humility prose
- both chapters preserve an important limit, which keeps them from collapsing into slogans

- cleaner opening pressure because the counterfeit reward appears immediately

- hard depth must preserve a real unresolved cost or boundary
- examples must stay concrete and modern rather than drifting into interchangeable office scenes
