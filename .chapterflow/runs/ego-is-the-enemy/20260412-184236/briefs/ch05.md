Core Claim:
The chapter argues that ego is weakened when ambition serves the larger work indirectly instead of demanding immediate spotlight. The canvas strategy makes progress by creating room, leverage, and value for the work rather than centering the self.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This chapter is about indirect leverage through service. It is not generic humility. Its distinct move is tactical: make yourself useful to the work in ways that compound rather than perform.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter contrasts ego's demand for direct importance with the strategic strength of making room.
Then it frames service and indirect leverage as practical anti-ego tools.
Then it concludes that useful obscurity can produce stronger long-term power than early self-centering.
