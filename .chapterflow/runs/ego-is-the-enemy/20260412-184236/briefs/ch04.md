Core Claim:
The chapter argues that passion, when ego-loaded, can become self-dramatizing intensity that outruns discipline. What ambition needs is not more emotional heat but steadier control, patience, and work that survives mood.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This chapter is not anti-caring. Its distinct move is to separate dramatic passion from disciplined commitment. Ego prefers the first because it feels vivid and important. The chapter prefers the second because it actually lasts.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter exposes how passion can flatter the self.
Then it contrasts that intensity with disciplined steadiness.
Then it concludes that durable work is built by control, not by emotional theater.
