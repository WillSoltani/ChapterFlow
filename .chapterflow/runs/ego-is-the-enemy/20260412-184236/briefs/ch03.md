Core Claim:
After exposing talk and status as ego traps, the chapter gives ambition a durable posture: become a student. Studenthood protects the work because it keeps the self correctable, unfinished, and oriented toward learning rather than self-certification.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This chapter is about apprenticeship as anti-ego discipline. It is not merely saying "keep learning." Its distinct move is that studenthood is the only stable answer once ambition gives up early certainty and status theater.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter shows why self-certainty is dangerous early in ambition.
Then it frames studenthood as the posture that keeps correction, humility, and growth available.
Then it concludes that ego hates being a student because studenthood requires unfinishedness.
