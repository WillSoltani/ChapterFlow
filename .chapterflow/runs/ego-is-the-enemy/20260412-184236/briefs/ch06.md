Core Claim:
The chapter argues that ego often loses most power when the ambitious person stops expressing every impulse, opinion, and intensity immediately. Restraint protects timing, control, and the work itself.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This chapter is about self-restraint as strategic control. It is not generic suppression. Its distinct move is to show that ego's need for expression can weaken the work if it arrives too early or too loudly.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter frames self-expression as an ego temptation.
Then it contrasts impulsive display with restraint, timing, and control.
Then it concludes that controlled ambition is stronger than constantly expressed ambition.
