Core Claim:
The chapter argues that early pride can harden identity around immature success. When ego inflates too soon, growth slows because the person starts defending a version of self that has not yet earned stability.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This chapter is about premature pride as a developmental threat. It is not generic criticism of confidence. Its distinct move is to show that early identity inflation can poison the learning stage before substance is secure.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter frames early pride as an ego response to initial signals of progress.
Then it contrasts premature self-congratulation with continued humility and development.
Then it concludes that ambition stays safer when identity grows more slowly than recognition.
