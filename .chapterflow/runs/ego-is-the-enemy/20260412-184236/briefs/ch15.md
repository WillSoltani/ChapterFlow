Core Claim:
The chapter argues that success stays stable only when the person governs the self instead of trying to dominate externals. Self-management is the answer to the defensive loop of entitlement, control, and paranoia.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This chapter is not generic self-discipline. Its distinct move is to relocate management inward: the real task is governing reactions, ego pressure, and conduct before trying to control the world around you.
