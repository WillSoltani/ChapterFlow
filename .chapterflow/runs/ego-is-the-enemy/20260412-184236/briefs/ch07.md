Core Claim:
The chapter argues that ego is not only loud and outward. It also traps ambition inside obsessive self-reference, including self-loathing and self-preoccupation. Progress requires attention leaving that loop and returning to the work.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This chapter is about ego's inward form. It is not generic advice to stop thinking. Its distinct move is to show that self-obsession can look negative and humble while still keeping the self at the center.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter reframes self-absorption as ego even when it looks like insecurity.
Then it contrasts inward looping with attention returned to action, service, and reality.
Then it concludes that ambition matures when it stops making every event a mirror of the self.
