Core Claim:
The chapter argues that success invites ego to build flattering inner narratives about what events mean and what kind of person the reader now is. Those stories distort reality and make correction harder.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This chapter is about self-narration as a success-stage ego trap. Its distinct move is not merely "avoid lying" or "stay realistic." It is about refusing the self-serving story that turns outcomes into identity proof.
