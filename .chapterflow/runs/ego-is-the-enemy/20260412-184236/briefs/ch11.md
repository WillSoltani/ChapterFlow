Core Claim:
The chapter argues that success does not remove the need for studenthood. It makes studenthood more necessary because recognition and proof tempt the self to harden into certainty.

What Makes This Chapter Distinct:
This is not a repeat of the Aspire chapter on becoming a student. Its distinct move is to show that success-stage ego attacks teachability by making the self feel finished, justified, or above correction.

Author Logic Chain:
First the chapter carries the interstitial warning into the first real Success lesson.
Then it shows that winning can make ego resist correction more than aspiration did.
Then it frames continued studenthood as the safeguard that keeps success from turning into complacency or self-certification.
